## Working rules

- Identify the MC requirements addressed by each nontrivial plan or work unit.
  Audit the plan before implementation, experiments or long diagnostics.
- Preserve the full goals while staging execution. A small financing demo,
  clean PDF build or six populated research ledgers cannot close requirements
  for whole-company representation, research quality or implementation detail.
- Distinguish direct user requirements, policy requirements and derived
  engineering choices. Do not silently convert a convenient library, model
  assumption or stage boundary into an immutable product requirement.
- Keep source evidence, interpretations, assumptions, joint history families
  and scenario-specific results separate and traceable. Preserve unknowns;
  do not invent facts, probabilities, accounting compliance or completeness.
- Close gaps with the specified evidence, and update the requirements register
  and checkpoint after meaningful changes. Add newly discovered gaps rather
  than treating the current list as exhaustive.
- Follow the applicable policies under
  `/home/chakwong/python/claudecodex/policies`. Preserve unrelated work and the
  original paper collection. Git was verified usable on 28 October 2026; use
  the existing repository and configured remote when committing authorised work.
  Do not initialise a replacement repository or rewrite historical provenance.
- Keep operational mission/checklist material out of the reader-facing
  scholarly narrative unless it serves a real implementation-reference purpose.
- Apply MC35, the user's internal-preparation-before-paid-review requirement.
  Finish feasible source research, facts, calculations, software checks and
  document repairs before referring a specific question to a professional.
  Supply a proposed answer, exact sources, competing interpretation and checked
  consequences. Keep unfinished internal work on the internal queue; “human
  review pending” is an acceptance status, not a reason to stop that work.
  Ordinary reader feedback need not be purchased from an accounting firm.
  No professional engagement, outreach or fees are authorised by this policy.
