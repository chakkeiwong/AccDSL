## Question and skeptical audit

For an issuer's genuinely compound convertible bond, where the holder exercises
an unchanged equity conversion right before maturity, what accounting policy
and measurement are supported? Comparator: the inspected IAS32.AG32 maturity
rule and the deliberately supplied earlier-conversion treatment in the EPS
example. The question is measurement on original-term early conversion, not
diluted-EPS time weighting, an induced conversion, a repurchase, a cash option,
or a renegotiated debt-for-equity exchange.

Earlier attempts produced a script-only Google page, irrelevant Bing results,
and an IAS Plus summary with no inspected affirmative early-conversion rule.
IFRIC19.3(c)'s original-terms exclusion and IAS8.10–12's policy hierarchy have
already been inspected; repeating them does not resolve measurement. Follow
new technical interpretive leads, starting with the accounting firms' own
convertible-instrument manuals, and retain actual passages locally. A search
snippet, the publisher's reputation and maturity wording are not support for
the target question. The strongest opposing reading is that IFRS9's general
extinguishment measurement would create a conversion gain/loss; any proposed
carry-over policy must explain the applicable exception or analogy.

Audit passes for bounded documentary inquiry. No experiment or new numerical
default is proposed. The source edition, issuer/holder perspective, original
versus changed terms and liability/equity classification are material
assumptions to inspect before using a passage. Stop a dependent assertion when
those conditions are absent; continue other ready work.
