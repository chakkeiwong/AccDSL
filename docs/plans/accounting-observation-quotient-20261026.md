## Skeptical audit

The source baseline is simulation/08-compression.tex and the earlier uncertainty
representation note. The 169 teaching checks include signal arithmetic and
persistent-path counterexamples, but are not a general quotient checker.
Equal current reports, expected outputs or separate marginals are wrong
comparators for the stated joint-law target. The exact comparator must be the
whole finite path law/relation under the same supplied model and policies.
No convenient transition probabilities may be inferred from history counts.
Require explicit exact rational probability masses for probabilistic input;
unweighted models use relations and never gain weights from enumeration.

This is an authored finite engineering/derivation unit, not a performance or
scientific ranking experiment. No new literature claim or algorithm selection
requires external search. The sufficient argument is derived in the project
notation; it does not claim a necessary criterion or apply to arbitrary hidden
non-Markov history. A finite state must include every persistent variable needed
by its supplied transition/observation law; absent variables stay a model-scope
limitation. Source and accounting admission remain false.

## Defaults and assumptions

Finite horizon, states, actions and scenarios are explicit supplied scope;
limits are engineering guards, not a completeness claim about a real company.
Exact Fraction arithmetic is justified by rational fixture inputs and exposes
mass errors before comparison. Observable policies are separately supplied
tables keyed by prior observations and actions; hidden state cannot be their
input. The same policy is used for original and compressed enumeration.
Initial distributions/support and initial observations are explicitly mapped.
All variables relevant to future dependence must be in state; keep a fixed
loan type as a worked adverse case. Reuse certificates only under exact model,
partition, output/information and horizon hashes. Budget stops return unknown
with an unfinished obligation frontier, never exact/approved.
