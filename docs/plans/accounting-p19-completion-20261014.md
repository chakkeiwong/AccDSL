## Question, baseline and evidence contract

Can the exact integrated proposal govern the full intended product and its
initial implementation profile without unresolved consequential design choices?
Compare the candidate against the unchanged
[mission requirements](accounting-mission-requirements.md), original G1–G18
acceptance obligations and supported P16/P17 contracts. The
[current assessment](../../data/accounting-master/post-certainty-readiness-20261013/assessment.json)
is the starting disposition; P18-06 supplies the final candidate.

Positive proposal readiness requires substantive design evidence, complete
required cases, consistent interfaces and an independent technical review of
the same candidate. It does not require completing the whole product runtime
or empirical forecasting program. Conversely, a missing method, necessary
source justification or required design case cannot be renamed later runtime
work to obtain readiness.

Maintain distinct judgments for:

- Specification: supported methods, contracts, examples and implementability.
- Implementation: conformance, adapters, recovery and executable behavior.
- Accounting/science: rule applicability, numerical validity and any empirical
  forecast claims, each with its own required evidence.
- Review and acceptance: independent technical findings, domain judgments and
  actual human understanding, recorded separately from author checks.

The primary readiness criterion is satisfaction of the original design
obligations with no unresolved consequential contradiction or decision.
Missing required sources/cases/methods, invalid transfer of a theorem, inconsistent
contracts, unbound reviewed inputs and unresolved consequential choices veto
positive readiness. Hash/link/row-count checks establish assessment integrity
only. Local arithmetic and clean builds retain their scoped meanings. A
negative assessment triggers its next feasible repair; it is not a continuation
veto against the research direction.

1. A source correction changes an admitted reading, history set, query result

2. Annual/interim/revised information views control what a scenario can know;

3. A financing/group/FX event crosses perimeter, currency, journal, state and

calculation or exchange through its assigned P17/P18 repair; do not start the
held runtime to compensate for an incomplete design explanation.

### P19-04 — Obtain independent technical review and repair findings

MC29/32–35; G5/G8/G10 and any substantive contracts examined.
Depends on an exact review-ready P18-06 candidate and P19-01–03 evidence.

Prepare a bounded review packet containing the question, candidate/source
hashes, original obligations, consequential design choices, proposed answers,
worked consequences, competing interpretations and known limits. Direct the
review at actual mathematics, accounting assumptions, interfaces and connected
cases. Divide a large review by substantive topic when needed, preserving a
final interaction review; do not accumulate superficial agreement counts.

Record reviewer identity/role, inspected scope, exact bytes, findings and
verdict. Repair findings in their responsible P17/P18 units and review material
changes. Verify the final verdict concerns the same candidate that P19 will
assess. Author self-review cannot be relabelled independent review, and model
review cannot establish human comprehension or professional accounting approval.

Prepare ordinary reader questions that ask the reader to reconstruct the
reasoning, not simply say whether the document looks clear. Keep those answers
separate from technical review. Under MC35, finish feasible internal work before
referring a bounded domain question: provide facts, dated sources, a proposed
answer, its counterargument and checked consequences. Any later outreach,
professional engagement or fee requires its own authorization; none is launched
by this plan. Missing feedback leaves its acceptance pending while independent
internal work continues.

Output: scoped independent findings, repairs and final review binding, plus
separate human/domain questions and any actual responses. Done for technical
review only when consequential findings are resolved on the exact candidate.
Pending human/domain judgments must remain visible, never implied by that pass.
