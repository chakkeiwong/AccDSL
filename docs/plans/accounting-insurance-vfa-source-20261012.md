The current insurance discussion supplies a deliberately conditional component
example: a 20 increase in underlying value and a fixed 10% entity share give
a candidate fee change of 2. It acknowledges guarantees and missing eligibility
facts but does not explain the economic mechanism that makes those facts
consequential. Read the retained official AASB 17 December 2022 compilation,
particularly paragraph 45 and B101–B118, following material definitions and
dependencies, before revising that explanation.

stated share of underlying returns? The comparator is the current conditional
10% component illustration, not a calibrated valuation or a complete VFA model.
