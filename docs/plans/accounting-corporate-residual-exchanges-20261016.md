## Skeptical audit before execution

The plan passes only with the following constraints. Do not replace zero
carrying weights by equal weights or presume that an underdetermined local
feasible set is an IAS36 allocation rule. Inspect IAS36.117–125 and distinguish
individual-asset reversal from CGU allocation. If no source-supported allocation
is available, preserve that exact design/authority residual while specifying
the refusal and continuing independent methods.

Lease scope must identify the actual surrendered rights and their attributable
carrying amounts/liabilities; one physical fraction cannot allocate heterogeneous
rights. Contract costs require the IAS2/IAS16/IAS38 priority, future-service
eligibility, systematic amortisation, impairment ordering and reversal cap;
revenue modifications cannot be used to capitalise past performance. Acquisition
DTA depends on whether new information concerns acquisition-date facts and
arrives within the measurement period, rather than merely on elapsed time.
Mixed tax rates/origins require traced lots and separate recoverability findings;
no automatic offset or zero-tax substitute is admitted.

Historical EU sources support conditional examples only. Source dates and
supplied legal/valuation findings are not October 2026 entity admission. The
financial oracle must compute from opening and event inputs, never copy the
expected journal. Reuse transport conventions only after checking their report
and account assumptions for these methods.
