## Intent and evidence contract

Main question: can the retained source records identify eligible pre-start
input versions for the proposed target intervals? Mechanism: join the selected
report identity to its distinct date-role evidence and compare conservative
bounds to a declared cutoff, while preserving the exact-byte admission hold.

Primary criterion: every issuer has a retained target report and a distinct
source-date role summary; the matrix never treats one date role as another and
reports counts by role/status. This is a data-contract criterion, not evidence
that any forecast was issued or that a model has predictive value.

Hard veto: malformed dates, mismatched hashes, missing issuer identity or a
role silently reused as selected-byte availability invalidates the matrix.
Continuation veto: mutation of frozen sources, prohibited acquisition or a
calculation that loses the original denominator. Missing roles and unknown
bytes are unresolved evidence that triggers the next preparation step.
Coverage counts, filing-date precedences and illustrative cutoffs are
explanatory diagnostics only; they cannot promote a future-origin law or
support a performance ranking.
