Question: can the exact timing rule have an explicit versioned owner while
preserving old bank records and receipts, ordinary financial results, and
source/schema identity? Baseline is the frozen BankAssessment/1 packet and its
auditor, plus the completed full-request timing comparison. Its ten cases are
not profile migration. Audit before changing any runtime/profile assumptions.

Inspect all operations, timestamp consumers, profile/schema/semantic locks,
submission bytes, publications and replay rules. Specify the successor's scope
and which old identities remain reusable. A new profile must have its own
schema/contract references and request identity. Never relabel or overwrite old
receipts or let a previously published query masquerade as a successor result.
Do not mechanically copy a global registry containing deliberate malformed
challenges into a supposedly valid migration fixture.

Primary criterion: complete matched successor exchanges for every supported
operation family, exact adverse timestamp cases, unchanged ordinary financial
results after explicit reference substitution, and rejection of cross-version
replay/profile mismatches. Whole-packet conversion is not required if a bounded
dependency-closure migration is the justified scope. Admission flags stay false.
Vetoes: unsupported source claims, stale schema/semantic locks, lost unknowns,
rewritten old identities, financial drift, false publication or unowned time
comparisons. Counts and clean schema validation are explanatory only.

Audit inherited date checks separately from information-time comparisons.
Retain the exact parser's explicit precision/calendar restrictions. Use the
smallest actual native positive closure per operation and meaningful refusal,
partial and replay cases; compare full results, not ratios alone. Local fixture
timestamps are synthetic. No claim of current regulatory interpretation.

Record the owner inventory and migration contract before implementation.
Repair any counterexample under the same scope, then save checks, complete
exchange deltas, source locks, manifests and adoption limits. Integrate the
coherent result into a preserved manuscript successor and refresh all controls.
No installs, agents, commits, GPU, fees, outreach, publication or forecasting.
