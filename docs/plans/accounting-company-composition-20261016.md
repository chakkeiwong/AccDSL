## Question and evidence contract

Can corporate, financing and consolidation methods share one pair of legal books
and produce a reconciled interim and annual group close? Comparator: the original
P17-04 closing/group promises and P17-05 ownership promises, not component counts.
Existing CorporateComponents/5, CorporateResiduals/1, FinancingComponents/2 and
GroupComponents/1 remain evidence and are not enlarged in place.

Candidate CompanyComposition/1 uses a supplied 2025 opening for parent P and
subsidiary S. P owns 80%, then buys 10% for 20 after the interim close without
losing control. Both use USD, the same dates/policies and historical EU IFRS
conditional sources. Return settlement, debt redemption, payroll/depreciation,
intercompany inventory transfer/settlement, external sales, current/deferred tax
and ownership changes use the same legal cash and balances. Group adjustments
are recomputed from legal books and lot history, never posted back to them.

Primary criterion: independent reconstruction from opening/events agrees with
separately authored journals, states and all legal/group statements; gross cash,
tax origins, elimination/release and NCI/parent equity reconcile. Every supported
operation has closed inputs and positive/refusal/replay/recovery paths. Migration
is proven only for named projected states, with preserved owner/source/date and
economic memory; unsupported fields refuse. Balance/schema/build counts alone
do not pass the accounting or interoperability criterion.

Wrong period, owner, source, origin, reciprocal amount, elimination, tax, NCI,
cash classification, duplicate effects or partial publication vetoes the affected
path and triggers repair. Protected-input/index change, corrupted evidence or
invalid target stops dependent work. Missing actual facts/law blocks only its
application. No random ranking, forecasting or runtime durability claim follows.
Artifacts: `data/accounting-master/company-composition-20261016/`, with numbered

## Skeptical audit and assumptions

The plan passes with these repairs to naive composition. Shared cash cannot be
copied from each component's independent opening. Group-only adjustments cannot
be counted as legal transactions. Ownership changes must preserve the accrued
NCI share before the purchase and allocate later profit at the new interest.
The source's IFRS10.23/B96 and IAS7.42A–42B support an equity/financing treatment
of a without-loss purchase, not a new acquisition or profit item.

Do not copy a January debt accrual into the next year's earnings merely because
its event date is January. This case starts with an already matured liability
and prior accrued interest: current interest expense is zero while interest
cash paid is ten. The opening and compound component carry that history explicitly.
Old annual report labels are not migrated. Full prior acquisition/issuance and
2024 reconstruction are not claimed by the supplied opening.

Migration and instantiation differ. A predecessor's synthetic owner cannot be
renamed P and called a lossless migration. Migration fixtures preserve their
original owners and dates in a separate target record. The company independently
instantiates the supported method with its own facts and component allocations.
