# Credit recovery after financial closure: source and interface scope

27 October 2026. MC03/05/08/11/13/15/16/17/19/24/25/26/28/33/34/35;
W07/09/11/12/16/17. Predecessor: credit-claim-continuity-20261027/result.md.

Question: what source-qualified information and accounting choices would be
required to represent an actual recovery after the retained financial book has
closed, while preserving its original write-off and nominal-component history?
This is design research before any new event implementation or monetary case.

Skeptical audit: event_after_close is an interface limit, not a legal conclusion.
Nominal written-off portions are neither carrying values nor proof of surviving
rights. A new recovery may require attribution, classification, recognition and
information-date evidence that the original no-reasonable-recovery finding does
not supply. Do not invent a receipt, choose a jurisdiction or assert current
standard applicability from the old EU original snapshot.

Evidence contract: inspect the locally retained primary authority around
write-off, continuing enforcement, subsequent recoveries, derecognition and
cash-flow presentation; check official current sources where needed and retain
exact excerpts/source anchors. Comparator is the frozen native engine and
existing documentary authority, not a fabricated new engine. Pass means a
source-supported list of determined and undetermined requirements plus a
concrete internal proposal and alternatives for each unresolved choice. Legal
scope uncertainty and absent transaction facts veto posting/admission, not
continued internal preparation. Explanatory evidence includes the nine observed
after-close refusals and dated nominal reconciliation.

Research intent: distinguish receipt evidence, allocation to a historical
portion, accounting measurement/presentation and legal assessment. Promotion
criterion is a checked source-to-requirement map; unsupported scope transfer
vetoes a rule. Repair source ambiguity by examining surrounding paragraphs and
linked definitions. No forecast, broad bank completeness, jurisdictional legal
determination, actual-issuer conclusion, or paid review follows from this work.

Default audit: original EU authority is a historical source version, not an
automatic current default. POCI and performing fixtures are distinct histories.
The nominal companion is a read-only baseline. Recovery amount/date, source
document and portion allocation remain unknown until supplied by evidence.
Do not carry an inherited gross/allowance formula across closure without a
derivation and authority. Budgets are convenience limits for this local audit,
not adequacy criteria: one focused source pass and at most three focused search
batches before documenting remaining gaps and continuing independent work.

Output: data/accounting-master/credit-recovery-scope-20261027/ with source map,
checked exact citations, implementation boundary, proposed event fields,
result/decision table and next plan. No external engagement is authorised.
