## Skeptical audit and intent

The path design carries economic memory across years, but every year still has
exactly one purchase, delivery, collection and payment. Repeating the entire
annual interpreter would repeat tax/closing and is wrong. Splitting only a cash
total would also lose claim identity and timing. Introduce uniquely identified
operations with operation-specific payloads. Keep annual depreciation, legacy
coupon, synthetic tax and close as once-per-year operations. Keep one fully
specified bridge contract; multiple facilities/accrual contracts remain separate.

The comparator is the existing complete Northstar2025 run, not balance alone.
Lower its authored inputs into economic operation payloads without projected
journals. Check every original journal and all29 report lines. Then split
purchase, delivery, collection, payment, payroll, capex and dividend operations
at the same instants, preserving original dependency order and aggregate inputs.
Such splitting should preserve accounting totals under these linear methods;
it need not preserve claim names or within-instant cash prefixes. Moving dates,
changing order, changing cohort lives or changing costs may alter economics;
no general aggregation invariance is claimed.

Question: can repeated, identified operations generate the same linked annual
statements while independently enforcing stock, claim and cash preconditions?
Expected failures include reused IDs, settlement before recognition,
over-settlement, unavailable stock, false/unknown control transfer, cash shortfall
and repeated closing. Missing values at an operation must stop there, retaining
the already computed prefix; unknown global methods/opening cannot run at all.

## Evidence contract

Primary criterion: exact baseline journals/reports; equivalent split examples;
claim/asset/inventory controls after each event; order-sensitive adverse examples;
no mutation by refused events; prefix reporting never fabricated as year end.
Method/source/body/result identity must be retained. A schema-valid unsupported
method is unsupported, not an implicit fallback. Splitting annual tax or close
is invalid. Zero checks/count/runtime do not substitute for these criteria.

Promotion vetoes: changed protected bytes, balance-only comparison, duplicate
recognition, ignored claim allocation, stock/cash underflow, hidden rounding,
unknown coerced to zero, annual totals presented after failure, mismatched
report identity. These trigger repair. Corrupt original source or contradictory
annual method is a continuation veto for dependent work. Passing means only this
documentary profile; no production runtime, legal treatment, empirical forecast,
arbitrary joint law, full sector semantics or whole-language completion.
