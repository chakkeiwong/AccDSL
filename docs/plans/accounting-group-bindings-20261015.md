15 October2026. MC02/04–07/11/13–20/24–30/32–35; G1–4/G6–10/G13–18.

## Intent and skeptical audit

Question: can the delivered foreign-operation history retain exact legal
entities, book owners, dated control/perimeter, currency/rates and owner/NCI
attribution when submitted through the common record/transaction model?
Comparator: existing2.81 arithmetic and R01/R02/I1 envelopes and replay rules.
Primary pass: exact immutable record/request/result closure, independently
recomputed financial history and adverse/recovery outcomes. Matching display
names, combining full/retained disposal branches, applying group overlays to
entity books, changing a selected source head, or using only balanced journals
vetoes the affected path. Shape/count/build checks are integrity diagnostics.
No implementation, real control/valuation or current applicability is claimed.

The audit finds the corporate profile is intentionally closed and has no
goodwill, CTA or NCI accounts. Reusing its CompanyState schema would silently
discard required group state. Specify an explicit Group profile extension
with its own payload schemas; preserve the common request/result vocabulary,
identity, content hashing, owner-bound compare-and-swap and replay order.
Do not edit a frozen profile or claim arbitrary cross-profile compatibility.

Another risk is treating a consolidation worksheet as a legal cash journal.
The group acquisition/disposal entries include acquired/removed subsidiary
cash, whereas the parent pays/receives only consideration. Legal entity books
remain pinned, and group transitions mutate only their separate group owner.
The full and retained-interest disposals are separate successors of the same
R state, never sequential disposals of one asset.
