## The question and the deliverable

Can AccDSL describe a company's economically relevant condition, its accounting
memory and different actors' information well enough to reconstruct alternative
histories, simulate decisions and shocks, and explain cash flows and claim
values without mistaking reported totals for a fully observed company?

The deliverable is an implementable, source-grounded scholarly specification
with connected reference cases and a reproducible release decision. A collection
of isolated examples, a large bibliography or a successful PDF build does not
meet that objective. The work has three inseparable strands:

1. Reconcile every original acceptance condition with present evidence and
   assign the remaining work to an executable dependency graph.
2. Compare the literature across accounting information systems, inverse
   problems, dynamic firms, valuation, reporting incentives, forensic evidence,
   filtering, control and computational approximation.
3. Develop a task-relative theory and construction procedure for company state,
   then connect observations, uncertain histories, actions, journals, reports,
   financing and claim valuation through existing typed interfaces.

The machine-readable task graph, original-condition crosswalk, source locks
and execution records live in data/accounting-master/unified-program-20261028/.
The program checker reports ready work and verifies dependencies and recorded
evidence; it does not launch agents, background campaigns, arbitrary commands
or professional engagements. Research judgments still require examination of
their evidence. A graph validator cannot confer scholarly acceptance.
