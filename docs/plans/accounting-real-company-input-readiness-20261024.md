cash and equivalents are not automatically unrestricted cash; net inventory
is not units/cost or a full recovered-lot schedule; net PPE is not cohort
life/depreciation state; displayed rounded amounts are not underlying exact
balances; a literal dash is not zero. Apple fiscal periods cannot be renamed
calendar years. Missing original-byte publication clocks do not block a named
source reading, but do block stronger as-of assertions. The actual history
family and absolute feasible remainder remain unresolved in the retained
package. None of these gaps permits importing Northstar or P's tax rules,
zero balances, operating assumptions, private contract terms or probabilities.

view, observations, preserved inventory obligations and the target profile.
Separate displayed evidence, conditional derivation, explicitly supplied
assumptions, unavailable required state and unsupported profile scope. For
each target dependency, show the relevant source observation or obligation,
its limitation, affected operation/report/carry and the next needed evidence
or method. Return no executable opening when requirements are unmet. Preserve
the original actual-history question and conditional subsystem results.

Inspect the exact source record fields and target validation before choosing
mapping candidates. Check all original inventory/row identities and named
view restrictions. Test cross-issuer/view leakage, dash coercion, consolidated
to-legal relabeling, false zero unknowns, fiscal-date changes, forged ready
status and changed dependency records. A known source amount with an unknown
state allocation should remain useful evidence, not become unknown text or
a fabricated numeric input. An unsupported finite target is a result about
that target, not evidence that the company cannot be simulated.

target profiles are bounded diagnostic comparators, not an immutable product
scope; all supplied financial facts are retained and unchanged. Primary pass

missing state triggers requirements/design repair. Only corrupt indispensable
source or an unresolved human choice blocks its dependent step.
