Audit rejects two tempting shortcuts. Rounding a supremum numerically need not
give the maximum rounded result when the endpoint is excluded. Adding extra

principal to cents. The adopted tie rule must be explicit and both signs tested;
component rounding cannot silently introduce a balancing plug. Certified
enclosures must be distinguished from the exact image of a known interval.
Do not install a new solver or call enumeration of a small test lattice a proof
over the continuum. Use local algebraic derivations plus exact fractions and
finite integer comparisons where applicable. No external method claim is needed.
