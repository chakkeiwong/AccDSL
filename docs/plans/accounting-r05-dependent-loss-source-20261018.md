## Question and skeptical audit

What do primary forecast-comparison sources establish about uncertainty in a
paired loss difference, and which assumptions remain unestablished for this
selected nine-issuer, annual/interim panel? The comparator is the protected
E3 contract in future-validation-20261015/protocol-ledger.md, its current
protocol supplements, and the completed r05-vintage-admission-20261018 record.
Gneiting/Raftery supplies score definitions, not a panel inference procedure.

The initial idea, “use Diebold–Mariano for dependence,” is materially
incomplete: it could turn a time-series asymptotic result into a small-panel
claim, confuse the forecast horizon with an observed dependence cutoff, or
ignore shared issuer/calendar shocks, estimation, exposed outcomes and vintages.
Revise the work to a bounded source investigation with an explicit transfer
audit. Nine issuers and 192 reports are not interchangeable sample sizes. A
finite-sample adjustment, if found, is not automatically an exact or panel-valid
test. No convenient equal weighting, pooling, HAC bandwidth or bootstrap is
selected. The revised documentary plan passes the skeptical audit because
completion requires source-specific assumptions and explicit unresolved inputs.

## Research intent and evidence contract

Main question: can any inspected result justify an E3 uncertainty procedure
under presently established inputs? Candidate mechanism: inference on the
time sequence of paired losses, accounting for its covariance. Expected failure:
an executable statistic with an invalid sampling or dependence interpretation.

Primary completion criterion: retain and technically inspect new primary text,
record exact sections/equations/tables/appendices and notice/version checks,
and map each supported conclusion and forbidden transfer to current E3 inputs.
Produce a concrete next-evidence queue rather than a generic review referral.
Acquisition failure is recorded as source-blocked and triggers another planned
lead, not a claim that no relevant method exists.

Promotion veto: a source formula or condition is misread, an unchecked source
supports a claim, a small-sample result is called exact, a time-series result is
silently extended across firms, or a score definition is treated as inference.
Repair those defects before recording. Continuation veto for this unit:
corruption of protected evidence, unresolved source-version identity, or a
source cannot be inspected sufficiently to support its proposed claim. Missing
outcomes, byte vintages, estimand or sample design blocks the dependent empirical
run; it does not block this documentary inquiry or independent internal work.
Source/simulation sample sizes and reported test rejection rates are
explanatory source evidence, never project promotion metrics.

No ranking, default, population representativeness, empirical calibration,
forecast benefit, selected margin/budget/weighting, professional readiness or
whole MC/R08 closure follows. No stochastic experiment is authorised here.
