## Refresh and skeptical audit

The P17-06 matrix still names the same-bank accounting-to-capital connection.
The manuscript already has US national-bank instrument/deduction rules and a
separate hypothetical cash/allowance path. Its 75% weight and deduction of 2
are explicitly illustrative. Repeating that arithmetic would not close the
gap. Nor may the historical Australian commitment case be labelled a US
regulatory book. Use a separately identified synthetic US national bank with
one stated prudential perimeter, dated rule package and supplied accounting
measurements. Preserve all prior cases and their jurisdictions.

The retained 1 January 2026 §3.2 distinguishes GAAP net assets from regulatory
carrying value: an ordinary loan's allowance does not reduce its exposure.
Its standardized-total-RWA definition subtracts allowance excluded from Tier 2.
Section 3.20(d)(3) caps eligible ALLL/AACL at 1.25% of the relevant RWA before
allowance adjustments. Treat the denominator ordering as a question to derive
from the complete clauses, not an inherited formula. Read §§3.31–3.32 for
deducted assets and exposure weights before authoring the worked case.

This audit passes for that bounded inquiry and subsequent source-supported
conditional calculation. Missing actual-bank facts preclude actual-bank
conclusions. They do not prevent a clearly authored complete specification
case. A high ratio cannot establish dividend permission, liquidity adequacy,
a complete legal rule package or compliance with every capital constraint.

## Research intent and evidence contract

Question: can the same identified bank events reconcile the accounting book,
eligible capital, allowance cap, exposure base and regulatory denominators,
with missing eligibility and averaging inputs remaining explicit?

Mechanism: dated source rules operate on a separate RegulatoryAssessment that
references the exact accounting view and admission records. The comparator
is the existing illustrative path and the actual operative clauses, not a
new method's speed or an arbitrarily favorable capital ratio.

Primary pass: independently reconstructible event journals, accounting and
capital reconciliations, exact numerical examples, rule applicability,
input/output fields and positive/adverse decisions. Vetoes: wrong perimeter
or snapshot, invented stock eligibility, double deduction of the allowance,
including deducted goodwill in RWA, a circular cap formula without source
support, substituting period-end assets for required average assets, or
presenting supplied allowances as calibrated CECL estimates. Each rejects the
affected result and triggers repair. Corrupt sources or an unresolvable
operative contradiction stop dependent work; continue independent work.

Balanced journals, matching totals, source counts and threshold passes are
explanatory/integrity checks. They do not establish actual-bank compliance,
forecast quality, permission to distribute capital or general runtime
correctness. No stochastic ranking is involved. Preserve results under
data/accounting-master/p17-completion-20261014/P17-06/prudential-connection/.
