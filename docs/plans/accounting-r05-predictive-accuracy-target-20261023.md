## Question and skeptical plan audit

The user selected research comparison of predictive accuracy for the first E3
operating-cash-flow comparison. This unit defines the directional research
target and its zero-equality boundary. It must not turn that research purpose
into a business-value claim or promote a convenient inference package. The
frozen candidate-minus-locked-comparator CRPS orientation, equal issuer weights,
common asset scale and full baseline ladder remain binding.

Audit findings: “improvement” can mean lower forecast loss or higher liquidity
value; only the former follows from the selected purpose. A zero expected loss
difference is a comparison boundary, not evidence of a nonzero effect. Equal
issuer weights do not specify within-issuer origin weights. A fixed-window
forecast paper does not select AccDSL's refit policy. Nine companies and 192
reports are not independent replications. A scalar CRPS result cannot establish
the secondary joint cash/debt task. The plan therefore records zero as a
directional research threshold, keeps material promotion margin unresolved,
and treats theory/sample adequacy as prerequisites.

## Research intent ledger and evidence contract

**Main question.** At future origins for the nine named companies, does the
alternative-history forecast policy have lower expected asset-scaled operating-
cash-flow CRPS than the prelocked fixed-interpretation comparator under the
same economics and information?

**Candidate/mechanism.** Preserve admissible histories and their conditional
predictive laws, with an explicitly locked refit/tuning policy. The comparator
uses one fixed interpretation. Both use identical targets, cutoffs, positive
as-of assets, scenario information and tuning caps.

**Primary comparison criterion.** The expected candidate-minus-comparator
effect `Delta` is below zero. Equality at zero is the research boundary. A
future result is directional evidence only when a checked uncertainty bound
supports the sign and all validity vetoes pass. The frozen E3 promotion rule
still requires a defensible bound below a separately justified negative margin;
the margin is not selected here.

**Promotion vetoes.** Invalid chronology, late/exposed inputs, incomplete or
non-finite laws, nonpositive or incomparable scale, failed comparator,
unresolved target/perimeter, missing required dependence diagnostics,
unsupported inference conditions, or a bound that does not establish the
predeclared sign. An unweighted set cannot be assigned a CRPS.

**Continuation vetoes.** Corrupted frozen sources or records, a calculation
that changes the selected estimand, or an execution that requires held runtime,
installation or empirical authority. A candidate failure motivates a repair;
it does not reject the research direction.

**Explanatory diagnostics.** Per-company and per-origin losses, coverage,
failure counts, CRPS decomposition, scale distribution, serial/cross-company
covariance, fit attempts, runtime and validation loss. These do not become
promotion criteria merely because they are available.

**Non-claims.** A passing directional comparison would not establish economic
value, accounting compliance, representative-company performance, future-
company generalisation, causal value of ambiguity, probability for unresolved
sets, or production readiness.

Pre-mortem: a model can show a negative mean loss while the comparator was
selected after seeing test outcomes, failures were dropped, or the sign arose
from an asset-scale mismatch. The earliest checks are comparator lock before
test outcomes, full original denominator, common positive scale and paired
per-cell sign arithmetic. A long run cannot repair a wrong estimand.

## Concrete protocol requirements

1. Identify each issuer's complete fiscal interval, pre-start cutoff, forecast
   lock, first-release outcome and exact version. Retain asynchronous intervals.
2. Select an issuer's eligible origin set and within-issuer weights before
   losses. The fixed nine-company weight is `1/9`; do not renormalize after
   failures. Preserve the full denominator and unresolved mass.
3. Lock model family, refit cadence/window, preprocessing, feature history,
   tuning budget, seeds, stopping rule and fallback before each affected test
   outcome. Training and validation data must obey every source-availability
   predecessor.
4. Lock the primary comparator by a pre-test rule. Include the complete naive,
   runoff, tuned-classical, fixed-interpretation, preserved-alternative and
   extension ladder; report the locked comparator separately from exploratory
   arms.
5. Compute per-cell raw and common-asset-scaled CRPS differences. Require a
   complete finite law for both arms and positive comparable assets. Retain
   failed cells and distinguish retrospective score from prospective evidence.
6. Estimate the weighted panel effect only under a declared future-origin law
   and a dependence-aware uncertainty procedure. The variance includes every
   cross-origin/cross-issuer covariance; independence is not a default.
7. Treat a negative descriptive mean as nomination evidence. Directional
   promotion requires a predeclared bound below zero plus validity vetoes; the
   frozen material-improvement promotion still needs a separate negative
   margin justified by the research decision.
8. Treat confidence, multiplicity, temporal block length, replication and
   resource budget as unresolved until the origin law, refit policy and error
   tolerance are specified. Source-file counts do not set sample size.

## Exact documentary checks and next dependency

The unit will check sign orientation on finite point laws, equal-company
denominators under unequal origin counts and missing cells, CRPS scaling by a
fixed positive asset amount, comparator-lock timing, and refusal of an
unweighted set or a nonpositive scale. It will also record the exact unresolved
fields needed before inference. No model, source acquisition, paid review,
runtime or empirical campaign is part of this unit.

If these checks pass, the next justified work is a source-bound target/perimeter
and origin/refit feasibility design. If a check fails, repair the documentary
contract. Do not infer a statistical ranking or economic threshold from the
fixtures.
