# Preserve measurement families when attaching a candidate history

24 October 2026. S2–S4; MC04/05/08/09/10/11/13/16/24/26/28.

The closed conditional monetary-history reference retains symbolic openings and
dated supplied events. The next question is how its snapshot linear forms can
be constrained by a complete measurement family without choosing one measurement
witness, replacing source intervals, dropping shared identities or promoting
proposed mappings to admitted history.

Audit existing source-state dependencies, temporal measurement constraints and
history mappings before writing the attachment. Keep both parent definitions
and their hashes. Require exact variable bindings, value dimensions/scale,
entity/perimeter/basis, time roles and explicit mapping premises. Intersect the
full measurement constraints with the supplied candidate history, not a selected
solver assignment. A changed source context or incompatible date must reject.
Snapshot history outputs and period movements need different declared forms;
do not flatten a flow into an ending balance to make the join work.

Start with a finite, constructed comparator whose measured quantities and history
roles are explicitly authored. The retained real-source network remains a separate
readiness case: absent source-to-account/economic-state mappings and missing
templates/openings stay unresolved. Never relabel synthetic cash or accounts as
Apple, and never infer unrestricted legal cash from consolidated captions.

Evidence contract: exhibit a case where parent families separately have witnesses
but their proposed joint attachment is infeasible, and a case with multiple joint
histories where selecting one parent witness would wrongly discard a valid member.
Check exact rational replay of original parent constraints and history incidence.
Preserve omitted/unmapped obligations and separate conditional candidate existence,
formulation completeness, search coverage and source/accounting admission.

Defaults: supplied finite mappings/templates remain hypotheses; no discovery or
all-company completeness follows. Reuse exact solver primitives and explicit
budgets, with zero-limit/unknown tests. Missing evidence limits the target family;
it does not automatically reject independent DSL work. Record a skeptical design
and source locks before implementation. No forecast or numerical default change.
