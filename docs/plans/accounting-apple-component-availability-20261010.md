separate when the model carries a product transition into supply, price,
outsourcing, commitments and inventory histories? The target is the complete
annual-06 Item 1A body beginning “Future operating results depend upon the

is already retained and is context, not a new closure unit.

The primary pass criterion is exact retention of both annual-06 paragraphs and
their modality, original-PDF verification, bounded dependencies, and a
reader-facing explanation that distinguishes shortage, price, supplier
solvency/consolidation, custom-component capacity, yield, supplier priority,
outsourcing delay and alternative-source timing. A candidate integration may
be promoted within author preparation only if the inherited 48 displays and 66
bibliography entries remain intact, the changed section has a clean build, and
the rendered changed/adjacent pages are read continuously.

Hard vetoes are a missed qualification, wrong source scope or hash, an issuer
assertion changed into an adjudicated fact, an invented supplier/output/price/
probability/contract remedy, a dependency taken from an uninspected span, a
deleted inherited substantive passage, or a failed preservation/build check.
Counts and surface diagnostics are explanatory. They cannot establish
accounting treatment, actual supply history, causal effects, probability,
forecast validity or whole-report semantic acceptance. Independent reader,
human and domain acceptance remain pending.

## Reader-facing route

The reader enters with competition and product transitions separated from the
question of whether the needed components can be obtained. The new question is
whether two supply histories can produce the same planned product path: a broad
industry shortage/price shock, or a custom-component route delayed by yield,
capacity, supplier priority or a failing single source. Before any integration,
the source body will be partitioned into those mechanisms and linked only to
the inspected commitments, custom-component, transition and outsourcing spans.
No journal, margin, probability or branch weight follows from the risk prose.

If the bounded illustration adds no distinct reader bridge, retain the current
candidate and write a source-only result. If it changes the transition or
requires a new formal object, inspect a new candidate within the same six-page
render budget before freezing the integration.
