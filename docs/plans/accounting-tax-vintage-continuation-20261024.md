# Continue with explicit payment allocation by tax vintage

24 October2026. S2–S5;MC05/11/13/16/24/26/28/35;G2/G4.

## Question and skeptical audit

Can selected ordinary events and explicitly allocated payments continue on the
actual following-period assessed book while keeping each tax vintage and the
aggregate payable consistent? The old aggregate payment event cannot identify
whether old or new tax is settled once both liabilities exist. Do not infer FIFO,
proration or caller intent. Require positive explicit allocations with unique
vintage ids and a sum equal to the payment amount.

Comparator: exact first-assessment result,two-vintage ledger and complete carried
opening. Recompute prior evidence;retain the selected assessment endpoint. Ordinary
events must be on/after that exclusive endpoint to avoid silently changing an
assessed interval;payments may occur at the assessment's recorded day. All events
must be chronological and inside the financial period. Financial states remain
the full26-account schema. Preserve deferred balances,loss ledger,prior findings
and original request history.

## Evidence contract

For each allocated payment,require known affirmative authority,positive rational
amounts,unique existing vintage ids,each amount no greater than its outstanding
balance and total no greater than actual shared cash. Debit payable and credit
cash by the total;append dated settlements with event/operation identity to each
allocated vintage. No current or deferred expense changes. Prior settlements and
zero-balance records remain. Aggregate payable equals sum of vintage balances
after every ordinary or payment operation. Native kernels must not change tax
accounts. No balancing plug or restoration of spent cash.

Primary criteria: exact predecessor,state/schema,per-vintage conservation,full
payment/flow linkage,shared cash,journal balance,ordered successful prefix and
four statement ties from the actual carried opening. Unknown authority,funding
failure,overpayment,duplicate ids,scope/timing mismatch and tampered results must
refuse only the corresponding operation while retaining the completed prefix.
Pure apply compares the entire result,ledger and predecessor. Counts/smokes are
explanatory. No statutory or annual promotion,no forecast ranking.

Use an explicit partial first assessment in a longer financial interval for
ordinary-continuation cases;an assessment at the financial endpoint permits no
later ordinary event. Record that timing assumption,not a fictitious calendar.
After tests,record/integrate/refresh coverage. Next audit cumulative reassessment
and new-period deferred recognition using the actual continued ledger before
repeated carry. Existing annual/source gaps remain promotion vetoes.
