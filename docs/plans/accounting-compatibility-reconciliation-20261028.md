## Evidence contract and assumptions

- Primary criterion: each of the 18 identified profiles has exact native owner,
  signature, inputs, clocks/context, effect ownership, outcomes/refusals,
  successor restrictions and source identity; the delivered edges are named
  correctly. Complete selected-term results reproduce, and the selected-reading
  composition retains every observation, relation, context and obligation.
- Vetoes: changed locked bytes, wrong profile ownership, input mutation, lost
  obligations, changed full term result or a promoted invalid transfer.
- Explanatory only: row/check counts, wall time and passing old fixtures.
- No conclusion of complete DSL compatibility, actual issuer opening,
  accounting compliance, production readiness, forecasting or independent
  implementation acceptance. No stochastic comparison is performed.
- The historical native error conventions are a reviewed constraint for this
  task; do not normalize exceptions and returned refusals into one success bit.
- Existing deterministic fixtures are regression baselines, not a completeness
  sample. Source/history independent Fraction checks are retained and rerun on
  the new witnesses. Exact solver witnesses may be nonunique; compare declared
  constraints and obligations rather than demand arbitrary witness equality.
