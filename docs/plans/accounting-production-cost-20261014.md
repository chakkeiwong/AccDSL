## Evidence contract and defaults

Mechanism: actual materials/labor/variable/fixed overhead → admitted normal
production cost → units sold and unsold → profit/balance/cash/equity. Use one
simple product, no WIP, uniform unit cost and explicit normal/actual output.
That is a teaching hypothesis, not a promoted costing default. Normal capacity
must have an evidence identity; unknown capacity must not be inferred from the
desired margin. Actual output may approximate normal capacity only under a
specified finding. Above-normal output may require lower allocation per unit.

Primary criterion: exact cost conservation, source-supported allocation and
separate abnormal/unallocated expenses; positive low/normal/high-volume cases
and adverse unknown/incorrect allocation, all linked to statements. Exact
independent recomputation checks numerical claims. Source hashes and PDF
counts are integrity diagnostics. No actual manufacturer facts, current-law
compliance, full company implementation or calibrated forecast is concluded.

Missing normal capacity, yield/cost purpose, revenue admission or necessary tax
facts veto the dependent claim and trigger targeted repair. They do not stop
an expressly pretax or otherwise fully admitted conditional case. Source
corruption/contradiction, conflicting writer or permission boundary stops only
dependent work. No training, benchmark, new runtime or installation is authorized.
