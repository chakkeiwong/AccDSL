# Assess current tax after the provisional period boundary

24 October2026. S2–S5;MC05/11/13/16/24/26/28/35;G2/G4.

## Skeptical audit and evidence contract

Question: can the actual following-period book receive its first conditional
current-tax assessment without using payments of carried tax as new-period
credits? The frozen cumulative reassessment computes paid tax as current expense
minus payable; that identity is wrong after carry resets expense but preserves
an opening liability. Use a distinct first-assessment profile and explicit tax
vintage ledger. Do not modify frozen predecessors or project out unpaid amounts.

Comparator: exact assessed carry result and its carried opening. Before first
assessment,current expense must be zero,each completed tax payment must settle
the carried vintage,and remaining aggregate payable must equal opening payable
less those dated payments. Record the prior assessment interval and origin hash.
New conditional tax C posts expense C and increases aggregate payable by C;cash
and prior-vintage settlements remain unchanged. Initial support excludes tax
prepayments,refunds,credits,scope changes and a second assessment of the same
new-period state; later settlement/reassessment must bind vintage allocations.

Use new-period income and adjustments only. Bind complete known/unknown treatment
and tax-base declarations;all native adjustments are allocated exactly once.
Carry loss inventory from its actual opening cursor,allow only explicit use/
expiry/generation under the existing selected loss rule,and retain full prior
history. Current/deferred information and recognition are separate: copied
deferred balances and prior recovery capacity do not settle current-period DTA/DTL.

Primary checks: complete predecessor and opening;exact interval/context/identity;
prior-payment/payable identity;explicit two-vintage accounting;complete loss
conservation and native allocations;balanced journal and four statement ties;
unchanged cash,deferred balances and components;pure complete-result apply.
Known unknowns remain visible;no annual or accounting admission. Invalid input
or financial mismatch vetoes the candidate and triggers repair. Counts and clean
builds are explanatory,not completeness criteria. No forecast or ranking claim.
