## Defaults and limits

Exact rational inputs and deterministic illustrative dates: convenience choices
for reconstructible arithmetic; adverse timing and eligibility checks expose
their limits. Zero transaction fees and an explicitly pretax scope avoid silently
inventing jurisdictional tax or fee rules. Historical authority is a scoped
source, not current compliance. Market quotes and paired fair-value changes
are supplied conditions; source/valuation-admission failure yields unknown
measurement, never zero. Constant classification over a case is conditional on
unchanged relevant facts; unknown actual terms block actual application.
