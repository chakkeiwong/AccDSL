# R03-E — Finite corporate methods, closing and authority decisions

15 October2026. MC02/05–07/11/13–22/24–30/32–35; G1–4/G6–10/G13–18.
Baseline: verified2.99, current P17-04 matrix and the protected corporate,
customer-contract, asset, production, tax and closing methods.
Scope is source research, documentary design, exact illustrative arithmetic
and manuscript integration; no product execution or empirical campaign.

## Intent, baseline and skeptical audit

Question: which named corporate operations still require a generic method,
and which require company-specific facts or current operative authority?
Compare against the original promised operations and inspected methods,
not a count of examples. A small completed-batch case cannot stand for WIP;
a service warranty does not measure an assurance obligation; a provision
unwind does not specify restoration-cost changes. Conversely, missing actual
rates, employee facts or contract values do not erase a delivered conditional
generic method. None may be relabeled optional simply to obtain readiness.

Primary criterion: each residual has source, input/admission, state transition,
report effect, failure behavior and a positive/adverse case, or an exact
remaining-authority/source dependency with a distinct supported next lead.
Wrong basis/version, invented probability/rate, incorrect cost floor,
unallocated mixed promise, income/cash double count or unknown tax treated as
zero vetoes the affected example and triggers repair. Successful arithmetic
and build counts do not establish completeness, actual applicability or
empirical validity. An absent required authority blocks that jurisdictional
application; continue supported mechanisms and other units.

The audit rejects importing EU IAS37 into an AU warranty profile without
explicit source admission, treating WIP percentages as measured facts, using
relative sales values for all joint products by default, and carrying
restoration-liability decreases below zero asset cost. It also rejects
inventing new tax cases when the actual gap is missing law or origin evidence.

## Finite sequence

1. E1 restoration: inspect retained IFRIC1/IAS16/IAS37/IAS36. Specify cost-model
   increases/decreases, asset floor and excess gain, impairment trigger,
   revaluation-origin split, remaining-life depreciation, end-of-life changes
   and noncapitalised discount unwind. Use exact amounts with separately
   supplied admissible estimates and a full conditional cash/equity bridge.
2. E2 customer obligations: inspect operative historical AASB137 if available
   from the official authority; otherwise retain the AU block and separately
   label supported historical EU cases. Complete assurance measurement and
   mixed21(c) modification using allocated distinct/partially performed
   components; preserve existing separate-contract, prospective and catch-up
   cases. No invented universal progress measure.
3. E3 production/payables: supply finite WIP equivalent-unit and joint-product
   allocation methods under IAS2/AASB102 with explicit rational/consistent
   allocation assumptions and normal-capacity policy. Connect unfinished,
   completed, unsold and sold amounts and financing in deferred supplier terms.
4. E4 tax/closing: reconcile the delivered current/deferred, OCI/equity-origin,
   error/estimate and pretax boundaries. Specify any genuinely absent generic
   transition; retain actual tax-law/recoverability/uncertain-tax dependencies.
   Close finite combined statements without zeroing uncomputed tax.
5. E5 authority and residual disposition: record each named corporate
   operation as delivered finite method, required design still missing,
   specific missing actual fact, operative source block or justified optional
   extension. Preserve US ASC access and ASBE applicability limits. Then
   reconcile A–E residuals before moving R04/R05/R06 forward.

## Assumptions and execution controls

Exact rational amounts, small finite outcome laws and supplied legal/valuation
findings are teaching hypotheses. Each case records why its simplification
isolates the mechanism and the first diagnostic for misuse. A published
probability example does not calibrate a company's warranties. A selected
equivalent-unit convention is an explicit costing policy, not a rule inferred
from the standard. Historical dates/sources do not establish current reporting
compliance. Default tax0 is forbidden: use an explicitly pretax result or
separately supported tax assumptions.

Evidence directory: data/accounting-master/finite-method-decisions-20261015/corporate/.
Before substantial generation, retain source passages and a finite case
inventory; independently calculate amounts and rejected targets. Freeze2.99
and add only necessary explanatory sections to a successor. Inspect rendered
pages and retain all prior equations, sources and original cases. After each
meaningful result refresh the ledger and master, then continue the ready
queue. Missing human feedback blocks acceptance, not provisional work.

Reader: a financial analyst or implementer who knows double-entry accounting.
Explain why the existing method cannot handle the new condition, show the
smallest missing calculation, then carry it through journals and statements.
Keep source/applicability and raw checks in companions; retain substantive
qualifications in the manuscript.
