# Carry the actual deferred-assessed book provisionally

24 October 2026. S2–S5; MC05/11/13/16/24/26/28/35; G2/G4.

## Skeptical audit and question

Can a distinct provisional caller carry the actual deferred-assessed book and
its recognition schedule into following-period operations without recomputing
the older current-tax-only opening? The frozen carry request accepts a current
reassessment; passing that old request would silently lose the new DTA/DTL
movement and its effect on profit. That is wrong relative to the intended target.
Use a new explicit profile with full deferred request/result binding; preserve
both predecessor implementations and evidence.

Comparator: exact conditional deferred closing book/report, original carry
algebra and independent rational calculations. Transfer actual recorded profit,
clear temporary accounts, preserve all other amounts and identities, and archive
the complete deferred assessment, original allocations, prior capacity and item
schedule. Historical recognition is not a new-period assessment or renewed
capacity. Copy recorded deferred balances into opening allocations only.

## Evidence contract

Primary criteria: exact full predecessor, carry journal and opening; unchanged
cash, tax payable, components, loss quantities and histories; no duplicate old
flows/adjustments; complete recognition archive and unknown/false findings;
following-period selected return/lease/payment events; four statement ties and
failed-prefix preservation. Strict annual mode remains unsupported.

The baseline mixed deferred book has profit -33/4, retained -12, DTA 8 and
DTL 10. Carry must produce retained -15/4, preserve cash 721/2 and payable
19/4, and start profit at zero. Paying the old liability does not repeat current
or deferred expense; subsequent depreciation is current-period activity.
Check empty, paid/unpaid, zero/decreased/increased deferred balances, loss
recognition, false/unknown findings, funding, timing, identities and tampering.

Model choice and retained algebra are explicit engineering assumptions, not an
accounting default. Missing annual/source evidence vetoes promotion, not this
conditional step. Broken state/history/cash ties veto the implementation and
trigger repair before recording. Counts/builds are explanatory. No forecast
claim or stochastic ranking. Record evidence, integrate in a protected successor,
refresh full coverage, then audit new-period assessment and repeated carry.
