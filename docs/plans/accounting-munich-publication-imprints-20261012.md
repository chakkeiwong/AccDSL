MC01/03/05/28/30/33–35 and G13.

The exact 2023 annual report's imprint (PDF 330, printed 328) states
Online publication date: 14 March 2024. This new report-specific source lead
is preserved in the Version 2.50 integration evidence. Question: which retained
Munich Re annual/interim reports from 2018–2025 contain an explicit publication
statement, and what date does each exact report assert?

Use the selected records in data/financial-report-benchmark/source-evidence.json
and their retained original PDFs. Search the existing extracted text for
publication/imprint markers, identify candidate pages, then extract/render
at most two PDF pages per call. Confirm issuer/report identity and read the
complete date context. Keep date labels, report periods, authorisation,
copyright, future financial calendars and extraction ambiguity distinct.
Do not infer publication solely from a filename, neighbouring archive group
or another year's report. If a report has no inspected explicit date, mark
that as unresolved within this search, not proof that no date exists.

Primary pass: one source-bound row per selected Munich Re report, with explicit
declared date, exact page/quote and interpretation, or a precise unresolved
disposition. Source identity mismatch, illegible digits, wrong temporal label
or a neighbouring report's date vetoes the row. A PDF's own publication statement
supports an attributed issuer-declared date; independent historical availability
of unchanged bytes requires separate evidence. Preserve the frozen corpus

release hashes or claiming a false controller run.
