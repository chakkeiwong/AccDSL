## Skeptical audit and research intent

Initial full-note inspection finds three risks in a convenient calculation:
13.2 minus 12.8 plus 3.7 gives 4.1 billion, but 3.7 is revenue from the opening
balance, not all releases during the quarter. The residual therefore cannot
identify gross new deferrals, billings or cash. Secondly, 64/25/9/2 percentages
are dated expected recognition of the then-existing total, not probabilities
or a complete revenue forecast. Thirdly, the statement's current deferred
revenue is reported in millions while the note's total is rounded to tenths
of a billion; direct equality with 64% would mix quantities and precision.

The mechanism under test is whether different explicitly authored movements
can preserve the reported totals. A successful pair of witnesses establishes
non-identification within the stated aggregate model; it does not establish
actual Apple histories or all feasible contract histories. A failed witness
triggers algebra/source repair, not abandonment of the wider reconstruction
question. Stop dependent work if source context or identity is invalid.

The audited plan passes after replacing the tempting cash-inference shortcut
with a residual identity and conditional witnesses. It uses the actual V2.6
baseline, retains the original package obligations, bounds the coverage claim
to named passages and tests the misleading-result risks before document work.

## Reader and pre-mortem

The reader should distinguish recognized revenue, new deferrals, opening-cohort
releases, total/current liabilities and collections. A short identity followed
by two different compatible movements should make the missing information
concrete. Explain the symbols and source precision before calculation; state
the effect on statements and future queries afterwards. Do not bury this in
an operational checklist inside the manuscript.

A successful calculation could mislead by treating a rounded centre as exact,
all deferred revenue as the same service cohort, a percentage bucket as a cash
due date, or a tax-note row labelled deferred revenue as a liability balance.
Original table headings, source-specific claims, scope-limited witnesses and
separate cash/recognition examples are the earliest checks. Reader review is
provisional author evidence and does not certify human comprehension.
