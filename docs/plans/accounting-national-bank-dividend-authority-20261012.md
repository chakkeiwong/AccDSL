# National-bank dividend authority after the issuer example

12 October 2026. E07/C05; MC02–05/15/16/19/21/25/28/30/32–35,
G4/G7/G8/G13/G18. The checked JPMorgan Note 26 example reports capacity
without prior approval but does not itself establish operative law. Inspect
primary national-bank dividend provisions to specify the required conditions
and factual inputs without calculating the bank's permitted dividend.

Target/comparator: the issuer's exact Note 26/Item 1 statements versus dated
official text of 12 USC 56/60 and the applicable OCC dividend provision
(candidate locator 12 CFR 5.64, to be verified before use). Prefer official
House US Code and eCFR sources. If a live route is unavailable, inspect a
clearly dated official GovInfo annual edition as historical evidence. Do not
infer 1 January 2026 applicability from a later current page or claim a complete
amendment chain merely because a page loads.

Evidence contract: preserve each URL, final response status, raw body/header,
retrieval time and hash. Verify section identity, edition/as-of information,
operative text, exceptions, definitions and relevant cross-references. The
pass criterion is a bounded authority-to-condition/input map whose temporal
scope is explicit. Wrong section, uninspected dependencies, stale edition,
challenge/HTML error or source identity mismatch veto the affected legal claim.
Transport success, third-party snippets and issuer summaries are explanatory
only. No current-law default, legal compliance, institution-specific clearance
or allowed-dividend amount follows from documentary success.

Skeptical/default audit: the section locators are research leads, not verified
rules. Check headings before collecting claims. The issuer's reported capital,
net income and dividends need not equal regulatory definitions. A simple
current-year-plus-two-year arithmetic formula cannot stand in for all capital,
surplus, supervisory and reporting conditions. Preserve the capital-distribution
and affiliate-transfer questions as separate scopes. No rule is executable
until its applicability, dependencies and input definitions are established.

Bound retrieval to official public documents, with 25-second connection/total
timeouts and at most six distinct routes initially. A sandbox network failure
requires one trusted retry; repeated unchanged source failures do not justify a
loop. Follow a returned explicit official dependency when it supplies genuinely
new evidence. No login, access-control bypass, paid access or outreach.
Stop an unsupported authority claim at a real source/edition boundary; continue
independent preparation. Use CPU parsing only, no product runtime or install.
