entity classes, what the separately identified 2025 Ministry explanation says

having done. Then work the consequence for original versus restated historical

conditions and issuer disclosures, with exact source anchors and a checked

standard, issuer assertion and local deduction distinct. A successful download
or clean build is engineering evidence only. Source identity/content mismatch,
missing material qualifications, incompatible reporting contexts or failed
arithmetic veto the affected claim. They stop dependent integration until
repaired, not independent retained-source work. Render/layout diagnostics
trigger document repairs; counts and page length are explanatory only.

Passing does not establish every entity's adoption, the complete amendment

actual adoption and the economic date of a restated comparison answer different
