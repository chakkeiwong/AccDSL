from closed dated facts, legal obligations, measurements, journals and reports?

award/participation methods, with FinancingComponents/1 as a compatibility

Primary pass criterion: each admitted finite method has closed inputs and

Wrong source/book/date, rate enclosure, fee scope, threshold, share count,
valuation allocation, award condition, EPS weighting, journal, report or atomic
publication veto the affected example and trigger repair. Missing facts return
unresolved; unsupported mathematics returns a method refusal. Corrupted
protected inputs/index or an unclear derivation is a continuation veto for the
dependent unit. A failed candidate alone is not a continuation veto. Schema,

Artifacts: new `extended-method-exchanges-20261015/financing-v2/`, numbered

The earlier plan survives only with these explicit repairs. Use the original
five promises rather than /1 method counts; keep contractual cash and legal
unpaid claims distinct from recognition; do not reuse a corporate equity
bridge that excludes owner repurchases. Source-rounded bond PVs are not exact
unrounded amounts. An EIR bracket proves an enclosure, not a rational rate or
permission to accrue at its midpoint. Tax remains uncomputed. A source-supported
finite reform decision requires cause, economic equivalence, additional-change
and designation findings; equal coupons alone are insufficient. Historical EU
and AU sources do not establish October 2026 law, US GAAP or ASBE admission.

Reuse of /1 transport is conditional on checking every modified schema,
financial invariant, operation signature and report. The builder must not
import the independent calculator. Extend protection once from completed P4b;
never rebuild predecessors or recreate an existing lock. These conditions
address stale context, weak baselines, hidden defaults, misleading proxy checks,
environment mismatch and missing stop conditions. Audit passes for the bounded
documentary work under these conditions, not for wholesale financing closure.
