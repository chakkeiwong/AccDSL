## Question and governing baseline

Can the proposed representation account for a real annual/interim disclosure
package, explain the consequences of its material statements and unresolved
references, and supply an implementable, source-supported example? The initial
test is the existing Apple FY2024/FY2025-Q1 case. It is a development case, not a
representative sample or an evaluation holdout. All MC01–MC35 and G1–G18 retain
their original acceptance wording. E01, E05, E07, E08 and E14 are open workstreams,
not five automatic phase acceptances.

The baseline is P02 case.json (23 notes, 70 exhibit rows, 125 observations and
34 selected reconciliations), P16/P17 contracts, the exact P19 readiness.json
negative assessment, and the separately corrected 125-page version 2.1
manuscript. The 123-page P18 manuscript and original corpus remain historical
evidence. The ASBE correction is already integrated and will not be redone.

## Research intent and evidence contract

The mechanism under examination is explicit source/reference/qualification
propagation into a proposed company representation. Expected failures include
incomplete containers, unidentified series-level holdings and ambiguous
cross-references. They trigger source repair or explicit unknowns, not an
abandonment of the mission.

Primary pass criterion for this work unit: an independently reconstructible
reference/disposition supplement and real dependency query, using exact retained
sources and checked arithmetic, with every known input edge accounted for.
E01 promotion additionally requires the original package/narrative/case
acceptance; completing this unit does not automatically close E01.

| Diagnostic | Role and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Exact source identity, quotations and units | Promotion veto; a mismatch triggers repair. Corrupt source/baseline blocks only dependent work until resolved |
| Missing material dependency or silently dropped qualification | Promotion veto and repair trigger; do not infer a complete answer |
| Inaccessible public source after bounded legitimate routes | Source-branch stop and disclosure, not a veto on independent internal work |
| False baseline/preservation mismatch, mixed entity/period, arithmetic error | Continuation veto for affected derivation; repair before continuing it |
| Download/anchor/check counts, note count, page count | Explanatory diagnostics only; no semantic accuracy or readiness claim |
| Known unidentifiable histories/holdings | Explanatory result and output restriction; not an automatic continuation veto |
| Human review missing | Acceptance limitation; continued internal preparation remains authorised |

Do not conclude accounting compliance, forecast validity, exhaustive history
identification, historical byte availability, complete literature coverage,
runtime compatibility, or positive proposal readiness from this unit.

Pre-mortem: a successful downloader could still fetch an empty or wrong exhibit;
a fully populated spreadsheet could still omit qualifications; a balanced
repayment journal could still misstate holdings, accounting measurement or tax.
Identity/content inspection, a note-wide disposition pass and explicit
conditional calculations are the smallest discriminating checks. Post-run
notes must identify the strongest alternative explanation, remaining internal
work, and the next justified action separately from acceptance status.
