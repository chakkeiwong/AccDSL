## Skeptical audit and question

Can an inspection of the existing three-year operation path preserve claim
creation, named settlement allocations, lifetime memory and refusal reasons
that aggregate financial amounts omit? The baseline is the unchanged
CompanyOperationPathDraft/1 executor and its complete authored example, not a
new accounting or policy engine. Native affine dependency inspection remains a
separate numeric result. REA's primary-source reading motivates relationship
coverage; it does not prove this implementation correct.

The audit found that operation identifiers repeat across years, claim identity
persists across years, and annual run claim counters reset to opening balances.
Use year-qualified operation identifiers and compare lifetime claims with the
existing closing-memory adapter. Opening claim records contain only id, ledger
and amount. Neither counterparties nor legal instrument/authority facts can be
recovered from those fields. Zero allocations still read a named claim and must
retain that endpoint. Do not mistake a successful relation trace for complete
economic-state construction or actor knowledge.

Audit verdict: proceed with a read-only native adapter and fresh output directory.
No frozen writer, manuscript builder, new dependency or empirical run is needed.

## Intent and evidence contract

- Question: which claim relationships and dependencies are actually supplied
  and used by the retained operation path?
- Mechanism: inspect native applied operations; add typed event/claim endpoints,
  creation and settlement edges, prefix claim memory and unresolved identities.
- Expected failure: equal aggregate balances conceal different claim allocations;
  dropping a zero allocation can erase a guard; annual counters can lose history.
- Primary criterion: every executed trade event has its exact named endpoints,
  allocations and lifetime amounts; independent rational arithmetic agrees with
  all native journals and complete/partial claim memories.
- Promotion veto: missing or fabricated endpoint, wrong amount, collapsed year,
  lost refusal, changed native input, or unsupported identity represented as known.
- Continuation veto: corrupted input or invalid native binding stops the affected
  unit until repair. A failed case triggers repair and a new attempt directory.
- Explanatory diagnostics: counts of events, claims, relations and checks.
- Repair trigger: comparator disagreement or an adverse case accepted contrary to
  the declared native scope.
- Not concluded: general state sufficiency, exhaustive dependencies, source or
  actor admission, legal enforceability, accounting compliance, prediction or
  empirical company behavior. No state reduction or probability is introduced.
- Preserved result: data/accounting-master/company-state-relationship-dependencies-20261028/.
