## Intent and evidence contract

Question: can an independent implementer reconstruct the promised bank
transitions, keeping credit history, legal claims, actual cash and accounting
measurement distinct? The baseline is all seven P17-06 rows in the protected
46-row coverage register, together with bank-methods-v1, R03-C/I/N and the
prudential connection. Existing corporate and financing profiles are transport
comparators, not a smaller denominator for bank completion.

Primary criterion: each declared finite method has closed inputs/state, exact
source and context binding, independently derived financial/state effects,
complete authorise/propose/commit/report exchanges, and meaningful adverse and
replay paths. Integrity counts explain coverage but cannot promote a method.
Wrong financial target, context, source/date, lost credit history, duplicated
exposure, missing receipt chain or partial publication veto the affected
exchange and trigger repair. Missing actual authority/calibration blocks that
claim, not independent documentary methods. Corruption of protected inputs,
predecessors, HEAD or the staged index is a continuation veto for this unit.
No full-bank, actual-law, credit-model, runtime or whole-company acceptance
follows. Preserve results under extended-method-exchanges-20261015/bank/.

Expected failure modes are balanced but wrong entries: internal loan credit
treated as cash; Stage 1 reset on drawing; undrawn probability retained after
observed full draw; credit cure inferred from modification; post-maturity
contractual interest invented; write-off treated as waiver; requested funding
treated as received; unpaid deposits recognised twice; fees recognised solely
because cash arrived. Each needs a discriminating adverse case. A failed
candidate calls for its planned repair, not abandonment of the bank direction.

## Skeptical audit before implementation

The inherited financing reports are unsuitable: they split a signed derivative
and omit loan allowances, commitment provisions and deposit liabilities. Replace
their financial assumptions, component checks and interest reporting. Reuse
only canonical bytes, immutable references, source binding, owner/world,
receipt-chain and exact-replay conventions after inspection. The independent
verifier must not import authored expected states or the packet builder.

The previous resume summary misstated the rational fee example: the inspected
method uses terminal107.8 on carrying98, hence exactly10%; terminal121 would
require a different rate. Use the actual retained example. For impaired loans,
same terminal equity does not validate interest/impairment classification.
Keep original and current risk identities, expected recovery dates/costs and
actual settlement evidence. A timeout alone never establishes zero receipt.
Do not apply the US capital illustration to an Australian accounting book.

Audit passes for the bounded first increment under those repairs. Calendar
grids, source windows and synthetic measurements remain explicit restrictions;
unknown facts produce obligations instead of convenient zeroes. Before each
later increment, inspect its particular methods and refresh this audit.
