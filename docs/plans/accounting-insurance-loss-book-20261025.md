hash-bound compiler from the retained native loss_schedule result to a finite

The plan passes because native output already contains exact rational journals
and origins; no endpoint selection or actuarial model is added. The loss component
is a subset of liability, carried only in memory. Native periods end on exact
supplied dates; use a declared host interval containing them rather than shifting

retained complete schedules; broader native inputs require separate evidence.

Primary promotion criterion: every generated signed journal, cash origin,

and independent case arithmetic on two charts; unrelated balances/memory remain.
Promotion vetoes: dependency/source mismatch, forged/rehashed native result,
wrong owner/currency/units/opening, aliasing roles, missing or extra mapping,
date movement, cash duplication, mistaken extra liability, partial foreign writes
or unhandled information findings. These validity failures also veto continuation
of dependent runs until repaired. A candidate implementation failure triggers
its smallest repair; it does not reject the sector direction. Runtime/counts
are explanatory. Exact execution does not admit accounting policy or source.

## Defaults and assumptions

- Native historical source, fixed allocation and zero-CSM/no-estimate-change
  findings are retained hypotheses with explicit provenance, not new defaults.
  A new source claim is out of scope. Check native exact replay first.
- Debit-positive rational encodings follow the declared book; convert reduced
  native rational strings canonically with no rounding. Compare every posting.
- Host interval begins2025-01-01 and ends2027-01-02 for the retained dates.
  This is a fixture interval, not an annual accounting convention. Refuse dates
  outside the supplied interval; reports are cumulative, not fiscal-close claims.
- Separate accounts represent liability, finance expense, service revenue,
  loss-reversal income and claims expense. K remains memory. Injective mappings
  and independent equity checks expose duplicate or misclassified balances.
- Explicit progress memory prevents skipped/repeated stages. Preserve original
  host cash/capital and every complement. A full proposed trace remains
  provisional; no durable commit or historical execution is inferred.
