## Research question and skeptical audit

Can repeated additions of eligible derivatives preserve both the full carrying
amount and the cumulative gain since each designation? The existing
RepeatedItemRebalancing/1 starts with a zero-value instrument and adds only
item layers. Its equality between derivative value and cumulative hedge gain
must not become an implicit rule when a purchased instrument begins with a
nonzero value. B6.5.19 measures the added instrument's CHANGE from rebalancing;
IFRS9.5.1.1 recognizes its acquired fair value separately. Neither source says
that paying200 for an asset creates200 of profit or OCI.

Baseline: source-bound original100-unit item at price80, measured at90 with
original derivative value/gain1200, reserve1000 and earnings200. The new
profile begins from that internally reconstructed state and opening synthetic

on the same date at its supplied nonnegative fair value, with equal cash
consideration, no fees and no pre-existing book position. All instruments are
eligible external non-option derivatives designated in their entirety; current
eligibility/valuation and risk-management findings remain supplied. This avoids
inventing transfer from a pre-existing trading book, transaction-price/fair-value

Audit: use the cumulative lower-of test on signed hedge GAINS, not asset
carrying amounts or segment minima. Acquisition origins and current per-leg

quantity is removed. New fair values are explicitly supplied valuations, not
generated by a pricing model. Rebalancing requires a changed relationship with
unchanged objective and updated documentation; observed profit is not itself
authority to adjust the ratio. The audit passes within those constraints.
