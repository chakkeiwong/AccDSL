# Reconcile a compatible group's period cash flows

25 October2026. S2–S4;MC04/06/08/10–13/16/19–22/24–28/30/32–35.
Question: can the read-only compatible-book worksheet extend across a period
without counting internal cash transfers as group receipts/payments?

Skeptical audit before execution: a pair of balanced snapshots does not establish
period cash flow or opening/closing lineage. Reuse CompanyBookReport/1 for each
legal book and CompatibleBookComposition/1 for each boundary. Do not flatten
memory or execute the native conditional group. Require the same legal-book
set, chart, account maps, periods, supplied compatibility premises and group
perimeter at both boundaries. Refuse ownership/control changes in this first
period operation. Period opening must be at the declared start; carry and
current-period event identity follow the existing report validator. All equity
movement explanations and missing notes remain book-specific.

Exact baseline: independently supplied opening/closing legal reports and hand
cash arithmetic. Constructed example: P opening cash100, investment100,
equity−200; S cash100/equity−100. P pays S30 internally, and S receives20 from
an external customer, recognising income20. Closing P cash70; S cash150,
payable−30, income−20, capital−100; P receivable30. Same full-ownership premise
and investment/capital100 elimination apply at both snapshots; reciprocal30
is eliminated only at closing. Group opening cash200/equity200; closing cash220
and equity220, profit20. Removing the named internal payment/receipt pair
leaves external gross receipts20, payments0, net20. Both direct operating and
cross-activity transfer variants must work; activity labels alone must not
identify counterparts or cancel unrelated external flows.

Primary criterion: exact legal reports, opening/closing group worksheet totals,
remaining gross flows by activity and cash/equity reconciliations; inputs unchanged.
Vetoes: stale/duplicate/unmatched transfer references, different dates/currency,
same-book pairs, missing current-period flow events, changed period/perimeter,
cash-changing eliminations, nonzero unmatched cash/equity reconciliation or
unresolved component obligations. Every removed flow requires an explicit
supplied matching-transfer premise. Zero/partial cancellation unsupported.

Assumptions: constructed facts, same policy/units, stable full-ownership control,
no NCI/goodwill/FX/tax/intragroup profit. These are local scope choices, not
defaults or accounting findings. Different legal activity classifications may
be removed independently when paired amounts/dates/currency match; no netting
by totals alone. Recognised profit and group equity must agree; changes that
need profit/elimination policy remain unsupported rather than patched.

This is a conditional period summary, not complete consolidated financial
statements. Indirect cash flow, full notes, sources and broader method coverage
remain open. Tests/record/replay under group-period-report-20261025; no external
code or forecasting. Audit passes for the bounded construction. A local failure
triggers repair; missing external source access is not a continuation veto.
