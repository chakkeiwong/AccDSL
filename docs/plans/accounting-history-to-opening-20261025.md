# Connect conditional histories to declared company openings

25 October 2026. S2–S4; MC01/02/05/08–13/15/17/19/24–28/34/35.

Question: can an explicitly selected, independently replayed conditional history
provide an exact company opening while preserving its assumptions, unresolved
obligations and alternative-history identity? The recent typed-map lifecycle
handles new invoices but still requires an empty opening claim population.
Existing affine attachment produces conditional witnesses, not admitted history.

Audit first: inspect the typed-history replay, source-time/measurement binding,
company-book context, report-opening conditions and exact existing schema/type
contracts. Identify each transformation: accounts, signs, units, currency,
entity/perimeter, date, fiscal boundary and memory. Missing contract identifiers,
due dates or counterparties must remain unresolved rather than being invented
from aggregate balances. Compare a direct declared map with a replay-based
opening; prefer the smallest approach preserving the evidence semantics.

Baseline: frozen TypedHistoryMeasurementAttachment/1 replay and CompanyBook/1
validation/reporting. Exact acceptance: independent account/memory mapping,
source-witness recheck, context and date correspondence, preserved alternatives,
unknown/unsupported outcomes, no balancing plug, no silent zero, no reinterpretation
of an interval as a point fact. The first adapter may expose a provisional
opening with explicit exclusions; it must not imply complete source admission.

Research intent: test whether the incompatible state representations can be
connected without weakening either. Expected failures are missing fields,
dimension/context/time mismatch, temporary balances at an opening, and loss of
unresolved obligations. Those are repair triggers or candidate vetoes. A corrupt
required source, unavailable runtime, or irreducible material research decision
is a continuation veto. Proxy counts and balanced journals cannot establish
historical truth, complete histories or accounting compliance.

Stages: (1) inspect actual contract and fixture boundaries; (2) record explicit
mapping/default/evidence audit; (3) implement the smallest additive conditional
adapter with independent fixtures and refusal tests; (4) demonstrate a feasible
continuation or preserve its precise remaining contract-population barrier;
(5) integrate only checked behavior and refresh complete 53/6/46 coverage.

No statistical ranking or forecast study is involved. S1–S5 remain partial,
S6 held and S7 deferred. Preserve all frozen evidence and unrelated work.
