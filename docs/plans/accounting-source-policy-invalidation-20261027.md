# Separate source rebinding from numerical policy invalidation

27 October2026. MC01/02/03/11/12/13/24/25/26/28/33/34;
W01/02/03/04/11/12/16. Predecessor: certified-source-views-20261026,

Question: which financial, observation and policy computations can be reused
when a source revision, interpretation, actor or time cutoff changes? A whole
view hash change establishes invalidation but does not prove minimal recomputation.
Write a small dependency layer over the accepted adapter, not a new selector.

Nodes: externally locked financial/observation evidence; selected financial
scenario; observation map; numerical policy set; selected financial-source and
observation-source bindings; final actor policy binding. Define each node's
exact parents. Distinguish byte/source identity from semantic equality. A same-
meaning correction can preserve numerical results while requiring new source
bindings; a changed receipt/family invalidates dependent observation and policy
computations; a changed observation map preserves the financial relation.
A future revision irrelevant at the old cutoff preserves every selected node.

Primary pass criterion: independently declared change masks agree for all
source-view scenarios; reused complete policy sets equal freshly recomputed
sets. External expected content digests, unique row identities and exact
population bindings reject altered, duplicated or removed rows and forged
prior results. Do not accept self-consistent packet hashes as authority.
Vetoes: lost worlds, stale source/actor scope, unsupported semantic merging,
trusted comparator alteration or recomputation used to conceal wrong reuse.
Counts/time are explanatory, with no performance claim. No probabilities,
actual access/source/measurement admission or durable recovery is concluded.

Audit before running: preserve baseline files; compare exact full policies,
not only counts; separate reuse of numerical evidence from source binding;
use a cold recomputation as comparator; require trusted expected identities
outside the transported bundle. Assumptions are the same closed two-slot world
family and whole-second UTC convention; neither is promoted to a general default.
At most two120second CPU attempts with failures preserved. No GPU/installs/
workers/fees/outreach/publication. Artifacts: source-policy-invalidation-20261027/.
