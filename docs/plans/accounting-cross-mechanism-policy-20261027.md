Question: which single decision rules remain feasible when the observation
mechanism itself is uncertain? The completed family binder preserves separate
policy problems but does not answer this decision question. A naive intersection
of coarse/refined policy-table labels is not a mathematical definition.

Inspect the actual first/second observation functions and chronology. Represent
a common action set and the actor's observable message/history space explicitly.
Keep two different assumptions separate: (a) the mechanism identifier is observed,
and (b) it is not observed. Neither is an empirical default. A source-selection
mechanism can reveal its identity only through information actually supplied to
the actor; internal source hashes/proof labels are not free observations.

Construct joint worlds indexed by financial history and admissible temporal
mechanism. Specify every cross-mechanism observation equivalence class. Decisions
must agree whenever the actor's full available histories are identical. Preserve
reachable-history and own-action memory across both dates; include a coarsening
that creates an incompatibility missed by solving each mechanism separately.
Do not assign probabilities or drop an unresolved mechanism. An unresolved source
outcome without a defined observation map must return an unresolved policy question.

Primary criterion: complete policy sets from an independently authored finite
constraint model equal the proposed shared-observation solver; worked hand-derived
conflicts and feasible cases agree. Show why separate feasibility need not imply
joint feasibility. Include observed/unobserved mechanism, equal observation tokens
with different meanings, mechanism resolution between dates, and zero-policy
coarse/refined example. Preserve full row populations and nonanticipation classes.
No runtime/count ranking; no actual actor-knowledge or general optimality claim.

Before code, record a skeptical audit and evidence/default contract, including
wrong comparator, leaked internal mechanism tags, anticipation, artificial equality

memory. New token encodings are explicit design choices with counterexamples.
If the original profile cannot support a substantive counterexample, specify a
