## Evidence contract and research intent

The mechanism under inspection is identity-preserving joining of independently
retained evidence classes. The expected failure is an apparently complete date
column that silently assigns an alternate version's date or promotes exposed
families to a test set.

Primary completion criterion: exactly one row per active coverage record,
original order and selected path/hash retained, with separate frozen metadata,
filing events, attributed declarations, alternate-source date candidates and
known exposure records. Every joined source claim has an exact artifact and
JSON-pointer anchor; selected bytes agree with its asserted identity.
The final admission note must distinguish E1/E2/E3/E4/E5/E6 and B0–B3, specify
the missing task inputs and give a concrete next internal step.

Promotion vetoes: dropped/duplicated active record, superseded issuer included,
hash-mismatched join, invented timestamp/timezone, an excluded date promoted,
pilot answers called independent labels, an unpromoted role used as a split,
or unsupported exact-byte/as-of admission. Such defects require repair.
Corrupt protected source/evidence is a continuation veto for this work unit.
Missing eligibility inputs block their dependent run, not independent
preparation. Date/annotation counts are explanatory, never predictive evidence.
No whole requirement, accounting validity, empirical superiority, confirmed
test set, selected budget/default or historical-byte availability is concluded.
