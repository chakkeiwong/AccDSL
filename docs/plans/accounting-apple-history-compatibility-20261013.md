Research question: can the previously authored PP&E histories H1/H2 be
combined with one fixed remainder of Apple's company-wide history, and what
exact conditions must a valid repair meet? The source/annual/interim joins
are now available; a Cartesian product of local witnesses is not preapproved.

annual/interim statement records. Preserve the original journal assumptions,

Read the existing PP&E derivation and conditions before changing any claim.

Intent/evidence contract: calculate the exact H2–H1 differences from recorded
journals, including profit, liabilities, cash and accumulated balances. Test
the same-remainder hypothesis against every affected aggregate identity. A
nonzero whole-company discrepancy is a hard veto of that combination, not a
rejection of both local histories or of historical-family reconstruction.
Derive the branch-dependent remainder conditions needed to restore the same
observed statement totals. These conditions are explanatory/repair requirements,
not evidence that such a remainder exists. Inspect the source/note constraints
that would discriminate between repairs. Preserve the missing mapping of
capital payables, expense classification, taxes and dates.

Promotion criterion: the compatibility verdict and necessary repair equations
follow from the exact prior journals and stated same-remainder assumption;
all quantities retain their reported/authored/derived classifications. Veto:
invented actual transactions, a plug called a history, unexamined reuse of
rounded inputs as exact facts, or necessary conditions presented as a sufficient
full-history construction. No probabilistic ranking or empirical forecast.

Skeptical audit: identical PP&E endpoints and capex cash do not imply identical
whole-company state. H1/H2 are explicitly conditional subsystems; their failure
with one fixed remainder is expected and motivates coupling. Do not relabel a
source limit as a continuation veto. A financial statement amount residual
cannot identify its offsetting transaction. The audit passes for this bounded
compatibility inquiry, with existence and whole-path claims excluded.

Default/assumption audit: the fixed-remainder hypothesis is a diagnostic
baseline, not an economic default. H1/H2 inputs and journal timing are inherited
conditional hypotheses, not Apple facts. At the next step, future settlement
or operations require their own explicit conditional terms; do not silently
carry an annual capital payable into the next quarter as an observed amount.
