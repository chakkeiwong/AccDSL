# Reassess following-period current tax after vintage continuation

24 October 2026. S2–S5; MC05/11/13/16/24/26/28/35; G2/G4.

## Question and skeptical audit

Can the same new-period cumulative assessment be replaced after selected ordinary
events and explicitly allocated payments, without consuming losses twice or
crediting payments of carried tax against new expense? The exact comparator is
the complete tax-vintage continuation result and original carried opening.
The older reassessment's expense-minus-total-payments identity is wrong after
carry. Do not reuse it without a successor that separates both vintages.

## Evidence contract and assumptions

Recompute the entire prior request. Require a complete successful prefix. Bind
full information, allocations, same jurisdiction/regime, current-year starting
point and a nondecreasing exclusive endpoint covering every recorded event.
Recalculate cumulative income and native adjustments exactly once. Recompute
loss use/expiry/generation from the original carried loss opening, preserving
historical prior-year records and identifying the replaced provisional branch.

Let C0 be recorded new-period expense, C1 the replacement cumulative amount,
Snew actual settlements assigned to its current vintage, and O remaining carried
payable. Journal only C1-C0; current vintage liability becomes C1-Snew and total
payable O+C1-Snew. Prior carried settlements and cash remain exact. The existing
positive-payable model cannot represent a tax receivable: if Snew>C1, refuse as
unsupported and preserve the predecessor for a separately scoped credit/refund
model; never clamp or silently erase a credit. This is a declared representation
limit, not a legal claim. Retain supersession history and all payments; do not
change or duplicate settlement identities when replacing assessment provenance.

Primary criteria: independent cumulative/incremental arithmetic, exact loss
replacement, vintage/aggregate conservation, full-account preservation, cash
unchanged, four actual-opening statement ties and complete apply. Broken source
binding, missing declarations, tax-scope/timing errors or incorrect conservation
veto the proposed update and trigger repair. Invalid fixtures veto their evidence;
rejected hypothetical candidates do not reject the research direction. Counts
and runtime are explanatory. No legal, forecast or annual-readiness inference.
