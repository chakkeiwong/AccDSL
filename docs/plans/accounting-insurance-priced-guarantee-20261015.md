## Intent, evidence contract and skeptical audit

Question: can the specified insurance group price its financial guarantee,
distinguish that price from a physical forecast and nonfinancial risk adjustment,
and carry the resulting components through service, losses and settlement?
A second interaction tests acquisition-cost transfer into an onerous issued
group and the separate, timing-dependent reinsurance loss recovery.

Comparator: the retained terminal-payoff illustration and connected GMM/treaty
cases, not an alleged calibrated insurer model. Primary pass is a reconstructible
valuation/transition method with an independent replication calculation, complete
conditional statement consequences and adverse cases. Wrong measure, missing
eligibility, doubled acquisition asset, premature treaty recovery, omitted
investment-component exclusion or inconsistent group components vetoes the
affected case and triggers repair. Builds and check counts are integrity
diagnostics. No stochastic ranking, current compliance, actual calibration,
general VFA engine or empirical forecast validation follows.

The skeptical audit rejects a supplied option price as completion of valuation.
It also rejects deriving VFA eligibility from the pricing weights, inferring
nonfinancial risk adjustment from the guarantee price, applying VFA to held
reinsurance, or retaining acquisition assets after they enter group measurement.
Timing matters: a pre-recognition acquisition asset needs recoverability evidence;
the adverse claim estimate arrives at recognition in the example, not during an
unrecorded earlier onerous interval. Financial repricing occurs before any
coverage unit is supplied in the discrete example. Actual elapsed service would
require its own dated allocation. These repairs make the finite plan executable.
