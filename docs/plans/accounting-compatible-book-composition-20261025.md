# Combine compatible books through an explicit elimination worksheet

25 October2026. S2–S4; MC04/06/08/10–13/16/19–22/24–28/30/32–35.

Question: can two separately valid CompanyBook/1 snapshots produce an exact
common-chart worksheet while preserving their legal books and making every
elimination traceable? This advances the already authorised group/sector plan.
Native group selectors remain referenced evidence, not executable books.

Skeptical audit: the native group retains shared state, so copying its member
balances into independent books would lose facts. Use separately constructed
valid synthetic books instead. Reuse the existing book validator, exact signed
arithmetic and CompanyWorld envelope. Require the same case, currency, period
and snapshot date, a complete explicit common-chart map and a supplied
full-ownership control premise. Classification equality is necessary but
does not establish equivalent accounting policies; supplied compatibility
assumptions stay explicit. Do not infer control from ownership or call the
worksheet a complete consolidation.

Baseline: unchanged legal books plus hand-computed signed account totals.
Primary pass criterion: raw totals, separately displayed elimination deltas
and resulting balances equal the independent expected values on two charts;
all original books, memories, flows and assumptions remain unchanged.
Refuse mismatched context, unresolved components/obligations, stale hashes,
missing maps, classification changes, reused source accounts, unmatched or
partially specified paired eliminations and unsupported sectors. Each paired
elimination reverses exact opposite source balances in distinct books, with
its own identifier and supplied premise. Only receivable/payable and
investment/equity pair roles are supported. Their roles are declared, not
inferred from labels. Zero or partial balance elimination is outside this
first contract; no automatic balancing entry is permitted.

Worked case: P holds cash80, investment100, receivable20 and equity−200.
S holds cash70, equipment50, payable−20 and capital−100. Both balance.
Raw combined assets320, liabilities20, equity300. Two supplied full-balance
eliminations remove investment/capital100 and reciprocal20. Result: cash150,
equipment50, assets200, liabilities0, equity200. This is exact constructed
arithmetic, not evidence that these assumptions describe an actual group.

Bounds are engineering convenience choices inherited from current validators;
they do not establish population completeness. Balanced totals are a veto
check, not accounting admission. Noncontrolling interests, goodwill, purchase
accounting, tax, FX, intragroup profit, period flows and sector postings remain
unsupported. No uncertainty ranking, forecasting or financial advice follows.

Implement the read-only worksheet, focused refusal/immutability tests and
two exact cases; run directly relevant regressions; record and replay under
data/accounting-master/compatible-book-composition-20261025/. Preserve source
locks and update the concise checkpoint. A failing fixture or adapter triggers
repair, not abandonment. Stop only for an unresolved semantic contradiction
or missing inputs needed by this bounded contract. Audit passes for that scope.
