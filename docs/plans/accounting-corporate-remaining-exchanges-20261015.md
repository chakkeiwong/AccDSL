## Intent, comparator and pre-run audit

Question: can the remaining promised asset, benefit, tax and customer operations
be reconstructed from closed types, exact source-bound inputs and full exchange
paths? Compare the original 46-row audit and existing P17/R03-E/F/G/L methods
and figures, retaining every still unresolved operation. Counts are integrity
diagnostics; they are not the primary completion criterion.

The initial audit rejects simply adding event tags to /2. It found material
requirements: postorder corporate-asset tests; unposted NCI goodwill; individual
revaluation origins; depreciation-adjusted reversal caps; benefit availability
findings; asymmetric investment-tax recognition; and the separately adopted
Pillar Two amendment. It also found that /2 reports infer OCI from reserve
changes. This is sufficient for its declared events, but wrong for a direct
surplus-to-retained transfer. /3 must track those transfers separately before
the class-revaluation case runs. AASB107's retained applicability ends before
annual starts on 2026-01-01; /3 will bind that endpoint explicitly. These
repairs make the plan suitable for execution; unresolved real valuations and
law remain explicit inputs and cannot be filled from examples.

Primary pass criterion: independently derived component, journal and report
transitions for each declared variant, with complete authorization, proposal,
atomic commit and report exchanges and targeted adverse refusals. Missing source,
wrong origin/date/member set, hidden notional posting, double-counted loss,
incorrect ceiling or report, altered replay and partial publication veto the
affected exchange and trigger repair. Corrupt protected inputs or changed index
are continuation vetoes until repaired. An absent actual valuation/law/fact blocks
its admission only; independent P3/P4 work continues. A refusal alone does not
deliver the refused method.
