# P15 — Reconcile proposal requirements and exploratory evidence

## Audit finding

This audit covers every MC01–MC35 requirement and G1–G18 gap against the current requirements register, the two gap audits, the Version 1.2 manuscript/specification and the retained P00–P14 records. The complete machine-readable matrix is [`coverage.json`](coverage.json). It has 35 requirement rows, 18 gap rows, nine explicit open decisions, all fifteen historical phases, candidate profile alternatives and hash-pinned source references.

The status counts are {'partial': 30, 'specified': 5} for requirements and {'open': 12, 'partial': 6} for gaps. `specified` means that the cited design states a bounded behaviour; `partial` means that the design or evidence covers only a declared slice; `open` means that the gap still needs a method, case or authority. None of these statuses means that product code exists or that an accounting treatment, forecast, or human judgement has been accepted.

The governing conclusion is that the proposal is materially more precise than the pre-repair draft, but it is not ready to choose an architecture or enable implementation. The source/record/history/journal/statement chain is demonstrated most clearly by Northstar and selected Apple preparation, while whole-package semantic closure, entity and aggregation semantics, historical query eligibility, cross-standard authority, full sector methods, adapter contracts, independent conformance and human acceptance remain open. A prototype's existence does not decide any of these questions.

## Requirement and gap assessment

**MC01–MC05.** Source coverage, numeric context, narrative interpretation, provenance and multiple clocks are specified in the manuscript and bounded fixtures, but package closure, rounding assumptions, layer admission and historical-byte eligibility remain partial.

**MC06–MC12.** Entity/perimeter, mappings, joint histories, template reconstruction, uncertainty categories, memory-preserving aggregation and optional weights have explicit design evidence. The checked cases are bounded; general registry, implementation and completeness remain open.

**MC13–MC18.** Northstar gives a checked economic/journal/statement spine and typed scenario/result intentions. Corporate, causal, noncash, financing and joint-output generality remains limited to declared synthetic scope.

**MC19–MC22.** Standards, company extensions, bank structures and insurance cohorts have structural or partial cases. Dated primary authority, prudential/accounting reconciliation, full measurement and cross-standard matched cases remain unresolved.

**MC23–MC28.** The DSL/shared-IR tradeoff, Core 0 contracts, APIs, solver limits, review actions and synthetic/real case obligations are stated. Independent implementation, adapter integration, complete Apple semantics and broad treatment packages are not established.

**MC29–MC31.** Literature and software ledgers preserve inspected sources and limits. Candidate-by-candidate technical follow-up and executed adapter evidence remain incomplete.

**MC32–MC35.** The manuscript, acceptance separation, staged traceability and internal-before-expert rule are documented. Rendered/human/independent acceptance, release criteria and professional-review readiness remain pending.

The rows preserve the exact requirement wording and current register assessment, then add a remaining obligation and design consequence. The evidence entries identify the requirement line, the relevant chapter/specification/fixture and, where available, a historical phase result. The eight previously prepared rows (MC01–MC05, G11, G13 and G17) remain intact apart from an explicit P15 boundary statement; they are not treated as closed merely because their references validate.

**G1–G3.** Reconstruction, Core 0 contracts and solver-fragment boundaries are materially repaired in design and fixtures, but coverage generation, complete serialization and ordinary rounded/nonlinear cases remain open.

**G4–G7.** A bounded synthetic corporate/financing spine and structural sector examples exist; complete filing-to-forecast integration, scenario/probability execution and standards/bank/insurance methods remain partial.

**G8–G10.** Screening, source preservation and acceptance records improved, while full-text depth, adapter exchanges, release workload and end-to-end acceptance remain open.

**G11–G18.** Coverage/narrative, mapping, clocks, scenario permissions, uncertainty admissibility, compression, review state and replay identities are now explicit obligations with bounded examples; each still needs integrated cases and/or executable contracts.

The most consequential interactions are: a filing query needs package coverage, concept mapping, evidence clocks and review state together; a rounded disclosure can create a bilinear history unless the declared parameterisation is valid; and a balanced journal does not establish a permitted accounting treatment. These interactions are why the remaining obligations are retained in the matrix rather than collapsed into a single completeness score.

## Candidate initial profiles

The proposed comparison candidate is: Provisional bounded corporate-history profile: source/package and narrative coverage records; explicit entity/perimeter/time contexts; Core 0 or typed shared-IR authoring; rational-linear/finite-choice history construction; one bounded corporate event/journal/statement path; explicit review/persistence and replay; Northstar synthetic fixture plus a bounded Apple source-preparation fixture. It is a comparison candidate only, with bank, insurance, full cross-standard authority, general nonlinear inference, complete real semantic closure and production adapters excluded until specified.

The alternatives are: At minimum compare (A) the bounded corporate/shared-IR profile above, (B) typed-library-first with the same shared IR and no DSL commitment, and (C) financing/instrument-first profile with later corporate package. A broad multi-standard/bank/insurance-first profile is a fourth stress alternative. Compare all on identical source-to-record, joint-history, journal/statement, temporal-query, review/invalidation, persistence/replay and acceptance obligations; prototype code availability is not a selection criterion.

The selection question is: Which candidate provides the smallest independently implementable slice that preserves whole-company requirements, joint histories, evidence clocks, accounting-layer separation and later extension points under explicit acceptance fixtures? P16 must document the comparison, vetoes, dependencies, workload and residual risks before any architecture is selected.

The comparison must use the same obligations and fixtures for each candidate. Relevant promotion evidence is downstream semantic and replay behaviour; validation loss, test counts, prototype availability or a clean PDF are explanatory evidence only. P16 is responsible for recording the comparison and keeping the result provisional until independently reviewed.

## Open decisions and evidence boundary

The nine decisions in `coverage.json` preserve proposed answers, alternatives, consequences and next steps. E01–E05 concern package completeness, narrative-to-constraint admission, rounding, temporal query admission and layer-specific review authority. E06–E09 concern initial-profile selection, cross-standard authority, readiness evidence and partial/failure result semantics. Every decision currently has `blocks_proposal: true`; this keeps unresolved design choices visible rather than turning a convenient default into a requirement.

Six evidence classes are kept separate throughout the matrix: specified design, exploratory code, historical tested invariants, accounting validity, empirical forecast validity and human acceptance. The current record contains evidence in the first three classes only. Accounting validity requires dated authority and appropriate treatment review; empirical forecast validity requires a target-specific evidence plan and uncertainty; human acceptance requires independent reader/domain feedback. None is inferred from this audit.
