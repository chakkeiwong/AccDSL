## MC02: numeric context and rounding

The general contract requires compatible entity, perimeter, measurement basis, currency/unit, calendar and dimensions before arithmetic. A sum may remove a dimension only through a valid partition. An explicit dated conversion is required for unlike currencies; a rate also needs its accrual convention and year fraction. Context and value therefore cannot be reduced to a label and floating-point number.

P02 gives a concrete positive representation: Apple's displayed assets of 364,980 in millions become exact USD 364980000000, with the source cell, row, date and unit header preserved. The proposed invalid counterparts are silently dropping the million scale, adding USD to another currency without conversion, treating a closing stock as an interval flow, combining different perimeters, or treating debt principal and carrying amount as interchangeable. The P03 conformance record supplies bounded engineering evidence but expressly excludes other currencies/calendars and multiple entities; it does not establish general validation.

A material unresolved default remains. P02 adopts closed half-unit nearest-rounding intervals and records an unknown issuer tie rule. The semantic contract says an unknown rounding convention is an unresolved reading, not an assumed half-unit interval. An unknown tie rule differs from an unknown nearest-rounding convention, so the mismatch cannot be resolved by silently treating them as identical. Decision P15-E03 proposes preserving the disclosed precision while labelling the nearest-rounding envelope as a conditional compatibility assumption unless source evidence supports it. Neither interval compatibility nor a displayed-identity reconciliation identifies the true unrounded amounts. MC02 stays partial pending that policy and the broader valid/rejected context examples.
