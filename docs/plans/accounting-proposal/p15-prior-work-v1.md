Each row has a scope, limitations, a consequence for the proposal and references with SHA-256 hashes and exact locators. The register is partial P15 work: it is not the missing requirement/gap coverage matrix.

This slice directly supports MC30, MC33 and MC34, preserves MC35's internal-preparation obligation, and informs G10/G18 without closing them. It follows the reviewed narrowed recovery plan. No product implementation, historical repair, authority acquisition or empirical experiment was performed. Existing source snapshots were verified against their capture manifest. Historical checks are reported as historical; selected code inspection is not a fresh conformance run.
