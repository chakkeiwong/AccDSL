## Intent and skeptical audit

Question: can a bank distinguish changing contractual rights, changing credit
expectations, ending credit impairment, returning to twelve-month ECL, and
actually receiving funding? The comparator is the existing loan121/100,
commitment/drawdown and conditional prudential cases. The issuer-liability
ten-percent rule is not an asset-derecognition test.

Primary pass: source-bound decisions, complete finite journal/state/report
paths and balanced-wrong adverse cases. Wrong original-risk baseline, invented
cure, wrong gross/net interest basis, double collateral recovery, erased unpaid
legal claims, or facility capacity posted as received cash vetoes the affected
case and requires repair. Builds/counts are integrity diagnostics. No PD/LGD
calibration, current multi-GAAP compliance, lending strategy or actual liquidity
access is established. Missing actual agreement facts blocks actual application;
it does not halt generic methods or independent clusters.

The audit identifies three shortcuts to prohibit. First, non-derecognition
preserves the initial risk comparator; a renamed modified contract cannot reset
it. Second, a credit-impaired cure and reversal of SICR are separate findings.
Third, collateral value and a draw request do not establish settlement cash.
Counterfactual equal closing equity can conceal wrong interest presentation.
Proceed only with these distinctions and explicit event timing.
