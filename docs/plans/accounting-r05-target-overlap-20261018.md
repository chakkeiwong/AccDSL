## Question and skeptical plan audit

What must a scored forecast observation and its sampling interpretation contain
before E3 can compare alternative histories with a fixed interpretation under
the same economics? The baseline is the unchanged 3.44 E3 contract, the completed
192-report vintage reconciliation and dependent-loss source inquiry. Neither a
report count nor a two-sided scalar loss test defines E3's target population.

Audit findings and repairs: (1) “nine issuers/eight years” is a source-panel
description, not a sampling law; specify finite-panel, repeated-origin and
future-issuer questions separately. (2) A horizon label does not identify a
cash-flow interval; pin interval endpoints, presentation and outcome vintage.
(3) An overlap graph is not a covariance estimator or proof of independence;
retain deterministic overlap and possible shared drivers as different relations.
(4) Observed common coverage must not silently redefine the eligible service
population. Keep failures and unknown outcomes in the original denominator.
(5) Prior source evidence does not justify new probability weights, a margin,
sample size, confidence level or inference procedure. Leave them unselected.

The revised plan passes this audit: it can complete a useful design without
scoring company data, admitting historical vintages, or selecting an inference
method. No manuscript repair is indicated by these already explicit E3 limits.

## Research intent and evidence contract

Main question: distinguish the actual observation/estimand required by three
possible prediction questions. Mechanism under examination: mixing report rows,
forecast origins, target periods and sampling units changes the claimed effect.
Expected failure: apparent improvement from outcome-dependent selection,
inconsistent targets/versions, duplicated economic exposure or assumed iid rows.

Primary documentary pass criterion: three explicit alternative target sheets;
one proposed origin/target/realization and prediction/comparison record contract;
full denominator and information-availability rules; an overlap/dependence map;
and exact synthetic witnesses for weighting, overlap and missingness. Preserve
all original E3 targets, comparator ladder, scoring, scaling and promotion rules.
Comparator: the frozen E3 text, existing protocol ledger and R05 source artifacts.

Promotion vetoes: changed target/baseline, implicit population/weights/margin,
invented byte availability, assigning probabilities to unweighted histories,
zero-imputed failures, or treating illustrative checks as empirical evidence.
Continuation vetoes: corrupted protected inputs, changed HEAD/index or unrelated
dirty content, or a required step outside proposal-only authority. A missing
sampling law vetoes dependent inference, not this specification or independent
R03–R06 work. Repair triggers: ambiguous identity, outcome/availability ordering,
or a failed synthetic invariant. Counts and runtimes are explanatory only.

No company prediction, superiority, population generalization, bootstrap
validity, power, scientific acceptance, default change or whole-row closure may
be concluded. Persist the specification, machine-readable alternatives and
examples, derivation/checks, decision note, manifests and additive R08 supplement.
