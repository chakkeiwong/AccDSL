## Source admission and final facts before calculation

Inspected the retained original EU text: IFRS3.3–19/32/B12C/B37,
IFRS10.5–14/B86–B99, IAS21.38–48D and IAS7.25–28/39–42B. The source requires
goodwill to be expressed in the foreign operation's functional currency,
separate parent/NCI attribution, derecognition of all subsidiary balances on
loss of control, and recycling of the parent's entire translation reserve even
when a minority interest remains. NCI's reserve is derecognized, not recycled.
Acquisition/disposal cash is separately reported net of the whole subsidiary's
cash; retained investment fair value is not cash. These inspected rules support
the proposed interaction without a new source dependency.

Final case admissions: the acquired set already has outputs and an organized
workforce performing a substantive process critical to continuing those outputs
(IFRS3.B12C(a)). No concentration-test shortcut is elected. The workforce is not
a separately identifiable intangible under B37; the stated absence of other
separately recognizable intangibles is a synthetic fact, not a consequence of
calling the residual goodwill. Parent is not an investment entity. Substantive
voting rights give it power over relevant activities, variable returns and the
ability to affect them; the other ordinary holders have no blocking substantive
rights. Acquisition, operations, reporting and disposal are ordered A/O/R/D
within a relative accounting interval. No pre-disposal held-for-sale trigger is
assumed; D is an unanticipated immediate sale after R. Actual evidence would
require IFRS5 assessment rather than this assumption.

At D, use two mutually exclusive scenarios. Full sale receives USD169 for80%.
Partial sale receives126.75 for60% and retains20% at independently admitted
fair value42.25. These sum to169 and assume equal per-share pricing/no control
premium solely to isolate deconsolidation. Fair value is a measurement input,
not derived from net assets or guaranteed by the accounting rules. Both
scenarios transfer actual control to the buyer; the retained20% gives the
seller no remaining power under any other arrangement. Subsequent significant
influence and instrument classification remain a separate admission.
