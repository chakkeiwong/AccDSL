# Recover and repair the company-world boundary

25 October 2026. S2–S4; MC04/06/07/08/11/19–22/24–28/30/32–35.
Successor to `accounting-group-sector-composition-20261025.md`; the original
plan and preliminary audit remain evidence of the interrupted attempt.

The question is whether separately identified legal books can retain their
sector, conditional sources and unresolved duties in a common representation.
The baseline is the existing finite CompanyComposition/1 case and the separate
BankAssessment/1 and InsuranceAssessment/1 evidence. The state profile is
CompanyBook/1; CompanyBookTransition/2 is a transition profile. The predecessor
plan's reference to CompanyBook/2 was wrong.

The recovery audit rejected the unfinished implementation: JSON encoding was
mistaken for reduced-fraction validation; source and view bindings were absent;
sector-to-book binding was absent; adverse tests could pass at an earlier,
unrelated refusal. The preliminary audit's plan-hash self-comparison and search
for the word "remain" establish neither preservation nor sector semantics.
Its limited source lock is not a lock of everything reportedly inspected.
These are repair triggers, not evidence against company composition.

Repair the unaccepted helper and tests, preserving all accepted dependencies.
Books are identified by (entity, book); components name a book key, so one
entity may have multiple books. This version permits one root sector handle
per book. Ownership is an exact reduced fraction in [0,1], separately from an
explicit control premise; no percentage establishes control. Currency and
calendar are deliberately restricted to a single declared context. Handle
digests are opaque in this step: structural agreement is not reference
resolution, state compatibility, a control assessment, or source admission.
Empty component populations remain visibly incomplete.

The primary criterion is exact retention of the supplied world and all its
obligations, with correct rejection of identity, sector, context and reference
binding errors. Require exact refusal reasons, nonmutation, deterministic
replay, bounded input structure and accepted dependency identities. Counts of
checks, a balanced book, and sector labels are explanatory only. Missing or
changed dependencies veto the affected result; a localized implementation
failure triggers repair. No statistical ranking, accounting compliance,
production readiness, consolidation or complete sector engine is inferred.

Inherited bounds (1 MB, 4,096 nodes/scalar characters, depth 48) are engineering
limits from MethodRequirements/1, not scientific defaults. Test their refusal
paths. Limits of 64 books, 128 edges/components and 512 obligations are finite
prototype choices; they have no economic meaning. Rational values use the
retained exact scalar parser. No new package or numerical environment is needed.

Execute focused unittest checks, repair the retained-source audit with exact
locks and a human-readable evidence map, then record immutable cases, results,
manifest and read-only replay under `company-world-envelope-20261025`.
Next resolve actual referenced bytes and inspect the sector method boundary;
only then decide what belongs in the governing manuscript. Repeated manuscript
builds are unnecessary for an unaccepted helper. Keep populated source-backed
invoices, revised terms, whole-company statement coverage and general sector
posting explicitly on the remaining queue. This work does not replace them.
