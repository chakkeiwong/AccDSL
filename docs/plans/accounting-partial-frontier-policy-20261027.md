## Research intent and reviewed plan

Question: what can be concluded about policies before temporal search completes,
without dropping pending domains or allowing the actor to observe analyst-only
branch identities? Baselines: ResumableTemporalFrontier/1 saved checkpoints,
BoundActorObservationPolicy/1 full mappings and the two existing complete policy
formulations. The mechanism under test binds certified completed source supports
to policy constraints while preserving the exact pending remainder.

Keep the original question separate from its implementation checklist. For a
fixed, explicitly declared policy domain and observation/action meanings, adding
completed temporal cells may exclude policies but cannot rescue a rejected one.
This monotonicity requires a fixed domain: adding new message keys can otherwise
change the type of a policy. Do not compare raw sets of partial functions whose
key domains differ. First inspect whether the current closed declarations cover
the full possible domain; define total policies over that fixed declared domain
or an explicit extension relation. Unresolved source outcomes are distinct from
pending search cells and from complete zero-policy problems.

Primary criterion: every partial-policy conclusion agrees with independently
computed restrictions of the same complete problem, and retains every completed
support and pending domain identity. A positive feasible policy over known cells
is only a candidate until all relevant cells are resolved. A witnessed failure in
a known cell rejects that particular full policy. A proof that no policy works on
known cells establishes infeasibility for supersets only under the fixed policy
class and unchanged observation semantics. If no completed cell exists, report
no observed constraints, not proven feasibility of the original problem.

Promotion vetoes: unknown becomes zero; unfinished becomes complete; changed actor,
map, method, time, source or policy-domain semantics reuse prior conclusions;
missing/duplicate source support; forged pending identity; partial-function
inclusion mistaken for total-policy inclusion. Repair these before promotion.
A corrupted complete baseline or inconsistent target invalidates the affected
comparison. Expected candidate rejection does not terminate the research.

Explanatory only: number of known cells, policy counts, runtime and saved work.
No speedup, empirical probability, actual source truth, authenticated access,
production recovery or broad scalability claim. Preserve results in a fresh
partial-frontier-policy-20261027 directory with source locks, manifests, full
policy sets, counterexamples and a decision/inference-status result table.

Defaults audit: the two-date financial fixture and exact finite maps are inherited
comparison scope, not a general default. Fixed DFS permits predecessor equality;
no schedule ranking follows. Unknown timing stays unweighted. The policy universe
must be explicitly reviewed rather than silently grown as cells appear. The
smallest diagnostic uses one complete correction case at root-only, first-leaf,
intermediate and complete checkpoints; compare all feasible full policies with
