Question: which financial and observation source heads can be jointly selected

Skeptical audit identifies two different questions. A report's selected financial
assertion can change; that does not establish that the underlying receipt or
contractual right changed at that moment. The inherited policy binding filters
complete financial action traces by one financial payload and requires equal
financial heads at both dates. Removing that check and using the second payload
for a complete trace would rewrite earlier conditions. Taking a Cartesian product
of old/new trace rows can invent a discontinuous economic history. Do neither.

This increment generalizes source selection only, explicitly returning
financial-policy binding as unassessed when financial heads differ. The next
design must separate the actor's assertion history from the joint economic
history consistent with admitted evidence. It must state how superseded claims,
real contract amendments and unresolved conflicts differ. Source supersession
alone is not an economic transition. This boundary permits safe progress without
choosing an unsupported financial rule or asking for a fabricated input.

subset; independent reconstruction of all new joint leaves; exact expected head

full original remainder and false source/accounting/forecast admission flags.
