## Skeptical/default audit

The plan passes with a required distinction: retaining all 27 known members
does not establish all future discovery edges or every legal obligation. Search
both XML comments and schema annotations; preserve full matching bodies and
all links before interpretation, then manually read them. The graph is a
declared population, not an inferred permission boundary. Empty inventory
gets an explicit unknown disposition. A record without a new permission body
cannot close the Software Notice question. The `xbrldt-2005.xsd` comment grants
specified copying and explanatory derivatives with notices; this does not
automatically grant modification of definitions in other files. Preserve that
local text without generalising it from namespace or host.

Execution: verify the preceding 1176-reference manifest and preserve the four

Otherwise retain Version 2.25 and record the source-only result. No network
request, installation, worker launch, publication or license admission is needed.

Pre-mortem: the inventory could appear to clear a bundle because every row has
a label. Labels are not permission; retain direct quotations, applicability
limits and unresolved proposed-use conditions. A missing notice is a source
gap, not a licence to infer one. If a more specific or conflicting source is
later located, reopen only its affected interpretation and proposed uses.
