## Research intent and evidence contract

Question: what does Apple's complete FY2024 annual-01 macroeconomic risk body
establish about demand, operating-counterparty, receivable, financing,
liquidity and financial-instrument channels, and what evidence is required
before any of those channels can enter a conditional accounting history?

Candidate mechanism: an adverse macroeconomic state can affect several linked
actors at once. The source body is an issuer risk assertion. It is not an
estimate of a shock, a probability distribution, a legal finding or a journal.
The repair target is source-bound representation of joint conditional channels.

Baseline: the pinned FY2024 annual and FY2025 Q1 interim originals/extractions,
the risk-coverage result and the frozen complete Note 4/6/9, segment and
perimeter source units. Primary criterion: exact annual-01 body retention,
source-bound dependent passages, explicit entity/concept/time/measure
distinctions, and a conditional mechanism whose missing facts are visible.

Promotion criterion: the unit may be accepted as bounded internal source
preparation only when every selected passage has exact offsets/hashes, each
claim is classified as source assertion, local derivation, assumption or open
question, and no numeric consequence is presented as Apple's history.

Promotion vetoes: an offset/hash mismatch; treating a broad interval as a
complete dependency without inspecting its local context; adding a probability,
elasticity, tariff/rate, geographic share, loss amount, accounting treatment or
automatic journal absent from the source; mixing annual and interim periods;
using statement totals with incompatible populations; or claiming whole-risk
semantic coverage. Continuation veto: source corruption, an unrepaired
partition defect, or a missing required diagnostic. A missing fact or blocked
interpretation triggers a narrower source/query record and the next frontier.

Explanatory diagnostics are paragraph counts, rendered pages, body length and
numeric examples. They cannot promote source preparation or manuscript
readiness. Nothing here ranks methods or candidates statistically.

## Skeptical audit and default/assumption audit

The plan survives audit because it uses annual-01 as the declared population,
keeps the interim incorporation statement separate, and starts with the
smallest relevant dependency passages. The risk body names mechanisms but not
their magnitudes. A source-overlap count cannot prove that a note was read or
that its denominator matches a risk claim. The source unit therefore records
comparability before any arithmetic and leaves a query unresolved when facts
are absent. It will not revise the manuscript unless the reader-facing
mechanism is materially improved.

Pre-mortem: the unit could appear successful while merely copying the risk
paragraph and appending unrelated balances. It could also fail for a scope
reason when a percentage is from a different date or population. The cheapest
checks are exact source locators, a dependency table with comparability fields,

source and narrow the claim; do not widen the interval or infer a number.

## Reader and method boundary

The target reader is an accounting modeller who has already seen Apple's
reported segment, liquidity and debt structures. The local question is why a
single macroeconomic state can alter several future branches while the filing
still leaves the joint history underdetermined. The sequence is: source risk
assertion; named affected actors and channels; checked reported balances and
populations; symbolic conditional state; recognition and settlement facts still
needed. No displayed equation is required unless it clarifies a genuinely new
relation.

Cold-reader questions fixed before drafting: (1) which channels does the source
name; (2) why are they joint rather than one receivable haircut; (3) which
reported amounts are dated observations rather than shock parameters; (4) what
facts distinguish a demand, counterparty, funding and valuation path; (5) what
would be needed before a journal or forecast; and (6) what does this unit leave
open?
