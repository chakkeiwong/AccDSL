unresolved identity, invented actual holdings or statutory interpretation.

## Assumption audit and skeptical review

The two targets come from exact FY2024 references, not a convenient modern
edition. Official governance documents may have changed: inspect internal
dates and the filing cross-reference first. Authorised shares may exceed
issued/outstanding shares: keep these concepts and effective dates separate.
Governance voting rules may contain exceptions or statutory dependencies:
retain whole relevant sections and explicitly identify uninspected law.
The plan passes this audit because the comparator and identity tests are
explicit, acquisition is bounded, and no count or summary promotes legal
completeness. Search results alone do not establish an inspected document.
