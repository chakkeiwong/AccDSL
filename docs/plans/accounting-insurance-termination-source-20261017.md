## Skeptical audit and research intent

Question: how is each original OCI balance treated when transfer or
derecognition interrupts an unfinished reclassification schedule following a
holdings-driven change between IFRS17.88(b) and 89(b)? Existing homogeneous
termination examples expressly exclude pending policy-change runoff. Reusing
them solely according to the current policy label would lose original origins.

Candidate interpretation: apply 91(a)/(b) to each balance according to the
paragraph that originally caused its recognition, with no duplicate release.
Competing interpretation: B135 continues the frozen old-policy schedule after
termination, potentially qualifying that reading of 91. Reproduce both fairly;
do not choose precedence from arithmetic or from the desired implementation.

Audit verdict: source investigation may proceed. A numerical candidate is held
until exact authority or a complete derivation resolves the interaction. Missing
authority blocks that dependent method, not independent documentary work.
This plan is source research, not an empirical comparison or compliance claim.
