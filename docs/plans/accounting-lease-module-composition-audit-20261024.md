# Lease-right lifecycle and shared-book composition audit

24 October 2026. S2-S4; MC02/04/05/11/13/16/17/18/24/26/28/35;
G2/G3 and P17-04.09. This is the next broader accounting module after conditional
return queries and exact evidence storage; forecasting and production stay held.

Question: what must a shared-company interface preserve to invoke the existing
finite lease.reduce/pay/depreciate methods without merging heterogeneous rights,
duplicating opening cash/equity or inheriting unsupported accounting authority?
Comparators are the retained CorporateResiduals/1 authored lease lifecycle and
its independently derived financial checks, with original source-policy scope.
The original CompanyModuleInvocationDraft/1 handles only an exact return event;
NativeReturnBookReference/1 lacks ROU/lease-liability accounts and lease memory.
A generic surrounding callback cannot silently fill that representation gap.

Skeptical audit: a balanced journal is not sufficient evidence of correct right
allocation, payment interval, company funding or complete state. An isolated
lease component's opening cash/equity cannot be added again to a caller's book.
Existing source-policy findings are conditional historical evidence, not current
legal advice or fresh source admission. Retain original source editions and
identity. No new accounting treatment or current compliance claim is introduced.

Audit the complete original lease derivation, authored sequence, schema/finding
checks, source policy and existing shared-book contracts before implementation.
Bounded probes must compare every original component state, journal and gross
cash category against exact Fraction deductions. Exercise heterogeneous rights,
full termination, subsequent reduction, annual payment and systematic depreciation;
wrong owner/account mapping, unsupported calendar, missing/false findings,
unknown amounts, duplicate rights and shared cash shortage. Record what the
old low-level method checks and what its outer schema/admission layer must check.
A missing callback validation is not automatically a defect in the full original
interface. Distinguish that boundary before proposing a repair.

Primary criteria: original exact replay plus independent arithmetic, full right
identity and state reconciliation, declared account footprint, no duplicated
opening balances and atomic refusal under unsupported conditions. Counts and
journal balance alone are explanatory. Unexpected date/funding/identity acceptance
vetoes proposed composition and triggers explicit boundary design; it does not
reject leases as a research direction. Frozen-input mutation is a continuation
veto. Unknown authority or actual contract facts block those claims only.

Assumptions: retained synthetic owner/book and historical election, exact rational
annual end-payment schedule, supplied traced-right allocation, supplied systematic
depreciation and no intervening accrual/modification. These are fixture premises,
not general policy defaults. Early checks compare original heterogeneous rights
with an aggregate shortcut and inspect method prerequisites before executing.
No stochastic comparison, valuation calibration or revised source rule is claimed.
