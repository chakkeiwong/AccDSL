## Intent and evidence contract

Question: can a single dated equity/security history generate reconstructible
basic and diluted EPS while retaining the same monetary accounts, ownership
quantities, continuing-operation control number and source applicability?

Candidate mechanism: a read-only EPS assessment referencing profit attribution,
ordinary-share intervals, split factors and separately assessed potential-share
instruments. Comparator: the retained IAS33 clauses and existing chapter's
limited assertion; closing-count division is an explicit adverse comparator.

Primary pass: a complete exact positive lifecycle and adverse cases, with
denominator weighting, numerator adjustments, dilution sequence and resulting
report quantities independently checked. Binding/eligibility, timing, split
adjustment, missing averages/market prices or unsupported cash-settlement
terms can veto the affected assessment and trigger repair. A corrupted source
or contradictory unreconciled rule stops dependent work only. Balanced journals,
matching one EPS value, citation counts and build success are explanatory
checks, not method completeness or accounting compliance.

The result must not claim actual Apple award expense, market valuation,
present-standard compliance, complete earnings quality, calibrated forecast,
runtime conformance or human acceptance. The authored potential shares and
income amounts remain assumptions. No stochastic ranking applies.
