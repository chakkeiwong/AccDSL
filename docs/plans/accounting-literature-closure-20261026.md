## Research intent and evidence contract

Main question: which literature-backed methods preserve the intended company
and information semantics, and under what explicit restrictions?

Candidate mechanisms: joint symbolic histories, information-preserving finite
aggregation, nested/adapted distances, persistent-parameter filtering, robust
updating, dated financing/recovery, observation selection, and learned equation
solvers. Comparator: exact finite histories and feasible policies, direct
rational arithmetic, original source equations and pinned official code.

Primary closure criterion: inspected correct source/version; exact source
anchors; a justified target translation or explicit rejection; a discriminating
case; and updated reader-facing claims. For software correspondence, also a
pinned, compatible specification/data/code mapping and meaningful conformance
evidence. For source access, actual inspected primary bytes are required.

Promotion vetoes: unsupported source claims; unmatched editions; anticipative
policies; changes to the admitted histories/measure without disclosure; wrong
cash/face/recovery/ownership algebra; missing code dependence; and failed
required reference results. A veto rejects the candidate claim, not the whole
direction. Repair by restricting the claim, fixing the derivation or testing
the next justified alternative.

Continuation vetoes: corrupted source bytes, invalid harness, an unavailable
required source for that particular claim, or required protected access not
available. Continue independent gaps. Access failures do not justify ranking
an unread paper or substituting metadata for technical evidence.

Explanatory only: number of downloads/citations/pages, bibliography resolution,
smoke checks, runtime and source visibility. Exact finite results support only
their declared models. No empirical superiority, global convergence, exhaustive
literature/failure coverage or real-company forecast validity is inferred.

## Source and screening procedure

Prefer author, journal, university and official project sources. Retain every
material primary paper and exact code revision locally. Use technical method,
theory, evaluation and relevant appendix sections, not abstract-only claims.
For scans, read rendered pages; record the actual scope. Record version and
correction queries with dates, including negative metadata limits. Do not
claim absence of corrections from an empty field.

For each material seed inspect related work/references, classify relevant
foundational, direct, competing and follow-up works, and query recent/highly
cited citing works when an index is accessible. Follow a newly material lead
until its method contribution is understood or an explicit blocker is recorded.
A first bounded pass is not survey completeness; unresolved follow-ups stay
open. Source failure after a new legal route triggers documented access needs,
not repeated identical requests or purchases.

Maintain source-support.json, citation-venue.json, backward-snowball.json,
forward-snowball.json, claim-support.json and omissions.json in the work unit.
No citation count/rank is a criterion for mathematical acceptance.
