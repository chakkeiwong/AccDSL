# R03-N — transferred options, collars and asset classification

15 October 2026. Baseline Version 3.11. MC13/16/17/19–21/24–28/30/32–35.
Documentary methods and exact conditional arithmetic only. Runtime is held.

Question: what remains on the bank's balance sheet when a transferred asset
has retained calls, written puts or collars, and which measurement route must
be admitted before applying a loan method? Comparator: R03-I's guarantee,
retained-risk funding and relative-fair-value partial transfer, plus the
existing amortised-cost loan and ECL examples. This addresses those named
residuals, not an exhaustive new collection of financial instruments.

Skeptical audit: the presence of an option does not automatically mean either
full derecognition or continuing involvement. First apply risks/rewards and
control. Readily obtainable assets and practical sale restrictions matter.
The option can already be represented by the net recognized asset/liability;
adding a second derivative can double count it. Asset classification requires
business-model and contractual-cash-flow findings; management intent for one
asset and a principal-looking payoff are insufficient. FVOCI debt impairment
does not reduce the asset's reported fair value. Equity FVOCI is a different
election, including its disposal treatment. A quoted option value is an input,
not a general valuation method. These flaws are prevented by the ordering and
limited claims below; the plan passes for this conditional work.

Primary criterion: inspected IFRS9 clauses, an explicit eligibility/order/
measurement/reporting procedure and independently computed asset/liability/
income/equity bridges. Double counting, applying the wrong derecognition
branch, conflating debt/equity OCI or using unsupported valuation is a case
veto and repair trigger. Missing actual law, strategy or pricing facts blocks
application, not independent method drafting. No stochastic ranking, actual
bank compliance, current-law completeness or runtime conformance is inferred.

Default audit: synthetic amounts and one-period horizons isolate the branch;
zero tax and supplied fair values/ECL simplify reconciliation. They remain
case hypotheses. Time values must be nonnegative and option values consistent
with intrinsic value; check the net option exposure before journals. Rates
and no-interim-cash schedules must match the amortisation example. Historical
EU2023/1803 is paragraph support only. Later effective rules need distinct
admission and cannot silently be supplied by a newer source title.

   5.7.5–11. Locate existing classification/valuation promises before extending.

4. Integrate beside existing bank methods, preserve predecessor text or record
   precise stale-transition repairs, build and inspect the changed pages.
   Refresh insurance methods and closed-exchange work before R07/R08.

Reader: understands the prior transfer and ECL examples. Teach why a sale with
a retained option leaves a net exposure, then why reporting depends on the
admitted measurement category. One call example motivates the formulas; the
put and collar are contrasting rights. Drafting remains provisional pending
human feedback. Evidence: data/accounting-master/bank-options-classification-20261015/.

Continuation audit, 15 October: B3.2.17's complete subordinated-interest example
has now also been inspected. It supports the separate retained-interest versus
credit-enhancement distinction, not a new pricing assumption. The seven drafted
cases answer the named residuals and retain the required prior derecognition
findings. The amortised-cost case explicitly assumes a zero allowance and no
coupon or other intervening cash flow; the option expires before the asset is
collected. Different debt categories use different admitted business models,
not a free policy election. All time values and ECL amounts are supplied; no
valuation quality or classification follows from arithmetic success. The

followed by source, preservation, build and rendered-page inspection. This
audit passes for documentary execution; incorrect algebra or missing source
