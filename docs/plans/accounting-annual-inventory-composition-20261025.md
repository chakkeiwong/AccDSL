annual financial state has26 accounts including income_tax_payable,plus tax
vintages,current/deferred assessments,losses and archived periods. Dropping the
extra account or moving it into equity would be wrong. The new optional
annual operation must act on the complete registered tax book and validate
its original invariant. No schema coercion,balancing plug or runtime patching.

remove its planned whole-lot sale before execution. This is an explicit alternate
future request over the checked unsold opening,not a fabricated inventory balance.
Verify the derived root state and bind its exact digest before comparisons.

Reuse the inspected proportional formulas and declaration checks in a separate
annual inventory extension. Unchanged ordinary/current/deferred/carry operations
must delegate directly to AnnualProgram/1 transition. Preserve all26 balances,
liability vintages,settlement history,findings and archives. Optional sales may
change cash,revenue,inventory,cost of sales,selected lot,date,event ids and flows
only. No tax amount is automatically recomputed. Mark activity after a current
assessment explicitly; any prior deferred measurement becomes stale. Events
inside an assessed interval must be refused under the existing timing rule.

## Evidence contract and assumptions

Question: does the optional sale compose with assessment/payment/measurement/
carry while preserving the complete annual accounting state and report history?
Baseline: old annual runs for unchanged programs; standalone exact allocation
for local numerical effects; independent two-unit and multiple-period controls.
Primary pass: complete old-operation results preserved,exact new financial
quantities and journals,unchanged tax/vintage history,appropriate stale statuses,
statement tie-outs and complete-result apply refusal of tampering.

Expected failure: partial quantity is correct locally but taxes or later
measurements silently retain stale information,or a wrapper loses historical
accounts. Such defects veto promotion and trigger repair. Corrupt baselines or
unresolvable required inputs veto continuation. Counts and timing explain only
finite engineering scope;they establish neither accounting admission nor a
whole-company or production system.

Explicit defaults: optional proportional model and its six findings are
inherited conditional hypotheses;register only the exact annual tax schema;
use the inspected synthetic retained-lot scenario;CPU only with
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1. Unknowns remain unknown. No current-law,new demand or
forecast assumptions are supplied.
