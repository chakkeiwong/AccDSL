## Skeptical audit / bounded execution contract

The accumulated methods have had local render review. That is insufficient for
the whole argument: a new method may duplicate a later one, silently change
scope, use undefined notation, or leave an earlier claim stale. A thumbnail
overview, script, count or repeated ledger boilerplate cannot supply continuous
reading. The audited plan therefore diagnoses the actual PDF in order, in
segments of at most six normal-resolution pages. Exact exit understanding
carries verbatim into the next segment's entry. Operational tables are evaluated
as reference material; narrative chapters must teach the argument.

Target reader: an accounting modeller/implementer who can reconstruct each
worked method, distinguish disclosed facts from assumed histories and local
deductions, and decide which interface or accounting claim is supported.
Purpose: implementation-governing design, with historical source limits and
independent/domain/human acceptance stated accurately.

Primary criterion: complete, specific diagnosis of the document's motivation,
argument, local prerequisites, mechanism-bearing equations, transitions,
counterexamples, source boundaries and final conclusions against the rendered
baseline. Preserve equations, derivations, citations, findings and qualifications.
Confirmed defects require reader-facing repair in a successor after diagnosis;
explicit source/human dependencies retain their actual status.

Vetoes: missing/changed baseline, omitted rendered pages or displays, incorrect
hierarchical parents, fabricated reading, generic repeated judgments, a
source/measurement mismatch, or source loss. Rendering and structural checks are
diagnostics only. No readability score, accounting certification, human voice
certification or scientific superiority claim is authorized. A missing source
blocks its dependent repair; other diagnosis continues.

## Work sequence and evidence

1. Inventory every chapter/section/subsection and displayed equation in expanded
   TeX order with stable IDs and actual ancestors. Bind the exact TeX/Bib hashes,
   PDF hash, every physical page and bookmark range. Protect baseline sources
   outside the manuscript. Use a small inventory/validation utility because no
   repository whole-document/hierarchy validator exists; this is not a new
   product runtime or execution controller.
2. Render sequential page groups at110dpi using the existing PyMuPDF/Pillow
   environment, with GPUs intentionally hidden and no framework import.
   Read normal-size pages and their text, at most six per group. Record distinct
   comprehension, mathematical, source and typography findings. Never mark
   unread nodes complete merely because their text was extracted.
3. Maintain one consolidated structure ledger plus ordered segment records and
   a defect register. Each substantive unit records entry belief, open question,
   parent contribution, changed understanding, residual uncertainty and the
   reason for its position. Displayed equations are actual hierarchy nodes;
   record their role and exposition when encountered. Deeper rederivation is
   selective and needs a separate plan if research-decision-making.
4. Complete diagnosis before copying/editing a successor candidate. Then plan
   the smallest repairs; compare actual baseline/candidate bodies and every
   removed equation, citation or qualification. Regress complete chapters in
   order, cumulative prefixes after each two chapters, and the entire final
   document. Unchanged bodies cannot substantiate repair.
5. Refresh requirements/assessment and the master only to the evidence earned.
   Final author-only status is `independent_reader_pending`; missing human
   feedback blocks acceptance, not feasible internal preparation.

Defaults:110dpi is a reading convenience, not evidence of legibility; zoom when
needed. Physical pages are authoritative because printed numbering restarts.
PDF bookmarks guide page mapping but never stand in for reading. Existing
method checks are preserved evidence, not rerun or promoted to narrative
acceptance. These choices are reviewed engineering conveniences, not scientific
defaults. Early diagnostics: inspect the actual first pages and hierarchy links;
stop a defective inventory before attributing coverage.

Pre-mortem: a large ledger could pass while containing only extracted headings
and stock comments. Keep unjudged fields empty, reading status pending, and
validate coverage separately from the quality of scholarly judgments. A local
fix could conceal losses elsewhere; full diagnosis and later ordered regression
prevent closure from a convenient changed slice alone.

No controller/product execution, installations, empirical campaigns,
publication, outreach or fees. I1 mixed-origin early termination, general F1/F2,
S1 and other named method/source residuals remain separate active obligations.
