# Partial discontinuation preserves the origin of the reserve

## Skeptical audit and research intent

The acquired-instrument and repeated-item methods preserve added measurement
origins but explicitly exclude removals. A negative quantity in an addition
event would erase accumulated history. The next unit must retain the reserve
associated with the discontinued part and distinguish it from subsequent
derivative changes recognized in earnings.

Do not treat all reductions as rebalancing. The first supported removal is
partial discontinuation because a specified homogeneous part of the forecast
purchase is no longer highly probable, while the remainder still qualifies.
IFRS 9 B6.5.27(b), 6.5.12 and 6.5.11(d) distinguish (i) still expected cash
flows, (ii) no longer expected cash flows, and (iii) an unrecoverable accumulated
loss. The source must be inspected before freezing inputs. Instrument-only
ratio reductions and heterogeneous item layers need separate allocation rules;
this unit must not quietly claim those cases.

Main question: can a reference report split an admitted homogeneous hedge,
continue measurement on its eligible remainder, preserve the discontinued
reserve until the appropriate outcome, and reconcile acquisition/settlement
cash without resetting cumulative measurement origins? The mechanism under
test is explicit continuing/discontinued portions plus a frozen reserve.
Expected failure: a whole-position lower-of calculation credits subsequent
gains on the discontinued derivative portion to OCI, or releases the reserve
merely because a purchase ceased to be highly probable.

Baseline: the protected repeated-item first observation, 100 units at an
80 origin, then price 90 and cumulative derivative gain/value 1200, reserve
1000 and profit 200. For a removed homogeneous 20 units the continuing values
are derivative 960/item −800/reserve 800/profit 160; discontinued derivative
240/reserve 200/prior profit 40. Splitting alone makes no journal. Homogeneity
and identical per-unit exposures justify the allocation algebraically:
`min(a|D|, a|H|) = a min(|D|, |H|)` for nonnegative a. This is not permission
to allocate heterogeneous or unidentified reserves by convenient percentages.

At a later supplied price 95 and whole-derivative value 1400, continuing
gain 1120/item −1200 produce reserve 1120/profit zero. The discontinued
derivative value is 280: its additional gain 40 goes to profit, alongside its
prior ineffectiveness 40. If the removed purchase is still expected, total
reserve/profit are 1320/80; a naive whole-position lower-of would give
1400/0, both wrong. If the removed purchase is no longer expected, release
its frozen reserve 200 into profit, giving reserve/profit 1120/280.

## Evidence, assumptions and stopping rules

Freeze exact independent stage/journal/cash targets before the calculator.
Cover continuing and canceled purchases, a negative reserve with a separately
quantified unrecoverable part, and repeated/same-mark observations. A later
purchase transfers only the remaining applicable reserve into inventory cost;
derivative settlement uses its whole carrying value, and released reserves
cannot be transferred again. Preserve one small fractional-quantity case or
split conservation check to expose integer-only shortcuts. Require separate
findings for identification/homogeneity, continuing qualification, stopped-part
probability/expectation, valuations, loss recovery, purchase and settlement.
Unknown findings must not silently become zero, cancellation or continued
qualification.

Primary criterion: all independently frozen amounts, balanced journals,
reserve/profit conservation, cash/asset/equity bridges and expected refusals
agree exactly. Comparator: the protected baseline plus independent per-unit
derivation and direct final cash/net-asset arithmetic. Promotion vetoes:
unmatched origins, wrong reserve release/basis, erased historical earnings,
unsupported allocation, invalid byte/context/date bindings, or non-atomic
failure. Counts and execution time are explanatory only. This is deterministic;
no stochastic ranking or forecasting claim is at issue.

Material assumptions: inherited zero-origin derivative and original price are
baseline hypotheses, checked by replay; homogeneous paired portions are a new
explicit scope hypothesis, checked by exact split conservation and refused when
unknown/false; supplied marks are valuation inputs, not a price model; no tax,
fees or other flows is a pretax illustration boundary, verified by final
net assets. No earlier-owner/acquired-cost, multiple-origin, offsetting or
full cash-flow-statement conclusion is inferred. Purchase and settlement
cash movements retain separate origins without asserting a universal activity
classification for a discontinued derivative.

Continuation vetoes are inconsistent required source, corrupted protected
inputs or work outside documentary authorization. A candidate defect triggers
retained repair, up to three focused attempts before replanning. A missing
application fact blocks its claim while independent method work continues.
Source-specific allocation gaps remain visible; no rule is invented to close
the whole financing row. Independent/domain and human acceptance remain pending.
