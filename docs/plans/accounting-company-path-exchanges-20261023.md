# Portable incomplete paths and assumption revisions

the checked complete-path design. The42-record evidence packet admits complete

Skeptical audit:a failed annual run is not a conditional closing; attaching the
complete branch's closing to it would invent a successor opening. A revision
cannot be validated from its reason string or changed-year index alone. Compare
the parent and child input bodies, permit only the stated annual replacement,
keep their opening/source identity, and check unchanged prefixes. Re-evaluate
the child from the historical root; portable records describe that replay rather
than claiming transactional storage or incremental execution.

Question:can complete/partial annual outcomes and parent-child assumption
revisions be exchanged without invented year-end state or stale suffixes?
Baseline:the65-check complete export and164-check operation path. Primary
criterion:every available annual run/report binds its input, predecessor and
applied prefix; only complete years have closing memory; later years remain
unexecuted after interruption. Revision records bind exact parent/child inputs,
reason and changed year, preserve earlier result identities and explicitly deny
historical restatement. Original complete records remain byte-identical.

Promotion vetoes:complete-state substitution, fabricated annual totals, discarded
unresolved years, changed source/opening, undeclared multiple-year edits, stale
prefix/suffix, or altered baseline evidence. Repair before retaining the draft.
Unavailable root bytes or contradictory revision identity is a continuation
veto for dependent exchange. Counts/speed are explanatory. Passing means local
portable synthetic consistency and replay, not production persistence, recovery
after a crash, authenticated review or retrospective factual correction.

Choices:reuse the accepted complete packet as immutable baseline/source/method
evidence; new YearOutcome and PathOutcome records distinguish partial results.
Use content-addressed byte payloads rather than copying opaque code into records.
Trusted-local replay checks semantics; hashes alone do not. Existing method and
source-time limits remain. Late unknown payroll and supplier failure supply
different partial outcomes; a declared price revision and its explicit repair
exercise the revision relation. Do not invent a new data-generating process.

right comparators. Content hashes alone cannot establish that a report follows
