## Research intent and skeptical audit

Question: after an already capitalized contract cost loses recoverability,
what further obligation must be recognized, and how do completion or exit
consume the asset/provision without charging the loss twice? The existing
CorporateResiduals/1 cost method supplies impairment/reversal but explicitly
leaves the later CGU review and wider contract routes separate. The already
delivered mixed amendment is not an onerous-contract measurement method.

Inspect retained historical EU2023/1803 IFRS15.99–104 and IAS37.14,36–45,
59–69. IAS37.68 compares least net fulfilment loss with available compensation
for failure to fulfil. Comparing gross cost130 directly with penalty50, then
subtracting revenue100, would give the wrong obligation. IAS37.68A includes
allocated directly related costs; treating incremental cash alone as the
whole cost can hide a loss. Paragraph69 requires prior asset impairment.
IFRS15.103 also requires the intervening CGU assessment; do not silently omit it.

The finite method assumes a single remaining point-in-time service, no partial
performance, no paid advance, receivable, refund, tax, FX, financing, reimbursement
or subsequent unrelated asset. One eligible contract-cost asset is supplied.
Other-standard impairment is completed and the subsequent CGU assessment has
no additional loss, each as a separate admitted finding. All future direct
costs are cash: incremental labor/material plus explicitly allocated directly
related contract cash overhead. Unrelated general administration is separate.
No probability or valuation model is inferred. Time value is immaterial under
a supplied finding, not by a blanket one-year convention.
