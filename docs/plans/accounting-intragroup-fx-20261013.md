# Explain the exchange result that survives an intragroup elimination

13 October 2026. P17/E07 feeding P18; MC02/06/08/10–11/13/15–16/19/28/30/32–35;
G4/G7/G12/G15/G16. Proposal preparation, with runtime held.

Question: why can the foreign-exchange result on an intragroup monetary loan
survive elimination, and what fact changes its consolidated presentation from
profit to other comprehensive income? Version 2.51's groups/currency section
states the distinction but supplies no complete journal/statement example.

Comparator: the existing section and the retained original 2023 EU Regulation
2023/1803, `docs/design/research/p08-v3-preparation/eu-2023-1803-original.pdf`.
IAS 21 occupies PDF 151–162. Read that full standard plus the relevant IAS 7
cash-flow paragraph and IFRS 10 elimination provisions, with exact paragraph
anchors and rendered source inspection. This is historical authority for a
dated worked example; do not claim current amendment completeness.

Candidate mechanism: a USD-functional parent funds its EUR-functional wholly
owned subsidiary with a EUR monetary loan. The subsidiary retains the euros
as cash. The liability and receivable eliminate at the closing rate, but the
group still owns foreign-currency cash. Compare an ordinary repayable loan
with a separately assumed loan for which settlement is neither planned nor
likely in the foreseeable future, satisfying the relevant net-investment
conditions. The classification is conditional on those facts, not a convenient
election inferred from maturity or common ownership.

Assumption audit: exchange rates and amounts are authored rational inputs;
use USD per EUR consistently. A 100%-owned subsidiary, established functional
currencies, opening USD cash/equity 200, EUR loan/cash 100 at 1.2 then 1.3,
no interest/tax/fees/impairment/other activity and no settlement are simplifying
facts. They are not issuer observations or market calibration. The zero-interest
step abstracts financing measurement; it does not assert that a real long-term
interest-free loan would initially be measured at its undiscounted face. Start
from a separately established carrying amount at the first observation and
isolate only the exchange movement over the interval. Inspect this boundary
before selecting entries so an IFRS 9 mismatch does not invalidate the example.

Pass criterion: exact rule support, coherent separate/group journal and balance
bridges, elimination with the right surviving result, and the cash exchange
effect distinguished from cash flows. Independently calculate with rational

incorrect profit/OCI treatment or an unbalanced statement veto the affected
example. Compilation and preservation check engineering only. No actual-company
compliance, probabilistic ranking, forecast validity or general tax/hedge result.

infer real instrument terms, use a stochastic model or acquire new sources.
