## Intent, evidence and default audit

Primary question: can one request resolve a hash-bound synthetic publication,
compute its original or restated columns from accounting inputs, and retain
correct correction origins and time eligibility? Primary criterion: exact
independently authored original/restated/current/opening statements, line-item
corrections, direct/indirect cash, equity bridge and publication-selection results.
The old dossier's independent monthly path is the arithmetic comparator; new
annual reconstruction must not calculate from its expected output columns.

Publication records are explicitly authored synthetic metadata, not issuer
filings or sourced real dates. Original2023 and2024 reports use their already
supplied authorizations31March2024/2025; synthetic publication/ingestion occur
on those dates. Discovery remains1April2025. The synthetic2025 report is
authorized31March2026, published2April2026 and ingested3April2026, and includes
restated2024 and the corrected opening. A latest-available query for2024 before
either required cutoff reaches the new edition must retain the original2024
edition. An explicit original-edition query remains possible after correction.
Neither ingestion nor discovery retroactively makes the restatement public.

The12/6-year lives, equal annual/monthly allocation, fixed fiscal deduction,
tax rate, cash income and capital are protected synthetic hypotheses, not new
defaults. Annual reconstruction is equivalent for the full years in scope;
partial years, estimate changes and new tax regimes require separate methods.
EPS scope remains a supplied private/non-public-filing/non-voluntary-EPS case
checked against IAS33.2–3. Public or voluntarily presented EPS refuses this
profile instead of silently omitting the required restatement. Materiality,
old-information availability, reasonable obtainability, practicability and
no-hindsight are separate supplied findings, never inferred from amounts.

Vetoes: changed/missing input bytes, wrong entity/currency/rule/year/edition,
unavailable publication or ingestion, unsupported EPS scope, missing correction
findings, swapped original/restated columns, wrong earliest opening date,
cash effects from noncash corrections and any requested posting. False
practicability needs another route; it does not authorize a silent fallback.
Report selection uses only editions that satisfy BOTH cutoffs. Counts, hashes,
page counts and speed explain evidence; none proves accounting applicability.

Continuation vetoes: inconsistent required source, corrupt protected inputs or
work beyond documentary authorization. Failed candidates trigger retained
repair; application/source gaps block only their dependent claims. No human
acceptance, production query engine, actual restatement advice, full EPS,
general comparative reconstruction or current-law compliance is concluded.

Pre-mortem: a latest-restated query could leak a2026 edition into a2025 view,
or use its corrected numbers while labeling it original. Another balanced
