express every retained full-intent eligibility check without invoking the
native lifecycle constructor? Parameterized sale/collection and ordered

Skeptical audit: MethodRequirements/3 combines consequences with precedence
invalid, unsupported, unresolved. The native lifecycle stops in a fixed order:
structural validity, unknown inputs, applicability, then operation-specific
eligibility. A flat conjunction may therefore change the public result when
several premises fail. Compare complete classification and earliest failed
stage, not only a Boolean conjunction. Native checks also include calendar-day
successor semantics that ordinary rational subtraction does not implement.

Inspect and map each source check: exact event population/types/provenance;
book/event chronology; unknown handling; approval; sealed cursor; claim absence
or existence and customer ownership; sale due date; assessment cursor, interval
nonemptiness, end-exclusive/assessment-date relation and period boundary.
Record what existing guarded predicates cover and what needs a typed binder or
explicit date operation. Keep native source as compatibility baseline, not
accounting authority. No caller-supplied Boolean may replace a computed check
without provenance and a declared trust boundary.

Primary criterion: source-anchored coverage of each rule and adversarial
multi-failure cases exposing any classification/order mismatch. Use complete
saved before-state/full-event records and native execution as comparator.
Veto unsupported source claims, altered protected files, invented date rules
or unknown-to-false coercion; repair before choosing implementation. Counts
are explanatory. No statistical ranking, real-source admission or E3 inference.

Deliver operation/guard map, deterministic probes, result and exact next plan.
Then implement the smallest ordered declarative gate plus typed binders only if
the audit establishes a faithful finite contract. Retain source traceability
and no-callback requirement. S6 held, E3 deferred. No agents, commits, installs,
