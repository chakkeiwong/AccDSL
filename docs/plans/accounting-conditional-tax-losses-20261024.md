explicit assumptions. A negative expression alone establishes neither refund
cash,carryforward eligibility,expiry nor a deferred-tax asset. The ready repair
is a separate conditional loss ledger with explicit rule premises and identified
allocations. It must not retrofit the frozen return method or invent an annual
close for the two-year synthetic window.

Question: can a finite pure loss ledger preserve supplied origin,scope,expiry
and allocations, conserve loss amounts across a contiguous interval, and keep
unknown eligibility unresolved? Positive tax arithmetic remains the selected
uniform-rate/no-credit assumption, with loss usage as an explicitly separate
deduction. Original signed reconciliation and all native results are retained.

lot usages and the permitted utilization amount for the interval with provenance.
Expiry is an exclusive date supplied for each lot, or an explicit no-expiry
assumption. The exact rule for expiry during an interval must be selected and
documented before implementation; do not silently choose a convenient date.
Opening loss inventory completeness and eligibility are explicit known/unknown
findings. A source-reference string still is not source admission.
