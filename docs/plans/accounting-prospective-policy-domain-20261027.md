# Declare a policy domain before completing temporal search

27 October2026. MC01/03/05/11/12/13/18/24/25/26/28/33/34;
W01/03/04/11/12/13/16. Product implementation remains held.

Question: can a source/actor declaration establish the policy's admissible message
keys before the complete temporal family is known,so partial-policy conclusions
no longer depend on a retrospectively supplied complete comparison? Baselines:
FixedDomainPartialPolicy/1 and BoundActorObservationPolicy/1. The former provides
an exact conditional comparison but obtains the full key universe from already
completed saved data; it is not an online discovery method.

First inspect declaration coverage: enumerate permitted source head/financial/view
combinations from the original finite source registry without selecting favorable
heads. Require stage-available keys and full action meaning declarations. An
alternative is a declaration indexed by all source heads,checked lazily when a
completed outcome requires it. Compare these approaches for whether unknown or
conflicting/retracted outcomes retain explicit unresolved status. Do not expand
raw actor histories with internal mechanism IDs or hidden financial facts.

Before implementation,write the exact contract and skeptical audit: the intended
universe may conservatively contain impossible combinations,but cannot omit any
later admissible combination. Domain extension changes the policy question unless
a checked extension relation preserves earlier restrictions. Existing declaration
fields are closed; define a successor profile rather than silently reinterpreting
them. Unknown timing/tag authorship remains unweighted and does not admit evidence.

Primary criterion: a universe declared from original inputs covers every policy
key discovered by complete exact temporal evaluation on independently checked
cases; partial/full policy sets agree under explicit projection/extension maps.
Hard vetoes: hindsight-derived keys presented as prospective,missing later keys,
unknown becomes zero,actor/source/time mismatch,changed domain reused,or dropped
pending support. Counts/runtime are explanatory only; no scalability,probability,
actual source authority,storage durability or production claim.

Defaults: finite six-revision,two-decision,pay/draw scope is inherited comparison
scope,not a general default. Keep maximum key/universe bounds explicit before
enumeration; under-budgeted larger domains remain unresolved. Cheap diagnostic:
a correction adds a previously unseen label,then a collision and an unresolved
source outcome. Compare direct finite enumeration with independent constraint
projection. At most three fresh120second CPU attempts after API/contract review.
Save plans,source locks,complete comparisons,failed attempts,manifests and explicit
decision/inference tables. Do not rerun successful evidence writers. No agents,
GPU,installs,fees,outreach,publication or E3. Continue live finance work and all18
broad packages. Tag-induced temporal refinement and general language/horizon,
scalable scheduling and durable recovery remain separate residuals.

Plan review: the question targets a material limitation of the accepted
retrospective comparison. An apparently correct complete result would not prove
prospective coverage; the universe must be constructed before inspecting the
complete-family outcome list. The exact declaration design remains an inspected
pre-implementation condition,not a promoted default.
