# Produce financial reports from the declared company book

25 October2026. S2–S5;MC02/06/11/13–18/23–26/28/32/34/35.

## Question and evidence contract

Can a declared reporting map turn two compatible CompanyBook/1 snapshots into
coherent balance-sheet,income,cash-flow and equity reports without guessing account
classification,source completeness or an unexplained balancing equity movement?
Comparator: independent debit-positive accounting arithmetic and selected native
report fields where opening/closing assumptions match. Prior generic effects and
native whole-lot adaptation supply complete event/state comparators.

Primary criterion: declared account partition covers every account once; exact
asset/liability/equity/profit and cash/equity reconciliations; equivalent reports
under two company charts; complete unchanged source books; explicit refusal of
unreconciled cash,equity,period or account populations. Veto: unexplained cash or
equity movement,classification guesses,dropped accounts,double counting,false
completeness or future-data claim. Counts/runtime and balanced trial balance alone
are explanatory. No standards compliance or complete note package follows.

## Skeptical and default audit before execution

First inspect existing native reports and portfolio/company reporting interfaces.
Their fixed charts and operation assumptions cannot silently become general rules.
Use an explicit reporting map and classify every account as already declared by
CompanyBook/1. Require a closed opening book for this first period-report profile:
opening income and expense balances are zero,with an explicit opening date. State
which current/equity signs follow from debit-positive storage. No rounding in
calculation. Reports may show signed income/expense amounts; no forced positive
amount convention should hide contra accounts.

Cash-flow activity is already supplied by the method's gross flows. Preserve the
opening prefix and use only subsequent flows; reconcile their total to cash change.
Do not infer operating/investing/financing from an account name. Equity movement
rows must be explicitly supplied with account,description and signed effect,
and reconcile to direct equity-account changes. Profit is computed separately
from income/expense balances; do not insert a residual equity plug.

The initial output is a report of the recorded conditional period. A balanced
report does not establish that all events occurred or that disclosures are complete.
Expose reporting cutoff,period and retained unknown/source-admission status. Notes
need explicit supported schedules; list unsupported/missing note requirements.

Closing/carry is a dependent extension after reporting passes: transfer temporary
income/expense balances to a named retained-equity account,then establish the next
period under an explicit carry contract. Do not reset method memory or tax clocks
without their declared policies. Archive the report/closing identity separately
if fixed memory cannot safely carry it. Audit the extension before implementation.

## Execution

1. Inspect current report contracts,write exact mapping and statement semantics,
   derive the independent reconciliations and document limits.
2. Implement the smallest data-only report reference;test two owner/chart layouts
   across multiple payments/sales and explicit direct-equity movements.
3. Compare complete supported native financial fields where timing assumptions
   agree;test all missing/overlapping map,cash,equity and period failures.
4. Record evidence and integrate checked report semantics;refresh full coverage.
5. Audit and implement explicit close/carry,then compose ordinary corporate methods
   and matched authoring toward complete recorded fiscal statements and notes.

Candidate/fixture failure triggers repair. Invalid indispensable baseline or a
material unresolved accounting choice blocks that dependent claim; continue other
ready work. S6 held,S7 deferred. No external engagement or forecast study authorized.
