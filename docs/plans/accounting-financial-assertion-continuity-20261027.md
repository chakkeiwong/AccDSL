# Bind assertion revisions to a persistent economic history

27 October 2026. MC04/05/08/10/11/12/13/18/24/25/26/28/33/34;
W01/02/03/04/11/12/13/16. Governing Version 5.42. Design evidence only.

Question: what explicitly supplied interpretation permits a revised financial
assertion to coexist with an earlier decision without replacing that decision's
economic state? The new joint source selector answers chronology only. The
existing numeric action rows contain a complete two-decision path for one receipt
and one right status; they cannot be sliced by selected source head and spliced
without checking state and timing. This is a material semantics dependency.

Audit first: inspect the retained numeric schedule and its independent recurrence.
Locate receipt settlement, first and second decisions, cash and principal/fee
carry. Distinguish (1) old report error followed by correction of the same event,
(2) a newly changed estimate before settlement, (3) an actual contractual change,
and (4) unresolved competing assertions. No default decides among these. Do not
use a later source payload to authenticate historical truth.

Ready bounded path: accept an explicit synthetic fixed-world declaration for the
economic receipt and rights, independently of the two source assertions. Preserve
both assertions and their clocks. Require a declared disposition explaining why
different payloads do or do not constrain that fixed world. Restrict the first
executable bridge to a supplied corrected-same-event interpretation; reject or
return unresolved for actual changes and unspecified truth. Carry one complete
certified financial row through both decisions and expose selected source content
only through an explicit actor-message declaration. No old/new numeric-row splice.

Evidence contract: first document exact counterexamples to careless splicing and
to intersecting mutually inconsistent corrected assertions as simultaneous facts.
Then compare a declared fixed-world execution against the protected complete-row
baseline and independent numeric recurrence. Primary criteria are full row/state
continuity and preservation of earlier messages/actions, not balancing totals.
Source-clock identity is necessary but not enough to establish financial truth.
Different supplied interpretations must not receive the same unexplained policy
answer. Unknown interpretation retains the unresolved original pair.

Before implementation, write a concrete input/result contract and assumptions
audit with no invented empirical facts. If a material actor-information choice
cannot be resolved from the existing model, state it and request direction while
continuing independent source/accounting work. No silent promotion of a convenient
message policy. At most three fresh 120-second CPU diagnostics after that audit,
under data/accounting-master/financial-assertion-continuity-20261027/. GPU hidden;
preserve source locks, failures, exact commands and result tables. No agents,
fees, publication, actual-company admission, defaults or E3 claim.
