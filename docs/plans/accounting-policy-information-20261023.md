## Skeptical audit and intent

Question: can a query distinguish the availability of a parent company path
from the availability of the separate rule that generates its settlements?
The comparator is CompanyInformationDraft/1 (103 checks) joined to the protected
SettlementPolicyDraft/1 (99 checks). The current generator deliberately returns
policy_information_not_admitted. Neither a parent's clock nor a child's dated
economic events establish when the policy was known.

The proposed increment binds timing declarations to the exact policy and the
exact locally trusted materializer implementation. Parent input availability
continues through the locked, semantically replayed exchange and existing
source/assumption selector. Recompute the complete materialization, including
every changed allocation and unchanged field. A child may be selected as a
derived synthetic consequence of eligible inputs; it must never receive a
fabricated authorship, publication, ingestion or acceptance timestamp. A claim
that a calculation actually existed at a past cutoff needs separate execution
evidence and is not supported by this increment.

Primary pass criterion: eligibility requires eligible parent information and
eligible policy/method observations under the same requested view/cutoff,
complete exact component binding and a recomputed materialization. A late
required component makes the query ineligible; missing/untrusted/ambiguous
required timing leaves it unresolved. Archival exploration can retain the
conditional calculation without temporal admission. Unknown policy values
remain unresolved and unsupported policy scope remains unsupported.

Promotion vetoes: trusting packet-supplied trust flags, reusing observations
after policy or implementation changes, inheriting parent dates for the child,
admitting private publication as public, treating a day as an instant, accepting
rehashed altered allocations/reports, or changing protected predecessor files.
Missing immutable baseline bytes is a continuation veto for dependent work.
A candidate failure triggers repair; it does not reject the simulator direction.
Check counts, runtime and report deltas are explanatory diagnostics. Passing
does not establish real source vintages, real author identity, historical
calculation existence, accounting compliance, forecast admission or accuracy.
