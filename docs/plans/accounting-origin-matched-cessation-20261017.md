## Intent and evidence contract

Mechanism: zero-value linear instruments, one per named item origin, with
quantity q_j, original date d_j and price f_j. The supplied zero-discount,
no-carry/credit/basis model gives G_j=q_j(S-f_j) and item-risk change -G_j.
At every supplied pre-stop measurement, active origin gains have the same weak
sign. Designated origins are individually eligible, managed together and have
proportional future variability; this is not an arbitrary net-position hedge.
The one relationship's total G and item change exactly offset. The complete
effective-gain record by documented origin therefore reconciles to its reserve.

At the cessation price S_d, stop quantity z from named origin k. Its trace is
z(S_d-f_k). Retain all actual instruments. Future undesignated gain is
z(S-S_d), while continuing reserve uses original quantities/prices minus z.
No new designation, acquired nonzero origin or historical ineffectiveness.
The remaining group qualifies. The stopped purchase is no longer highly
probable; whether it remains expected is a separate supplied fact.

Comparator: independently authored exact rational expectations and direct
terminal cash/equity/basis identities. Primary pass criterion: named origins
give15 versus10 in the counterexample, all chronological journals balance,
each date's derivative/reserve/profit/equity bridge agrees, and supplier and
derivative cash remain gross. Check retained/canceled cash flows, cancellation
after stopping, zero/negative/reversed gains, partial unrecoverable loss and
scaled quantities, prices and funding. Freeze expectations before reference code.

Promotion vetoes: wrong origin, changed continuing inception, disagreement with
the global lower-of, historical unmatched gain or shared ineffectiveness,
missing/false eligibility or matching findings, retrospective future facts,
opposing active-origin signs outside the finite scope, nonzero entry value,
invented cash finance, invalid journals or partial output on refusal.
These veto promotion of the method; an invalid source/math/artifact or missing
required diagnostic is a continuation veto for the dependent work only.
