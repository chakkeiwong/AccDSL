## Research intent and skeptical audit

Question: can the audited issuer observations feed existing time-aware joint
measurement semantics while preserving source identity, rounded precision and
missing native state? Comparator: frozen Henkel source audit plus the unchanged
TemporalMeasurementConstraints/1 and JointMeasurementConstraints/1 references.
The Apple source binder and synthetic USD lease engine are unsuitable issuer
baselines and must not be rebound or rewritten.

Candidate: a finite HenkelLeaseMeasurements/1 source adapter. Default output is
display-only/unresolved. Optional hypotheses propose nearest EUR-million
rounding with ties away from zero and selected exact unrounded relations.
They remain caller hypotheses, never source admissions. A printed '-0' retains
its literal but imposes no sign restriction beyond the explicit numeric rounding
hypothesis. Dashes have no numeric domain in this phase.

Primary criterion: closed request semantics, exact source/context/time binding,
faithful reuse of the existing temporal engine, full preservation of the104
observations and14 dependencies, and independently checked conditional witnesses
or rechecked infeasibility. Missing premises must remain unresolved. Mutated
inputs must fail closed. Check counts and solver runtime explain coverage only.

Promotion veto: source or comparator mutation, incorrect time/sign/context,
implicit exact rounded values, unknown-to-zero coercion, lost dependence or
false native admission. Repair trigger: schema/adapter or witness failure.
Continuation veto: corrupted source or irrecoverable comparator execution.
Conditional infeasibility rejects that declared measurement system, not the
issuer's accounts or the research direction. No actual unrounded amounts,
contract inventory, executable history, accounting compliance, scientific
ranking or predictive accuracy will be concluded.
