# Assess deferred tax on the actual mixed book

24 October 2026. S2–S5; MC05/11/13/16/24/26/28/35; G2/G4.

## Question and skeptical audit

Can an explicitly selected conditional model reconcile item-level tax bases and
recognition decisions to the shared 26-account deferred balances, and post only
their movement without changing cash, current tax, losses or the prior history?
The comparator is the frozen reassessed book, with DTA 10 and DTL 4, plus exact
independent rational calculations. The older native automatic reversal cannot
establish deferred recognition; copying balances through carry also cannot.

Before implementation, inspect the existing information schema, basis checks,
financial invariants and retained primary-source treatment. Separate asset and
liability tax-base signs; distinguish recognition from measurement and gross
balances from any permitted offset. Do not assume all movements belong in profit
or loss: require explicit support for the selected allocation, and refuse unsupported
equity/OCI or business-combination routes rather than silently misclassify them.
The implementation audit must identify the exact supported subset.

## Evidence contract and default audit

Explicit hypothetical rates and recovery/settlement bases remain assumptions.
No 25% default, automatic DTA, legal acceptance, event completeness or current-tax
completion follows from them. Unknown necessary findings stay unresolved; false
findings refuse the asserted method. A recognition ceiling, if used, must have
explicit units, scope and provenance and must not be double-counted across items.
Existing recorded opening balances need a complete item allocation or an explicitly
bounded alternative; no unexplained balancing residual is permitted.

Primary engineering criteria: complete basis population, item identity, rate and
recognition checks, allocation sums, exact incremental journal and book balance,
unchanged cash/current tax/loss history, complete predecessor binding and four
statement ties. Missing source support vetoes a legal claim; missing information
or an unsupported allocation vetoes that proposal. Counts and clean builds are
explanatory only. A failed candidate triggers a scoped repair; it does not reject
the simulator direction. No stochastic ranking or forecast claim is considered.

2. Define a pure proposal/apply contract and independent positive, negative, zero,
   unrecognized-asset, unknown, duplicate, sign and allocation cases.
3. Implement only the audited subset; test root causes before evidence freezing.
4. Record commands, environment, protected inputs and decisions; integrate the
   checked explanation in a protected successor and refresh full coverage.
5. Reassess next-period tax and repeated carry, event/source completeness and
   wider language/domain obligations without treating this increment as annual
