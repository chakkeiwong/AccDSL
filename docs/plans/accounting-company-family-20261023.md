## Question, audit and evidence contract

Can an author express several explicit scenarios for the same historical
company and retain every member's joint financial path through matched text
and typed inputs? The exact comparator is the existing CompanyPathTextDraft/1
and CompanyOperationPathDraft/1 execution of the retained six paths, including
the explicit collection-policy result. Reuse them; introduce no accounting
method, economic estimate or unrelated demonstration company.

The material baseline risks are mistaking a list of authored scenarios for a
reconstructed feasible-history family, or mistaking componentwise output ranges
for jointly attainable outcomes. This increment is explicitly an unweighted
set of authored exogenous scenarios. Each named member retains its entire
path, result and status. It asserts neither historical admissibility nor
exhaustive possible futures. No weights, averaging or probability assignment
are admitted. Equal economic results under different declarations remain
distinct named assumptions and do not imply extra probability mass.

Primary pass criterion: text and typed forms have identical canonical content;
every member replays to its protected standalone result; common opening and
reporting horizons agree; all members and their partial/unexecuted outcomes
remain visible. A requested joint report returns full report vectors by member,
including unavailable years. It never combines independently chosen extrema
into a fictitious company. Empty authored families remain empty selections,
not proof that no feasible history exists.

Promotion vetoes: duplicate member identity, changed historical opening or
context within one family, probability fields, silent member dropping, input
mutation, treating partial annual values as zeros, accepting rehashed forged
results without trusted recomputation, or changed protected predecessor bytes.
A malformed family is invalid before execution; an admitted member's failed
accounting execution is retained alongside its siblings. Missing immutable
input bytes is a continuation veto for dependent work. Candidate bugs trigger
repair, not abandonment of the simulator direction. Counts and runtime are
explanatory diagnostics. Passing does not establish a durable runtime, actual
source timing, historical reconstruction, accounting compliance or forecasting
accuracy.
