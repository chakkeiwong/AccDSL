The research question remains future operating-cash-flow predictive accuracy
for the same nine companies, next complete fiscal year, pre-start cutoff and
equal company contribution. This work maps the latest **retained** interim
balance sheets to candidate positive consolidated asset denominators. It does
not select origins or estimate predictive performance.

Skeptical audit passed with two limits made explicit: latest retained does
not mean latest publicly released, and a publication declaration does not
prove availability of the selected file bytes. Annual closing assets can be
too late for the proposed cutoff. Parent, segment and investment assets are
wrong denominators for a consolidated total-assets definition. No new runtime,
empirical campaign or retrospective score is needed to answer this source
question. The prior annual map is the comparator for units and group meaning,
not a requirement that annual and interim amounts agree.

material extraction/column traps in nine coverage-selected interim reports.

availability vetoes historical admission, not continued source investigation.

renormalisation is introduced. No forecast claim follows from this unit.
