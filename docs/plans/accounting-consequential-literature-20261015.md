# R04 — Consequential claim and literature reconciliation

15 October2026. Baseline Version3.00, MC23/26/28–30/35 and G8; also source
dependencies of MC08–22. Governing finite route: convergence-audit-20261015.

Question: does each consequential method claim have the inspected technical
support its strength requires, and have direct alternatives/corrections been
considered? Comparator is the actual current manuscript and six retained
ledgers, not the number of records. Existing checked support is reused.

Skeptical audit: ledger presence and citation-key matches do not establish
source faithfulness. Different claims from one paper need separate passages.
Historical metadata cannot establish today's correction status. Missing original
full text blocks that source claim; a later survey cannot impersonate it. A
local derivation can support the stated target without claiming the imported
paper proves it. Current accounting authority remains operation-specific.

1. Pin the candidate, six ledgers and consequential claim inventory. Reconcile
   reconstruction/provenance/language, identification/inference/causality,
   bank/insurance methods and extraction/tool claims to exact support records.
2. Inspect the actual manuscript claims and retained source passages for any
   mismatch or unexamined consequential claim. Repair source/claim boundaries
   directly; preserve blocked original sources and historical ledger entries.
3. Review source-specific backward/forward and omission decisions; identify
   direct competitors, corrections and the actual next source lead. Do not
   re-download a known blocked source without a distinct promising path.
4. Write a claim-by-claim disposition and honest completion decision; update
   six ledgers only with actually inspected supplemental evidence. Any needed
   manuscript change enters a protected successor. Then continue R05/R06 and
   named R03 design repairs, keeping R07/R08 as final assessment dependencies.

Pass criterion: explicit supported/local-derived/context/blocked disposition
with correct technical anchors and competitors. Unsupported theorem transfer,
inconsistent target/measure, or missing material correction vetoes the claim
and triggers repair. Source absence does not stop independent reconciliation.
No empirical experiment, runtime, installation, review agent or paid engagement.
Output: data/accounting-master/consequential-literature-20261015/.
