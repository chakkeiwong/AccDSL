## Evidence contract and skeptical audit

Primary pass: identify and retain an authentic official ASU 2016-13 edition,
hash it, inspect its cover/status, relevant ASC 326-20 off-balance paragraphs,
scope exceptions, measurement and presentation passages, and effective-date
language. Record exact PDF page and paragraph spans, and distinguish source
text from local consequences for the bank specification. If the official text
is inaccessible, a precise transport receipt and no admitted claim is a
valid negative result; move to another feasible queue after one bounded new
route. Investigate later amendments only from concrete references found in
the official source or a separately verified lead.

Veto admission on uncertain issuer, wrong edition, missing/ambiguous paragraph,
unreadable amendment typography, or a claim that later text was checked when
it was not. The explanatory diagnostic is whether the issuer policy resembles
the historical amendment; similarity is not a promotion criterion. The
promotion boundary is a historical source reading only. Current operative
authority needs a verified change chain and Codification applicability; no
forecast, journal or legal conclusion follows from locating a PDF.

Default audit: the proposed official storage URL follows the already retained
FASB ASU 2020-06 path convention and is a locator hypothesis, not a trusted
source identity. A response must pass PDF signature, cover, publisher, issue
date and bounded paragraph checks. The 2016 edition is a historical baseline,
not a current-law default. Its examples may assume particular instruments;
check scope/exceptions before applying them to 2025 JPMorgan populations.

Skeptical audit passes because the question, baseline and stop conditions are
specific. The largest misleading-success risk is treating a genuine historical

authority statement and an amendment/version register before any application.
No long experiment, runtime, model, GPU or new default is involved.
