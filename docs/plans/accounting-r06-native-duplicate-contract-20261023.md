## Question and evidence contract

Does the current proposed native-XBRL importer preserve source occurrences and
keep duplicate reduction, Formula processing and financial admission separate
in the ways required by the retained 2018, Inline and Formula findings?

The exact baseline is Version 3.48's current architecture adjuncts and selected
reuse method, including their pinned documentary packets and profiles. Compare
them with the completed source inquiries, not with an installed processor or
an imagined future implementation. Primary completion requires an exact
clause-to-source comparison covering occurrence identity, content sharing,
assertion candidates, permitted reductions, Formula binding/evaluation and
financial admission. For each boundary record either a confirmed contradictory
clause, a supported preservation rule, or an unimplemented/unspecified consumer
obligation. A search miss alone cannot prove an absent rule.

Protected-byte corruption, invalid evidence or an action still held is a
continuation veto for that dependent action. A missing future consumer contract
is a repair/implementation obligation, not evidence that the current
occurrence-preserving importer is wrong. Text matches, exact field counts and
illustrative arithmetic explain the audit; they cannot establish conformance,
semantic equivalence, full filing meaning or runtime behavior. Preserve the
result in a comparison ledger, authored boundary examples, decision note,
author review, run manifest, additive R08 supplement and read-only verifier.

## Skeptical audit and defaults

The plan passes with these explicit limits. The selected first importer disables
Formula; it is therefore a wrong baseline to demand a Formula engine from it.
AccDSL ReportedAssertion is an evidence candidate, not a Formula assertion data
set. An equal numeric value, shared content hash, contextual assertion alias or
source-document count cannot stand in for node identity or a reduction proof.
Different consumer rules must not be combined into a universal duplicate key.
The final Calculations 1.1 rule and its unresolved sections remain separate.

## Pre-mortem and acceptance limits

This audit could pass by quoting preservation prose while ignoring an actual
fixture that silently aliases occurrences. Inspect the selected and full-file
record inventories and correspondence rules, not only their summaries. It
could also fail for the wrong reason by asking the source importer to implement
all Formula, Inline or calculation consumers. Keep source preservation and each
consumer's future admission distinct. An information-losing operation needs a
declared target and checked equivalence on the observations it promises to
preserve; human review alone is not a mathematical equivalence proof.
