# Group, bank and insurance composition contract

25 October 2026. S2–S4; MC04/06/07/08/10–13/16/17/19–22/24–28/30/32–35.

Question: can the finite group, banking and insurance methods share one
company-world boundary without flattening sector-specific state, source
conditions or alternative histories into an unsupported common chart?

Baseline evidence is deliberately separated. CompanyComposition/1 attempt-03
contains a closed synthetic parent/subsidiary world, 681 independent checks,
two explicit migrations and one subsequent transition per migration. Bank and
insurance dossiers contain conditional method cases and inspected sources, but
their residual audits explicitly leave broader operations, calibration,
jurisdictional rules, actuarial uncertainty and source-specific admission open.
None is a general production engine or a license to merge incompatible state.

Skeptical audit before implementation:

- Verify the P5 packet, validation and input hashes against its manifest without
  rerunning or rewriting frozen evidence.
- Compare its world/request/receipt boundary with current CompanyBook/2 and
  conditional history provenance. Identify what can be reused as an adapter
  contract and what must remain a separate profile.
- Inventory bank and insurer required state: deposits/credit/allowance/capital;
  contract groups/claims/service-finance/reinsurance. Record source and law
  conditions for each method.
- Test the proposed common envelope with empty, mismatched, duplicate,
  cross-context and unresolved sector components. It must refuse an untyped
  merge and retain each component's own account, memory, time and evidence.

Smallest implementation: a closed CompanyWorld/1 envelope containing
identified legal books, ownership/control edges, sector profile, source/view
references and conditional component handles. It performs no consolidation,
tax, FX, bank or insurance posting. A second bounded adapter may join two
already compatible company books using an explicit elimination ledger; sector
methods remain callable only through their own typed component contracts.

Promotion requires exact envelope identity, context/perimeter checks, no
cross-sector field collision, preserved component provenance, and immutable
refusal. A balanced aggregate, component count or inherited P5 check is
explanatory only. Source admission, accounting compliance, sector completeness,
general consolidation and production durability remain open. No forecast study
or statistical ranking is involved.

Stages: artifact audit; envelope schema and refusal tests; one compatible
parent/subsidiary handle case; exact replay and text/provenance checks; bounded
manuscript integration; refresh 53/6/46 coverage. Continue to sector-specific
method bridges only after this boundary survives the audit.
