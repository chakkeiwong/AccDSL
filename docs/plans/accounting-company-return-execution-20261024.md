# Native shared-book return reference execution

S3/S4; MC08/10/13/18/24/25/28/34. This is executable design conformance,
not the held S6 production engine or the deferred S7 forecast study.

## Question and evidence contract

Can the new authoring record drive exact return settlement, recovered-lot sale,
current/deferred tax, annual legal statements and next-year memory in one
company book? Baseline: the protected P legal-book trace in retained
CompanyComposition attempt-03, all 13 dated transitions, its journals and
gross cash, and the governing native-return specification. Six existing
company text paths remain protected and replayed.

Use new independent return/sale and P tax calculations. For other corporate
events reuse the retained checked method through an explicit adapter; their
saved expected journals must never be implementation inputs. This hybrid is
a reference composition, not native implementation of every corporate method.
Compare complete P balances, journal rows and gross cash after every event,
including no-P-change events. Keep S and group state in the retained adapter;
do not move group cash into P's legal statements.

Pass criteria: all trace comparisons exact; lot and account allocations hold;
current tax increments are 8 and 2.5, deferred expense 6; legal annual profit
49.5, cash 491.5 and equity 671.5; direct and indirect operating cash 101.5.
Annual carry resets income/period cash, retains source/lot/component history
and lifetime event identities, and cannot replay the -24 adjustment next year.
Missing/false findings, stale/changed proposals, duplicate events, cash
shortfall and incomplete tax close must not publish a complete transition or
annual opening. Earlier valid prefixes remain inspectable.

## Skeptical audit and assumptions

The wrong baseline would be the 2024 module wallet, Northstar, or a group
report. Use original P and 2025. A balanced journal alone is insufficient:
compare full states and classified gross cash. Source hashes bind local bytes,
not external authenticity. A retained method used for surrounding events is
explicit reuse, not an independent oracle for those same events. New return
and P tax methods have an independent retained comparator. The finite report
uses declared account classifications and opening-interest cash treatment;
do not infer a general reporting framework from this case.

Defaults: exact rational arithmetic is the established compatibility choice;
the account registry comes from the retained shared book; whole-lot sale and
the synthetic 1/4 tax premises are finite baseline scope. Opening asset/liability
and deferred-tax allocations must match the typed record. Missing source
findings are unresolved, false ones unsupported, known contradictions invalid.
The first cheap checks are source/opening binding and the settlement journal;
the next are second tax-close de-duplication and the opening-interest bridge.

## Execution and stop rules

1. Build explicit shared-state, account registry and pure proposals with
   caller-bound before/event/method identities; verify again when applying.
2. Add return settlement, lot sale and cumulative tax updates. Test failure
   atomicity and preserve failed evidence before consequential repairs.
3. Replay the 13-step mixed-method company trace, then derive classified legal
   statements and annual carry. Test missing future facts and no full carry
   after a partial prefix. Refresh the manuscript only after this group passes.

Promotion veto: any comparator, invariant, identity or evidence-preservation
failure. Repair trigger: a failed candidate caused by a known implementation
or scope defect. Continuation veto: indispensable corrupted source/comparator
or a material domain choice unresolved by governing evidence. A missing
production engine or pending human review is not a continuation veto here.
Explanatory diagnostics are check counts and timing, never speed superiority.
No current-law, actual-company admission, durability or forecast inference.
