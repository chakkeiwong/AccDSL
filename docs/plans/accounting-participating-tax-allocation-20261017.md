## Question and skeptical audit

Specify basic EPS when participating-equity distributions create a tax benefit
whose rights belong to ordinary holders. This is the concrete omission exposed
by the June 2017 IFRS Interpretations Committee IAS 33 decision and complete
five-page accompanying example, acquired in the preceding source investigation.
It belongs to the original participating-rights obligation; it is not a new
empirical campaign or a solution to nonadditive diluted selection.

The comparator is the source's three-period numerical example, including its
negative third-period residual, and an independent direct current-period
allocation. A calculation based only on dividends paid would miss the first
period's benefit and double count earlier hypothetical allocations in later
periods. A blanket ban on a negative residual would incorrectly reject the
source's third period. Conversely, assuming a tax benefit on every tax loss or
every instrument would exceed the source. Distinguish actual accounting profit,
EPS numerator adjustments and hypothetical allocations; this method posts no
dividend, tax receivable, cash or journal.

Audit passes for a finite two-class, fixed proportional-rights profile with
ordinary-only benefit rights, explicit actual benefit recognition location,
complete supplied dividend/tax findings and positive ordinary denominator.
The finite payout profile follows the published example: base dividends follow
the fixed class ratio, and each actual participating-dividend tax benefit is
distributed to ordinary holders in the same period. Opening undistributed base
profit funds dividends relating to earlier periods. Current base profit and
opening base balance are nonnegative; a negative current residual after payout
is allowed. Retaining the actual tax benefit needs a separate allocation of
that ordinary-only reserve and is not silently passed through proportional
residual sharing. This choice is a source-example boundary, not a company default.
General tax-benefit sharing, capped/senior rights, loss-DTA recognition,
nonadditive dilution, operative-law admission and actual contract classification
remain separate. Do not change a frozen predecessor to hide these limits.

## Evidence contract and defaults

Primary criterion: reproduce all three published ordinary earnings numerators
(120, 200, 40), conserve allocation at each period, and agree with independently
derived current-period entitlements for permitted payout histories. Test actual
benefits recognized in profit versus equity and ensure the same EPS numerator
without treating those accounting balances as identical. Include zero dividend,
zero rate, zero participation, full payout and prior-period payout cases.

Hard vetoes: inconsistent actual dividends/benefits, missing rights or tax
findings, unsupported sharing/classification/loss facts, stale source or future
information, invalid dates or denominator, partial output after refusal. A
failed source reproduction vetoes the method and triggers local repair. A source
gap blocks only its dependent extension. No successful arithmetic check proves
current legal applicability, production readiness, forecast quality or human
comprehension.

Defaults: the source's 10:1 ratio and 30% rate are published example inputs,
not company defaults. Any other exact rational parameters are authored
synthetic cases. The historical decision/AASB/EU editions support the stated
principle, not all jurisdictions. Fixed rights, tax recognition facts and
denominator are supplied/admitted findings; no model estimates them. Canonical
rational arithmetic avoids an invented monetary tolerance. Runtime, controller,
installations, empirical work, publication, outreach and fees remain held.
