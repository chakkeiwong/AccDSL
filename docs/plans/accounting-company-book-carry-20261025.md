## Question and exact comparator

named retained-equity account,advance the period and preserve or reset memory only

## Skeptical/default audit

CompanyBookReport/1 explicitly lacks event completeness and complete notes. Its
success cannot authorize an admitted fiscal close. Therefore the new reference is
provisional recorded-balance carry with fiscal_complete:false and all admission
flags false. Require a closing snapshot dated the last included day of its period
as a structural timing rule;this does not prove no event was omitted. Do not fill
missing intervening dates or pretend a midperiod example is an annual record.

Inspect native_reports.carry:it also resets opening_interest_paid,advances tax
closed-until,updates opening DTA/DTL and records prior-year identity. The generic
method must not guess those rules. Require an exact policy for every current
memory leaf:preserve,reset to an explicit typed literal,or set to next_period_start
for a date leaf. Each policy has a stated rationale/provenance. Apply only scalar
replacements through the existing memory engine;fixed population remains enforced.
Unknown reset values or unsupported policies refuse. Snapshot equality alone cannot
justify business correctness of a declared reset;record policy identities.

Closing journal has -b for every income/expense account and sum(b) for the named
retained-equity account. Since profit=-sum(b),the retained credit increases by
profit. Journal sum is zero;cash flow is zero. Next book begins at prior period's
exclusive end,ends at a supplied later date,clears current-period flows,retains
lifetime seen IDs plus close ID,and archives the prior report/state externally in
the result. No hidden mutable archive is inserted into the fixed memory schema.
