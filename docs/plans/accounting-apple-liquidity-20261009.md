## Skeptical audit and assumption ledger

The plan passes after these corrections to the naive approach. Annual cash-flow
cash has a wider label than balance-sheet cash; compare their endpoint populations
before bridging. The securities table says **adjusted cost**, not a uniform
amortised-cost account for every investment. Total unrealised gains/losses include
more than the net-of-tax marketable-debt OCI line; endpoint changes cannot be
equated to that period flow. Net purchases/maturities/sales do not identify book
disposals, reclassifications or currency/measurement movements. Receivable
concentrations use separate trade and vendor denominators, and customer/carrier
membership is unknown. Do not infer individual names or stable ranks across dates.
