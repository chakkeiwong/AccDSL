# Preserve exact credit journals in an optional single-root book

25 October 2026. S2-S5; MC08/10/11/13/16-18/24-28/32-35.
Baseline: accepted CreditLinkedSchedule/1 and the host-boundary diagnostic.
CompanyBook/1, its transitions and reporting remain unchanged. This is a

## Skeptical audit and research intent

Question: can all nine supplied schedules enter a separate proposed book trace
without losing dependence, native journal order, economic state or unrelated
rational balances? A rational-only path omits six cases. An optional
CreditLinkedBook/1 has one native root, complete host context, explicit role
mapping, and a one-to-one illustrative CU-to-XCU alias. It has no implicit
rounding, ordering, general polynomial inverse or multi-root composition.

The audit passes for the finite projection. A new balance dictionary alone
would discard economic state: bind and retain the full exact/native request
and result, origins, expected cash, write-off portions and stage identities.
The stage-to-journal prefix must be derived from validated native events,
including stages with no additional journal and terminal clearing journals.
The proposed trace remains read-only and supplies no posting authority.

Opening is a validated synthetic rational CompanyBook/1 before acquisition.
Cash equals native opening_cash; gross, allowance and all three income roles
are zero. Allowance is a signed contra asset, classified asset. Interest,
impairment and catchup are income roles with signed balances, allowing losses.
Retain all unrelated rational balances and all memory unchanged. Opening
temporary income/expense balances must be zero so cumulative native profit
has an unambiguous comparator. Account mapping must be exact and injective.

## Evidence contract and assumptions

Primary pass: nine cases, two charts, with/without equipment17 and matching
opening capital; all63 native journals and57 stage observations preserved per
chart/complement family. Every signed entry and zero entry must survive.
Every journal and complete book sums to certified zero. At every stage compare
cash, gross, signed allowance and all income roles with the exact schedule;
also compare assets=liabilities+equity, profit and cash change with native
targets. The three rational cases must project to the unchanged host's
validated books. A complete unchanged host report also requires classified
cash flows: inspect and record that prerequisite rather than fabricating a
classification. Compare cash changes to the retained native cash origins.
This is an exact equality comparison, not a performance benchmark. Numerical
enclosures remain in the bound native layer.

Report scope: cumulative balance sheet and profit, plus exact native cash
movements. No operating/investing/financing classification is invented. The
native price/cost/fee components remain distinct even where their net journal
is combined. No annual closing or note completeness is claimed.

Defaults: native root/economic assumptions come from the accepted schedules;
the host alias is a supplied synthetic convention and scale1/1; intervals
cover original native dates and are explicitly cumulative; two charts and
equipment17 are constructed isolation tests. Algebra budgets are inherited
and remain explicit unsupported outcomes. No ordering is needed to sum signed
classes. Any inequality, unknown root, different rate or foreign context is
unsupported. Limits: at most128 journals and128 observations, with a bounded
serialized request/result; do not weaken existing host limits.

Expected failures: wrong allowance sign, skipped zero entry, same-date event
reordering, forged/rehashed schedule, alias mismatch, lost complementary
memory, incomplete role maps, nonzero temporary opening, date truncation,
uncertified equality, unsupported multi-root/ordering operation. These veto
candidate acceptance and trigger bounded repair, not rejection of the research
direction. Changed protected evidence vetoes dependent execution.
