## Skeptical audit and evidence contract

IAS 33.35 requires consequential profit-sharing/tax changes, whereas 41–44
require individual-series screening and an ordering by earnings per incremental
share. The inherited P80 example has individually antidilutive instruments but
a lower combined-world EPS. A raw subset minimum cannot silently supply the
accounting answer. Nor does the additive ordering proof establish an algorithm
when one issue changes another issue's earnings effect.

Inspect the actual primary accompanying examples and any identified technical
interpretation for multi-series ordering. First inspect retained IAS 33.33–57
again. Follow the official AASB 133 standards/compilation route for the
accompanying IAS 33 examples; an exact URL hypothesis is a discovery request,
not authority until its body, title, edition and technical content are checked.
Use a retained original or original-author method if one supplies the missing
selection argument. Do not install software or run an empirical campaign.

Primary criterion: identify a source-supported method for the declared
nonadditive family, or a precise unresolved boundary with a discriminating
counterexample. Compare fixed standalone ranking, recomputed marginal ranking,
and admissible-subset enumeration against every applicable source condition.
Missing authority, ambiguous precedence or an incompatible example vetoes that
method claim; a source gap does not stop independent ready work. Citations,
successful downloads and lower EPS alone are explanatory diagnostics.

Defaults: historical AASB/EU sources are historical support, not current issuer
law. Bonus/tax terms are supplied synthetic facts. The two-series family is a
tractable test, not all financial instruments. No source silence establishes
permission or a unique accounting rule. Inspect method/example details and
retain exact local copies/anchors before claiming support.

Stop this source unit when the official compilation/examples and identified
technical leads have a recorded disposition. Preserve failed requests without
repeated anonymous retries. If a method remains unsupported, keep final EPS
unresolved and continue the next source-independent original method. This
unit cannot turn independent/domain/human acceptance into an internal-work
blocker or authorize external engagement.
