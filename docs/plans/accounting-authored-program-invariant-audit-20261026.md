# Audit cross-record invariants in authored programs

26 October 2026. MC02/08/15/20/24/25/33/34; S2/S3/S4.

Question: which economic-state invariants enforced by the retained receivable
lifecycle remain outside the authored method/registry/program path? Baseline is
declared_receivable_lifecycle_v1.control, its input/specialization checks, and
the complete native histories used by the authored program comparison.

Skeptical audit: equal successful traces cannot establish equal refusal scope.
The typed state checks shape, dimensions and finite map structure, but do not
necessarily check claim totals, event membership or assessment chronology.
Probe those boundaries directly before adding syntax. Do not call an authored
algorithm economically correct merely because its journal balances. A deliberate
wrong algorithm may be syntactically valid; the question is whether a declared
company invariant can reject it consistently.

Inventory outer checks and classify their semantic owner: book invariant,
event prerequisite, generation/specialization requirement, report/carry control,
or source/accounting finding. Create bounded derived counterexamples with exact
changed fields and preserved baseline. Reuse MethodCollections/1 and
MethodCollectionTotals/1 where they express the rule; inspect limits rather
than adding parallel arithmetic. Record unsupported relations explicitly.

Primary criterion: full coverage map and native-versus-authored verdicts for
each tested relation, with no invented actual-company fact. A successful example
is explanatory only. Corrupted baseline or an ill-formed mutation vetoes the
probe. A genuine semantic gap triggers a separately specified binding/guard
successor, not a hidden patch to frozen results or abandonment of the mission.
