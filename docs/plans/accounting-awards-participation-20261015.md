# R03-H2 — award conditions, settlement choice and participation

15 October 2026. Protected Version 3.05. MC13/16/19/20/24–28/30/32–35.
Documentary specification and exact examples only.

Question: how do service, non-market and market conditions, settlement rights,
and participating shares affect recognition and per-share allocation? Compare
the current fixed equity award/EPS lifecycle with distinct condition and cash
choice branches. Do not collapse a liability into equity because shares are
available, or assume an EPS instrument is dilutive because it exists.

Skeptical audit before execution: grant-date valuation and quantities are
different inputs; failed market versus service conditions have different
effects. Cash remeasurement cannot use a frozen equity grant value. Holder
choice differs from issuer choice. EPS depends on actual participation rights
and allocation, not only the headline share count. Source edition and local
applicability are assumptions, not current-law certification.
