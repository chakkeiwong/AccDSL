# Mix native economic events with current-state decisions

25 October2026. S2–S4;MC13/14/15/16/17/18/23–26/28/32/34/35.

## Question and evidence contract

Can one authored timeline preserve native accounting operations while scheduled
decisions respond to their effects on the current company book? The existing
decision scheduler assumes no separately supplied economic events. A company
timeline must account for explicit cash movements and recognition before the
next decision reads cash or stock.

Primary comparator: unchanged AnnualInventoryProgram/1 transitions interleaved
with unchanged StateDecisionEvent/1 requests, assembled manually in declared
order. Primary criteria are exact operation/request,complete transition,state,
financial statements and independent cash/inventory/tax-control arithmetic.
At least one native cash payment must change a cash-sensitive decision, and
reversing an explicitly authored same-day order must change the expected outcome
under that rule. Exact reconciliation alone cannot establish decision behavior.

Veto: timing mismatch, ambiguous same-day priority, duplicate identities, stale
initial book, missing refusal prefix, malformed supplied operation or changed
pinned source. Counts/runtime are explanatory. An unknown native finding remains
unresolved; a false finding remains unsupported. No synthetic default dates,
plug journals, tax law, external funding access or historical truth is invented.

## Skeptical/default audit and staged scope

Read native operation dispatch before choosing adapters. A direct event has a
date; current assessment has activity_through/end_exclusive; deferred measurement
has at and a state hash; period carry names the next-period boundary. These clocks
are not interchangeable. Initial support should explicitly list the operation
shapes whose dispatch dates are unambiguous and retain unsupported outcomes for
the rest. Do not rewrite measurement snapshots to make stale assessments pass.

The initial finite test may use allocated payments of carried tax vintages,
which are already native and dated, alongside inventory decisions. This shows
cash and tax-history interaction without guessing a borrowing method. Broader
financing, assessments and period crossing remain planned extensions, not silently
omitted requirements. No production implementation or general scheduler claim.

Authored scenario and explicit priorities are inherited hypotheses. Bound total
timeline actions and validate all calendar/type structure before execution.
Semantic native refusal stays at the action where its actual state is known;
do not pre-execute a later event on the wrong initial book merely to validate it.

## Execution

1. Inspect native event shapes and clocks; record supported/unsupported adapters
   and the exact cash-sensitive rule with units and availability.
2. Specify a finite mixed calendar with same-day priority, identity collisions,
   operation-specific dates and current-state binding for decisions.
3. Implement an optional reference; compare complete manually interleaved paths,
   order effects, refusals, tax vintage conservation and all financial fields.
4. Preserve evidence/manifest, integrate only checked semantics and refresh the
   full mission. Then reassess larger architecture gaps before another adapter.

Repair trigger: candidate or fixture failure. Continuation veto: indispensable
baseline invalidity or unresolved material human decision. No global blocker
known. Forecasting remains deferred.
