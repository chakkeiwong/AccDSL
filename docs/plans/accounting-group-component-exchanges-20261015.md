## Skeptical audit and assumptions

The audit passes after correcting the following risks before implementation:

- /1 cannot carry indices, reciprocal allocations or exchangeability. Use a new
  namespace and schema lock; never mutate /1 or any frozen predecessor.
- Balance elimination uses reporting-date control; performance elimination uses
  transaction control intervals. A pre-control sale is not retroactively erased.
  Amount equality cannot establish counterpart identity. Preserve unallocated
  differences and monetary FX differences rather than force a balanced match.
- Inventory release needs the prior eliminated lot and external-sale reference.
  It cannot recognize the original profit again or lose the remaining group cost.
- Restate before translation. Derive monetary effects from dated interval exposure,
  not a balancing plug. Purchasing-power cash reconciliation is not cash payment.
- IFRIC7 remeasures opening deferred tax before indexing it. Partial goodwill
  uses admitted notional goodwill, never a silent ownership-based valuation.
  Corporate-asset group tests use already impaired unit values and asset floors.
- Exchangeability is dated, directional and purpose-specific. The significance
  finding and an orderly-market rate remain supplied admissions, not mechanical
  thresholds or estimates produced by this packet. Initial application uses
  opening retained earnings or accumulated translation differences separately.
- Acquisition inside-basis and investment outside-basis tax are different tests.
  Missing law/control/probability findings remain unknown. No legal payment is
  created by a consolidation worksheet.
