# Make mixed-book tax information requirements executable

24 October 2026. S2–S4; MC05/11/13/16/24/26/28/35; G2/G4;
P17-04.10 and P17-04.11.

## Skeptical audit and research intent

Direct native tax reuse failed for identified reasons, while two original tax
controls still pass. The next repair must first express the missing facts, not
invent lease tax treatment or widen the old account list. This work defines a
finite information-readiness assessment for a separately declared tax interval.
Readiness is engineering completeness under explicit premises; it is not tax
assessment, tax-law admission, annual close or authorization to post cash.

Question: can a closed request identify every missing mixed-book tax premise,
preserve exact supplied evidence and distinguish unresolved facts from malformed
or incompatible input? Baseline is the complete saved mixed state and the tax
compatibility diagnosis, including the two-year window and original controls.
Primary criterion is correct missing-fact/unsupported classification with full
input preservation. Diagnostics of counts or successful parsing are explanatory.
Silent assumed tax bases, loss-to-refund conversion, acceptance of mismatched
intervals or a false/unknown finding as true veto this implementation.

## Proposed information contract to inspect and implement

Use the existing state/report and prerequisite tools where they preserve the
contract. Keep the pinned tax method and authoring reference unchanged. Before
implementation, inspect reusable readiness/requirement records and document
the exact choice in the new package.

1. Bind the unchanged shared state and full event/report evidence to an explicit
   tax interval inside the declared execution window. Keep as-of state time,
   inclusive last activity and exclusive tax endpoint distinct. Do not require
   the exclusive endpoint to precede the last day it closes.
2. Require explicitly identified tax jurisdiction/regime, period coverage,
   rate evidence and the treatment of all pretax account categories. Preserve
   return adjustment history and require explicit current-tax reconciliation
   coverage. Missing facts remain unknown, including facts after the last event.
3. Represent lease-right tax bases and deduction/gain findings explicitly,
   including rights removed during the period. A closing balance alone cannot
   describe the tax treatment of the earlier modification. Retain zero balances
   and terminated rights rather than deriving the population from closing assets.
4. Treat loss/refund/carryforward treatment and deferred-tax recognition/recovery
   as separate facts. Supplied declarations are assumptions unless source
   admission exists; readiness never creates that admission or an annual flag.
5. Test complete original missing-fact cases, explicit false/unknown premises,
   forged identity, missing/extra coverage, closed schemas and interval edges.
   Positive fixtures are clearly hypothetical information-completeness controls,
   with no numerical tax or legal result. Record all results, refresh controls,
   then design the smallest calculation successor justified by the facts.

No actual facts, probabilities or legal rules may be invented. Missing actual
facts block their tax assessment, not the internal representation of requirements.
