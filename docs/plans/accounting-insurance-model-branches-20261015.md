# R03-O — insurance modification, service models and finance

15 October 2026. Successor to R03-N. MC13/15/18–20/24–28/30/32–35.
Documentary methods and deterministic illustrative calculations only.

Question: which remaining insurance procedures differ when an agreement is
modified, qualifies for direct participation or premium allocation, or has
financially variable benefits? Comparator: the current GMM/partial-treaty,
fixed-claim OCI and finite guarantee methods. Preserve a separate ledger for
actual valuation/process-law assumptions; supplied prices cannot close it.

Audit before execution: a modification is not an estimate change or exercise
of an existing right. A new contract's equivalent premium is not the removed
carrying amount. VFA is not a free model election, and a fund-linked payment
alone does not prove all eligibility conditions. Changes in the entity's
share, policyholder obligation and future-service costs must not be counted
twice. PAA's LRC financing and LIC discount exemptions use different clocks.
Investment-component repayment is not insurance revenue or service expense.
OCI allocation is constrained by the admitted model and portfolio policy.
The source-supported branch order and independent balance bridges are the
earliest checks against these errors. The plan passes for conditional methods;
unexamined valuation or contractual assumptions remain visible dependencies.

Primary evidence: inspected IFRS17.45–59,72–77,87–92 and B101–B118,B126–B136,
plus the relevant cash-flow/discount/RA clauses. Exact CSM, FCF, cash, investment
component, profit and OCI derivations must reconcile. A wrong source branch,
duplicate financial effect, missing disposal adjustment or unsupported law is
a case veto and repair trigger. Missing actual contract/pricing/calibration
blocks application only; it does not stop independent method specification.
No actuarial superiority, actual insurer compliance, current-law completeness,
runtime conformance or full readiness follows from these cases.

Default audit: no tax/FX and few service dates are case simplifications for
isolating timing and measurement. Rates, risk adjustment, expected benefits,
equivalent premiums and service quantities are explicit hypotheses/inputs,
not calibrated defaults. Historical retained authority does not supply later
law. Review sign conventions and expiry/settlement order before arithmetic.
