## Question, evidence contract and skeptical audit

Can an implementer recover the named financing, bank and group transitions from
the closed input/state/source records, including settlement, annual statements,
adverse admission and exact replay? Compare against all relevant P17-05/06 rows
in the preserved 46-row coverage register and the R03-A/C/D/H/I/K/M/N methods.
The old closed profiles are compatibility comparators; their narrow examples
are not the denominator of completion.

Primary pass criterion: a declared finite method has closed input/component
types, bound authority and source bytes, independently derived state and journal
effects, complete authorize/propose/commit/report exchanges and named adverse
paths. A remaining method or authority dependency stays explicit and prevents
wholesale row closure. Missing references, wrong source/date/book, mismeasured
service or reserve, duplicated economic effects, wrong statements, changed replay
identity or partial publication veto the affected example and require repair.
Corrupt protected inputs/index or an unclear accounting derivation veto further
execution of that dependent unit. A refused candidate does not stop independent
ready units. Counts, schema passes and balance alone explain integrity; they do
not establish accounting validity, runtime durability or full mission readiness.

Audit finding: the corporate profit/OCI equity bridge is wrong for these inputs.
Issue consideration and award equity credits must be separate equity movements;
basis transfers remove reserves directly without becoming OCI reclassifications.
An award's expense and equity credit leave total equity unchanged. EUR net hedges
cannot inherit hardcoded USD. Measure cumulative hedge effectiveness before a
prospective ratio change; preserve actual gross transactions and earning periods.
Do not treat later share delivery as a new amendment-date measurement. Do not
merge historical Australian annual-start limits with the separate EU compilation.
These are mandatory repairs before using the generic exchange machinery.

The audit permits reuse of canonical transport, immutable reference and atomic
world conventions after exact checks. It rejects a free-form component payload,
an expected-output oracle, implicit tax zero, blanket historical-source admission,
and claiming all-company compatibility from isolated component reports. P1's
204 inputs, all five corporate profiles, active Version3.13 PDF, HEAD and the
100-entry unrelated staged index remain protected. No commit is part of this unit.
