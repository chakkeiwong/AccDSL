## Question, comparator and audit

Can an independent implementer reconstruct the promised extended transitions
from complete typed input, source, state, authorisation and result records,
including failures and replay? The comparator is the existing closed
CorporateJointHistory/1, GroupForeignOperation/1 and insurance atomic profiles,
alongside the original P17-04–08 operation matrices and the R03-A–O methods.
The baseline is the full promise, not just the latest easy example.

The initial schema inspection finds a real gap: CorporateJointHistory/1 fixes
its MethodDefinition to northstar.purchase@1 and its event parameters to
amount/units/consideration; its tax and OCI assumptions are also fixed. Those
records cannot silently accept pension, restoration, OCI, tax-origin or
insurance components. Existing finite group/insurance profiles have their own
declared scope. A balanced new journal or free-form JSON attachment does not
establish their compatibility.

The audit rejects in-place enlargement of closed schemas, a catch-all payload
that hides missing types, unauthorised runtime execution, source labels without
exact bytes, and use of supplied expected outputs as the independent oracle.
It also rejects reopening already delivered method examples merely because
their historical operation-matrix row is stale. Proceed by exact coverage and
contract comparison, preserving current source and output hashes. No unresolved
actual-entity fact is filled from a synthetic case.

Primary criterion: each required promised operation maps to a versioned closed
schema and reconstructible positive/adverse request-result path, or to a precise
remaining authority/design dependency with its next supported action. A missing
required method remains a readiness veto. Schema passes, record counts and
build success are integrity evidence only. Wrong context/source/rule/date,
incomplete dependencies, duplicate economic effects, changed replay bytes,
partial commit or independently wrong finances veto the affected exchange and
trigger repair. They do not stop independent ready modules.

Defaults and assumptions: canonical JSON, SHA256 references and reduced rational
quantities inherit the reviewed documentary transport conventions; verify exact
compatibility rather than inferring it from similar names. New schema/profile
names are derived engineering choices. Synthetic legal admissions, values and
no-tax assumptions remain explicit case premises, never defaults for real books.
No runtime durability, numerical calibration, universal interoperability,
current-law completeness or full readiness follows from authored exchanges.

1. **P1, coverage and type audit.** Preserve snapshots of the original matrices
   and closed schemas. Map every operation family to current method/source/case
   evidence, existing field coverage, exact missing records and dependencies.
   Reconcile R03-F–O repairs with old residuals. Separate absent actual facts,
   authority blocks, valuation-method gaps and wire/specification gaps. Do not
   invent closure from automatic keyword matching.
2. **P2, corporate successor.** Define closed component/state and event variants
   for the required extended corporate methods: production cohorts, assets and
   impairment, benefits, leases, provisions and tax origins. Preserve separate
   economic versus journal events, source/rule/date context and uncomputed tax.
   Connect opening state, admission, proposal, commit and statements for complete
   cases; include state-only events and explicit refusal of incompatible old
   profile inputs. Existing Northstar exchanges remain valid under their lock.
3. **P3, financing/bank/group successors.** Type the named amendment, award,
   hedge-reserve, classification, transfer-option and group/date joins. Bind
   actual settlement to the relevant legal book and preserve component income,
   OCI and retained exposures. Supply complete adverse paths for duplicate
   derivatives, wrong reserve origin, wrong effective date and cross-book joins.
4. **P4, insurance successor.** Type coverage joins, equivalent premiums,
   acquisition/CSM/loss components, PAA and VFA branches, rate/OCI origins and
   process-law identities. Keep issued/held records distinct; test modification
   cash versus deemed transfers, investment-component exclusion, dependence
   preservation and atomic component/journal publication.
5. **P5, closure and integration.** Independently derive finances from declared
   opening/event facts; validate every reference/blob/schema/operation and
   positive/adverse/replay/recovery path. Prove migration only for an explicitly
   mapped subset, with refusal outside it. Integrate the contracts into the
   protected manuscript, inspect the rendered changes and refresh R04/R06,
   whole-candidate R07 and all53/interaction assessment R08.

Each unit is bounded by the existing promised operations and named R03 repairs.
Do not add a new accounting example unless the audit identifies the missing
method or distinguishes a required semantic failure. One unresolved source
blocks its claim; independent exact exchanges and review preparation continue.
