# W05 financing and conditional memory at fiscal carry

26 October 2026. MC05/13/15/16/17/18/19/20/24/25/28/33/34/35.

Question: can a selected financing history cross a fiscal boundary with its
claim, shares, recorded missed payment and last conditional remedy findings
intact, while refusing to reuse an old finding as a current one?

Baseline: the existing CompanyBookCarry/1 closes temporary accounts, moves the
period, clears current-period flows and accepts an explicit policy for every
memory leaf. It does not by itself require preservation of financing history.
The financing-opening-invariants result supplies snapshot relations; the
financing-remedy-state-binding result supplies the current-evidence condition.
Inspect and reuse both without editing their frozen sources or successful runs.

Skeptical audit: an all-preserve policy is a selected synthetic premise, not a
general carry rule. A reset can erase historical missed payment even though
the generic carry is type-correct. Conversely, a retained clause label is an
observation of the past, not automatic authority for the new period. The
economic digest changes at carry because period and flows change; date must
also be checked before acceleration. Do not regenerate that digest or reset
the observation date simply to make continuation pass. The closed-period
archive retains cash-flow evidence before the next opening clears it.

Use an explicitly extended fiscal opening derived from E01's original opening,
with unknown conditional status and supplied provenance. Compose the inspected
E01 event sequence and existing conditional sequence; do not relabel a mid-year
snapshot as a fiscal opening. Report at the actual final day and use the
existing exact closing/carry engine. Author an independent full next-opening,
closing-journal and history expectation. A separate open-claim case should
retain an unpaid obligation across the boundary rather than require it to be
paid as a condition of simulation.

Evidence contract: retained complete financial snapshots and reports must agree;
closing and next-opening states, exact journals, flow archive and all memory
leaves must match independent expected values. Complete selected preservation
policies are required before invoking carry. Missing/reset/retimed histories,
stale report, wrong boundary, altered account role and stale observation controls
must refuse without an accepted opening. Missing needed findings remain unknown
or unresolved. Counts, runtime and document build are explanatory only.

Research intent: mechanism is explicit carry selection and preserved history;
expected failure is lost obligations or automatic freshness. Primary pass is
complete-state equivalence with adverse controls. Hash drift, invalid comparator,
untyped state or accepted partial update veto promotion and trigger repair.
Failure of one selected policy does not reject the simulation direction. No
actual-source, legal, fiscal/accounting admission or general financing closure
follows. Wider decisions and rights changes remain open.

Default audit: year-end and 2028 period follow the supplied E01 calendar;
all-preserve is reviewed only for these claims/shares/history/status leaves;
retained account follows the original report map; exact rational arithmetic
and synthetic source qualifications remain unchanged. Use one paid and one
unpaid path as discriminating cases, not as complete coverage. Deterministic
CPU execution with CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1; no stochastic ranking or GPU use.

Artifacts: data/accounting-master/financing-carry-20261026/. Record audit,
selected declarations, bounded comparisons, adverse controls, pinned inputs,
run manifest, result and remaining work. Use one-shot numbered evidence
attempts; preserve failures and repair before rerun. Integrate evidence only
