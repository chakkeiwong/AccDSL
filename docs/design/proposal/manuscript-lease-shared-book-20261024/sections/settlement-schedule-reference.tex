\section{Dated settlement annotations}
\label{sec:settlement-schedule-draft}

Northstar's annual projection supplies amounts and an event sequence. The
proposed \code{SettlementScheduleDraft/1} annotation adds dates and explicit
funding knowledge without changing those amounts or the frozen company
profile. It is a bounded documentary proposal; claim-level generation,
behavioural-policy records and whole-company textual authoring remain further
work. The existing \code{scenario.project@1} and accounting operations retain
their distinct responsibilities.

The body has exactly five fields: \code{profile}, \code{projection\_ref},
\code{horizon}, \code{funding\_knowledge} and \code{events}. The projection
reference identifies the existing immutable projection. The horizon has a
start and exclusive end, at midnight UTC on the retained projection's fiscal
boundary dates. Each event names one projection
event, its instant and any required predecessors. An unknown instant carries
a reason and named outstanding obligations. Each original event occurs once;
neither an omitted event nor an invented payment is admissible.

Validate structure, the referenced projection and complete event coverage
before considering execution. Dependencies must be acyclic and compatible
with dates. Purchase precedes delivery and supplier settlement; delivery
precedes collection; the income and tax events precede closing. A date outside
the half-open horizon rejects. Two events at the same instant need an explicit
order when their order matters. The bounded annotation conservatively retains
an unresolved result for an unordered pair; it does not use event names or
input-array position to decide who receives cash first.

The initial funding alternatives are an explicit scenario assumption that
there is no eligible facility, or unknown facility access with its outstanding
obligations. A committed facility needs a fuller contract describing limits,
eligibility and draw timing. When cash suffices, payment can proceed without
one. When cash is short and no facility is eligible, return an economic
funding failure and leave the proposed payment unapplied. When access is
unknown, retain unresolved funding instead. Subsequent bankruptcy or
restructuring requires its own method; it is not inferred from this refusal.

The reference schema and fifteen complete schedules are retained in
\path{data/accounting-master/simulator-timing-20261023/}. The cases distinguish
collection-before-payment, payment-before-collection, unknown collection timing
and unknown funding. They also exercise omitted or duplicated events, cycles,
contradictory dates, invalid calendar dates, unresolved references and explicit
same-instant ordering. The original accounting comparison retains both
interest-rate scenarios at floating shares zero, one half and one. These
checks establish the stated documentary examples, not a deployed scheduler.
