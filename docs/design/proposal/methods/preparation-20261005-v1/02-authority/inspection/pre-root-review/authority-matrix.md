# Lease and income-tax authority on matched facts

Prepared 5 October 2026 for proposal phase P17. This is a source-grounded design
comparison, with different evidence strength in each column. It does not select
the product's accounting framework or admit a production treatment. The source
register preserves exact bytes, inspection anchors and access failures.

The useful conclusion is that **the same recognised lease liability can coexist
with materially different expense and right-of-use-asset paths**. A second useful
conclusion is that **a balanced lease journal does not finish the tax analysis**:
the applicable tax bases can require separate deferred tax assets and liabilities.
Version, legal enactment and entity elections must therefore be inputs to rule
selection, not labels added to an otherwise identical calculation.

## Fixed transaction and reporting facts

Use a for-profit lessee preparing a calendar2024 annual report. Its reporting
basis is varied only for comparison. It receives exclusive economic benefits
from identified equipment and directs its use; the supplier has no substantive
substitution right. The equipment is available on1Jan2024. The noncancellable
term ends31Dec2025, with cash121 due on each year end. There are no service
components, ownership transfer, purchase/renewal/termination options, incentives,
initial direct costs, prepaid rent, residual guarantee, restoration obligation,
impairment or modification in the base case. Benefits are consumed evenly and
the costs are operating expenses rather than costs of another asset.

Supplementary facts, agreed with packet1 to make classification meaningful:
equipment has remaining economic/useful life10years, fair value1000 and an
alternative use to the lessor after return. The lease begins well before the end
of economic life. The implicit rate is not readily determinable; an assumed,
appropriately matched annual effective IBR is10%. This rate is a synthetic input,
not a rate estimate established by accounting authority. No low-value exemption
is elected; the numerical unit alone does not prove or disprove low-value
eligibility. For the US private-entity alternative, no risk-free-rate election is
made. No short-term exemption can be inferred for a two-year noncancellable term.

The independent tax case is a purchased PPE asset costing1000 on1Jan2024 with
zero residual and a five-year useful life. Book depreciation is200/year. The
assumed law allows deductions400,300,100,100,100; recovery occurs through use.
Pretax accounting profit is500 in2024 and, for the rate comparison,500 in2025.
An enacted flat25% rate applies, with no credits, losses, uncertain tax position,
permanent difference, group transfer, business combination, OCI or equity item.
These assumptions are not descriptions of Australian, US or Chinese tax law.
All schedules after2024 hold the inspected rule versions fixed to illustrate
consequences; they do not establish reporting applicability in each later year.

A discriminating alternative retains the same future rate and reversal schedule

## The lease-tax interaction that an isolated journal misses

AASB112.22A and98J–98L are already relevant to the2024 illustration. Equal
taxable and deductible temporary differences from a single lease transaction
are not excused merely because the commencement journal leaves accounting profit
and taxable profit unchanged. The source explicitly removes that initial
recognition exemption, with recognition of a DTA subject to the relevant
recoverability conditions.

The official MoF application case, published4Apr2023, says the same thing for
its stated facts under Interpretation16, section1, and ASBE18. It recognises
the DTA and DTL separately. This is a substantive primary finding even while
the underlying standard pages are inaccessible. The case assumes sufficient
future taxable income; that premise cannot be dropped. Its seven-year PV is
also deliberately rounded from2600098 to2600000; the source's footnote says
so. Treating its numbers as an exact annuity oracle would be wrong.

For an optional extension of our base lease, assume the law attributes future
rental deductions to the liability, both ROU and liability tax bases are zero,
and sufficient taxable profit supports the DTA. At inception210×25%=52.5 DTA
and52.5 DTL are separately recognised. At the end of2024 under the AASB lease
path, DTA=110×25%=27.5 while DTL=105×25%=26.25. A net1.25 asset follows
arithmetically. **Net presentation is a separate decision**: AASB112.74 requires
offset conditions, including legally enforceable current-tax offset rights and
the relevant tax authority/entity conditions. Equal initial amounts do not
establish those conditions. This extension is not automatically imposed on the
main lease fixture, which deliberately excludes tax.

## Changes justified for the proposal

1. Preserve method identity as jurisdiction/framework, operative provision,
   source version, entity scope, effective period and elections. Store enactment
   date separately from the date a tax rate applies. A source unavailable for
   one operation does not prohibit other source-supported preparation.
2. Add distinct lessee subsequent-expense branches and exact expected ROU
   balances to conformance design. Do not merge branches solely because cash,
   liability and lifetime cost agree.
3. Carry paired deferred-tax consequences with transactions, preserving DTA
   recoverability and offset decisions independently. The single-transaction
   exemption must be selected by dated amendment, not inherited from an old
   ASBE18 or IAS12 reproduction.
4. Treat the shared2-year example as a bounded educational/oracle case. Keep
   variable payments, impairment, sale/leaseback, modifications, current
   Codification, original ASBE provisions and actual tax law on explicit follow-up
   queues. No complete lease/tax engine is specified by these two methods.
