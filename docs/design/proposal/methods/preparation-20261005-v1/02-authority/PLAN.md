# Matched lease and tax authority comparison

Prepared 5 October 2026. Packet owner: authority matrix; candidate P17 evidence.
Requirements: MC19–20, MC29–30 and MC35; G7/G8. The initial packet list omitted
MC19, which directly governs the cross-standard comparison; it is included here.

The question is which recognition, measurement and applicability propositions are
actually supported for the same synthetic 2024 lessee and depreciating-asset tax
facts under the inspected IFRS-equivalent, US GAAP and ASBE sources. This is source
research and arithmetic comparison, not product implementation or compliance
certification. The comparator is the precise retained provision and its stated
effective period, not company practice or a framework label.

Source baseline: existing P08 v 1/v 2 authority/access/inspection records, P08 v 3
source-preparation and accepted P08 deliverable; snapshot mission/requirements,
current checkpoint and gap records. P08 previously obtained an official FASB ASU
after a first blocked URL, but full ASC authority remained blocked. ASBE 22/37
MoF routes were blocked; this does not establish inaccessibility of leases/tax.
Only genuinely different lease/tax routes are warranted.

Matched lease: 1 January 2024 commencement; two years; end-year fixed payments
121; assumed annual discount rate 10%; no advances, incentives, direct costs,
restoration, purchase/renewal options; controlled equipment use. Initial PV 210,
first-year liability 110, second-year zero. Underlying fair value, life and
specialisation require explicit treatment for a US classification conclusion.
Matched tax: PPE 1000; five-year book depreciation 200/year; assumed tax deductions
400, 300, 100, 100, 100; initially enacted rate 25%; 2024 book pretax profit 500;
optional 2025 enacted rate 30% case. Tax law is synthetic and cannot establish a
real entity's deductions or rate.

Evidence contract: retain exact bytes, provenance, hashes and paragraph/page or
article/line anchors. Pass is a reconstructible supported proposition plus its
scope and a checked consequence. Hash mismatch, absent text, uninspected relevant
provision, unmatched facts or missing effective-period support veto that claim.
Source counts, successful downloads and matching arithmetic are explanatory
only; they cannot promote a current authoritative rule. Missing access blocks
only the affected claim. Retain competing treatments and the proposed answer.

Default audit: the 2024 period is shared with packet 1 to permit comparison;
10%/25% and the deduction schedule are transparent synthetic assumptions, not
recommended defaults. The ASU is an official publisher document but expressly
not authoritative; it may ground a historical amendment reading, never silently
substitute for the current Codification. Official AASB texts establish Australian
applicability and an inspected IFRS relationship, not every jurisdiction's IFRS
endorsement. A government reproduction establishes the text on that source/date,
not an exhaustively current ASBE compilation. All three distinctions are vetoes
against stronger claims.

Skeptical audit: wrong-baseline and stale-context risks are controlled by source
and period pins; no proxy metric is a pass criterion. Fixed transaction facts
prevent a false standards comparison. Source acquisition has bounded timeouts;
one blocked route is recorded rather than repeatedly retried. No installs,
payments, product defaults or modifications to the original worktree. Stop after
useful checked matrix/source register/open questions; preserve unresolved current
authority rather than expand into unbounded retrieval. Strongest pre-mortem is a
numerically balanced result built on the wrong edition or omitted classification
fact; early checks inspect cover/effective-period and classification before
stating an accounting answer. Audit decision: proceed with these limits.
