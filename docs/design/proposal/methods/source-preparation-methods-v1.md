## D1. Three populations and a resumable procedure

Retained-unit coverage partitions acquired documents into nonoverlapping leaf
units, preserving all bytes/pages plus retained/structured/duplicate/unresolved
dispositions. Assertion-review coverage uses separately enumerated material
assertions and their admission history. Query-exposure coverage enumerates the
sources, facts, methods and authority needed for the particular reconstruction
or forecast. These denominators cannot be substituted for one another.

Declare annual/interim roots and incorporated-reference policy; retain the
original source/version; enumerate pages/sections/tables/notes and references;
classify each edge as satisfied, duplicate/cycle, out-of-declared-scope with
reason, inaccessible or unexpanded. A bounded traversal saves frontier and
budget receipt for resume. Unknown external extent is not zero. Then propose
located readings and contexts, review translations and build query dependencies.
Any relevant missing referenced contract, policy or legal term remains exposed
in the query result. A report with all pages downloaded may still lack those
requirements. Current Apple evidence therefore remains package_incomplete.

P02's underlying-agreement interpretation retains missing executed amendments,
issuance-specific terms and other required references. The acquired proxy and
registration document improve retention but do not prove every exhibit's
complete closure or every contract's semantic meaning. Keep E01 open until a
declared real package and actual dependency query is complete at the required
level. The source-preservation protocol itself can still be specified now.

ASBE21 article6 adds a different type of narrative: conditional normative rule.
Substantive substitution requires practical ability throughout use AND economic
benefit from exercising it. Difficulty determining the right triggers the
standard's express presumption; it is not a source assertion that the lessor
actually lacks a right. An operating-case source, a normative condition and a
modeling assumption have different admission classes. Qualitative phrases such
as probable, highly probable, significant or substantive retain their supported
judgment method; the translator cannot invent a percentage threshold.

## D3. Rounding disposition

P02's stored rounding_model says closed half-unit nearest-rounding intervals,
issuer_tie_rule unknown and compatibility only. Treat that as an explicit
conditional model assumption in every derived P17 reference; it does not
establish that Apple's displayed millions were rounded by that rule. Preserve
the historical calculations and their original identity. Do not mutate P02 to
retroactively assert stronger source evidence.

For displayed integer m at scale s, admitted nearest rounding with unknown ties
permits the conservative compatibility enclosure[s(m−1/2),s(m+1/2)]. A specified
ties-to-even rule changes endpoint inclusion according to parity. Unknown
rounding convention without that modeling assumption gives display_precision_known,
rounding_convention_unknown and no hard half-unit constraint. A display scale
alone does not prove nearest rounding. Summing rounded components need not equal
the displayed total, and a mismatch of one unit is not by itself a genuine
conflicting disclosure. Named conditional analysis may still ask whether an
identity is compatible with the assumed intervals.

For displayed100 million under the explicit nearest model, the enclosure is
99.5 to100.5 million. Under truncation of positive amounts it would be100 to101
million with upper endpoint excluded. The different sets show why provenance
matters. Rounding a normalized library value a second time or assigning its
interval without source/assumption admission is invalid. E03 can be resolved
for the proposed interpretation boundary after review; P02 remains an exploratory
conditional calculation and no issuer convention has been established.
