# A foreign subsidiary from acquisition to loss of control

P17-05/P18; MC02/04–07/11/13/15–17/19/20/28/30/32–35. The exact case and
source manifest accompany this method under P17-05/group-fx-disposal.
Selected IFRS3/IFRS10/IAS21/IAS7 provisions in the retained original EU2023/1803
support the historical mechanism. Relative A/O/R/D events do not assert current
reporting applicability. This is a pretax documentary case, not runtime execution.

Admission requires a business/control assessment, ordinary-rights and NCI
measurement, all identifiable acquisition assets/liabilities and their currency,
rates at every event, parent investment cost, separate subsidiary books, goodwill
currency and impairment history, owner/NCI profit/OCI attribution, disposition
terms/control change, retained-interest fair value and source/evidence identities.
Missing rate/value gives measurement_unresolved; unknown power gives
perimeter_unresolved; missing reserve attribution gives disposal_unresolved.
These statuses preserve alternatives and inputs; they do not prescribe a plug.

At A, parent functional/presentation currencyUSD cash/equity300. It obtains
control and80% of ordinary interests in a EUR-functional business forEUR96 at
USD1.20/EUR. Existing outputs, a skilled organized workforce and a substantive
critical operating process satisfy the assumed IFRS3.B12C(a) facts; the optional
concentration test is not elected. No separately identifiable intangible is
omitted in these synthetic facts; workforce value is subsumed under B37.
Substantive voting rights give power, variable returns and their connection;
parent is not an investment entity. No common control, previous interest,
contingent consideration, tax difference or acquisition costs apply in this slice.

At D, an unanticipated transaction immediately after R loses control. No earlier

not an IFRS requirement or valuation result. It makes total consideration plus

Subsidiary cash is removed from the consolidation even though it stays in that
legal entity's own bank account. This is not a parent payment of156.

Adverse checks must examine intermediate states. Freezing goodwill atUSD19.2
understates goodwill and parent OCI by1.6 at R; the gain omission later cancels
against reduced recycling, leaving the same33.8 disposal total. Allocating20%
of goodwill FX to NCI misattributes0.32 before disposal. Recycling all12.6 while
still removing NCI31.2 overstates profit2.2 and leaves a spurious negative parent

Counting retained value as cash overstates cash42.25. None is rescued by equal
final total equity. Actual business/rights, valuation/tax/impairment, policy
alignment, current source application and whole-company integration remain open.
