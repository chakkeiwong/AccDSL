# Contractual rights, credit repair and cash that has actually arrived

15 October2026. R03-C; historical AU for-profit calendar2024/25 accounting
under retained AASB9 F2023C00399. All cases are synthetic, conditional and
pretax. The plan is accounting-bank-restructuring-funding-20261015.md.

## Different decisions at a restructuring

A Modification record retains original and amended cash flows, dates,
currency, fees, lender/borrower identities, original effective rate, original
credit-risk assessment and the legal rights before and after amendment.
DerecognitionAssessment first identifies the unit under3.2.2 and whether
rights expire under3.2.3(a). A transfer requires3.2.4–5's conditions and
3.2.6's risks/rewards/control analysis. Substantially retained risks preserve
the asset; neither transferred nor retained risks require the control and
continuing-involvement branch. A fixed-price repurchase cannot automatically
be treated as a sale(.3.2.7). Unknown legal/transfer evidence returns
derecognition_unresolved. The issuer-liability ten-percent comparison cannot
replace these asset decisions.

For a supported non-derecognising change,5.4.3 recalculates gross carrying
amount as the PV of modified contractual cash flows at the original EIR,
subject to its POCI/hedge exceptions. Recognise the modification result
separately from ECL. Costs/fees adjust carrying value and amortise over the
remaining term; the examples have none. Assess current expected receipts
and SICR after the modification. Under5.5.12/B5.5.27, the original
unmodified risk baseline survives; relief or one timely payment is not a cure.

If rights expire or a transfer qualifies, remove the appropriate old
gross/allowance balance and recognise the net difference from consideration
under3.2.12. A new financial asset requires its own classification, initial
measurement, effective rate and POCI finding. B5.5.25–26 explicitly prevents
assuming every replacement of a distressed asset is an ordinary new Stage1
loan. Partial transfers and continuing involvement retain their identified
measurement obligations under3.2.13–23; these finite full-rights cases do not
invent their values.

## Collateral timing and the surviving legal claim

A different loan advances100 on1January2024 and requires121 on1January2025.
Its contractual half-year accumulation is1.1, equivalent to21% effective
annual. This convention is distinct from the preceding annual10% loan.
Initial disjoint scenarios are full payment with probability3/5 and default
with probability2/5. All possible defaults occur in the first twelve months.
In the default state, expected net recovery66.55 on1July2025 is the mean of
121,66.55 and12.10 with conditional weights1/4,1/2,1/4. All three receipts
have that date. Before the sale a supported reasonable prospect of full
nominal recovery remains; the mean is not a known unrecoverable portion.
The realised middle outcome is an agent sale for79.86 less contractual
enforcement costs13.31, remitting net66.55. The bank does not separately
recognise the collateral as an
owned asset. The estimate explicitly includes cash after contractual maturity
and costs once, as required by B5.5.55.

The full-payment PV is121/1.1²=100. Default-recovery PV is66.55/1.1³=50.
Expected receipt PV is(3/5)100+(2/5)50=80; ECL is20. Here twelve-month
and lifetime ECL agree because all default events are in the first year;
the recovery date need not be in that year. Using only payments before
maturity gives the wrong ECL40. Adding the collateral proceeds again to a
loss estimate already containing them also computes the wrong target.

With opening cash200,deposits180 and equity20, advance100 and recognise
allowance20. At1July2024, accrue gross interest10; the original scenario
mixture, rolled forward, gives allowance22. Then a separately observed
qualitative credit-impairment event establishes the collateral-only expectation,
now PV55, requiring
allowance55 against gross110. This subsequent credit impairment occurs
after the first period's gross-interest accrual.

During the next half-year, original-rate interest on net55 is5.5.
Gross increases11 to121 and allowance increases5.5 to60.5, leaving net60.5
at contractual maturity. The loan has no contractual post-maturity interest.
Over the next half-year, the expected receipt PV rises60.5→66.55.
Recognise net interest6.05 by debiting allowance and crediting interest;
gross due remains121 and allowance falls to54.45. AppendixA defines gross
carrying amount before allowance and amortised cost through the cumulative
amortisation of the initial-to-maturity difference. Here that difference is
fully amortised at maturity; there are no fees, premium or post-maturity
contractual interest. B5.5.33 gives allowance as gross less the original-rate
PV of expected receipts:121-60.5=60.5 at maturity, then121-66.55=54.45 at
collection. This derives the split for these terms; it is not a rule that all
overdue loans stop contractual accrual. A new contractual claim
of12.1 must not be invented to make the gross ledger accrete.

Collect net66.55 from the agent, leaving gross and allowance54.45.
A separate supported finding at sale that no further recovery is reasonably
expected requires write-off:
debit allowance54.45/credit gross54.45 under5.4.4. Final cash166.55,
deposits180 and equity-13.45 reconcile; lifetime profit-33.45 equals

contract history unless an independently evidenced release expires them.
An accounting write-off is not a legal waiver, and no capital injection is
created to conceal the negative equity.
If the no-recovery finding had arisen earlier,5.4.4 would require the
corresponding earlier write-off; the sale date is not a general waiting rule.

## Facility capacity and actual settlement

Start another bank state with cash20,securities50,gross loans80,allowance10,
deposits120 and equity20. A synthetic secured facility has limit10 and
admits a specified unencumbered collateral lot of market value10 with a20%
haircut. Other securities are ineligible. Initial usable capacity is
min(10,10×0.8)=8; there is no pre-existing facility drawing. These are
fictional contract terms, not a central-bank entitlement or prudential rule.
The eligible lot remains the bank's asset and becomes encumbered; borrowing
does not sell it or create an additional collateral asset.

A request for8 produces no cash journal. Confirmed settlement8 gives
cash28 and funding liability8; a confirmed partial fill6 gives cash26 and
funding liability6. A third history retains a pending request and independent
evidence that no receipt arrived before the payment deadline. That history
still has cash20 and no funding liability. A timeout without the independent
cash evidence is observation_unresolved, not the third history.

At the deadline a withdrawal30 is due. The specified legal model permits
partial payment and marks the unpaid due amount as a liquidity default,
without waiving the deposit. Pay28,26 or20 respectively. Cash reaches0;
deposit liabilities become92,94 or100; funding liabilities8,6 or0.
Securities50 plus net loans70 equal assets120 and liabilities100 plus
equity20 in all three histories. Unpaid due amounts2,4 or10 remain within
the deposit liability, rather than a second liability. Profit and OCI are0.
Depositor settlement is operating cash; the separately specified borrowing
is financing cash under the retained cash-flow principles. A different
facility's terms, costs or settlement purpose needs its own classification.

## Immutable inputs and failure behavior

CreditAssessment binds loan ID, original recognition/terms/risk view, current
terms/risk view, assessment date, information cutoff, credit-impaired finding,
SICR finding, supporting events, law reference and authorised measurement
profile. A numeric PD ratio is explanatory unless an admitted model separately
justifies its decision rule. Unknown findings yield conditional results or
credit_assessment_unresolved; no default stage is installed.

CollateralRecovery binds the specific loan/security/priority, enforceability,
ownership/encumbrance, expected realisation and costs by date, scenario weights,
currency, discount convention and recognition status. Terms belonging to
different entities or dates cannot be joined by similar labels. It produces
dated expected receipts, not cash or an owned asset. Missing priority, costs,
timing or joint law yields recovery_measurement_unresolved.

FundingCapacity binds facility/version, borrower, venue/currency, legal
conditions, limit, eligible lots, market observations, haircut, existing
drawings, other encumbrances and expiry. Capacity is recomputed as of the
request; unknown eligibility is not zero-valued collateral or approved credit.
FundingRequest and FundingSettlement have distinct identities, amounts and
evidence. Only a confirmed settlement commits cash and borrowing. Lost
acknowledgement retains both possible publication worlds under the existing
atomic/replay contract; it cannot be repaired by posting a second receipt.
An over-capacity proposed request fails admission. An independently evidenced
unexpected receipt must still be recorded as an actual cash/liability event
with the control violation retained; rejecting a proposed action is not a
permission to omit money that has arrived.

Each operation keeps source/assumption/version dependencies and exact prior
state. Replayed identical events have no new effect; changed payloads under
one ID conflict. Queries retain negative equity, unpaid obligations and
unresolved measurements. The separate frozen US prudential illustration is
not automatically applicable to these AU historical accounting cases.
Actual credit calibration, legal admission, fees, partial transfers, continuing
involvement and revolving collateral keep their explicit later obligations.
