## Model, chronology and source meaning

opening, two half-year economic inputs and financial expectations define the
historical case. Source records retain exact original bytes, source unit,
synthetic public date, byte-vintage support, ingestion and review instants.
New byte/time receipts are authored fixture evidence, never real issuer facts.
Each source unit has its own coverage disposition; a missing incorporated
attachment blocks its dependent target, not the entire retained package.
The variant with a missing debt attachment is separate from Northstar's closed
original source package. A source claim “substantially all” has no numeric
threshold. Competing proposed readings remain unadmitted until an explicit
assumption selects one. Displayed precision similarly supplies no rounding law.

Use the current manuscript's dates: original H1 publication1 August2024,
ingestion3 August; corrected H1/FY publication2 March2025, ingestion5 March.
For this fixture, date-only publication is in UTC; admission is certain only
at/after next midnight. The newly supplied ingestion/review receipts have

and never silently imported. Investor information checks public-byte linkage
and all interpretation information dependencies; later ingestion is permitted.

time/byte linkage stays unresolved. A known later correction does not suppress
an earlier eligible original. Original selection returns roots. Latest selects

## Financial representation and independent expectations

An AffineValue denotes a+b*x, where x is the same floating share everywhere,
0<=x<=1. Values are exact reduced rationals, never sampled endpoints. Money
inherits the record's Context; the context fixes entity, book, perimeter, USD,
unit scale, basis, period and dimensions. Rates, counts and dates have their
own fields. A field of the wrong context cannot be added. The family has no
probability law. Its observed 2024 interest5 admits all fixed/floating splits;
working-capital bridges imply collections245, purchases155, payments145.
The authored model contains explicit linear constraints and their support.
The text frontend covers only the published debt fragment. Typed construction
also supplies the full corporate module. Textual whole-company authoring is
outside this frontend subset and returns a located diagnostic.

Historical schedules use purchase before atomic delivery (receivable/revenue
and cost/inventory together), then collections before funded payments.
Closing entries transfer temporary balances without creating cash or a second
income amount. This maps the old aggregate posting fixture, whose sales and
cost rows were separate, to a valid aggregate economic sequence. It does not
recover invoices. Plant additions enter service next period. Tax expense is
20% of positive profit; H1 overpayment2 becomes a prepayment used in H2.

At a supplied floating rate r, interest is5+(100r-5)x. Under the stated 2025
drivers, preinterest pretax profit87 gives profit(87-interest)*4/5. Collections
280, supplier payments160, wages50 and tax/interest give CFO; capex20 and
dividends8 give cash. Complete statements, zero OCI, equity, debt and plant
movements share this exact x. At7%, cash95.2-1.6x and profit65.6-1.6x;
at9%, cash95.2-3.2x and profit65.6-3.2x. Endpoint examples are checks, not
an exhaustive discrete representation.

Unknown current collection fraction leaves collections/cash/receivables/CFO/
assets unresolved; profit remains computable under independent delivery facts.
Unsupported tax similarly leaves affected profit/equity/cash unresolved without
replacing them by zero. A report records line-level obligations and preserves
the full requested domain. Product and geography totals describe the same
sales population; summing both rejects. A report cannot mix different family,
scenario, view or period versions.

## Closure and effects

Every record has the L1 wrapper and content digest. All body Refs appear in its
canonical dependency set; all referenced records, schema locks and required
blobs exist in a finite acyclic closure. The schema file and this semantic
contract are actual required blobs. Exact submission bytes are retained before
validation. Invalid inputs have null validated request_ref and null payload;
valid partial computation keeps its request and domain. Domain partitions
distinguish completed/unresolved/unsupported/cancelled regions. No law is added.

Read operations have expected_head=null and commit_observation=not_committed.
Construction returns immutable candidates and does not change accounting books.
Review commit changes only its review owner; a new view preserves the old view
and invalidates dependent current uses through candidate membership as well
as selected records. A delayed descendant must check its ancestors. Frozen
explanation can still describe the old result. Accepting a source reading never
authorizes posting. A newly proposed record can exist in the global fixture
registry without belonging to any world's published record set.

The accounting commit example is the first Northstar inventory purchase70:
inventory30→100, payable20→90, cash60 unchanged. Its exact method, event,
opening, journal and independent economic delta must bind the same synthetic
book/view. Both journal balance and quantity/cost conservation are necessary.
The event's stable entity/book/branch/business key controls receipt lookup.
Full input/binding/authorization checks precede lookup; exact replay precedes
current-head freshness. The request fingerprint includes request ID, budget
and expected head. Changed bytes for the same business event conflict.
New valid publication includes all state/journal/delta/receipt dependencies,
head and event lookup atomically. Rejection changes none of them.

A lost acknowledgement is a transport observation, not a received result with
a guessed commit outcome. The same exact request commits in the precommit
world and replays in the postcommit world. A stale concurrent head rejects a
new event. A crash before publication preserves the old world. A proposed
balanced purchase with the wrong inventory quantity fails before lookup.

## Search, correction, migration and scope

A search checkpoint binds family, semantic manifest, ordered partition, completed
and unresolved regions and the next index. The two cells are x<=1/2 and x>1/2;
their union is the continuous original family and they are disjoint. A one-cell
budget does not narrow the family. Resume with unchanged semantics and more
budget visits the second cell. Changing evidence/scenario/method is a new job,
not a resume. The current finite examples specify traversal; no solver proof
or generic polyhedral engine is implemented.

Representation migration retains original records and compares values, contexts,
family domain, source/admission, event identity and the supported query outputs.
A candidate that changes floating share to a fixed endpoint fails and leaves
the original store unchanged. Export/import closures preserve originals and
grant no financial admission. The scope of these exact fixtures is documented
per operation in the schema/signature catalog; an unsupported operation returns
its missing capability rather than calling an opaque host function.

The checker separately derives financial results from preserved economic inputs,
checks exact shape/hash/reference/blob closure, evaluates time and binding
conditions and compares authored store worlds. These are specification checks.
Later implementations need frontend/solver/adapter and real fault-injection
tests; human comprehension and domain acceptance remain pending.

The debt text is a newly specified bounded frontend, not the old Core0 grammar.
Its complete syntax is the ordered production below. ASCII space, tab, CR and
LF may separate tokens; adjacent keyword/number tokens require a separator.
Keywords and punctuation are case sensitive; no comments, strings or extra
declarations are accepted. `nat` is `0` or a nonzero decimal digit followed by
decimal digits; `positive` excludes zero; `q` is `nat` or `nat/positive`.
Fractions are reduced when constructing values. The exact grammar is:

```text
document := "NorthstarDebt" "{" "principal" "=" q ";"
            "x" ":" "Rational" "in" "[" q "," q "]" ";"
            "fixed_rate" "=" q ";" "floating_rate" "=" q ";"
            "interest" "=" q "*" "(" "(" "1" "-" "x" ")"
            "*" "fixed_rate" "+" "x" "*" "floating_rate" ")" ";" "}"
```
