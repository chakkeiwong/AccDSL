# RealDisclosureConnection/1 — named evidence and conditional results

15 October 2026. R02 under accounting-real-package-connection-20261015.md.
This extension retains I1/L1/F1 envelopes but registers separate real-source
contexts, observations, family questions and conditional transforms. Northstar's
synthetic tax, zero OCI, dates, state and posting permission do not carry over.

## Exact target and source scope

The Apple package consists of the retained FY2024 annual PDF and FY2025Q1
HTML, their complete selected statement rows, all23 note dispositions and the
frozen source frontier:70 references,7 package obligations,32 selected assertions
and7 added frontier entries. The later FY2025 annual HTML is a separate source
view for its same-period comparative segment information. The original retained
inventory and its counterpart map are both required blobs. Inventory entries
point to exact JSON locations and preserve their frozen disposition and current
missing information; they grant no semantic admission. Recursive closure remains
false. Retaining all known inventory entries does not discover unknown references.

A SourceVersion pins actual original bytes and its preserved reading dossier.
Publication, ingestion and review clocks remain unknown where these exact bytes
have no accepted clock evidence in this package. Filing-event evidence can be
retained as its own record; it is not proof that a converted PDF is the filed
byte stream. A named-source content query is allowed. An investor or system-as-of
request requiring unsupported clocks returns partial unknown values with the
specific obligation. A later source view never supersedes an earlier source by
date alone, and it is not automatically an accounting correction.

Observations distinguish displayed numbers from reported dashes. A Known value
is the exact rational representation of the displayed token, not a claim of
unrounded underlying equality. A ReportedDash retains its original literal;
its value is neither zero nor a missing numeric field repaired by interpolation.
Unknown results use a tagged reason and obligation references. Statement rows
retain context, fiscal period, unit, basis, source locator and measurement kind.
Per-share amounts and thousands-of-shares rows cannot be summed with USD millions.

## Family question and connected results

The family question asks for actual whole-company economic histories compatible
with these annual/interim observations and note meanings. Its formulation and
feasible remainder are unresolved, so it has no admitted member list, bounds or
probability law. An Unknown does not mean the family is empty. In particular,
the retained H1/H2 asset constructions are conditional subsystem candidates,
not certified members of the actual issuer family.

The ConnectedResult references the entire package, original/later views,
all selected annual/interim observations and their report, note constraints,
the unresolved family question, conditional transformations and settlement
projections. Result status is partial even when a named-source subquery is
complete. Domain regions are requested question/claim scopes, not economic
probabilities or proportions of admissible company histories. Known and unknown
items remain identified individually. No averaging, probability or completeness
score is inferred from their counts.

The annual H2−H1 asset delta derives from retained journals. Relative to H1,
H2 buys and retires an extra1000 of gross cost, has an extra1000 retirement
loss and capital payable, and has unchanged net PPE and cash. A separately
hypothesised non-tax expense liability is q in H1 and q−1000 in H2, q>=1000.
Only conditional on its actual existence, common expense/liability classes,
unchanged tax and other activities can it offset those annual differences.
In the exact example, retirement loss and the separate expense map to product
cost; capital payable and expense liability map to other current liabilities
within the same non-tax bucket. Shares, distributions and OCI are unchanged.
The parameter alpha in[0,1] allocates that expense-liability difference once
between the cash-flow noncash adjustment and working-capital adjustment. It is
a reporting assumption, not a probability or Northstar debt share. The two row
differences are1000(1−alpha) and−1000(1−alpha). At alpha=1 all102 displayed
row differences are zero. This does not establish an absolute feasible base,
actual retirement, provision, settlement date or unrounded source equality.

Conditional settlement keeps each branch's capital and expense liability
separate. At illustrative q=2000 the joint (operating,investing) cash changes are
(−2000,−1082) for H1 and(−1000,−2082) for H2 when both settle in the first
period. Both totals are−3082, but independent extrema are not a permitted pair.
For differing dates, cash by period also differs. Carrying-value settlement,
sufficient unrestricted funding and zero incremental tax/FX/interest are explicit
conditions. No cash balance is invented, and these relative-period projections
are not historical investor forecasts. Missing dates return unresolved timing
while preserving the conditional eventual obligation amounts. Future depreciation
is unsupported without asset/cohort/tax state. Unknown actual base and source
admission accompany every conditional result.

## Portable effects and failures

All record bodies are closed and versioned. Their wrapper, reference digests,
canonical bytes and separate BlobRefs follow L1. The schema lock retains the
semantic contract and schema bytes. OperationRequest uses the same I1 envelope
and arguments {tag: operation, value: fields}; operation/tag/signature must agree.
The profile is candidate/read-only: every request expected_head is null and
every result commit_observation is not_committed. Candidate persistence is not
an accounting or review commit. Exact before/after worlds preserve all owners.

Supported signatures are source.prepare@1, history.query@1, scenario.project@1,
report.build@1 and review.propose@1. Their operation-specific unions fix the
query targets, versions, modes and constraints. Unknown source clocks, base
feasibility or parameter values return partial/unsupported results without a
substitute number. Context, company, profile or semantic binding violations
return rejected input with null validated request and payload. Unsupported
probability and depreciation requests preserve the requested scope. No implied
opaque callback, schema validation, row count or balanced journal is a proof
of actual issuer-history feasibility or accounting compliance.

Settlement periods are relative indices in {1,2}; null means unknown timing,
not zero or period one. Alpha must lie in [0,1]. A known eventual amount with
unknown timing uses conditional_derived, references its ConditionalBasis and
is never tagged known_displayed. Statement observations alone use the latter
tag. Missing-date coverage separates completed eventual conditional amounts
from unresolved period timing. Input decoding precedes operation/signature,
issuer, view, parameter-domain and payload checks. Truncated bytes, a mismatched
operation tag, out-of-domain alpha/period and cross-company context are rejected
with no validated request, payload, coverage or mutation.

Each unchanged world is an explicit empty documentary candidate world. This
checks the absence of specified effects, not persistence against a live store.
The combined Apple result links a later-view supplement separately; its original
statement report and original-view queries may only use the annual/interim
sources admitted by their own views. A connection does not widen those views.
