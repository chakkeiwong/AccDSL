# E05 retained-source investigation, version 1

This bounded investigation addresses MC04/MC27 (G2/G17), with MC05/MC25.
The comparator is P15-E05 and the evidence/time v2 contracts. A faithful
extraction authorises a reading of a source, not an accounting treatment.
Product implementation and independent acceptance remain pending.

## Admission predicate and search boundary

A qualifying genuine conflict needs two distinct source assertions with
incompatible meanings about the same entity, group perimeter, concept,
dimensions, units, measurement basis and economic instant or reporting period.
The comparison must retain table headings and qualifications, distinguish
principal from carrying value and gross terms from net exposure, and account
for supported precision. Display precision alone does not justify a numerical
rounding interval. Identical files, mirrors and differently extracted copies
of one assertion do not supply independent assertions. Faithfulness must be
checked against the retained primary representation, not presumed from an
extraction's existence. A supported correction/supersession relation explains
a revision and does not by itself satisfy the unresolved-conflict example.
Unknown context or chronology stays unknown. A conflict can be investigated
archivally with unknown public availability, but cannot be called historically
admissible at an information cutoff without the time contract's evidence.

The reviewed search consists of three groups: Apple annual 2024/interim 2025Q1
with P02 bindings; P06/P07 lineage examples; and directly identified candidates
in retained corpus indexes. This is not an exhaustive search of all reports.
No new acquisition campaign or standards-treatment research is part of it.
An absence of a qualifying pair leaves E05 open and does not veto independent
contract work. Search ends before the reserved checks/response interval.

## Walkthrough obligations fixed before source selection

| Stage | Required input | Expected output or refusal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source inspection | immutable bytes, occurrences, provenance and chronology | inspected spans with explicit faithfulness and availability limits |
| Extraction review | literal reading and layer-specific proposed decision | faithful readings preserved independently; no treatment admission |
| Interpretation | context comparison and meanings | alternatives or unresolved context; no invented probability |
| Accounting admission | interpretation, treatment authority and prerequisites | qualified constraints only; unsupported treatment remains unresolved |
| Revision | expected view, new evidence and scoped review decision | new immutable view; transitive invalidation for affected dependencies |
| Reuse and access | complete dependency closure and requested view | unaffected reuse proof, stale-current refusal, frozen historical access |

The final record must compare layer-specific admission with one undifferentiated
accepted flag using identical example inputs. The latter's convenience cannot
justify promoting a faithful but disputed assertion into an accounting fact.

## Inspected groups

Group findings will be saved here as each group is completed; structured spans,
hashes and candidate dispositions are retained in e05-source-candidates-v1.json.

### Group 1: Apple annual 2024 and interim 2025Q1

The original annual PDF's debt table and swap paragraph were visually inspected
at physical page 46 (printed 43). The original interim HTML confirms Note 6's
wording and the balance-sheet column dates. Exact extracted spans and original
hashes are in the candidate JSON; P02 binds these particular originals and
extractions and warns that interim extraction offsets cannot be transferred.

A1 is compatible exposure: swaps change exposure on a portion of fixed-rate
notes without changing the statement that the notes have fixed contractual
rates. A2 compares different measures: the annual table reports principal
97,341 million less net discount/issuance costs 321 and hedge adjustments 358,
giving displayed carrying amount 96,662. A3 compares this with the interim's
96.7 billion for the same September date. The displayed precision differs;
nearest rounding would explain it but is not established as issuer policy.
No incompatible underlying values can be inferred. A4 repeats total assets
364,980 million for September 28 in both filings; 344,085 in the interim is
December 28 and is not a competing value for September.

These four candidates establish no genuine conflict. Annual publication is
not verified in the P02 binding; the interim has regulator filing day
2025-01-31. The earlier manifest's general filing-day wording does not resolve
annual availability or prove either historical byte vintage. These are archival
comparisons, not information-cutoff-qualified assertions. Author inspection is
preparation for proposed extraction review, not actual human review acceptance.

### Group 2: P06/P07 lineage

P07's test helper writes `Revenue 120 units` into a temporary text file. Its
720 reading is deliberately wrong relative to that literal; the later 110
source and its supersession relation are also generated by the fixture. The
separate alternative test supplies a choice group for generated 120 and 110
readings. P06 labels its original, revised and annual sources `fixture:*` and
explicitly connects the revision to the original. These are synthetic examples
of extraction error, stipulated correction and competing alternatives. Calling
P07's transition an “actual correction” in a result note describes an executed
test transition, not an actual issuer correction. Neither group supplies E05's
real conflicting disclosure. The source constructors and relevant assertions
were inspected; no historical suite was rerun or adopted as normative.

### Group 3: retained-index candidate

The source-review disposition index identifies two Ping An interim 2019 reports.
Their original preparation pages were rendered and inspected: IFRS PDF physical
109/printed 107 states IAS 34; the A-share PDF physical 115/printed 112 states
Chinese Accounting Standards and ASBE 32. Both cover the six months ended
June 30, but the accounting basis differs. R1 therefore fails the same-basis
predicate. The index is a navigation aid; the primary preparation notes support
this classification. Other index entries mark identity/completeness failures;
that label does not supply two incompatible assertions and was not promoted to
conflict evidence. This task does not compare IFRS and ASBE accounting treatments.

## Bounded conclusion and required repair

No qualifying pair was established among A1–A4, P1–P2 and R1. This finding is
limited to these inspected passages and constructors, not the whole corpus.
P15-E05 stays open: its real-source requirement is not satisfied. A synthetic
walkthrough can specify the missing layer transitions while future targeted
source research seeks two genuinely incompatible same-context assertions.
Such research needs exact originals/spans, supported context and precision,
and an explicit investigation of revision relations. Unknown chronology must
remain visible even if a real archival conflict is later found.

A new source-locator finding affects the saved evidence contract: Note 9's
fixed-rate table and swap paragraph are on annual PDF physical page 46,
printed 43, not printed 42 as evidence-contract-v2.md says. The v3 successor
corrects that locator only; earlier versions remain historical evidence.

This bounded result awaits substantive independent review. Pending E05 result
review and the absent genuine pair do not prevent the next freshly reviewed
history_families plan. Full independent P16 review is required before acceptance.
