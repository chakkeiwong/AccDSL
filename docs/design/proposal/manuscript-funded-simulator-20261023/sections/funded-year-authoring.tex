\section{Claims, funding and matched annual authoring}
\label{sec:funded-year-authoring}

The early supplier payment also needs an identified obligation. Northstar's
opening payables of 30 and new purchase of 170 make a payable control balance
of 200. In the authored allocation, the payment of 160 reduces the new purchase
claim to 10 while the opening claims remain 30. Opening receivables of 35 and
sales of 300 are settled by collections of 10 and 270 respectively, leaving
25 and 30. These allocations agree with the retained annual totals; they are
explicit scenario inputs, not invoice facts recovered from a filing.

A claim records its origin, recognised amount, adjustments where supported,
settlements and outstanding amount. Recognition and settlement have separate
events. Collecting an existing receivable does not recognise revenue again;
settling a supplier does not recognise the purchase again. After each affected
event, identified claims must sum to the receivable or payable control account.
An excessive settlement rejects before changing either the claim or journal.
The full-year example explicitly supports partial claim settlement; the earlier
full-payment decision subprofile retains its narrower meaning.

\paragraph{Choosing and settling a draw.}
The proposed \code{SettlementDecisionDraft/1} separates cash, the identified
claim, facility knowledge and the chosen action. A facility's usable capacity
depends on its limit, prior drawings and reservations, with eligibility and
the draw window kept explicit. A decision names a facility, amount and expected
settlement instant, or explicitly chooses cash alone. It observes only its
permitted information. Unknown eligibility leaves a necessary draw unresolved;
it does not supply a zero limit or establish default. Available cash may still
support a cash-only decision.

Authorisation produces a proposal. Cash and debt change when a draw settles;
the payable and cash change when the supplier is paid. These are separate
events with separate atomic accounting transitions. A payment that fails after
successful borrowing leaves the borrowing outstanding. The documentary checker
can calculate a conditional successor assuming stipulated successful events,
but that state is explicitly uncommitted. Durable storage and concurrent
reservation semantics still require their own implementation evidence.

\paragraph{Authoring the complete conditional year.}
\code{FundedYearInputsDraft/1} imports an immutable annual projection and names
its source hash, company, book, currency and floating-rate share. It then
specifies the four claim allocations, facility capacity, new principal,
annual rate, day-count convention, fees, tax method, assumptions and a dated
event sequence. The example names zero fees and an eligible facility; it does
not infer either from annual accounts. A nonzero fee is unsupported until a
fee method is supplied. An unknown collection instant remains an unresolved
input with a named obligation.

The restricted text form and typed JSON have identical fields and lower to
the same closed body. Each text line declares a profile, reference, context,
claim, facility, loan term, tax method, assumption or event. Amounts use reduced
rationals; dates use UTC instants. The documentary lexical rule uses quoted
tokens with an explicit semicolon terminator. The grammar and both complete
inputs are retained in
\path{data/accounting-master/funded-authoring-20261023/}. This grammar is a
bounded proposed extension, not a change to the existing product compiler.

The pure reference executor consumes that body and the imported projection.
It generates the added draw, interest and principal events, checks claim
movements, derives tax from the accrued income accounts and closes those
accounts. It does not obtain the result by copying expected closing balances.
The 18 conditional comparisons combine the two retained stress rates, three
floating shares and explicit facility rates of zero, five and seven percent.
Every journal, cash prefix, closing account and statement agrees with the
separate exact annual calculation. The loan's interest and tax effects are
included; at zero interest the original closing accounts return exactly.

These comparisons establish the stated reference meaning under authored
assumptions. They do not establish access to credit, customer payment behaviour,
general economic-policy generation or complete company-language coverage.
The annual operating and accounting model is still imported by reference.
Broader facility terms, long-dated accruals, fees, refinancing and sector rules
retain their separate specification obligations. The reference execution is
conditional and uncommitted; production execution remains a later deliverable.
