\paragraph{Keep accounting profiles and capital assessments distinct.}
The timing successor can be reproduced beside the other bank methods, but
their common transport does not make them one accounting book.
\code{BankComponents/1} represents a conditional historical Australian book
with funded loans, credit memory, deposits, commitments, fees and settlement
liquidity. Capacity and a funding request create no cash; confirmed receipts
and withdrawals do. Its complete receipt chains support annual and lifetime
reports. The separate \code{BankComponents/2} covers transfers, options and
asset classification, including retained exposure, associated liabilities,
interest, impairment and OCI. It explicitly refuses the first profile as a
foreign input. A combined book therefore needs a specified migration or
composition preserving both sets of accounts, legal rights and credit memory.

\code{BankAssessment/1} instead contains the separate synthetic US book in
Section~\ref{sec:bank-prudential-connection}. Its dated events reconstruct the
opening and closing accounts before the prudential query uses them. The case's
equity 23.6, goodwill deduction 2, admitted allowance 1.5 and risk-weighted
assets 119.1 are different quantities. A deposit demand of 40 leaves 9 unpaid
even though the supplied capital ratios are unchanged. A missing period-average
asset input leaves leverage unresolved. These outcomes connect accounting,
funding and prudential consequences within that profile; importing an
Australian book by renaming its accounts would not establish the required
US measurement or applicability findings.

The economic loss query likewise does not estimate the supplied CECL allowance.
It preserves its declared joint cells and candidate laws; policy description
selects no action and grants no distribution permission. The exact-time
successor changes information comparisons and request identities while keeping
those financial definitions. A successful or partial query publishes records
and a receipt, without posting journals or changing the accounting head.

The internal companion at \path{companions/bank-interfaces/} reproduces the
three retained packets and all saved exact-time cases from a separate declared
input copy. It includes full reports, queries, refused requests, retries and
lost-acknowledgement cases, with the original independent financial checks.
The time successor shares those financial methods; its expected outputs are
the retained publications with explicit reference substitution. Historical
Git/index preservation checks remain earlier workspace evidence and are not
recipient reproduction checks. The companion instead verifies every declared
copied input before and after execution, with that difference stated explicitly.

and which still require an adapter. Neither shared transport nor successful
