# Source and applicability for the selected PAA bridge

The inspected retained source is docs/design/research/standards/aasb17.pdf and
its text, the December2022 compilation cited by the selected manuscript. The
text identifies Authorised Version F2023C00382, registered26April2023. This is
an exact retained edition; it does not establish later jurisdictional adoption.

Paragraph53(b) permits PAA where each issued contract's coverage, including
services from all premiums within the contract boundary, is one year or less.
Paragraph69(b) supplies the separate held-contract eligibility route. Paragraph55
starts remaining coverage from premium cash and reduces it by earned service;
the omitted acquisition, financing and investment components are explicit zero
premises here. Paragraph56 and59(b) allow the selected short-period financing
exceptions only under their respective timing conditions. Claims fulfilment
cash flows still include the separate risk adjustment.

Paragraphs62/62A require held recognition at the relevant coverage/underlying
recognition dates; a proportionate treaty cannot be recognised before an otherwise
later underlying contract. Paragraph63 requires non-performance effects in held
cash-flow estimates. Paragraph64 measures risk transferred, not a fixed ratio of
the issued risk adjustment. B126/B127 allocate expected premiums by passage of
time unless expected risk release differs significantly. Uniform service/risk
release and unchanged facts are supplied in this example.

The proposed interpretation is therefore conditional: two separately identified
one-year groups start together; half service earns60 from gross premium120 and
expenses12 from held premium24. Incurred expected claims40 plus retained risk2
less paid30 leave liability12. Covered claims20 plus transferred risk1 less
non-performance0 and recovery cash15 leave held claims asset6. The dossier's
independent alternatives transferred risk3/2 or non-performance2 alter only the
held result. An interpretation that automatically halves every gross liability
or risk adjustment is wrong relative to this specified calculation.

The new calendar is expressly synthetic and gives the exact half-service
fraction183/366. Actual contract boundaries, risk patterns, claims timing,
recoverability, current standards applicability and cash-flow presentation need
their own evidence. Unknown or contradicted supplied findings must produce
unresolved or unsupported outcomes. This source review supports a narrow example
and its tests, not general insurance accounting admission.
