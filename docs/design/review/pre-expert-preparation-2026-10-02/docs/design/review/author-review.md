# Author review of the repaired design

Date: 2 October 2026. Reviewer: the executing Codex author. This is a substantive
author check, not an independent accounting or human comprehension review.
The reviewed version and SHA-256 are fixed in
[`repair-verification.json`](repair-verification.json) and the
[render manifest](repair-render/final/manifest.json).

## Verdict and scope

Version 1.1 is ready to deliver as a repaired design candidate. The manuscript
now explains concrete company histories and accounting consequences before
introducing the general methods. It has considerably stronger specification
and example evidence than the protected 60-page draft. It still cannot be
described as a complete, independently validated specification for every
requested standard, sector and uncertainty method.

Reviewed the changed manuscript sources, contract/schema/grammar, complete
fixtures and check reports, research screening logic, source qualifications,
mission requirements and G1–G18 result map. Compared removed source passages
against the protected baseline. Inspected the rendered document in order
using all contact sheets; inspected full page images for the complete-company
tables, Apple stress, strict-bound derivation, posting rule and diagnostics,
bank/insurance passages, grammar/records and bibliography. A final text-page
comparison identifies the pages changed by the last layout edits so those
pages receive renewed inspection. This visual pass checks presentation; it
does not establish how a human reader understands the work.

## Substantive findings and repairs

- The complete Northstar case supplies opening state, annual/interim flows,
  a source correction, working-capital reconstruction, a financing family and
  complete conditional statements. The original and corrected interim views
  are preserved. Direct and indirect cash routes reconcile.
- The rounded-debt reformulation avoids an unsupported bilinear expression.
  Strict endpoint attainment is distinct from a valid closed enclosure.
  Rational solver checks preserve alternatives instead of choosing a witness
  as the recovered truth.
- Apple principal, carrying amount, fair value and swap notional remain
  different measurements. Source inspection corrected the swap notional to
  14,575 million. The stress deliberately assumes its effective exposure;
  neither the point nor the interval is claimed as Apple's measured exposure.
- Corporate operations, finite probability updates, funding failure,
  consolidation, inventory, compound bonds and PAA/reinsurance now have exact
  examples. A derived credit-loss expression retains the conditional product
  of exposure and severity. The chain-ladder point example does not claim a
  predictive law or an insurance risk adjustment.
- Rounding/display fields, assumption provenance/validity/role, and alternative
  author/date/scope metadata were restored explicitly in the closed schema.
  The interest rule now uses a declared annual-simple rate and `simpleInterest`
  rather than silently treating a rate as dimensionless. The debt source has
  a complete dependency package and authored expected IR. General compilation
  and external-registry validation are still disclosed as unimplemented.
- Individual citation decisions replace generic generated exclusions. The
  generator rejects an unknown candidate before emitting outputs. Screening
  means title/metadata relevance review; adopted technical support has its own
  inspected source anchors. Broader sector coverage remains incomplete.
- Local checks and acceptance are kept separate. Reference assertions are not
  a product test suite; library hashes do not certify source claims; no user
  study, accounting certification or forecast comparison is inferred.

## Fidelity to the protected draft

The automated [comparison](baseline-comparison.json) finds no removed display
equations or citation keys. The source diff also received author inspection:

- Historical, mathematical, execution, sector and implementation material is
  extended rather than removed. The earlier literature and architecture
  discussions retain their source boundaries and qualifications.
- Appendix A's old partial grammar and record sketches are replaced by the
  normative Lark/schema/contract files. Its display/rounding, missingness,
  assumption provenance, evidence clocks, economic-state and result semantics
  are retained in those definitions. The ASCII identifier choice is an
  explicit Core 0 syntax decision; UTF-8 evidence and strings remain intact.
- Two interest-expression lines change to enforce the stated type/accrual
  rules. The old small-financing-only immediate next step is replaced by the
  whole-company and sector-aware implementation sequence. The paper count is
  updated for Mack. These changes strengthen or clarify scope; they do not
  remove the requested domains.
- MC01–MC34 requirement and acceptance columns are compared with the protected
  register. Only assessments and current disposition are updated. Snapshot
  hashes preserve the prior evidence even though the workspace has no usable
  Git revision.

## Presentation

Confirmed repairs include almost-empty chapter spillovers, a posting rule
broken after its opening lines, a split record listing, and an isolated
diagnostic-table row. Code/records now stay together where needed. Bibliography
entries use ragged-right setting to remove excessive justified spacing; URLs,
citations and qualifications remain. The final log has no overfull or underfull
boxes, undefined citations/references or missing characters. Small appendix
grammar text is legible at full size and has an exact machine-readable
companion; contact sheets are only an overview.

The chapter-opening white space and some shorter chapter-ending pages are
retained to preserve coherent examples and headings. No clipping or overlapping
content was found. Human judgments about pacing, natural voice and comprehension
remain pending; an author's visual inspection cannot certify them.

## Unresolved acceptance

The [result map](gap-repair-results-2026-10-02.md) and updated MC register are the
current open-work record. Principal residuals are full real-disclosure semantics
and incorporation closure, primary US/ASBE authority, broader bank/insurance
and hybrid operations, complete compiler/adapter/runtime validation and
independent review. AASB editions are inspected support for selected common
IFRS-equivalent paragraphs, not proof of every current IFRS jurisdictional rule.
The ASBE reproduction is provisional and Caterpillar is company-policy evidence,
not full ASC authority.

The central residual risk is a shared author error between text and fixtures.
An independent implementation or accountant may expose it. Such evidence takes
precedence over the passing reference checks and reopens the affected finding.
No remaining gap has been dismissed merely because the new PDF builds.
