# Common state meanings and exact native ownership

28 October 2026. T01 implementer definition. The18-interface index and
five-profile sector annex remain authoritative for their respective inputs,
signatures, statuses and inspected limitations. This supplement relates their
meanings to the current company-path inspection. It does not create a common
financial evaluator or permit an implicit migration.

## State is relative to a requested operation

A construction request names an exact native owner and the operation, query
or decision it must support. Its relevant state comprises economic resources
and obligations, accounting memory, parameters, information and retained
history. These categories describe responsibilities; they are not interchangeable
fields that every profile must expose under the same names. A report compiler,
an event executor, a source constraint and a prudential query have different
inputs and effects even when each contains a quantity named cash.

The native owner decides admissibility and financial effects. A common caller
must retain its exact status, exceptions or returned refusal, full successful
prefix, failed operation and unexecuted suffix. A wrapper may add a descriptive
classification, but must not replace unknown with zero, partial with complete,
proposed with committed, or a measurement witness with an executable opening.
The original source and canonical request identities remain distinct.

## Commitments and terms are not one universal object

The common definition must preserve supported commitments where they already
exist. BankComponents/1 includes a finite dated loan-commitment example with
supplied draw/default probabilities, an undrawn reserve, full drawing and
expiry conditions. That contract supplies more than the current general
company path. It does not establish arbitrary orders, resource reservations,
contract amendment or mixed-industry composition. Calling the entire repository
commitment-free would be wrong; calling this bank example a universal commitment
engine would also be wrong.

SelectedTermStateContract/1 is the name of a software admission definition.
Its selected native financial meaning concerns receivable terms, assessments,
amendments and collections. A software contract name is not proof of a legal
undertaking. A bridge from these supported terms to another profile must
state the underlying claim identity, unit, amount, timing, old/new terms,
financial-effect owner and preserved query history. The REA commitment reading
motivates those distinctions without supplying the bridge.

## Information and actions

An actor requirement identifies the actor, proposed action or observation,
economic time, decision cutoff, system cutoff and evidence-view membership.
Authority and receipt are separate requirements. A declaration of custody,
responsibility or public availability does not resolve either automatically.
Unknown metadata must remain an obligation attached to the affected request.

Some native profiles already impose conditional information rules.
BankAssessment/1 checks evidence membership and availability/ingestion against
its declared view/cutoffs. BankAssessmentExactTime/1 tightens clock identity.
The certified temporal/native-history join preserves possible source/revision
histories under a separate temporal contract. These are scoped synthetic
information mechanisms. Their presence does not admit a real issuer's
chronology or authorize passing their full audit record to a manager's policy.
The current CompanyStateInspectionRequest/1 and CompanyPolicyEffectRequest/1
therefore continue to refuse public/manager requests.

## Conditions for a proposed mapping

A mapping must name the source and destination profiles and versions, exact
context and population, retained economic identities, accounting and memory
ownership, dates and information rules, units/scales, financial effects and
historical queries. It must describe refusal/unknown propagation and give
complete expected before/after outputs. Empty or similarly named fields do
not establish a mapping. Every monetary effect has exactly one owner.

Reuse the16 existing compatibility edges before proposing new ones. In
particular, SelectedTermProgram/1 and SelectedReadingHistoryAttachment/1 are
already delivered. A general term-event embedding into SelectedCompanyText/1,
direct measurement-to-opening conversion, and arbitrary bank-profile join
remain unsupported. A new common caller should dispatch only reviewed
capabilities and preserve their complete native results, rather than normalize
the entire registry to an invented success/failure convention.

This review supplies common meanings and an owner map. It leaves common
cross-owner request/response bindings and justified actor-action mappings open.
T05 owns industry/basis implementation after its prerequisites; T03/C01 own
actual source and actor admission. Original acceptance conditions remain in
force. The source-hashed registry and inspection record are retained under
data/accounting-master/company-state-owner-semantics-20261028/.
