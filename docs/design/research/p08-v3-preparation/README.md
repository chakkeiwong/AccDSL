# EU authority preparation for the next P08 repair

The official Publications Office route now supplies Commission Regulation
(EU) 2023/1803 and its annex in English. This is a new usable source input for
P08. P08 criterion 3 remains incomplete: no EU comparator has been admitted,
implemented or independently accepted by this preparation.

The retained original PDF prints **13 August 2023** in its title and concluding
formula. The separately retained corrigendum, Official Journal L 239/39 of
28 September 2023, explicitly changes both to **13 September 2023**. Preserve
both documents. The original publication is dated 26 September 2023; Article 5
provides entry into force on the twentieth day following publication, and the
official metadata gives 16 October 2023. These dates alone do not establish
all later accounting applicability.

Sixteen selected quoted passages were checked in the extracted text. These
include adoption and entry into force, IAS 32.22 and .28–32, AG31–AG32, IFRS
9.3.1.1, .3.3.1–3, .4.2.1, .5.1.1 and Appendix A, and IFRS 7.8(g) and .20(b).
Page 229 was also rendered and visually inspected. The full selected quote
record is in `source-preparation.json`. This is selected technical reading,
not a claim that the full 992 pages or all cross-references were inspected.

The metadata supplies amendment and corrigendum links. Four amendment PDFs
were retrieved through their official links and verified over HTTPS: 2023/2468
(IAS 12), 2023/2579 (IFRS 16), 2023/2822 (IAS 1 liability classification and
covenants), and 2024/1317 (IAS 7/IFRS 7 supplier finance disclosures). Their
titles, recitals and application articles were inspected. Their annexes and
consequences for this particular instrument remain to be checked. Several
later candidate entry points returned 404, and the remaining corrigenda have
not been inspected. Metadata and titles cannot establish that an amendment
has no effect on the selected contract.

The next repair should:

1. Check the full applicable amendment/corrigendum set for the 2024–2025 case,
   along with issuer reporting scope and the exact instrument eligibility.
2. Decide whether the retained sources support the same economic facts under
   an explicit EU reporting context. Preserve identical principal, proceeds,
   comparable yield, coupons, funding, shares and settlement assumptions.
3. Implement or precisely specify initial measurement, both annual periods,
   cash redemption and conversion with paragraph-linked calculations. Explain
   supported equivalence or differences; do not invent a numerical difference.
4. Add meaningful comparator and provenance mutation checks and obtain the
   master program's independent result review before phase acceptance.

Historical failed EUR-Lex requests and the partial US/ASBE preparation remain
valid historical evidence and must not be rewritten. The new FASB Codification
landing-page request returned a 403 challenge; it was stopped without bypass.
No professional engagement, paid source, account, or global installation was
used. This source preparation changes no accounting default or acceptance state.
