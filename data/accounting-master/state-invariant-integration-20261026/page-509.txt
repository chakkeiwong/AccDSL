A.54.10
A typed claim can still disagree with the book
The preceding successful histories do not establish that every authored method preserves the
original lifecycle’s economic controls. Suppose an otherwise unchanged sale posts receivables of
30 but creates a claim whose remaining amount is 31. Both values have the right monetary
type, the journal balances, and the memory record satisfies its schema. The original lifecycle
nevertheless rejects that state because the claims do not sum to receivables. Typed-book
validation alone does not express this cross-record relation.
A focused audit changes one claim field at a time in the authored sale and then requests a
report from the genuine period opening. Across two company charts, sixteen cases expose
eight outer-control gaps: claim totals, the sale event’s membership in the book, issue/due
chronology, future issue dates, negative assessment amounts, nonzero unassessed placeholders,
future assessment and an incorrect overdue snapshot. An empty customer string is already
refused by the memory schema. That covered structural rule must not be counted as a missing
economic invariant.
BookStateInvariant/1 supplies the missing connection to the existing collection evaluators. Its
explicit bindings select the book’s date and receivable account, the identified rows of its claim
map and its seen-event identities. Each scalar’s type and context must agree with the declared
input, including for an empty map. Values come from that exact supplied book; a caller cannot
substitute a Boolean assertion that its controls passed.
The unchanged collection evaluator checks unique claim identities, references from each claim
to its sale event and guarded conditions on every row. The unchanged collection-total evaluator
sums remaining amounts and compares that total with receivables. An assessed claim must
have an assessment date between issue and the current date. Its overdue snapshot equals the
assessed amount when assessment occurs strictly after the due date, and zero otherwise. An
unassessed claim retains zero assessment and overdue placeholders. Remaining, assessed and
overdue amounts are nonnegative. These are declared controls of this constructed lifecycle, not
a new impairment model.
The separate GuardedAuthoredCompanyProgram/1 selects the invariant by its exact hash. It checks
the opening, each current book and each proposed after-state. Only a proposed state that
satisfies the selected controls enters the successful prefix. In the 31-versus-30 example, the
proposed sale is refused, the opening book remains current, and the sale and report remain in
the unexecuted suffix. A failed post-state check cannot quietly retain the invalid claim or its
otherwise balanced journal.
Both eleven-command histories retain their complete original results, and all 24 opening and
after-state books pass the original lifecycle control. All sixteen changed-method counterexamples
are refused. Wrong pointers, contexts and field types also refuse. The original evaluators retain
unresolved results for partial or unknown collection inputs, while an unrelated unknown does not
