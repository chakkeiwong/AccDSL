The book advances only after the resulting state also passes its type and invariant checks. Con-
sider an authored sale whose postings increase receivables but whose inserted claim incorrectly
records zero remaining amount. The typed transition can propose that state because each

and opening books, records the refused command, and leaves the complete remaining suffix
unexecuted. It adds no compensating journal or financing event. This is in-memory refusal
behaviour, not a claim of durable database rollback.
The two retained eleven-command programs preserve all complete native command requests
and results, including reports and carry archives, along with their before/after state identities
and final books. Two further eleven-command programs start from the same declared openings
but create three new claims each, using changed keys, customers and amounts. Their report,
year-end carry and following-year assessment also agree. All native lifecycle functions are
disabled during candidate execution. The saved result is checked again after serialization; no
native after-state is supplied to the candidate.
Twenty-six adverse programs include the original eighteen refusal cases, combined unknown/ap-
proval/missing-claim conditions, and negative or excessive collections that fail financial precon-
ditions after eligibility has passed. The latter retain the complete refused request and transition.
Additional controls check stale selections, changed input order, duplicate identities, incomplete
assessment order, a changed carry policy and the invariant-invalid sale. Each stopped run
preserves its successful prefix, current opening, reports and unexecuted suffix.
The comparison concerns complete substantive command payloads. The candidate adds explicit
eligibility, construction and invariant records; the native result carries different implementation
and observation records. Those differences are retained and identified, rather than removed
and described as whole-result equality. Native observations are separately recomputed from
the candidate states. Shared transition, report and carry evaluators remain the owners of their
respective financial calculations.
Opening admission is still a separate native prerequisite in this comparison. The original
validation checks each retained opening/program before native functions are disabled. The
candidate checks its own structure, types and authored invariant, and explicitly reports that it
has not evaluated the full native opening contract. Role selection, initial population conventions,
opening cursor, unit-cost binding, policy and opening report validation must therefore be audited
before this dependency can be replaced. The earlier company-source frontend also retains its
own native-contract target. These finite execution results do not establish general opening
admission, a complete financial language, accounting validity or independent implementation
acceptance.
