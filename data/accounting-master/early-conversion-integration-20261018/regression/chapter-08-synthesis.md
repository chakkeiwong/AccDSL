Event timing and state changes then acquire explicit safeguards. Declared
phases and dependencies must expose materially ambiguous order. A method
assessment, permission, journal and independent economic delta bind the same
event basis and opening state; balanced arithmetic alone is insufficient.
The lost-acknowledgement sale earns the distinction between a stable business
key and a complete request fingerprint. Validation precedes replay, and replay
precedes freshness, so an exact retry returns its receipt after the head has
advanced. State, journal, receipt and head commit together; a correction is a
new linked event. An unknown timeout preserves the original request.

Simultaneous funding, tax and interest relations require declared equations,
admissibility, residuals and multiplicity checks. Unlimited borrowing cannot
rescue an infeasible branch. The fixed/floating debt returns with a new payoff:
the same past balance now generates expense 5 or 7, cash 90 or 88, and equity
minus10 or minus12 under the stated stress. No branch probabilities follow.
The bond schedule similarly separates noncash accretion, carrying amount,
contractual payment and market value. These are consequences of retained
economic state, not merely differently named accounts.

overselling, overcollection and hidden balancing movements. The twenty-percent
tax and same-date accrual/payment remain demonstration choices rather than
universal methods. Explanation graphs trace results but cannot certify the
economic model from which they arose.
