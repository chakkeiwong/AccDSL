There is one important qualification to the ordinary method choice. Under C5A, a group with
direct participation features may elect fair-value transition even when retrospective application
is possible, if the entity chooses the B115 risk-mitigation option prospectively from transition
and used qualifying mitigation before that date. The applicable instrument, qualification and
designation conditions still matter. Treating every fair-value election as proof of impracticability
would therefore be wrong (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2022f, paragraphs C3(b),
C5A and B115–B118).
The reconstruction must consequently retain the method and its basis for each group, each
modification used, the information date and evidence supporting it, the separately measured
FCF, CSM or loss component, relevant OCI and acquisition balances, and the opening-equity
reconciliation. Some transition rules expressly permit transition-date information for specified
assessments. That permission concerns the accounting restatement; it does not make a later
report admissible in a forecast made before its publication. Detailed impracticability judgments
under AASB 108 and valuation under AASB 13, together with actual group facts, remain
necessary before this method description can produce a supported opening measurement.
