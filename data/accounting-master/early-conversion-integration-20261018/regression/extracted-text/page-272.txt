A revolving facility does not necessarily use its short cancellation notice as its loss horizon.
Under AASB 9.5.5.20 and B5.5.39–40 (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2022h, 5.5.20
and B5.5.39–40), some jointly managed drawn/undrawn facilities expose the lender beyond that
notice period because normal risk-management actions do not prevent those losses. Admit the
exposure period from the facility’s features, comparable historical exposure/default experience
and expected risk actions. A one-day cancellation clause and a three-year assumed horizon are
both insufficient alone. Missing evidence leaves the horizon unresolved; the mechanical capacity
rollforward does not calibrate it.
11.10.2
Fee purpose determines whether it enters yield
Under AASB 9.B5.4.1–4 (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2022h, B5.4.1–4), fees
integral to origination or a probable specific lending arrangement enter effective interest for
instruments measured at amortised cost. A fee’s name does not settle its purpose. Servicing
fees, commitment fees when a specific loan is unlikely, and qualifying syndication fees instead
follow AASB15. Preserve the services promised, probability of a specific loan, instrument
classification, timing, recipient and any allocation between distinct purposes. For instruments
at fair value through profit or loss, the source’s treatment of integral fees differs; do not copy
the amortised-cost deferral.
Suppose a probable specific loan attracts commitment fee 2. Receive cash 2 and defer the fee;
receipt alone is not service revenue. On origination, advance contractual principal 100 and
incorporate the deferred fee in the loan’s carrying amount, giving 98. A single contractual
receipt of 107.8 one year later gives effective interest
r = 107.8/98 −1 = 10%,
98r = 9.8.
Accrete 9.8 and collect 107.8. Net cash from the fee and loan is 2 −100 + 107.8 = 9.8, equal to
interest income. A supplied zero loss allowance isolates fee timing in this example; fee receipt
does not imply zero credit risk. If the commitment instead expires without a loan, B5.4.2(b)
recognises the fee as revenue on expiry. This branch contains no loan carrying amount.
If a fee is outside effective interest, determine revenue under the separate customer-service
method. Calling it a service fee is not permission to recognise it immediately. For a separately
admitted two-period stand-ready service with equal service each period and total consideration
2, the same cash receipt releases revenue 1 per period from a contract liability. A different
obligation or satisfaction pattern changes that timing. The exact services must be supplied
before this branch can be applied.
