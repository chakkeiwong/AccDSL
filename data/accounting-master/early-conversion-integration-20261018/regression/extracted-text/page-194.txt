Capacity still does not tell the employee what will be delivered or when. Section 9 permits
restricted-stock-unit agreements to differ: the agreement specifies vesting, settlement may be in
shares, cash or a combination, and delivery may be deferred under its terms. Dividend-equivalent
rights may be granted; the units themselves carry no voting rights. The filed time-based form
then makes a more specific promise. Its Section 7 provides for shares equal to vested units less
the applicable tax-related withholding, delivered promptly and no later than two and one-half
months after vesting, subject to the stated conditions. The plan’s wider permission does not
replace that form’s narrower promise (Apple Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.1, Section 9; Exhibit 10.2,
Sections 7 and 11).
The form’s grant notice leaves the recipient, quantity, award date and vesting schedule blank.
Those blanks matter: the annual report’s general four-year practice cannot fill every individual
notice. For example, the 2025 proxy describes named executives’ 1 October 2023 time-based
awards as vesting in three equal annual instalments beginning 1 April 2026, over approximately
four and one-half years. It also describes termination terms under which earned or vested rights
and delivery dates must be kept separate: Cook’s retirement treatment does not accelerate the
originally scheduled delivery of shares or dividend equivalents (Apple Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.2,
grant notice and Section 8) (Apple Inc., 2025b, printed pp. 51 and 53–54). These examples
establish that a general vesting convention cannot substitute for the applicable award; they do
not identify every employee’s terms or a complete amendment history.
For the simulation, the consequence is concrete. Retain the plan version and reserve rule
alongside the actual award terms, vesting conditions, units earned, settlement medium, delivery
date and withholding choice. A permitted plan alternative becomes a branch for a particular
award only when its terms or explicitly stated unresolved facts allow it. Otherwise cash-
settlement paths would be invented for a share-only promise, or vesting would create shares
before their contractual delivery. Tax amounts, accounting expense and diluted EPS require
their own facts and methods. The performance-award form poses the next question: how are
units earned when the contractual performance schedule itself has not been filled in?
10.4
Performance conditions determine units before settlement
The performance form turns the distinction between a plan and a grant into a calculation
problem. Its notice leaves the target quantity, grant date, performance period and vesting
schedule blank. Exhibit A likewise leaves the percentile thresholds and payout percentages
blank, although it specifies interpolation between thresholds, a cap when Apple’s absolute
return is negative, and rounding to whole units. The form therefore supplies part of the method
without supplying an individual award’s inputs (Apple Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.3, notice and
Exhibit A).
The 2025 proxy provides a concrete schedule for the named executives’ 1 October 2023 per-
formance awards. It describes a three-year performance period from the beginning of fiscal
2024 through the end of fiscal 2026, with vesting on 1 October 2026 subject to the agreements.
This is a dated description of that grant population, not the result of the performance period.
