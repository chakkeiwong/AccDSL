C.2
Version and access qualifications
Several discrepancies mattered. Doshi’s thesis is dated 2024 despite the original filename. The
archived Mernik text is the 2003 CWI report underlying the 2005 article. Giacomini and
Kitagawa’s copy is the April 2020 working paper underlying the 2021 article. The Docling
v5 copy lists Auer as first author. The probabilistic-programming tutorial is an arXiv version
revised in 2021, first submitted in 2018; a merged index DOI was not used.
The Libkin
author PDF has a footer DOI that resolves to another paper; title/author metadata from the
indexes resolves the cited work to DOI 10.1145/2594538.2594561. These discrepancies remain
documented rather than being hidden by a cleaned bibliography.
McCarthy (1982), Imieliński and Lipski (1984), Tamer (2010), and Caiani and co-authors
(2016) are retained as important blocked candidates. Access failures and rejected wrong-content
downloads are recorded. The design does not use their uninspected technical claims. More
specialised work on accounting measurement, process mining, multi-company equilibrium,
actuarial inference and learned forecasting is recorded as expansion work for the corresponding
implementation stage. The survey supports the core architecture without pretending that those
later modules have a complete literature or rule base.
