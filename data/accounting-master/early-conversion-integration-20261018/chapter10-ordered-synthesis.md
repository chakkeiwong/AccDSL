## The chapter's argument

The opening debt reconciliation establishes why a balance movement is not a
cash event or a complete contract history. The dated share example carries
that distinction into EPS: the denominator follows actual share days, while
assumed options, conversion and preference rights change the hypothetical
earnings and shares. The fixed-additive selection proof supplies a bounded
ordering argument, with signed earnings and a positive denominator. Its
inventory and completeness checks prevent a known series from disappearing
because its consequences are unresolved.

The new actual-conversion discussion answers a different question from the
assumed-conversion EPS calculation. The holder exercises the unchanged original
equity-classified right. Liability 9,200 transfers to equity, original equity
800 stays there and remaining interest 400 is not accelerated. BDO section 4.5
is identified as the selected practice interpretation; AG32's express maturity
wording, IFRIC19 exclusion, IFRS9 alternative and IAS8 hierarchy remain visible.
Tax effects are supplied premises. Potential shares for the first 92 days and
actual shares for the next 92 days occupy separate windows. The separate
company-to-EPS exchange still converts at maturity; its differing tax-base
case follows current and deferred tax through earnings rather than reusing the
initial equity charge as an EPS addback.

The Apple share and award material shows why aggregate movement, shares
delivered, plan capacity, expense and an individual award's contract are
different observations. Performance schedules, survival conditions, delivery
dates and later forms supply distinct conditional calculations. Repurchase
and inducement then separate liability/equity allocation, effective amendment,
legal release and delivery. These passages preserve the contrast needed to
understand the unchanged-original-right conversion.

The hedge sequence explains cumulative measurement before reserve release.
The actual derivative and hypothetical item have different jobs; a balanced
journal can still use the wrong payoff. Rebalancing and cessation retain
acquisition values, designation dates and frozen reserve origins. Perfectly
matched finite examples permit a derived allocation, while unmatched or signed
heterogeneous attribution remains unresolved. Later inventory sale and NRV
recovery retain only unsold cost and allowance. Equal terminal balances cannot
validate earlier profit or funding states.

Share awards and participation bring the EPS argument back to rights and
conditional earnings. Service, market conditions, settlement choice and
modification clocks enter separately. The capped-family proof requires complete
additive gross effects. A common bonus breaks that premise: conditional worlds
are calculated, but the text does not claim a general dilution-selection rule.
The participating-dividend tax example permits a negative later adjustment to
reverse an earlier allocation without inventing a cash dividend or tax asset.

The group cases then explain why legal books and group adjustments need
distinct identities. Control is supported and dated rather than inferred from
ownership alone. Goodwill, NCI, profit, cash translation and currency reserves
have separate origins. A retained-interest disposal removes the whole
subsidiary's cash, so a positive parent receipt can accompany negative group
investing cash flow. Intragroup balance elimination does not remove currency
exposure. Transaction matching, inflation restatement, first-application tax
and purpose-specific exchangeability extend this dependence argument while
preserving the narrower closed group profile.
