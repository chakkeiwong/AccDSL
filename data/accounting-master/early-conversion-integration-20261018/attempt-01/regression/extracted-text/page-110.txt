Issuance debits cash and credits the liability by 100. Each accretion debits interest expense
and credits the liability. Redemption debits the liability and credits cash by 110.25. Legal face
amount, carrying amount and market fair value remain separate fields. A market-rate shock
may change fair value without changing this contractual payment schedule or this demonstration
amortised-cost path.
Cash-flow reporting uses the actual payment event and its economic components; the annual
accretion is non-cash. The applicable reporting package decides how to classify the maturity
payment, rather than inferring it solely from the liability debit. Fees, modifications, early
redemption, embedded options, hedges and foreign currency require additional event and
measurement rules.
Ordinary equity issuance similarly separates cash, share quantity and legal capital. Ten new
shares at 12, with par value 1, produce cash of 120, share capital of 10 and premium of 110
under a simple par-value profile. Shares outstanding increase by ten units. Direct issuance costs
of 3 reduce the specified equity account and cash under the chosen illustrative treatment. The
accounting rule and the cost’s eligibility must be explicit; this example does not prescribe every
jurisdiction’s treatment.
8.9
Reporting and explanations
The reporting engine produces the balance sheet, income statement, comprehensive income,
cash-flow statement, changes in equity and note schedules when the necessary modules are
present.
Each output carries its basis, perimeter, period, scenario and interpretation.
A
comparative table cannot combine an original prior-year figure and a restated current-year
comparative without displaying that choice.
An explanation is a dependency graph with typed edges: extracted from, interpreted as, assumed
by, constrained by, generated by, measured by, posted by, aggregated into and supersedes. A
cash-flow line must lead back to payment events; a fair-value movement must lead back to
valuation inputs; a consolidated amount must show eliminations. A provenance path establishes
how a result was obtained. It does not by itself establish that the underlying economic model is
correct.
