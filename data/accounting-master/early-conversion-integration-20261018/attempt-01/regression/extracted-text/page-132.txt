Recognising a separate obligation
ProvisionState retains obligating past event, present legal/constructive duty, outflow assessment,
estimate distribution, settlement dates, risk/discount basis, reimbursements and evidence. IAS
37.14 requires present obligation, probable outflow and reliable estimate; .36–47 governs best
estimate/risk/discount. An intended future operating expenditure without that duty is not a
provision. Assume an established obligation to pay 121 in two years, deterministic amount,
appropriate 10% rate and no double-counted risk: recognize expense/provision 100, accrete
interest 10 then 11, settle 121. The passage-of-time increase is borrowing cost under 60. If the
obligation is an eligible asset restoration cost, initial debit follows that asset’s method rather
than always expense; later changes need the applicable restoration interpretation. Contingent
liabilities/disclosures remain visible when recognition conditions are not met; no generic 0.5
threshold may substitute for the relevant standard’s required analysis.

rights and obligations while retaining their earlier measurement histories.
