12.8.2
Reinsurance and direct participation
Reinsurance has its own group, boundary, recognition time, counterparty risk, cash-flow,
CSM/cost and possibly loss-recovery component under AASB 17 paragraphs 60–70A. Do not
multiply every gross insurance liability by a quota share: excluded claims, limits, deductibles,
timing, reinstatements and counterparty non-performance matter. Claim 100 with an eligible
50% recovery but expected 90% collectability gives conditional expected recovery 45, not 50;
altering transferred insurance risk and reinsurer non-performance are different inputs. This
simple calculation does not establish treaty recognition or RA by itself. Gross liability 100
remains; reinsurance recovery is a separate asset/measurement with its own authority.
For direct participation, test B101’s conditions using the actual underlying items, substantial-
share and variation judgments. Retain underlying fair values, entity fee share and guarantee/risk-
mitigation choices; paragraphs 45 and B101–B118 specify the variable-fee adjustments to CSM,
with onerous and mitigation exceptions. A local component illustration of a qualifying share:
underlying-value increase 20 and entity’s fixed 10% share gives candidate variable-fee change
2; eligibility, guarantees and other FCF changes must be evaluated before posting. The full
option/guarantee valuation and transition calculations are further supported profiles, with
source and numerical obligations, not an assumed linear fee model.
Eligibility depends on the contract and the insurer’s expectations at inception. The policyholder
must participate in a clearly identified pool, with the required substantial share of fair-value
returns and substantial variability in payments. The variability assessment covers the contract’s
duration on a probability-weighted present-value basis. A minimum guarantee can bind in
some scenarios without automatically excluding direct participation. Later market movements
do not reopen the classification; contract modification follows its own rules. The identified
reference pool also need not be held by the insurer, so the contractual link and the actual asset
holdings require separate records (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2022f, paragraphs
B101–B108 and 72).
