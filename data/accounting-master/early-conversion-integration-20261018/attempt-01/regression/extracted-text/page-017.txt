CHAPTER 2.
A COMPLETE COMPANY AND A REAL DISCLOSURE
8
Delivery follows purchase. Its four journal lines recognise the receivable and revenue together
with cost of sales and the reduction in inventory. For the first half-year, delivery of fifteen units
leaves five units costing 25. Separating the revenue and inventory effects into independently
committable events could expose a state in which the customer has the goods but the seller still
reports them. Treating delivery as one economic event prevents that intermediate inconsistency.
Collections then precede the payments they fund. This order establishes a feasible aggregate
history; it does not recover the dates of individual invoices.
The portable company example retains twenty-two successive states and journal steps: ten
economic events and a closing entry in each half-year. Each step identifies its prior state,
economic inputs, accounting template and resulting state. The corrected June statements and
December statements refer to this same chain. The December closing state, with cash 74.6
and equity 90.6, is also the explicit opening of the 2025 projection. Consequently, changing a
historical purchase or tax payment cannot leave the future opening silently unchanged. These
are proposed histories; publishing them as candidates does not post them to an accounting
book.
The rate comparison checks a different connection. Recall that x is the floating share of the
unchanged principal of 100. For an imposed floating rate r, the fixed part earns interest at 5%,
so annual interest is
I(x; r) = 100(1 −x)0.05 + 100xr = 5 + (100r −5)x.
(2.4)
At 7%, increasing x from zero to one adds 2 to interest; at 9%, it adds 4. Under the stipulated
20% tax rule, profit and cash therefore fall by 1.6 or 3.2 respectively. The second rate supplies
a useful control: two authoring frontends might agree because they share an erroneous constant.
Independent arithmetic must recover the changed slope from the changed rate. The reference
exchanges retain the exact expression for every x ∈[0, 1], including its dependence across cash,
profit, assets and equity. They attach no probabilities to the endpoints or to intermediate debt
allocations.
The opening also determines what kind of forecast this is. The corrected December and interim
information becomes public in March 2025, after the January origin of the scenario. Using
it is an archival conditional exercise: given that corrected opening, what follows from these
imposed decisions and rates? It cannot establish what an investor could have forecast in January.
An investor-information request at that earlier cutoff rejects the later disclosure dependency.
Separately, a management policy cannot read a future realised collection merely because the
simulation scheduler already stores it. An imposed scenario assumption and a later observed
outcome have different information meanings.
Partial information must survive the same chain. If the fraction of current sales collected is
missing, delivery can still determine revenue and cost of sales. Cash, receivables and the ability
to fund the proposed settlements remain unresolved. The partial report identifies its affected
lines and retains the full debt family. If the tax method is unavailable, profit after tax, retained
earnings and the final closing entry are unresolved as well. A zero in a temporary account would
incorrectly suggest that closing had been completed. The portable values therefore carry an
explicit reason and its outstanding obligation wherever the calculation is unavailable.
There is a similar distinction in reading a note. The separate authored phrase “substantially
all customers pay promptly” does not imply a 90% threshold. An analyst may impose an all-
payers assumption for a conditional calculation, but its decision must preserve the unquantified
source reading. Likewise, interpreting a displayed 100 as nearest-unit rounding requires an
explicit rounding assumption. With ties rounded upward, that assumption permits values
in [99.5, 100.5); absent the assumption, the display alone supplies no such interval. Neither
decision grants permission to post a journal.
