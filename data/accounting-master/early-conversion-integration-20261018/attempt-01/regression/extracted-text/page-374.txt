A.29
Company-to-EPS projection
CompanyEPSJoin/1 is the finite read-only bridge illustrated in Section 10.2.7. Its request has
exactly the fields wire, operation, world, base_packet, premises and eps. The operation is report;
world is conversion or redemption. The base reference gives the path, SHA256 and anchor of the
preserved CompanyComposition/1 attempt03 packet, pinned to that packet’s recorded manifest
hash. The premise record supplies the instrument, dates, rate, tax bases, tax consequences,
opening share count and completeness findings taught in the example. The proposed EPS
request obeys OrderedEPS/1. Exact closed Request/Result schemas and the complete premise
record accompany the company-EPS evidence directory.
The bridge resolves protected source bytes and checks their hashes, reproduces the original
company, merges the added journals in date order, recalculates tax and consolidates the resulting
books. Its head is the hash of the complete closing legal books; its evidence view is the hash of
the supplied premises. Both use sorted compact UTF-8 JSON with ASCII escaping and no
trailing newline. The EPS record must exactly match the derived population, period, class,
share basis, currency, earnings, weighted shares and potential/actual windows. Thus group
profit, separate-parent profit and closing share counts cannot be substituted for the required
ordinary numerator and weighted denominator.
The result has exactly wire, request_sha256, status, company, eps, issues, mutations and
acceptance.
Company contains both complete legal books, the financial statement recon-
ciliation, every dated journal and cash origin, and the daily ordinary/potential-share history.
EPS contains the complete ordered report. The accepted status is conditional_complete; a
failed company input leaves both results null. A rejected proposed EPS projection can retain
the independently calculated company result while returning null EPS. Missing bytes or in-
complete findings are unresolved; different known methods are unsupported; invalid shapes,
changed bytes or projection mismatches reject. Mutations are always empty and acceptance is
conditional_documentary_only. The schemas also retain the selector’s combined blocked status
for a propagated unresolved/unsupported result.
This exchange checks two explicit documentary worlds. It supplies neither an arbitrary-company
import nor a migration of the whole financing profile. The separate early-conversion policy
in Section 10.2.1 is limited to the unchanged original equity right; this exchange still checks
conversion at maturity. Other early-conversion terms, differing tax bases, broader rights, actual
authority and live publication require their separate methods and evidence. Independent
technical and human/domain acceptance remain pending.
