9.7
Apple: recognising tax and settling the obligation
The lease example separates a tax charge from the payment that follows it. Apple’s fiscal 2024
disclosures show why that separation matters when several tax jurisdictions, deferred balances
and a large disputed obligation enter the same accounts. Its annual income-tax expense was
29,749 million dollars; net cash paid for income taxes was 26,102 million (Apple Inc., 2024a,
printed pp. 29 and 33). The 3,647 difference is not, by itself, an increase in tax payable. Deferred
tax and other balance movements must first be distinguished from current tax and settlement.
