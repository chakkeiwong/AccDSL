C.1
Source collection and reading scope
The companion library contains 32 verified paper PDFs: 25 retrieved from external sources
and seven preserved copies from the original collection. Filenames use the title, first author’s
surname and year. Publication years are used where an archived working version underlies a
later publication; the catalogue identifies the actual version read. The original collection has not
been renamed or modified. Standards pages, contract specifications, software source snapshots
and metadata are stored separately from papers.
The survey considered accounting algebra and DSLs, corporate and bank simulation, incomplete
and temporal databases, provenance, inverse problems, partial identification, model averaging,
causal scenarios, contract languages, probabilistic programming, document extraction and
financial reasoning. OpenAlex and Crossref supplied dated metadata, while primary publisher,
institutional and author copies supplied technical support. Public-index results were checked
against the downloaded title pages; HTTP success alone was not accepted as a valid paper
download.
Technical reading was targeted to the mechanisms used in this design.
The full PDF is
archived even where only specified sections support the argument. No claim is made that every
theorem, appendix or empirical result in every archived paper was independently replicated.
