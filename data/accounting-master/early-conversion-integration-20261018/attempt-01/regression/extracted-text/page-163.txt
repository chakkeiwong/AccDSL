The remaining funds also retain their asset classifications. At December 2024, restricted cash and
cash equivalents were 126 million dollars and restricted current marketable securities 3.3 billion
(Apple Inc., 2025c, Note 4, printed p. 8). Their displayed sum, 3,426 million, is compatible
with the rounded 3.4 billion obligation. It is not all cash available for ordinary operations. The
opening 2.6 billion of restricted cash alone could not have funded the reported 11.9 billion
payment; securities sales, maturities, transfers and valuations need their own histories.
For an isolated settlement illustration, suppose 11,900 million of restricted cash is available and
the amount paid equals the liability’s carrying value at settlement. Debit tax payable and credit
restricted cash by 11,900. Assets and liabilities each fall by 11,900; equity and current-period
tax expense do not change in this posting. This assumes the required cash conversion has
already occurred and omits any associated gain, loss or fee. It explains settlement of an existing
obligation without pretending to reconstruct Apple’s funding and currency entries. The quarter
separately reports tax expense of 6,254 and net cash taxes of 18,651 million (Apple Inc., 2025c,
printed pp. 1 and 5); neither should be replaced with the Ireland payment or treated as another
copy of the preceding year’s charge.
9.7.3
Uncertain tax positions do not supply a payment distribution
The last part of Note 7 preserves another population: gross unrecognised tax benefits. Its 2024
movement is 19,454 + 1,727 −386 + 2,542 −1,070 −229 = 22,038 million. The increases concern
prior- and current-year positions; the decreases concern changed prior-year positions, settlements
and expired limitation periods. Interest and penalties are excluded from this rollforward (Apple
Inc., 2024a, Note 7, printed p. 41). The 1,070 settlement-related decrease is a movement in that
gross balance, not an independently identified total of cash paid to tax authorities.
