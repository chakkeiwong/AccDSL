# Cross-mechanism policy evidence contract

27 October2026. MC03/04/11/12/13/14/18/24/25/26/28/33/34;
W03/04/11/12/13/16. Design/reference computation, not product implementation.

Question: when does preserving separate feasible policy sets fail to produce one
implementable policy across possible observation mechanisms? Exact baseline:
protected receipt7/committed/refined financial rows, each with four feasible
reachable policies in the accepted reference. Compare an enumerative common-history
solver with an independently authored forbidden-action constraint formulation.
Do not intersect differently labeled tables.

Mechanism A transmits L/H/X for (low,no trigger)/(high,no trigger)/(high,trigger).
Mechanism B swaps H and X. This is an explicit synthetic encoding alternative,
not a description of a real actor, company, filing or communication system.
The unchanged financial rows imply pay at H under A and draw at H under B.
Each separate mechanism therefore remains feasible, but a shared untagged first
message is incompatible. Observing the mechanism at date1 should restore the
Cartesian policy product (16); observing it only at date2 cannot repair an
already incompatible first instruction (zero). These are hand-derived primary
case expectations. Exact policy-set equality with the independent solver and
known relabelings is required, not just matching these counts.

Second dates retain the full first message, the actor's own action and the
available second message. The mechanism identifier enters only at a declared
revelation date1/date2, or never. The message itself can reveal information, but
internal proof/source/row/mechanism IDs never become observations without that
assumption. Rows for distinct mechanisms retain separate support even when their
financial histories coincide. No probabilities are assigned.

The existing timing family with coarse/refined alternatives contains a member
with zero policies. It must remain infeasible jointly under any revelation
regime; observing the mechanism does not reveal an unavailable refined signal.
An unresolved source outcome with no observation map keeps the policy question
unresolved. A budgeted temporal family is incomplete, not a selected universe.

The original financial rows do not provide a late-only conflict. Use a separate,
clearly labeled two-date cash decision table: first instruction is wait; at date2,
world cash1 has due1/no draw right (only pay), world cash0 has due1/draw right1
(only draw). Mechanisms swap the two message meanings. Each observed mechanism
has one feasible policy, hidden mechanism has none, and revelation at date2
restores one policy because the first action was harmless. This is a synthetic
liquidity illustration, not a journal, acquisition or financial-report method.
Also include an ambiguous first message followed by informative second messages
with no mechanism revelation, and a first instruction affecting the reachable
second history, to check learning and own-action memory.

Primary criterion: complete reachable policy sets agree with the independent
constraint formulation, exact baseline relabeling and hand-derived conflict/
resolution cases; all original financial rows and family supports survive.
Promotion veto: leaked hidden tags, missing support, anticipation, impossible
message mapping, unsupported observations, incorrect reachable-cell projection
or lost unresolved family. Continuation veto: corrupted protected evidence or
invalid comparison. Correct infeasibility is expected evidence, not a global stop.
Counts/runtime are explanatory; no statistical ranking or performance claim.

Default audit: L/H/X and swapped maps are test hypotheses. Their justification
is a concrete indistinguishability counterexample. A misleading result would
arise if the actor was assumed to know the mapping while the solver hid it; both
observed and unobserved regimes are therefore explicit. Cash-table actions and
feasibility are a new synthetic scope with no accounting inference. No map is
chosen as a product default. Fixed action domains and finite full enumeration
bound the proof; broad hidden-state, memory and continuous domains remain open.

Skeptical audit passes with these explicit regimes and baselines. No causal or
probability claim, empirical actor-knowledge assertion or policy optimality follows.
Run a cheap baseline/conflict diagnostic first, then a120second full comparison
with at most three preserved attempts. Save source hashes, expected sets, full
rows, clauses, policies, manifest and result. No accepted writer reruns, agents,
GPU, installs, fees, outreach, actual financial posting or publication.

Pre-full-run refinement: the late cash case uses only wait at date1 and pay/draw
at date2, with explicit cash/right feasibility. No invented preparation funding
is introduced. Own-action memory is retained and checked in every second history;
under deterministic perfect recall it can be reconstructed from the first history
and policy, so this run does not claim an independent memory-loss impossibility
result. Missing action rows must refuse rather than silently make an action safe.
Original financial/world completeness is additionally bound to protected evidence.
