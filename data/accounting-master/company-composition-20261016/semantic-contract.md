## Closed exchanges

Canonical UTF-8 JSON rejects duplicate keys, floats, non-finite values and
unreduced rationals. Immutable record hashes bind kind, closed schema and exact
body. Typed references resolve transitively to exact records/blobs. The profile
locks this contract, schemas, source bytes and opening factual premises.
Each event has an explicit type, year/date, legal owners, source/view and facts.

Authorise validates the current view/head and method premises. Propose returns
the checked legal journals, shared closing state and cash origins. Commit
atomically publishes these, its receipt and identity decision while unrelated
owners remain unchanged. Group reports are read-only and recompute their joins.
Exact retry returns the immutable stored decision before checking a later
head/view; identity reuse with changed bytes conflicts. Refusal, missing facts
and unsupported methods leave the entire world unchanged. A lost acknowledgement
means unknown commit; retry distinguishes no commit from complete commit and
never repeats effects. Partial publication is invalid.
