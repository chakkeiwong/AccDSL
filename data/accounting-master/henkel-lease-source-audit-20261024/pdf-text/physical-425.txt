HENKEL ANNUAL REPORT 2025 
As part of its strategic supplier management concept, Henkel offers selected 
suppliers around the world the option to participate in its supplier finance 
arrangements (supplier finance programs). Further details are discussed in 
Note 21 on page 399. The payment targets agreed with suppliers for the 
relevant trade accounts payable are not contractually tied to the existence 
of supplier finance arrangements. They are used to finance suppliers and do 
not therefore expose Henkel to any material liquidity or concentration risk.  
Overall, the liquidity risk of the Group is therefore very low.
