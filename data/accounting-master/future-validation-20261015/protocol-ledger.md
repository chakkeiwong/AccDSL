## Required future admission and budget records

The run contract must contain target, unit/perimeter/basis, origin and horizon,
original-data and outcome hashes, availability evidence and exposure audit,
development/test assignment with embargo for overlapping outcome windows,
comparator implementations and equal tuning caps, decision-justified primary
margin and inference method (or explicit descriptive-only purpose), validity
roles, repair rules, resource limits, and future execution authorization.
All fitted scalers, hyperparameters and rule selections are frozen from training.
One field cannot be filled with a promise to decide after seeing test outcomes.

B0 exact illustration checks are deterministic and use independent derivations.
B1 fixed exposed data and three convenience seeds are debugging only. Its
measured costs and variability inform a separate B2 resource/sample plan; no
automatic growth loop is specified. B2 cannot be called untouched confirmation
on this already inspected historical panel. B3 needs genuinely sequestered new
data/annotations or participants plus a justified sample and uncertainty design.
No arbitrary N, chain length, epochs or CPU-hours are promoted by this document.
Target-specific numbers remain an explicit pre-run dependency, not missing
generic method semantics.

Pre-mortem: successful scoring code can compare different information sets,
different reporting vintages, easier surviving cases or uncalibrated laws; a
failed run can instead reveal data/encoding/tuning defects. Cheap date-order,
finite-score, target-identity and failure-denominator checks distinguish these.
Post-write red team: the strongest limitation is that this is a future protocol
with no actual eligible test population or power calculation. It specifies how
to decide; it does not establish that the existing panel can support a ranking.
