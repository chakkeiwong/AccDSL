## Shared deliverable and evidence contract

For each actual residual operation, supply its input/output contract, state
owner, temporal/jurisdictional applicability, exact source anchors, algorithm
and ordering, journal and non-journal changes, report consequences, numerical
capability, unknown/error behavior, and positive/adverse worked case. Separate
issuer facts, reported assertions, author assumptions and permitted treatments.
Record a proposed answer, strongest competing interpretation and the facts or
authority that would distinguish them. Reuse exact unchanged source judgments
with their original scope; inspect changed dependencies.

Keep one concise dossier per unit under
`data/accounting-master/p17-completion-20261014/<unit>/`, except P17-01, which
finishes the existing SQL inquiry directory. Each dossier needs a substantive
result with sources, checked calculations/expected exchanges where relevant,
remaining claims, and a P18 destination or reason for no edit. A small manifest
records the Git revision, actual commands/environment, input/output paths and
hashes, timing, and plan/result. Mark seeds or GPU status N/A when no numerical
framework or stochastic run is involved. Do not generate administration in
place of completing a method.

Pass means a reviewer can reconstruct the specified behavior and its limits.
Wrong source/version/applicability, an unproved transfer, a contradictory
state transition or an inconsistent calculation vetoes the affected claim and
triggers repair. It does not veto the entire direction. Source counts,
balanced journals and hash checks are explanatory/integrity evidence only.
No runtime compatibility, accounting compliance, calibrated forecast or method
superiority follows from these documentary cases.
