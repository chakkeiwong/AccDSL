# Conditional histories retain symbolic openings and unresolved obligations

24 October 2026. ConditionalMonetaryHistory/1 represents one explicitly authored
linear monetary book with shared opening-account/state variables, fixed balanced
template incidence, dated nonnegative event amounts and snapshot measurements.
The complete supplied definition is retained; one witness is not an enumeration
of its continuous family. Civil-date chronology and same-day event order are
explicit. Nonmonetary units, general contracts/cohorts, endogenous events,
nonlinear valuation, operational preconditions and accounting admission remain
outside this finite reference scope.

Independent Fraction replay checks every opening domain, opening balance, event
amount, balanced posting, state update and snapshot measurement. Two checked
histories share closing cash 15 but have different openings, intermediate states
and cumulative sales 5 versus 7. The same opening unknown is used at every date;
conflicting opening/closing observations cannot choose different openings.
The constant-opening fragment matches the existing reconstruction constructor
under identical supplied templates, dates, amounts and measurements.

Missing premises prevent solving. Explicit unresolved target obligations may
leave a candidate witness while target existence remains unknown, formulation
completeness false and economic scope unresolved. Candidate feasibility, declared
search and target existence are separate fields. Source/accounting admission,
native execution and executable opening remain false/absent in every outcome.
Zero-resource outcomes are unknown, and conditional conflict cores are rechecked
by the same solver. Alternative valid histories are accepted on semantic replay;
forged definitions, states, scope and admission claims are rejected.

270 checks, 45 targeted unit methods, ten cases and 231 protected inputs pass.
The unit count comprises 18 history, 11 generic joint, 12 temporal and four solver
regression methods. It does not rerun all source adapters or financial modules.
Early tests exposed an incorrect comparator helper reference and a missing
coverage field on early resource exits; both were repaired before recording.
