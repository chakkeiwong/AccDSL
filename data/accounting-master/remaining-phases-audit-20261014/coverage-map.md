# Remaining-phase coverage map

14 October 2026. All 35 mission requirements and 18 original gaps are assigned
to the current completion plans. This is an allocation of work, not a new
assessment or acceptance. The Version 2.72 assessment remains **not_ready**.

The tables retain the original acceptance obligations and the unchanged
`partial`/`specified` statuses from the
[current author assessment](../post-certainty-readiness-20261013/assessment.json).
`specified` means the bounded contract in that record, not full acceptance.
The [original requirements](../../../docs/plans/accounting-mission-requirements.md)
and [initial](../../../docs/design/review/gap-audit-2026-10-02.md) and
[expanded](../../../docs/design/review/mission-gap-audit-2026-10-02.md) gap audits
remain controlling. Exact strings and both historical/current recorded
statuses are retained in [coverage-map.json](coverage-map.json).

Unit numbers below refer to the [P17 method/source plan](../../../docs/plans/accounting-p17-completion-20261014.md)
and [P18 manuscript plan](../../../docs/plans/accounting-p18-completion-20261014.md).
All rows also pass through P17-11 interaction assembly, P18-04–06 preservation,
rendering and final-candidate assembly, and
[P19-01–05](../../../docs/plans/accounting-p19-completion-20261014.md) for located
assessment, blocker/interaction decisions, implementation staging, independent
review and final disposition. The table names the primary substantive units;
those common checks are not repeated in every cell.

MC32 governs readable and technically faithful integration; MC33 the meanings
of checks and acceptance; MC34 coverage, dependencies and continuation; MC35
internal preparation before any later professional referral. They apply across
the units, even when not individually listed beside every operation.

P15 maintains this requirement/gap register. P16 maintains shared architecture
through P17-08/10/11 and P18-03. Later runtime/empirical obligations remain
allocated through P19-03 to the held P00–P14 program; a missing design method or
required case cannot be deferred there merely to obtain proposal readiness.
