## Findings and decisions

1. Plans already exist for P17, P18 and P19. Their old campaign limits and
   original manuscript baselines are historical; the current master mostly
   listed recurring source work rather than a connected completion route.
   Add current completion plans with dependencies, outputs, completion criteria
   and local blockers while preserving the original phase obligations.
2. Five E workstreams are useful scheduling categories, but cannot replace all
   18 original gaps. The [coverage map](coverage-map.md) allocates every original
   requirement and gap. P15/P16 remain maintenance responsibilities; P17/P18
   complete the methods and integrated proposal; P19 judges readiness.
3. The SQL directory has source bytes, metadata and a preliminary technical
   review, but no final result/manifest or settled integration decision.
   Correct the next action to technical closeout, not acquisition or a claim
   of completion. Reconsider the no-edit disposition against the distinction
   between sound incomplete inference and demonstrated ambiguity. Placeholder
   PDF metadata also prevents treating journal identity as exact version
   equivalence. This audit does not complete that technical inquiry.
4. The actual JPMorgan source-reference conflict is delivered. Its comparison
   with the full MC27/G5/G17 acceptance obligations and human adjudication are
   separate. Credit the example; do not restart a closed search or imply a
   monetary conflict has been established.
5. Several residual lists mix specification gaps with later runtime evidence.
   P19 requires an implementable, source-supported design and independent
   technical review, not completion of the whole runtime or forecast campaign.
   Preserve both obligations separately; missing design cannot be relabelled
   implementation to force positive readiness.
6. Preserve source-specific limits: the frozen 40-reference inventory and its
   three supplemental counterparts; the frozen 56/136 date assessment and 35
   supplemental issuer declarations; blocked originals and failed discovery
   routes. Counts do not prove material semantic closure, identified private
   histories or historical availability of retained bytes.
