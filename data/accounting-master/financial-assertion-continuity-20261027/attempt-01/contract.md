# Diagnostic: when an assertion revision would change a carried state

27 October 2026. MC04/05/08/10/11/12/13/18/24/25/26/28/33/34.
This is the first bounded diagnostic under the financial-assertion-continuity
plan, before choosing any source-to-world policy. No actor message is added.

Question: where does replacing a supplied receipt or contractual-right value
change the existing complete financial path? Baselines are the protected
symbolic-path-composition cases and the independent expected_path recurrence.
Evaluate entire accounts, memory, flow records and guards with exact rational
substitution. Compare joins at all seven state boundaries, not balances alone.

Audit of the recurrence refines the original concern: receipt R enters cash,
income and flows at receipt2, after the first decision. Changing a future receipt
before that event can leave the complete carried state unchanged; therefore a
categorical rejection of every old/new parameter join would be wrong. After
settlement, changing R silently changes cash, income and the recorded receipt.
Contractual right is carried in memory from opening, so its change is visible
even where no numeric account changes. A matching state remains necessary but
does not establish source truth or authorise an actual contract change.

Primary criteria: exact equality with independent symbolic recurrence before
substitution, explicit unchanged/changed states at each boundary, and whole-path
equality for a pre-receipt join under one declared corrected-same-event world.
Two viable paths (fixed pay/pay and floating draw/pay) supply concrete independent
numeric checks. Additional algebraic action sequences are not called viable when
their guards fail. A post-receipt balanced splice must be rejected for continuity.
Right changes must be visible in memory even if cash is identical.

Assumptions: original opening cash 11/2, fee 1/4, draw/repayment 1, receipts 6/7,
original event sequence and coupon examples 5/5 or 6/5 are supplied test values
from the protected finite case. They are not inferred company values. Before
policy binding, retain unresolved interpretation for unknown truth, an actual
amendment without an explicit transition, or incompatible simultaneous facts.

The exact counterexample is engineering and mathematical evidence, not a
forecast experiment. Counts/runtime explanatory. Corrupt baseline invalidates
the comparison; candidate defects trigger repair within three 120-second CPU
attempts. Preserve complete numeric states and failures. GPU hidden. No new
policy population, actual truth, information-access or production claim.
