# R03-N — primary-source inspection

15 October 2026. Retained official EU2023/1803 PDF and text are the paragraph
source, hash-bound in cases.json. Historical support is distinct from a current
issuer's applicable edition, adoption and subsequent amendments.

Inspected IFRS9.3.2.16–23 and B3.2.4–17, alongside the previously inspected
3.2.2–15 transfer procedure. Risks/rewards precede control and continuing
involvement. B3.2.7–9 asks about practical, unilateral sale of the whole asset
without restrictions; market availability changes the call example. B3.2.16
distinguishes deep in/out-of-money options, fair-value repurchase, net cash
settlement, restricted calls, guarantees, total-return and interest-rate swaps.
No universal numerical meaning of deeply or substantially all is invented.

B3.2.13(b) gives amortised-cost associated-liability accretion (98/95/100);
(c)–(e) gives retained-call, written-put and collar measurements. The manuscript
derives their net exposure from intrinsic and supplied time values. The source's
80/95/5 call, 120/100/5 put and 100/120/80/5/1 collar examples were read in
full. B3.2.14 prohibits a second derivative when it duplicates rights already
recognized through the asset/liability. Paragraphs 3.2.19 and 22 require
consistent changes and separate asset/liability and income/expense presentation.

Inspected 4.1.1–5, 4.3.2 and 4.4.1; B4.1.1–3B, 4A–4B, 5–12A, the relevant
B4.1.14 convertible/inverse/perpetual examples, and B4.1.15–26. Management
evidence concerns the level at which assets are managed, not a label for one
instrument. Sales need context; no sales-count threshold follows. Basic lending
cash flows differ from leverage/equity-linked terms. A financial-asset hybrid
is assessed in its entirety. Modified time value compares undiscounted period
and cumulative flows to a matched benchmark across reasonably possible
scenarios. Today's yield curve and discounted equality alone do not settle it.
Nonrecourse and linked-tranche look-through, pool restrictions and credit
concentration need distinct findings. B4.1.26's mandatory FVTPL when a holder
cannot assess a tranche is an accounting finding, not permission to turn every
missing local evidence file into a completed classification.

Inspected 5.5.1–2, 5.7.5–11 and B5.7.1–1A. Debt FVOCI carries the asset at fair
value, recognizes ECL in profit with its allowance counterpart in OCI, and
recycles cumulative OCI on derecognition. The equity election is irrevocable,
excludes trading and specified contingent consideration, and does not recycle
fair-value gains/losses to profit. Dividends and intra-equity transfers retain
their separate source conditions. Financial liabilities' own-credit OCI in
5.7.7–9 is a different topic and is not applied to these assets.

The source does not supply actual option time values, risk-transfer findings,
ECL calibration, current valuation or bank business-model facts. All such
quantities in the cases are conditional inputs. Transfer prices equal the
specified underlying value less retained net option value in the no-initial-
gain examples; that equality is a deliberate case construction, not a universal
transaction-price rule. The formulas implement accounting measurement from
admitted values, not option pricing. R04/R05 and closed exchanges stay open.
