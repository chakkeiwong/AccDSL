Primary criterion is exact raw, elimination and adjusted vectors with unchanged
legal state. Balance equality, complete maps, reference identity, context and
refusal checks are correctness vetoes; passing arithmetic does not promote
accounting validity. Counts, test duration and asset totals alone explain the
case but cannot establish general correctness. No stochastic comparison occurs.

Premortem: a balanced worksheet could cancel unrelated balances. Therefore
each elimination includes supplied semantic roles and a retained premise.
The adapter checks consistency, not truth of that premise. It may still pass
under false supplied facts; source/accounting admission and completeness stay
false. Retain unresolved requirements for goodwill, NCI, tax, FX, intragroup
profit and period statements. No complete-consolidation claim is permitted.

Code review found no need to modify the frozen engines or native group packet.
The source and view references remain opaque in this worksheet; this is
explicit in references_resolved=false. Legal memory and cash flows are returned
unchanged, not merged. The worksheet returns signed snapshot totals only;
it cannot be used as an executable group book. Audit passes for this scope.
