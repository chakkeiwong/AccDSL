# Munich Re's sixteen declared publication dates

Completed 13 October 2026. P17; E01/C02–C03; MC01/03/05/28/30/33–35; G13.

All sixteen selected 2018–2025 annual and half-year reports contain explicit
publication statements. Fourteen say “Online publication date”; the 2018 and
2019 half-year reports say “Publication date” without specifying the channel.
The date register and JSON retain the exact labels, quotes, page numbers,
source URLs and PDF hashes. All seventeen candidate page images were read.
The extra 2025 annual-report hit concerns an EU regulation and is excluded.
The 2018 editorial deadlines and separate print date remain distinct records.

The repaired checker passes 115 checks. The initial run had 95 passes and two
failures because two 2020 source records qualify `not_verified` with a
semicolon-delimited explanation about dates in URLs. That was a checker error.
The original output and code survive in `recovery-before/`; the repaired check
retains both full qualifications and compares each selected record with the
unchanged corpus. No failed evidence has been silently replaced.

Engineering evidence establishes the identity of these source readings.
The substantive claim is that the retained reports assert these dates; the
computed records match those assertions. Independent publication-history
evidence has not been supplied. The frozen corpus still records 56 filing
dates and 136 unverified publication dates. This supplement supplies sixteen
new attributed assertions within that latter population and leaves 120 reports
outside this inquiry; it is not a revised corpus-acceptance count.

All sixteen Munich Re reports now have additional author annotation exposure.
These readings are preparation evidence, not independent gold labels. The
English reports refer to German originals, which were not acquired or compared
in this unit. No stochastic ranking or forecast experiment was performed.

Post-run red team: a report can carry a declared date without proving that the
retained file was available unchanged on that day. Independent archival
evidence or a source correction could change that assessment. Visual inspection
supports the date readings but cannot establish historical file availability.
