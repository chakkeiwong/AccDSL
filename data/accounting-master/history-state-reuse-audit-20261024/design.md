# Reuse audit: measurement families are not executable openings

24 October 2026. Inspected reconstruction_v1.construct/admit/expression,
rational_solver_v1, P04 fixture/tests, history-families-contract-v1 H1–H4,
source-constrained-state.partial_state and current statement-network definitions.
This is a bounded engineering audit; no admitted real-company history is supplied.

The existing reconstruction constructor already supplies finite choices, shared
real/integer variables, registered balanced posting/state incidence, dated slots,
observation mappings, obligations and solver coverage. It requires numeric opening
account/state constants: q(start) is used in each generated balance/state. A
symbol name in opening cannot refer to the declared symbols. Its dates are unique
ordered labels, not validated civil dates; the supplied order defines cumulative
effects. Its context is a single caller record. An observation's Accept field
and nonempty source are caller declarations, not a connection to current retained
source admission. The architecture already distinguishes those layers correctly.

Audit question: which of these are deliberate finite prototype restrictions and
which prevent reusing it with the newly checked conditional measurement network?
Primary criterion is a precise reuse boundary backed by source and small probes,
not a new implementation or a favorable feasibility count. Probe the unchanged
synthetic constructor, symbolic opening rejection, explicitly ordered date labels
and retained source requirements. No probe may populate a real-company opening.

Predictions: a simple fixed-opening finite history remains viable under its old
scope; a symbolic opening passed directly to construct is rejected; date strings
are interpreted in supplied array order rather than calendar order; a reported
measurement witness still leaves the source report's 15 execution dependencies
present. These are prototype properties, not historical conclusions. If a probe
contradicts inspection, repair the audit before selecting work.

Default audit: exact synthetic fixture amounts and finite slots are inherited
test choices; keep them labeled synthetic. Existing constructor rounding is
exact_rational unless explicitly quantized, while disclosure helpers may be
half-even. Do not pass the source's ties-away hypothesis through that helper.
The small solver budget is explicit and not a new runtime default. CPU-only,
seconds-scale probes; no numerical ranking or forecast. Output goes to audit.json
with source hashes and actual command. Preserve the full prior inventory in a
scoped successor and update affected obligations only.

Repair candidate to assess after probes: retain the existing finite history
constructor for fixed openings, while defining a closed conditional family
construction layer with explicit symbolic opening domains, dated event scope,
measurement bindings and unresolved admission/state obligations. The initial
question must be whether a supported finite history family exists under its
authored scope, not whether it is the retained issuer's true history. A solver
witness cannot fill contract/cohort/legal-book state or authorize native execution.
