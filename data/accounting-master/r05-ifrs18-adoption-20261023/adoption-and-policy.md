# UK adoption is supported by the decision and adopted text

The UK Endorsement Board adopted IFRS 18 on 10 December 2025. Paragraph 2 of
its retained adoption statement adopts the standard for use within the UK;
paragraph 3 includes consequential amendments, explicitly listing IAS 7.
Paragraph 6 requires use for financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2027,
permits earlier application with disclosure and makes the initial application
date the beginning of a reporting period. Paragraph 7 identifies the annex as
the adopted standard and amendments. The final approval page is dated
10 December 2025. The official 2025 register and September 2026 status report
give publication on 12 December 2025, which is a separate date role.

The linked annex contains IAS 7 amendments 18/20,31–34D and 64, and IFRS 18
B30–B41 and C1–C3. These selected technical passages were inspected directly.
They support the cash-flow mechanisms identified in the preceding Australian
source inquiry: operating profit as the indirect starting point; dividends
paid in financing; ordinary interest/dividend treatment under 34A; and the
specified-main-business rules and whole-flow policy choices under 34B–D.
Physical pages 134–135 were actually viewed to distinguish deletions from
insertions. This is a selected-paragraph comparison, not a full document
equivalence audit. The historical AASB record remains unchanged.

EFRAG's endorsement report updated 24 September 2026 lists IFRS 18 as endorsed
in the EU on 13 February 2026, published on 16 February 2026, with application
from 1 January 2027. That is explicit institutional status evidence. The retained
report's general EUR-Lex link points to an older consolidated regulation;
it does not identify the IFRS 18 amendment. The new EUR-Lex search returned
HTTP202 and no body. The actual EU adopting regulation and annex remain
uninspected, so this unit does not claim their precise wording, modifications
or legal-effect clauses. It also supplies no evidence that EU adoption is
absent. A distinct precise regulation identifier or official register lead is
