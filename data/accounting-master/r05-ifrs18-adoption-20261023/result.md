Red team: an issuer may already use the same relevant classifications, so a
large discontinuity cannot be inferred from the new standard's existence.
Conversely, a no-material-impact expectation is not proof of exact equality.
The strongest future disconfirming evidence would be dated company transition
figures and elections, assessed under the applicable adopted standard. The
weakest link remains company-specific application and exact vintage evidence.
