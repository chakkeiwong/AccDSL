27 October 2026. The original fifteen dependencies concern two fixed targets.
Later source-context, fiscal, overlap, conditional-opening and complete-claims
interfaces are real progress, but they do not change those target schemas or
identify the issuer's private events. Repeating the old calendar-only repair
would duplicate delivered work. Declaring the issuer executable would be wrong.

This unit reads frozen results without rerunning their writers or solvers.
It preserves every original dependency record for both targets, checks source
locks and directly checks selected stored results: full statement coverage,
source-network preservation, unresolved dash and incomplete history flags,
explicit population distinctions, and complete conditional distribution outputs.
Those predicates are independently specified in this unit; historical numerical
proofs and results outside them remain inherited evidence.

Pass requires all fifteen rows, exact source identities, all selected predicates
and meaningful next discriminators. Missing/corrupt bytes or an unsupported
admission claim veto this reconciliation. Counts are integrity diagnostics only.
No solver run, actual legal-book inference, probability, accounting approval,
general compatibility or independent implementation is established.

Default audit: named-source reading is the original mode; the fifteen-row scope
comes from the original requirements, not a new completeness assumption. Older
target refusal and newer representation support are recorded independently.
The strongest alternative explanation is an uninspected newer composition;
the decisions make only the supported scoped capability claim, never a claim
that no other method exists. Exact files and inspected call chains are locked.

Research direction remains viable: unknown issuer facts veto actual admission,
not representation work. The next concrete source question is whether the
retained debt-issuance dash has an independently supported tagged numerical
interpretation. Do not infer zero from arithmetic or rewrite the old evidence.
