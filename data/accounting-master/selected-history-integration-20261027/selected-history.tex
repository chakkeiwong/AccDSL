\subsection{The selected reading can constrain a supplied history}
\label{sec:selected-reading-history}

The numerical reading can now enter the two previously supplied historical
candidates while retaining the complete statement network. The annual and
interim candidates keep their separate source contexts. Each has nineteen
declared accounts, two hypothetical capital transactions and thirty explicit
links from reported quantities to historical amounts. The new composition
preserves those histories and links, partitions every selected observation
and equation by its original context, and attaches the selected issuance
interval without replacing the PDF-only parent.

Both candidates satisfy their declared constraints. An independent rational
calculation reconstructs the account movements and snapshots, checks the
source-to-history links, and substitutes the resulting measurements into all
236 selected intervals and 101 equations. Reintroducing the earlier hypothesis
that the opening cash populations are identical still makes that historical
candidate incompatible. A numerical debt-issuance reading cannot repair a
wrong cash-population mapping.

These candidates remain sparsely connected to the filing. Of the 236 selected
measurements, 176 have no supplied history mapping. Their equations remain
in the calculation, but they do not thereby identify an account or transaction.
Four further obligations preserve the unknown actual history and wider source
coverage in the two contexts. All 180 unresolved obligations remain attached
to the result. Nor have the annual and interim candidates been joined into one
company history. Their separate feasibility therefore supplies no executable
legal-book opening or probability over possible histories.

\code{SelectedReadingHistoryAttachment/1} implements this explicit composition.
It accepts the exact original source parent, selected reading, rounding premise,
context-specific histories and declared computation budget. Missing premises
remain unresolved; altered parents, equations or contexts are refused; an
exhausted search remains unknown. The earlier interface still recomputes its
own PDF-only source and retains its original results. Complete requests,
independent checks and adverse cases are retained under
\path{data/accounting-master/selected-reading-history-20261027/}.
The companion \path{spec/source-opening-compatibility-v1.md} specifies the
supported joins through conditional opening, financial transition, reporting
and period carry, including the inputs that each join still requires.
