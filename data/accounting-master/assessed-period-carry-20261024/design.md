# Provisional carry after conditional deferred assessment

AssessedSharedPeriodCarry/1 binds the full ConditionalSharedDeferredAssessment/1
request and digest. It selects provisional_assessed_balance_carry_v1 explicitly,
identifies the carry and contiguous next period, supplies all four provisional
findings and orders new operations. The entire deferred proposal recomputes,
including its prior current-tax result, bases, opening allocations and capacity.
The older current-tax-only carry remains frozen and receives no altered inputs.

Use the actual assessed closing balances and recorded profit. The same signed
retained-earnings transfer clears temporary income, including deferred expense,
and preserves every other account. New opening tax allocations copy actual DTA
and DTL. Components, loss lots, lifetime identifiers, lease dates and the exact
prior-year record survive. Prior cash and tax adjustments are archived and new
period accumulators begin empty. Every conditional recognition item, finding and
prior capacity remains in the archive and complete predecessor result.

Neither current nor deferred tax has been assessed for the new period. Previous
capacity is historical evidence, not permission to recognize deductions again.
Unknown/false carry findings remain visible even if prior conditional assessment
passed. Annual readiness, admission and durable posting remain false.

Selected return/lease/tax-payment transitions act on the full carried state and
use actual shared cash. Reports use this opening, so settling carried current tax
does not repeat expense. Failed events preserve the complete successful prefix.
Pure apply replays and compares the complete proposal, including all history and
both unassessed-period statuses. Gap/overlap, duplicated identities, mismatched
prior digest, unsupported annual mode and unresolved prior recognition refuse
the corresponding request.

New-period tax assessment, repeated carry, general authoring and complete source
admission remain future methods. This successor repairs compatibility for one
explicit assessed predecessor and does not claim general annual execution.
