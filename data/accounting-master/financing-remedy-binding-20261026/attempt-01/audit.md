# Conditional remedies: pre-implementation audit

26 October 2026. MC05/13/16/17/19/20/24/25/28/33/34/35; W05.

The preserved E03 classifier has 58 independent hypothetical cases. Its seven
routes are event-clause satisfaction, authorized notice, new acceleration,
remaining acceleration after rescission, protected amendment consent, majority
waiver and target-series findings. The first work unit will translate all seven
to explicit MethodRequirements/3 predicates and compare the complete retained
classification table without invoking the native classifier from the candidate.

Inputs are supplied conditional findings. Missing Boolean findings remain
unknown. Rational normalization preserves E03's integer/string input domain,
rejects negative durations and out-of-range holder shares, and supplies explicit
typed quantities. E03's exact-day boundary deliberately remains unknown; a
declared unknown boundary-order input preserves that condition. Edition and
actor selection remain explicit dispatch. A known automatic acceleration
category does not evaluate unused notice fields, including unsupported editions.
Eagerly validating that unused branch would change the reference semantics.

The typed execution unit records known conditional findings in distinct
contract memory, using the existing generic memory/monetary transition engine.
It changes no cash, debt carrying amount, issued shares or historic missed
amount. A selected clause finding is separate from acceleration; lack of a new
trigger preserves existing acceleration; only a resolved rescission assessment
sets the remaining acceleration state. Unknown findings stop before a state
write. Full supplied evidence stays in the command/result with its digest.
Actual-source applicability, operative indentures, all default/remedy routes,
calendar boundary adjudication and economic effects of acceleration remain open.

The independently authored sequence must demonstrate: missed payment can be
retained while clause applicability is unknown; a conditional principal clause
finding does not itself accelerate; a separate acceleration declaration changes
contract memory; a cured current event leaves acceleration unchanged; effective
rescission changes acceleration while historical missed payment survives.
Use E01's funded but unpaid intermediate book, not a newly admitted fiscal
opening. Record the hypothetical conditional principal finding and declared
acceleration, apply the existing late-settlement method under an explicit
extended schema, observe the now-paid clause, and finally apply the supplied
rescission. Compare complete state/journal/flow results and failed prefixes.

Pre-run refinement after focused diagnostics: a free-standing classifier match
is insufficient for the combined trace. The principal unpaid finding must
agree with the typed outstanding claim. New acceleration must bind the current
stored clause finding and its same-date observation; a stale observation stops
as unresolved rather than asserting that the clause continues. Same-date order
is the caller's explicit sequence, not invented intra-day time. Rescission binds
the stored existing acceleration. Applicability and legal due status remain
supplied hypothetical findings. The selected hypothetical contract assumptions
are declared explicitly; this does not admit their application to an issuer.

Primary acceptance is exact classification and full-state agreement. Native
classifier fallback, typed-engine bypass, changed frozen evidence, coerced
unknowns, unexplained mismatches or accepted partial updates veto acceptance.
Counts and speed are explanatory only. All retained thresholds and durations
are conditional source-specific premises, not financial defaults. No new legal
interpretation or stochastic ranking is attempted. Source-law acquisition is
not required to test this bounded translation of existing examples.
