# Source and implementation boundary

The two annual bodies supply eight author-interpreted assertions with eight
query dispositions. They do not validate named competitor infringement,
external market share or future competition. The product-switch and component
compatibility examples are explicit assumptions, not Apple histories.
Qualitative minority share does not supply a numeric fraction, and no branch
weights or accounting amounts are created.

The revenue paragraphs describe Apple's reported policy, including shipment
as the general product-control point, bundled-service allocation/deferment and
collection timing. They do not establish every new product's contract terms.
The custom-component paragraph describes conditional capacity and availability,
not a named supplier outage or forecasted yield. Actual product/contract facts
and applicable accounting methods remain required.
