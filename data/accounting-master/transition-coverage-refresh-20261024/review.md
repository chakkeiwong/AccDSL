## Skeptical decision

A joint constraint check can establish that declared rounded measurements and
explicitly assumed identities have a common assignment. It cannot establish an
admissible accounting history, fill missing contract/cohort state, choose a
probability law or make the target company executable. The next audit must keep
that limited mathematical target separate from those larger goals and reuse
the existing reconstruction solver if its exact strict-bound behavior fits.

| Decision | Primary criterion | Veto status | Main uncertainty | Next action | Not concluded |

| Audit joint measurement-constraint support | Current gap is explicit in retained source relation results | No global continuation veto; no fabricated executable opening | Existing solver may already support required exact semantics | Inspect and compare solver/interval contracts, then implement smallest missing layer | Full reconstruction, whole DSL or production completion |

The strongest alternative is that the existing solver already resolves this
precise source-bound task. The audit starts there. Missing company facts block
real execution, but they do not block a limited consistency check over declared
measurements. No whole MC requirement closes.
