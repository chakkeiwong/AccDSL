# Chapter 4: useful precedents and the remaining integration

Fresh ordered reading covers physical pages74–79. The review is organized by
the needs already demonstrated:accounting structure,shared unknowns,provenance,
inference,languages,extraction and execution. Each research group contributes a
particular mechanism while the text states the conditions under which that
mechanism can transfer. This is a readable scoped design survey rather than a
claim to exhaust the literature or establish novelty.

The most consequential distinctions stay explicit. Conservation does not prove
transaction admissibility. Certain answers do not assign probability to
histories. Provenance annotations do not cover every nonlinear or conditional
operation. Identification limits survive a computational method,and duplicate
representations cannot gain model weight merely by being counted twice.
Conditioning and intervention remain different from an imposed stress path.

The language discussion preserves a typed library as a serious alternative and
uses rule-language examples for exception semantics without importing their
compiler guarantees as accounting correctness. Contract-event and payoff
semantics motivate economic state but do not determine recognition. The
extraction discussion is especially concrete about TableFormer's cropped input,
externally supplied PDF text,structural corrections and score population.
Those metrics do not certify financial meaning. Likewise,question answering,
RAG,SMT and probabilistic execution contribute bounded capabilities.

The concluding integration problem follows from the review:one explanation must
connect evidence,histories,events,journals and statements while preserving the
distinctions each component needs. Practical merit remains subject to accounting
review,acceptance cases and comparative implementation. No confirmed new
readability,prerequisite or internal source-boundary defect was observed. This
review checked the manuscript's use and explanation of sources;it did not repeat
the technical audits of every underlying publication. Independent and human
acceptance remain pending.
