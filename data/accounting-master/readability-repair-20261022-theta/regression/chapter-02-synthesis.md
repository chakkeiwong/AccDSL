# Chapter 2: complete examples and incomplete company knowledge

Fresh ordered reading covers physical pages13–61 of Version3.48. The reading
records distinguish the controlled Northstar company, the corporate and sector
groups, and Apple's actual disclosures. These are different kinds of evidence;
the chapter does not silently turn the real-company examples into a complete
reconstruction.

Northstar pays off the first chapter's promise by joining opening stocks,
half-year movements, annual statements, a revision and conditional future
statements. Recoverable sales and purchases follow from explicit accounting
premises, while the debt composition remains free. One alpha must travel through
profit, cash and equity. The revision example separates corrected archival
calculation from an investor forecast available at an earlier date. The portable
delivery example then attaches retry and missing-value behavior to those same
amounts rather than introducing an unrelated software exercise.

The group examples increase the burden where isolated method demonstrations
would be insufficient. Returns, debt cash, intragroup inventory profit, dated
ownership and separate tax losses interact before the consolidated close.
The bank/insurer case adds legal taxpayers, currency translation, cash FX and
internal-deposit elimination. Opening premises and event calendars precede the
calculations. Separate cash and equity reconciliations support the stated
illustrative closes; they do not establish the accounting treatment of every
possible transaction or an implemented engine.

Apple changes the question from constructing known histories to preserving what
disclosures actually identify. Principal, carrying value, fair value and
notional are different measurements. Statement reconciliation does not license
invented residual journals. Revenue recognition does not determine collection;
purchase-order change does not determine delivery; delivery does not determine
payment. Later liquidity, geography, legal-entity, litigation and supply examples
add the missing relationships that a future scenario would need. Their repeated
distinctions have different financial consequences, rather than merely repeating
one generic warning about incomplete data.

The later segment-expense comparison is a particularly useful return to an
earlier gap. It supplies actual comparative detail while retaining the original
source and uncertainty about exact-version availability. The closing connected
example likewise advances the earlier asset-history construction: equal annual
rows can leave different liabilities, and future operating/investing cash must
remain paired by history and payment date. Its alpha is a reporting allocation,
not a probability. A complete token-reading answer can therefore sit within a
partial financial-history answer without contradiction.

The chapter is long, but the movement from controlled completeness to actual
disclosure limits gives its accumulation a purpose. The last section collects
that purpose into a useful partial answer and prepares the representation
chapter. The JPMorgan navigation example adds source-dependency correction
without merging issuers or granting posting authority. No confirmed mathematical,
prerequisite,source-boundary or navigation defect was found in this author read.
The judgments do not substitute for fresh accounting verification of every cited
source or independent reader,domain and human acceptance, which remain pending.
