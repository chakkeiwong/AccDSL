The read-only AlignedOptionComponents/1 report binds each observation to both contract iden-
tities and checks its intrinsic/time decomposition. Six paths include matched terms, out-of-
the-money expiry and doubled quantities. Unknown component values, copied critical terms,
omitted premiums, mixed rule profiles and unsupported cumulative changes produce no partial
report. Actual pricing, qualifying findings, general signed components and subsequent company
or sector integration retain their separate requirements.
10.12.4
A zero-cost collar can have a signed interim cost reserve
Equal option premiums can conceal a changing net time value. A purchaser may pay for a call
that limits price increases and finance it by writing a put that gives up price decreases below a
lower strike. Even when those premiums cancel, the two options can have different time values
before expiry. IFRS 9.B6.5.31 expressly includes a purchased/written option combination with
net nil time value at designation: its related time-value changes enter other comprehensive
income, although the cumulative change over the full relationship is zero (European Commission,
2023a, IFRS 9.B6.5.31–32).
