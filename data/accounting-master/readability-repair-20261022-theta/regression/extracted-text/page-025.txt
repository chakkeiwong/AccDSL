Tax now connects these movements to legal equity. The supplied law taxes book profit at
20%, including guarantee changes, with usable loss carryforwards. There is separate convincing
evidence supporting the losses’ recovery; a future book profit alone is not that evidence
(European Commission, 2023a, IAS 12.34–36,46–47). There is no loss pooling between P, B
and I. Outside-basis exceptions and absence of tax on translation are also explicitly supported
premises, not conclusions about an actual jurisdiction.
For each taxpayer let ℓdenote the opening unused tax loss and p the period’s pretax profit. Under
this finite supplied law, loss used is u = min{ℓ, max(p, 0)}, current tax is 0.2[max(p, 0) −u],
and closing loss is ℓ−u + max(−p, 0). The recognized DTA is 20% of that loss. Deferred tax
expense equals opening DTA minus closing DTA; hence the tax benefit on a new supported loss
is negative expense, without a current cash refund.
