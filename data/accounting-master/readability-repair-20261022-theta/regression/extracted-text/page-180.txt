Hedge accounting and cash presentation also remain distinct. Annual Note 1 presents derivative
assets and liabilities at gross fair values. Note 4 classifies derivative cash flows with the items
hedged, which are generally operating activities; that qualifier does not classify every derivative
settlement as operating. The maximum disclosed horizon for hedging debt-related foreign-
currency cash-flow variability is 18 years, and Apple states that some exposures may be left
unhedged and that offsets may be partial (Apple Inc., 2024a, Notes 1 and 4, printed pp. 34 and
37–38).
Finally, Item 7A’s currency-risk statistic has its own population. Apple reports a Monte Carlo
estimate, at 95% confidence over one day, of 538 million dollars at September 2024 and 669
million at September 2023 for adverse fair-value changes in its foreign-currency derivatives. The
assessment excludes underlying currency exposures, whose changes generally offset derivative
changes. This derivative VaR is neither a hard maximum loss nor a measure of the company’s net
currency risk; the issuer’s simulation has not been replicated here. A financing simulation can
retain these dated scenarios as disclosed constraints, but still needs actual holdings, currencies,
payment calendars, reset terms and hedge relationships to calculate future cash and accounting
consequences. The next question is how financing choices change the quantities and claims of
the company’s owners.
10.2
Equity and ownership
Equity has both monetary accounts and security quantities. The security register records share
class, issued and outstanding quantities, treasury holdings, par or stated value where applica-
ble, voting rights, dividend rights, redemption features, options and conversion relationships.
Monetary equity accounts include contributed capital, reserves, retained earnings and other
components required by the selected basis. These records evolve together through issuance,
repurchase, cancellation, split, dividend and conversion events.
A stock split changes quantities and per-share measures without necessarily changing aggregate
monetary equity. A cash dividend affects distributions and cash rather than current operating
expense under the corresponding ordinary equity treatment. Repurchases and employee share
