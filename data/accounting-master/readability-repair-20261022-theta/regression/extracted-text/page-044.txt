into an invented identity.
2.15.1
Membership does not determine the ownership path
Wholly-owned membership and immediate ownership answer different questions. For a simple
hypothetical example, a subsidiary could be owned directly by the parent, or through another
subsidiary that the parent wholly owns. If every link is wholly owned, both arrangements
preserve ultimate wholly-owned membership while producing different immediate parents. This
logical example does not select either arrangement for any company in the table. Neither the
similar names of Apple Operations Limited and Apple Operations International Limited nor
their common incorporation jurisdiction establishes an ownership link between them.
A model can therefore retain each named subsidiary, its reported jurisdiction and the general
consolidation assertion without drawing unsupported edges. It should record an immediate
parent, share class, ownership interest and effective interval only when the evidence identifies
them. The September 2024 exhibit is not a control history for earlier years or an updated
