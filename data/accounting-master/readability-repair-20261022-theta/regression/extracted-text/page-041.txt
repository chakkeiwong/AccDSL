Management attributes the annual Greater China sales decline mainly to lower iPhone and
iPad sales and reports an unfavourable renminbi effect. For the first quarter of fiscal 2025 it
attributes the decline mainly to iPhone, while describing a favourable currency effect (Apple
Inc., 2024a, 2025c, annual and interim segment operating performance). These are dated issuer
explanations, not measured contributions of each driver or an estimated response equation.
A future region-specific demand or exchange-rate path needs product, currency and contract
information that the regional totals alone do not supply.

Why segment income cannot be treated as fully allocated profit
Apple defines each segment’s operating income as third-party sales less the related cost of sales
and directly attributable operating expenses. The annual total of segment operating income
is 162,044 million, but the consolidated operating statement reports 123,216 million. Note 13
supplies the bridge: 31,370 million of research and development and 7,458 million of other
corporate expenses, net, are subtracted. The latter category includes general and administrative
compensation, various nonrecurring charges and other separately managed costs. The reported
relation is
162,044 −31,370 −7,458 = 123,216
million dollars, fiscal 2024.

The first-quarter fiscal 2025 bridge has the same form: 53,174 −8,268 −2,074 = 42,832
million. Both endpoints agree with the consolidated statements of operations (Apple Inc., 2024a,
statements of operations and Note 13, printed pp. 29 and 47) (Apple Inc., 2025c, statement of
operations and Note 10, printed pp. 1 and 11). The annual “other corporate” amount is not
identical to the 26,097 million selling, general and administrative expense line in the consolidated
statement. Equating the two would change the reported classification.
Equation (2.23) identifies the company-wide cost pool, not how it would be attributed to regions.
