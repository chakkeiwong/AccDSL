12.18.2
Stopping preserves what was actually computed
A result reports requested, attempted, completed, failed and pending draws separately. Its
coverage domain is the set of computational replicate IDs. It is not the company’s family of
economic histories. Complete arrays do not establish calibrated forecasts, and an incomplete
set of arrays does not justify a probability distribution conditional on computational success.
If cancellation follows the first complete draw in the example, retain its total 12 and mark the
second draw unattempted. The diagnostic completed-draw mean is 12; the requested sample
mean remains unresolved. A stop request alone does not establish that the worker has stopped.
Unknown termination is reported as partial with unresolved worker state. Resume requires a
durable checkpoint that binds the semantic manifest, original draw IDs and random generator
state; the native function’s seed argument supplies no such checkpoint.
The inspected source resets its supplied seed in both residual sampling and process generation.
That behavior must retain its own method identity and cannot be described as verified indepen-
dent streams (Gesmann and contributors, 2023, sampleResiduals and the process-law branches).
A proposed replacement must specify generator version, stream derivation and restart behavior,
and then check them in execution. Changing a method, law or request identifier creates a
different request fingerprint. A resumed scheduling request must link to the old checkpoint
under the persistence rules; it cannot silently alter the old request.
The retained snapshot’s GPL terms, transitive dependencies and any future instrumented
source remain part of the proposed runtime decision. No package installation, distribution
clearance, crash-durability result or empirical performance follows from these exchanges. What
they establish is narrower and necessary: a successful payment, a missing value and a failed
computation remain distinguishable when reserving results enter the financial model.
