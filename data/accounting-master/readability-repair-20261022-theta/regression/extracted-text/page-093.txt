A projection is not a reusable transition state
For a concrete counterexample, take two separately retained histories in which the timing of a
reinsurance treaty changes when income is recognised. Both end with cash 88.3. Their initial
and first-year equities are 85 and 83 for the timely treaty, but 70 and 74.75 for the late treaty.
These are the supplied comparison values here; Section 12.6 derives them from the separate
issued and held contracts. The terminal-cash projection is therefore equal while the transition
states and service results are different. A query requesting only terminal cash may project both
histories to 88.3; a later query requesting first-year equity, loss recovery or treaty timing must
revalidate the resolution and retain the full group states.
The bank commitment supplies the same warning. Funded gross loan 100 does not contain the
original commitment date, credit baseline or observed draw. The funded allowance 4 cannot
be recomputed from that scalar alone. If the original records were preserved, refinement can
recover the state. If they were never known, the result is DETAIL_UNAVAILABLE or measurement
unresolved, not a fabricated staging history. A source revision or changed query creates a new
manifest; it does not silently resume an old result.
Conditional mass remains attached to unresolved outcomes
For a small authored law, assign prior masses 1/3, 2/3 to immutable timely and late histories
and likelihoods 3/4, 1 to a newly admitted observation. The evidence normaliser is 11/12, so
posterior masses are 3/11 and 8/11. The invalid pooled treatment is not a third law atom: it
was rejected as unsupported accounting treatment. If the late history’s execution is unresolved,
its mass remains 8/11; reporting the timely result with probability one silently conditions on
successful execution. If both first-year equities are available under that law, their mean is
(3/11)83 + (8/11)74.75 = 77. With only the timely branch evaluated, the known weighted
contribution is (3/11)83, not the complete mean. The same event IDs, measure provenance and
unresolved mass must travel with the output.
These examples do not add a general solver or a cent-based accounting policy. They specify
the distinctions that a compatible implementation must preserve: input domain, output
operation, endpoint attainment, event order, retained economic memory, conditional mass
and failure status. A changed output, horizon, grouping rule or scenario therefore returns
RESOLUTION_REVALIDATION_REQUIRED until sufficiency is checked again.

Numerical precision is part of a financial method
The affine family is useful for Northstar because the scenario fixes prices, rates and operating
coefficients while the debt share remains unknown. Other contracts require roots or discount
factors. Their numerical treatment must state what is exact, what is enclosed and when a
posted amount is determined. An accounting name on a library call does not make its result
exact.
