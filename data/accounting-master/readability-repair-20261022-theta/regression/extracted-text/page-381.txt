B.2
Required core record definitions
The historical closed machine-readable definitions remain in the original design directory at
spec/core-ir.schema.json. They define contexts, source spans, observations, narratives, cov-
erage units, mappings, partitions, entity/control records, books, accounts, symbols, choices,
expressions, constraints, assumptions, alternatives, contracts, events, rules, packages, measure-
ments, states, journals, scenarios, information views, review decisions, evidence views, model
scopes, historical families, probability models, report mappings, result cells, branches, runs and
diagnostics. For those historical records the schema fixes field spelling and structural shape,
and its companion contract fixes reference resolution and operations. New portable records
instead obey the exact versioned contracts introduced in Chapters 7, 8 and 13. No historical
field list grants new posting authority.
