The text frontend deliberately has a smaller authoring scope than typed construction. Its
published grammar accepts the ordered NorthstarDebt declaration: principal, the rational
interval for x, fixed and floating rates, and the stated interest formula. The frozen module
supplies the context, year fraction, source and assumption dependencies. Both principal tokens,
both interval endpoints and both rates must match those module records. The typed debt input
identifies the same module; the full company input separately includes all operating templates
and economic facts. An unsupported declaration has a located diagnostic. This is a specified
new bounded frontend, not a claim that the earlier Core0 parser implements it.
Accounting commits change the accounting owner, event lookup, journals, independent deltas
and receipts atomically. Review commits change their review owner and publish a new immutable
view. Checkpoint commits preserve the completed and unresolved cells under their work owner.
Import verifies all original record mappings and required bytes before changing its metadata
owner; it admits no financial claim. Each exchange states every unaffected domain as well,
permitting exact comparison of the two worlds.
Failure location matters. Malformed JSON, extra fields, missing required references, mismatched
operation tags or corrupt supplied blobs leave the validated request and financial payload null.
A transaction conflict or stale head after valid input retains the validated request and structural
validity, while reporting no new commit. The result’s coverage retains the continuous family
for financial queries and the original partition on resume. Neither an unexplored cell nor an
unsupported calculation becomes an empty family.
The independent checks reconstruct both half-years from preserved economic inputs, derive
the annual bridges and 2025 affine coefficients, replay the proposed journals, and compare the
permitted store changes. Deliberately altered rate sensitivity, inventory quantity, receipt history,
publication date and unknown closing balance challenge those checks. Their scope is the finite
documentary specification. Generic frontend and solver execution, admitted-law extensions,
crash/reopen testing, accounting acceptance and human comprehension retain their separate
evidence requirements.
A.2
Joined company and explicit component migration
The company in Section 2.7 is specified by CompanyComposition/1. Its state contains both legal
trial balances, the common currency and dates, ownership/control history, return and matured
compound-instrument memory, the internal inventory lot, tax-close history and dated gross
cash movements. The schema admits nine event kinds: redemption, return settlement, payroll,
depreciation, internal inventory transfer, external inventory sale, reciprocal settlement, tax close
and a purchase from NCI without loss of control. Every kind has a closed parameter record.
