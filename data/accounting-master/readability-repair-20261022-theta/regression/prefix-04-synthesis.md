# Cumulative reading through chapters 1–4

Physical pages1–79 have been read in order for Version3.48. The argument now has
four cumulative steps:the product problem,company demonstrations,the historical
representation,and the research methods available to support it. The order is
substantive. The observation fields and mapping rules in chapter3 answer the
financial failures shown in chapter2;the literature in chapter4 addresses those
specified needs rather than choosing the product's goal for it.

Several recurring distinctions have acquired new consequences. Historical
ambiguity begins with rate composition,extends to complete company statements,
then becomes shared unknowns and scoped certain answers. Source revision begins
with Northstar,extends to actual comparative disclosures and a wrong reference,
then receives temporal and dependency semantics. Conditional scenarios become
questions about explicit model assumptions and the difference between stress,
conditioning and intervention. The repetition therefore advances the argument.

The prefix does not contradict the introduction's ambition by preserving
incomplete real-company histories. It gives useful partial answers and records
the additional facts that a stronger answer would require. Nor does the survey
claim that existing tools complete the design:typed-library alternatives remain
viable,and extraction or solver success does not establish accounting or
forecast validity. The whole-company ambition still requires the detailed
methods and implementation evidence promised later.

No confirmed contradiction,terminology drift or missing local prerequisite was
found in this prefix. The remaining question is now architectural:how to connect
reusable components through a shared representation without losing the
financial,temporal and inferential distinctions already established. This is an
author synthesis,with independent-reader and human acceptance pending.
