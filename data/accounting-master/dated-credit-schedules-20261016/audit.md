Numerical audit: fractional annual compounding is generally irrational.
Each factor has rational bounds certified by integer powers. Interval arithmetic
encloses ideal balances; overlapping intervals do not establish literal equality
of independently rounded endpoints. Explicit width limits apply to every
reported monetary interval. The financial profiles require an exact initial PV
identity and a zero initial tolerance; the separate root bracket utility does
not turn a rounded point root into an admitted rate. General rate-interval
propagation remains a residual method. Root brackets need independently checked
endpoint signs. No rounded root is called exact. Computational limits are admission constraints, not
economic claims: at most64 cash dates,128 events,7305 elapsed days and bounded
rational encodings, precision12–24 digits. No numerical tolerance is implicit.

Accounting audit: distinguish non-credit estimate changes under B5.4.6 from
POCI credit revisions under5.5.13–14. Full or partial write-off needs a separate
no-recovery finding and identified zero-expected-cash portion; positive total
cash cannot excuse delayed write-off. Accrual requires an explicit finding that
no earlier write-off was required during the interval. A revision plus required
write-off is one atomic event. Favorable subsequent POCI changes retain a signed
negative allowance. Terminal collection clears residual gross/allowance with no
second impairment only after net carrying is measured to confirmed cash.

The supplied ACT/365F convention, credit forecasts and classification remain

actual issuer facts, a production solver or a general credit implementation.
Cash, source and numerical validity are primary criteria; counts, page counts
and compilation are explanatory. No statistical ranking is involved.

delayed-write-off counterexamples reduce that risk. Independent domain and
