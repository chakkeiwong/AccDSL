The capacity limit also explains how to handle an unsuccessful allocation. A diagnostic input
requesting 50 against capacities 5 and 35 can allocate only 40, leaving 10. That arithmetic does
not establish that the supplied unit and individual valuations are jointly coherent. It calls for

108 permits a liability for a remaining unit loss only if another IFRS requires one. AccDSL
must retain the residual and the unresolved decision; it cannot invent a payable to make the
unit reach a requested total. This diagnostic is not a second admitted company example.
A recovery is limited by the depreciation that would have occurred
Suppose both pieces of equipment have five remaining years of even use and zero residual
values at T0. Under paragraph 63, their revised carrying amounts give next-year depreciation
55/5 = 11 and 15/5 = 3. At T1, one full year later, they carry 44 and 12. The company must
also retain the path that would have occurred without impairment. On that path depreciation
would have been 60/5 = 12 and 40/5 = 8, leaving 48 and 32. Restoring a past loss therefore
cannot mean restoring the old nominal balances.
Assume a supported change in expected future cash flows now gives the unit a recoverable
amount of 90. Individual recoverable amounts remain admitted as not determinable. Paragraphs
114–116 require the change in estimates; mere passage of time and discount unwinding would
not support this reversal. Paragraphs 122–123 allocate a unit reversal over assets other than
goodwill pro rata, subject to each asset’s recoverable amount if determinable and its depreciated
no-impairment amount. Goodwill itself cannot be restored under paragraph 124 (European
Commission, 2023a).
The requested increase is 90 −(44 + 12) = 34. A has room for only 48 −44 = 4; B has room
for 32 −12 = 20. The same redistribution procedure therefore recognises 4 + 20 = 24, leaving
equipment at 48 and 32 and goodwill at zero. The recognized total of 80 is below the unit’s
value of 90. That difference does not identify an omitted asset, authorize goodwill recognition
or imply cash has been received.
