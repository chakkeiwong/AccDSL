These joins expose the missing transaction links more precisely. Annual receivables rise by

Nor can the equity rows simply replace financing cash flows. The reported repurchase reduction

2024a, printed pp. 32–33). The later note examples retain their own evidence and conditional
histories.
A reconstruction must now satisfy the statement relationships together. Correct total cash
cannot excuse wrong equity components, and correct net assets cannot excuse omitted cash-flow
adjustments. The five statements constrain a common history, but their agreement does not
reveal all gross events, dates, counterparties or accounting treatments. Those unresolved links
determine which historical alternatives can be supported and what additional facts a conditional
future path still needs.
2.10.1
The following quarter must start from the same balances
The next filing makes the time connection testable. Its balance-sheet comparison is 28 September
2024, and all 27 monetary rows reproduce the annual closing column. Its income, comprehensive-
income, equity and cash-flow comparisons instead cover the three months ending 30 December
2023. Those earlier-quarter flows cannot become the opening amounts for the quarter ending
28 December 2024. The interim statements are explicitly unaudited (Apple Inc., 2025c, printed
pp. 1–5).
Quarterly products revenue of 97,960 and services revenue of 26,340 total 124,300. Cost of sales
of 66,025 and operating expenses of 15,443 leave operating income of 42,832; other expense of
248 and tax expense of 6,254 give net income of 36,330. Translation OCI of −625, derivative
OCI of 2,435 and marketable-debt OCI of −1,427 add 383, giving comprehensive income of
36,713 (Apple Inc., 2025c, printed pp. 1–2). The resulting equity movement begins at the same
56,950 that closed the annual record. Compensation adds 3,383, withholding removes 1,891 from
capital and 1,102 from retained earnings, declarations remove 3,819 and repurchases remove
23,476. Together these other movements are −26,905, so 56,950 + 36,713 −26,905 = 66,758.
Capital, accumulated deficit and accumulated OCI also join separately at both endpoints (Apple
Inc., 2025c, printed pp. 3–4).
