The generator G emits events, such as a sale, rate reset, payment or default. The accounting

This ordering is a reference abstraction. Within a date, event dependencies and phases specify
the actual order. Expectations used to measure an allowance or provision must be calculated
from the information permitted at that date. They cannot use the realised future path of
the simulation. An inner expectation model may use quadrature, analytic formulas or nested
simulation; its approximation and seed belong in the measurement annotation.
Some scenarios specify a deterministic macroeconomic path while leaving idiosyncratic defaults
random. Others supply a joint stochastic driver model. A risk-neutral measure used to value a
derivative is distinct from the real-world measure used to simulate business outcomes. Every
stochastic adapter declares its measure and conditioning information. The accounting function
receives the resulting valuation with that declaration.
6.6
Preserve sets and distributions separately
Let Kh,s be the probability law of future reports produced by the forward model conditional on
history h, scenario s and its declared behavioural assumptions. A deterministic simulation is
the special case of a point mass. When no probabilities over historical explanations are justified,
the natural answer is a family of laws,
KM,s(Dk) = {Kh,s : h ∈HM(Dk)}.
(6.8)
The system can report each explanation, bounds on a deterministic outcome, or lower and upper
event probabilities across this family. Those are different result types. A range of conditional
means is not a confidence interval, and an envelope of conditional quantiles is not itself a single
predictive distribution.

and report outcomes must have specified measurable spaces. Assume that Kh,s is a probability
