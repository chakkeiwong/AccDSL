11.12
Funding, capital and decision policies
Liquidity simulation settles the dated net payment queue by venue/currency, respecting encum-
brance, available cash, committed line terms and admissible actions. Haircut/eligibility and
transferability come from a versioned policy; market value alone is not available liquidity. With
cash 20, due withdrawal 30 and an admitted usable facility 8, even a full draw leaves shortfall 2.
Record the default/unresolved settlement according to the event’s legal model. Raising exactly
2 of new equity without an investor event is an invented financing source. Interbank contagion
requires explicit counterparties and settlement priorities; it cannot be inferred from aggregate
deposits.
11.12.1
A draw request and three cash histories
Make the facility example precise. Cash 20, securities 50 and gross loans 80 less allowance 10
give assets 140; deposits 120 leave equity 20. A synthetic agreement limits borrowing to 10 and
accepts one unencumbered security lot with market value 10 and a 20% haircut. The other
securities 40 are ineligible. With no existing drawing, usable capacity is min(10, 10(1−0.2)) = 8.
The agreement is an authored assumption, not evidence of a central-bank entitlement. The
collateral remains the bank’s asset; pledging it does not create another asset or sell the existing
one.
Requesting 8 creates no cash journal. Confirmed receipt 8 debits cash 8 and credits borrowing 8.
Confirmed receipt 6 instead posts 6, leaving the unfilled request visible. A third history retains
a pending request and independent evidence that no receipt reached the bank before the
withdrawal deadline. Its cash stays 20. An acknowledgement timeout alone cannot establish
that third history: the receipt may have occurred even though the acknowledgement was lost.
Assume the deposit contract permits partial payment of the due withdrawal 30 and classifies
the unpaid portion as a liquidity default without waiving it. Pay the cash actually available.

Each payment debits deposits and credits cash. In every column final cash is 0 and securities 50
plus net loans 70 give assets 120. Deposits plus borrowing total 100, leaving equity 20. Profit
and OCI are 0. The unpaid amount is part of the deposit liability, so adding it again would
overstate liabilities. Under the specified activities, deposit settlement is operating cash and the
separate borrowing receipt is financing cash (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2023a,
paragraphs 14–17).
The corresponding records must retain facility version, borrower, currency, settlement venue,
eligibility, encumbrance, valuation date, haircut and existing drawings. Capacity, request and
settlement have distinct identities. A proposed request exceeding capacity is rejected. An
independently evidenced unexpected actual receipt must nevertheless be recorded, together
with the control violation; admission rules cannot justify omitting money that arrived. The
existing publication and replay rules apply to the cash and borrowing as one transaction. A
repeated identifier with identical content has no second effect; a changed payload conflicts. Lost
acknowledgement preserves the possible committed and uncommitted histories until reconciled.
