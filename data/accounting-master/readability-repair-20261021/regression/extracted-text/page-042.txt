2.14.3
Country sales and long-lived assets answer different questions
The annual note separately presents country sales for fiscal 2024, 2023 and 2022 and country
long-lived-asset stocks at the ends of fiscal 2024 and 2023. It separately reports countries
individually accounting for at least 10% of the respective totals and groups the rest; that
selection rule does not make “Other countries” a single location. The 2024 displayed amounts

The China country-sales row equals Greater China segment sales in all three annual columns.
The note’s China footnote includes Hong Kong and Taiwan, so the equality is a useful check on
these displayed populations. It does not turn that row into a legal-entity or production-location
ledger. The U.S. country-sales row is below Americas segment sales by 24,849 million in 2024;
assigning that difference to particular countries requires the precise country-to-region attribution
and customer facts. Europe’s segment label cannot be substituted for a European-country total
because it includes India, the Middle East and Africa (Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 13, printed
pp. 46–47).
The 45,680 million long-lived-asset total equals net property, plant and equipment at the 2024
balance date, and the 2023 totals both equal 43,715 million at their displayed precision (Apple
Inc., 2024a, Notes 5 and 13, printed pp. 39 and 47). The equality is a checked comparison,
not a definition of every asset’s nature, owner or productive capacity. Country asset stocks
are measured at an instant, while sales cover a year. Further, Apple says the information
supplied to its chief operating decision maker for segment assessment excludes asset information
(Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 13, printed p. 46). Thus neither a segment return on assets nor an
allocation of production capacity to the five sales regions is identified by the disclosed tables.
The statement that hardware is made substantially by outsourcing partners located primarily in
China mainland, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and Vietnam concerns another population,
and does not repair that missing asset/segment mapping (Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 12, printed
p. 46).
