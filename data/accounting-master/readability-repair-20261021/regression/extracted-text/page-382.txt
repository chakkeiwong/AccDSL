A value is a reduced rational, or a typed symbolic/domain reference. A posting is nonnegative
with its side carrying the sign, in the book’s functional currency. Original units and the valuation
reference survive conversion. Monetary contexts require currency; a period flow requires a
calendar and exclusive end boundary. Historical zero postings may preserve traceability but
establish no economic event. New portable proposals omit zero lines and retain their explanation
in the event and findings instead. The amount may have finer precision before the declared
posting-rounding operation.
Schema validation rejects unknown fields but cannot prove that a treatment is permitted, a
reference points to the correct kind of record, or two periods are comparable. Those semantic
checks precede solver or runtime dispatch. The structural fixture and complete demonstration
source/package are supplied separately so that a future implementation can check both layers
explicitly. fixtures/core-ir-demo.json supplies an authored expected lowering; the reference
checker evaluates its selected debt expressions. A general source-to-IR compiler and complete
external-registry validation remain to be implemented. Domain records retain disclosed display
text and rounding rules alongside their exact bounds.
B.3
Typing and partial-information rules
For expressions x and y in the same currency C, the money addition rule is
x : Money⟨C⟩
y : Money⟨C⟩
x + y : Money⟨C⟩
.
Context compatibility is a separate premise for observations. There is no analogous implicit
rule for different currencies. Explicit conversion uses a dated measurement and records its rate
and purpose. A rate-times-principal calculation is typed through a declared accrual convention;
the example’s annual simple rate and year fraction produce money in the principal’s currency.
