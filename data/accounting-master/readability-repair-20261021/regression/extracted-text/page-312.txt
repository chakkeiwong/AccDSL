This failure is relevant to the retained BootChainLadder source. Before forming its returned
process triangles and totals, it replaces every process NA by zero. The returned array has no
corresponding mask distinguishing failed future draws from structural cells. A valid payment
vector (0, 4, 0) and a vector (0, 4, failed) therefore become the same numeric vector and total.
Inspecting the final array cannot recover the lost distinction (Gesmann and contributors, 2023,

The unmodified output is consequently retained as source evidence but is unsupported as a
complete predictive result under this proposal. A future instrumented implementation could
preserve statuses before coercion, but would need its own source identity, dependency lock and
numerical checks. Merely wrapping the returned array is insufficient. The documentary fixtures
specify that boundary; they do not establish an implemented or tested R adapter.
12.18.1
The exchange retains the law and the whole draw
An optional reserving.evaluate@1 signature uses the common operation envelope. Its declared
profile names the claims triangle, method and process law, requested draw identifiers, information
view and producer evidence. The present profile is a specification example that consumes
authored evidence. It makes no financial-book mutation and grants no probability-law admission.
Its request and result references use the same typed identity and dependency rules as the other
operations.
The triangle carries the currency, base payment unit, scale, origin and development labels,
payment-period mapping, valuation date and explicit cell mask. Observed cumulative amounts,
future cells and unavailable observed amounts stay distinct. The inspected routine rejects
unequal row and column counts, so the proposed initial adapter subset is square even though
the native error message describes a broader shape. Rectangular support requires a separately
inspected implementation.
Each replicate carries one fit identifier and its complete joint vector of future cells. A cell
retains both its fitted mean and its process payment, with a status and reason if the payment is
unavailable. A valid zero remains a value. Missing cells, repeated draw identifiers, a different
fit inside one draw, or a currency mismatch prevent acceptance. Any failed cell leaves the
draw total unresolved. Native evidence lacking the original status mask cannot be relabeled as
complete by an adapter.
