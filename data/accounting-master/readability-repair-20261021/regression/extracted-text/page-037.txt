Delivery assumptions also need the narrative that surrounds the purchase table. Apple describes
single or limited sources for some components, industry-wide shortages and price fluctuations,
and capacity or yield constraints for new technologies. Suppliers may favour common components
over custom ones. Its named manufacturing locations describe outsourced production, not a
product-by-revenue-region allocation or ownership map (Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 12, printed
p. 46). A scenario in which input availability changes may alter output, variable lease payments,
inventory and receipts together. The passage motivates those dependencies without identifying
their size or probabilities.
The same note, and the interim contingency note, preserve management’s dated assessment
of unresolved legal claims. At each date, management states that there was not at least a
reasonable possibility that Apple had incurred a material loss, or a material loss greater than
a recorded accrual (Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 12, printed p. 46) (Apple Inc., 2025c, Note 9,
printed p. 11). It does not make litigation exposure zero. Contract terms, supply constraints,
commencement, delivery and settlement must therefore remain in the historical record alongside
reported balances. They determine which future journal paths can be supported when a funding
question is asked.
