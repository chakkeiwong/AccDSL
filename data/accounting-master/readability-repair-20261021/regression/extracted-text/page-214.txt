IFRS 9.B6.5.27(b) requires partial discontinuation for the forecast volume that is no longer
highly probable; paragraphs 6.5.6 and B6.5.22 make the change prospective. Paragraph B6.5.23
prevents voluntary discontinuation of a relationship that continues to qualify. These are the
inspected historical EU 2023/1803 provisions (European Commission, 2023a). The example
supplies the qualification and documentation findings; a percentage reduction alone cannot
establish them.
For this case, the 100 tonnes have identical per-unit exposures, one original measurement date
and one price origin. The identified 20 tonnes correspond to 20% of the entire derivative,
whose proportional designation is permitted by paragraph 6.2.4(c). This homogeneity matters.

The stopped portion now carries two distinct origins: derivative value 240 and accumulated
reserve 200. Suppose a later observation gives price 95 and whole-derivative value 1400. The
continuing derivative portion has gained 0.8×1400 = 1120 since original designation; its hedged
cash-flow change is −80(95 −80) = −1200. Its reserve is therefore 1120 and its cumulative
ineffectiveness is zero. The earlier continuing ineffectiveness of 160 has reversed.
The stopped derivative portion is now worth 0.2×1400 = 280. Its additional gain of 280−240 =
40 enters profit under paragraph 5.7.1; it does not increase the stopped reserve. Alongside
the prior stopped ineffectiveness of 40, it leaves total cumulative profit of 80. If the stopped
purchase is still expected, total reserve is 1120 + 200 = 1320. Applying the lower-of rule to the
whole original position would instead give reserve 1400 and profit zero. That calculation would
incorrectly keep hedging the portion whose designation has ended.
The distinction between “highly probable” and “expected” decides what happens to the frozen
200. Paragraph 6.5.12 explicitly allows a forecast transaction to remain expected after it ceases
to be highly probable. Its reserve then remains until the cash flows occur, subject to the
unrecoverable-loss rule. If the stopped purchase is no longer expected, the remaining 200 moves
immediately from the reserve to profit. Total reserve and profit become 1120 and 280. A query
or later valuation does not itself make that expectation judgment.

100 tonnes arrive, supplier payment is 9500 and the remaining reserve of 1320 reduces initial
