10.1.1
Apple: debt balances and cash repayment

adjustments included in the carrying amount. The following amounts are in millions of US

The cash-flow statement reports no net term-debt issuance proceeds and cash repayments of
9,958 million for fiscal 2024 (Apple Inc., 2024a, printed p. 33). To connect that cash movement
to the table, first calculate the difference between the change in reported principal and net
term-debt cash flow. It is −9,231 −(−9,958) = 727 million. Adding the reported measurement-
component changes then gives the carrying-amount movement. With ∆denoting closing less
opening balance, the two displayed-amount identities are
∆principal = −9,958 + 727 = −9,231,
∆carrying amount = −9,958 + 727 + 35 + 755 = −8,441.
The 727 is an unallocated balance-to-cash difference. It does not identify an exchange movement
or a journal. Likewise, the 35 is the change in a net unamortised balance, rather than an
identified issuance fee or interest charge; the 755 is a change in the debt’s hedge adjustment,
rather than a cash receipt. Together they explain 790 of the 1,517 million difference between the
carrying-amount movement and net cash repayment. A forecast that used the 8,441 carrying
decline as repayment would therefore compute the wrong cash amount for this observed year.
The cash statement’s proceeds measure is net; it must not automatically become legal face
issued in another period.
The maturity schedule answers a different funding question. Its six future principal buckets
sum to 97,341 million, with 10,930 in fiscal 2025. The current carrying amount is 10,912. Their
18 million difference does not allocate a discount or hedge adjustment to the current portion.
The measurements differ, and their time boundaries must also be retained: Note 1 supplies the
fiscal-year default, the issuance table explicitly labels its 2024–2062 range as calendar years, and
MD&A separately reports amounts payable within twelve months. MD&A also reports future
term-debt interest payments of 38.5 billion dollars, including 2.6 billion within twelve months
(Apple Inc., 2024a, printed pp. 25, 34 and 43). Those cash requirements cannot replace period
interest expense or a present value. The dated Level 2 fair-value disclosures likewise measure
something different: the annual note reports 88.4 billion dollars at September 2024, while the
interim note reports 82.7 billion at December 2024 alongside a rounded carrying amount of 94.8
billion (Apple Inc., 2025c, Note 6, printed p. 10).
