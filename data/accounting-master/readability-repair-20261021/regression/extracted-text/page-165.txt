9.10
Fair value and capitalization boundaries
A ValuationRequest names the unit of account, market/date, asset/liability characteristics,
principal/accessible market, technique, input hierarchy, counterparty/own non-performance risk
where relevant, calibration and sensitivity. IFRS 13.24, .42–43 and .67–80 guide the selected
measurement. Quoted identical asset price 7 for 10 units gives 70 in a qualifying Level 1 case; a
blockage discount based only on holding size cannot reduce it to 63 under 69/80. A present-value
illustration 111 due next year at an independently supported 11% rate gives 100; that equality
does not establish the rate, market comparability or accounting classification. Historical EIR,
an impairment discount, risk-neutral pricing and a physical expected return answer different
questions.
Borrowing-cost capitalization requires a qualifying asset, eligible costs, commencement/suspen-
sion/cessation dates and the specific/general-borrowing calculation, before moving interest into
cost. IAS 23.8–25 supplies these rules in the retained historical EU text: for specific borrowing,
eligible actual costs less temporary investment income; for general borrowing, the applicable
weighted rate and cost ceiling. Commencement requires expenditures, borrowing costs and
necessary preparation activities; extended inactive periods can suspend it, and substantial
completion ends it. Assume specific borrowing 100 outstanding during a full eligible construction
year at 5%, with an admitted expenditure schedule and temporary investment income 1 on
temporarily unused funds during that period. Net capitalized cost is 4. Real admission still
needs the dated standard, actual schedule and eligibility evidence. It is not a default 5% add-on
to all assets. Internally developed intangibles likewise need research/development separation
and all applicable recognition criteria; a forecast of future sales does not establish an asset.
The extension interface returns the stated measured components, proposed journal, economic
delta, disclosures and unresolved inputs under the event-basis, authorization and commit con-
tracts. Calculation success does not permit another operation to fill a missing authority/fact
with a default. Real-company acquisition/tax/employee/valuation evidence and the full excep-
tions remain explicit internal preparation, with proposed answers and competing treatments
retained for any later authorized domain review.
