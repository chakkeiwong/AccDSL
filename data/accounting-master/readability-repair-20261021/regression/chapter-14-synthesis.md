# Chapter 14 — what each kind of evidence can decide

Exact Version3.46, physical pages355–368. Author diagnosis only; independent

The chapter begins from the consequences of the preceding methods: balanced
journals, reproducible software and a fitted forecast answer different questions.
Its counterexamples prevent a correct total or successful program run from
standing in for the accounting or scientific argument. The conformance cases
retain unsupported and incomplete outcomes as outputs to check, rather than
omitting them from the denominator.

The six evaluation protocols give those distinctions operational consequences.
Extraction needs source preservation; reconstruction needs the declared family
and conditional accounting target; forecasting needs a released outcome and
matched information. Credit and insurance require data and conditioning beyond
aggregate disclosures. Inference is checked against its mathematical target
before speed is compared. Language usability compares the same tasks and IR,
while adapter evidence distinguishes source inspection from actual integration.
The CRPS example is small enough to reconstruct and explicitly demonstrates why
one outcome cannot rank predictive methods.

The budget ladder preserves the difference between exact checks, exposed
development, retrospective comparison and new heldout evidence. Defaults and
debugging seeds are provisional choices. A poor candidate can motivate a repair;
a broken target, invalid data or absent essential diagnostic can invalidate the
comparison. Joined company examples return this distinction to actual loan,
tax, ownership and insurance quantities. A correct group bridge cannot validate
the valuations or forecasts fed into it.

The capability and dependency tables have a legitimate comparison purpose.
They specify what each sector must retain and why common records precede later
engines. P0–P6 are proposed implementation dependencies, not completed product
phases. The matched textual frontend remains restricted to the debt fragment;
broader typed construction does not silently expand the parser promise.
