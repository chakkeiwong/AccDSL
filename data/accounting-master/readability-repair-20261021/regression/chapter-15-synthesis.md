# Appendix A — precise references after the taught mechanisms

Exact Version3.46, physical pages369–379. Author diagnosis only.

The appendix's reference form serves an implementer who has already read the
body. It first collects three increasingly rich packages: the Northstar
corporate case, joined company state and migration, and real disclosures with
partial answers. Source-reference closure, accounting admission and actual
execution remain separate. Exact filenames and record versions are useful
here because they identify the finite exchanges; they do not replace an
explanation of the financial mechanisms.

The primitive registry preserves the important sequence of effects. Extraction
prepares candidates; review proposals do not accept themselves; temporal
selection invents no availability; compilation does not run a solver. History
construction retains unresolved formulas instead of defining a family by its
witnesses. Uncertainty evaluation, admission and selection remain separate.
Scenario proposals do not rewrite historical books. Assessment, authorization,
proposal and atomic accounting commit are different operations.

Storage entries keep transport and financial decisions distinct. Import
verification does not admit an accounting assertion, export has an explicit
incomplete outcome, and migration must preserve semantics. The closed records
make these distinctions concrete through exact references, immutable book mode
and the absence of forward references to the final receipt. Method definitions
include algorithms, applicability, unknown preconditions and unsupported cases;
a schema name alone does not establish a method.

The EPS records continue the finite method taught in the body. Context hashes,
reduced rational amounts, complete inventories, weighted shares and chronology
are checked independently of source interpretation. Failure in diluted EPS can
retain independently ready basic EPS. The company bridge then derives inputs
from dated books, tax, consolidation and share histories; group profit or a
closing share count cannot substitute for the ordinary numerator and weighted
denominator. Its two documentary worlds and conversion-at-maturity scope remain
explicit.

No new defect was confirmed. Long field listings are appropriate for this
lookup role, although independent implementability and human comprehension
remain untested. The appendix consistently refuses to treat finite exchanges
as a working engine, general importer or durable storage proof.
