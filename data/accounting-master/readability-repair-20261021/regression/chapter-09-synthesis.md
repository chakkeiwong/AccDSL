# Chapter 9 — operating methods, retained history and applicable rules

Exact Version 3.46, physical pages 113–176, read continuously in the saved
segments beginning 109–114 and ending 175–180. This is author diagnosis;
independent accounting, implementability and human-reader acceptance remain
pending. No manuscript source was changed during this review.

The chapter makes the common-core proposal concrete without making the
reporting bases interchangeable. Economic terms, accounting treatment and
company labels enter as separate meanings. The matched inventory case then
demonstrates the consequence: the same deliveries and purchases can give
different cost of sales and carrying inventory. A label conversion cannot
repair that difference, and a disclosed company policy is not universal
standards authority.

The long operating-events section develops a recurring substantive question:
what past facts must survive for the next amount to be measured correctly?
Advance credits require separate billing and cash histories. Returns retain
refund obligations and recovery rights. Service warranties and mixed contract
changes distinguish earned performance from cash and from the allocation to
remaining promises. Production retains capacity, the purpose of expenditure,
opening cohorts and completion by cost component. PPE retains availability,
old component cost, depreciation, revaluation origin and impairment ceilings.
Benefits retain measured obligations, plan assets, event-date assumptions and
the terms that make a surplus available. The examples repeatedly show a
balanced but wrong alternative, so debit/credit conservation is not silently
promoted into measurement correctness.

The inventory cash bridge is particularly useful. Adding total depreciation
and deducting total inventory is consistent; so is adding only depreciation
that reached profit and deducting cash-cost inventory. Mixing those treatments
would fail. The mixed-contract billing example similarly derives its net-asset
identity by cancellation, then shows early billing and full prepayment as
different paths to the same earned revenue. These examples give the reader a
reason for the extra state fields rather than merely enumerating them.

The estimate/error case joins accounting history to information history. A
new operating restriction changes remaining depreciation prospectively; an
originally available schedule entered wrongly changes comparatives and
opening equity. The numerical tax and discovery-entry consequences keep
current-quarter correction separate from prior-period adjustment. Original
publications remain retrievable, and the public and system cutoffs select
report editions without backdating discovery. A corrected earliest opening
position is not a full restated income-statement column.

The Apple cases test how much of this state disclosures actually identify.
Inventory adjustments are not purchases. Gross and accumulated PPE reject one
simple cash-additions reconstruction but leave conditional alternatives. The
next sections strengthen the test: each alternative requires a different
company remainder; an abstract offset must also preserve detailed rows; and
even conditional annual-statement invariance need not preserve future cash
categories or payment dates. The text keeps necessary subtraction results,
conditional transformations and actual whole-company admission separate.
Joint cash pairs explain why independently combining marginal intervals can
invent a path that neither history produces.

The tax evidence follows the same discipline. Current expense, deferred
expense, combined payable, net deferred assets, escrow and settlement have
different populations. The State Aid discussion retains currency-specific
rounding assumptions and does not turn an unexplained movement into an FX
journal. The possible decrease in unrecognised benefits supplies neither a
payment law nor a probability distribution. The return to Northstar preserves
one shared debt fraction across the statements while retaining its simplified
tax assumption and annual-time limitation.

Subsequent-event methods then pay off the promise that recognition is not the
end of a component's life. Actual return settlement replaces estimates with
admitted refund, handling and recovered-stock facts. Zero carrying weights
leave a CGU reversal allocation unresolved; individual capacity does not
supply group weights. Heterogeneous lease rights need their own removal
amounts. Contract-cost impairment precedes the separate onerous provision,
and later recovery or cancellation facts revise the two amounts differently.
The minimum unavoidable loss is not a choice of management's eventual action.

The standards ending makes dates and entity facts operational. Historical
AASB/EU texts, current operative authority, company elections and actual tax
law retain distinct roles. US methods remain limited by the stated current
Codification gaps. ASBE lease adoption and the later tax interpretation have
different dates and different comparative-transition obligations. Ping An's
December comparative, January adoption and June carrying values illustrate
those distinctions with actual disclosures; a dash is not zero and the
opening asset/liability difference is not automatically retained earnings.
