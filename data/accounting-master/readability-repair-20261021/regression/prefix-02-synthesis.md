# Cumulative reading through Chapter 2

Exact Version 3.46, physical pages 1–61. The front matter and Chapter 1 were
read in segments 001–006 and 007–012; Chapter 2 continues that same saved
reading chain. This synthesis uses those exact-candidate judgments, not the
completed diagnosis of an earlier edition.

The opening proposes preserving different histories when reported aggregates
do not distinguish economically important exposures. Chapter 1's borrowing
example establishes the mechanism before asking for a whole-company account.
Chapter 2 answers with progressively richer evidence: complete synthetic books,
revisions, corporate and sector joins, then actual disclosures whose missing
contracts and movements cannot be supplied from totals. The final conditional
settlement makes the practical consequence explicit: even matched annual rows
need not identify future cash categories or dates, and incompatible marginal
answers must not be combined.

The initial promise and the chapter's conclusion remain consistent. A complete
synthetic close is not presented as a complete reconstruction of Apple. The
real-source cases preserve observations, derived residuals, supplied premises,
management expectations and unknown facts separately. Generating two examples
does not assign them probabilities or establish that only two histories exist.
The connected partial answer is therefore a substantive payoff, not a retreat
from the opening ambition. It identifies what can already be answered and what
the representation must still preserve.

Repeated references to cash, recognition and information timing serve different
calculations: revenue versus collections, commitments versus delivery,
securities versus proceeds, and original versus later disclosure. No confirmed
cross-chapter contradiction or terminology drift has emerged in this prefix.
The one confirmed local intrusion, RR346-001 on page 28, remains open. The long
Apple sequence has a coherent dependency chain, but author diagnosis cannot
establish how readily an independent reader sustains it.

Outstanding promises now belong to the following chapters: a reusable
assertion and mapping representation, construction of compatible histories,
conditions for probabilistic inference, accounting-rule scope, execution
contracts and evidence for the design's acceptance. This prefix does not close
those obligations or imply completed implementation. Whole-document diagnosis,
independent-reader feedback and human acceptance remain pending.
