# Chapter 10 — financing rights and the reporting perimeter

Exact Version 3.46, physical pages 177–243. Ordered reading is preserved in
segments175–180 through241–246. This synthesis uses the current candidate's
saved reading judgments; earlier-edition credit is not imported. Independent
domain,implementability and human-reader acceptance remain pending.

The chapter begins with a distinction that its examples sustain: the operating
assets can remain while the funding claims and ownership change. Debt carrying
amounts do not describe coupons,repayment rights or future liquidity. Apple's
reported debt movements demonstrate why principal,unamortised balances,hedge
adjustments and gross cash movements cannot be substituted for one another.
The short borrowing example adds a consequential omission: an annual net flow
can hide a temporary funding need. These examples make contract state relevant
before the chapter introduces more elaborate securities.

The equity discussion then separates money from share quantities and share
quantities from dated participation rights. A split-adjusted EPS denominator
does not rewrite historical outstanding shares. The dilution derivation gives
a sign-safe inclusion condition and a restricted additive ordering result;its
worked wrong comparison explains why testing every instrument against basic
EPS is insufficient. The reporting proposal also admits what it cannot find:
an omitted instrument cannot be discovered from an allegedly complete list.

Conversion connects those rules to company statements. Actual and potential
share windows remain disjoint,and parent earnings differ from group earnings.
The principal92 and principal100 examples can give equal diluted EPS without
equal actual earnings or contractual obligations. The later Apple share-history
witnesses sharpen the same point: annual and interim constraints can coexist
with different holdings between observations. The text preserves the limited
quantity scope of those witnesses and assigns them no probability.

Awards extend the distinction from share counts to rights. Reserve consumption,
earned awards,delivered shares and cash rights are different populations. The
performance examples explain interpolation,threshold jumps,return caps and
observation windows before agreement-version changes alter the conditional
outcomes. The synthetic IFRS calculations are expressly separated from Apple's
US-GAAP treatment. Modification,cancellation and settlement cases retain event
dates and earlier recognised amounts instead of treating each event as a fresh
award. These repetitions add new consequences to the same history.

The hedge sequence is the chapter's longest local argument. It begins with
cumulative measurement and makes later releases depend on retained origins.
Inventory basis adjustment,interest reclassification and discontinuation do
different things to earnings and equity. Protection premium,aligned intrinsic
exposure and time value require distinct explanations. The collar weights are
limited consistency witnesses,not a probability forecast. Added instruments
and reductions then show why equal remaining nominal exposure does not imply
equal reserves or profit.

Hypothetical participating-dividend tax allocation affects EPS without creating

The group ending changes the reporting population while preserving the same
discipline. Control,ownership percentage,legal books and consolidation entries
are separate inputs. The foreign-subsidiary lifecycle links acquisition,
operations,translation and disposal. Its cash exchange effect7 differs from
translation OCI12.6;only the parent's reserve10.4 recycles on loss of control,
even when20% remains. Both sales end at equity353.8 but leave different cash

so the intermediate statements do indispensable work.

The implementation discussion gives those figures owners,source versions and
immutable branches. Its replay examples have a substantive purpose: a lost
acknowledgement must not cause a second disposal,and removing subsidiary cash
must not create a fictitious parent payment. It remains a proposed profile and
documentary transaction specification,with runtime failure tests outstanding.

The closing loan example shows that elimination of an internal debt does not
remove the group's currency exposure. Settlement facts change profit versus
OCI even with identical cash and equity. Identified-transaction eliminations
then prevent equal amounts from standing in for counterpart evidence,and
inventory markup stays attached to the remaining lot. Purchasing-power
restatement precedes currency translation;monetary exposure,first-application
tax and comparative histories remain distinct. Finally,exchangeability belongs
to a direction,date and purpose. An observable rate is not automatically a rate
usable for the required measurement,and the new methods need an explicitly
extended group profile.

These mechanisms pay off the chapter's opening funding-and-boundary question
and make bank liquidity and capital the next substantive step. The chapter is
large and demanding;the saved judgments establish specific author observations,
not effortless comprehension. Its open methods,current authority,actual entity
facts and implementation correspondence remain separate from the confirmed
local prose defect. No requirement row closes from this review.
