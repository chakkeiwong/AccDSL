## Reader checks answered from the repaired pages

1. Closing shares compute a different ratio: about $6.201 against reported
   basic EPS $6.11. Income millions require a factor of 1,000 when divided by
   shares thousands. The distinction is visible before formal notation.
2. The weighted average supplies an additional constraint on how long changes
   persist. It leaves more than one admissible quantity schedule in this model.
3. The two illustrated schedules preserve annual quantities, the 91-day
   comparative average and disclosed fourth-quarter quantities. At day 100
   their balances differ by 66,097,000 shares, because issuance is earlier in
   the second history. A dated ownership query therefore needs more facts.
4. Item 2's 99,790 thousand quarter repurchases narrows the net other increase
   to 23,735 thousand at displayed centres. It does not name those events.
5. RSU movements, net common-share issuance and incremental dilution measure
   different populations/timing. Generic four-year vesting and the FY2023
   antidilution exclusion cannot be used as universal rules.
6. Monetary equity reductions, cash payments, compensation expense and unused
   authority retain their separate meanings. The table supports their numerical
   differences but does not allocate causes. Future unrecognised cost is not
   automatically a fixed annual expense or cash liability.
