## Research intent and evidence

Question: does a typed prior-equity extension preserve goodwill dependencies
while routing remeasurement and cumulative OCI without silently selecting a
legal-book or tax method? Baseline: protected symbolic cases,including debt
and missing/future sources. Expected failure: treating debt as equity,adding
carrying value to new consideration,recycling equity FVOCI,or allocating the
parent's prior-investment gain to subsidiary NCI.

Primary criterion: independently authored complete conditional expressions,
dependencies and retained predecessor outputs. Invalid type/date/unit/source
bindings veto the candidate. Unresolved prior classification,source timing,
separate transactions or legal measurement retain dependencies and no report;
they are valid unresolved outcomes,not continuation vetoes. Corrupted protected
inputs invalidate the affected comparison; candidate failures trigger repair.
Counts/runtime are explanatory. This stage cannot establish full acquisition
accounting,measurement,tax,separate-book entries or report correctness.

For the finite reviewed branches,C is the prior carrying amount before the
acquisition-date update,P the prior fair value,and O cumulative prior OCI.
FVTPL routes P−C to pretax parent profit with O constrained to explicit zero.
Eligible,elected equity FVOCI routes P−C to OCI; the old O is never recycled.
An explicit retain choice leaves O+P−C in the reserve; an explicit transfer
choice moves that cumulative amount within equity. This operation is owner-only
and noncash; the acquisition excess continues to use P once. No numerical
amounts or complete report are produced. Tax remains an explicit dependency.
