# The two transition dependencies now have primary-source support

12 October 2026. P17; E07/C05, E01/C03; MC05/19/22/28/30/32–35.
The official discovery route acquired both primary PDFs within eight requests.
All **55 source/edition/preservation checks pass**. Forty-five selected pages,
29 exact anchors and ten inspected renders support the scoped dependency map.
Ten additional renders were generated but not visually inspected. The prior
failed URLs were not repeated and the closed transition units remain intact.

AASB 108's checked provisions require every reasonable effort before concluding
impracticability and distinguish evidence about past circumstances that would
have been available at prior statement authorisation from later information.
An estimate developed later is not automatically inadmissible; hindsight is.
The particular transition provisions in AASB 17 still govern its permitted
modified/fair-value information uses. Public investor availability is a separate
project clock and is not established by statement authorisation.

AASB 13 defines a market-participant exit price. A hypothetical liability
transfer leaves the obligation outstanding; it is not settlement with the
policyholder. Relevant observable inputs have priority, but supported
unobservable inputs and suitably adjusted own data are permitted. A liability's
fair value reflects non-performance risk, including own credit. AASB 17.31
expressly excludes the issuer's own non-performance risk from issued-contract
FCF. The two C20 quantities are different measurement targets; their numerical
equality, difference or amount has not been computed for any group here.

The sources are historical: 108 compilation 3, 31 December 2022, applying from
1 January 2023; 13 compilation 2, 30 June 2021, applying from 1 July 2021 and
selected by the official 2023-period search. The first 13 result was a
2024-applicable edition and was excluded from the historical technical support.
That exclusion is a supported edition veto, not a criticism of the later
standard. Exact applicability for an actual entity, early adoption and the
complete later amendment chain remain unresolved. No ASBE equivalence follows.
