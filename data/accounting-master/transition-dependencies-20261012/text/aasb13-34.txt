Compilation details 
Accounting Standard AASB 13 Fair Value Measurement (as amended) 
Compilation details are not part of AASB 13. 
This compiled Standard applies to annual periods beginning on or after 1 July 2021.  It takes into account amendments 
up to and including 6 March 2020 and was prepared on 8 June 2021 by the staff of the Australian Accounting 
Standards Board (AASB). 
This compilation is not a separate Accounting Standard made by the AASB.  Instead, it is a representation of 
AASB 13 (August 2015) as amended by other Accounting Standards, which are listed in the table below.
