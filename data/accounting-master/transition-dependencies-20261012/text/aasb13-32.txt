APPENDIX C 
Appendix C 
Effective date and transition 
This appendix is an integral part of the Standard and has the same authority as the other parts of the Standard. 
C1 
An entity shall apply this Standard for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2018. Earlier 
application is permitted for periods beginning after 24 July 2014 but before 1 January 2018. If an entity 
applies this Standard for an earlier period, it shall disclose that fact. 
AusC1.1 
Paragraphs C2 and C3 shall not be applied by an entity that has previously applied AASB 13, 
unless required to do so by another Standard. 
C2 
This Standard shall be applied prospectively as of the beginning of the annual period in which it is initially 
applied. 
C3 
The disclosure requirements of this Standard need not be applied in comparative information provided for 
periods before initial application of this Standard.
