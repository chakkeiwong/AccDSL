AccDSL
Company Histories,
Accounting Interpretations,
and Scenario Simulation
Product and method proposal
Version 2.3 (proposal candidate)
8 October 2026
This document specifies a system that translates company disclosures into traceable evidence,
represents several compatible economic and accounting histories, and simulates their future
journals and financial statements under explicit economic scenarios.
The proposed data contracts, methods and acceptance cases specify the intended implementation.
Current source and domain limitations remain explicit; this candidate is not a completed product
or a claim of full proposal readiness. Proposed libraries can be replaced while preserving those
contracts. Sector rule packages require their own accounting review before they can be described
as compliant with a reporting standard.
Research materials, source qualifications and review records accompany this document in
docs/design/research and docs/design/review.

Chapter 1
The product decision
A company’s accounts describe the financial consequences of its activities, but they leave
much of the activity itself unobserved. Revenue combines many sales, a borrowing balance
combines contracts with different terms, and a provision combines estimates about uncertain
future payments. Aggregation is necessary for reporting. It also makes the inverse task—
recovering economic history from published reports—fundamentally different from entering
known transactions into a ledger.
AccDSL will preserve this distinction. Its first product is an evidence-backed representation of
a company’s historical record. Its second is a set of compatible histories and current economic
states. Its third is a collection of conditional simulations, each explaining how a particular
history, accounting treatment and business response produces future journals and reports. These
products share identifiers and provenance, so an analyst can move from a forecast balance to
the assumption or disclosure responsible for it.
The recommended design is a small, typed accounting language over a shared intermediate
representation, combined with an evidence database, contract calculators and replaceable
inference engines. A language earns its place by making recurring accounting decisions explicit
and checkable: which entity and period an amount belongs to, whether it is known or assumed,
which rule applies, and how an event changes a balanced ledger. The language does not supply
missing facts or determine a company’s future behaviour.
1.1
Why journals are necessary but insufficient
Consider a company with a borrowing of 100 currency units and annual interest expense of
5. Assume that the entire principal was outstanding throughout the year, interest was paid at
year-end, and there were no fees, defaults, hedges or foreign-exchange effects. Both a fixed-rate
loan at 5% and a floating-rate loan whose rate happened to be 5% generate the same entries:

The historical accounts are compatible with the whole interval [5, 7] of future interest. This is a
range of feasible amounts. It becomes a probability distribution only when a distribution for α
is supplied. Neither the number of generated interpretations nor a language model’s confidence
provides that distribution.
Journals remain essential because simulated economic events must have coherent accounting
consequences. However, the simulated state must also contain contract terms, maturities,
customer or borrower cohorts, collateral, unrecognised commitments, and management decisions.
These determine what the journals will be under a changed economy. Reconstructing every
historical transaction is unnecessary when a smaller state preserves the responses needed for
the intended scenarios. Establishing that smaller state is an explicit modelling task, examined
in Chapter 6.
The analogy with competing legal interpretations is useful at the level of representation.
Several readings may survive the evidence and require separate reasoning. In accounting,
some alternatives concern the meaning of a disclosure; others concern undisclosed facts or
future behaviour. The system must identify which kind of uncertainty is involved. A known
contractual term must not become an arbitrary interpretive choice merely because another
choice is convenient for a simulation.
1.2
Users, questions and deliverables
An accounting analyst needs to inspect a number in its original table, verify its entity, currency
and period, and see how it reconciles with the notes. A financial modeller needs to distinguish
a reported balance from assumptions about its composition.
A language engineer needs
deterministic semantics and tests. A researcher needs to compare explanations under the same
scenario, including explanations that fit history equally well.
The first release must answer the following complete workflow. An analyst loads a set of reports
and specifies an information cutoff. AccDSL preserves the original documents and extracts
source-linked observations. It identifies reconciliation failures and constructs an explicitly
bounded family of compatible histories. The analyst declares additional assumptions where
simulation requires them. The system executes each requested scenario and returns statements,
journal traces, explanation-specific distributions or bounds, and unresolved limitations. When a
later filing arrives, the analyst can compare the revised interpretation with the earlier information
set.

1.3
What constitutes a useful first implementation
The first demonstrator should reconstruct three reporting dates and the intervening annual
and interim flows for one company, including at least one restatement or revised comparative.
It must preserve a borrowing-composition ambiguity and execute the fixed/floating example
through both journals and all affected statement lines. A synthetic company supplies known
hidden history; a public company supplies realistic disclosure and extraction problems. Their
roles differ: the synthetic case tests preservation and rejection of known alternatives, while the
public case tests traceability and practical coverage.
Success is a reproducible chain of evidence and computation, with identified gaps. A convincing-
looking complete ledger reconstructed from incomplete disclosures would fail this product
contract if its assumptions were hidden. Forecast accuracy requires a later evaluation with
historical information cutoffs and suitable baselines; it is not established by the first demonstra-
tor.

To demonstrate a source-to-forecast calculation, freeze the interim balance sheet and apply a
marginal financing stress for a following 13-week period: a 200-basis-point rate increase, no
principal change, no offsetting hedge beyond the explicitly selected exposure, immediate cash
settlement and no tax effect. An assumed effective floating exposure E = 14,575 million produces

balance-sheet difference equals the equity difference exactly.

Northstar’s corrected receivable and Apple’s several debt amounts show why the source record
must retain more than a label and a number. The procedures below determine which assertions
enter a model and which remain unresolved.
3.1
The record is a collection of assertions
The original record includes financial statements, notes, accounting policies, auditor opinions,
management discussion, segment tables, security terms, regulatory returns and subsequent
announcements. A complete ingestion process accounts for every page and every extracted
block. A complete financial model is a stronger claim: it would require economically relevant
information that public reports may never disclose. AccDSL therefore reports document coverage,
semantic coverage and simulation coverage separately.
Each source span receives a disposition: structured observation, narrative assertion, relationship,
policy evidence, contextual material, duplicate, or unresolved extraction. “Unresolved” remains
visible. A commitment buried in a note is not discarded because it has no current balance-sheet
line. An auditor’s qualification is retained as evidence about the record; it does not become an
automatic numerical haircut. Management’s forecast is a dated assertion with an author and
modality, not an observed future outcome.
An observation is an immutable record with the fields below. Values inferred from observations
are separate records, linked by a derivation. This avoids overwriting a company’s statement
with an analyst’s preferred reconstruction.

A narrative assertion adds actor, predicate, object, scope, quantifier and modality. For example,
“substantially all debt is fixed rate” has a source span and a qualitative quantifier. It does not
assert α = 1. A reviewed interpretation can introduce a numerical restriction such as α ≥a,
but the threshold a remains an analyst assumption unless the source defines it. Boilerplate
policy language also does not prove that a company holds a particular instrument.
3.2
Different names, different measurements
An accounting concept is more than a row label. “Debt” can refer to legal principal, amortised
carrying amount, undiscounted contractual cash flows or a fair-value disclosure. These amounts
can all be correct at the same date. Likewise, “revenue” may be reported gross or net, a segment
measure may differ from the consolidated measure, and an alternative performance measure
may exclude selected expenses. A mapping must state its relationship: equivalent meaning,
component of, broader than, presentation reclassification, or measurement transformation.
The common vocabulary will preserve source concepts as first-class identifiers. Company exten-
sions may inherit a broad economic category without being declared identical to a standardised
concept. For instance, an extension can be a component of borrowings while retaining an
unresolved split between lease liabilities and other financing. Many-to-many mappings are
allowed; each mapping has applicable dates, dimensions and evidence. An aggregation mapping
does not silently become a measurement conversion.
Standards differ in recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure.
Consequently,
conversion between IFRS, US GAAP and Chinese Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises
(ASBE) is not generally a dictionary lookup. The original basis remains preserved, and any
comparison basis is a separate derived book. The same principle applies to tax and prudential
capital. A tax loss carryforward, accounting deferred tax asset and regulatory capital deduction
can concern the same economic loss while belonging to different measurement systems.
3.3
Time, revisions and information availability
At least four times matter. A machine may enter service in November; its cost appears in the
accounts for the year ending December; the annual report is published in March; and AccDSL
ingests the report in April. A later report may revise the December comparative. These times
answer different questions and must remain distinct.

The storage model uses validity intervals for economic assertions and transaction intervals for
database versions, following the distinction in Snodgrass and Ahn (1986). Publication and
availability timestamps are additional domain fields. Database transaction time records what
AccDSL contained; it cannot substitute for what investors could have known. An “as known
on” query applies an explicit availability rule and does not include a later restatement simply
because its economic period is earlier.
AccDSL supports three named views: the original filing view; the latest restated view, with a
stated information cutoff; and a harmonised research view, with explicit transformations. No
view changes the stored source facts. Corrections have links to the replaced assertion and a
reason. Where two sources conflict without a clear supersession relationship, both survive with
a conflict record.
Instant values and interval flows have different types. A balance at 30 June is not a six-month
flow. Calendar intervals use half-open boundaries [t0, t1) internally; a filing period ending 30
June maps to the next boundary at 1 July in its reporting calendar. Quarter-only earnings may
be derived from year-to-date values only if the periods, basis, perimeter, units and revision
vintage are compatible. The derivation retains both source observations and their correlated
rounding uncertainty. Fifty-two/fifty-three-week calendars, stub periods and reporting-date
changes require explicit calendar objects.
Accounting-policy changes, estimate changes and error corrections also have different temporal
effects. The applicable rule package decides whether a change is prospective, requires restatement,
or adjusts opening balances. The engine never infers that effect from a generic “revision” flag.
3.4
The company boundary changes
Legal entity, reporting group and economic exposure are separate dimensions. Acquisitions,
disposals, changes in control, joint arrangements, special-purpose vehicles and non-controlling
interests can change the reporting perimeter. A group’s increase in property need not be capital
expenditure; it may reflect an acquisition, a currency translation or a revaluation. The bridge
between opening and closing property must identify these movements before a scenario model
can extrapolate investment.
The company graph therefore stores dated ownership, control and counterparty relationships.
Control is an accounting conclusion with supporting evidence, not a rule that blindly consolidates
every majority-owned entity. Group reporting combines entity books with a separate adjustment
and elimination book. Intragroup receivables and payables require matching counterparties,
currencies and dates. Unmatched eliminations remain unresolved; a balancing “plug” is not a
recovered transaction.
Acquired opening balances, goodwill, purchase-price allocation, divested operations and non-
controlling interests each have their own event types. Analyses can preserve reported scope or
define a constant perimeter, but the latter is a derived modelling assumption, not the original
record.
3.5
Further problems that the representation must handle
Several difficulties extend beyond differences in standards, industries and company labels.
Recognition and settlement occur on different dates: a sale, invoice, cash receipt and impairment
can belong to four periods. Net balances hide gross flows: repeated borrowing and repayment
can leave the same closing debt. Netting can conceal offsetting exposures and liquidity needs.

Different contractual maturities and repricing dates can share the same current carrying amount.
Guarantees, undrawn credit facilities, litigation and covenants can be economically important
before an asset or liability is recognised.
Foreign currency introduces transaction currency, functional currency and presentation currency,
together with different translation conventions for stocks and flows. Currency translation effects
must not be mistaken for cash receipts. Inventory and depreciation depend on vintages or
lots; an aggregate stock and average age may be insufficient under a shock. Tax bases can
evolve differently from carrying amounts. Deferred tax recognition requires assumptions about
recoverability, rather than multiplication by a tax rate alone.
Public information is selectively disclosed and rounded. Duplicate XBRL facts may agree,
overlap within rounding bounds or contradict one another. A dash can mean zero, immaterial,
unavailable or inapplicable. Tables can mix units, span pages and embed signs in parentheses.
Text and table references can be wrong in the source itself. AccDSL retains these distinctions
and gives an analyst a local repair path without pretending that extraction confidence settles
economic interpretation.
Finally, strategic behaviour matters. A firm may refinance, cut investment, raise equity, change
prices, breach a covenant or fail. A bank may preserve a capital ratio by shrinking assets; an
insurer may change reinsurance or asset allocation. Such responses cannot be deduced from the
balance-sheet identity. They require explicitly calibrated or assumed policies, and alternative
policies must remain separate when the available record does not distinguish them.
3.6
Account for a disclosure before translating it
The importer first freezes a filing package: documents, byte hashes, reporting dates, availability
evidence and incorporated references. It compares the document’s contents page and statement
index with detected section and note headings. A missing heading, table continuation or
referenced exhibit creates an obligation to resolve; successful text extraction cannot discharge it.
Each PDF page and each HTML content unit then has a stable locator and a disposition. Child
units such as a table or note belong to a parent page/section; the importer records overlap
instead of counting the same content twice.
Three denominators remain separate. Acquired-document coverage counts source units with a
disposition, including unresolved units. Semantic coverage counts identified assertions whose
meaning and context have been reviewed. Simulation coverage counts the operations and
exposures required by a specified query that have supported state and rules. The first can be
complete while the other two are partial. An assertion inventory can itself be incomplete; until
the page/section reconciliation is reviewed, semantic coverage is a lower-bound inventory rather
than a percentage of all financial meaning.
Cross-reference traversal is a work-list algorithm. Start with the filing’s statement and note
references, resolve each target against the acquired manifest, add newly discovered targets,
and stop when no unvisited target remains or a declared resource limit is reached. Document
hash plus target locator prevents cycles from expanding forever. A target has one of four
results: resolved, duplicate of a resolved source, unavailable, or ambiguous. The last two
remain obligations. Apple’s proxy and prior-filed agreements show why closure over acquired
files is weaker than closure over incorporated disclosures. A manifest may declare a bounded
acquisition scope, but must show these outstanding references beside its completeness status.
A narrative translator records the actor, predicate, object, quantifier, modality, applicable
entities/dates and qualifications before proposing any constraint. “All notes are fixed-rate”
constrains contractual coupon type; it does not constrain the combined note-and-swap exposure.

3.7
Mappings must establish a financial relationship
A mapping record identifies source concepts, target concept, relationship, applicable entity/ba-
sis/period/dimensions, evidence, reviewer and any required transformation. Equivalence permits
substitution only when all these contexts match. A component relationship permits inclusion
in a sum only through a declared disjoint partition. A broader concept supplies containment,
not an equality. Reclassification changes presentation while preserving the stated population
and total; measurement transformation must supply an actual formula and inputs. These
relationships cannot compose indiscriminately: component followed by equivalence remains
component, while two measurement transforms compose only if their intermediate bases and
information dates agree.
For example, Northstar’s product revenue might split into 180 domestic and 80 overseas, while
its customer channel split is 200 wholesale and 60 retail. Both classify the same 260. Adding all
four numbers gives 520 and is invalid. The partition records therefore share a population ID
but have different axes. To remove an axis, sumOver requires evidence that members are disjoint
and exhaustive for that context. Where the source supplies only named segments, an explicit
unallocated member carries the residual; it is not assumed zero. Geography-by-product cells
cannot be recovered from the two marginals without further constraints or assumptions.
Apple supplies another counterexample: its vendor non-trade receivables arise from component
sales to manufacturing vendors, whose gains reduce product cost of sales when the final products
are sold (annual Note 4, p. 38). They cannot be relabelled as ordinary revenue-generating trade
receivables without changing meaning. Likewise gross derivative assets and liabilities cannot
be silently netted. Company extensions can define these distinctions and dated presentation
changes; they cannot override mandatory recognition rules in a selected standards package.
3.8
Selecting original and revised assertions
The query procedure first filters assertions by the declared availability cutoff, then by an
optional database-time cutoff. A day-only filing timestamp is conservatively available at the
next local midnight in its named timezone; any more precise rule needs evidence. Within the
eligible set, explicit supersession removes only the targeted assertions in matching contexts.
The superseding assertion must itself be eligible. Follow the acyclic graph transitively; retain
all maximal assertions if two eligible corrections conflict. File order and ingestion recency never
decide which is true.
The original view selects the initial assertion chain in a named filing; the latest-available
view selects eligible maximal versions; the harmonised view adds reviewed transformations to
those selections. A revision to revenue does not automatically revise every note or change the
company’s accounting basis. An estimate change takes effect in future measurements from
its specified date. An error correction may change a comparative and an opening balance
bridge, each as a new assertion with its own lineage. Northstar’s explicit query fixture in
Chapter 2 checks all these clocks without assuming that the most recently stored row is always
the appropriate history.

3.9
What an accepted reading permits
Apple’s inventory policy illustrates the distance between reading a sentence and reconstructing
a business. Its reported use of FIFO supports a proposed cost-formula reading for the inventory
and reporting contexts covered by that policy. It does not reveal the quantities and ages
of the actual lots, establish that no write-down occurred, or supply the authority for every
US accounting treatment. Those are separate inputs to a cost and impairment calculation.
Similarly, a straight-line depreciation policy leaves useful lives, residual values, components and
available-for-use dates to be established.
The source preparation retains six located Apple policy passages: consolidation of Apple and
wholly owned subsidiaries; comparative reclassification; FIFO; straight-line depreciation; the
interim statement’s estimates and annual-report reference; and its 52/53-week fiscal calendar.
Each proposed reading carries the original document hash, extraction hash and exact character
slice. These are company assertions. The consolidation sentence, for example, cannot exclude
every partly owned entity without a control analysis. The calendar sentence does not make
every future fiscal year 52 weeks or make a contractual interest year 365 days. The exact
quotations and proposed predicates are retained in ../methods/source-translations-v1.json;
the meaning explained here is not an invented human acceptance of them.
A reading progresses through separate decisions. First, extraction review asks whether the
located words and numbers were transcribed faithfully. Translation review asks whether the
proposed predicate preserves their scope, quantifier, exceptions and reporting context. A model
then admits that predicate, or an explicitly labelled assumption, for a particular purpose
and information view. Accounting eligibility subsequently asks whether a method applies to
the actual event. A faithful transcription of an erroneous company assertion is still faithful
transcription. The four decisions therefore cannot share a single “approved” flag.
An explicit presumption in a standard also differs from filling an unknown with a convenient
answer. Article 6 of the retained official ASBE 21 text requires both practical ability throughout
use and economic benefit for a substantive substitution right.
It specifies a presumption
when that right is difficult to determine. Applying that conditional rule requires its stated
circumstances; it does not transform missing lessor information into a reported fact. Nor
may a translator assign arbitrary numerical thresholds to “substantive”, “probable” or “highly
probable”.
3.10
Three meanings of disclosure coverage
The Apple annual/interim preparation inventories 125 quantities, 13 annual notes, 10 interim
notes, 70 exhibit references and seven further package obligations. All 23 notes retain unresolved
semantic content; none is recorded as fully formalised. Downloading their pages answers a
useful but narrower question than constructing a complete company model.
Three populations make that distinction inspectable. Retained-unit coverage partitions acquired
documents into nonoverlapping pages or smaller leaf units, including duplicates and unreadable
material. Assertion-review coverage counts the separately enumerated material assertions and
their review decisions. Query-exposure coverage identifies the sources, legal facts, methods
and authority that a particular result requires. A fully downloaded annual report can have
incomplete assertion review, and a reviewed debt total can leave a refinancing query unresolved
because an incorporated agreement is missing. No weighted average of these populations
establishes completeness.

3.12
Public availability and system possession are different views
Northstar’s correction explains why a result needs a reporting vintage. A historical forecast
also needs to establish when the exact document bytes were available. A report downloaded
today may carry an old date on its current web page; that does not prove that the same bytes
were public on that date. Public availability evidence and an ingestion receipt answer different
questions.
An investor-information query supplies a cutoff K. Each admitted source must have supported
public availability no later than K and evidence linking the selected bytes to that availability.
Later ingestion is allowed. Later extraction review can read eligible old bytes, but an interpreta-
tion that uses later information fails the cutoff on its own dependencies. This view establishes
public availability, not actual investor awareness or the ability to trade.
A system-as-of query instead supplies an instant S and requires an immutable receipt showing
possession of those bytes by S. Publication cannot replace that receipt. An accepted-layer query

What the literature contributes
The research problem crosses accounting, databases, programming languages, econometrics
and document understanding. Each field resolves part of it. The review below distinguishes
results that can be adopted directly, engineering ideas that need adaptation, and evidence that
does not establish the intended product capability. Source versions and technical anchors are
recorded in the accompanying research ledgers. The search includes work outside the original
collection, with public-index searches and backward and forward citation searches through 2
October 2026. It is a scoped design survey, rather than a claim to enumerate every paper in
these fields.
4.1
Accounting structure and company dynamics
Nehmer and Robinson (1997), especially Sections 2–5, supplies a useful algebraic starting point:
balances occupy a structured state space, transactions change that state, and aggregation relates
detailed accounts to reports. This supports a distinction between conservation constraints and
permitted transactions. An entry can conserve the accounting total and still be an inadmissible
transaction. AccDSL adopts that distinction in its posting validator. Its particular event
vocabulary, evidence model and inverse-history semantics are new design decisions, not results
established by the algebraic paper.
Doshi (2024) develops an accounting DSL with time, transaction logs and correction mechanisms.
The relevant material is in Chapters 4 and 7–9 of the 2024 thesis; the original collection’s
filename misleadingly suggests 2026. It demonstrates why corrections and dates belong in
the language rather than in informal comments. The thesis does not establish recovery of an
entire company history from public reports or inference over economically distinct histories.
Babcinețchi et al. (2022), pp. 342–345, offers a much smaller accounting language and grammar
proposal. It is useful as a syntax precedent, but it provides insufficient semantics or evaluation
to govern AccDSL by itself.
Shahnazarian (2004) connects accounting dynamics with behavioural and econometric equa-
tions. Sections 2.3–2.8 describe the accounting relationships; the optimisation and econometric
discussion in Chapter 3 and the data and evaluation material in Chapters 4 and 8 show how the
model is used with standardised Swedish corporate data. This is an important precedent for
simulating companies under changed conditions. Its standardised tax-data starting point differs
from heterogeneous public-report extraction, and its working-capital funds formulation should
not be imported as a general modern cash-flow statement implementation. The transferable
idea is the explicit separation of accounting evolution and behavioural assumptions.

Pierson (2020), Sections 3–4 and the reporting structures, makes that separation operational

Two important neighbouring traditions remain explicit coverage qualifications. McCarthy’s

4.2
Incomplete information and provenance
Financial disclosures are incomplete databases in a substantive sense. Multiple underlying
records can yield the same published observations.
Libkin (2014), Sections 2–5, explains
the semantics of incomplete databases, labelled nulls, conditional tables and certain answers.
Repeated occurrences of the same unknown must share a value. Conditional tables can represent
dependence between alternatives that independent missing cells cannot express. This is directly
relevant to a debt note and a cash-flow table that refer to the same unknown repayment.
AccDSL adopts shared symbolic unknowns and conditions. It extends the database idea with
accounting constraints, continuous amounts, temporal transitions and economic event models.
A “certain” answer means a statement that holds for every history in the declared feasible
family. The family itself may omit unknown mechanisms; certainty is therefore always relative
to a model scope. Imieliński and Lipski’s original 1984 paper is a foundational backward lead
whose publisher copy was inaccessible in this campaign. The inspected Libkin paper supplies
the technical discussion actually used here.
Green et al. (2007), Definitions 3.1–3.2 and Section 4, treats provenance as annotations that
compose through relational operations. Combining sources and alternative derivations can
therefore have formal semantics, rather than being a list of unrelated citations. This is useful
for report aggregation and explanation queries.
Positive relational operations covered by
provenance semirings do not by themselves specify every operation AccDSL needs: negation,
nonlinear valuation, optimisation and probabilistic conditioning require their own derivation
records. Accordingly, the implementation starts with a typed dependency graph and may use
semiring annotations for supported relational subqueries. A dependency edge is not a posterior

Snodgrass and Ahn (1986) distinguishes historical validity from the time facts are stored. The
archived paper is Temporal Databases, by Snodgrass and Ahn, not the later Jensen–Snodgrass

4.3
Inverse problems, identification and probability
Stuart (2010), Section 2.2, starts from an observation equation y = G(u) and distinguishes
inversion, regularisation and Bayesian inference. The discussion makes explicit that a regular-
ising norm or prior is an additional assumption. AccDSL has a similar observation problem,
although its unknowns include discrete accounting interpretations and event structures as well
as continuous parameters. The infinite-dimensional well-posedness theorems in that paper are
not automatically applicable to this mixed, potentially discontinuous model.
The relevant issue is often identification rather than computation alone. Giacomini and Kitagawa
(2021), Sections 2.1–2.6 and Theorem 1 of the archived 2020 working version, shows how a
class of priors can preserve uncertainty within an identified set. A likelihood that is flat over
the unresolved part does not learn the conditional prior over that part. Posterior lower and
upper probabilities summarise a class of posterior beliefs. The paper’s regularity conditions
and application to structural vector autoregressions do not prove that an arbitrary accounting
program admits the same algorithms or guarantees. The design borrows the distinction between
learnable quantities and assumption-dependent distributions; the simple accounting bounds in
Chapter 6 are derived directly.
Hoeting et al. (1999), equations (1)–(3), gives Bayesian model averaging as a weighted sum
of model-conditional distributions, with weights determined by model priors and marginal
likelihoods. Sections 3 and 5 discuss computation and prior specification. This supports an
optional weighted mode, provided AccDSL specifies the likelihood, priors and candidate model
space. It does not justify giving equal mass to every syntactically different journal reconstruction.
Two encodings of the same economic history must not gain extra weight by duplication. Model
averaging also remains conditional on the considered model family, rather than repairing missing
mechanisms.
Scenario semantics need similar care.
Pearl (2009), Section 3.2.1 and equations (5)–(6),
distinguishes an intervention that replaces a structural equation from ordinary conditioning.
AccDSL will distinguish a supplied exogenous path, an observational conditional forecast and
an intervention. A rise in rates imposed by a stress test is not automatically distributed as the
collection of historical periods in which rates happened to be high. Identifying a causal effect
requires assumptions beyond reproducing accounts.

van Deursen et al. (2000) surveys DSL benefits, risks and implementation options. Mernik

as serious competitors and distinguishes deciding, analysing, designing and implementing a
DSL. Its implementation decision tree explicitly includes embedding in a host language. The
literature therefore supports a conditional design decision: a DSL is attractive when domain

Merigoux et al. (2021), Sections 3–4 and Figures 8–9, provides Catala’s scopes, exceptions,

Peyton Jones et al. (2000), Sections 3–5, builds complex financial contracts from combinators

Solvers and probabilistic execution
de Moura and Bjørner (2008), Sections 2–3, describes Z3’s interfaces and theory-solving

The first solver fragment is

Unsupported formulas remain in the representation with a solver diagnostic.
van de Meent et al. (2018) develops probabilistic programs through sampling, observations
and inference; the inspected material includes the first-order language and weighted sampling
discussion. Stan (Carpenter et al., 2017), its model language and sampler-output discussion, and

economic model. Exact accounting identities and discrete rule selection should not be turned
into nearly satisfied likelihood penalties merely to fit an inference library. Nor should successful
sampler execution be described as evidence that all interpretations were explored.
4.7
The remaining design problem
The combined literature provides algebra for journals, semantics for missing information,
temporal storage, provenance, contract languages, rule languages, inference methods and
extraction tools.
The unresolved integration problem is to retain their distinctions while
connecting them: a disclosure constrains economic histories; a history supplies contract and
behavioural state; a scenario drives events; accounting rules produce journals; reporting rules
render statements; and every step remains explainable.
The design is therefore an integration proposal with several local mathematical constructions.
Its originality and empirical advantage have not been established by this survey. Its practical
merit will depend on the acceptance cases, accounting review and comparative implementation
work in Chapter 14.

5.4
Responsibilities that AccDSL must implement
AccDSL owns the evidence and temporal model, typed accounting concepts, shared unknowns
and alternatives, rule-package selection, journal validation, consolidation provenance and report
explanations.
It also owns the distinction between feasible sets and distributions.
These
responsibilities cannot be delegated implicitly to whichever parser or solver is convenient.
External components receive narrow requests and return typed responses. A contract adapter
returns scheduled events, cash-flow quantities, state changes and source/model references; it does
not directly mutate the ledger. A valuation adapter returns a measure, currency, date, method
and numerical status; the accounting package decides whether and where to recognise the
change. A solver returns feasibility or bounds with scope and completeness status. A language
model proposes source-linked mappings or interpretations; it cannot commit an unsupported
assertion as fact.
The architecture consequently permits deterministic reference execution, alternative solvers and
later acceleration without changing the meaning of a historical observation or a journal entry.
Performance work begins only after the same semantic cases pass through these interfaces.

6.1
Define what is observed before inverting it
Let Dk be the disclosures available under information cutoff k. Each numeric observation di has
a context: entity or group perimeter, concept, measurement basis, dimensions, currency, period
and precision. These contexts are part of the observation operator, not additional columns that
can be discarded after extraction.
A candidate history h contains an opening economic and accounting state, events through

latent quantities. Let M denote the declared model scope: its event templates, admissible rule

to represent. The reporting operator Oi(h) produces the quantity to which observation i refers,
including its basis and perimeter.
Reported numbers are often rounded. If a number vi is rounded to the nearest unit qi, an
illustrative admissible interval is [vi −qi/2, vi + qi/2) under the corresponding tie convention.

the conservative closed enclosure when nearest rounding is assumed but the tie rule is unknown.

6.2
Why published statements cannot identify all transactions
The simplest derivation uses fixed account mappings and a fixed basis. Let z ∈Rn contain
unknown historical movement amounts. For m disclosed aggregates, let A ∈Rm×n be the
aggregation matrix and y = Az ∈Rm. If there is a nonzero vector v with Av = 0, then
A(z + v) = Az + Av = y.
Both movement vectors give the same reports whenever both satisfy the remaining constraints.
Thus the feasible family is the affine set z +ker A intersected with those constraints. Accounting
requirements can shrink this family, but do not generally eliminate it. For example, cash
collections in a receivables bridge are constrained by
ART = AR0 + credit sales −collections −write-offs + other classified movements.
If the note does not distinguish acquisitions, currency effects and write-offs, several collections
paths may fit the same closing balance.
There is a second ambiguity beyond aggregation. In Equation (1.1), identical historical move-
ments arise from different contractual reset terms. Even recovering z exactly would leave these
terms unidentified. AccDSL therefore infers economically relevant states and contract terms
alongside journal movements. A linear inverse solver over statement lines alone cannot solve
this second problem.

6.6
Resolution and computational limits
For a requested horizon H and scenario family S, histories may be grouped only when they
have the same conditional law for all requested outputs:
h1 ∼H,S h2
⇐⇒
Kh1,s = Kh2,s on those outputs for every s ∈S.
This definition explains why individual sales can sometimes be replaced by cohorts, while fixed
and floating loans usually cannot be merged for an interest-rate stress. Exact equivalence can
be difficult to prove. A grouping justified only by approximate numerical agreement carries
an error criterion and is not labelled exact. Source provenance remains available even when
execution shares a common state.
Bounds require comparable honesty. A linear program can provide certified extrema for a
supported linear model. A set of feasible sampled histories provides observed extrema: its
minimum is no smaller than the true infimum, and its maximum is no larger than the true
supremum. It therefore supplies an inner exploration of possibilities, not a certified outer bound.

the request specifies whether bounds apply before quantisation, after a monotone declared
rounding map, or to a bounded integer model. Approximate numerical valuations carry error
metadata outside the exact rational kernel. Solver timeout, unsupported theory and unbounded
objective have distinct responses; none is relabelled as infeasibility.

The reconstruction input is a model-scope record, not an invitation to invent unlimited journals.

arise by substituting its collection, purchase and payment templates into opening-plus-movement
identities.

amount. Instantiate the template’s posting and state constraints, unify variables referring to

6.9
Which details may safely be combined
A resolution record names the outputs, horizon, scenario family, grouping key, required state,
transformation and exact or approximate status. Safe exact transformations are deliberately
few. Cash balances in the same book and currency can be added. Homogeneous inventory lots
may be combined under a weighted-average policy if ownership, cost basis, impairment state
and future costing treatment coincide. Debt principal can be added only within a group sharing
currency, coupon/reset function, fixing and payment calendars, maturity, options, guarantees
and measurement method. The transition and reporting maps must be additive on that group.
The last condition supplies a short proof obligation. If a transition obeys T(x1 + x2, u) =
T(x1, u) + T(x2, u) and reporting is additive for every admitted driver u, induction preserves
equality after grouping. This does not apply to covenant thresholds, counterparty netting,
nonlinear guarantees or lot-specific impairment. Two fixed loans with the same dates and rate
can share an execution state under this test. The fixed/floating pair cannot; neither can two
policies with different guarantees merely because their current reserves match.
A changed horizon, scenario, rule version or requested output invalidates the resolution approval
and reruns the sufficiency check. Extending beyond a debt maturity requires schedule detail
that an earlier short-horizon view could omit. Approximate cohorts retain the detailed source
references and a stated error criterion tied to outputs. Unless that error is bounded or evaluated,
the grouping remains an approximation awaiting evidence, not an exact quotient of the history
family.

different, success-conditioned question.
An observation has a semantic information identity as well as a file locator. Reimporting the
same observation through another file does not create a second measurement. Repeating an
identical admitted condition is idempotent: it keeps the law and information lineage unchanged.
Submitting an already consumed observation as a fresh likelihood block is rejected as duplicate
information. Equal selected member sets alone do not prove that two conditions have the same
meaning. An admitted conditional kernel may divide a region’s existing mass when it is refined;
the number of new representatives never determines that division.
Continuous inference adds a measure choice. One must specify coordinates z, domain, base
measure, prior, observation mechanism and any transformation Jacobian. Stuart (2010, Section
2.2, equations (2.5)–(2.8)) supplies the inverse-problem formulation, but does not establish
identification or easy sampling for a mixed accounting model. In particular, an exact accounting
equality can confine a model to a lower-dimensional set. Multiplying an ordinary Lebesgue
density by its zero-volume indicator is not a posterior construction. One must parameterise
the constrained model with its declared measure or supply an actual measurement-error model.
Adding convenient small noise changes the target.

6.14
A stress is not an observed condition or an intervention
The interest-rate stress in Northstar fixes a future driver path. It supplies no new evidence
about the historical debt share. An observational forecast instead conditions a joint law on
information. A causal intervention needs structural equations and an exogenous disturbance law,
because it replaces the mechanism determining a chosen variable while leaving other mechanisms
in place (Pearl, 2009, Section 3.2, equations (5)–(9) and (15)–(17)).
An elementary example makes the difference numerical. Let U equal zero or one with probability
1/2 each, and assume
X = U,
Y = 2X + U.
Observing X = 1 establishes U = 1 and hence Y = 3. Intervening to set X = 1 replaces the
first equation. It leaves the original law of U, so Y is 2 or 3 with equal mass and mean 2.5.
Selection of a high-investment company and an imposed investment policy can therefore answer
different questions even under one fully specified model. Financial filings alone do not establish
these causal equations or identify their effects.
An intervention request must name its target, replacement equation, structural parents and
disturbance law and satisfy acyclicity or a separately supported equilibrium rule. Correlated
disturbances remain joint. The initial company profile supports imposed exogenous stress; the
continuous and causal methods described here require separately admitted extension schemas
and signatures. They do not silently expand the initial finite-law interface.

Scope of the first language
The historical AccDSL Core 0 proposal describes entities and books, accounts, observations,
shared unknowns, constraints, alternative interpretations, contract instances, accounting rules
and reporting mappings. It has pure expressions and a restricted posting effect. Economic
forecasting algorithms can live in typed plugins; unrestricted numerical code does not need
to be reimplemented in the DSL. The historical grammar in Appendix B fixes the preserved
teaching examples. The proposed portable records and first company profile below define the
new authoring boundary; the first matched text frontend supports the declared debt fragment.
The historical Core 0 compiler has six ordered stages: parsing with source locations; name
and module resolution; type and temporal-context checking; lowering to the IR; constraint
and rule-applicability analysis; and execution-plan construction. The compiler never silently
supplies an economic assumption to make a program compile. A program can be valid as a
historical representation while requiring additional assumptions before a particular simulation
can run. The proposed portable compilation sequence is specified separately in Section 7.3.
The language’s meaning is defined by its IR and evaluator, rather than by the surface spelling
alone. Formatting changes cannot change a model’s semantic hash. A changed source span or
reviewer decision changes evidence provenance even if the numerical model remains the same.
The system therefore maintains separate evidence, semantic-model and execution identifiers.
For the preserved Core 0 examples, the complete grammar is supplied as spec/core-0.lark; the
original design directory retains spec/core-ir.schema.json and spec/core-contract.md. They
define reference resolution, expression arity, type/context checks, operations, failures and
identity. Structural schema validation alone does not establish those semantic properties.
ASCII identifiers and UTF-8 strings are used in this revision; currency suffixes, dates and
decimal literals have explicit lexical rules. The grammar is checked by parsing the complete
demonstration file, not by claiming that its parser also implements a compiler or solver.

7.3
A portable record has an exact meaning
A constructor and a textual frontend can describe the same financial model only if they
exchange values whose identity is independent of a Python object or an incidental JSON writer.
The proposed portable record therefore has six fields: kind, schema, key, revision, body and
dependencies. Kind and schema identify a registered version; key is its stable, schema-scoped
logical name; revision is a nonnegative integer. The body obeys that schema’s required, optional
and nullable fields. Unknown fields reject.
A typed reference has exactly kind, schema and digest. The digest covers the complete target
record. Every body reference is also listed in dependencies; additional dependencies need a
schema-declared role. The submitted closure is finite and acyclic. A receipt can refer to its
request and old state, but the old state cannot refer forward to that receipt. Stable business
names can relate counterparties without creating a cycle of content references.
Financial quantities use a reduced rational with signed numerator text n and positive denomina-
tor text d, with gcd(|n|, d) = 1 and zero represented by 0/1. The decimal token 2.50 therefore
denotes 5/2 exactly. Revisions, ordinals and integer counts remain integers; a Boolean is never
an integer. Approximate measurements instead retain decimal text, method, error description
and environment. A rounded decimal cannot silently acquire exact status.
An absent required field is invalid. Unknown financial content is an explicit value with a reason
and outstanding evidence references. It can be retained, but cannot satisfy a method that
requires the missing fact. Null is permitted only where the particular schema gives it a meaning;
not applicable, unknown and zero are distinct. A candidate also differs from a value admitted
in a specific immutable view. Admission checks the actual decision and its ancestors; wrapping
a candidate in an “admitted” type is insufficient.
Portable bytes use UTF-8 JSON with ASCII object keys sorted by their byte order, no
insignificant whitespace, no floats, duplicate keys, nonfinite numbers or invalid Unicode scalar
values. Decoded strings retain their code points. An escaped Unicode character and the same
literal character encode equally; composed and decomposed spellings remain different unless an
explicit named transformation relates them. Global NFC normalisation would silently change
some source text and is therefore not part of this encoding.
The writer emits literal non-ASCII scalars, escapes quotation marks and reverse solidus, and
escapes control characters U+0000–U+001F. It uses the five named JSON escapes where defined
and lowercase \u00xx for the remaining controls; solidus is not escaped. Integers use minimal
base-ten spelling without plus signs, leading zeroes or negative zero. Dependencies, module
declarations by key, dimensions by axis and explicit all-of/any-of operand sets are sorted after
rejecting duplicates. Other arrays, including journal lines, event schedules and diagnostic
priority, retain order.

7.4
The first company profile and its authoring boundary
The proposed CorporateJointHistory/1 profile connects one legal entity and book per branch to
annual/interim sources, revisions, working capital, payroll, tangible assets, simple financing, an
explicitly declared tax model and complete statements. Its types preserve entity, perimeter and
currency even where an operation is outside this initial scope. A real company cannot become
eligible for the profile by dropping its subsidiaries, leases or contingencies.
Typed constructors must express every included declaration in the portable IR without opaque
Python functions. The initially matched text frontend covers the bounded debt and constraint
fragment. Whole-company textual authoring remains a subsequent frontend obligation; the
whole-company typed model is part of the initial domain. Thus domain scope and authoring
syntax are separate decisions. The following Core 0 listing teaches the shared debt family under
its preserved historical grammar. It is not a promise that the first new parser supports every
historical declaration. The matched portable debt example uses floating share x, while the
following listing uses fixed share α. The substitution x = 1 −α preserves the entire family,
including its endpoint results.
Backend admission examines expanded expressions. Multiplication of an unknown quantity by
an unknown price is bilinear even when hidden inside a function called revenue. The initial
exact reconstruction fragment admits rational affine constraints, finite choices and bounded
integers under explicit templates. Unsupported functions return a capability diagnostic. A
type-correct historical representation may therefore be retained even when a requested forecast
still requires an unavailable method or an additional admitted assumption.
The portable CompileRequest supplies source kind, source bytes or a typed module, schema and
profile references, the full package lock, evidence/time view and requested capability target. Its
stages are strict decoding and source locations; name/import resolution; dependency and cycle
checks; static type and context checks; admission-layer checks; expanded-operation capability
analysis; and canonical record production. The earlier six-stage workflow describes the historical
Core 0 frontend; it does not add execution planning to this portable compiler or permit it to
call a solver.
The result contains candidate IR, source map, unresolved inputs, capability analysis and a
validation receipt bound to every input. Invalid structure or types yield no executable IR.
Valid structure with an unresolved financial input can yield a retained candidate whose affected
target has execution admission false. Source maps retain decoded Unicode code-point half-open
ranges for text or constructor paths for typed input. Formatting can change source-map identity
without changing financial-model identity.

7.6
Posting rules and explicit exceptions
An event type declares the information needed by a rule. The example below assumes an event
representing interest accrued and paid on the same date. Separate accrual and settlement events
are needed when those dates differ.
event InterestPaid(principal: Money<USD>,
rate: Rate<annual_simple>,
fraction: YearFraction) book Local;
rule recogniseInterest for InterestPaid as e
when e.principal >= 0 USD {
let charge = roundMoney(simpleInterest(e.principal, e.rate, e.fraction),
currencyScale(USD), "half_even");
require charge >= 0 USD;
post {
debit InterestExpense charge;
credit Cash charge;
}
}
The rule cannot perform file I/O, mutate another entity or sample a random number. It returns
a proposed batch. The runtime verifies balance and all preconditions before committing state.
Negative interest requires an explicit supported rule; it is not mishandled by this intentionally
restricted example. Rounding is a declared demonstration convention, not a universal accounting
default.
Rules can declare overrides ruleName. The resulting override graph must be acyclic. For a
concrete event, the evaluator determines all applicable rules and removes any that are overridden
by another applicable rule. Exactly one maximal rule must remain for a required recognition
operation. An empty set of applicable rules produces UnsupportedTreatment; more than one
maximal rule produces RuleConflict. File order and a hidden numeric priority never break ties.
If a guard depends on a symbolic value, the solver partitions the current feasible family into
the guard and its negation, subject to the existing constraints. This is a logical partition, not a
random draw. An incomplete solver returns UnknownApplicability, not a guessed rule. Statically
provable disjointness or total coverage can be cached; otherwise the runtime performs the same
checks on every concrete event.
7.7
Alternative readings, assumptions and updates
An alternative adds constraints and possibly a supported semantic mapping to its parent model.
Its record contains a rationale, supporting spans, opposing evidence, author, creation time
and scope. Child alternatives inherit all parent constraints; they cannot quietly discard an
inconvenient disclosure. A deliberate source rejection is a separate review decision with an
explicit reason and a new evidence view.
Two independent alternatives may coexist, while two mutually exclusive readings of the same
text require an explicit choice group. The representation stores these relationships in a constraint
graph. It does not take a Cartesian product blindly. For example, interpreting an instrument
as a liability affects both its balance-sheet account and the treatment of its periodic payments;
those choices must be linked through one shared treatment variable.
New evidence narrows an existing family by conjunction when it concerns the same definitions
and reporting basis. If it changes the basis or corrects an earlier assertion, the system creates a

revised evidence view and records the relationship. Posterior reweighting, when enabled, is a
separate operation with likelihood provenance. A branch that becomes inconsistent is retained
as historically considered and rejected, with the evidence that rejected it.
7.8
Modules and stable intermediate representation
A package identifies its namespace, semantic version, dependencies, effective dates, jurisdiction,
supported entity classes, recognition operations and measurement policies. Standard packages
define allowable treatments; industry packages add economic objects and specialised rules;
company packages map source concepts and declare evidenced elections. A company extension
cannot override a mandatory standard rule merely because its package is loaded last. An
override is valid only at a declared extension point and within the selected standard’s policy
domain.
The IR has separate node classes for evidence, contextual observations, expressions, domains,
constraints, alternatives, contracts, economic events, measurement results, rules, journal batches,
report mappings and runs. Each node has a stable identifier and explicit input references. No
expression stores executable host-language text. Plugins are referenced by versioned identifiers
with typed signatures. This permits a second implementation to execute the same supported
program without evaluating arbitrary embedded code.
The IR serialisation version is independent of the DSL version and of any accounting-standard
edition. Migrations preserve the original payload and record changed meanings. A syntax
migration can be automatic when semantics are unchanged. A changed recognition policy
requires a new model version, even if the source file still parses.

8.1
Ingest, reconcile and construct histories
The historical procedure starts with a filing manifest. It records documents, reporting dates,
publication dates, revisions and expected statements and notes. Tagged filings are parsed
through an XBRL adapter; PDF and HTML sources are segmented into text, tables, captions
and references. The original bytes and extraction output remain immutable. Re-running an
improved extractor creates a new extraction version with links to the same source.
For each table, the importer first resolves headers, units, signs, periods and dimensions. It then
proposes concept mappings. A deterministic validator checks subtotal arithmetic, duplicate
facts, comparative consistency and cross-note references. Every proposed repair creates a new
reading. The system may suggest that an OCR value is 10 rather than 70, but only a reviewed
correction or an explicit alternative allows that reading into a validated model.
Reconciliation proceeds from local totals to movement schedules. A debt bridge may contain
opening debt, issuance, repayment, acquired debt, interest accretion, currency effects, fair-value
changes and closing debt. Every component receives a movement type. An unexplained residual
is stored as a residual with a source and period; it is not automatically classified as repayment
or issuance. A model can introduce an unknown decomposition of that residual, subject to
evidence and economic constraints.

scope:

3. Propagate types, units, intervals and simple equalities. Reject context mismatches before
invoking a numerical solver.

8.2
Condition a run without rewriting history
A run selects an evidence view and cutoff, a historical family, a forecast starting date, scenario
definitions, accounting packages, management policies, output queries and numerical settings.
A run may use a concrete history, an exact symbolic family, a finite set of conditional histories,
or weighted draws from a declared distribution. The output records which mode was used.
Suppose no debt maturity is disclosed. An analyst may run a conditional case that assumes all
debt matures after the forecast horizon. The run is valid under that assumption even though
the historical representation remains incomplete. The interface must display the assumption
next to the result and provide sensitivity cases when the assumption is material. It must not
label the assumed maturity as a discovered historical fact.
Management behaviour normally uses only information available at the decision date. A perfect-
foresight stress case is a separate, explicitly labelled policy. Scenarios should share the same
underlying innovations when estimating paired differences where appropriate, with stable stream
identifiers so branch order does not change the draws. Common random numbers improve
the comparability of paired simulations; they do not imply that every branch has the same
economic event counts or shock distribution.

treatment, proposed entries and the transaction that makes them durable.

book merely because the resulting arithmetic is identical.

8.6
Funding, tax and other simultaneous relations
Some financial models have circular dependencies. Interest affects taxable profit; tax and
investment affect funding needs; funding affects interest. The reference executor first tries to
resolve these through explicit timing. For example, beginning-of-period debt can determine
current interest, with new borrowing affecting the next accrual period. That is acceptable only
when the model’s timing assumption is stated and appropriate.
Where the economic model genuinely requires simultaneous decisions, the dependency graph
identifies the strongly connected component and delegates it to a declared equation or optimisa-
tion solver. The solver must return a solution, residuals, admissibility checks and uniqueness
or multiplicity status. A contraction iteration requires a justified convergence condition; an
arbitrary fixed iteration count is not a mathematical solution. Multiple admissible equilibria

8.11
Scenario meaning and information access
An ExogenousStress supplies a path or joint driver law while holding the historical evidence
model fixed. ObservationalConditioning additionally supplies actual observations and a joint
probability model; the finite method in Chapter 6 evaluates the conditional distribution when
its normalising mass is positive. StructuralIntervention requires a named structural model
and replaces designated equations, retaining its exogenous noise. An absent structural model
gives UnsupportedScenario, not an intervention inferred from an accounting identity (Pearl, 2009,
Section 3.2.1). The textual Core 0 scenario declaration is an exogenous stress only; the other
modes require the explicit typed API records.
At each decision date the runtime constructs an information view containing eligible disclosures,
committed state through that date, currently observed drivers and the declared forecasting
model. Future realised innovations and future outcomes are inaccessible. A stress path may
be announced in advance, but this is an explicit visibility policy. Otherwise management sees
only its current and past values. A bank’s allowance at the opening date may use a forecast
distribution of defaults; it must not inspect the outer simulation’s future realised default. Inner
valuation randomness has a separate stream domain from outer realised outcomes.
Funding follows a declared waterfall: available cash, committed eligible facilities up to their
limits, and separately authorised capital actions. Each action checks collateral, covenants and
settlement timing. If an amount remains due, record an economic funding failure and the unpaid
obligation. If required valuation or accounting is unsupported, stop the affected dependencies
as incomplete. The branch manifest preserves the last committed state, completed outputs,
unavailable outputs and reason. These distinctions let an analyst see a meaningful bad outcome
without confusing it with broken computation.

9.1
A common core with distinct accounting books
The common core governs evidence, time, money, events, journals and provenance. Reporting
standards govern recognition, measurement and presentation within that core. The design
preserves three levels of meaning: economic terms such as a promise to pay cash; an accounting
treatment under a particular standard; and a company’s reported label or extension. These
levels are connected by explicit rules and mappings, rather than collapsed into one universal
chart of accounts.
A rule package includes a standards register with jurisdiction, issuer class, effective dates,
transition provisions, permitted elections, paragraph references, implementation version and
tests. The package selector uses the entity, reporting basis, period and adopted elections. It
must distinguish a standard’s mandatory effective date from permitted early adoption. For
example, the IFRS Foundation’s IFRS 18 overview states an effective date of 1 January 2027
with earlier application permitted (IFRS Foundation, 2026c). A 2026 historical report cannot
be assigned that presentation regime solely because the latest installed package includes it.
IFRS, US GAAP and ASBE packages share economic contracts and events where appropriate,
but produce separate accounting books. A reconciliation between books consists of explained
adjustments with origins in differing treatments, not a renaming of accounts. The first implemen-
tation can preserve all three source bases even while only a selected subset has executable rules.
Such a source is represented as observed information with unsupported simulation treatments,
rather than being silently converted to the supported basis.
The official IFRS overviews for IFRS 9, IAS 32 and IFRS 17 establish the relevant scope of
financial-instrument measurement, presentation and insurance contracts (IFRS Foundation,
2026d,a,b). They are orientation sources, not substitutes for reviewed paragraph-level imple-
mentations. The retained official Ministry of Finance ASBE 21 page now supports selected lease
provisions. Its entity-specific issuance and amendment chain and other ASBE authority remain
incomplete. Current ASC access is also unresolved. Historical amendments and issuer policies
inform proposed comparisons without substituting for operative authority. These source limits
are addressed explicitly in the following dated-method discussion.

9.3
A matched inventory case across reporting profiles
Standards packages need operation-level decisions. For interchangeable goods, the inspected
AASB 102 text incorporating IAS 2 permits FIFO or weighted average in paragraph 25 and
explains both in paragraph 27 (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2015). The reproduced
ASBE 1 text specifies FIFO, weighted average or specific identification in Article 14; its official
Ministry version could not be retrieved in this review, so that reproduction has provisional source
status (Ministry of Finance of the People’s Republic of China, 2006a). Caterpillar’s company-
authored 2024 report states that it principally uses LIFO and supplies a FIFO comparison
(Caterpillar Inc., 2024, Note 1.C). That is evidence of a disclosed US-GAAP company policy;
full ASC 330 paragraph authority remains an acquisition task. It is not a rule that every US
company must use LIFO.
Take the same purchases in three separate books: ten units at 5, followed by ten at 7, then
delivery of twelve units. FIFO charges 10(5) + 2(7) = 64 to cost of sales and leaves inventory
56. LIFO charges 10(7) + 2(5) = 80 and leaves inventory 40. Periodic weighted average uses
cost 120/20 = 6, charges 72 and leaves 48. The physical deliveries and supplier cash flows
agree; profit and carrying inventory differ by the selected costing rule. A concept mapping
cannot remove that difference. The FIFO/average selector in an IAS 2 profile rejects LIFO
as an unsupported election under the inspected paragraph. The US company profile admits
its evidenced LIFO policy while retaining its narrower authority. An ASBE package remains
source-provisional until the attributed text and applicable amendments are officially verified.
Inventory impairment illustrates another required memory. Under inspected AASB 102 para-
graphs 28–34, an item costing 56 written down to net realisable value 50 records expense 6. If
the conditions reverse and net realisable value becomes 54, reversal is 4, capped by original cost;
the previous write-down must therefore remain in state. The reproduced ASBE 1 Article 19
also addresses reversal within the earlier provision. This paragraph is not used to assert the US
treatment, whose primary authority was unavailable. Source limitations constrain the treatment
claim, while the separate-book method and exact costing calculations remain fully specified.

9.4
Operating events
The operating methods use the selected revenue, inventory and asset provisions of Australian
Accounting Standards Board (2022c, 2015, 2022a). The lease and gross-tax case uses the
dated lease, income-tax and cash-flow compilations (Australian Accounting Standards Board,
2023c,b,a). Each rule still needs the entity, period, transaction facts and elections described
below.
Every event uses the event-basis, authorization and commit contracts described in Section 8.4.
The event inputs described here are required in addition to identity and date. Each object
identifier resolves to its contract, context and admitted source or assumption. A purported cash
collection does not establish a sale; a purchase order does not establish receipt.
Revenue, billing and collection are separate transitions. A delivery event names the contract,
performance obligation, quantity, allocated price and control evidence. Recognition first releases
a related contract liability; earned consideration not yet unconditional creates a contract
asset. Both credit revenue. A missing control finding or unresolved price constraint leaves the
recognition unresolved.
An invoice or right-to-payment event names the contract, bill and unconditional amount. It
debits the receivable and credits an existing contract asset, with any excess recorded as a
contract liability. A collection names that receivable, settlement and amount and debits cash
while crediting the receivable. An excess payment needs a separate customer-advance method; it
cannot be credited to revenue merely because cash arrived. These transitions retain cumulative
performance, billing and collection independently.
Purchasing starts when the company receives owned goods, not when it places an order. The
event records lot, quantity, unit cost and ownership evidence, debits inventory and credits the
payable. Consigned or unreceived goods cannot pass that ownership condition. Consumption
or sale identifies an available lot allocation, quantity and admitted cost formula; it debits cost
of goods sold or an eligible production asset and credits inventory. Overselling does not create
stock. Supplier payment separately debits the payable and credits actual cash. Insufficient cash
blocks that settlement and can trigger a separately modelled default; it never creates a hidden
loan.
A tangible-asset event records component cost, residual value, useful life, depreciation method
and the date it becomes available for use. Initial debit to PPE has a corresponding cash or
payable credit; depreciation later debits expense or an eligible asset and credits accumulated
depreciation. Employee service records earned pay, deductions and employer obligations for the
service period, separately from settlement. Unknown labour or tax law cannot become a zero
payable.
Closing derives revenue, expenses, OCI and owner movements from those events using admitted
statement mappings. It checks cash, inventory, financing and tax roll-forwards rather than
introducing a balancing entry. These event fields form the method’s required inputs; each
identifier resolves to the same contract, context and evidence used by its authorization.
AASB 15 paragraphs 9, 22, 31, 35–43, 47–59, 73–86 and 105–108 provide the selected revenue
conditions; the retained compilation dated 31 December 2022 applies to annual periods beginning
on or after 1 January 2023 and before 1 July 2026, covering the stated for-profit FY 2024
example. Variable consideration is estimated under a selected method and admitted only
subject to the significant-reversal constraint, not recognized solely because its unconstrained
expectation is positive. Principal versus agent, returns, warranties, contract modifications and

10.2
Equity and ownership
Equity has both monetary accounts and security quantities. The security register records share
class, issued and outstanding quantities, treasury holdings, par or stated value where applica-

Monetary equity accounts include contributed capital, reserves, retained earnings and other

11.1
Banks: accounting capital is not regulatory capital
A bank package needs instrument and cohort states for loans, securities, deposits, wholesale
funding, repos, derivatives, collateral and commitments. For interest-rate analysis, currency and
repricing buckets matter alongside remaining maturity. For credit analysis, exposure, default
state, loss severity, recoveries, guarantees and their dependencies matter. For liquidity analysis,
deposit behaviour, collateral eligibility, encumbrance and payment timing matter. These states
can be represented at an explicitly chosen aggregation level; the public record rarely identifies
every contract.
Loan origination can debit loans and credit a customer deposit in the bank’s book. Subsequent
transfer to another bank produces settlement flows. Treating all loan growth as an immediate
cash outflow would therefore encode a specific funding assumption, not a universal banking
identity. A system-wide model must also link counterparties and settlement assets; single-
company simulation records external counterparties without claiming to close the whole economy.
An allowance calculation is a dated measurement based on forecast information. For a single
horizon, let EAD be a fixed exposure at default, PD the probability of default, and LGD the
mean loss fraction conditional on default. Ignoring discounting, the illustrative expected loss in
currency units is
L = EAD × PD × LGD.
With exposure 100, default probability 0.02 and loss given default 0.4, it produces 0.8. Changing
those inputs to 0.04 and 0.5 produces 2, so the allowance increase is 1.2 if there are no other
movements. This calculation is a demonstration model. A standard-specific expected-credit-loss
implementation must determine horizon, staging or other classification, discounting, forward-
looking information, write-offs and purchased-credit-impaired or other special cases. The simple
formula is not an IFRS 9 or US GAAP compliance implementation.

deductions, risk weights and transition provisions. Accounting equity feeds a reconciliation

11.4
Expected credit loss
For each admitted scenario j and date t, calculate contractual due flows minus the cash expected
to be received, including supported collateral/enforcement cash and timing; discount the shortfall
under the applicable original EIR or specified exception. With mutually exclusive exhaustive
scenario weights w_j, ECL is the sum of w_j times those present-value shortfalls. Default-time
hazards must be converted to disjoint default-time probabilities; summing cumulative PDs
over dates counts the same default repeatedly. Collateral recoveries cannot be credited both
in LGD and as a second recovery cash flow. A shorthand PD×LGD×EAD is admissible only
if its definitions and discounting reproduce the specified cash-shortfall calculation; it is not a
universal replacement.
Expected credit loss (ECL) measures the discounted expected cash shortfall. A significant
increase in credit risk (SICR) changes the general method’s default horizon. Purchased or
originated credit-impaired (POCI) assets follow a separate method. For the general approach,
twelve-month ECL considers lifetime shortfalls arising from possible defaults during the next
twelve months; it is not just unpaid cash in those twelve months. SICR uses the change
in lifetime default risk since initial recognition, reasonable forward-looking information and
the applicable rebuttable presumptions. Do not install a two-times-PD or 30-day rule as an
unconditional universal trigger. Lifetime ECL follows a supported SICR finding; cure needs
a supported reversal of that finding. Subsequently credit-impaired assets use original EIR on
amortised cost for interest in subsequent periods; POCI has a separate credit-adjusted-rate and
cumulative-change method.
The general measurement interface requires scenario_cashflows, law_ref, information_view,
horizon_admission, credit_assessment and discount_rule. An unweighted history set can
produce conditional losses or loss bounds, but cannot produce an unbiased probability-weighted
ECL by silently using equal weights. Return measurement_unresolved for the ECL claim.
Trade receivables and certain other assets use the separately applicable simplified approach; the
general loan staging rule must not overwrite AASB 9.5.5.15–16.

12.1
Insurance: cohorts and future service

The general method distinguishes fulfilment cash flows (FCF), including an explicit risk ad-

General measurement follows 32–38: fulfilment cash flows are probability-weighted future net

13.7
Plugin interfaces
Every plugin declares a version, supported types, input information dates, determinism, nu-

and payoff; it may return future conditional events. A behaviour plugin implements decide
and declares which state and scenario information it uses. A measurement plugin implements

The plugin response must include all inputs that materially affect results. An external calculator’s
global evaluation date, market-curve cache, calendar version or random seed cannot remain

state. ACTUS risk-factor observations receive explicit dates and identifiers.
The constraint interface accepts a typed formula and a requested operation: feasibility, witness,
enumeration of declared finite choices, or bound. It returns sat, unsat or unknown, plus evidence
appropriate to the operation. Timeouts and unsupported theories return unknown. The history
layer exposes that solver outcome as SolverIncomplete, retains the affected region and marks

infeasibility. A witness proves existence within the formula, not completeness of a recovered

Human review and model-assisted extraction
The review interface should put the source passage beside the proposed observation, context and

a proposal until its evidentiary status is established. Token probabilities and self-reported
confidence are never automatically used as weights over economic histories.

outside this initial design. They can be added for a concrete operational requirement without

13.9
Failure recovery and reproducibility
Extraction jobs and simulation jobs write explicit progress records and atomic results. Restarting
a failed document conversion reuses its document hash and creates either the same idempotent
extraction result or a new version under changed settings. Restarting a simulation resumes
only from a committed state whose model and input hashes match the run. Numerical failures
retain the inputs needed for a focused diagnostic.
A run manifest records code revision when available, package lock, source hashes, cutoff rule,
model hash, scenario data, priors or weights, seed/stream scheme, solver settings, decimal and
rounding conventions, calendars, environment and result locations. Absence of a usable Git
revision is recorded as unavailable with content hashes, rather than inventing a commit.
13.10
Review decisions have consequences for later results
A proposed reading starts in Proposed. Acceptance records a reviewer, date, reason, verified
locator and context, together with the reconciliation result. Acceptance means that the stated
layer has been reviewed: accepting an extraction of a company’s sentence does not accept its
accounting treatment. Competing defensible readings enter AcceptedAlternative under a choice
group. Rejection preserves the source and reason. A correction creates a new reading; replacing
an accepted reading requires an explicit Supersede action. A prior reading therefore remains
reproducible without entering the latest model.
Suppose OCR reads Northstar’s original 120 as 720. The source span supports 120; correcting
extraction creates a new reading linked to the erroneous one. The later revenue correction from
120 to 110 is different: it changes the company’s reported assertion. The original extraction
remains faithful to its document. Finally, a reviewer may accept that two notes conflict without
accepting either as a unique model constraint. The view then contains a conflict set requiring
alternatives or further evidence.
A changed reading or mapping invalidates descendant constraints, families, measurements,
reports and cached runs for a new view. Unaffected dependencies can be reused. Old frozen

13.11
Identity must survive harmless reordering
The portable serialisation in Section 7.3 preserves decoded Unicode code points without global
NFC normalisation. It uses sorted ASCII keys and exact rational financial quantities, while
integer metadata remains integer. Only schema-declared sets are sorted. Source identity hashes
original bytes. The old Core 0 encoding survives only for its historical fixtures through an
explicit legacy import; it does not define new portable digests. Neither encoding proves arbitrary
economic equivalence.
SHA-256 with a domain/version prefix identifies three different closures. Evidence identity
includes source bytes, locations, extraction and review versions. Model identity includes typed
IR, assumptions, rules, calculator locks and resolution, while excluding formatting and source
byte offsets. Run identity adds the evidence view, horizon, scenarios, outputs, calendars,
numerical policy, random-stream scheme and environment. A corrected source can change
evidence and run identity without changing the numerical model; a new rate assumption changes
the model as well.
Each economic business event has a stable identity within its entity, book and branch. Its lookup
key is the four-tuple in Section 8.5; event version, payload, expected head and request settings
belong to the full fingerprint. Exact replay returns the original receipt before a new-event
freshness check. Changed inputs under that key conflict. A new run label or version cannot
make the event post again. Independent events may reorder only when read/write sets are
disjoint or a package proves commutation, including funding constraints. Alphabetical ordering
cannot settle an economic timing ambiguity.
Stochastic keys contain seed, coupling group, entity, contract/cohort, economic date, shock kind
and draw index. Branch enumeration order is absent. Shared coupling groups may use the same
uniform draw with different marginal transforms. The reference fixture maps the first 64 bits of
a SHA-256 key digest, interpreted as unsigned big-endian integer v, to (v + 1/2)/264. This fixes
example replay; it does not validate a production Monte Carlo generator. Research runs select
an assessed counter-based generator and record transforms and numerical environment. Inner
measurement draws have different shock domains from outer realised defaults.
Schema migration preserves old and new payloads and a field/meaning mapping.
A
representation-only migration must demonstrate equivalent supported semantics; a changed
recognition rule creates a new model. Restart resumes only a committed state whose locks
and identities match. SQLite owns source, review, accounting and run metadata transactions;
content-addressed files hold immutable bytes and JSON, while DuckDB reads versioned
analytical exports. Each mutable transition has one owner.

13.13
A successful extraction can still lose the fact
Consider an Inline XBRL amount whose literal is 1,234, whose admitted numeric transform
treats the comma as grouping, whose scale is −3, and whose context and unit identify a particular
dollar observation. The resulting amount is 1234 × 10−3 = 1.234. Scaling that normalized value
again gives 0.001234 and is wrong. An adapter must retain the literal, transform, scale, context,
unit, nil status and original occurrence so that this error can be found.
The same principle applies to dates and dimensions. A lexical instant of 31 December remains
distinct from the exclusive 1 January boundary used for an interval. Publication and ingestion
are separate timestamps. Two equal numbers with different contexts remain separate occurrences.
A nil fact is unknown, not zero. Dropping a typed dimension or context reference yields a
preservation failure for that fact even if the resulting Markdown looks clear.
The inspected Arelle interfaces support native XBRL contexts, dimensions, transformations and
validation. Its internal object IDs do not replace stable source identity. The inspected Docling
source offers useful layout and table proposals, but its examined XBRL graph path does not
retain every required fact/context/unit/typed-dimension field. That path needs a preservation
sidecar or must report unsupported conversion for affected records. These are source audit
findings, not measured accuracy rankings of the tools.
The retained Apple annual filing makes the dependency problem concrete. Its issuer archive
includes the instance, the issuer’s schema and its calculation, definition, label and presentation
linkbases. Those files also refer to US GAAP and SEC Reporting Taxonomy definitions, SEC
schemas and XBRL definitions held elsewhere. The issuer archive alone therefore cannot
supply every definition needed to interpret its tagged facts. For this particular archive, a static
inspection now resolves the recorded imports, link references and typed-domain references to
retained files, checks target namespaces and fragment identifiers, and checks that instance facts
have declared concepts, contexts and units. This establishes a source dependency map. XML
Schema and XBRL validation, the issuer’s interpretation of each concept, and the completeness
of the wider disclosure package remain separate questions.

14.1
Three different questions require different evidence
Engineering correctness asks whether the implementation follows the specified types, transitions
and arithmetic. Accounting validity asks whether the selected rules faithfully implement the
applicable standards and disclosed policies.
Scientific validity asks whether the economic
assumptions and uncertainty representation support the intended forecasts or comparisons. A
balanced journal answers only part of the first question.
The implementation must therefore maintain separate validation results. Compilation and
deterministic examples cannot establish forecasting quality. Agreement with a public report
cannot identify an undisclosed contract term. A lower extraction error on a document benchmark
cannot establish that the engine preserved every materially different scenario response.

14.3
Extraction and reconstruction evaluation
A company corpus must include different standards, industries, languages, filing formats,
extensions, interim calendars and restatements. Company and time splits are required so
repeated report templates or revised versions do not leak into evaluation. Public benchmark
tables are useful development material, while acceptance uses complete disclosure packages that
include notes and their relationships.
Reviewers annotate source facts, contexts, mappings, required relationships, known ambiguities
and unsupported assertions. Relevant measures include correct source-linked values and contexts,
unresolved extraction volume, coverage of note obligations, reconciliation failures and invented
facts. Report each separately. A single overall score can hide a serious error in a small but
consequential financing note.
Historical reconstruction should test whether the known synthetic history remains feasible,
whether known incompatible histories are rejected, and whether economically distinct alterna-
tives are preserved. On real companies, public data generally cannot establish the true journal.
Review instead assesses evidential compatibility, unsupported commitments and conditional
economic consequences. Later disclosures may provide discriminating evidence for previously
ambiguous terms.
No universal numerical threshold for semantic coverage or extraction accuracy is fixed in
this document. Before a release evaluation, an accounting-reviewed test corpus and task-
specific acceptance thresholds must be recorded in an evidence contract. Hard conformance
failures, fabricated citations, silent discarding of valid alternatives and undocumented accounting
adjustments veto acceptance regardless of an aggregate score.

14.5
Forecasting evaluation comes later
The proposed experiment asks whether a conditional company method adds out-of-time predic-
tive information while preserving statement coherence and unresolved exposure. The comparator
ladder uses the same available sources and targets: last-observation and seasonal naive forecasts;
contractual runoff and accounting-identity forecasts; tuned classical statistical and conventional
financial models; the structural model with one selected interpretation; the same structural
model preserving alternatives; and declared extensions. This separates changes in economics
from changes in the representation of ambiguity. Tuning stays within training windows. Test
issuers or time blocks remain untouched, and unavailable predictions stay in the reported
denominator.
For probabilities, predeclare proper scores, calibration, interval coverage, sharpness and
dependence-sensitive joint checks. Unweighted feasible sets have coverage and width under a
stated set interpretation, not probability intervals. Promotion needs a declared score difference
with uncertainty evidence on matched eligible targets and no accounting or information-time
veto. Issuer and time dependence require a justified paired resampling or equivalent inference
design; overlapping reports are not independent replications. Runtime, a few losses and small-
seed tail differences remain descriptive without that support. No such forecast experiment has
been run for this proposal.
Look-ahead, broken conservation, a changed target or missing scope can invalidate an experiment.
A valid comparison in which the proposed predictor performs poorly instead rejects that
candidate and motivates its stated repair. The two outcomes must not be conflated when
choosing the next experiment.
Forecast evaluation uses historical information cutoffs, fixed data vintages, declared economic
scenarios and proper separation of training and testing periods. Every level of the comparator
ladder above uses that same information boundary, so extra information cannot silently account
for an apparent benefit.
Construct the inexpensive comparators before choosing a complex forecasting method. A
no-growth operating forecast with scheduled contractual financing tests whether the model
adds value beyond existing obligations. A constant margin and turnover model tests whether
detailed operations improve on familiar financial ratios. A trailing-average growth model tests
whether fitted dynamics improve on recent experience. A fixed-exposure contractual stress
model tests whether estimated management responses help beyond repricing the existing book.
Each comparator must generate coherent accounts through the same reporting rules and use
the same historical information. In synthetic cases, the known hidden state also supplies an
oracle conditional on the stipulated scenario.
Evaluate these comparisons separately under stable conditions, large rate resets, refinancing
pressure, credit deterioration and changes in reporting perimeter. A pooled average can conceal
failure in the situation the product is intended to explain. Supported underperformance against

14.10
Workload limits and release evidence
The first integration workload is one local company package, up to 10,000 observations, 100
declared aggregate event slots per period, 64 materialised finite-choice cells and a 30-second
budget per solver operation. These are provisional engineering limits for bounded failure
behaviour, not measured capacity or scientific defaults. They must be configurable and recorded.
Reaching a limit yields partial coverage with the unvisited formula/cells, never an exhaustive-
history claim. Peak memory and latency are measured only after an implementation exists; a
slower but semantically correct result does not become a scientific failure.
Engineering release needs compiler/API equivalence, transactional replay and adapter subset
checks. Accounting release additionally needs paragraph-level authority, dated applicability,
exact fixtures and independent domain review for every promoted operation. Forecast promotion
additionally needs preserved information sets, the stated baseline ladder, statistical uncertainty
and critical-regime vetoes. Human comprehension review remains separate from all three.
This version supplies a richer design candidate and reference checks while preserving these
outstanding acceptance decisions.

B.3
Typing and partial-information rules
For expressions x and y in the same currency C, the money addition rule is
x : Money⟨C⟩
y : Money⟨C⟩
x + y : Money⟨C⟩
.
Context compatibility is a separate premise for observations. There is no analogous implicit
rule for different currencies. Explicit conversion uses a dated measurement and records its rate
and purpose. A rate-times-principal calculation is typed through a declared accrual convention;
the example’s annual simple rate and year fraction produce money in the principal’s currency.

Known expressions evaluate normally. Shared symbols evaluate to symbolic expressions subject
to a joint constraint store. Missing source values cannot enter arithmetic until the model
supplies a symbol or a conditional assumption. Boolean guards over symbols produce formulas,
not the value false. This avoids the common error in which “unknown whether impaired” silently
means “not impaired.” Division requires a proven nonzero denominator or a partitioned domain
that excludes zero; otherwise the expression remains unsupported for concrete execution.
Logical queries first require a nonempty feasible family. An empty family returns Infeasible; it
must not produce vacuous answers to substantive questions. For a nonempty family, constraint
evaluation has three results. entailed means every state in the declared feasible family satisfies
the proposition. refuted means none does. undetermined means both remain feasible or the
solver has not decided; the response distinguishes those causes. Probabilistic truth is a separate
query requiring a measure. A language-level Boolean value must never be inferred from an
arbitrary probability threshold.
B.4
Operational rule selection
Let V (e, h) be the applicable rules for event e in concrete history h, after package/date selection
and guard evaluation. Let r ≺r′ mean that r′ validly overrides r through the declared acyclic
graph. Define
Max(V ) = {r ∈V : ∄r′ ∈V with r ≺r′}.
For one required recognition operation, a singleton selects that rule; the empty set yields
UnsupportedTreatment; a larger set yields RuleConflict. Multiple distinct operations, such as
measurement and recognition, are evaluated through separate declared interfaces. A rule that
intentionally produces no postings must belong to a supported non-recognition event operation,
whose economic state effect is still recorded.
Given a selected rule, the evaluator computes all local bindings and preconditions, emits
proposed postings, verifies Equation (6.2), validates account/context restrictions and commits
the event transaction. Failure returns the original state plus a diagnostic. This defines the
atomicity requirement independently of the storage technology.
