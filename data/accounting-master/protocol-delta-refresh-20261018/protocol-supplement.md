## B1: one certified original rate and all dependent amounts

**Target and comparator (3.38; E2 and deterministic E5).** Compute the unique
original rate r* > -1 discounting the original expected receipts to acquisition
carrying, including integral costs. Under supplied ACT/365F annual compounding,
initial future receipts are nonnegative and at least one is positive. Compare
the certified root and each supported gross/allowance/net/interest/catch-up/
impairment/cash/write-off recurrence with the independently derived scalar
target at that same r*. Retain the predecessor exact-rate examples as a
regression comparator. A rounded point rate or a newly fitted revised rate is
a different target and cannot serve as the reference.

**Information vintage.** Bind the initial expected schedule and acquisition
amount separately from contractual receipts, dated later expectations, cash
and no-recovery findings. Later estimates change the admitted recurrence, not
the original rate. Record integral fees, calendar convention and original-rate
certificate. Future findings cannot enter an earlier information view.

**Primary criterion and validity roles.** Opposite certified residual signs and
monotonicity identify r*. Positive-factor rational arithmetic and the written
induction must enclose every dependent scalar amount; direct origin/PV targets
provide separate checks. Require the declared root and downstream monetary
error budgets for the actual query. A lost certificate, wrong recurrence,
wrong calendar, omitted fee or failed containment vetoes that result. An invalid
reference or missing required certificate is a dependent continuation veto;
insufficient precision or excessive width under otherwise valid premises
triggers numerical repair. Neither narrow widths nor overlapping intervals
alone establish correctness. Widths and runtime are explanatory until a
target-specific error budget assigns width a pass/fail role.

The protected analytic case uses carrying 100 = 98 + 2 and expected 50/60;
x = 1+r* = (5+sqrt(265))/20. Revised 40/48, a supported fifth write-off and
recovery 60 give terminal gross 48, allowance -12 and net 60; final cash 300
and profit zero. The 2^-40 root width and 1e-7 monetary cap belong to that
example, not a new default. Attempt 01's contractual terminal 72 left only
57.6 after write-off and was invalid; repaired terminal 75 leaves 60. Its
failure is preserved and does not change the expected-cash/rate target.

**Artifact and limit.** Save the original certificate, per-stage interval and
independent target/error report, inputs and refusals under the future E2/E5
run directory. Existing evidence is `rate-interval-propagation-20261017/`,
including the proof and attempt-02 manifest/checks (2,056 deterministic checks).
All intervals share one fixed r*: their marginal endpoints are neither jointly
selectable histories nor posting journals. No mixed-sign-root guarantee,
empirical credit validation, Monte Carlo requirement or preferred precision
default follows. Predicted receipts remain a separate E4 question.

entitlement (b+ta)P. Here P is current base profit, r is the participating-to-
ordinary base-rights ratio, a=r/(1+r), b=1/(1+r), and t is the supplied tax rate.
Also reproduce source
numerators 120/200/40, base balances 0→330→550→0 and signed residual adjustment
-150. Profit-versus-equity recognition of the actual benefit must change
reported profit as supplied while preserving the admitted EPS allocation.
Journal balance is not the comparator: this is a read-only basic-EPS operation.

**Vintage, roles and artifact.** Bind dated class rights, complete dividends,
opening base balance, nonnegative base profit, actual tax-benefit recognition,
denominator and law/source version at the information date. E1 independently
locates those facts; it does not infer them from an EPS match. E2 requires exact
entitlement, class conservation, base-balance and timing agreement. Wrong or
missing rights, retained/shared benefit, inconsistent facts or hindsight veto
application. A defective oracle stops the dependent comparison; a valid
implementation mismatch triggers repair. Check counts and step traces explain
coverage only. Preserve rights/tax admission, per-period allocation, direct
entitlement, refusal and source-vintage records. Existing contracts and
624-check evidence are in `participating-tax-allocation-20261017/`.

## Continuation and future execution

`data/accounting-validation/<protocol-id>/<frozen-run-id>/`, carrying the
original manifest, evidence and decision/inference-status requirements.

exact transitive locks, component terms and independent human evidence; static
dependency intersections and author reading do not establish compatibility or
comprehension. The proposal remains not_ready. Runtime, installations,
