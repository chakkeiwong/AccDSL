The two retained eleven-command programs preserve all complete native command requests

A.54.16
Admitting the opening that the selected program will use
A balanced, typed opening book can still violate the selected company’s operating conventions.
Its claim map may be empty while receivables are nonzero; its unit-cost leaf may have the
wrong dimensions; its account roles may overlap; or its statement mapping may omit an account.
The opening check must establish these additional conditions before events run. Yet it must
not silently promise that every future command is valid: an unknown future customer or an
unsupported event is still decided at its execution boundary.
