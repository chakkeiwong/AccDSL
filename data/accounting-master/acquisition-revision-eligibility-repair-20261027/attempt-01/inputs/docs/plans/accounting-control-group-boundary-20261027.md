# Bind conditional control histories to group-reporting scope

27 October 2026. MC05/17/19/20/24/25/28/33/34/35;
W05/06/08/11/12/16. This is the next financing unit after the checked
whole-interval reference and its manuscript integration. Dated acquisition
authority remains a separate dependency; no actual reporting admission follows.

Research intent: can a selected actor's complete control-history result be
carried into the group-reporting boundary without turning equal endpoints,
conditional assertions or a share percentage into accounting permission?
The mechanism under test is a narrow, immutable request-to-scope adapter over
ConditionalControlIntervalHistory/1 and the existing GroupPeriodReport/1.
Expected failure: a caller uses a stable-perimeter report across a loss/regain,
gain/loss, unknown interval, or 75%-ownership acquisition and receives a result
that silently omits acquisition, disposal or NCI requirements.

Baseline: inspect the actual history evaluator, GroupPeriodReport/1,
CompatibleBookComposition/1, world-edge semantics and their retained fixtures.
Do not write a replacement accounting engine. A supplied whole-interval control
assertion is a conditional premise. Ownership percentages are separate facts;
an explicit 100% premise does not establish control. Date-only book boundaries
cannot silently round exact instants. The adapter must preserve the selected
actor, basis, interval, source identities and unresolved pieces.

Primary engineering criterion: compare complete expected scope results for
stable control, stable no-control, gain, loss, gain/loss, loss/regain, missing
coverage, conflicts, actor revisions and exact interval boundaries. Demonstrate
the existing report's acceptance on its unchanged fixture and refusal of a
changed perimeter. The scope adapter never itself executes or admits a report.
Retain an independent expected-route table rather than deriving expectations
from the implementation. Preserve original book and history inputs bytewise.

Vetoes: stale source/result binding, fabricated actor knowledge, hidden unresolved
atoms, date truncation, admitted control from votes alone, incomplete history
represented as stable, or a false report/posting/admission flag. Such failures
trigger repair; corrupt source identities or a broken baseline invalidate the
affected comparison. They do not invalidate the wider research direction.
Counts and runtime are explanatory only. No statistical ranking, production
readiness, whole-company completeness or standards compliance is inferred.

Assumption/default audit: existing fixed-offset timestamps and source hashes are
inherited interface constraints, not authentication; closed bounded records are
a convenience scope, with explicit refusal outside it; a whole-interval 100%
ownership premise is separately labelled synthetic and tested against partial
ownership. No goodwill, fair values, acquisition date, tax or cost is invented.

Skeptical audit must be completed against the inspected signatures before
implementation. Cheap discriminator: show the valid unchanged group fixture
and its changed-control-edge refusal before any extension. Then write a pure
scope adapter and finite deterministic cases. Freeze dependencies, run on CPU
with GPU intentionally hidden, save command/environment/git identity, exact
results, refusal controls and source locks. Up to three bounded attempts, each
at most 120 seconds; preserve failures and never rerun successful writers.
