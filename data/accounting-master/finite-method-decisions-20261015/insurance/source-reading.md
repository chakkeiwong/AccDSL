Source findings that affect the calculation:

- B102 retains the inception VFA assessment when the floor binds later.
- B111 routes the full underlying obligation through finance; B112–113
  separately adjust the variable fee and guarantee, subject to the CSM limit.
  Under.87(c), an excess that becomes an onerous loss enters service expense.
- .85 excludes non-distinct investment components from service revenue and
  expense. The example supplies mutually terminating rights under B32(b),
  with no separately exercisable component. Different rights need reassessment.
- B119D uses the recognised loss times expected claims-recovery percentage.
  The loss includes the acquisition transfer in the defined case; this does
  not create a contractual recovery of the acquisition invoice.
- B125 adds equal acquisition-recovery revenue and service expense. Recreating
  an acquisition asset after its transfer would be wrong.
- B119C disallows the original-loss recovery for a treaty entered into later.
  B119F bounds the remaining component; it is inside the held asset.
