# Inspected source support for R03-E

15 October 2026. Historical documentary scope only. The following records
support mechanisms; none establishes actual company eligibility or a complete
current amendment census. Original sources and all predecessor dossiers remain.

## Restoration and production

Retained source: docs/design/research/p08-v3-preparation/eu-2023-1803-original.pdf
and its full text. IFRIC1 paragraphs1–8, printed OJ pages926–927, were read in
full. Paragraph5 requires cost adjustment, the asset floor and consideration of
impairment; paragraph6 requires asset-specific revaluation origin, the
hypothetical cost carrying amount, and any necessary whole-class revaluation.
Paragraph7 changes remaining-life depreciation and sends changes after useful
life to profit. Paragraph8 forbids capitalising the discount unwind.

IAS2 paragraphs12–18, OJ37–38, were read in full. Equivalent conversion units
are a local costing technique justified by the stated production process.
Normal capacity limits fixed overhead. Joint costs need a rational, consistent
allocation; relative sales values are one supported example, not a unique rule.
An immaterial byproduct may reduce main-product cost at its NRV. Paragraph18
separates a deferred purchase's financing component from inventory cost.
The existing historical impairment and PPE source dossiers retain IAS36 and
IAS16 conditions. New examples supply their valuation and remaining-life inputs;
they do not infer a recoverable amount from a balanced journal.

## Australian assurance and mixed modification

New retained official source: sources/aasb137-dec2022.pdf and text; compilation5,
31 December2022, prepared6 April2023, registered8 May2023 as F2023C00419.
The inspected cover and standard both state annual periods beginning on/after
1 January2023 and before1 July2026. The retained AASB webpage instead describes
periods ending before28 February2026. Preserve that discrepancy: the synthetic
2024 and2025 calendar periods satisfy both, and the examples claim nothing
about a boundary or 2026 application. A current operative-source decision needs
the relevant filing period and the later pronouncement, not this metadata alone.

Inspected AASB137 paragraphs14,24–26,36–47,48–62. The present obligation,
probable population outflow and reliable estimate tests precede measurement.
Paragraph39 weights a large population's possible outcomes; paragraph40's
single-obligation analysis must not be replaced by an automatic mean.
Paragraph41 keeps tax separate. Paragraphs43 and47 prevent double-counted risk;
material time value is discounted. Paragraph53 requires a separately recognised
reimbursement asset only when receipt on settlement is virtually certain, capped
at the provision. Paragraph54 permits net expense presentation, not balance-sheet
netting. Paragraphs59–62 require remeasurement and use only for the original
obligation. No assumption of independent product failures is needed for the mean.

Existing source: docs/design/proposal/methods/sources/aasb15-compiled-dec2022.pdf
and text, F2023C00402, with the same beginning-period interval. Paragraphs18–21
were read in full, supplementing the preserved scope, allocation, progress and
B28–B33 warranty reading. Paragraph21(c) directs consistent treatment of mixed
remaining promises; it does not prescribe an arbitrary allocation of a single
modification price. The new fixed-price case has separately supported equal
standalone-price allocations, construction integrated into the original asset,
and distinct inspection services. Its cost-to-cost construction progress is an
explicit faithful-measure finding, excluding inefficiencies or uninstalled
material that would make that measure misleading. Unknown allocation or progress
cannot produce the stated catch-up.

## Cash and tax limits

The existing historical cash-flow and tax methods remain authoritative within
their stated scopes. Restoration's payment amount does not itself determine the
classification of a decommissioning settlement. The example preserves its total
cash bridge and returns a classification obligation; it does not silently label
the whole payment investing or operating. Supplier finance has an explicit
ordinary trade-purchase finding, with interest paid elected operating in the
historical IAS7 policy. Finance-provider substitution requires a new analysis.
New examples remain pretax until an admitted jurisdiction, tax base, rate,
recognition and origin analysis is supplied; unknown tax is never a zero.

## Uncertain tax treatment

Retained EU IFRIC23 paragraphs1–14 and AppendixA1–A5, OJ980–982, were read
in full. Choose separate or grouped treatment by which better predicts resolution
(6–7); assume examination with full information (8). Probable acceptance permits
the filed/planned treatment (9–10). Otherwise choose most-likely amount or expected
value by predictive suitability (11), with consistent current/deferred judgments
(12). Facts/new information prompt reassessment under IAS8/IAS10 (13–14).
Silence alone does not establish a change (A3). Retain judgment, uncertainty and
contingency disclosures (A4–5). Probabilities and actual law are supplied
illustrations, not legal findings or an empirical tax-risk model.
