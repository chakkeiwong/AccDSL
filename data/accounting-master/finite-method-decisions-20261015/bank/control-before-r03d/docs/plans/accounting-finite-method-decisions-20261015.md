## Research intent and skeptical audit

Question: which promised operations still lack a reconstructible method, and
what is the smallest source-supported repair for each? Compare against the
original mission and method promises, not against an ever-growing catalog of
possible accounting examples. A supplied fair value is not a valuation method.
An actual issuer's absent private facts are not a missing generic procedure.
A historical source is not current multi-jurisdictional admission.

Primary criterion: each finite residual below has source/assumption,
eligibility, state/transition, report effects, failure behavior and a located
positive/adverse case, or an exact unsupported authority claim with a distinct
supported next lead. Required missing design stays a readiness veto. Optional
extensions must be justified against the original requirement, not relabeled
to obtain closure. Counts, successful builds and balanced journals are integrity
or explanatory checks. Wrong target/source/version or incomplete required
transition vetoes the affected method and triggers repair; it does not stop
independent ready work. No valuation accuracy or accounting compliance follows.

The audit rejects three tempting shortcuts: declaring every residual to be
private facts; declaring every named extension mandatory without inspecting
the promised profile; and using terminal payoff as a priced guarantee.
Proceed with exact source and scope decisions first. Existing completed
examples and guarded generation scripts are read-only evidence.

all source/code/version dependencies to any repaired method. A source block
does not erase required work or halt a different ready cluster.

## Assumptions and implementation sequence

Exact rational toy amounts are reviewed illustration choices, not calibration.
Their earliest diagnostic is an independently derived balance/transition and
an adverse case that can remain balanced but violate the method. Historical
standard text is a scoped source, with applicability checked before any example;
unavailable current authority remains explicitly unresolved. Default zero tax,
probability laws, supplied option prices and unverified legal rights are forbidden
unless their conditional purpose and downstream limits are explicit.

Write a bounded source/claim inventory in
data/accounting-master/finite-method-decisions-20261015/; locate and inspect
the next passages before a substantive method repair. Integrate only completed
repairs into a protected successor to2.95. Verify arithmetic, preserved sources,

complete while any required design residual remains without the stipulated
