The first draft plan's annual-only screen was too weak to answer input
feasibility and used a calendar cutoff that would have fallen inside Apple's
FY2025. The revised plan uses older annuals, current interims and immediately
preceding annuals under explicitly conditional FY2026 starts. It establishes
date-role feasibility only, with all byte-time and forecasting admissions held.
The date offsets are a conservative illustrative envelope and are not promoted
to a timezone policy. No inherited fitting/window or inference default is used.

The source-pointer checks compare the retained reconciliation records to their
referenced JSON values and selected original hashes. The 27-row selection
contains no official-filing-event records; the broader 192-row count of twelve
is retained separately. All counts describe these records and have no sampling
or representativeness interpretation. Unknown evidence is not a negative
finding about a company's actual publication history.

The strongest alternative explanation is a distinct earlier public release
that provides the same target or input. Such a source could overturn a claim
that no usable information existed, which this result does not make. It would
not make a later annual report's selected bytes available earlier. The weakest
part of the eventual forecast claim is still the unadmitted information and
financial scope, followed by the unselected time law and inference procedure.

The earlier unit's post-completion edits were a recordkeeping error. Its
four initial after-snapshots are reconstructed from the saved before-text and
literal replacement, and each matches the initial hash printed in the tool
history. The initial completion is explicitly labelled reconstructed; the
current completion is an observed copy. No predecessor bytes were altered by
this reconciliation. Updating recorded hashes and then replaying was not
evidence that the original completion had remained immutable.

No statistical ranking, default readiness, source availability, prospective
issuance or human acceptance is established. The defect affected recordkeeping
and does not negate the exact calendar or rational-weight examples. This
successor must preserve its snapshots and completion without later rewriting.
