# Retained source dates do not yet admit the full-year comparison

Of 27 input candidates for a hypothetical FY2026 forecast, twelve have a
retained filing-date or issuer-publication-date role. Seven of those date
events precede the illustrative cutoff and five follow it. Fifteen candidates
have neither role in this selection. None has an admitted exact-byte
availability time. These results identify specific preparation work; they do
not admit a historical forecast, training set or current fiscal calendar.

The illustration compares each company's 2024 annual report, last 2025 interim
report and 2025 annual report. Assume, solely for this screen, that FY2026 starts
immediately after its reported FY2025 end. The resulting cutoff dates are
27 September 2025 for Apple and 31 December 2025 for the other eight. The
cutoff's actual time and timezone are unselected. No actual forecast was made
at those dates. These choices demonstrate the preceding-annual-report delay
without presuming it is the same across firms.

these inspected records, not absence of a public report or an earlier release.

For unknown timezones, the date calculation deliberately allows UTC offsets
from -24 to +24 hours, enclosing a full civil day as [day-24h,next-day+24h].
This is a conservative illustrative envelope, not an observed timestamp.
Before/after is supported only when those envelopes establish the required
ordering; same-day or near-boundary dates remain unverified. All twelve actual
date comparisons are sufficiently separated for this broad screen.

The five after-cutoff annual publication/filing roles cannot support an input
claim using those particular releases. The seven before-cutoff events are
candidate source leads only. An earlier announcement may supply some amounts,
but that would be a separately identified source/version requiring its own
accounting scope, release and exact-byte evidence. Do not fill missing source
dates from URL strings, signatures, report labels or future annual reports.
