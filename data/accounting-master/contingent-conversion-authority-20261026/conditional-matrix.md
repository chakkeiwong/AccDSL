# Contingent conversion: terms and accounting alternatives

26 October 2026. This is internal preparation under MC17/19/24/25/28/30/33–35.
The economic example supplies a funding price and alternative cash/control
outcomes. It does not yet supply the facts needed to select an accounting
treatment. The matrix uses inspected AASB paragraphs as conditional support;
it does not select Australian reporting as the sole AccDSL basis. Physical

## Facts retained from the synthetic example

Issue is 1 January 2027; a signal on 1 January 2028 selects debt continuation
or conversion; terminal cash is dated 1 January 2029. Senior face is 40 and
convertible face is 80. Conversion extinguishes the 80 claim and issues 120
shares alongside 40 existing shares. The example assumes the resulting 75%
vote/cash share controls the later project decision. Its price is 17000/441;
the old owner's contribution is 9460/441. These are model quantities.

The contract does not yet identify who determines or can affect the signal,
the exact cash-settlement obligation in each contingency, conversion elections,
currency/fixed-for-fixed details, rights attached to issued shares, early
redemption/modification clauses, or relevant group agreements. Reporting basis,
entity class, reporting perimeter, policy elections, fair values, comparable
credit yield, fees and intervening measurement inputs are also unresolved.

## Edition and amendment boundaries

AASB2022-9 §18 removes specified Australian public-sector insurance paragraphs
in AASB9 and AASB132. It does not establish that every core classification
paragraph changed. AASB18 AppendixD lists AASB132 §§34,39–41,AG29 and associated
effective-date/footnote changes; §§22,25,28–32,AG30–32 are not in that amendment
list. This is evidence about that amending instrument, not proof that no other
amendment applies.

For AASB9, AppendixD's introductory amendment list and its inserted §7.1.11
differ: §7.1.11 includes §7.2.1. Preserve both source statements. The rendered
redline was inspected because extracted text merges insertions/deletions.
AASB18 C1 generally applies from annual periods starting 1 January2027;
AusC1.1 specifies 1 January2028 for named not-for-profit and superannuation
entities. Earlier application and transition must be represented explicitly.
