## Interaction cases required before runtime promotion

A rate shock can change floating coupons, trigger a covenant, alter a lender
price and induce selective disclosure; these cannot be validated by testing
the four components in isolation. A source revision can alter actor knowledge
and invalidate a previously merged state. A compressed rare type can have
small probability but dominate refinancing refusal or creditor recovery. A
conversion can change governance and future investment policy. A good pricing
residual can coexist with impossible ownership allocation. The next integration
fixtures must exercise these combined mechanisms with independently calculated
expected paths, not merely check that their individual modules return values.
