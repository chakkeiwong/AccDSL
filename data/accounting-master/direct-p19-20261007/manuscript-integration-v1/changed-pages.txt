Company Histories,

Product and method proposal
Version 2.1 (proposal candidate)
7 October 2026
This document specifies a system that translates company disclosures into traceable evidence,
represents several compatible economic and accounting histories, and simulates their future
journals and financial statements under explicit economic scenarios.
The proposed data contracts, methods and acceptance cases specify the intended implementation.
Current source and domain limitations remain explicit; this candidate is not a completed product
or a claim of full proposal readiness. Proposed libraries can be replaced while preserving those
contracts. Sector rule packages require their own accounting review before they can be described
as compliant with a reporting standard.
Research materials, source qualifications and review records accompany this document in
docs/design/research and docs/design/review.

9.11
Rule selection
The proposed selection procedure distinguishes operative authority from historical source
support. The retained sources include official AASB compilations, the original EU incorporating
regulation and official ASBE lease and income-tax texts (Australian Accounting Standards
Board, 2022e,c, 2023c,b; European Commission, 2023; Ministry of Finance of the People’s
Republic of China, 2019, 2018, 2006b, 2022). Historical US lease additions are separately
identified in the retained amendment source (Financial Accounting Standards Board, 2016).
AuthorityRequirement contains issuer/framework, jurisdiction, entity class, annual-period/ap-
plication interval, paragraph and local source Ref, incorporating instrument, amendment chain,
transition, elections and unresolved dependencies. RuleSelection binds those requirements to
the accounting event and evidence view. The algorithm evaluates chronological applicability
first, then scope and facts, then available elections/transition, then measurement/presentation.
If two supported rules can apply, retain the explicit alternative and required election; if one
is contradicted by facts, mark ineligible. Missing current authority gives unresolved, never
eligibility through a familiar rule name.
An unchanged historical report remains an assertion under its own basis and vintage. A
harmonized comparison creates an adjustment book and reconciliation, with changed recog-
nition/measurement explained. Label mapping alone cannot convert US GAAP to IFRS or
ASBE. The four clocks and source version remain part of each rule dependency; later knowledge
cannot leak into an original as-of view. Transition entries reconcile opening assets, liabilities,
tax and equity, rather than changing old source bytes or plugging an unexplained amount.
9.12
IFRS and incorporating jurisdictions
The retained official AASB texts provide explicit IAS/IFRS incorporation for their stated
entity/version scope. AASB 9/15 compilation dates are 31 December 2022 with the source
covers’ application window; AASB 16 compilation 8 is 31 December 2023, AASB 112 compilation
7 is 29 September 2023. Their filenames are not date evidence. AASB 102’s older retained edition
is selected historical support for inventory, not current 2024 applicability certification. The
retained EU 2023/1803 original provides official EU-adopted IFRS 3/10/IAS 19/21/36/37/IFRS
2/13 passages inspected in this method investigation; its historical adoption scope and later
amendments remain separate. The retained amendment investigation supplies additional source
evidence, not a blanket acceptance of all rules in these standards.
Initial real-method admission is therefore claim-specific: the selected revenue, lease/tax, financ-
ing, credit, group/FX and insurance cases must resolve each named applicability prerequisite.
For synthetic demonstrations, declare the chosen dated provisions and assumptions and use the
synthetic book mode. No assertion of whole-IFRS compliance follows from those entries.
The base lease in the saved dossiers (two annual 121 payments, 10% rate, no qualifying
exemptions or extra costs) has liability 210 initially, 110 after year one, 0 after year two. IFRS-
equivalent cost-model ROU is 210/105/0; expenses 126 then 116. Income tax needs its own
legal deductions and gross temporary differences, as the connected corporate case demonstrates.
Revenue timing, inventory cost formula, financial instrument classification, control and contract
boundaries are required rule predicates, not accounting options chosen after seeing which
outcome fits best.

9.13
US GAAP proposed methods and authority limitation
The saved official FASB ASU 2016-02 Section A and later 2020-05/2021-09 amendments contain
inspected historical ASC 842 additions. Their covers explicitly say an ASU is not authoritative.
Current Codification access returned 403 again in this source investigation; the proposed
methods cannot be marked current-authority-admitted. ASU amendments, historical FAS 109,
issuer accounting policy and a model’s memory are all insufficient substitutes for the operative
ASC text and full applicable amendment chain.
The same lease facts include ten-year remaining equipment life, fair value 1000, alternative
use to lessor, no ownership transfer, no purchase option, two-year term and PV 210. Under
the inspected historical 842-10-25-2/25-3 provisions, the stated facts support the proposed
operating classification. Historical 842-20-25-6 and 35-3 yield single lease expense 121 per year,
liability 210/110/0 and ROU 210/ 110/0. First year debit lease expense 121/credit liability
21/credit ROU 100; payment debits liability 121/credits cash 121. Next expense 121 decomposes
into liability interest 11 and ROU reduction 110. The finance comparison uses the separate
depreciation 105 plus interest 21/11 path. Changing classification facts is not a free election.
The 75%/90% heuristics in the historical guidance cannot become universal requirements. This
complete numerical contrast is useful despite the remaining current-authority limitation.
Proposed ASC 740 temporary-difference treatment retains tax base, reversal dates, enacted
rates, valuation allowance and source-specific allocation/presentation. The saved historical FAS
109 and ASU 2019-12 provide inspected enactment support, not a completed current ASC 740
implementation. Under the matched PPE case, book 800/tax 600 at assumed 25% yields DTL
50 and current tax 75 on taxable 300. A future 30% rate that is merely substantively enacted
but not legally enacted cannot be imported from the AASB 112 rule into this proposed US
treatment; the declared 2025 closing temporary difference is 300, giving DTL 75 at the enacted
25% rate versus DTL 90 at 30%. This deferred-tax comparison is separate from current tax 75
on taxable profit 300. Actual legal enactment remains a separate jurisdictional fact.
The proposed CECL method for in-scope amortised-cost assets estimates lifetime expected cash
shortfalls from origination using past/current/forecast evidence, with a documented reversion
method beyond a reasonable/supportable forecast horizon and applicable instrument exceptions.
It retains allowance roll-forwards, write-offs, recoveries, purchased-credit-deteriorated handling
and off-balance commitments separately. It must not copy IFRS 9’s 12-month/SICR staging
to the same record. A candidate contrast: lifetime discounted loss 6 with 2 attributable to
default events in the next year gives IFRS general Stage 1 candidate 2 versus CECL lifetime
candidate 6, conditional on scope and method support. No current ASC 326 text was retrieved,
and the attempted official amendment path returned 403; this contrast remains an explicitly
unadmitted proposed reading, not a source-verified US requirement or posting example. Full
current ASC 326, insurance 944, consolidation 810, FX 830 and relevant later amendments are
specific internal source tasks before corresponding real admission.
9.14
ASBE lease adoption and deferred tax
For an ASBE lease, selecting the lease standard and selecting the related income-tax treatment
require different application dates. Article 68 of the revised ASBE 21 states a general date of 1
January 2019, but the Ministry of Finance’s 2018 issuance notice specifies the enterprise groups.
Paragraph I requires application from that date for enterprises listed both domestically and
overseas, and for overseas-listed enterprises preparing financial statements under IFRS or ASBE.
Other enterprises applying ASBE must apply the revision from 1 January 2021 (Ministry of
Finance of the People’s Republic of China, 2018, issuance notice, para. I).

Paragraph II permits earlier application for enterprises whose parent or subsidiary is listed
overseas and prepares its overseas financial statements under IFRS or ASBE. Their early date
cannot precede simultaneous application of the specified 2017 financial-instruments and revenue
standards. Paragraph III discontinues the 2006 lease standard and its application guidance
when the enterprise applies the revision. Thus a 2020 reporting date alone does not establish
use of the revised lease standard for an enterprise in the second group: its qualifying early
adoption would need evidence. The retained official Chinese texts include the 2018 notice and
attachment and the Ministry’s later ASBE 21 page, published on 28 October 2019 (Ministry
of Finance of the People’s Republic of China, 2018, 2019). They replace the earlier secondary
reproduction as support for the inspected wording.
Articles 4–8 require control of identified use, economic benefits and direction; article 6 includes
both conditions for substantive substitution and the express presumption when difficult to
determine. Articles 14–18 specify commencement, ROU cost and PV, using implicit rate
or incremental borrowing rate if the former cannot be determined. Articles 20–23 separate
cost-model depreciation and periodic interest; articles 25–29 distinguish reassessment/index
changes/modifications and their discount-rate rules. Articles 30–33 define the exemptions; a
two-year term is not short-term. Articles 60–67 specify transition choices and disclosures; the
chosen transition cannot be inferred from a present closing liability (Ministry of Finance of the
People’s Republic of China, 2018, attached standard, arts. 4–8, 14–33, 60–67).
For the declared base lease, these inspected recognition/measurement provisions support
proposed ROU 210/105/0 and liability 210/110/0 with expense 126/116, under the same
stated facts, depreciation assumption and fixed rule version. A same-asset modification is tested
against 28–29, rather than labeled a separate lease without increased scope and commensurate
consideration. A 2019 first-adopter using article 61(2) records cumulative opening changes
without adjusting comparatives, while 61(1) is retrospective; comparison datasets must retain
that distinction and the entity’s actual election.
The tax question is why equal opening lease balances can still require separate deferred-tax assets
and liabilities. The Ministry’s original ASBE 18 defines tax bases and temporary differences in
articles 4–9 and contains initial-recognition exceptions in articles 11 and 13. Article 13 also
limits a deferred-tax asset to the probable taxable profit available to use the deduction. Articles
16–23 govern measurement, recovery or settlement assumptions, subsequent review, allocation
and noncurrent classification (Ministry of Finance of the People’s Republic of China, 2006b).
The original text must be read with later interpretations.
Interpretation 16 removes the initial-recognition exemption for a single transaction that is
not a business combination, initially affects neither accounting profit nor taxable income or
loss, and creates equal taxable and deductible temporary differences. Section I(a) expressly
includes qualifying initial lease liabilities and right-of-use assets; the corresponding deferred-tax
liabilities and assets are recognized separately under ASBE 18. Equal carrying amounts alone
do not establish equal temporary differences, and the asset’s recoverability condition still applies.
Section IV makes this treatment effective from 1 January 2023; Section I(b) permits early
application from the year of issuance, 2022, with disclosure (Ministry of Finance of the People’s
Republic of China, 2022, secs. I(a)–(b), IV). This tax date is distinct from the enterprise’s lease-
standard adoption date. The operative text now supports the treatment previously illustrated
by the retained official 2023 tax application case.
