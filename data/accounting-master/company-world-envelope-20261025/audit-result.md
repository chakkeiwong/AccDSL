# What the retained group and sector evidence establishes

25 October 2026. This correction supersedes the interpretation of the
preliminary `group-sector-composition-20261025` audit, without changing its
files. The old plan-hash self-comparison was tautological. Searching sector
result prose for "remain" did not validate sector semantics. The old source
lock covered only five files and cannot support a claim to lock the entire
inspection. The repaired audit records every dependency it actually reads.

CompanyComposition/1 attempt-03 remains a finite P/S example. Its immutable
packet and validation match the manifest; the validation records 681 passing
checks. Fourteen input snapshots and the corporate audit's eleven locked files
are checked against their previously recorded hashes. This is a fresh
preservation check of prior evidence, not a fresh execution of the 681 tests.
The semantic contract's opening and closed-exchange sections retain separate
legal books, shared component allocations, source/view identities and explicit
group adjustments. Its two migrations admit only the specified return and
matured-compound states. Neither accepts bank or insurance state.

The bank residual audit's `admitted_scope` and `nonclaims` describe a restricted
US book, supplied law and allowance premises, capital ordering and documentary
publication/replay. Its manifest points to 53 recorded checks. Its statement
that POCI remains open is dated: the later `bank-poci-rollforward-20261016`
attempt-02 result supplies a conditional fixed-terminal POCI lifecycle with
710 recorded checks. That later result does not establish arbitrary schedules,
calibrated credit forecasts, actual eligibility or cross-sector composition.
The transfer/fee result separates funding, capacity, allowances and supplied
derecognition findings. Cash receipt alone establishes none of those findings.

The insurance residual audit records a 2,705-check documentary result with
groupwise transition, recovery-context and reserving limits. The acquisition
incorporation example uses supplied nononerous/no-service conditions: CSM 10
and 15 replaces acquisition assets 35 and 15 once; premium receipt raises cash
40 to 200 and creates liabilities 65 and 45 without revenue. Its balanced but
wrong counterexample shows why preserving components matters. The VFA note
distinguishes its settlement-share example from full fulfilment cash flows,
CSM and eligibility. Neither result establishes actuarial calibration or a
general insurer adapter. These source/result claims were read here; their
technical source derivations were not rerun in this preservation audit.

The first repaired-audit run found an additional provenance defect: the
insurance residual manifest labels its validation as 2,705 checks but links to
the 92-check focused diagnostic. The original exchange run manifest resolves
this discrepancy: attempt-02 separately records 2,705 passing checks and binds
its packet and verification bytes. The repaired audit checks both records and
their different roles. The old residual manifest remains unchanged; its link
must not be used as evidence that the diagnostic itself ran 2,705 checks.

The module-boundary design warns that SectorCompany is report-only. Matching
a component profile to a book therefore grants no posting capability. The
new envelope retains that distinction explicitly. Its unresolved duties may
not be erased by an otherwise structurally valid join.
