# Target/overlap specification: author red-team record

18 October 2026. Read the actual proposed specification against the current E3
subsection and prior R05 admission/covariance passages. This is author review,
not independent scientific or human acceptance.

The main risk was treating three plausible questions as interchangeable. They
now have separate fixed units, estimands, randomness, generalization and missing
assumptions. The finite description can be exact about named observed cells
without pretending that a population interval is already justified. Repeated
origins need a joint time/data law; future issuers additionally need a frame and
selection/transport argument. Neither is inferred from report counts.

The observation contract distinguishes flow intervals from stock dates, fiscal
labels from horizons, first release from restatement and later comparative,
availability from acquisition, and target identity from forecast origin. It
also preserves derived cumulative-flow lineage, same-target repeated origins,
the full eligibility denominator and explicit common-coverage interpretation.
The synthetic A4 is openly partly elapsed; it is not another full future-year
replication. The examples use a fictional standalone presentation rather than
asserting that actual issuers report these particular quarterly/rolling amounts.

Review repair: an information cutoff and a forecast's actual creation/lock date
answer different questions. The specification now requires both, and expressly
distinguishes a later retrospective reconstruction from an untouched prospective
forecast. Availability comparisons use an explicit inclusive-cutoff convention;
imprecise dates do not acquire invented clock times or selected-byte identity.

Known overlap and shared mechanisms are recorded separately. Fifteen endpoint
intersection calculations agree with independently enumerated calendar-day
sets. The relation map demonstrates repeated targets, cross-issuer calendar
exposure and shared fitting with disjoint targets. It estimates no covariance
and does not certify independence, blocks or effective sample size.

The 72 checks include sixteen rejected altered fixtures. The scalar fixture
gate checks selected illustrative identities and ordering only; it is not a
complete implementation or conformance test of the proposed record contract.
Boolean support flags in fictional fixtures do not prove moment existence or
probability-law validity for actual models. Frozen file hashes establish
preservation, not correctness of all source claims.

No hidden selection was found for population, weights, margin, interval level,
sample size or procedure. The exact weighting sign reversal is a local
arithmetic result; neither weighting nor candidate is promoted. Missing losses
remain unknown even though the paired surviving mean is calculable. All source
vintage/exposure limits remain. The plan and initial new fixtures were authored
before the one-time protection snapshot; exact calculation ran only afterward.

Strongest alternative explanation: a precise data specification can still leave
the actual research design impossible or underpowered. That remains unresolved.
A matched sampling argument, eligible data and sufficient information could
make a future procedure defensible; an invalid source/target or hidden leakage
would veto its dependent comparison. No such procedure is claimed here.
