## Internal work versus local source limits

Existing group, bank and insurance cases already support some original clauses.
Their authority, shared interface and complete-output suitability need explicit
review. New conversion-to-control, positive recovery and dated issued/held
scenarios stay open where required by a promoted claim. They are not automatically
the only admissible examples for MC06/21/22. Preserve their exact local source
questions rather than calling every internal work item externally blocked.

For history uncertainty, retain correlated whole histories, actor-specific
information, possible nonresolution, partial frontiers and query-dependent
compression. Verify that any merging/reweighting/sampling rule preserves the
claimed target under its stated assumptions; check rare branches, financing
feedback, revised observations, incompatible clocks and later policy changes.
The general mixed/continuous problem is not settled by finite exact examples.

For debt/equity, retain issuer and investor states, gross subscription/proceeds,
contractual terms, endogenous price/allocation, existing-holder effects, default
and recovery timing, and physical versus pricing measures. An accounting
identity or clearing numerical example does not establish equilibrium existence,
uniqueness, factual applicability or behavioral validity. Every promoted case
needs its own complete required-output list and evidence.

Failure review must cover shared-state interactions and unsupported models,
not just count original rows. Newly discovered failures are added with exact
mechanism and query. Unknown probability weights, missing feasible histories,
future-information leakage, funding plugs and silent renormalization remain
local vetoes. No finite register is an exhaustive statement of all possible failures.

## Stop and continuation rules

Stop an affected mutation on baseline drift, corrupt evidence, a changed target,
circular expected results, lost required output or unsupported accounting
admission. Repair the harness or scope statement and preserve the failed result.
A candidate failure does not reject the research direction. Continue independent
ready work after a local source limitation. Human review is an acceptance status;
it is not a substitute for unfinished internal source or implementation work.

Runtime/product delivery still needs durable storage, concurrency/crash behavior,
operational security and resource evidence. Empirical forecasting still needs
historical availability, targets, fair tuned comparators, heldout data and
uncertainty-aware downstream evaluation. Those later duties are preserved; S6
and E3 are not promoted by this design-package work.
