## Post-run red team

The strongest alternative explanation for apparently extensive progress is
repeated verification of the same bounded examples. The weakest evidence remains
whole-company integration, consequential technical-source review and independent
reader/domain/implementation acceptance. A lost source clause, hidden dependency,
unsupported promoted claim, mismatched financial result or stale-pointer overwrite
would invalidate the affected conclusion. The present checks support the stated
repairs; they do not prove that no further gaps exist.
