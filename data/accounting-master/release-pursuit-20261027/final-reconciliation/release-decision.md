## What the effort materially improves

The simulation argument now makes several previously missing distinctions
explicit. The coupling bound requires related initial states and a loss that
uses preserved records. Particle-weight updates distinguish ancestor selection
from an additional likelihood weight and show the error from counting the old
weight twice. Financing has a continuous admissible amount domain: a finite
menu of sale choices does not discretize borrowing amounts. Minimum required
funding, best cash buffer and final cash are different questions, and changing
same-day settlement order can create an intermediate shortfall. Midpoint-grid
error converges under the derived bound but need not decrease at every increase
in grid size. The repaired text supplies the floor-function proof and a direct
counterexample. These conclusions rest on written assumptions and derivations;
finite checks reproduce their examples rather than proving general convergence.

The dated PAA example now connects the selected gross and reinsurance
roll-forward to a dated group, common book and interim report. Its supplied
calendar, eight-event sequence, eligibility findings and conditional status are
explicit. This supplies the previously missing bounded interface connection in
MC22.04. It does not settle actual applicability, general actuarial estimation
or independent domain acceptance. The bank material now collects the separate
AU and US profiles, their exact-time behavior and the existing US book-to-capital
example. The old supplement's request to integrate that map into the manuscript
has been performed by 5.55 and preserved by 5.55-R1.

Source/history/opening/report replay is included in the selected package.
Separate source-construction and intragroup archives have additional complete
reproduction evidence; they are linked, but are not silently described as
members of the 452-file manuscript package. The intragroup replay retains all
351 complete exchanges, including 28 unresolved-tax responses. Unknown issuer
history, unknown availability and conditional accounting premises remain
unknown. Preserving such uncertainty is correct behavior; inventing a history
would not close the source or integration requirements.

The seven companions cover historical Core0, a selected term program, the
refinancing domain, retained source/history replay, dated PAA, bank interfaces
and numerical reference computations. Both current volumes were rebuilt from
a separate copy, with every normalized page text equal and no original-worktree
TeX inputs. The new numerical companion reproduces all 169 retained teaching
checks and 6,552 exact rational boundary cases; the six predecessor companions
retain their checked bytes. The local build/source record has 460 passing
checks and 12 rendered-page bindings. These results establish their stated
engineering boundaries, not a clean installation or full implementation.

All 27 claims in the selected claim map have current bounded dispositions.
The inspected source editions, original-code comparisons and local deductions
remain distinguishable. This closes that mapped queue, not the larger survey,
every citation, or the search for relevant competing methods and corrections.

## Boundaries that affect the decision

**Legitimate unknowns:** the 236 retained observations, 101 equations and 180
issuer obligations do not identify an actual issuer opening or unique journal
history. A complete source disposition may correctly conclude that a question
is unresolved. The remaining defect is absent required disposition or missing
specified behavior, not uncertainty itself.

**Claim-dependent work:** measured conversion-to-control allocation, positive
post-write-off recovery admission and more complex insurance changes need their
own facts and authority if promoted. They do not invalidate the existing bounded
bank/PAA cases or become the only admissible route to their original acceptance
conditions. No required method is waived merely by calling it unsupported.

**Later product and empirical work:** durable storage, concurrent/crash recovery
in a real service, deployed adapters, operational performance and predictive
validation are retained duties. They are not automatically prerequisites to
every scholarly design claim. Their required semantics and validation protocols
do belong in the design. S1–S5 remain partial; S6 remains held and E3 deferred.

**Independent acceptance:** author checks, generated fixtures, a successful
build and this reconciliation cannot supply reader comprehension, professional
accounting judgment or a separately compatible implementation. Conversely,
pending acceptance is no excuse to leave feasible internal work unfinished.
No outreach, professional fee, installation or publication is authorized here.

The strongest alternative explanation for successful examples is a common
mistaken interpretation shared by authored fixtures and code. Exact arithmetic
does not rule it out. Source-specific review, independent expected consequences
and independent implementation/domain judgment address that risk. A changed
source identity, contradictory technical passage, lost feasible history or
incompatible profile would reopen the affected bounded result. The most fragile
part of the current release case is the jump from numerous local examples to
the full connected specification; this report explicitly refuses that jump.
