# Whole-manuscript review inventory and ordered diagnosis

MC29/30/32/33/34; C3/C5. Read the current candidate in ordered units of at most
six rendered pages. The reader is an accounting/finance researcher and a
financially literate implementer. They should be able to reconstruct each
mechanism, assumptions, source boundary and implementation consequence.

Create one consolidated structure/evidence ledger before claiming whole-document
coverage. Recursively inspect only included source files; inventory every part,
chapter, section, subsection, support heading and displayed equation as a node.
Retain actual parent/argument ancestors and exact source/PDF hashes. Bind every
rendered page; leave unreviewed states explicit. Build auxiliary bookmarks and
labels can locate anchors; an approximate equation location is not a read.

Primary criterion for this first inventory: actual source reachability, every
identified display in the hierarchy, accurate parent links, complete physical
page inventory and no invented reader judgment. Inventory counts and successful
parsing are explanatory; they cannot establish narrative or mathematical quality.

Each actual reading segment records its exact PDF identity, pages, entry and
exit understanding, specific findings, reviewed equations/source claims and
next dependency. Carry the prior exit state into the next entry. Distinguish
structural check, author continuous reading, repaired/regressed, independent
reader and human acceptance. Review bibliography and front matter as separate
units. Contact sheets do not substitute for normal-resolution reading.

Skeptical audit: a filename list misses nested inputs and an outline misses
equations. Use a comment/verbatim-aware recursive scanner and inspect its
source/bookmark correspondence. Do not resolve unknown locations by pretending
they were reviewed. The ledger will evolve when a source defect is found;
retain exact baselines and rebind affected descendants after a repair. A
completed bounded coupling repair is recorded separately and does not imply
a completed whole-book diagnosis. No controller, score or acceptance automation
is being built.
