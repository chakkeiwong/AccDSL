24 October 2026. Forty-five exact checks across eight retained probes pass.
The original complete sequence compiler and full native replay are the
comparator. Probes inspect terminal inventory, intermediate alternatives,

Let F be the complete declared conditional family and Q a typed account
predicate. Partition F into F∩Q and F∩¬Q. A witness in either part is checked
by original complete replay. If both parts contain witnesses, Q is contingent.
If F∩Q is nonempty and F∩¬Q is checked empty, Q is necessary within F. The
opposite establishes impossibility. If both are empty, return empty_family;
do not present vacuous truth as an informative financial outcome. Missing
proof or exhausted budget leaves the affected answer unknown. Unresolved
target obligations prevent promotion from a candidate-family statement to an
actual-history statement even if the relaxed formula is decided.
