# Chapter 3 — preserve evidence while conclusions change

Version 3.45, physical pages 62–73, inspected in two consecutive segments.
Independent and human acceptance remain pending.

The chapter converts the financial distinctions already demonstrated into
representation rules. An immutable assertion retains its literal source,
measurement, period, perimeter and information status. Mapping then establishes
a financial relationship rather than a label match; temporal selection chooses
eligible assertions rather than the latest stored row. Separate coverage
populations prevent acquisition or extraction success from standing in for
semantic and simulation completeness.

The concrete adverse cases give these rules consequences. The wrong auditor
reference is preserved as faithful text but rejected as a verified location.
Nearest rounding supplies a constraint only when that convention is admitted.
Publication and possession answer different historical queries. The treaty
correction finally shows why candidate-set changes can invalidate selection
without rewriting a committed event or supplying authority for a new journal.

The progression is coherent: first establish records and relationships, then
test their meaning against source error, precision, timing and revision. The
examples preserve limits without discarding narrower supported answers. No new
confirmed defect was found in this author pass. Implementation, domain review
and exact-source availability remain separate requirements. The literature
chapter can now be assessed against these concrete representational needs.
