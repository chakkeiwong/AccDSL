The equation adds discounted capital costs. It does not supply the capital amounts or select
their risk measure. A projected best-estimate runoff, a standard-deviation profile and a quantile
profile can have the same opening capital and different future charges. Near equality in one
source example does not make them equivalent. If capital or discount factors are random, the
intended conditional expectation and their joint dependence must be specified; discounting
expected capital generally differs from expecting discounted capital. Capital-charge timing also
need not equal claim-payment timing.
The paper’s chosen capital charges, discount rates and tolerance levels belong to its historical
discussion and illustrative calculations (England et al., 2019, Sections 5–7). They are not
AccDSL defaults. An actual insurance risk adjustment additionally requires the applicable
accounting basis, the entity’s compensation for non-financial risk, the relevant fulfilment cash
flows and appropriate treatment of reinsurance and group allocation. The paper’s payment-
triangle example does not supply all those quantities, and its discussion leaves allocation and
diversification beyond scope.
For implementation, the finite evaluation request must therefore identify the law and its
provenance, information date, horizon, contract perimeter, currency and unit, payment or loss
sign, discount convention, chosen functional and explicit parameter. It returns the mean, total
risk measure, relative amount, boundary convention and cumulative coverage, together with any
exact quantile-equivalence interval and unresolved condition. A negative relative quantile amount
is retained and flagged if the declared compensation profile requires nonnegativity; silently
flooring it would change the method. Unknown probabilities, mixed currencies, incomplete
draws, missing preferences and signed inputs outside the nonnegative PH method block their
dependent calculation. They cannot be repaired by borrowing a percentage from a paper. The
next software boundary must preserve precisely the joint outcomes on which these calculations
depend.
