E4. Bank losses and insurance reserves
Bank prediction distinguishes contractual cash collections, realized defaults, recoveries and
reported allowance measurements. An allowance is an accounting estimate, not an observed
realization of latent credit loss. A bank protocol needs exposure definitions, vintage/cohort
boundaries, default/cure policy, original rating history, censoring, collateral and recovery timing,
and the information available at each measurement. Aggregate filings do not supply those
fields merely because they disclose a total allowance. Until eligible cohort data exist, compare
exact conditional transition examples and reported aggregate bridges; do not claim calibrated
borrower-level PD/LGD/EAD.
Insurance validation separates ultimate reserve prediction conditional on an observed upper
triangle from one-year paid claims plus re-estimated reserves. Use the retained Mack and
bootstrap dossiers for the exact conditioning and net-reserve center. Deterministic chain ladder
is a point baseline; moment-based Mack uncertainty, a fully specified process-law bootstrap, and
any enhanced model are different comparators. Mack moments alone do not specify a simulation
law. Preserve shared fitted-parameter dependence across accident years and the backward-
law assumptions required by the inspected consistency argument. One-year validation refits
after simulated/revealed development; it cannot substitute an ultimate prediction distribution.
Missing increments, negative/zero bases, unverified development dates or absent process law
veto the dependent comparison and trigger data/method repair. Financial-disclosure panels
alone do not provide independent insurance triangles or actuarial calibration evidence.
E5. Numerical inference and scenario interpretation
Start with rational enumeration and the normal conditional model derived earlier: prior mean
0 and variance 4, observation 2 with variance 1 give posterior mean 8/5 and variance 4/5. Cash
100 + 10z then has mean 116 and variance 80. Check the measure, normalizer, transformations
and downstream cash distribution before speed. Compare analytic/factored linear algebra,
error-controlled quadrature where applicable, an ordinary target-specific sampler, and only then
an enhanced sampler. Mixed branches require evidence weights, not chain counts. Exact-equality
constraints require a declared constrained measure; adding noise changes the target and requires
a separate comparison.
Numerical tolerances derive from the requested monetary enclosure or decision accuracy. Pos-
terior agreement, unresolved mass, divergences, convergence and Monte Carlo standard error
have declared veto roles before throughput is interpreted.
Independent chain initials are
required; seed repetition alone is not an independent target. Failure on an analytic target
triggers implementation or target repair. Failure only on a complex target triggers the specified
parameterization/tuning repair, not abandonment of all inference.
The earlier causal example supplies a separate semantic test. Under U ∼Bernoulli(1/2), X = U
and Y = 2X + U, conditioning on X = 1 gives Y = 3; intervening to set X = 1 gives Y in
{2,3} with mean 2.5. The test checks the supplied structural model. A successful test does
not identify a real corporate intervention from filings. Empirical causal promotion requires its
own identification design, confounding assumptions, data and estimand; observational forecast
performance cannot provide those premises.
