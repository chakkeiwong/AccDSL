Employment conditions then determine which otherwise earned units survive. Under Section
8 of the filed 2022 performance form, ordinary termination generally cancels unvested rights.
Death or disability before vesting instead allows a fraction of the performance-qualified award to
vest on the specified vesting date. The fraction is the total performance-period days less the days
remaining at severance, divided by total days. For an expressly hypothetical eligible fraction of
3/5 and the uncapped 1,500-unit result, 900 units survive to that date; if the negative-return
cap first reduces the result to 1,000, 600 survive. These calculations use the filed form’s terms
and assume no other modification, leave adjustment or recoupment event (Apple Inc., 2022,
Exhibit 10.3, Sections 3–4 and 7–9).
The proxy’s termination summary must remain alongside that versioned form, rather than
overwrite it. It describes pro-rata accelerated vesting for named executives other than Cook upon
death or disability, and different provisions for Cook. The 2022 form’s Section 8 instead points to
the vesting date. An applicable executed agreement, amendment or further disclosure is needed
to establish which timing governs a particular executive. This is an unresolved applicability
and timing distinction between the inspected sources, not an established contradiction for one
identified award (Apple Inc., 2025b, printed p. 53).
Dividend-equivalent rights add a cash consequence to the same conditional award. The filed
form credits ordinary cash dividends using the outstanding target units and pays the vested
proportion in cash, subject to withholding and the other terms. Suppose one dividend of
$0.25 occurs while all 1,000 target units are outstanding before severance, and there are no
other dividends or adjustments. The initial credit is $250. A 1.5 performance multiplier with
full eligibility pays $375 gross; under the assumed 3/5 eligible fraction, the vested proportion
is 900/1,000, so $225 is payable. Applying the negative-return cap reduces that conditional
payment to $150. This cash right is different from the hypothetical dividend reinvestment used
to measure shareholder return; adding it to that return would count a different entitlement
(Apple Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.3, Section 5(b)).
The model consequently needs distinct stages: determine the applicable award and observations;
calculate the return and rank under evidenced conventions; apply the schedule and cap; determine
surviving rights; and apply settlement and withholding terms. The checked examples cover
selected consequences with explicit inputs. They do not recover the October 2026 outcome,
reproduce all individual awards, value compensation expense or establish diluted EPS. Those
questions require further data and their own accounting methods.
