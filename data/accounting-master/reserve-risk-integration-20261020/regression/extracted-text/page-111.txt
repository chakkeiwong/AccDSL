The corporate reference package as operations
The Northstar example defines a closed, reproducible corporate package rather than a universal
standard. Its events separate performance, invoicing and settlement even where the example
makes the first two simultaneous. Every payload includes entity/book, currency, economic date,
period, source or assumption IDs and an information-view ID. Amounts are exact before the
declared posting quantisation. Quantities and money are checked separately.
