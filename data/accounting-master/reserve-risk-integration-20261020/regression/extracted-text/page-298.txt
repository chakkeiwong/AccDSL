12.12.1
Held underlying items determine the matching presentation
When a direct-participation insurer holds the underlying items, paragraph 89(b) permits a
different OCI policy. B134 places in profit insurance finance that exactly offsets the profit
income or expense on those items, with both amounts presented separately. The residual
insurance finance goes to OCI. Revisit the preceding pool increase of 20, but now supply a valid
asset measurement that reports 8 in profit and 12 in OCI. The matching insurance finance is
expense 8 in profit and expense 12 in OCI, eliminating both net financial movements. The
insurer’s fee increase still enters CSM under the service method; matching is not an additional
fee adjustment (European Commission, 2023a, IFRS 17.87A–90,B134).
Actual holdings must be recorded separately from the pool named by the contract. If holdings
change the available choice between 88(b) and 89(b), B135–136 preserves the previously
accumulated OCI under a separate reclassification schedule based on the former approach and
assumptions at the change date. Those assumptions are then frozen for that schedule. Prior
comparatives are not restated and accumulated OCI is not recomputed as if the new policy had
always applied. New-period finance follows the newly applicable policy. A method that simply
replaces the old rate and reserve with a newly computed one would discard required history
(European Commission, 2023a, IFRS 17.B135–136).
