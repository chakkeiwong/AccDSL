The notes make these remaining conditions specific. Note 5 separates gross PP&E by asset
class: the extra acquisition and retirement must cancel within each affected class to leave that

current liabilities, and 9,254 of non-current income taxes payable from 36,634 of other non-

Note 8 identifies finance-lease right-of-use assets of 1,069 within PP&E and distinguishes lease
liabilities and noncash lease acquisitions. Those disclosures do not identify the hypothetical

42, 45–46).
The distinction therefore survives the repair: an algebraic offset is available; a row-preserving
transformation is available under stated economic, tax and reporting assumptions; an actual
Apple completion is unestablished. To admit one, the historical model needs the underlying
obligation, gross asset classes, expense and liability assignments, tax bases and noncash treat-
ment, together with a feasible rest of the company. The subsequent quarter additionally needs
settlement dates and surviving asset cohorts. Identical annual statements do not make those
future responses identical.
9.5.5
Equal liabilities can require different cash paths
The conditional repair leaves different obligations beneath the same annual liability total. In
the q = 2,000 illustration, History 1 owes 1,082 for capital purchases and 2,000 for the separate
expense; History 2 owes 2,082 and 1,000 respectively. Each owes 3,082. Consider what paying
those balances would do, conditional on the economic and reporting assumptions needed for
the preceding construction. Assume payment at carrying value, sufficient unrestricted cash,
and no additional interest, foreign exchange, remeasurement or tax effect. Capital settlement is
classified as investing cash and expense settlement as operating cash in this example. Neither
the obligation nor its settlement terms have been identified in Apple’s disclosures.
If both obligations are paid at the first subsequent date, each payment debits its liability and
credits cash. No new expense arises: the expense or acquisition was recognised in the prior period.
Both histories reduce cash and liabilities by 3,082, leave assets other than cash unchanged,
and leave profit, OCI and equity unchanged. The indirect operating-cash calculation includes
the expense liability’s decrease once; it excludes capital settlement from the operating-liability
adjustment. The cash-flow statement nevertheless distinguishes the histories. Writing each
ordered pair as operating cash followed by investing cash, the joint alternatives are
(CFO, CFI)H1 = (−2,000, −1,082),
(CFO, CFI)H2 = (−1,000, −2,082).
