A qualifying change to formal hedge designation is neither automatic discontinuation nor

10.12.1
The option premium pays for protection
A purchased option can protect an inventory purchase even if it expires with no intrinsic value.
Its time value is the price of that protection. When only intrinsic value is designated, applying
the earlier whole-derivative method would mix the price of protection with the payoff that
offsets the purchase. AASB 9.6.5.15 (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2022h, 6.5.15 and
B6.5.29–33) separates them. The designated intrinsic-value movement follows the applicable
hedge method; the related time-value movement accumulates in a different equity component.
The nature of the hedged item determines when that cost affects earnings. A transaction
such as an inventory purchase uses a cost attached to that transaction. Protection over a
period uses systematic, rational amortisation over the period in which the intrinsic-value hedge
adjustment can affect earnings. The life of the option alone does not decide which route applies
(B6.5.29–30).
