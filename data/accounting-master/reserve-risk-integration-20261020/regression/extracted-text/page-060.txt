Paying the capital liability is an investing movement; paying the separately accrued expense
liability is an operating movement. Each payment debits its liability and credits cash. Under the
explicit assumption of no incremental tax, currency or interest effects, it creates no new profit,
other comprehensive income or equity movement. Sufficient unrestricted funding is assumed;
no opening cash balance is manufactured.
Taking the lowest operating amount from H1 and the lowest investing amount from H2 would
produce −4,082. Neither history produces that total. The result must return the two pairs, not
independently selectable marginal endpoints. Moving capital payment to the second period
changes first-period cash to −2,000 in H1 and −1,000 in H2; moving the expense payment
instead changes it to −1,082 and −2,082. Thus equal eventual cash does not identify its timing.
The six specified paths retain both branch identity and payment period.
When dates are missing, the response keeps the conditional eventual amounts and marks period
timing unresolved. It distinguishes these calculated amounts from reported tokens. A request
for future depreciation remains unsupported without the asset cohorts, tax state and other
required facts. A probability request remains unsupported without an admitted law. Neither
refusal erases the amounts that a narrower question can answer.
2.24.2
The information used belongs to a particular view
The later annual source provides a different kind of progress. Its FY2024 Americas comparative
shows sales of 167,045, cost of sales of −89, 587 and selling and marketing expense of −9, 802,
giving operating income of 67,656 (Apple Inc., 2025a, Note 13, FY2024 Americas comparative).
The selected original source lacks those two separate expense rows. An original-source query
therefore returns unresolved components, while a query explicitly naming the later source
can return them. The result links the later view separately; it does not silently enlarge the
original view or assert that measurement scope was unchanged. Later detail is not, by itself, an
accounting-error correction.
Named-source access answers what retained bytes contain. Investor-information and system-
as-of queries additionally require accepted clocks for those exact bytes. Where publication,
ingestion or review time is unestablished, the historical query remains partial with a named
timing obligation. A filing event alone cannot date a separately converted PDF byte for byte.
This prevents the later comparative detail from becoming evidence that an earlier investor could
already have used.
