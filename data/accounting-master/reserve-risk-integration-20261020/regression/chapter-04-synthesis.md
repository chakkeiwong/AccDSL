order. Accounting structure establishes conservation and admissible events;

not universal defaults, source gaps do not provide technical support, table
metrics do not validate accounting meaning, and a successful sampler does not
prove exhaustive exploration. The final integration argument follows from
these limits rather than declaring novelty or empirical advantage.
