# Appendix A: portable operation reference

21 October 2026. Version 3.45, physical pages 369–379, two ordered actual-view segments. Provisional author review; independent and human acceptance remain pending.

The appendix is a lookup reference after the body has explained the financial meaning. Corporate, joined-company and real-source packages identify the exact records, dependencies and effect boundaries. They preserve the difference between documentary checks, actual issuer evidence and runtime behavior. A complete token query does not resolve a company history; a finite migration preserves only its declared quantities and following operations.

The operation sequence maintains the distinctions needed by the argument. Preparation does not admit a fact, compilation does not solve or post, witnesses do not define an entire family, and evaluation does not automatically select a law. Assessment, authorization, proposal and atomic commit are separate steps. Reports retain the joint branch and period; explanation retains the frozen view; resume retains the original semantics. Export, import and migration do not silently admit financial facts or change models.

Closed accounting records connect the economic event, posting profile, journal and independent delta. Synthetic authority cannot authorize a real-book posting. Method definitions retain explicit preconditions and unsupported cases. The EPS records preserve exact context, complete series inventory, potential and actual dates, dilution ordering and partial statuses. The company bridge derives the required earnings and weighted shares rather than substituting group profit or closing shares. Its two documentary worlds do not establish arbitrary-company support.

The reference format is appropriate here: it performs an implementation lookup function and follows the narrative prerequisites. No new confirmed manuscript defect was found.
