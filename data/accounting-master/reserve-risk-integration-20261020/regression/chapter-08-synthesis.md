# Chapter 8 — from evidence to committed financial state

Version 3.45, physical pages 105–112, inspected in order. This remains author
evidence; independent, domain and human acceptance are pending.

The execution chapter turns the language into a sequence of operations.
Ingestion versions readings without replacing source bytes. Reconstruction
combines constraints across accounts and preserves unexplained residuals.
Conditional runs can supply an assumed maturity without rewriting history.
Scheduling then makes material event order explicit.

The authorization and replay sections supply the central engineering detail.
A journal's balance does not authorize recognition. Event basis, permission,
journal and economic delta must agree before an atomic transaction changes
the book. If a committed sale loses its acknowledgement, the original request
returns the old receipt before freshness is tested. Changed payloads conflict;
an unknown commit outcome is not permission to invent a new event identity.

The financial examples retain their meaning through these procedures. Fixed
and floating debt produce different but internally coherent statements. Bond
accretion separates carrying amount from cash payment and market value. The
Northstar operation table connects preconditions to both quantities and
postings, and the closing information rules prevent realized future defaults
from entering current measurement. Economic funding failure remains a model
outcome; unavailable computation remains incomplete.

The tables, pseudocode and authorization diagram are legible. No new confirmed
defect was found. The procedures are specifications, not an implementation
test, accounting-law opinion or empirical validation. The next chapter must
show how external components fit these responsibilities.
