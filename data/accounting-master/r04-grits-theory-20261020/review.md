20 October 2026. Author review; independent acceptance remains pending.

The strongest alternative explanation for the code/paper reward mismatch is
version history: the pinned current Microsoft source and this Python standard
library may differ from the exact experimental environment. This does not
erase the checked mismatch in the inspected expression, but it blocks claims
about the original experiment's numerical impact. Historical evaluator hashes,
data, preprocessing and dependency identities could establish or refute that
impact. A function named lcs_similarity is not a correctness guarantee.

The 2x2 witness depends on the inspected tie rule and a deliberately small
domain. It is sufficient to reject a universal tightness or positive-ratio
claim for that procedure, but not to estimate typical losses. The lower/upper
proof does not need tightness. Both current shortcuts have a separate bounded-
reward proof; they are not accused of causing the demonstrated failure.
Observed2x2 upper-bound equality is not promoted to a general theorem.

The paper fairly states that its algorithm is a heuristic and reports its
lower bound. The finding therefore rejects stronger exactness claims, not
the paper's explicit bound account. One must also distinguish grid cells from
logical spanning cells, whose text repeats across grid locations. The source's
44,381-table corruption subset excludes spanning cells; it is not a general
financial-document or accounting test.

gap. Published-version and comprehensive correction/forward-search coverage
also remain incomplete. All18 references are triaged, not misreported as
