# Exact conditional clock families and policy consequences

27 October2026. MC01/03/05/11/12/13/24/25/26/28/33/34;
W01/03/04/12/13/16. Baseline5.26; existing whole-second views and exact instant
projection stay unchanged. This is a bounded conditional reference, not an
installed production policy or evidence of an actual actor's knowledge.

Research intent: identify all selected revision views compatible with supplied
clock ranges, chronology, source revisions and an explicit access route. Test
whether uncertain time changes which already-certified policy problem applies.
Expected failure: independent per-clock labels create impossible joint views,
or midnight/midpoint rounding leaks a later correction into an earlier decision.
Promotion criterion: complete possible-view sets agree with independently
stated cases and finite clock-order enumeration; every feasibility witness or
empty-cell contradiction is independently checked. Promotion veto: omitted
view/boundary, invented access, chronology violation, altered immutable source
or policy population. A failed candidate triggers repair; a corrupted baseline
or invalid comparator is a continuation veto. Counts/runtime are explanatory.
No probability, measured speedup, source truth or actual-company admission follows.

Inputs use exact rational UTC seconds from the retained instant parser. Point
clocks are closed; bounded ranges and UTC calendar days are [start,end). Unknown
clock observations keep an unknown marker and an unconstrained variable over
the supported time axis. Their values are never replaced by download/publication.
A caller-supplied trusted observation digest binds the proposed clock to its
source/actor/event; untrusted supplied values are retained but cannot restrict
its time. Malformed or mismatched bytes are invalid regardless of trust.

Record identity and replacement ancestry use existing content-hash conventions.
The new profile carries two required slots and at most six relevant revisions.
Payloads still select the same eight financial/observation problems. Chronology
premises are explicit: authored<=ingested<=accepted; authored<=accessed;
authored<=published for a supplied publication; and nondecreasing authored,
ingested, accepted and same-actor access along a replacement chain. Effective
time need not be monotone: corrections may be retrospective. Publication order
is imposed only when both revisions supply it. These revision chronology rules
are selected-profile premises, not universal laws of document correction.

Access has an explicit route. public_document requires supplied public-release
context, published<=accessed, and both times<=decision. private_delivery requires
an actor-addressed private-delivery observation and authored<=accessed<=decision;
no public release is inferred or required. An absent/untrusted access observation
remains unknown and is explored as such. A known private-delivery record is not
relabeled public. Published public information alone never proves personal access.
System ingestion/review<=system_asof and effective<=effective_at apply separately.
Ancestor availability remains required for a complete selected revision under
this deliberately conservative profile, matching the predecessor's dependency
rule; a child-alone path is exposed as unresolved, not silently admitted.

A public access earlier than known publication is a new contradiction under the
explicit public-retrieval route. The predecessor fixture future_published has
that chronology; preserve its accepted old scope and record the new profile's
explicit disagreement instead of claiming all29 cases are identical. All other
fully specified compatible cases must agree after stripping profile/digest
spelling. Unknown clocks can now yield a complete family of possible views;
that is a refinement of predecessor unresolved output, not guessed knowledge.

Mechanism: strict/non-strict difference constraints x_v-x_u<=c. Exact
lexicographic Bellman–Ford detects an inconsistent cycle or constructs rational
potentials with a positive epsilon chosen from actual rational slacks. An
independent checker verifies a witness against every original edge, or sums the
reported original cycle edges to a negative (or zero-strict) contradiction.
Eligibility is a conjunction of threshold guards. Its false branch is the
ordered disjoint first-failing-guard partition. Both sides are certified; unknown
clocks and correlations remain in the same global chronology problem. At most
4096 certified tree nodes per request; a budget failure returns unresolved work
and no completeness claim. Scope/shape errors refuse before enumeration.

Independent comparator: for small families enumerate all ordered point/gap
placements relative to the original interval endpoints and cutoff thresholds,
including enough distinct positions per gap to realize all event orders.
Compare full feasible views, not witness counts. Larger test cases use explicit
independently authored expected views plus checked partition certificates. All
comparisons retain conflicts, missing/retracted slots and unavailable ancestry
as outcomes. Policy sets remain separately labeled by view; no averaging or
cross-view optimality is asserted.
