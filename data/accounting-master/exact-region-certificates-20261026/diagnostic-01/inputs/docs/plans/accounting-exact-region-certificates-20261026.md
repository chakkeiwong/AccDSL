# Certify demand-driven two-rate region execution

26 October2026. W04/W11/W12/W13/W16; MC11/12/17/24/25/26/28/33/34.
Predecessors: accepted symbolic method and sequence certificates. Preserve them.

Question: can each selected rate-region/order job be closed using a checkable
partition and exact financial relation, instead of promoting a completed
witness? Comparator: the full conditional sequence certificate plus the earlier
independent two-date economic oracle. The3872 job IDs are the preserved finite
profile: two modes, two rights,11 atoms per rate, two orders per date, two receipts.
Charts are equivalent through the preceding two-chart certificate; region work
uses the alpha chart explicitly. It does not cover other charts or horizons.

Research intent: test whether branch pruning is justified for every point of
each selected region. Mechanism: traverse the certified substituted guards,
splitting a region on each nonconstant affine comparison and its exact negation.
A discarded child must carry a rational nonnegative combination of its own
inequalities that eliminates both variables and yields a positive constant, or
zero with a positively weighted strict inequality. A retained leaf must carry
a witness satisfying every weak/strict inequality, and the complete path state
and refusal position must equal the independent symbolic oracle. Splits are
exhaustive and disjoint by construction, not by testing a grid.

Implement a small two-variable Fourier–Motzkin producer and a separate verifier
of its weighted-sum certificates. The verifier must not trust the producer's
feasibility verdict. Feasible witnesses are reconstructed by exact back
substitution and checked independently. Dimensional scope is exactly two
bounded rates; no floating-point solver, external package or general scale
claim. Store all producer choices and certificates. Preserve infeasible and
strict-boundary cases, including point atoms. Failure to produce a certificate
leaves the job unresolved; it must never mark an uncertain branch empty.

Pass criterion: all requested jobs have checked exhaustive partitions, every
empty branch has a valid contradiction certificate, every retained leaf has a
valid witness, and all complete/refusal financial formulas match the independent
oracle. Controls must alter a multiplier, omit strictness, drop a child, change
a coupon piece, erase unpaid memory, alter a source digest, and falsely complete
an unresolved job. Scope-bound queue completion needs its own exact validation
and pause/resume checks. Point tests/counts/runtime only explain; they do not
establish region correctness or scalability. Observation and nonanticipative
policy mapping remain separate. No probability weights are invented.

Default audit: inherited atom boundaries are a selected refinement, not a
claimed minimal partition; their union and strict endpoints must be checked.
Fixed/floating mapping is a supplied model hypothesis, not inferred from filings.
The producer's elimination order is a convenience with independent certificates;
bounded byte/job budgets stop with honest pending work rather than approximation.
Mutation failures veto a candidate, not the research direction; corrupted source
locks invalidate dependent execution. No human/source admission is inferred.

Skeptical audit: an unverified geometry helper would merely move the proof gap;
therefore producer and verifier must be separate, and every pruned cell must
retain a certificate checked directly against original inequalities. A witness
proves only nonemptiness, never universal outcome equality. Universal outcome
equality comes from affine composition and verified branch partition. This
plan passes with those distinctions and explicit selected-domain restrictions.
Start with a small deterministic four-job diagnostic under the same checks,
then the full roster. Each attempt120seconds CPU, up to three preserved full
attempts. No GPU, installs, external workers, stochastic comparison or default
change. Artifacts: exact-region-certificates-20261026/.
