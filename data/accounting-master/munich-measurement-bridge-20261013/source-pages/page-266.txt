In the ERGO Life and Health Germany segment, we 
generated insurance revenue in the amount of €7,966m 
(8,038m) in the financial year from insurance contracts 
issued measured using the variable fee approach or the 
general measurement model. Insurance revenue of €1,976m 
(1,764m) was attributable to insurance contracts measured 
using the premium allocation approach.  
The reinsurance contracts held in the ERGO Life and 
Health Germany segment are measured using the general 
measurement model.
