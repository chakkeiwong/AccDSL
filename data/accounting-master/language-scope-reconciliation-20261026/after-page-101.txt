The IR and evaluator define meaning separately from surface spelling. Formatting that preserves
the lowered method preserves its semantic digest. The complete authored program also retains
the original method source, so its program digest changes when those bytes change. In the
checked common-program example, adding one space leaves the lowered method and materialized
financial request identical while changing the source and program digests. Source spans and
reviewer decisions can change evidence provenance without changing the numerical model.
Evidence, semantic-model and execution identities therefore remain distinct; a field named hash
must identify what it actually binds.

7.2
Types that protect meaning
The following table uses the preserved Core 0 type vocabulary. Its distinctions motivate the
more precise portable types introduced below.
Type
Meaning and checks
Money<C>
Exact amount in currency C. Addition requires identical
currency; conversion requires a dated rate and policy.
Quantity<U>
Units of shares, goods or other commodities. Quantities
cannot be added to money or used as a
functional-currency posting.
Rate<B> and
YearFraction
Rate with basis B, plus a separately computed accrual
fraction. Simple and compound accrual functions are
distinct.
Instant, Period,
Calendar
Instant observations and flows over intervals; interval
operations preserve calendar conventions.
Account<K,Book>
Account role K and owning book. Cross-book effects
require separate balanced batches linked by an economic
event.
Observation<T>
Source assertion with contextual and precision metadata;
accessing its value retains its constraint and provenance.
Symbol<T,D>
Shared unknown with domain D; repeated references
denote the same value in a history.
Choice<E>
A finite structural choice over enumeration E. No
probability is implied.
Distribution<T>
Explicit probability measure with parameter, conditioning
and measure metadata. It is not a Symbol or a Choice.
