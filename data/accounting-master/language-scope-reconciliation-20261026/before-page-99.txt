Chapter 7
The accounting language
7.1
Scope of the first language
The historical AccDSL Core 0 proposal describes entities and books, accounts, observations,
shared unknowns, constraints, alternative interpretations, contract instances, accounting rules
and reporting mappings. It has pure expressions and a restricted posting effect. Economic
forecasting algorithms can live in typed plugins; unrestricted numerical code does not need
to be reimplemented in the DSL. The historical grammar in Appendix B fixes the preserved
teaching examples. The proposed portable records and first company profile below define the
new authoring boundary; the first matched text frontend supports the declared debt fragment.
A subsequent documentary subset authors the funded-year extension of that company profile:
identified claim allocations, explicit credit terms and a dated event sequence. Text and typed
records lower to the same body, which drives the conditional reference calculation in Section A.3.
The imported annual projection still supplies the other operating and accounting rules. This
extends the matched example without establishing a complete whole-company text grammar.
The next subset makes the operating inputs explicit as well. Section A.4 starts from the
historical opening, then derives purchases, sales, claims, expenses and closing statements from
authored quantities, prices, asset cohorts and contract terms. It imports no forecast journals.
This makes a price or service-date change traceable through the accounts, while retaining the
example’s stated method restrictions.
Identified operations can then repeat within a year and carry their unpaid claims and asset
cohorts into the next year. The conditional path in Section A.5 follows Northstar through
three annual periods, including revised assumptions and an interrupted year. Its typed records
specify this composition. The bounded text frontend in Section A.6 preserves those records
exactly, including named settlement allocations and incomplete paths. Section A.7 adds named
unweighted scenarios and pure typed expressions for the declared collection policy. Section A.8
preserves their portable identities and specifies the additional state needed by a shared-book
return method. Section A.9 gives its matched authoring and finite shared-book execution
reference. Section A.10 connects explicit company method selection to source-constrained partial
state. Section A.11 extends the retained source representation to all statement observations
and their distinct relation roles. Section A.12 then tests selected balance relations jointly
under explicit rounding and identity hypotheses. Section A.13 preserves opening, movement
and closing times while extending the selected system across the statements. Section A.14
gives a finite conditional history family with symbolic openings and explicit measurement
attachment. Section A.15 adds typed economic quantities, constraints at every event and
matched declarations for conditional families.
