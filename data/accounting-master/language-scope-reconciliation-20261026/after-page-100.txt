The historical AccDSL Core 0 proposal remains the teaching baseline. It describes entities
and books, accounts, observations, shared unknowns, constraints, alternative interpretations,
contract instances, accounting rules and reporting mappings, with pure expressions and a
restricted posting effect. Appendix B preserves its grammar and examples. The proposed
portable records and first company profile refine that baseline. Numerical and forecasting
algorithms can live in typed plugins; unrestricted numerical code need not be reimplemented in
the DSL. General recursive or simultaneous algorithms, endogenous decisions, source admission
and broader accounting profiles remain separate language and method work.
