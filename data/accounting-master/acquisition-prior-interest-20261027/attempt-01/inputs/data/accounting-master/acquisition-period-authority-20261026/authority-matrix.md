# Dated authority for a conditional acquisition-period design

Inspected 27 October 2026. This is a bounded source analysis of the AASB option.
It selects no jurisdiction, entity type, election, actual contract or control
date for the user. Periods beginning in 2027 or 2028 are future periods at this
inspection date. Their rules must be checked again before actual use.

## Source search and its boundary

The official accounting-standards index and its linked future-pronouncement
pages 1–5 were retained with headers, exact curl commands, transfer metadata and
SHA256 digests. The selected rows for AASB3,10 and127 include their adjacent
amendment lists. Current/default and future tables are distinct: the default
reporting-period fields say1 July2025 to30 June2026; these are not the fixture's
2027/2028 reporting periods. Row association is retained in index-inventory-01
and index-inventory-02. No claim is made that every AASB standard, legislative
instrument, superseded edition or future amendment was searched.

The first sandboxed request failed with DNS exit6 and returned no source bytes.
The approved trusted retry succeeded. Subsequent sources were selected from
the retained official links; downloads succeeded. Earlier empty web-tool output
supports no finding. Publication/preparation dates, compilation dates, legislative
commencement and annual-period application dates are recorded separately.

This is a bounded correspondence finding, not an all-standard cash-flow audit.

This narrows the authority dependency. It does not admit the real contract,
determine acquisition consideration, supply goodwill/NCI amounts, or establish
standards compliance for a2027/2028 entity. Uninspected early-adoption/sector
branches remain explicit dependencies rather than being silently excluded.
