COMPARISON
Comparison with IAS 7
AASB 107 Statement of Cash Flows as amended incorporates IAS 7 Statement of Cash Flows as issued and amended 
by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB).  Australian-specific paragraphs (which are not included in 
IAS 7) are identified with the prefix “Aus”. Paragraphs that apply only to not-for-profit entities begin by identifying 
their limited applicability.
Tier 1
For-profit entities complying with AASB 107 also comply with IAS 7.
Not-for-profit entities’ compliance with IAS 7 will depend on whether any “Aus” paragraphs that specifically apply to 
not-for-profit entities provide additional guidance or contain applicable requirements that are inconsistent with IAS 7.
AASB 1053 Application of Tiers of Australian Accounting Standards explains the two tiers of reporting requirements.
