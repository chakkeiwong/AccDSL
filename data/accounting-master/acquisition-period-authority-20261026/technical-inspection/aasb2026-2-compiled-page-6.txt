STANDARD 
Accounting Standard AASB 2026-2 
The Australian Accounting Standards Board made Accounting Standard AASB 2026-2 Amendments to Australian 
Accounting Standards – Extending the Application of the Conceptual Framework and Limiting the Ability of Not-for-
Profit Entities to Prepare Special Purpose Financial Statements under section 334 of the Corporations Act 2001 on 21 
April 2026. 
This compiled version of AASB 2026-2 applies to annual periods beginning on or after 1 July 2029.  It incorporates 
relevant amendments contained in other AASB Standards made by the AASB up to and including 27 August 2026 (see 
Compilation Details).
