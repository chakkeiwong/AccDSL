Compilation details are not part of AASB 2026-2. 
This compiled Standard applies to annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 July 2029.  It takes into account 
amendments up to and including 27 August 2026 and was prepared on 21 October 2026 by the staff of the Australian 
Accounting Standards Board (AASB). 
This compilation is not a separate Accounting Standard made by the AASB.  Instead, it is a representation of 
AASB 2026-2 (April 2026) as amended by other Accounting Standards, which are listed in the table below.
