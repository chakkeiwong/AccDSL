An entity shall exclude from the financing category and classify in the operating category: 
(a) 
income and expenses from issued investment contracts with participation features recognised 
applying AASB 9 Financial Instruments (see paragraph B58); and 
(b) 
insurance finance income and expenses included in the statement of profit or loss applying 
AASB 17 Insurance Contracts; and. 
(c) 
regulatory interest income or regulatory interest expense included in the statement of profit or loss 
applying AASB 20 Regulatory Assets and Regulatory Liabilities.
