Amendments to AASB 116 Property, Plant and Equipment 
35 
Paragraph AusCF1 is amended as follows:  
AusCF1  AusCF entities are:  
(a)  
not-for-profit entities; and  
(b)  
for-profit entities that are not applying the Conceptual Framework for Financial 
Reporting (as identified in AASB 1048 Interpretation of Standards).  
For AusCF entities, the term ‘reporting entity’ is defined in AASB 1057 Application of Australian 
Accounting Standards and Statement of Accounting Concepts SAC 1 Definition of the Reporting 
Entity also applies. For-profit eEntities applying the Conceptual Framework for Financial 
Reporting are set out in paragraph Aus1.1 of the Conceptual Framework.
