# The financed payment reconciles through the full year

23 October 2026. S3/S4; MC13–18/24/26/28. All 192 checks pass across 18
explicit paths: the two retained interest stresses, three floating shares and
three authored facility rates. These are deterministic controls, not a sample
or statistical comparison. The six original annual cases are reproduced first.

The successor pays suppliers before collections using an explicit draw of
85.4, collects the retained 280, and repays the new principal with its interest.
Every journal balances, every cash prefix is nonnegative, and the year-end
balance sheet, income, direct/indirect operating cash flow and equity reconcile.
The 200 payable control is split explicitly: opening payables 30 and the new
purchase 170. Payment of 160 leaves 30 plus 10. Opening receivables 35 plus
sales 300, less collections of 10 and 270 respectively, leave 25 plus 30.
Claim balances agree with their control accounts after every affected event.
The partial purchase-claim settlement is a new explicitly defined lifecycle
operation, not silent use of the earlier full-payment-only decision draft.

With the illustrative 5% simple Actual/365 facility rate and two calendar days,
interest is exactly 427/18250 currency units (about 0.023397). Under the retained
synthetic 20% tax method, cash, profit and equity fall by 854/45625 (about
0.018718) relative to the original annual case. The added draw and principal
repayment appear as separate financing flows and net to zero. Year-end debt
therefore remains 100; excluding the cost of the borrowing would give the
wrong conditional annual result for these terms. At zero interest the original
closing balances return exactly.

The 0%, 5% and 7% rates, two-day loan, no fees and hypothetical facility are
authored controls, not market observations or selected defaults. Dates and
invoice allocation are explicit scenario assumptions. Interest deductibility,
immediate tax payment and interest-as-operating-cash follow the retained
demonstration's method; no jurisdictional compliance conclusion is made.

No stochastic ranking is attempted. The tests show the conditional consequences
of declared terms, not that this is the best financing policy. Invalid claim
amounts and excessive principal repayment are refused before mutation. The
complete ordered dated journal schedules and conditional closing states are in
checks.json; run-manifest.json binds the source, environment and measured run.

Red-team assessment: the schedule stipulates collection success and no adverse
events between borrowing and repayment. It cannot establish that a company can
obtain the assumed commitment, that debt service remains feasible in every
scenario, or that the draft checker implements durable transactions. Unknown
collection and funding remain separate unresolved cases in the earlier records.
Repeated draws, fees, revolver reservations, long-dated accruals and refinancing
need additional explicit semantics. No full mission row closes.
