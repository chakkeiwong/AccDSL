# SharedLeaseBook/1 and LeaseBookEvent/1

This is a hypothetical shared-company proposal reference for the inspected
CorporateResiduals/1 lease methods. Original method source, context, owner,
currency and historical scope are fixed in original-scope.json. A caller cannot
rename that original company or imply current authority. A complete supplied
synthetic opening and explicit component membership are premises, not recovered
company facts. Additional independently instantiated synthetic leases require
their own supplied premise and identities; they do not enlarge source coverage.

SharedLeaseBook/1 has the closed fields profile, context, original_scope_sha256,
period, date, balances, leases, collection_complete, membership_premise,
funding_policy, seen, flows and conditional. The complete account registry is the
original CorporateResiduals/1 registry, not the return-only native registry.
All amounts are canonical rational strings. The signed balances sum to zero.
ROU equals the sum of all named rights' ROU, and the negative lease-liability
balance equals their total liability. Lease IDs are unique; right IDs are unique
within a lease. Rights in different leases may reuse a local name because the
identity is the pair (lease_id,right_id). Unknown membership, unknown required
amounts or missing premises remain unresolved. Cash may not be negative under
the explicit no_overdraft policy. No invented loan or balancing entry repairs it.

Each lease record contains its original-shaped component (lease_id and rights)
and a nonempty instantiation premise. Every right retains ROU, liability, rate
and last_measurement_date. Dates are canonical and fall no later than current
book date. The period and every event remain within the retained source window
2024-01-01 to2026-01-01 exclusive. This period is an execution horizon, not a claim
to have implemented annual tax or financial-report closing. Context and source
scope are exact; source/accounting admission remains false.

LeaseBookEvent/1 contains profile,id,kind,method,lease_id,date,parameters and
original_scope_sha256. kind is lease. The three supported methods are the exact
original reduce/pay/depreciate identifiers. Parameters use the original closed
field structure with canonical rational strings converted to the original exact
rational objects only after schema checks. No code path or callback is accepted.
Every original method prerequisite and its refusal category is preserved. Missing
nullable findings return unresolved; false unsupported findings keep the original
rejected/unsupported distinction. The book wrapper does not claim that arbitrary
missing required JSON fields are valid unknowns.

Events must have new identities and nondecreasing dates within the execution
period. Selected right measurement dates cannot be later than the event. Annual
payment retains the original exact anniversary and no-intervening-change checks;
no leap-day convention is invented. Unknown dates cannot create a dated prefix.
The original calculator derives a complete selected-lease result, journal and
cash categories. The wrapper checks its declared footprint, applies only the
journal delta to the one shared book, updates only that lease and records gross
cash origins by event. It never adds component opening cash or equity again.

Allowed account effects: reduce touches rou,lease_liability,other_gains; pay
touches cash,finance_expense,lease_liability; depreciate touches rou,depreciation.
Nonselected leases, unrelated accounts, context, source, period and premises
remain unchanged. After-state checks reconcile all rights, balance the complete
book and enforce cash nonnegativity. A failed proposal returns status/reason and
no after-state or journal. The original input remains unchanged.

A LeaseBookProposal/1 binds before/event/original method hashes and contains
the complete hypothetical after-state, exact journal and gross cash effects.
committed,source_admission,accounting_admission and method_eligible remain false.
apply verifies the proposal by recomputing the original method against the full
before-state and event, then returns only the hypothetical next state. It does
not publish a database transaction. Ordered sequence failure retains completed
proposals, their last state and the failed-event index without applying that event.

Evidence must compare the original four-step fixture, an explicit second
synthetic lease sharing cash, and adverse identity/source/date/allocation/funding
cases. Full JSON replay is required before recording. Complete annual reports,
tax effects, FX, lease valuation/calibration, group accounting, general module
imports and actual-source admission remain open. Exact-byte archive receipts
may preserve these records but cannot establish the proposal's financial validity.
