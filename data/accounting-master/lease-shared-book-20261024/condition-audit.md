# Shared-book lease evidence conditions

Question: reuse the original finite lease-right calculations within one complete
hypothetical company book, retaining ownership, state, dates and funding. Original
CorporateResiduals/1 authored values, independent financial calculations and
outer basis checks are the comparators; the JSON-stable r2 audit supplies70
replayed checks. Counts and balanced journals alone are not acceptance criteria.

Explicit premises: fixed original historical synthetic context/source; supplied
complete account and lease membership; canonical rational values; per-right
allocation and dates; no_overdraft company funding. The latter is a deliberate
company policy added at composition, not a standard inferred from the component
calculator. Same local right name in different leases is allowed because its
identity is (lease_id,right_id). Every selected and unselected right reconciles
with total ROU and negative lease liability before and after an event.

Primary evidence: original four-step exact state/journal/cash agreement, two
separately declared synthetic leases with one shared wallet, unrelated-state
preservation, original finding/date refusals and complete successful-prefix
retention on funding failure. A second lease cannot spend the cash already used
by the first. Balanced forged proposals reject under original recomputation.
All proposals remain uncommitted and no source/accounting admission is inferred.

The initial focused fixture encoding failed because original authored integer
amounts had not been rendered as canonical rational strings. The encoder now
converts exact integers and Fraction values while preserving Boolean findings.
This did not weaken public validation: floats, integers, noncanonical fractions
and truthy non-Booleans still reject where exact typed inputs are required.
Review additionally protects original-scope bytes and rejects numeric zero as a
collection-coverage Boolean. All15 focused tests pass after those repairs.

Unknown inputs preserve unresolved; unsupported method/calendar/scope and invalid
records remain separate. Loss of allocation, wrong funding, changed originals
or false admission veto recording. Hash corruption stops continuation; expected
unsupported cases motivate later design rather than rejecting the simulator.
No current accounting authority, actual company state, general valuation,
annual tax/report close, stochastic ranking, FX or production durability claim.
