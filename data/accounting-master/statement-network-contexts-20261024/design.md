# A selected statement network with all exclusions visible

24 October 2026. S2–S4; MC05/08/09/10/13/16/24/26/28. The audit inspected
the exact combined statement request and all relation kinds, operand units,
invariant contexts, time declarations, known/nonnumeric values and omitted rows.
It also inspected the checked temporal and same-context contracts and the
source-bound cash-flow bridge adapter. No new accounting authority is inferred.

There are 104 candidate relations: 40 snapshot sums, 30 period sums, eight
same-period comparisons, 16 same-date endpoints and ten stock-flow bridges.
Every operand is in displayed USD millions; invariant issuer/perimeter/basis
matches within each relation. All non-bridge candidates have equal operand
times. All ten bridges have one opening instant, one closing instant and
intervening period movements with exact source dates. Thus the temporal profile
already has the necessary numerical and time forms; no new solver is needed.

Three candidates retain omitted annual concepts in the interim source:
interim.capital and interim.whole_equity_bridge omit equity.issued;
interim.financing_cash omits cash_flow.debt_issuance. Their current displayed
comparison does not establish zero missing movements. This adapter will exclude
them explicitly until a separately designed omission interpretation exists.
Of the other 101 supported candidates, annual.financing_cash references a
nonnumeric annual.cash_flow.debt_issuance observation. Selecting that relation
must leave the entire requested conjunction unresolved, even with proposed
rounding and identity assumptions. Never infer zero for its dash.

The remaining 39 original relations are outside the admitted numerical forms:
six derived subtotals, 24 explanatory differences, two endpoint differences,
two cash-population comparisons and five cross-period/basis endpoints. They
retain their full prior records and reasons. No assertion is inferred from a
zero displayed residual or a same-date amount. The report always preserves all
256 observations and all 143 dispositions, with an additional explicit selection
classification. The 104 initial candidates use 244 observations; after excluding
the three omission-dependent relations, the 101 supported relations use 236
observations. A caller may explicitly
request the 100 relations without nonnumeric operands, but its result is labeled
a narrower selected system and lists the excluded relation; it cannot replace
the unresolved result for the full supported request.

Source binding is fixed to the exact combined request. One original digest is
one variable, even when it occurs in multiple statements. Date, measurement kind,
source context and declared quantity remain separate from scalar coefficients.
Stock-flow endpoint quantities use only a finite explicit mapping for capital,
retained earnings, accumulated other comprehensive income, cash-flow cash and
whole equity; those mappings are proposed interpretations requiring named
premises. No inferred source identity merging or legal cash conversion occurs.
Every selected relation has separate optional identity and population-mapping
hypotheses; nearest/ties-away rounding is separately optional.

Evidence contract: exact source/context preservation, no implicit exclusions,
explicit unresolved findings, exact original-term witness checks and adverse
source/selection/premise/lowering tests are the pass criterion. An infeasible
selected system is an admissible research result, not a cue to drop equations
until it fits. Solver unknown remains unknown. Wrong mappings, corrupted sources
or invalid witnesses veto interpretation and trigger repair. No historical or
accounting admission follows even if the whole selected numeric system is feasible.

Defaults audit: supported relation forms come from inspected existing schemas;
exclusions are explicit finite capability boundaries, not new financial facts.
The 100-row diagnostic is an explicit alternative selection, not a default
fallback. Economic contexts keep issuer/perimeter/basis; the full original
period and measurement kind are preserved in source mappings and exact time
types. Existing reference budgets are supplied explicitly and retain their
convenience-test status. CPU-only bounded conformance, no forecast or ranking.

Initial bounded implementation check: the full 101-relation request is unresolved
on the exact retained annual debt-issuance dash. The explicitly requested
100-relation numeric subset uses 229 observations and is conditionally feasible.
The seven observations removed by that selection, including the dash, are still
present in the full preserved source report. These counts were recomputed from
shared digests, not inferred from the initial candidate count. No relation was
dropped in response to solver infeasibility; the exclusion was specified first
by the existing nonnumeric source disposition.
