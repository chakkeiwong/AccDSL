# JPMorgan Series A instrument and guarantee terms

11 October 2026. This bounded source unit reads the relevant Series A and
guarantee descriptions in the complete issuer-hosted 2022–2025 Form 10-K
candidates that the Exhibit 22.2 summaries identify. The four source PDFs are
retained with their prior acquisition hashes and contain 44 text/PDF identity
and marker checks plus four year-specific funding checks: **48 checks pass**.
The checked pages are PDF indices 327/330 (2022), 355/357 (2023), 357/360
(2024) and 351/354 (2025). Eight rendered pages were inspected at normal
resolution.

The descriptions state that the debt securities are the issuer's direct,
unsecured general obligations and rank with its other unsecured and
unsubordinated debt. They describe the parent as a holding company whose
guarantee claims generally rank behind claims of subsidiary creditors. In the
2023–2025 passages, the parent also says that it contributes substantially all
net proceeds from securities issuance to the IHC, that Bank/IHC payments to the
parent are limited, and that its ability to pay depends on dividends from the
Bank and dividends or credit extensions from the IHC. The 2022 passage contains
the subordination statement but does not contain the later Bank/IHC wording;
that difference is retained rather than backfilled.

The guarantee section says the parent guarantees full and punctual payment of
principal, interest and other amounts when due, on demand, and that the
guarantee is of payment rather than collection. The same section qualifies the
wording: the guarantor is not liable for an amount the issuer is excused from
paying or for more than the amount actually due, and issuer defenses generally
remain available subject to the stated insolvency exception. These clauses
define the reported contractual description; they do not establish a currently
outstanding Series A balance, fair value, accounting recognition, legal
enforceability or default probability.

The four filings explicitly describe the material terms as summaries and refer
the reader to the 2016 Indenture and applicable prospectus supplements. A
registration statement or “may issue” description therefore remains separate
from an outstanding-instrument population. The source ledger preserves the
exact line spans, PDF page hits, source hashes and Exhibit 22.2 summary paths in
`terms.json`.
