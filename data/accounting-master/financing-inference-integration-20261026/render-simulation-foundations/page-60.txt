Adaptive resolution is possible.
A long period with no state-dependent decisions can be
aggregated analytically. A period containing maturity, reset, dividend and covenant dates may
require explicit boundaries. The aggregation must preserve ordering and any intermediate
constraint that matters.
For example, cash 10, a receipt of 20 and payment of 25 produce closing cash 5 regardless of
order. If payment comes first and no overdraft is permitted, the path is infeasible despite the
positive closing balance. A minimum-cash query requires the event sequence. A closing-balance
query alone would not detect the problem.
7.16
Stock-flow consistency across counterparties
When several entities are modeled, a payment by one is a receipt by another, subject to fees,
taxes and timing. Keeping counterpart links helps detect money creation or disappearance. In
an external-counterparty boundary, the system can record the flow leaving the modeled domain
rather than pretending the counterparty does not exist.
The stock-flow consistent approach in Caiani et al. (2016) gives a useful example of explicit
sectors and transaction sequence. Its named cash-flow measures must be interpreted in its own
definitions: a quantity called operating cash flow in that model subtracts principal repayments
and should not automatically be mapped to an accounting statement category. Reusing labels
without inspecting definitions is a semantic error.
AccDSL should therefore give each reported or derived quantity a definition and units. A label
match is a candidate mapping, not proof of equivalence. This is especially important when
connecting economic simulators, accounting standards and regulatory templates.
7.17
A coherent event-to-report method
The proposed method first admits an economic event under its contractual and behavioral
premises. It updates quantities, rights and obligations. It then invokes the accounting rule for
the declared basis and context, validates the posting bundle, and updates report accumulators.
Source and scenario provenance accompany each stage.
At reporting boundaries, the system reconciles opening balances, movements and closing balances,
checks units and perimeter, and preserves unresolved amounts or conditional interpretations. It
does not force an unsupported movement into a generic other line merely to close the statement.
A residual can be displayed as a diagnostic requiring explanation.
This sequence supports both concrete witnesses and symbolic families. If every member of a
region has the same event rule, amounts can remain symbolic. If a guard changes the rule, the
region must split or retain a piecewise expression. Accounting coherence then applies within
every admissible branch, not just to an average statement that no individual history produces.
