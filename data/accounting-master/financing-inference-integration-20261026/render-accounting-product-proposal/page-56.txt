Consent cannot be supplied by a numerical convenience either. The summary requires each
affected holder’s consent for specified protected changes, including changes to stated maturity
and reductions of principal or interest. Its general majority-waiver provision excludes payment
defaults and certain individually protected provisions. A waiver concerns the specified past
default; it does not license a future one (Apple Inc., 2024a, Exhibit 4.1, printed pp. 11–12).
A current contractual default may cease under a valid waiver while the economic record still
shows that payment arrived late. Keeping those two statements is consistent.
The surrounding qualifications matter economically. Individual payment enforcement differs
from collective proceedings; legal discharge differs from covenant defeasance. The description
warns that assets sufficient to fund scheduled maturity may be insufficient after acceleration
(Apple Inc., 2024a, Exhibit 4.1, printed pp. 10–13). A simulator must therefore retain the
relevant rights, legal owner, timing and usable funding, even when a consolidated closing
statement appears fully funded.
The typed conditional implementation translates the retained examples and records selected
findings through the common monetary and memory engine. An unresolved finding produces
no successor state. Recording a known finding changes no cash, carrying amount, shares
or historical missed amount. This establishes a limited representation and execution result.
Multiple concurrent clauses, the full instruments, actual notices and amendments, and the
economic consequences of acceleration still require their own admitted methods.
5.17
Implementation proposal
The debt module should maintain immutable contract versions, a dated obligation schedule,
current economic state and separate accounting measurements. Events include issue, draw, reset,
accrue, coupon, amortize, redeem, amend, breach, cure, accelerate, restructure and recover.
Each event has preconditions, amounts, dates and successor state.
Existing repository methods already cover bounded issue, accrual, coupon, redemption and
conversion calculations, and newer declared methods preserve timing and debt memory. They
are useful foundations. They do not by themselves establish a general pricing model, market
capacity, legal default process or complete instrument admission. The extension should compose
with their checked boundaries rather than describe those capabilities as absent.
The smallest discriminating financing fixture should include proceeds different from face, positive
recovery, a fee paid at issuance, a maturity shortfall and an unavailable refinancing offer. A
second fixture should add two creditors with priority and delayed recovery. A third should test
unknown rate terms and a covenant boundary. Passing these checks establishes the specified
mechanics; empirical pricing validity and legal applicability require separate evidence.
