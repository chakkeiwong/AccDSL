The same-day payment violates the middle equality. A status recording with no economic effect
does not. A later evidence correction can violate the last equality while leaving every balance
unchanged.
The evidence revision identifies a supplied immutable view; it is not a certificate that the
view contains all relevant facts. A caller that omits a new notice or supplies a false old view
cannot be corrected by comparing digests. Source acquisition, availability and revision selection
remain separate requirements. Similarly, checking a conservative whole-economic projection can
demand reassessment after an unrelated change. Minimal clause-specific dependency analysis
would be a further optimization needing its own correctness argument.
Clause kind prevents a different misuse. A satisfied principal-payment clause cannot supply the
missing premise for automatic bankruptcy acceleration. The selected implementation rejects
that mismatch; a separately supplied qualifying bankruptcy observation can support its own
automatic route. Its truth remains conditional on the applicable terms and supplied event
finding. The system preserves unresolved applicability rather than turning an unsupported
factual inference into a remedy.
This repair illustrates a broader simulation requirement. Assertions about current state need
the dependencies under which they were observed, not only an observation timestamp. Reusing
a cached covenant finding after a cash transfer, or a borrowing-capacity calculation after a
collateral release, has the same risk. Keeping the previous assertion permits historical replay;
withholding its current use prevents a past result from deciding a different present problem.
Neither operation requires enumerating a separate tree node for every stored copy. Shared data
can retain the dependence explicitly.
The checked continuation preserves complete states and the prior missed amount through
payment, reassessment and rescission. Unknown or stale findings return without an accepted
successor. The reference covers one selected synthetic series and supplied evidence views.
Automated source revision, several simultaneous clauses and general rights-changing financial
effects remain additional work, rather than consequences of this freshness check.
7.15
Reporting frequency and event frequency
A company may report quarterly while obligations settle daily. A quarterly engine that nets
all flows can miss a temporary liquidity failure. A daily engine can still report quarterly by
aggregation. The appropriate event resolution depends on the question and the contracts.
