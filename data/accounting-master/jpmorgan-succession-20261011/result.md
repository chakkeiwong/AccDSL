# JPMorgan succession and transfer summaries: bounded result

11 October 2026. The 2022–2025 complete issuer-hosted 10-Ks each describe
conditions on issuer and guarantor mergers, consolidations and transfers of all
or substantially all assets. The local source ledger passes **84/0** exact-byte,
PDF-span and clause checks and retains five spans for each filing. The four
succession bodies have the same wording after removing only line-leading list
markers and whitespace; each unmodified body is retained. The descriptions
are summaries of general Series A terms that may apply, not complete operative
law or an account of any actual transaction.

The issuer condition permits the continuing company or a U.S. corporation or
limited liability company successor/transferee that expressly assumes due
principal, interest, other amounts and the issuer's Indenture covenants. The
guarantor condition instead describes the continuing corporation or a U.S.
corporation successor/transferee that expressly assumes the full and
unconditional guarantee and guarantor covenants. Each branch also requires
that no Event of Default, or event that would become one with notice or time,
has occurred or would be continuing immediately after the transaction. These
are separate party and condition sets; the source gives no numeric definition
of “all or substantially all.”

The summaries deem certain guarantor transfers of material assets related to
bankruptcy, resolution or receivership proceedings to be transfers of all or
substantially all of its assets. They also say the Indenture gives no additional
holder protection merely for recapitalization, change of control or a highly
leveraged transaction unless structured as a covered merger, consolidation or
asset transfer. A particular security may carry specific protection such as a
put or higher interest in its applicable supplement. Successor assumption is
not proof that the old obligor has repaid debt or that an accounting liability
is extinguished; neither an actual transaction nor its accounting was checked.
