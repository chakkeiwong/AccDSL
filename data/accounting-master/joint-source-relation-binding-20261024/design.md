# Joint assessment of retained current balance measurements

24 October 2026. Inspected statement_context.fixture/evaluate/context_check and
source-relations/partial-state interval semantics. The complete combined report
contains 256 original observations and 143 relation dispositions. Forty relations
have snapshot form, but some are opening/equity joins with different source
measurement contexts. This first adapter selects only the 18 current balance
relations (nine annual, nine interim), whose terms already bind consistently.
All other relations and observations remain in the returned preserved report.

The adapter takes the exact full-statement request, explicit selected IDs,
an optional named rounding hypothesis and one optional identity hypothesis per
selected relation. It recomputes the existing source-bound statement result;
source request mutation is rejected by that frozen implementation. Coefficients,
terms, dates, units and original observation digests come only from that result.
It does not accept caller-supplied intervals or a caller-supplied statement result.

Display-only plus unasserted identities is the default and yields unresolved.
The explicit conditional example names nearest ties away from zero with quantum
one in the displayed USD-million unit, labeled proposed_not_admitted. Existing
partial_state.rounding_interval supplies exact signed endpoint inclusion.
Source unit USD million maps explicitly to currency:USD with scale 1000000/1;
coefficients and interval values retain displayed units. Issuer, perimeter,
basis, original period, measurement kind and exact instant remain in context.
Observation digest is the shared variable identity; no aggregate is relabeled
as legal cash or inventory units.

Selected identities are hypotheses, never inferred from zero displayed residual.
Relations outside current balance snapshots, unselected supported relations,
derived subtotals, dashes, omissions and explanatory/cross-context comparisons
retain their full existing disposition and original records. The joint result
only concerns the selected system. It neither changes the immutable preserved
report's historical uncomputed annotation nor claims to solve its whole latent
state; the new conditional joint result is a separate field.

Pass criterion: exact source mapping and preservation, independent rational
witness checks, selected local/joint consistency under the same assumptions,
and rejection of altered coefficients, source references, contexts, unsupported
relation selection, renamed premises, modified source output or forged readiness.
Missing hypotheses remain unresolved. Budgets remain explicitly supplied.
No native source assumption, numerical default, accounting admission or actual
history conclusion is changed. Counts are bookkeeping; expected feasible results
are compatibility evidence for the assumed measurement equations only.
