# Cumulative argument through Chapter 10

The ordered prefix now reaches physical page 243. Its argument still begins
with a company question: what can published accounts determine about economic
history and future consequences? Identical fixed- and floating-rate journals
can conceal different exposure. The scoped company examples show connected
histories and statements, while Apple demonstrates that disclosed amounts and
narrative generally leave multiple compatible histories. Those histories must
remain joint; their existence does not establish probabilities over them.

Chapters 3–5 make that problem operational without reducing it to extraction.
Assertions carry source context, dates and populations. Coverage of a document,
coverage of meaning and ability to answer a query are different. Corrections
preserve earlier evidence rather than rewriting it. The literature contributes
separate tools for conservation, incomplete information, provenance, inference,
contracts and numerical solving. The architecture assigns those tools bounded
roles around typed records; a DSL still has to justify its value over a capable
typed library.

Chapters 6–8 then give the histories mathematical and procedural meaning.
Compatibility sets, query semantics, nullspaces, conservation and conditional
simulation explain what the representation must preserve. A justified measure
is needed before a family can become a probabilistic mixture. Numerical
domains, endpoints and rounding remain part of the calculation. Types and
portable records preserve contexts and kinds of missingness, while exact byte
identity does not establish economic equivalence. Events, evidence admission,
authorization and atomic publication connect those meanings to a bounded
company package. The lost-acknowledgement example shows why recovery depends
on exact request identity.

Chapters 9 and 10 supply the consequential accounting work demanded by that
architecture. Operating methods retain earned service, refund obligations,
asset components, production effort, pension rights, impairment populations,
tax origins and comparative corrections. Financing methods add contractual
cash, effective interest, actual and potential shares, award terms and
ownership attribution. They repeatedly expose errors that a balanced journal
cannot detect. A replacement must remove the old component; a hypothetical
hedged item must not inherit the actual option's wrong strike; a reserve cannot
be restarted when a hedge ratio changes; a sold inventory unit cannot later
receive a write-down reversal.

The later mathematical examples deepen rather than abandon the initial
information problem. EPS ordering works under stated additive effects, then
participation and joint bonus consequences reveal its limits. A correct
hypothetical outcome does not settle whether the accounting selection rule
admits that outcome. Homogeneous reserve allocation has a scaling proof;
different designation origins require retained gain histories and a narrower
derivation. The chapter does not replace unresolved general allocation with a
convenient percentage. These distinctions preserve the original mission's
unknowns rather than forcing every request into a complete answer.

The real-source examples remain a counterweight to synthetic calculations.
Apple's cash, shares, awards and asset disclosures have different measurement
populations. Ping An's transitions and the jurisdictional comparisons retain
authority, adoption and applicability separately. Dated plan forms can narrow
possible rights without identifying a particular grant. Later exchangeability
rules require their own amendment and purpose-specific assessment. Arithmetic
consequences of an admitted input do not prove that the input is supported.

Consolidation closes an important loop back to the software design. A foreign
subsidiary's cash leaves the group population on loss of control without a
payment in the parent's legal book. Parent and NCI reserves, goodwill,
translation and period income therefore need exact book and ownership
references. The implementation-binding section reuses the earlier transaction
identity and recovery principles for a substantive financial reason. Corrected
evidence views, separate scenario branches and atomic publication protect the
accounting history; they remain specification requirements awaiting runtime
validation.

Across the full prefix, the proposed design has thus moved from a motivating
ambiguity to formal semantics, typed operations and detailed conditional
accounting methods. The later cases neither reduce the goal to bookkeeping
nor establish comprehensive company coverage. Sector methods, the remaining
implementation specification and the rest of the manuscript must still be
read. Actual valuation, operative authority, general dependent dilution
selection and heterogeneous attribution retain their identified obligations.
