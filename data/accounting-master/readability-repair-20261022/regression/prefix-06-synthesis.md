# Cumulative argument through Chapter 6

Physical pages 1–96 now carry the argument from the original financial puzzle
to a formal implementation target. Chapter 1's identical journals and
different fixed/floating exposures remain the organising problem. Accounting
agreement does not identify economic history, and compatibility does not
assign probability. The future financial question therefore determines which
historical distinctions must survive representation.

Chapter 2 tests that need against complete scoped company examples and then
incomplete public evidence. Northstar makes a full source-to-result path
inspectable; corporate and sector cases broaden the needed state. Apple
exposes what published amounts and narrative actually leave unresolved.
Joint conditional histories retain dependencies and separate reported facts
from assumed transactions, legal or operational interpretations and future
behaviour. Neither arithmetic reconciliation nor a few witnesses establishes
the complete economic history.

Chapter 3 converts those examples into observation and revision procedures.
Measurement-aware mapping, disjoint populations, coverage denominators and
four kinds of time determine which assertions can jointly enter a model.
Correcting navigation or a source reading changes selected consequences
without rewriting the source. A later treaty interpretation can invalidate a
derived result without mutating a committed journal or authorising a new one.
This supplies the evidence discipline needed by the eventual inverse problem.

Chapter 4 relates those needs to existing methods while preserving their
technical limits. Conservation, incomplete databases, provenance, partial
identification, rule languages, extraction and solvers each contribute a
piece. The survey does not prove the integrated product or predetermine the
DSL comparison. Chapter 5 then assigns those pieces to an architecture: one
typed representation can support reviewed data, a library and textual syntax.
External tools return bounded typed results, while accounting, information
and uncertainty meaning remain the enclosing system's responsibility.

Chapter 6 supplies the formal content of that responsibility. Compatibility
is a joint condition over contextual observations within declared scope.
Certain rows are not all shared knowledge, as the creditor example shows.
Nullspace ambiguity and unobserved contract terms explain why reconstruction
can remain nonunique. Conserved books, classified reporting and staged event
execution then connect each retained history to conditional future results.
The distinction between a family and a mixture is derived explicitly, and
the rounded-debt, failed-branch and observation-identity examples make its
implementation consequences testable.

These chapters accumulate rather than change the original question. The
fixed/floating pair first motivates economic state, later demonstrates
nonidentification beyond journals, and finally provides a finite probability
and grouping example. The treaty cases similarly move from company meaning
to revision, state memory and unresolved mass. Recurrence adds a consequence
at each step. Terms for assertions, histories, events, postings, information
views and conditional laws remain distinct across the six chapters.

The principal new local interruption is R347-D01: theta enters the simulation
policy and event functions without a definition. Its repair must explain
parameter provenance and conditioning without inventing calibration. The
surrounding process remains understandable, so this finding triggers a later
local repair rather than invalidating the compatibility or probability
development. No other new defect has been confirmed in the ordered prefix.

The remaining burden is now sharper. The language must encode the stated
domains, dependencies, timing, information and failure meanings; execution and
accounting modules must preserve them; comparative evidence must establish
practical value. Neither source citations, correct illustrative algebra nor
the architecture proves those later obligations. The complete candidate stays
not_ready with all 53 obligations, six interactions and 46 operations retained
and zero whole-row closures. This cumulative author synthesis covers the
entire ordered prefix, not just its latest equations; independent reader,
domain, implementability and human acceptance remain pending.
