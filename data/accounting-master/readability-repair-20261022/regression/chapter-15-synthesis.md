# Appendix A — physical pages 369–379

The appendix works as a lookup reference because the body has already taught
the financial and transactional meanings. Its opening identifies the common
envelope and says that these are specified exchanges, not deployed endpoints.
Corporate, joined-company and real-source packages first establish the
scope of the exact examples. The operation registry then distinguishes
preparation, proposal, admission, selection, calculation and commitment.

Several compact qualifications carry substantive meaning. History witnesses
do not define the whole family; a corrected constraint need not be an
intersection; uncertainty evaluation cannot silently select its result.
Accounting assessment does not grant posting permission, and permission does
not publish a journal. Reporting, explanation and resume preserve the original
family, view and semantic manifest. Import grants no financial admission.
These entries rest on the body rather than substituting headings for missing
explanation.

The closed accounting records retain same-basis and same-authorization links,
typed references and synthetic/real book modes. Method registration alone
does not establish applicability. The EPS schemas are longer, but their
field definitions, time windows, rational arithmetic and status precedence
serve the reference genre. The final company bridge checks the actual company
population, earnings and weighted share history before accepting the EPS
projection. A plausible group-profit or closing-share substitution is rejected.

The code blocks and continued fields are legible in the inspected rendering.
No new local defect is confirmed. Documentary finite exchanges remain separate
from generic compilation, live persistence, current accounting authority and
independent implementation or human acceptance. The following historical
Core0 appendix must retain that distinction rather than extend current scope.
