Continuing and discontinued EPS sum to total EPS before presentation rounding. The report
retains that reconciliation, each admitted numerator and denominator increment, and the
excluded preference series. Basic and diluted EPS receive equal prominence; negative EPS is
still presented (European Commission, 2023a, IAS 33, paragraphs 66–70).
Two sign changes test the control number. If continuing ordinary earnings are instead a loss
of 5,000 while total ordinary earnings remain a profit of 15,000, none of these three series
is admitted: each would reduce the loss per share from continuing operations. Total profit
cannot reverse that decision. Conversely, retain continuing earnings of 12,000 but change total
ordinary earnings to a loss of 9,000. Options and debt still enter, so diluted total EPS uses
−9000 + 600 = −8400 over the same diluted denominator. It is less negative than basic total
EPS. That is the consequence of the continuing-operations control, illustrated explicitly in the
standard’s Appendix A (European Commission, 2023a, IAS 33, paragraphs 41–43 and A3).
10.2.4
One comparison rule for a finite set of series
The preference shares failed because the options and debt had already reduced continuing EPS.
An implementation needs to reproduce that changing comparison for any finite number of series.
