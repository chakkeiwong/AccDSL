Each adapter therefore pins code, dependency, model and taxonomy versions, licenses, input
and output schemas, transformations and field-loss behavior. Preparation preserves bytes and
receipts before parsing, inventories the package, attaches occurrences and contexts, records
normalization, checks required fields independently and submits candidates for review. Partial
conversion retains both successful units and the exact failures or unexplored frontier. Conversion
counts cannot accept a package or an accounting treatment.
The proposed extraction comparison uses the same retained files, roots, budgets and taxonomy
resources. Manual located source units and transparent native extraction provide the reference;
applicable pdfplumber/Arelle configurations, plain Docling and explicit context/footnote repairs
form the candidate ladder. Transcription, context, table structure, mapping, source support,
retention, historical admission and cost are separate outcomes. Lost hashes or locators, look-
ahead, silent omissions and untracked downloads veto the affected exchange. The current 31
development annotations cannot establish a heldout accuracy ranking, and cell counts within
one report are not independent observations.

and dependency terms. The Beancount and hledger comparison needs an explicit distribution
boundary for copyleft components. License admission records actual versions, notices, models
