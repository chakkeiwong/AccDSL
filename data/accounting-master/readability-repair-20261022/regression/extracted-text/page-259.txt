11.4
State and event order
The dated credit methods use the selected amortised-cost, interest and impairment provisions
of AASB 9 (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2022h). The following loan has different
inputs from the earlier liquidity example, so its allowance and final equity are calculated
separately.
BankState contains cash/reserves by settlement venue and currency; deposits by customer,
access rights and maturity; assets by contract, gross carrying amount, allowance, original
EIR, accrued cash flows, collateral and credit state; undrawn commitments; funding lines and
covenants; accounting equity; regulatory mappings; and evidence/information views. LoanState
separately retains origination credit risk and current risk, SICR finding, credit-impaired/POCI
status, forbearance, modification, write-off and recovery history. Aggregating by today’s loan
balance alone loses both staging evidence and later response to shocks.
Each time step first admits information known then, forms permitted credit and liquidity
forecasts, chooses behavior under that information, and schedules due events. Accrual, actual
settlement, impairment measurement and regulatory queries have separate business identities. A
deposit received from outside debits cash/reserves and credits deposit liability. A loan credited
to the borrower’s deposit debits loans and credits deposits without initially transferring reserves;
a transfer to another bank then debits deposits and credits reserves. A loan paid directly outside
instead debits loan/credits reserves. These two funding paths cannot be treated as identical cash
events. Deposit interest debits interest expense/credits deposit or accrued payable; withdrawal
requires settlement funds.
11.5
Expected credit loss
For each admitted scenario j and date t, calculate contractual due flows minus the cash expected
to be received, including supported collateral/enforcement cash and timing; discount the shortfall
under the applicable original EIR or specified exception. With mutually exclusive exhaustive
scenario weights w_j, ECL is the sum of w_j times those present-value shortfalls. Default-time
hazards must be converted to disjoint default-time probabilities; summing cumulative PDs
over dates counts the same default repeatedly. Collateral recoveries cannot be credited both
in LGD and as a second recovery cash flow. A shorthand PD×LGD×EAD is admissible only
if its definitions and discounting reproduce the specified cash-shortfall calculation; it is not a
universal replacement.
