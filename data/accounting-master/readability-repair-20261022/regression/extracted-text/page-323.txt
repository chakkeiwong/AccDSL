12.24
Adoption dates and the restatement of 2022
A standard’s effective date and the economic date of a reported balance need not coincide.
An insurer adopting new rules in 2023 may report a revised balance for December 2022 while
retaining the earlier report as a record of what it originally disclosed. The system must preserve
both versions and explain which one a calculation uses.
The Ministry of Finance’s explanation dated 12 June 2025 describes the original ASBE 25
timetable. It specifies application from 1 January 2023 for enterprises listed both domestically
and overseas, and for overseas-listed enterprises preparing financial statements under IFRS or
ASBE. Other enterprises applying ASBE were scheduled to apply the revised standard from 1
January 2026. Its account of the 2025 notice says existing adopters, including enterprises that
had adopted early under the 2020 notice, must continue applying it. Insurers facing genuine
difficulties and seeking deferral must submit written reasons to the Ministry’s Accounting
Department and the relevant institutional supervision departments of the National Financial
Regulatory Administration. That statement does not establish an automatic deferral merely be-
cause preparation is difficult (Ministry of Finance of the People’s Republic of China, Accounting
Department, 2025, answers on background and implementation arrangements).
The same explanation limits the new simplification choices to unlisted enterprises; matters
outside those choices remain subject to ASBE 25. It names grouping, measurement, presentation
and transition as affected areas, including comparative information and the transition date.
These are source-supported descriptions of the Ministry’s explanation. The operative 2020 and
2025 notices have not yet been directly verified in this collection, so the explanation does not
supply the detailed conditions needed to implement those choices (Ministry of Finance of the
People’s Republic of China, Accounting Department, 2025, answer on the relationship between
the notice and the standard).
Ping An provides a concrete adoption history. Its 2022 ASBE annual report states that the group
would adopt the revised insurance standard for accounting years beginning on 1 January 2023.
Its 2023 report confirms adoption on that date and restatement of comparative information
(Ping An Insurance (Group) Company of China, Ltd., 2023, Note XIV.2, pp. 315–316) (Ping
An Insurance (Group) Company of China, Ltd., 2024, Note IV.34, p. 192). The following
