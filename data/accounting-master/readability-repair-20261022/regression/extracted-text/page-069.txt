3.11
Three meanings of disclosure coverage
The initial Apple annual/interim preparation inventories 125 quantities, 13 annual notes, 10
interim notes, 70 exhibit references and seven further package obligations. In that starting
assessment, all 23 notes retain unresolved semantic content; none is recorded as fully formalised.
The later worked readings now trace representative revenue, commitment, asset, tax and funding
consequences, while retaining missing facts and wider package obligations. Downloading a note,
reviewing its material passages and using it in a complete company model remain different
achievements.
Three populations make that distinction inspectable. Retained-unit coverage partitions acquired
documents into nonoverlapping pages or smaller leaf units, including duplicates and unreadable
material. Assertion-review coverage counts the separately enumerated material assertions and
their review decisions. Query-exposure coverage identifies the sources, legal facts, methods
and authority that a particular result requires. A fully downloaded annual report can have
incomplete assertion review, and a reviewed debt total can leave a refinancing query unresolved
because an incorporated agreement is missing. No weighted average of these populations
establishes completeness.
Preparation begins with declared annual and interim roots and a policy for following incorporated
references. Each edge is recorded as satisfied, duplicate or cyclic, outside the declared scope with
a reason, inaccessible, or still unexpanded. A bounded traversal saves its unexplored frontier
and budget so it can resume. An unknown external extent remains unknown. Acquired proxy
and registration documents improve the Apple record, but do not prove that every executed
amendment, issuance term and exhibit dependency has been closed. The present real package
remains incomplete for that reason.
Container choice can itself create a false missing-source diagnosis. Apple’s retained annual
PDF contains securities and award exhibits absent from the retained HTML container; other
incorporated award forms are present in earlier reports in the retained corpus. Locating those
bodies resolves a source-access question. It still leaves their qualifications and outgoing references
to be interpreted. Conversely, the cash restriction in Section 2.9 shows why a located note can
change the meaning of an already extracted figure: correct arithmetic alone would not detect
an erroneous “unrestricted” label.
