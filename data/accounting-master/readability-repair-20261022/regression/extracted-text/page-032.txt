The quarter preserves several distinctions that a common schema might otherwise erase. The
annual equity table contains a common-stock issuance row and the annual cash-flow table
contains a term-debt issuance row; the interim tables omit both. The interim reported totals
reconcile over their own disclosed rows. A comparison record may describe those omissions, but
must not manufacture two issuer-reported zeros. The prior-year quarter’s term-debt repayment
dash is likewise retained in its own dated column (Apple Inc., 2025c, printed pp. 4–5).
Transaction uncertainty remains across the joined interval. In the current quarter, the receivable
change plus its indirect adjustment leaves −174; inventory leaves −160. The payable change
is −7,050, differing from the −6,671 adjustment by −379. Cash dividends exceed declarations
by 37, and cash repurchases exceed the equity reduction by 130. None identifies the missing
settlement, valuation or timing components. The subsequent note inquiries must retain these
relationships together when testing historical alternatives. Combining individually compatible
note examples does not by itself establish that they share a compatible cash, income and equity
history.
2.11
Apple: revenue recognition and cash collection
An iPhone sale can create revenue today and an obligation to provide services later. Apple’s
annual revenue note separates delivered hardware and bundled software from the right to
receive product-related services such as iCloud, Siri and Maps. It allocates the price and
related discounts by relative stand-alone selling prices. For the bundled services those prices
are estimates, because observable separate prices are unavailable. The hardware portion is
recognised when control transfers, generally on shipment; the service portion is deferred and
recognised straight-line over the estimated period of provision (Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 2,
printed p. 35). A historical model consequently needs the allocation and service cohort as well
as the sale date and amount. Neither the product label nor the customer payment supplies
those missing terms.
