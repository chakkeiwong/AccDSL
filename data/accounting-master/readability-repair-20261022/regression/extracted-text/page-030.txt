Other comprehensive income supplies the next connection. Foreign-currency translation con-
tributes 395 after tax. Derivative fair-value changes of −832 and reclassification adjustments
of −1,337 contribute −2,169; marketable-debt fair-value changes of 5,850 and reclassifications
of 204 contribute 6,054. Thus OCI is 395 −2,169 + 6,054 = 4,280, and total comprehensive
income is 93,736 + 4,280 = 98,016 (Apple Inc., 2024a, printed p. 30). Adding the period OCI
to opening accumulated other comprehensive loss of −11,452 gives the closing −7,172. That
accumulated balance, rather than the period flow, enters the balance sheet.
Despite positive comprehensive income, total equity falls. The equity statement explains the
difference: common-stock issuance adds 1,423 and share-based compensation adds 12,034;
withholding reduces the capital component by 3,993 and retained earnings by 1,612; declarations
reduce retained earnings by 15,218 and repurchases by 95,846 (Apple Inc., 2024a, printed p. 32).
These movements, apart from comprehensive income, total −103,212. The complete equity
reconciliation is therefore
62,146 + 98,016 + 1,423 + 12,034 −3,993 −1,612 −15,218 −95,846 = 56,950.
The components reconcile separately: capital rises from 73,812 to 83,276, the accumulated
deficit moves from −214 to −19,154, and accumulated other comprehensive loss moves from
−11,452 to −7,172. These are the three closing equity components reported on the balance
sheet. Their agreement prevents a model from explaining the correct total with the wrong
allocation among capital, retained earnings and accumulated OCI.

Cash follows a different reconciliation. Starting from the same net income, the cash-flow
statement adds depreciation and amortisation of 11,445, share-based compensation expense
of 11,688 and other adjustments of −2,266. These three adjustments sum to 20,867. Its six
operating-asset and liability adjustments are net receivables −3,788, vendor non-trade receivables
−1,356, inventory −1,046, other assets −11,731, payables +6,020 and other liabilities +15,552.
They sum to 3,651, giving operating cash of 93,736 + 20,867 + 3,651 = 118,254 (Apple Inc.,
2024a, printed p. 33). The full indirect bridge is thus present; none of its broad adjustment
rows has been replaced by a presumed change in a similarly named balance-sheet account.
