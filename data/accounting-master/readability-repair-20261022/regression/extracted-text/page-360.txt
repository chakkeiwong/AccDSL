E2. Reconstruction, query and conditional accounting
Use the same admitted evidence, finite template registry and unknown domains for an enumer-
ated exact reference, a transparent typed-library model, the plain proposed reconstruction and
any pruning/abstraction extension. Small rational polyhedra and finite history trees provide
exact known feasible and infeasible cases. Test both soundness and completeness within that
declared finite scope: each returned history satisfies every constraint, each reference history
remains represented, and each query interval agrees with independently derived bounds. The
continuous floating-share example has interest in [5,7]; duplicate encodings must leave that
interval and any separately supplied probability measure unchanged.
Include cross-statement dependence, open endpoints, contradictory evidence, unknown solver
results, interrupted enumeration, unsupported nonlinear terms, correction/replay and failed
funding. The oracle for an interrupted case is an explicit incomplete result, not an invented full
answer. Check journal and statement invariants plus economic timing and conditional response.
A timeout is a resource outcome; representing it as infeasible is a correctness failure. On actual
companies, evaluate evidential compatibility and retained alternatives; the hidden true journal
is generally unavailable. Exact small cases do not prove completeness of unrestricted histories
or the correctness of an entire solver.
