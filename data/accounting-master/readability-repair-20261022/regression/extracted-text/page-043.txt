If a scenario assumes a 10% decline in Greater China iPhone sales alone, the two illustrative fiscal
2024 matrices imply 3,600 and 3,800 million less sales, respectively. The scenario percentage is
a teaching assumption, not an estimated shock; neither sales change determines profit, cash
flow, currency translation or future behaviour. The gap between those two conditional answers
comes from the unobserved joint allocation, even before cost responses are considered. A
reproducible model should retain the reported marginals, the qualitative mix statement, any
admitted cross-classification and its source, and the unresolved allocation when the evidence
does not determine it.

2.15
From geographic sales to legal entities
A regional sales forecast still leaves open which company sells the goods, owns the inventory or
owes the supplier. Those identities matter when a scenario changes a particular entity’s tax,
funding or supply arrangements. Apple’s geographic segments cannot answer that question:
their sales are generally attributed by customer location and retail-store geography.
The
consolidation policy and subsidiary exhibit supply a different kind of information, about the
companies represented in the group statements.
Annual Note 1 says that the consolidated financial statements include the accounts of Apple Inc.
and its wholly owned subsidiaries. The filing also defines “Company” and “Apple” collectively
in that way, unless otherwise stated. Interim Note 1 repeats the wholly-owned scope for its
condensed statements and requires them to be read with the fiscal 2024 annual statements
and notes (Apple Inc., 2024a, definition of Company and Note 1, printed pp. 1 and 34) (Apple
Inc., 2025c, Note 1, printed p. 5). This is positive evidence about the reported consolidation
perimeter. It is not a set of separate parent-company accounts, nor does it identify every legal
entity or ownership link within that perimeter.
