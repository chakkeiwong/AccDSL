10.3
A plan’s capacity is different from an employee’s award
The share register has already separated awards, vesting and common shares issued. The
documents governing an award explain why those quantities can move differently. Apple’s 4
March 2022 Form 8-K supplies the 2022 Employee Stock Plan, a time-based restricted-stock-unit
form and a performance-award form as Exhibits 10.1–10.3. These are the counterparts of three
references in the annual report. The filing reports shareholder approval and effectiveness on 4
March 2022. The plan also records Board adoption on 9 November 2021 and uses that earlier
date as its defined Effective Date when setting its term. The two dates perform different jobs;
replacing them with one undifferentiated date would lose information needed to interpret the
plan (Apple Inc., 2022, Items 5.02 and 5.07; Exhibit 10.1, Sections 1 and 15(a)).
Start with the capacity to make awards. Section 5(a) supplies 510 million shares plus specified
amounts available or recycled from the 2014 plan, subject to a maximum of 1,274,374,682. That
maximum is neither the current unused capacity nor a promise to issue that many common
shares. Section 5(b) counts a full-value award at two reserve shares for each underlying share,
whereas exercised options and stock appreciation rights use one reserve share for each share to
which the exercise relates. Options and stock appreciation rights count their gross underlying
amount even when fewer shares are delivered (Apple Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.1, Section 5(a)–(b)).

were initially charged against the reserve at two for one, all 100 vest and 20 shares are withheld
for employee taxes. Assume no other adjustments. The employee receives 80 shares. Under the
plan’s full-value withholding rule, the 20 withheld shares return 40 reserve shares, because the
original charge used that ratio. The two quantities are therefore

The reserve records authority used, rather than shares in the employee’s account. An option
exercise covering 100 shares can also deliver 80 after withholding, yet uses 100 reserve shares
under the gross-count rule; the option withholding does not restore capacity. The same observed
delivery therefore cannot identify the reserve movement without the award type and counting
rule. Cash settlement creates another distinction: the plan excludes the shares that would
otherwise have been delivered from its share count. These are consequences of Section 5(b),
not an assumed employee tax rate, cash payment or compensation expense (Apple Inc., 2022,
Exhibit 10.1, Section 5(b)).
