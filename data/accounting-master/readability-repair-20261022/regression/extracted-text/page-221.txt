The group record therefore preserves each gross exposure’s identity, reporting entity, currency,
risk, amount, recognition period and admitted valuation, plus the strategy, designation, in-
strument, effectiveness calculation and reserve history. A layer also needs an identified and
tracked overall pool and the conditions in 6.6.3. A group without offsetting risks apportions
the instrument’s net gain or loss systematically to the affected lines without grossing it up; a
fair-value hedge adjusts each relevant asset or liability separately (European Commission, 2023a,
IFRS 9.6.6.3–5, B6.6.11–16). A group of items is not permission to ignore consolidated-group
external-party requirements: retain 6.2.3 and 6.3.5–6’s distinct tests. These methods need
explicit successor records and complete exchanges; the existing closed financing profiles are
preserved until those additions are specified.
