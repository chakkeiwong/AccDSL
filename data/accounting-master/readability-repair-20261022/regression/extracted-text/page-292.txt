At the financial observation, the future-service fee change is the change in the insurer’s 10%
share less the change in the top-up value (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2022f,
paragraph 45 and B111–B114). In the up state this is 2 −(0 −9) = 11, increasing CSM from 8
to 19. Fulfilment cash flows become 108+2+1 = 111 and the liability becomes 130. Investment
gain 20 and insurance finance expense 20 offset.
In the down state the fee change is −2−(18−9) = −11. The existing CSM absorbs 8; the excess
3 becomes a service loss. Fulfilment cash flows and the liability are 90 + 2 + 1 = 93, with CSM
zero and a loss component of 3 within that liability. Investment loss 20 and insurance finance
income 20 offset, while the service loss reduces equity to 47. The loss component does not
create a second liability, and the binding floor does not reopen the inception VFA classification
(Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2022f, paragraphs 45, 48–52, 87(c) and B102).
Completing the service interval releases the expected mortality cost, risk adjustment and
remaining CSM. Up-state revenue is 2 + 1 + 19 = 22. In the down state the service-related
release of 3 reverses the loss component, leaving revenue zero. Actual mortality expense is
either zero or 20, first recognised through the incurred-claim liability and then paid. Selling the
underlying and paying the investment component of 108 or 90 completes all balances.

The variable fee separates investment returns from service
The preceding guarantee valuation measures a contractual payoff. Direct participation adds a
service question: which changes belong to the insurer’s unearned fee? Admission requires all
three B101 conditions, assessed using expectations at inception: an enforceable share of a clearly
identified pool, a substantial share of fair-value returns payable to policyholders, and substantial
variability of their payments with those returns. The assessment considers the contract’s
duration and probability-weighted present values, including scenarios in which a guarantee
binds. It is reassessed only upon a relevant modification. Reinsurance issued or held cannot
qualify as direct participation under B109 (European Commission, 2023a, IFRS 17.B101–109).
