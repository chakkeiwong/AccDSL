The tax discussion says the rate reconciliation will include additional information and detail for
items meeting a quantitative threshold. Taxes paid will be disaggregated into federal, state and
foreign taxes, with further detail for significant individual jurisdictions. It gives neither the
threshold value nor the future jurisdictional amounts. Those cash-tax populations also remain
distinct from tax expense, payable and deferred balances; their reconciliation is developed in
Section 9.7. An announced disclosure change cannot be used to allocate an old tax total among
jurisdictions by assumption.
The earlier segment discussion concerns expenses that are significant and regularly provided to
the chief operating decision maker, together with that person’s title, position and use of segment
profit or loss in assessing performance and allocating resources. That anticipated disclosure
could answer questions about the information used by management. It did not yet identify the
regional allocation of corporate expenses or supply segment assets. The adoption sentence was
a dated plan at that information cutoff. The retained fiscal 2025 annual report now lets us
check what followed.
Apple reports that it adopted ASU 2023-07 for its 2025 annual period using a retrospective
transition method (Apple Inc., 2025a, Note 1). Its Note 13 presents 2024 and 2023 comparative
segment expenses. Take the Americas in fiscal 2024. The earlier Note 13 reports net sales
of 167,045 million and operating income of 67,656 million, but no separate regional cost of
sales or selling-and-marketing amount (Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 13, printed p. 47). The later
comparative gives 89,587 million of cost of sales and 9,802 million of selling and marketing for
that same region and fiscal year (Apple Inc., 2025a, Note 13, printed p. 47). The displayed
relationship is
167,045 −89,587 −9,802 = 67,656
(USD millions).
The 2025 table gives corresponding expense rows for the other four regions. Together, their
disclosed selling-and-marketing expenses total 18,639 million. The five operating incomes still
sum to 162,044 million; after the displayed corporate research and development of 31,370 million
and general and administrative amount of 7,458 million, the company reports the same 123,216
million of operating income. The 2024 report called its 7,458 million line “other corporate
expenses, net” and qualified its contents. The identical displayed amount is not, by itself, proof
that the two labels have identical scope. Both original and later assertions retain their source
identities.
This later presentation answers a prior-period disclosure question without identifying new
fiscal-2024 transactions or creating a correction journal. Reading the named 2024 source leaves
the regional expense detail unresolved; reading the later source supplies its 2025 comparative
assertions. An investor-information view additionally needs evidence that the exact chosen
source version was publicly available by its requested cutoff. The accepted corpus and filing-event
metadata do not prove that for the retained 2024 PDF or the later issuer-hosted HTML. Nor
do the new regional expense rows reveal the cross-classified product-by-region sales, entity cash
allocation, or behavioural response needed by the earlier regional stress example.
