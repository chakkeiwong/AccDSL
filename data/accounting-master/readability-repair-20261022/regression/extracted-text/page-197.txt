FINANCING, OWNERSHIP AND THE GROUP BOUNDARY

The 2024 general form does not remove the earlier timing distinction. Section 8 still provides that
qualifying death or disability before vesting allows the performance-qualified award, multiplied
by the severance fraction, to vest on the Vesting Date. Section 7 requires share delivery as
soon as administratively practical thereafter, within two and one-half months and subject
to its conditions. Thus neither inspected general form supplies a rule to deliver the shares
immediately on severance. The proxy’s description of accelerated vesting remains a summary
whose application to a particular award needs further evidence (Apple Inc., 2024a, Exhibit
10.20, Sections 7–8) (Apple Inc., 2025b, printed p. 53). Later filing alone is not evidence that
an earlier grant was amended.
The CEO forms change the surviving entitlement. In both the September 2022 and September
2024 versions, Section 8(b) allows unvested units to remain eligible for the Vesting Date following
death, disability, or qualifying retirement, subject to the Committee’s stated authority. These
clauses do not apply the general form’s severance fraction. Retirement combines an age of
at least 60 and at least ten years of service with the stated service scope; the retirement
exception normally also requires termination on or after the first anniversary of the Award Date.
Earlier retirement does not receive that exception unless the Committee determines otherwise.
Continued eligibility still depends on performance and does not make the eventual number of
shares known on departure (Apple Inc., 2023, Exhibit 10.2, Sections 3–4 and 8) (Apple Inc.,
2024a, Exhibit 10.22, Sections 3–4 and 8).
To isolate the effect, reuse the hypothetical 1,000-unit target and a performance multiplier of
1.5, with no modification, recoupment or other condition changing the result. Suppose death or
disability occurs at a point giving a general-form severance fraction of 3/5. Under the general
form, 1,000 × 1.5 × 3/5 = 900 units remain eligible to vest. Under the CEO form’s stated
continuation provision, all 1,000 × 1.5 = 1,500 units remain eligible. With a negative-return
cap reducing the multiplier to one, the corresponding quantities are 600 and 1,000. These
comparisons hold the performance result fixed to expose the contract difference; they are not
facts about any executive’s award.
The difference carries into cash rights without creating an earlier payment. With the same
hypothetical $250 dividend-equivalent credit established before departure, the uncapped gross
payments are $225 under the general-form fraction and $375 under the CEO continuation
provision. The units and cash rights remain linked to the same applicable terms and performance
conditions. The CEO forms’ Section 7 anchors delivery to the Vesting Date, and Section 5(b)
ties dividend equivalents to the award’s vesting and payment terms. Moving those amounts to
the severance date would change the specified cash and share path (Apple Inc., 2023, Exhibit
10.2, Sections 5(b), 7–8) (Apple Inc., 2024a, Exhibits 10.20 and 10.22, Sections 5(b), 7–8).
A second boundary is independent of the performance calculation. An employee aged 60 with ten
years of service has not necessarily satisfied the CEO form’s first-award-anniversary condition.
Conversely, passing that anniversary does not establish the age and service conditions. The
record therefore needs the applicable agreement, Award Date, termination date and reason,
service history and any relevant Committee decision. Unknown values remain unknown; they
cannot be converted into automatic eligibility or forfeiture.
The later forms retain unfilled performance schedules and the same kinds of return, comparison-
group and adjustment dependencies. They do not fill the rank convention or establish the
actual October 2023 award terms by themselves (Apple Inc., 2024a, Exhibits 10.20 and 10.22,
Exhibit A). The appropriate result of this comparison is a narrower conditional model: the
general and CEO forms support different survival rules, both preserve later delivery, and the
proxy’s timing description still needs an award-specific explanation. A source-reference match
establishes which form was retained; applicability establishes which form can govern a financial
path.
