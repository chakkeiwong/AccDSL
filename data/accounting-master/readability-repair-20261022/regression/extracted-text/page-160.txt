settlement terms and joint branch identity is therefore necessary for those queries.
The actual following quarter constrains a proposed continuation without identifying it. Apple
reports PP&E payments of 2,940, operating cash of 29,935, investing cash of 9,792 and total
cash growth of 356 in the quarter ended 28 December 2024 (Apple Inc., 2025c, printed p. 5). If
both hypothetical obligations settled in that quarter, the remaining PP&E payments would
have to be 1,858 under History 1 or 858 under History 2. The rest of the company would have to
contribute operating cash of 31,935 or 30,935, and investing cash of 10,874 or 11,874, respectively.
After unchanged financing cash of −39,371, either required remainder has cash growth of 3,438;
subtracting the hypothetical 3,082 settlement recovers the reported 356. These are necessary
conditions on unconstructed remainders. Positive remaining payments and a matching cash
identity do not establish the underlying transactions or prove that either continuation fits the
complete quarterly record.
This calculation extends the conditional history into explicit journals and statement effects
while keeping actual-history admission unresolved. If cash is insufficient, the model must retain
the resulting funding or default question; it cannot invent a loan to preserve the path. A later
depreciation or production query requires different information again: asset cohorts, remaining
lives, residual values, capacity and tax bases. The settlement example supplies none of those
quantities. A change in the query must therefore reopen the state-sufficiency check, even when
the annual statements and the final cash total coincide.
9.6
Lease and tax integration
A lease connects asset use, a financing obligation and income tax. The following schedule
extends the operating-company example by separating these three measurements. The retained
lease and tax dossiers supply the dated provisions, modification case and assumptions. The
official lease compilation is dated December 2023. The right-of-use (ROU) asset represents
the admitted use of the leased asset. Its cost, the lease liability, accumulated depreciation,
remaining payments, rate version and option judgments are separate state fields. Tax retains
carrying amounts, legal tax bases, reversal dates, enacted/substantively-enacted rate evidence,
recovery support, and original profit/OCI/equity allocation. The deferred tax asset (DTA)
and deferred tax liability (DTL) are retained gross before any permitted offset presentation. A
liability schedule is not a tax-base schedule.
For the two annual 121 payments at 10%, initial liability and ROU are 210. Year-one interest is
21, liability after payment 110, depreciation 105 and ROU 105. Year two interest is 11, and
both balances finish at zero. A same-asset modification just after year one creates two future
payments 144 at revised 20%, PV 220, so liability increases 110 and ROU increases 110 to 215.
A payment index reset has different rate rules; it cannot reuse that modification blindly.
