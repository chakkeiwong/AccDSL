Joint products and delayed supplier payment
The joint-production record retains inseparable joint costs, split-off quantities, separate sub-
sequent costs, allocation-basis evidence, selling values/dates and NRV. For joint cost 300, A
60 at split-off price 4 and B 40 at 9 give values 240/360. The selected rational and consistent
relative-sales method assigns 120/180, or unit costs 2/4.5. Selling 30 A and 10 B gives revenue
210, cost 105 and profit 105. Opening cash 500 leaves cash 410, unsold A 60/B 135 and
equity 605. Weighting by mass instead changes the admitted policy; equal weights are not a
missing-value fix.
Separately, production 300 with an independently established immaterial byproduct of NRV 20
allocates main inventory 280 and byproduct 20. Sale of that byproduct for 20 removes its 20
carrying amount; it earns no margin here. Opening cash 500 ends cash 220+main 280=equity
500. Retaining main 300 as well as byproduct 20 invents cost; deducting 20 without recording
the unsold byproduct loses an asset.
The supplier record names the original creditor, normal-credit purchase price, actual terms,
financing assessment, initial liability, effective rate, payment schedule and actual payments.
Normal cash price 100 and a fixed payment 121 in two years imply 10% effective interest under
the supplied terms. Record inventory and payable 100; interest 10 then 11 raises the liability
121. Ordinary inventory is not a qualifying asset in this case. Sell for cash 150 in year 1: opening
cash/equity 100 becomes cash 250, payable 110 and equity 140. Year 2 interest 11 and payment
121 leave cash/equity 129; lifetime profit 29. Under the explicit historical IAS 7 election, both
original supplier payment and interest are operating cash. Year 1 indirect 40+payable increase
110=150; year 2 −11 −110= −121. A substituted finance provider or altered arrangement
requires reclassification analysis.
Uncertain tax has its own measurement decision
Uncertainty about whether a tax authority will accept a treatment affects both current tax and
the tax bases used for deferred tax. The calculation must retain gross DTA/DTL, recoverability,
rate changes, lease interactions and the original profit, OCI or equity allocation. It also needs a
measurement decision about the uncertain treatment. Under the historical EU IFRIC 23.6–14
(European Commission, 2023a, IFRIC 23.6–14,A1–A5) method, the uncertain-tax record adds
treatment grouping, filing position, authority/examination scope, full-information acceptance
finding, candidate outcomes, predictive-measure choice, consistent current/deferred bases,
reassessment event and disclosure obligations. Assume examination with full knowledge; do not
multiply exposure by an estimated chance of avoiding examination.
