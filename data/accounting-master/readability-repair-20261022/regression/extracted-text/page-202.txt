A hedge reserve is neither cash nor a second gain
Suppose a company hedges a highly probable inventory purchase.
A derivative gain can
arrive before the inventory affects earnings. Cash-flow hedge accounting preserves that timing
through a separate equity reserve, but only after the relationship qualifies. The designation
must identify an eligible instrument and item, the hedged risk, the risk-management objective
and the assessment method at inception. An economic relationship, the absence of credit-
risk dominance and an appropriate actual hedge ratio are separate requirements (Australian
Accounting Standards Board, 2022h, 6.2–6.4 and B6.4.1–19). Favorable price correlation alone
does not establish them. Unknown designation or valuation information remains unresolved; it
cannot be repaired by assigning zero or placing a gain in other comprehensive income.
Take one documented, offsetting exposure and a derivative worth zero at designation. The
illustration includes the whole derivative, no excluded option or forward component and no
intermediate reserve release. Let Dt be its cumulative gain, including settled gains, and Ht the
cumulative change in the hedged item’s present value attributable to the designated risk. Their
signs refer to their economic values. With admitted offsetting changes, the cumulative effective
gain Et and ineffectiveness It are
Et = sgn(Dt) min{|Dt|, |Ht|},
