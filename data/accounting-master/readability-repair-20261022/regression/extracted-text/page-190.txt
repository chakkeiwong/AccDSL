10.2.10
Apple: shares through time
Apple’s disclosures show why the ownership record needs dates as well as quantities. In fiscal
2024, its opening 15,550,061 thousand common shares, less 499,372 thousand repurchased and
plus 66,097 thousand issued net of employee-tax withholding, give 15,116,786 thousand closing
shares. That bridge accounts for the change in the reported stock. It does not say how long each
share participated in the year’s earnings. The reported basic weighted average is 15,343,783
thousand shares (Apple Inc., 2024a, Notes 3 and 10, printed pp. 36 and 44).
The unit conversion matters before any timing inference. Net income is 93,736 million dollars,
whereas the denominator is in thousands of shares. Thus 93,736 × 1,000/15,343,783 ≈6.109
dollars per share, consistent with reported basic EPS of $6.11. Using closing shares instead gives
about $6.201: it computes a different ratio. Adding the reported 64,312 thousand incremental
shares from dilutive awards gives the diluted denominator of 15,408,095 thousand and EPS
of about $6.084, reported as $6.08. These are checks using displayed amounts. Conditional
nearest-rounding intervals also overlap the reported two-decimal EPS; the issuer’s tie convention
and undisclosed exact amounts remain unverified.
The first quarter of fiscal 2025 makes the distinction again. Income of 36,330 million divided by
15,081,724 thousand basic shares, with the same factor of 1,000, gives about $2.409. The diluted
denominator adds 69,141 thousand shares and gives about $2.398, consistent with the reported
$2.41 and $2.40 (Apple Inc., 2025c, Note 3, printed p. 7). Those denominators describe three
months; adding them to other quarterly averages would not produce an annual share count.
Period lengths and consistent measurement rules are needed to combine averages.
