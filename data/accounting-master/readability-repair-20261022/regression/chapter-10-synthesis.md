# Chapter 10 — financing, ownership and the group boundary

Version 3.47, physical pages 177–243, read in order. This is author diagnosis;
independent reader, domain, implementability and human acceptance remain pending.

The chapter extends the operating-company histories into financing and group
reporting. Its recurring question is substantive: which rights, dates and
populations determine a financial consequence when a cash total or balanced
journal cannot identify it? The debt opening makes the distinction concrete.
Contractual principal, carrying value and cash flows measure different things;
a net borrowing figure can conceal an intraperiod funding need. Apple's reported
movements constrain a reconstruction without supplying every transaction.

Ownership then adds dates and attribution. Basic EPS uses weighted actual
shares and earnings belonging to ordinary holders. Potential shares require
complete assumed earnings effects and a dilution rule. The fixed-additive
selector has a local derivation; later participation and shared-bonus examples
show why its assumptions matter. Capped class rights change the numerator when
shares are added. A shared bonus makes isolated conversion effects nonadditive.
The manuscript correctly keeps a computed conditional world separate from an
unresolved selection rule. A hypothetical distribution tax benefit changes the
EPS allocation without creating an actual tax asset or dividend journal.

The Apple material connects those abstract requirements to reported quantities
and governing documents. Awards, issued shares, plan reserves, equity amounts,
cash payments and expense remain distinct populations. The performance
schedule explains its percentile scale, interpolation and threshold jump before
adding the negative-return cap and employment conditions. Later agreement
versions narrow survival rights without asserting that they govern every
individual grant. Contractual averages and dates are consequential, not merely
metadata. The general IFRS award methods are explicitly separate from Apple's
US GAAP reporting and use declared synthetic inputs.

The long hedge sequence is cumulative rather than a list of disconnected
entries. It first distinguishes effective gains, ineffectiveness, cash and
inventory basis. Excluded option cost then needs a separate reserve. Different
actual and hedged strikes expose a balanced but incorrectly measured result.
Rebalancing retains layer origins; acquired derivatives retain their paid
starting values; instrument reductions preserve earlier gains while changing
future designation. Stopping a purchase portion instead freezes its reserve
and introduces expectation and recovery questions. The origin-matched case
derives one limited attribution and leaves general heterogeneous allocation
open. Purchase, partial sale, write-down and recovery carry the retained
history into inventory without releasing the hedge reserve a second time.
Net hedges finally show why gross statement lines and reporting periods remain
necessary even when lifetime profit is unchanged.

Consolidation applies the same reasoning to reporting populations. Control
requires evidence beyond ownership percentage. The foreign subsidiary's
acquisition, operation, translation and disposal distinguish parent and NCI
rights, goodwill translation, cash exchange effects and reserve recycling.
Several wrong routes reach the same final equity or gain, so the intermediate
statements do real explanatory work. The binding section then explains why
legal books, control intervals, rate units, scenario branches and atomic
publication must preserve those distinctions. It supplies documentary behavior
without claiming the storage implementation has been tested.

The final currency sections retain the distinction between an eliminated loan
and the exposure it financed. Matching requires identified transactions rather
than equal balances. Purchasing-power restatement precedes translation, with
dated monetary exposure and first-application tax treated explicitly. A quoted
rate is assessed for the required direction, date and purpose under the
identified later amendment. Conditional numerical consequences do not establish
that the supplied estimate satisfies its measurement objective.

Equations generally follow defined quantities, assumptions and a motivating
numerical question. Their interpretations and counterexamples distinguish
mathematical identity from admissibility, source authority and completeness.
The chapter is substantial, but its sequence supplies prerequisites for the
later extensions: removing those stages would lose the reason to retain their
state. No additional local defect was confirmed. The earlier undefined theta
on physical page 88, R347-D01, remains queued for repair after full diagnosis.

This reading supports the chapter's internal argument, not standards
compliance, whole-company completeness or working implementation. All 53
obligations, six interactions and 46 operations remain, with zero whole-row
closures and not_ready. Continue with Chapter 11's bank and credit-loss methods.
