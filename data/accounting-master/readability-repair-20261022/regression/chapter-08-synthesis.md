# Chapter 8: from a filing to a committed financial event

Physical pages 105–112 give the formal and language contracts an operational
order. A filing manifest preserves original bytes and extraction versions;
proposed corrections require reviewed admission. Reconciliation leaves a debt
residual unresolved unless its economic classification is supported. The
construction procedure then shares variables and constraints across the whole
model, retains continuous families and records limits on finite search. A
timeout is not evidence that the family is impossible.

Run conditioning adds assumptions without turning them into historical facts.
The missing-maturity case shows why a useful forecast can be conditional while
the record remains incomplete. Event scheduling then separates occurrence,
accrual and settlement time. A deterministic tie-break cannot settle an ordering
that changes financial meaning. These prerequisites make the atomic execution
pseudocode understandable before its detailed transaction contract appears.

The authorization and lost-acknowledgement examples are the chapter's central
procedural tests. A delivery requires a supported event basis and treatment;
an assessment is not itself permission to post. The journal and independent
economic delta bind to the same basis. Figure 8.1 makes the progression to the
transaction visible. The next section shows why business-event identity must
remain stable while the full request fingerprint detects changes. Validating
the original bindings before lookup, then checking replay before current-head
freshness, lets an exact retry return its original receipt without duplicating
revenue. Unknown timeout status preserves the original request.

The financial examples connect these controls to their purpose. The original
fixed/floating pair produces different interest, cash and equity outcomes while
both balance sheets balance. The bond schedule separates noncash accretion,
face amount, carrying value, fair value and redemption; the equity example
separates proceeds from share counts and legal capital. Reporting follows the
actual events and chosen treatment, and provenance explains derivation without
establishing the economic model's correctness.

Northstar's operation table supplies a bounded company package with explicit
preconditions beside journal entries. Its reference role is justified by the
preceding explanations and complete examples. The final scenario section
enforces Chapter 6's distinctions among stress, conditioning and intervention.
It also separates available decision information from the scheduler's knowledge
of a future path and keeps nested valuation randomness distinct from realised
outer outcomes. Funding failure retains economic obligations; unsupported
methods preserve incomplete dependencies and the last committed state.

No new confirmed defect was found. The tables, diagram and pseudocode were
readable, and the stable-key display has its explicit judgment. The transition
to accounting methods is justified because the executor still needs supported
treatments beyond the teaching package. This is an author review of the design,
not runtime execution, independent domain acceptance or a claim that every
accounting module has been implemented.
