The chapter starts with an insurer's substantive timing problem: premium cash,
service revenue, claims and profit follow different schedules. The short-coverage
cohort introduces gross and held balances before the general-measurement cases
add fulfilment cash flows, risk adjustment and contractual service margin. A
zero-rate case isolates service and loss ordering; the nonzero-rate case then
shows why revenue and finance must be reconciled together. This progression
supplies prerequisites rather than placing unexplained formulas after an easy
opening. Future-service revisions, incurred claims and financial changes have
different destinations even when they alter the same total liability.

The connected-group example makes the whole-company requirement concrete.
A profitable group cannot lend its unearned margin to an onerous group. A held
treaty has its own value, entry date and loss-recovery component. Financing
changes the loss component before service releases it. The wrong pooled
calculation and a late treaty can reach the same terminal cash while reporting
different intermediate equity. The following publication section therefore has
a financial reason for requiring complete declared dependencies and agreement
between journals and component memory. Exact retries return earlier receipts
without replacing today's state. Those are documentary operation requirements,
not evidence of storage isolation or crash recovery.

PAA extensions distinguish coverage length, premium timing and claim settlement.
A one-year policy can still produce a claim requiring two-year discounting.
Acquisition-cost elections change interim results; claim-incurrence rates can
govern the stated profit allocation even when inception rates differ. Reinsurance
collectibility and direct-participation eligibility remain separate findings.
The minimum-benefit example then creates a visible need for valuation. Two-state

with a physical expected payment. Mortality, risk adjustment and insurance
admission have their own inputs. Four conditional histories carry that same
measurement through service and payment, preserving the downside outcomes.

The bootstrap consistency discussion then separates conditioning on a latest

Risk measures add another choice. The atom-aware expected-shortfall formula and

different targets. Additive revision variances require true conditional-mean

charges then need their own basis, rate and payment timing. None of those

Acquisition costs and transition reconnect the technical methods to reported
