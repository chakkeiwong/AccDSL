# Cumulative argument through Chapter 12

The continuous Version 3.47 reading now reaches physical page 336. The argument
still begins with what public accounts can establish about a company history
and its future consequences. Matching journals or totals can conceal different
economic exposures. The initial company examples and Apple inquiry motivate
joint compatible histories, source-linked assertions and explicit unknowns.
They do not supply probabilities merely by leaving several histories possible.

The evidence, literature and architecture chapters turn that problem into a
design with bounded roles. Document coverage differs from semantic completeness
and query answerability. Corrected evidence preserves prior versions. The
literature contributes tools for conservation, incomplete information,
provenance, inference, contracts and numerical solving without making any one
tool a complete company model. Typed records and a DSL must still justify their
value against a capable typed library.

The mathematical and procedural chapters supply the next prerequisites.
Compatibility sets, queries, nullspaces, conservation and conditional simulation
define what histories mean. A measure must be justified before a family becomes
a probabilistic mixture. Numerical domains and rounding belong to the result.
Types preserve contexts and kinds of missingness; byte identity does not prove
economic equivalence. Evidence admission, posting authority and atomic events
then connect those meanings to a company package. Lost acknowledgements require
preserved request identity and uncertain commit state until reconciliation.

The operating and financing chapters give those abstractions substantive work.
Earned service, refunds, asset components, production, pensions, impairment and
tax carry distinct populations and dates. Financing adds contractual cash,
effective interest, actual and potential shares, award terms, hedge origins and
ownership attribution. Conditional EPS worlds do not automatically determine
an admissible selection rule. Different hedge strikes, designation origins and
inventory histories require different calculations even when journals balance.
Consolidation, translation and purchasing-power adjustments preserve legal-book
events separately from changes in reporting population. Real disclosures bound
these examples without revealing every hidden transaction or contract.

Banking extends the same logic to credit and settlement. Reported parent and
group balances, debt rights, guarantees and capital rules have different scopes.
Loan classification precedes measurement. Draw conditioning retains original
credit history; revised forecasts, significant risk, impairment and cure are
different findings. Purchased-impaired assets require a credit-adjusted rate and
signed subsequent-loss adjustments. Dated receipts expose numerical uncertainty
in both fractional powers and original rates. Those bounds describe one
calculation, not independent economic possibilities. Transfers retain guarantees
and option rights through their appropriate measurements. Facility capacity,
requests and confirmed cash cannot be interchanged. A capital ratio does not
establish ability to make a deposit payment.

Insurance deepens the time and information argument. Premium cash, service,
claims, finance and unearned profit follow different schedules. Separate issued
groups and held treaties remain distinct inside a common balance sheet. Their
component dependencies make atomic publication consequential. Guarantee pricing
needs market-consistent valuation, while mortality and other physical risks need
their own laws. A reserve estimate, predictive payment distribution and insurer
compensation for risk are three different quantities. The manuscript derives
the distinctions through finite counterexamples, conditional laws and explicit
variance calculations rather than treating a clean numerical result as evidence
for all three.

The sector chapters also return to implementation without breaking the argument.
Numerically balanced entries can still use the wrong interest basis, pool
incompatible groups, add a tracked component twice or lose old OCI origins.
An adapter can return a complete-looking array after turning failed draws into
zeros. Exact requests, typed identities, retained component histories and explicit
unresolved states therefore follow from financial and statistical consequences.
They remain specification obligations awaiting runtime and independent checks.

The actual-insurer passages keep reconstruction honest. Acquisition-cost
disclosures concern different populations and periods; a residual does not name
an asset. Original and restated reports remain separately attributable. Public
availability is not established by an economic date or statement-authorisation
date. Transition routes depend on admitted information and group-specific
eligibility. Their conditional arithmetic does not provide missing valuation,
actuarial or operative-authority evidence. Known historical cash survives a
prescribed missing-information treatment.
