# Phase review before broader module work

The coverage delta is justified by the exact retained source audit, finite
measurement adapter and reviewed manuscript successor. It does not promote
rounded display diagnostics, check counts or a clean build into evidence of
actual history, accounting correctness or whole-company completeness. All
unaffected rows keep their earlier residuals and evidence intact.

The next distinct question is composition. Existing return, lease, company and
sector references differ in legal-book context, account registries, memory,
method eligibility, report outputs and durable behavior. A source adapter that
preserves measurements cannot by itself resolve those differences. The next
plan therefore audits retained capabilities before designing a reusable module
boundary. It must inspect later native return work rather than repeat an old
negative capability finding from the original boundary.

This avoids two unhelpful continuations: another synthetic lease example that
does not advance general composition, or automatic admission of Henkel amounts
to a fixed synthetic book. Candidate failure should identify a missing interface
or premise, not terminate the program. No current continuation veto has fired.
Missing human/domain acceptance remains a promotion limit, not a stop to
authorised internal work. No forecasting, paid review or external action begins.
