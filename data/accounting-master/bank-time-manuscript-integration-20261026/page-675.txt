A.54.7
Exact timing must retain the whole evidence dependency
Replacing timestamp arithmetic is useful only if the assessment still checks every source on which
its result depends. A current value may depend on an earlier vintage whose evidence arrived
too late. Checking only the current record would admit that dependency even when its own
timestamp comparison is exact. The separately named ExactBankInformation/1 therefore changes
only the retained auditor’s information check. It walks the same complete dependency closure
and retains the original evidence-membership, ingestion and measurement-date obligations.
For each evidence record in that closure, the checker requires its exact reference to appear in
the selected information view. Its available-at instant must be no later than the decision cutoff,
and its ingested-at instant no later than the system cutoff. Its measurement date must be no
later than the UTC civil day containing the decision cutoff. That day is obtained from the
exact coordinate, including offset normalization. Unsupported or invalid timestamp encodings
are refused explicitly; there is no fallback to the lossy parser.
All 271 retained regulatory responses agree under the original and exact auditors. Eleven
additional information-check cases exercise later evidence, ingestion, membership, measurement
dates, equivalent offsets and precision. Five outcomes intentionally change: later submicrosecond
availability, later submicrosecond ingestion and later superseded evidence are refused, as are
excess precision and the unknown-offset spelling -00:00. The superseded case uses a native
vintage record, which actually declares its predecessor.
These eleven examples exercise the information submethod; they are not eleven new complete
regulatory exchanges. A subsequent comparison checks ten complete derived regulatory ex-
changes and four exact replays, including five intended timing refusals. Its twelve adverse
controls check the remaining request and publication obligations. The original profile, packet and
auditor remain preserved. The separately identified profile below specifies conversion and replay
without changing those originals. Correct timing also leaves source truth, signatures, state,
budgets and accounting applicability to their existing checks. The complete comparisons and
derived examples are retained under data/accounting-master/exact-native-timing-20261026/.
A.54.8
Changing the timing rule creates a new bank profile
A saved assessment records which rules were applied to which information. Changing the time
comparator must therefore leave the old assessment interpretable. Consider evidence available
one ten-millionth of a second after the decision cutoff. The original parser can erase that
difference and accept the request; the exact comparator refuses it. Returning an old cached
answer under the new interpretation would conceal the change, even if the arithmetic in the
assessment were otherwise identical.
