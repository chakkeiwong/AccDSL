# Cash-flow classifications can change the reported prediction target

The June 2024 AASB 18 amendments provide a technical answer to the issue
raised by the three IFRS reporters. Two different changes must be kept
separate: the starting point for an indirect operating-cash-flow reconciliation,
and the classification of cash receipts and payments among operating,
investing and financing. Only the second necessarily moves amounts between
cash-flow subtotals when the underlying payments are held fixed.

The retained official AASB 18 page describes the standard as the Australian
equivalent of IFRS 18 and identifies Australian-specific paragraphs by the
Aus prefix. Its linked Appendix D contains the amended AASB 107 requirements
and deleted wording. This comparison is grounded in those exact Australian
texts and their attributed IFRS correspondence. It does not establish the
current EU or UK endorsed version, later amendments, a company's adoption
date or its chosen policy. The existing IFRS web summary corroborates only
the general 2027 application date, not the detailed classification rules.

## Exact illustration of the distinction

Hold actual cash receipts and payments fixed. Let operating receipts less
payments other than the listed interest/dividends be100; interest paid12,
interest received5, dividends received3 and dividends paid8. Payments enter
cash totals with negative signs. If interest paid and both receipts were
previously operating, while dividends paid were financing, old OCF is

    100 - 12 + 5 + 3 = 96.

Under34A with33A it becomes100, investing cash flow includes+8 and financing
includes-20. Total cash remains88 in both presentations. The increase of4
is a classification effect, not improved cash generation. If only the
receipts had been operating before adoption, OCF instead falls from108 to100.
If only dividends paid moved from operating to financing, OCF rises by8.
No universal sign follows without the previous policy and actual amounts.

More generally, for signed cash flows x_j and old/new operating indicators
o_j and n_j,

    OCF_new - OCF_old = sum_j (n_j-o_j) x_j.

Every reclassified cash flow leaves one category and enters another once,
so the changes in operating, investing and financing totals sum to zero.
This identity assumes unchanged underlying transactions, cash definition,
perimeter and measurement. It is not a bridge for acquisitions, currency
changes, corrections or other simultaneous changes without further evidence.

For a pure starting-line change, old profit70 plus net adjustments26 and
new operating profit90 plus net adjustments6 both reconcile to OCF96. The
changed profit subtotal supplies no evidence of a changed cash subtotal.
For a conditional34D example with interest expenses in both operating and
financing categories and other listed classifications fixed, assigning all12
of paid interest to operating gives OCF96; assigning all to financing gives108.
Both keep total cash88. This is an illustration of the stated policy choice,
not an election for HSBC or Munich Re.

## What must be resolved for the three companies

Henkel's 2025 report discusses interest/dividend classification and states
its expected impact. Check the applicable endorsed amendment and its actual
current cash-flow policy before treating that expectation as an established
zero transition effect. HSBC's statement that presentation effects are still
being evaluated leaves classification and policy choices unresolved. Munich
Re's disclosure about the starting profit measure supports a reconciliation
change, not by itself a quantified OCF change. Its applicable main-business
assessment and any relevant34D policy still need source support.

The forecast target should retain the first-release amount on the basis
actually applicable to its full fiscal year. Historical observations retain
the policy and source version known at the forecast cutoff. A common-basis
series can be a separate analysis only when the bridge and availability are
established; a later restated2026 comparative cannot be backdated into a
pre-2027 information set. A future announcement that the first-release2027
target differs from the presumed basis triggers an explicit target-version
resolution, preserving failures and original weights.

The next source action is jurisdiction-specific adoption evidence for the
EU/UK reporters, followed by company policy and exact-version availability.
This technical source check narrows the transition question but does not
admit an issuer-specific rule, harmonised training series or empirical study.
