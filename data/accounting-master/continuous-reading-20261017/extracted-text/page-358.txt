Budget, seeds, repairs and future records
The future ladder is bounded by decisions, not by an invented universal sample size. B0 runs
exact tractable/adverse cases only. B1 uses exposed development packets to estimate failure
rates, resource costs and within-target variability; its results cannot promote a method. B2
permits a frozen, honestly labelled retrospective panel comparison after availability and exposure
repair. B3 uses newly sequestered, task-appropriate data or participants and a justified sample/
uncertainty design for external claims. Failure of B0 invalidates the dependent run; a weak
candidate in B1/B2 triggers its declared repair. A later rung begins only when its prerequisite
artifacts exist and execution is authorized.
For stochastic B1 debugging, seeds 101, 202 and 303 are a reproducibility convenience, not a
sufficient replication count. Paired arms share randomness only when the coupling preserves
each arm’s marginal law. Subsequent replication counts, independent data units, chains/draws,
search trials, wall-time and memory caps are selected from target-specific development evidence
and frozen before evaluation. Optional learned components need a separate objective/scaling,
capacity, optimizer/search, budget, heldout and downstream protocol. No neural architecture
or optimizer is selected by this general design. Insufficient budget produces under_budgeted,
not a convenient inherited default.
Each future run saves eligibility and exclusions; data, source and exposure hashes; split
and temporal embargo; target and outcome-vintage definitions; comparison/tuning budgets;
random seeds; environment and CPU/GPU mode; exact command and commit; wall time;
per-unit predictions and failures; independent validity checks; uncertainty calculations; decision
and inference-status tables; and a repair/next-action record. Changes after outcomes are
inspected receive a new exploratory identifier and cannot overwrite the frozen confirmatory
result. Unknown critical source time, corrupted data, wrong target, missing essential diagnostics
or exceeded resource limits stop that run. A valid poor candidate blocks its promotion and
leaves independently ready repairs available.
14.6.1
The joined calculations retain separate validation targets
The sector group in Section 2.8 clarifies what an end-to-end check must preserve. The bank’s
expected loan cash, its contractual principal and coupon, the insurer’s liability estimate, the
parent’s guarantee, and each taxpayer’s usable loss have different meanings. A group statement
can balance after one has been substituted for another. Its reference must therefore retain the
separate legal books, gross external cash and origin of each adjustment, as well as the final
totals.
