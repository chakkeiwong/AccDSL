The complete Form 10-Ks describe Series A as the issuer’s direct, unsecured general obliga-
tions, ranking equally with its other unsecured and unsubordinated debt. Claims under the
parent guarantee generally rank behind those of subsidiary creditors. Their summary of the
2016 Indenture requires the parent, on demand after issuer nonpayment, to make full and
punctual payment of principal, interest and other amounts due: a guarantee of payment, not
collection (Sections 14.01–14.02). It excludes amounts the issuer is excused from paying or
beyond those actually due. Issuer defenses and counterclaims carry over, except those arising
solely from insolvency or bankruptcy status. These descriptions refer to the Indenture and
applicable prospectus supplements; they specify reported terms but do not supply a current
balance, valuation or accounting recognition.
The complete filings also describe one particular Series A population: the Callable Fixed Rate
Notes due 10 June 2032. They report US$5,000,000 of aggregate principal outstanding at 31
December 2022 in the 2022 filing, at 31 December 2023 in the 2023 filing, and at 31 December
2024 in both the 2024 and 2025 filings. The last report repeats a 2024 as-of date; this passage
supplies no 2025 year-end balance. The notes were issued on 10 June 2021 and bear 2.60%
annual interest payable semiannually on 10 June and 10 December. They mature on 10 June
2032 unless an earlier contractual outcome applies. The issuer may redeem all, but not part, on
10 June 2031 at principal plus accrued and unpaid interest. Upon acceleration after a continuing
event of default, the stated amount is US$1,000 per US$1,000 principal plus accrued and unpaid
interest. The descriptions report NYSE listing under JPM/32, DTC global book-entry issuance,
and the possibility that a reopening increases aggregate principal outstanding (JPMorgan Chase
& Co., 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026).
These dated amounts are reported principal, not carrying or fair value. Nor do they establish
the accounting classification or value of the parent guarantee, the entire Series A population,
or an unchanged balance between the stated dates. Each filing’s Exhibit 4.4 identifies the 19
February 2016 Indenture and incorporates it by reference to Exhibit 4(a)(7) of Form S-3 file
333-209682, filed 24 February 2016. The complete filings summarize that instrument; its text
and applicable supplements or amendments require their own inspection before being used as
complete legal terms.
