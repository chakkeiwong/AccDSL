9.4.8
Inspections, surrendered rights and changed employee benefits
A replacement inspection, a smaller leased area and a pension curtailment all remove something
from an existing arrangement, but they remove different economic rights or obligations. The
accounting must identify what survives before measuring the new arrangement. The following
methods use the retained historical EU text and synthetic pretax amounts. Actual authority,
contract and actuarial facts remain necessary for application.
Replacing the inspection component
