Interfaces, exclusions and acceptance cases
Insurance events are group_recognize, premium_settle, service_release, claim_incur, claim_set-
tle, assumption_change, onerous_change and treaty_change, each bound through the event-
basis, authorization and commit contracts to the exact group/version, evidence and information
view. Journals and component roll-forwards must reconcile independently; a component realloca-
tion is not always a net liability change. Invalid grouping, missing rate/law, unsupported option
valuation, ambiguous contract boundary or unknown service units produces the corresponding
unresolved/outside-scope result. Failed numerical paths retain their coverage/mass, without
successful-only normalization. Modified contracts need the applicable derecognition analysis;
no new ID can erase unfavorable old-group obligations.
Initial transition requires an actual method choice and eligibility for full retrospective, modified-
retrospective or fair-value transition under AASB 17 Appendix C, with acquisition, grouping,
past service and historical-rate records. This collection does not claim an executed transition
or a general VFA valuation. The nonzero-rate general case and onerous allocation through
expiry above are finite specification examples. Required later acceptance still includes a priced
guarantee, acquisition/reinsurance interaction, transition and actuarial backtesting. These
retained obligations prevent the two-year illustration from being described as full insurance-
domain readiness.
