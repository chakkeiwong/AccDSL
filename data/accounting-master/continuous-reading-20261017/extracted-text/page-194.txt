10.4
Performance conditions determine units before settlement
The performance form turns the distinction between a plan and a grant into a calculation
problem. Its notice leaves the target quantity, grant date, performance period and vesting
schedule blank. Exhibit A likewise leaves the percentile thresholds and payout percentages
blank, although it specifies interpolation between thresholds, a cap when Apple’s absolute
return is negative, and rounding to whole units. The form therefore supplies part of the method
without supplying an individual award’s inputs (Apple Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.3, notice and
Exhibit A).
The 2025 proxy provides a concrete schedule for the named executives’ 1 October 2023 per-
formance awards. It describes a three-year performance period from the beginning of fiscal
2024 through the end of fiscal 2026, with vesting on 1 October 2026 subject to the agreements.
This is a dated description of that grant population, not the result of the performance period.
The proxy separately reports results for awards granted on 27 September 2020 and vested on 1
October 2023. Those older results must not be attached to the newer grants merely because
the same proxy reports both (Apple Inc., 2025b, printed pp. 51–53).

correctly. Write it as p on a scale from 0 to 100, so that p = 70 means the seventieth percentile.

supplied percentile of 40 earns 0.625 units per target unit, while 70 earns 1.5. The threshold at
25 is a jump, not a line running from zero at some unreported lower percentile. If two unresolved
rank calculations produced only p = 24.9 or p = 25, a hypothetical target of 1,000 units would
yield either zero or 250 units before other conditions. Replacing those two possibilities by every
quantity from zero to 250 would create unsupported outcomes.
