Construct the exposure base before applying the allowance cap
For these ordinary loans, the regulatory carrying-value definition does not subtract the associated
allowance. The exposure amount is therefore the gross loan balance. Section 3.31 multiplies that
amount by the applicable weight; section 3.32 assigns 100% to the stated corporate exposures
and zero to the direct central-bank balance. Goodwill is deducted from CET1 and excluded from
risk-weighted assets. With no associated deferred tax liability, its deduction is the full 2 (Office
of the Comptroller of the Currency, 2026h, 12 CFR 3.31(a)–(b)) (Office of the Comptroller of
the Currency, 2026g, 12 CFR 3.32(a)(1)(i)(A), (f)(1), (k)) (Office of the Comptroller of the
Currency, 2026a, 12 CFR 3.22(a)(1), (e), (g)).
Let G be gross loans, A eligible AACL and B the risk-weighted base used to limit the allowance
admitted to Tier 2. For this restricted book, B = G. The capital rule caps that admission at
1.25% of the relevant base. The official reporting instructions make the order concrete: Part I
item 42 uses Part II item 26 for the cap; Part II subsequently subtracts the excess allowance in
arriving at its total. Define admitted allowance T, excess X and final standardized risk-weighted
assets D accordingly:
T = min{A, 0.0125B},
X = A −T,
D = B −X.
The last equality uses this example’s exclusions. In a broader book, the cap base and pre-
excess standardized denominator need not coincide. The instructions exclude section 22(a)
deductions such as goodwill from the cap base but include specified section 22(c)–(f) deductions,
which remain excluded from the final standardized denominator. The case has none of those
other deductions or market-risk amounts (Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, 2026c,
12 CFR 3.20(d)(3)) (Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council, 2024a,b, Part I,
item 42, pp. RC-R-27–28; Part II, items 26 and 28–31, pp. RC-R-109–110).
These retained instructions are the December 2024 files, with March 2024 revision footers on
the cap pages. They corroborate the ordering in the specified historical rule calculation; they
do not supply a complete amendment history for an actual later filing. The source date and the
conditional reporting date remain separate.
Initially B = 100, the cap is 1.25 and all the allowance 0.8 enters Tier 2, leaving D = 100.
