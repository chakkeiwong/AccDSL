An old error changes comparative profit and opening equity
In the error history, correct annual depreciation has always been 120/6 = 20. Assume the error
is material and all period-specific and cumulative effects are practicably determinable. The
2025 report presents 2024 as its comparative year. AASB 108.42 requires correction of that
comparative and of the opening balances for errors before its start; paragraph 46 excludes the
prior-period correction from 2025 profit. We therefore need two linked amounts: the error
accumulated before 1 January 2024 and the additional error during 2024.
Tax makes the equity effect smaller than the PPE correction. Supply a conditional tax law that
permits deductions of 20 annually in 2023–2028, with an enacted rate of 25% throughout the
relevant recovery periods. The asset is recovered through use, and initial book and tax bases are
both 120. There are no other differences or uncertain positions. Each year the company earns
cash operating income of 50 before depreciation; current tax is paid on the following 31 March.
These are authored tax assumptions, not statements of Australian tax law. AASB 112.7, 15–17,
46–47 and 51 support the tax-base and rate method; paragraph 53 prohibits discounting the
deferred tax (Australian Accounting Standards Board, 2023b).
The originally reported 2023 PPE of 110 exceeded tax base 100 by 10, giving a deferred tax
liability (DTL) of 2.5. In 2024, reported PPE 100 exceeded tax base 80 by 20, giving DTL 5.
Correct PPE is instead 100 and 80 respectively, equal to the tax bases, so those DTL balances
disappear. Original pretax profit of 40 carried current tax 7.5 and deferred expense 2.5, leaving
profit 30. Correct pretax profit of 30 carries current tax 7.5 and no deferred expense, leaving
profit 22.5 in each year. Cash tax does not change: the tax deductions were correct throughout.
At the earliest comparative opening, 1 January 2024, reduce PPE by 10, DTL by 2.5 and
retained earnings by 7.5. During comparative 2024, the extra depreciation of 10 and removal of
deferred expense 2.5 reduce that year’s profit by another 7.5. Thus the cumulative 1 January
2025 adjustment is PPE minus 20, DTL minus 5 and retained earnings minus 15. The opening
and comparative movements sum to the same cumulative correction; they are not two separate
postings of it.
