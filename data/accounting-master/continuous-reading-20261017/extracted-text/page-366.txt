A basis binds the opening state, event, profile, information view and economic event identity.
Authorisation checks these bindings and supplied findings. Proposal contains legal journals for
both entities, the common closing state and dated cash origins. Commit publishes the proposal,
closing state, receipt and immutable request decision together. An internal sale cannot publish
only P’s receivable while omitting S’s payable. Unrelated owners remain unchanged.
An exact retry returns its stored result before current-head or current-view checks. Reusing the
identity with changed bytes conflicts. A new request needs the exact current head and view.
If an acknowledgement is lost, the observable outcome is unknown: either no commit exists,
or the complete commit is durable. The retry commits once in the first world and replays in
the second. The documentary examples specify both worlds; they do not measure runtime
durability.
The report operation is read-only. It requires published receipts that connect the requested
opening and closing in the complete date interval. It recomputes the consolidation worksheet,
tax effects, gross external cash and ownership allocation. Reports carry the explicit joined-finite-
company scope. A request for an uncomputed tax close, incompatible calendar or unsupported
sector is not satisfied by adding independent component totals.
The source and schema locks bind exact bytes. Canonical JSON excludes duplicate keys, floats,
non-finite values and unreduced rational quantities. Record identity includes schema and body;
dependency lists are the exact transitive typed references. The retained independently checked
packet is under data/accounting-master/company-composition-20261016/attempt-03/. It has 13
transactions, three reports and two migration demonstrations. Those counts describe the finite
evidence, not a complete-company support measure.
Migration is deliberately narrower than method instantiation. The return projection maps
the named CorporateResiduals/1 opening state into a closed target containing its complete
return memory, source context/view, owner/book/branch, dates and cash/equity accumulators.
The compound projection does the same for the named matured FinancingComponents/2 state.
Nonzero balances outside the explicit map, unknown fields and foreign profiles are refused.
Zero-only omitted accounts are reconstructed from the locked source schema in the round trip.
For these two operations, every quantity used by the subsequent settlement or redemption is
preserved. Their exact sums, products and minimum therefore commute with the mapping:
deriving the target result equals mapping the source result under the same findings. The checks
exercise both the round trip and this following transition. They do not migrate old requests,
annual-report labels or arbitrary methods. In particular, changing the original synthetic owner
to P would be a new instantiation, not a lossless migration.
The separate CorporateResiduals/1 namespace fixes the 17 finite methods in Section 9.13; its
prior CorporateComponents/5 requests remain bound to their original lock. The all-zero CGU
allocation, full retrospective acquisition comparisons, general mixed-contract methods and
current-law admission remain explicit residuals. No profile name itself provides interoperability
or extends an earlier operation’s accepted fields.
