The year end contains four events, in order: finance, service, claim and recovery incurrence,
then settlement. Each has its own business-event identifier and immutable opening state.
Keeping those boundaries preserves the incurred balances and the distinction between earning
income and moving cash. The following intermediate states follow from the preceding journals.

Service releases the coverage amounts before the separate claim expenses and recovery income
are recognised. Incurrence creates liabilities of 44 and 66 and a recovery asset of 33. Those
balances explain why equity is 83 at that stage even though cash remains 250. Settlement clears
them and moves cash by −44 −66 + 33 = −77, leaving equity unchanged. Collapsing the four
events into a single final balance would conceal this distinction.
A valid component calculation is part of permission to post
The proposed operation uses the common OperationRequest for accounting.commit@1. Its four
arguments remain references to the accounting basis, posting authorization, journal proposal and
economic delta. The expected company head remains in the common request envelope. Each
group measurement identifies the group, issued or held role, covered group where applicable,
event phase, opening and proposed closing components, book, branch, time, information view,
method and source. A complete measurement has actual component values; an unresolved one
has no closing value and gives its reason.
Before looking up a previous receipt, the operation validates the complete group set, common
ownership and information view, admitted evidence, measurement calculation and posting
authorization.

The existing receipt index cannot turn malformed historical input into an eligible request. This

permission check.
For an eligible new event, the journal and economic delta must then agree with the measurements.
During finance, R’s asset rises by 8 because its claim present value gains 6 and its cost CSM
gains 2. Its loss-recovery component separately rises by 18/13, as a component within the asset.
An extra debit to R’s asset and credit to finance income for 18/13 would balance, yet overstate
both the asset and income. The operation rejects that proposal. It also rejects a proposal whose
journal is correct but whose economic delta leaves the recovery component at its old value. The
monetary accounts and component memory must both be correct.
