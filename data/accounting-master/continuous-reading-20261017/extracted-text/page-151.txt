Subtracting that 15 again would count the same error twice. The report presents the corrected
history; requesting it does not create another journal or charge the prior error to 2025 profit.
The earliest opening also has a precise informational limit. Although it equals the corrected
2023 closing position, it is not a full restated 2023 income statement in the 2025 edition. A
query for the latest published full-year 2023 column therefore still selects the 2024 edition,
which contains that earlier comparative. The return value must identify it as original, even
though later evidence establishes an error. A request for a reconstructed 2023 income statement
would ask a different question. Keeping the period, edition and information cutoffs together
prevents the system from silently substituting the answer to that other question.
9.5
Apple: asset balances do not identify asset movements
The preceding methods need receipts, lots, useful lives and settlement dates. Apple’s disclosures
supply some of the balances those events must explain, but they do not supply the events
themselves. Its FIFO and straight-line policies narrow the permitted cost calculations without
identifying the actual inventory lots or asset cohorts (Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 1, printed p. 34).
Comparing the notes with the cash-flow statement shows why filling those missing inputs by
subtracting balances can produce a wrong history.
9.5.1
An inventory adjustment is not purchases or payments
Between 28 September and 28 December 2024, components fall from 3,627 to 2,792 million
dollars while finished goods rise from 3,659 to 4,119 million. Total inventory falls from 7,286 to
6,911: the component changes, −835 and +460, sum to −375 million (Apple Inc., 2025c, Note
5, printed p. 9). Those two classes do not reveal quantities, unit costs or the transfers and sales
that produced the balances.
The quarter’s cash-flow statement reports +215 million for inventories within its reconciliation of
net income to operating cash (Apple Inc., 2025c, printed p. 5). That is an indirect reconciliation
adjustment, not total cash paid to suppliers. Even replacing it with minus the stock change would
give +375, a different quantity. The difference 215−375 = −160 million remains unexplained by
these selected disclosures. In fiscal 2024, inventory rose from 6,331 to 7,286, while the cash-flow
adjustment was −1,046; relative to the −955 stock-change shortcut the difference is −91 million
(Apple Inc., 2024a, printed pp. 31 and 33). Neither residual is an identified currency effect,
write-down or other journal entry. Production, consumption, ownership, valuation and payment
histories must be established before they can explain the differences.

Testing a proposed fixed-asset history
