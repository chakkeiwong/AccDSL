Computing R introduces its own source requirements.
The form uses twenty-trading-day
beginning and ending average prices, dividend reinvestment, and adjustments for specified
capital changes.
Consider a deliberately simple path with no dividends, distributions or
corporate actions. Suppose each of the twenty beginning prices is 100; nineteen ending-window
prices are 80 and the final price is 120. The beginning average is 100 and the ending average is
(19 × 80 + 120)/20 = 82, giving R = (82 −100)/100 = −18%. Substituting only the first and
last prices would instead give +20%. With the supplied percentile held at 70, that substitution
would wrongly remove the cap and increase the hypothetical award from 1,000 to 1,500 units.
Even in this simple case, the observation window changes a contractual consequence (Apple
Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.3, Exhibit A, Beginning Price and Ending Price).
The general return and rank calculation requires more than those two averages. The form
defines the comparison group using initial S&P 500 membership and continued listing, with
specific bankruptcy, successor and merger provisions. It ranks Apple ahead of a comparison
member with equal return. That tie rule does not by itself specify a unique numerical percentile
formula; a library’s default rank conversion cannot silently supply one. Dividends enter the
return through reinvestment, noncash distributions need values, and corporate actions need
the stated adjustments. A calculation must document how those provisions are implemented,
including avoiding a second addition of dividends to a price series that already includes them.
Unresolved ambiguities belong with the Committee’s determination under the form; they are
not permission to invent historical inputs (Apple Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.3, Exhibit A).
Employment conditions then determine which otherwise earned units survive. Under Section
8 of the filed 2022 performance form, ordinary termination generally cancels unvested rights.
Death or disability before vesting instead allows a fraction of the performance-qualified award to
vest on the specified vesting date. The fraction is the total performance-period days less the days
remaining at severance, divided by total days. For an expressly hypothetical eligible fraction of
3/5 and the uncapped 1,500-unit result, 900 units survive to that date; if the negative-return
cap first reduces the result to 1,000, 600 survive. These calculations use the filed form’s terms
and assume no other modification, leave adjustment or recoupment event (Apple Inc., 2022,
Exhibit 10.3, Sections 3–4 and 7–9).
