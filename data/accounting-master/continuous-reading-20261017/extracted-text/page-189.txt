The relation extends to the specified one-period fiscal treatment. Let P denote both proceeds
and principal at maturity, L the initial debt, C the coupon and t the unchanged tax rate. Gross
conversion equity is E = P −L; effective interest is I = P + C −L = E + C. The initial
deferred liability tE reverses as book and tax amounts meet, while the coupon supplies a current
deduction of C. Therefore
I −tC −tE = (1 −t)I.
A zero coupon still leaves discount accretion and deferred tax income. If E = 0, the deferred
difference vanishes and only the coupon deduction remains. Different fiscal bases, changing
rates or restricted deductions require their own schedule before this identity can be used.

The documentary CompoundTaxRollforward/1 calculation reports separate carrying amounts,
fiscal bases, tax origins and journal movements. CompanyTaxEPS/1 resolves the fixed company
inputs and reconstructs the legal books before accepting an EPS projection. It refuses a coupon-
only addback, a missing deferred-tax consequence, the wrong parent earnings or overlapping
share periods. Missing fiscal findings remain unresolved, and different rights or tax schedules
require another supported method. These calculations specify the supplied one-period case;
early-conversion measurement, changing tax rates, loss limitations and operative jurisdictional
authority remain separate obligations.
