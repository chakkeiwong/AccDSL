10.5
The applicable agreement changes which rights survive
The timing question can be narrowed by reading the later forms already incorporated into
Apple’s filings. The annual exhibit list identifies a CEO performance form effective 25 September
2022, filed as Exhibit 10.2 with the quarter ended 31 December 2022. It also includes general
and CEO performance forms effective 29 September 2024 as Exhibits 10.20 and 10.22. These
are different versions and participant categories, even though their notices use similar headings
and leave the individual grant particulars blank (Apple Inc., 2024a, annual exhibit list; Exhibits
10.20 and 10.22) (Apple Inc., 2023, Exhibit 10.2).
The 2024 general form does not remove the earlier timing distinction. Section 8 still provides that
qualifying death or disability before vesting allows the performance-qualified award, multiplied
by the severance fraction, to vest on the Vesting Date. Section 7 requires share delivery as
soon as administratively practical thereafter, within two and one-half months and subject
to its conditions. Thus neither inspected general form supplies a rule to deliver the shares
immediately on severance. The proxy’s description of accelerated vesting remains a summary
whose application to a particular award needs further evidence (Apple Inc., 2024a, Exhibit
10.20, Sections 7–8) (Apple Inc., 2025b, printed p. 53). Later filing alone is not evidence that
an earlier grant was amended.
The CEO forms change the surviving entitlement. In both the September 2022 and September
2024 versions, Section 8(b) allows unvested units to remain eligible for the Vesting Date following
death, disability, or qualifying retirement, subject to the Committee’s stated authority. These
clauses do not apply the general form’s severance fraction. Retirement combines an age of
at least 60 and at least ten years of service with the stated service scope; the retirement
exception normally also requires termination on or after the first anniversary of the Award Date.
Earlier retirement does not receive that exception unless the Committee determines otherwise.
Continued eligibility still depends on performance and does not make the eventual number of
shares known on departure (Apple Inc., 2023, Exhibit 10.2, Sections 3–4 and 8) (Apple Inc.,
2024a, Exhibit 10.22, Sections 3–4 and 8).
