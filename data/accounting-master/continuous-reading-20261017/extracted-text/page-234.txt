Identifiable net assets are 20 + 140 −60 = 100 euros. The parent chooses proportionate-share

20 and goodwill under paragraph 32 is 96 + 20 −100 = 16 euros. This recognised goodwill
belongs to the acquired parent interest; no NCI goodwill has been recognised. The parent’s
separate entry debits investment and credits cash by USD 115.2. The consolidation adjustment
debits acquisition equity 120 and goodwill 19.2, and credits the investment 115.2 and NCI 24.
Immediately after acquisition, group cash is 208.8, inventory 168, goodwill 19.2 and the payable
72. Net assets of 324 equal parent equity 300 plus NCI 24.
The subsidiary then sells all the inventory for EUR 160, receives cash, and pays its EUR 60
payable. Both events occur at USD 1.25 per euro. The specified event interval has no further
expense, impairment or distribution. In the subsidiary’s books, revenue 160 less cost of sales
140 gives profit 20; cash closes at 20 + 160 −60 = 120 euros. Translated revenue is 200, cost of
sales 175 and profit 25. Parent and NCI shares of profit are 20 and 5. The translation worksheet
translates the opening balances to the event rate before recording these movements; it does not
charge cost 175 against unadjusted acquisition inventory 168 and leave a temporary negative
stock. These are presentation adjustments, not a remeasurement of historical-cost inventory in
the subsidiary’s own functional currency.
