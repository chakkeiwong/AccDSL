Annual revenue of 260 less cost of sales 165, payroll 42, depreciation 11 and interest 5 gives
profit before tax of 37. Tax expense is 7.4 and profit is 29.6. Other comprehensive income is
zero under the declared scope, so total comprehensive income is also 29.6. Equity moves from
70 to 70 + 29.6 −9 = 90.6 after dividends.
The cash-flow statement supplies an independent reconciliation. Collections 245 less supplier
payments 145, payroll 42, interest 5 and tax payments 7.4 produce operating cash of 45.6.
Investing cash is −22 and financing cash is −9, giving a cash increase of 14.6. The indirect route
gives the same operating cash: profit 29.6 plus depreciation 11, minus the receivables increase
15, plus the inventory decrease 10 and the payables increase 10. The prepaid-tax balance is
zero at both annual endpoints. The two cash routes use different information and therefore
provide a useful check on classification.
2.4
A correction changes what was knowable
The original interim filing reported revenue of 120 and receivables of 30. Ten units of revenue
had been recorded before delivery. Its tax expense was 3.5, profit 14 and equity 80. The annual
filing subsequently corrected the interim revenue and receivable by 10 and recognised the excess
tax payment as prepaid tax of 2. Corrected interim profit is 6 and equity is 72, as above. The
original statement remains an accurate record of what was published, even though its revenue
recognition was wrong under the stated delivery rule.
For the temporal fixture, the original report becomes available on 1 August 2024 and is ingested
on 3 August. The correction and annual report become available on 2 March 2025 and are
ingested on 5 March. An investor-information query at 1 September 2024 00:00 UTC returns
120. For this synthetic example, take UTC as the reporting timezone and use an instant cutoff
of 3 March 2025 at 00:00 UTC, after the correction’s entire publication-date interval has ended.
That query returns 110 when evaluated using the subsequently acquired archive. A query that
additionally asks what the database contained on 4 March still returns 120. Publication and
ingestion answer different questions.
The annual total 260 and corrected interim total 110 share revision group v2, so second-half
revenue is 260 −110 = 150. Subtracting the original 120 would yield 140, but that result mixes
incompatible revisions. The query must reject the subtraction unless a reviewed compatibility
relationship supports it. A later date alone does not establish such a relationship.
