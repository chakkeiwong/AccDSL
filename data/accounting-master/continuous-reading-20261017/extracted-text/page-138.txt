9.4.12
Impairment across a group and the origin of tax
The following methods use the retained historical EU 2023/1803 text and the separately
retained November 2023 IAS 12 amendment. Each example is conditional on its declared
authority, perimeter, allocation and tax assumptions. These texts do not establish current
issuer compliance or cross-GAAP equivalence.
Testing assets that serve more than one business
IAS 36.100–102 (European Commission, 2023a, IAS 36.100–108) identifies corporate assets by
their contribution to multiple cash-generating units without independent cash inflows. Do not
invent a separate recoverable amount for a headquarters just to make every row independently
testable. When a reasonable consistent allocation to a unit exists, include that share with the
unit. When it does not, first test the individual unit excluding the corporate asset, recognize
that loss, then test the smallest larger group to which an allocation is reasonable and consistent.
The second test uses the already adjusted carrying amounts.
The impairment record retains the hierarchy of units, legal/group/perimeter identities, corpo-
rate asset links, allocation basis, carrying and recoverable amount dates, goodwill allocation,
individual floors and prior loss origins. The calculation is postorder: individual tests precede
wider group tests. IAS 36.104 applies loss first to goodwill, then pro rata to other asset carrying
amounts. Under paragraph 105, no asset falls below its measurable fair value less costs of
disposal, determinable value in use, or zero, whichever is highest. Allocate a capped asset’s
unabsorbed loss again over the eligible remainder. Do not create a balancing liability for an
unallocated remainder unless another standard actually requires it. Revised depreciation follows
the adjusted amounts.

and headquarters 40. Headquarters cannot be reasonably allocated to either unit alone, but
it serves their combined group. Unit A’s recoverable amount 70 first causes loss 10, allocated
5 to each A asset. B’s recoverable amount 130 causes no loss. The group’s adjusted carrying
amount is then 70 + 120 + 40 = 230. Given group recoverable amount 207, additional loss
