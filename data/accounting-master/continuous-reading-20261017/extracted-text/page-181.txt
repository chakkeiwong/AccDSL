Funding simulations must connect the quantity register to market assumptions. An equity issue
at a scenario-dependent price changes cash raised, shares and dilution. The model must state
whether it targets a fixed cash amount, a fixed share count or a capital ratio. These are different
management decisions even when the historical statement would report the same aggregate

10.2.1
A share history changes the denominator
A company can earn the same profit with different numbers of shares outstanding during the
year. Dividing by the closing count would attribute the entire year’s earnings to shares that may
have existed for only a few months. Basic earnings per share instead relates earnings attributable
to ordinary shareholders to the time those shares were outstanding. Diluted earnings per share
asks a further, conditional question: how would that measure change if eligible potential shares
had become ordinary shares? Neither calculation exercises an option or converts a bond in the
company’s actual accounts.
The following synthetic case uses IAS 33 in the retained original EU Regulation 2023/1803,
including its integral Appendix A. It develops a method under that historical text; applying it
to a real issuer requires the issuer’s reporting basis, operative version and instrument terms.
It is separate from the Apple disclosures that follow (European Commission, 2023a, IAS 33,
paragraphs 2–4, 9–12, 30–36). Assume one fully paid ordinary-share class, one consolidated
reporting population, and a 365-day year from 1 January to 31 December 2025. Monetary
amounts are currency units; share counts are numbers of shares, so EPS is currency units per
share.
The year opens with 1,000 ordinary shares and no treasury holdings. On 1 April, consideration
becomes receivable for 200 new shares at 12 each and is paid that day: cash and contributed
equity rise by 2,400. On 1 July, a two-for-one split doubles every outstanding ordinary share. On
1 October, the company buys 300 shares for 7 each and holds them in treasury. Cash and equity
fall by 2,100; issued shares remain 2,400, treasury shares become 300, and outstanding shares
become 2,100. The split contributes no additional resources. IAS 33 therefore restates earlier
share counts onto the new share basis, whereas the resource-changing issue and repurchase enter
the average from their respective dates (European Commission, 2023a, IAS 33, paragraphs
19–21, 26–29).
