The reserve records authority used, rather than shares in the employee’s account. An option
exercise covering 100 shares can also deliver 80 after withholding, yet uses 100 reserve shares
under the gross-count rule; the option withholding does not restore capacity. The same observed
delivery therefore cannot identify the reserve movement without the award type and counting
rule. Cash settlement creates another distinction: the plan excludes the shares that would
otherwise have been delivered from its share count. These are consequences of Section 5(b),
not an assumed employee tax rate, cash payment or compensation expense (Apple Inc., 2022,
Exhibit 10.1, Section 5(b)).
The 2025 proxy illustrates why the reporting population must travel with each number. At 28
September 2024 its equity-compensation table reports 125,973,077 shares subject to outstanding
awards under the 2022 plan, 37,387,521 under the 2014 plan and 10,612 under the director plan,
totalling 163,371,210. The 2014 amount includes 39,984 options and 37,347,537 restricted stock
units. Removing those options leaves 163,331,226 shares subject to restricted stock units across
the three listed populations. The annual note’s 163,326 thousand restricted stock units is a
different reported amount; these disclosures alone do not identify a reconciliation. The proxy
separately reports 1,022,428,528 shares available under the 2022 plan, 66,924,428 under the
employee purchase plan and 4,110,122 under the director plan, totalling 1,093,463,078. That
total includes different plans from the outstanding-award total and is governed by their counting
conventions (Apple Inc., 2025b, printed p. 97 and table footnotes). A reconstruction must
retain these populations rather than subtracting unlike totals to infer grants, dilution or unused
authority.
Capacity still does not tell the employee what will be delivered or when. Section 9 permits
restricted-stock-unit agreements to differ: the agreement specifies vesting, settlement may be in
shares, cash or a combination, and delivery may be deferred under its terms. Dividend-equivalent
rights may be granted; the units themselves carry no voting rights. The filed time-based form
then makes a more specific promise. Its Section 7 provides for shares equal to vested units less
the applicable tax-related withholding, delivered promptly and no later than two and one-half
months after vesting, subject to the stated conditions. The plan’s wider permission does not
replace that form’s narrower promise (Apple Inc., 2022, Exhibit 10.1, Section 9; Exhibit 10.2,
Sections 7 and 11).
