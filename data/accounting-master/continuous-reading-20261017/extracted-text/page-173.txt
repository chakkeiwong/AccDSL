If the
remaining lease year falls within the comparative year, the DTA of 27.5 and DTL of 26.25
reverse during that year, giving comparative deferred-tax expense 1.25. The opening adjustment
alone therefore does not complete the transition. Neither ASBE 21’s article 61(2) lease-adoption
election nor Interpretation 16 Section III’s no-comparative-adjustment rule for its separate
share-payment topic may be substituted for Section I(b)’s tax transition.
These are gross recognition and measurement amounts. Separate recognition and noncurrent
classification do not determine whether an actual balance sheet may offset DTA and DTL. That
decision needs the applicable presentation provisions and entity/tax-authority facts. Actual tax
law, tax bases and rates, recoverable profit, the enterprise’s adoption and elections, and later
amendments remain to be established before applying the example to an issuer. Full ASBE 22
credit, consolidated statements, group combinations and insurance revisions also retain specific
source dependencies; similar amounts do not establish full IFRS equivalence.
