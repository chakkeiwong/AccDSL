11.7.1
A rate bracket must travel with the loan
The preceding dated examples have a convenient property: their initial cash flows identify an
exactly representable effective rate. Ordinary schedules need not do so. Consider an asset
acquired on 1 January 2025 for 98 with integral acquisition costs of 2. Expected receipts are
50 on 1 January 2026 and 60 on 1 January 2027. Its initial carrying amount is 100, including
those costs. For a supplied POCI classification, the original credit-adjusted rate discounts these
initial expected receipts; subsequent interest applies that rate to amortised cost (European
Commission, 2023a, IFRS 9.5.4.1(a),B5.4.1–2,B5.4.7–8).

Each endpoint factor is itself enclosed by the integer-power calculation used in the preceding
dated method. Adding, subtracting and multiplying these intervals, and taking reciprocals only
of positive factors, encloses the corresponding financial calculation. Starting with exact gross
carrying 100 and allowance zero, the argument applies successively to accrual, receipts, credit
revisions, non-credit catch-ups and equal gross/allowance write-offs. The original rate is never
refitted when expected credit changes.
