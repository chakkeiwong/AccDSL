For this calculation, a certified rate bracket of width 2−40 gives monetary intervals narrower
than 5.38 × 10−10 currency units. The profit interval contains zero, while cash is exactly 300.
These are bounds under the declared numerical budget. A wider rate interval can make the
financial result too imprecise; the method then reports that failure instead of silently rounding
a balanced result.
The returned bounds share the same original rate and cash history. Their separate endpoints
cannot be freely combined into a journal or a set of jointly attainable statements.
