## What was established

The claimed target is the strictly dilutive continuing-operations population
for a finite complete inventory of independently fixed additive series, using
supplied attributed basic earnings and time-weighted shares. The selector
computes that target with exact current-ratio comparisons and carries total
earnings over the same population. A local derivation explains why it attains
the subset minimum under those premises; the independent enumeration agrees
for every grid case, including signed and tied increments. The protected
three-series example retains options and debt, excludes the preference series,
and returns continuing EPS 7665/1684 and total EPS 4745/842. The actual-conversion
example avoids overlapping actual and potential shares.

The closed schemas and semantic validator reject malformed, mismatched,
duplicate, missing-inventory and overlapping-date records. Missing findings
or source bytes remain unresolved; unsupported scope remains unsupported;
both obstacles remain visible together. No incomplete selection produces
diluted EPS. Every result is read-only. Support-byte verification establishes
the bytes, not the truth of an anchor, attribution or completeness assertion.
Live book-head freshness and strict raw-JSON transport parsing remain future
application responsibilities; this oracle consumes already parsed records.

Actual instrument preprocessing, broader participation, dependent conversions,
zero-share/nonzero-earnings effects, source interpretation, valuation, tax and
operative authority remain outside this finite report. The P5 company-to-EPS
join is the next ready design task. Passing this selector does not close the
original P17-05.03–04 promises or the full company/sector requirements.
