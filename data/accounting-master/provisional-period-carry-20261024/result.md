# Recorded balances can cross a provisional period boundary

24 October 2026. All 585 deterministic checks across 30 cases pass. Fourteen
focused and 25 reassessment/continuation regression methods pass; eight explicit
inputs and their transitive protected dependencies remain unchanged.

The carry transfers recorded profit into retained earnings, clears temporary
accounts and preserves every other balance, unpaid tax, loss lot, component,
lease date and lifetime identity. Exact prior reports, cash flows and adjustments
remain archived. New activity uses the carried opening. Unknown and false
findings remain attached; true caller assertions cannot promote annual readiness.

The mixed scenario carries cash 721/2 and tax payable 19/4. Paying that liability
leaves cash 1423/4 and no new tax expense. A further depreciation charge of 10
produces profit -10, assets 2743/4, liabilities 104 and equity 2327/4. Separately,
an unsettled return crosses the boundary: settlement and resale produce profit
6, cash 482 and operating cash -18. These are exact hypothetical controls.

The claimed and computed target is an exact recorded-balance carry under an
explicit model. Independent rational arithmetic and full-state comparisons agree
for the selected cases. This is engineering evidence, not a stochastic ranking or
evidence about forecast accuracy. See design.md, checks.json, cases.json and the
run manifest for the request chain, commands, environment, timing and hashes.

Red team: the weakest evidence is breadth—selected return/lease kernels and one
boundary do not establish general annual execution. A new supported event that
breaks the cash bridge or loses prior memory would invalidate this implementation
scope and trigger repair. New-period tax assessment, repeated carry, deferred
recognition, real-source admission and wider domain coverage remain open. The
recovery-audit records a source-lock diagnostic and a corrected fixture arithmetic
error; neither was promoted into scientific evidence. No whole MC row closes.
