# Provisional recorded-balance carry and following-period execution

ProvisionalSharedPeriodCarry/1 contains the exact prior reassessment request and
digest,a unique carry identity,explicitly selected provisional model,next period
start/end,complete findings and ordered operations. Recompute the predecessor
from its original evidence. Current assessment and loss cursor must reach its
financial endpoint;its final date must be the preceding included day. The next
period starts exactly there and has an explicitly later endpoint. No one-year
calendar is inferred. A strict annual model is unsupported.

The carry model provisional_recorded_balance_carry_v1 permits a scenario opening
while retaining unresolved source,coverage,deferred-tax and accounting findings.
Each finding is explicitly known/unknown with provenance or reason. False and
unknown findings remain visible;even true caller assertions do not supply the
missing deferred/source methods or promote annual_close_ready. The predecessor
result and its exact report remain attached. Fixed unresolved obligations stay
visible independently of caller findings.

Transfer recorded profit to retained earnings using debit-positive signs:
new retained = old retained - recorded profit. Clear every temporary income
account and leave all other balances,cash and tax payable unchanged. Preserve
return memory,all lots including disposed identities,lease rights/measurement
dates,lifetime event ids and loss history/quantity. Add the carry id. Archive
old flows and tax adjustments in the attached predecessor and start empty current
period lists. Preserve opening-interest remainder;reset opening-interest-paid.
Copy recorded DTA/DTL into opening allocations without interpreting it as a tax
assessment. Add exact prior closing/report hashes to prior_years. The new tax
cursor marks an empty assessed interval at the opening;current assessment is
explicitly unassessed,not complete.

The resulting book has the same complete26-account execution schema and can use
selected return/lease methods and explicit tax payments. Payments consume carried
payable and cash without new tax expense. Native events do not change carried
DTA/DTL or invent taxes. Lifetime duplicate ids remain refused. Event failure
retains the completed prefix and provisional findings. New current-tax assessment
and repeated carry are separate extensions,not inferred from this one transition.

Following-period financial views reference the actual new opening,not the old
period's opening. Equity starts after profit transfer; income starts at zero;
only new cash origins and account movements enter cash flow. The indirect bridge
uses change in tax payable relative to its carried opening,so payment of a prior
liability is an operating cash movement without repeated expense. Every state,
journal and four statement identities must balance exactly. Pure apply recomputes
the full prior chain and ordered new prefix;no durable commit or external admission.

The evidence contract compares independent rational carry and original native
carry algebra,plus exact preservation of all histories and balances. Positive,
negative/zero profit,unpaid tax and losses require separate cases. Unknown/false
findings,strict annual mode,gap/overlap,prior incomplete assessment,funding,date,
duplicate and full-result tamper cases are required. Audit passes for provisional
simulation only. Missing deferred/coverage evidence remains a promotion veto,
not a reason to destroy valid conditional state or stop independent internal work.
