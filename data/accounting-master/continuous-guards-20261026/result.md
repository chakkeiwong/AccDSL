Four retained modes combine fixed/floating interest and available/unavailable
facilities. Their continuous rate domains are represented by 32 point/open
regions. Fourteen calculation nodes suffice in this fixture without deleting
any region, mode or facility right. Partition metadata is eager; transitions
are evaluated on demand and identical results shared. This is a representation
check, not a speed or asymptotic-complexity comparison.

With committed facilities and a coarse low/high rate signal, no total robust
pay/draw policy exists. The high signal includes both sides of the liquidity
trigger: pay fails on one side and draw is prohibited on the other. Adding
the stipulated pre-decision trigger signal admits exactly one robust policy:
pay when interest is at most5.5, draw and pay otherwise. An unavailable
facility at high floating rates makes the larger family infeasible even with
full information. Contingent orders, new actions and probabilistic feasibility
are different policy problems, not covered by that conclusion.

Refused actions preserve accrued interest payable, due memory and the accrual
journal. For a committed floating loan above5.5% and below6%, ending cash after
draw/fee/payment has infimum0.25 and supremum0.75, neither attained on that open
interval. At the cap, cash0.25 is attained. The strict threshold belongs to the
pay region. The output preserves this distinction instead of rounding it away.

The first attempt found an implementation defect: a root from the interior
coupon formula was extrapolated beyond the cap. Candidate construction now
checks that each root belongs to the region where its formula applies. The
original failed attempt, sources and repair are preserved. This failure did
not invalidate the exact-region approach or the independent comparator.
