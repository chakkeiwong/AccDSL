## Evidence and conclusions

The annual Item 3 and interim Part II Item 1 bodies were read completely for
all four headings: Digital Markets Act Investigations, Department of Justice
Lawsuit, Epic Games and Other Legal Proceedings. Annual Note 12, interim Note 9,
annual MD&A legal-recognition language and the selected annual/interim risk

The filings describe different kinds of facts. The DMA passages report two
formal investigations opened on 25 March 2024, preliminary findings and a third
investigation announced on 24 June, together with a conditional cease-and-desist
and fine cap of up to 10% of annual worldwide net sales after a final
determination. The order's stated immediate effectiveness during appeal is
qualified by a possible stay, and Apple's compliance belief is separately
attributed. The 10% cap is a conditional issuer-reported consequence, not an
expected loss, a fine already imposed or a booked liability.

The DOJ passage reports a 21 March 2024 civil antitrust lawsuit, alleged
monopolization or attempted monopolization, equitable relief, and related civil
claims seeking damages and other relief. It reports Apple's defenses. The Epic
passage reports a California unfair-competition finding and an injunction
covering external purchase links on the U.S. iOS/iPadOS storefront, Apple's
January 16 compliance statement, a pending Epic enforcement motion and Apple's
September 30 motion to narrow or vacate. The reported court finding, pending
procedure and management position stay separate; no sales effect or accepted
compliance plan is disclosed.

Other proceedings remain unresolved. Annual Item 3 refers to Q4 FY2024
settlements; the interim filing refers to Q1 FY2025 settlements. Neither
identifies the matters, amounts, payment dates or earlier accruals. The period
change is evidence of two dated assertions, not a complete settlement history.

Annual Note 12 and interim Note 9 state management's qualified opinion about
the reasonable possibility of an incurred material loss or a loss greater than
a recorded accrual. That sentence does not establish zero exposure, a total
accrual, a matter-level accrual, a probability or a numerical materiality
threshold. Annual MD&A says a liability is recorded when a loss is probable and
reasonably estimable, subject to significant judgment. It is issuer-policy
evidence; operative US GAAP and case-specific facts remain to be checked.

The interim risk passage adds a dated 5 August 2024 Google antitrust finding
and DOJ proposed remedies that could restrict licensing or prohibit commercial
search-distribution terms with Apple for up to ten years. Those are proposed
partner remedies, not an Apple fine or an imposed outcome. They could affect
Apple's licensing revenue through contract terms, duration, geography and
substitution. The filings do not disclose the agreement economics needed to
calculate that effect. App Store remedies likewise require storefront,
developer-term, fee and response facts.
