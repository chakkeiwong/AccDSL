## Rule selection

AuthorityRequirement contains issuer/framework, jurisdiction, entity class,
annual-period/application interval, paragraph and local source Ref, incorporating
instrument, amendment chain, transition, elections and unresolved dependencies.
RuleSelection binds those requirements to the accounting event and evidence view.
The algorithm evaluates chronological applicability first, then scope and facts,
then available elections/transition, then measurement/presentation. If two
supported rules can apply, retain the explicit alternative and required election;
if one is contradicted by facts, mark ineligible. Missing current authority gives
unresolved, never eligibility through a familiar rule name.

An unchanged historical report remains an assertion under its own basis and
vintage. A harmonized comparison creates an adjustment book and reconciliation,
with changed recognition/measurement explained. Label mapping alone cannot
convert US GAAP to IFRS or ASBE. The the portable four clocks and source version remain
part of each rule dependency; later knowledge cannot leak into an original
as-of view. Transition entries reconcile opening assets, liabilities, tax and
equity, rather than changing old source bytes or plugging an unexplained amount.

the chosen transition cannot be inferred from a present closing liability.

## Matched-case outcomes and decision

| Input changed or withheld | Required consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Framework changes with same lease facts | Preserve different expense/ROU paths and source strength, not just rename accounts |
| Method source present but target entity's adoption unknown | Unresolved applicability, no real posting authorization |
| ROU/liability initially equal | Still evaluate gross tax differences and later divergent balances |
| Historical rule supports method but a later reporting year is requested | Recheck operative amendments/transition; no automatic extension |
| Issuer policy discloses LIFO, regulator text unavailable | Retain issuer assertion; do not upgrade it into universal US authority |
| Foreign basis label replaced by local label | Reject as a standards conversion without recognition/measurement bridge |

These decisions establish a common procedure and specific proposed treatments.
P15-E07 remains a proposal blocker for the broader promised authority coverage.
The new official ASBE lease support narrows that blocker; it does not erase the
specific US, ASBE tax/sector, legal and current-applicability work. Feasible
internal preparation continues without a paid engagement.
