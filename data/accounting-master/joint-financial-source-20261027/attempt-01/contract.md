Question: which financial assertions and observation heads can be selected at
the two dates under the same supplied source clocks? Candidate: the protected
joint partition algorithm with its fixed-single-financial-head restriction
removed in a new module. The original module and all successful results stay
unchanged. Baselines: exact protected joint trees/outcomes/supports on all

head-pair expectations on financial correction, retraction and conflict cases.

Audit: the temporal preparation and independent head selector already validate
multiple source records and supersession. The new source selector may use those
semantics at both dates. It must not attach a financial policy population or
stitch old/new financial rows. Every result explicitly says financial-policy
binding is unassessed and keeps admission flags false. Different source heads
can assert different receipts without causing an economic cash-flow transition.

The primary criterion is exact partition/support equality on the old subset,
independently reconstructed new leaves, correct authored joint pairs and exact
marginal projection. A strict Cartesian counterexample is required because
marginal agreement alone does not establish cross-date dependence. Rejection
controls alter both stage outcomes, pending/whole-domain remainder, head support,
profile, policy-binding conclusion, source identity and admission flags.

Defaults: two ordered decision dates and nondecreasing effective cutoffs,
synthetic exact/interval/unknown clocks, original finite receipt/right schema,
and 4096 nodes. Provenance is the protected temporal implementation and fixture
library. These restrictions define the comparison, not actual company facts.
An early between-date receipt correction must give old/new, not new/old.
Unknown access preserves supplied possibilities. Runtime/counts explain workload
only. No probability, optimality, actual actor access or financial-continuity
claim is inferred from successful source selection.
