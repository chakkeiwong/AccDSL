## Choosing the first import path

The first optional importer uses the retained Arelle source for native XBRL,
with the Apple 2024 instance as its concrete preparation case. Native facts
already carry contexts, units and occurrence relationships that a flattened
table cannot reconstruct. The selection therefore follows the preservation
requirements established above. It selects a source and an interface for the
first implementation slice; the source commit is not an installed release or
a tested environment. The complete commit and body hashes are retained in the
source-selection manifest.

Use the previously inspected session lifecycle with no optional plugins or
additional package groups: request validation and its error-sensitive return,
disable formula execution, and copy facts and their contexts before closing the
session. Each owning process admits one session at a time. For this particular
Apple archive, the recorded map has 27 resources and 3,551 reference edges.
Resolving those static references does not establish XBRL validation or closure
of every financial note.

The deterministic PDF comparator is the retained pdfplumber source, used for
character and page geometry on machine-generated documents. Its output remains
a proposal to be checked against the located page. A page that needs unadmitted
OCR remains unresolved, with its original bytes and manual inspection route
preserved. Docling, TableFormer and OCR are separate candidate paths. They do
not become an automatic fallback that silently changes the selected method,
model terms or resource budget.

There are three distinct forms of reproducibility here. A source selection
identifies the code and technical evidence. A resolved environment identifies
the actual platform, Python build, every direct and transitive package version
and hash, enabled extras, model and taxonomy bytes, and applicable notices.
An executed compatibility check establishes what those installed components
actually do together. The present selection establishes the first. Arelle's
15 base requirement ranges and three build requirements remain an inventory;
they are not an exact transitive lock. Before runtime, resolve that inventory
for the selected platform and inspect the resulting compatibility and terms.
Any changed dependency, transformation registry, taxonomy or configuration
creates a new identity and invalidates dependent admission.

Offline access is exact URI-to-bytes resolution. An absent file, changed hash,
wrong namespace or missing required fragment returns the affected dependency
as unresolved before accepting its financial values. A cached pathname is
insufficient. Original schemas remain unchanged, and the isolated cache or
external resolver may provide only admitted bytes. Substituting a newer
taxonomy would change the interpretation problem.

Terms also attach to different things: code, dependencies, issuer documents,
taxonomies and model weights. The earlier source-specific dispositions remain
binding evidence limits. In particular, an unversioned FASB page displaying
2027 does not establish the terms applicable to a retained 2024 package; the
missing component-specific XBRL Software Notice remains a source dependency.
Unresolved model-weight terms constrain the corresponding model path. They do
not resolve or negate the separate native-XBRL source decision. No distribution
or blanket legal clearance follows from this selection.

Eleven complete documentary exchanges test the proposed boundary without
executing a converter. Every synthetic source body, context, unit and occurrence
span is supplied. Raw “1,234” under an admitted grouping transform and scale
$-3$ produces $1.234$; precision metadata does not scale it again. A nil
occurrence remains unknown. Equal values in different contexts stay separate.
Unknown transforms, missing contexts or typed-dimension values, absent resources
and changed hashes retain unresolved records instead of guessed facts.

A cancelled case retains its completed proposal and unprocessed frontier.
Repeating an identical request preserves its proposal receipt; changing bytes
under the same identity is a conflict. All results remain proposed or unresolved,
and none claims package completeness, review acceptance or an accounting commit.
These are authored expected exchanges with independently checked transformations.
Their closed documentary envelope is a verification format for the proposal;
it does not alter the portable product schemas or establish runtime translation
into them. That translation and the exact environment remain prerequisites of
the later implementation.
