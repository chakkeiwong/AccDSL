# Issue-date compound-instrument tax: source and derivation

IAS12.23 specifically describes a liability tax base equal to the initial
combined liability/equity amount, creating a taxable temporary difference
when the equity component is recognized separately. It requires a deferred
tax liability charged directly to equity at issue. Its stated subsequent
changes go through profit or loss; that is a distinct period calculation.

Let proceeds be P, the supplied debt valuation L and residual equity E=P-L.
For the source-described combined-proceeds tax base T=P, the difference is
T-L=E. At the supplied applicable rate t, DTL=tE and net equity is E-tE.
The issue journal is debit cash P; credit debt L; credit equity E. The tax
journal debits equity tE and credits DTL tE. Then P=L+tE+(E-tE), so it
balances without current tax or an issue-date expense. For the separately
supplied matching tax base T=L, this finite method records zero difference.
Intermediate or lower tax bases require another explicitly supported method.

Zero tax rate gives zero DTL by the same formula; zero equity leaves no split
difference. A changed debt valuation is permissible if the supplied components
still add to proceeds. Therefore neither rate25% nor debt92 is a validity
condition. The reference rejects malformed/negative quantities and inconsistent
components, treats missing findings as unresolved, and refuses other tax-base
profiles. Source interpretation and actual rate/base facts remain supplied.
