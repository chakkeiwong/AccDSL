## What the composition establishes

The older temporal example describes financing-policy evidence; the insurance
example describes a different company population. Their join is an explicitly
authored interpretation test. It establishes that certified alternative views
can survive a native full-history attachment. It does not establish that the
old financing source text implies an insurance measurement, that both examples
describe one company, or that an issuer document has been correctly interpreted.
This remaining semantic integration requires a common source/quantity schema
and supported industry/basis meaning. The synthetic mapping is not a default.

No approximate state reduction is used. Complete proof families avoid selecting
one arbitrary view, but their finite six-revision and4096-node inherited limits
remain. More elaborate family construction, source reliability, common actor
decision interfaces, changing populations and bounded-error compression remain
open. Distinct audit source IDs must not become free observable policy signals.
