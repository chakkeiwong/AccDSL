# R03-O primary-source inspection and case audit

15 October 2026. Historical EU2023/1803 IFRS17 text and PDF are retained in
docs/design/research/p08-v3-preparation/. Cases bind their exact hashes.

Inspected 45–59,72–77,81–85,87–92,B101–B119,B126–B136 and the relevant B37–B41,
B49–B55,B65,B72–B78,B83–B89 measurement passages. Earlier R03-J and the
insurance-methods section preserve the GMM/reinsurance/cash-flow groundwork.

The modification test is counterfactual to inception, except the stated
participation/PAA changes; exercising an existing right is excluded. Paragraph
77 uses the equivalent new-contract premium less actual additional premium,
not a pro-rata share of CSM. It adjusts the old group and also measures the
new contract. A transfer uses the third party's premium. Paragraph91 separates
the recycling of 88(b) OCI from the non-recycling of 89(b) OCI.

PAA53(b) tests coverage, 56 tests service-to-premium-due timing, and59(b) tests
claim-incurrence-to-settlement timing. Acquisition deferral versus permitted
immediate expense changes LRC and interim profit. The new example adds those
choices and a claim-incurrence-rate OCI schedule to the existing long-tail
PAA example, rather than claiming that eligibility was newly discovered.
An onerous addition is the excess of FCF over the existing LRC, not all FCF.

B101 requires all three direct-participation conditions; B102 fixes the
inception assessment unless modified, and B109 excludes issued/held
reinsurance from that definition. B111 distinguishes the underlying-value
obligation from the entity's future-service fee in B112. B113 uses current
rates and includes financial-guarantee changes, subject to B115. Risk
mitigation requires the prior strategy and economic-offset/credit conditions
of B116; B117A and B118 govern presentation and prospective cessation.

B132 is a prospective allocation of revised expected finance at a constant
remaining rate, or the credited-amount route when eligible. It is not the
B131 fixed-payment initial rate. B134 requires matching separately presented
profit income/expense on held underlying items. B135–136 preserve a separate
frozen schedule for accumulated OCI when holdings change; they prohibit
rewriting history under the new policy. PAA LIC instead uses the B72(e)(iii)
claim-incurrence rate under B133.

Case audit: modification amounts include allocated RA and show separately
actual cash and the deemed premium transfer. VFA has admitted insurance and
participation eligibility, equal service units, supplied RA and an investment
component payable without an insured event; no eligibility threshold or
valuation is inferred from a fixed share. Financial shocks precede service
release. The PAA claim occurs at coverage end and is paid two years later,
so acquisition-cost alternatives converge before the same LIC develops.
All rates/claims/RA/prices remain example inputs.

The final process-law comparison is an exact local derivation in a binary
two-date support. Identical claim marginals leave a free joint-probability
parameter. Stop-loss expectations differ with that dependence. This supplies
a discriminating finite-law procedure; it does not calibrate an actual insurer,
assign weights between competing laws, or prescribe an RA method. Source B38
requires probability-weighted discounted cash flows, B49–53 constrain evidence
and market consistency, B74–78 prevent inconsistent risk/discount treatment,
and B86–89 distinguish entity compensation from a generic dispersion measure.
