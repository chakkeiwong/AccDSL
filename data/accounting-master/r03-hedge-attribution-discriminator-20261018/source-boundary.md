Proposed internal answer: retain the designated relationship as the measurement
unit. Reject a general per-origin-minimum replacement. For a stopped part,
require documented effective-gain attribution and an argument governing shared
ineffectiveness. Where these are missing, refuse to invent the split.

Strongest competing interpretation: contemporaneous designation and a
systematic allocation of historical effective gains might determine the
answer once fully specified. These examples do not refute that possibility.
A useful next source must say how that attribution is constructed, handle
mixed component excesses and opposed origin gains, and explain the actual
stopped amount rather than merely restating the relationship total or
presentation rule. No professional referral is needed to preserve this
precise remaining internal question.
