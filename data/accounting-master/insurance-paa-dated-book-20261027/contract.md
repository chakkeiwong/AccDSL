# InsurancePAADatedBook/1: conditional interim group/report connection

This profile connects the chapter16 short-duration PAA example to unchanged
DeclaredCompanyProgram/2, CompanyBook/1 and CompanyBookReport/1. It is a proposed
synthetic reference interface, not a registered production endpoint. It never
adopts actual company facts or grants accounting/posting authority.

Call `bridge.propose(request)`. `authored.fixture(case, chart, complement)` is a
complete independent example constructor, not a source extractor. The top-level
request has exactly `profile`, `payload` and `payload_sha256`. The digest uses
the existing core sorted-key compact UTF-8 JSON encoding with finite numbers.
All money and fractions are canonical numerator/denominator strings.

The payload contains the selected case/chart/complement, entity/currency,
calendar, two group records, amounts, explicit findings, cash policy, exact
source-edition identifier and synthetic assumption scope. The closed field sets
and complete serialized examples are retained in authored.py and attempt-01/
cases.json. The native program additionally identifies every method, schema,
account map, input binding, event and report by the existing core identities.

The three selected cases are base, separate_transferred_risk and
nonperformance_loss. The account chart is named or renamed; an optional supplied
equipment17/equity17 complement is preserved by every event. The amounts and
financial premises are the retained chapter/dossier inputs. This finite profile
refuses other amounts instead of presenting a fixed worked example as a general
actuarial calculator. Distinct issued/held IDs, their common owner, annual cohort,
coverage/recognition dates and the held-to-issued link are mandatory. The group
IDs remain in typed book memory and each event input; all noncash and cash
movements retain their group through the event and method bindings.

Coverage runs from2024-01-01 to2025-01-01 exclusive. The interim observation
boundary is2024-07-02 00:00, exactly183/366 elapsed days. These are supplied
synthetic dates. Recognition and premium flows are January1; subsequent service,
claims and recovery measurements/payments are July2, in the explicit eight-event
order. A step memory and exact prior balances prevent skipping, duplicating or
reordering the fixed computation. Event-date and group bindings refuse changed
dates or issued/held substitution. This does not recover actual chronology.

The result `proposed_conditional` contains the full compiled native program,
complete native execution, group records, calendar qualification and unresolved
conditions. Native trace records contain every before/after state, journal,
gross cash flow and report request/result. The wrapper's expected_steps are an
explanation of its compilation, not the acceptance oracle; independently authored
expectations and complete financial report comparisons supply that oracle.

The issued group rolls premium120 into remaining coverage120 and releases60;
claims42 less paid30 leave12. The held group rolls premium24 into coverage24
and releases12; expected covered claims20 plus independently supplied transferred
risk less non-performance and paid recovery15 leave its claims asset. The base
held asset is6, the transferred-risk alternative13/2 and the non-performance
alternative4. These separately mapped balances remain gross in the report.

Report outputs include every mapped account's opening/closing line, assets and
liabilities, income/expense/profit, equity movement reconciliation, gross
operating/investing/financing cash flows and the supplied unresolved note.
Cash101 is identical across the three variants. Baseline assets119, liabilities72,
profit27 and equity47 change only through the specified held measurement.
The book uses the full coverage-year interval and reports the July2 interim
state; it does not invent the remaining half-year history or close the year.

Missing findings or risk/non-performance amounts return `unresolved` with no
compiled execution. Contradicted assumptions and values outside the selected
financial/calendar domain return `unsupported`. Malformed structures, stale
digests, identity/link mismatches and changed source locks return `invalid`.
If a compiled native program refuses, the wrapper returns `native_refusal` and
retains its stopped execution. It does not fabricate a final report. Native
prefix/suffix records and complete current-state identity have been independently
checked for five event-sequence/date/group failures.

All adapter source/accounting/posting/group/chronology admission flags are false.
Native records also keep committed, physical_durability, method_eligible,
fiscal_complete and notes_complete false. Uniform risk release, PAA eligibility,
short claims settlement, no onerous remaining coverage and the operating cash
classification are supplied premises. The exact retained AASB17 edition supports
the stated conditional rules; current applicability and actual facts remain
separate. The later undated recovery-change ledger is a different profile with
its original missing facts unchanged.
