The bank leverage-buffer provision specifically references the controlling
G-SIB BHC's Method 1 surcharge and §217.403(d)'s effective-date provisions.
The holding company's overall surcharge instead takes the greater of Method 1
and Method 2. Those are different quantities. Identification at the 130-basis-
point Method 1 threshold, annual score calculation, table lookup, initial
application and later increases/decreases have separate conditions and dates.
Board authority to adjust scope, amounts and compliance dates also remains.

Method 1 uses twelve reported systemic indicators, Board-published global
denominators, literal table weights and a cap on the substitutability category.
The reported indicator date, denominator vintage/publication, determination
date and effective date cannot be merged. The retained rule has integer-ended
score bands, but this unit does not establish the treatment of fractional
scores or implement the above-table rule. Published 6.67% weights are retained
literally rather than silently normalized. No actual bank input, score,
surcharge, leverage buffer or dividend is computed.
