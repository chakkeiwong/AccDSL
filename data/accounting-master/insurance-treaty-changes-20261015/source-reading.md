# Insurance treaty and estimate source record

15 October2026. Retained EU2023/1803 IFRS17 technical clauses read:44–52,
62–68,72–77,87–91,B72,B96–97,B119C–F,B128–132. Exact selected passages and
PDF/text hashes are in source-support.json. Historical authority only.

Critical source distinctions: B119E first allocates group loss to covered
contracts; B119D applies expected claims recovery to that loss; B119F limits
the loss-recovery component. Paragraph66(c)(i) takes the matched underlying
loss reversal to held profit rather than CSM, and66(bb) avoids re-posting
changes already in fulfilment cash flows. Paragraph67 distinguishes reinsurer
non-performance from future service. Modifications72–77 require legal-term
and equivalent-premium inputs; an existing-right exercise is not a modification.
GMM future-service changes consume CSM and then loss component in the stated
order. Current-rate measurement differs from initial-rate CSM/finance allocation.

Five groups pass58 exact checks, including complete issued/held lifetime cash
and equity, loss-recovery tracking, CSM exhaustion/restoration and fixed-claim
OCI unwind. Risk adjustment and all valuations are explicit inputs, not a
complete stochastic/financial model. The zero-risk-adjustment finance example
has no remaining nonfinancial uncertainty and does not prescribe zero RA for
uncertain claims. A portfolio OCI election is supplied, not inferred from an
observed reported OCI balance. The modification procedure remains conditional
on legal terms and an equivalent-premium valuation; it is not tested on an
actual changed treaty.

The target reader is an insurance modeller/implementer familiar with the prior
issued and held group examples. They should follow the covered subset, the
estimate change, service release and finance bridge without opening fixtures.
Human voice/comprehension and independent domain review remain distinct.
