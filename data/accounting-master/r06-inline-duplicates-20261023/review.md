# Skeptical source and result review

23 October 2026; author review, no independent or human acceptance.

The pre-run audit correctly rejected an historical-only baseline: the retained
official index lists corrected 2026 sources as well as the exact 2013 pair linked
by the note. The final inquiry preserves both, and keeps the 2018 guidance
separate. Successful acquisition, matching selected text, source counts and
integrity checks are not promotion criteria for an implementation or policy.

The strongest alternative reading is that the Primer's permission supplies a
general merging rule. It does not remove the per-input obligations in Part 1.
The result therefore states necessary compatibility conditions and expressly
leaves sufficiency open. The ID argument applies to unequal non-absent IDs; the
attribute argument applies to unequal values for the same expanded name. An
earlier overbroad inference about any custom-attribute difference was narrowed
before recording. Disjoint attributes or missing optional constraints do not
create that particular contradiction.

An early interpretation treated the language addition as normative. Actual
HTML inspection disproved that classification: both anchored sentences occur
inside a nonNormativeNote div. The ledgers and result use clarification, preserve
the Primer tension, and infer neither a new MUST nor a precise correction date.
Appendix E's omission of a separate 11.2.2 entry cannot prove unchanged text.
The actual selected comparison, rather than the history summary, supports the
presence/absence conclusion.

The exact tuple wording can appear redundant because tuple-content uniqueness
and a same-order validation sentence coexist. The result retains both clauses,
their attribute-inclusive whitespace definition and ordered mapping. It does
not quietly replace the text with an invented algorithm or certify a schema.
The corrected F/N relationship expression remains distinct from the historical
f/F expression; neither is silently repaired in the original source.

All 14 claim rows refer to the 42 exact retained anchors. Nine selected section
blocks were compared; four are identical. Equality does not give independent
reading credit, and no whole-specification reading is claimed. Broad truncated
outputs supplied no reading credit; the supporting spans were subsequently
read in bounded complete pieces. The only history images are navigation logos;
there is no omitted technical image in this six-document set.

The weakest evidence concerns broader conformance, exact transformation
functions and installed behavior, because those were outside this unit. They
are limitations, not positive findings. A checked normative provision requiring
distinct output occurrences, changing the mapped attribute conditions, or
contradicting the selected obligations would overturn or narrow the local
compatibility deductions. A different official edition would require a new
dated comparison, rather than retroactively changing this retained evidence.

No mathematical tooling or acquired implementation was used. The two
incompatibility deductions use only equality for one output attribute; no XML
schema validity claim follows. No sources, manuscript content or independent
acceptance were manufactured. No candidate rejection has become a research-
direction rejection, and no global continuation veto has fired.
