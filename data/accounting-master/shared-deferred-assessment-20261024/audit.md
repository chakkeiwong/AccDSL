# Deferred assessment audit before implementation

24 October 2026. MC05/11/13/16/24/26/28/35; G2/G4. The active plan survives
only with the explicit restrictions below. Current reassessment already carries
aggregate and lease-right tax bases but calculates current tax only. Its examples
use zero bases for information-completeness testing; treating those zeros as
actual deferred-tax facts would be wrong. New fixtures must declare economically
interpretable bases and rebind the complete prior request.

The complete 26-account state and statement bridge can accommodate movements in
DTA, DTL and deferred expense while preserving cash and current tax. Existing
opening DTA 10 and DTL 4 have no item-level assessed schedule in this chain. A
caller must supply a complete, explicitly supported allocation of those recorded
amounts before any incremental calculation. Matching totals alone does not prove
the accounting admission of that allocation. This remains a conditional model.

## Assumption and default audit

| Choice | Provenance/status | Justification | Failure mode | Early check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pure same-date successor of reassessment | Derived engineering subset | Reuses actual financial book | Lost history or duplicate assessment | Exact predecessor and unique identity |

Primary pass criteria are exact item population, immutable history, complete
opening allocation, correct sign and recognition limits, one-time capacity use,
incremental journal and four statement identities. Source/admission uncertainty
blocks statutory promotion, not this conditional engineering test. Refused cases
must return no changed financial state. Counts are explanatory. No whole phase
closes; new-period assessment, carry compatibility and broader methods stay open.
