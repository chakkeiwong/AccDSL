## Equation review

The annual bridge is an identity on displayed USD-million centres. The first
line establishes the unallocated principal difference; the second adds the two
reported measurement changes. It does not turn a net-balance movement into
interest expense, hedge cash or an FX entry. Exact rational checks reproduce
the values from source rows. No unknown sign or probability enters the identity.

The interest display is a conditional contract calculation: USD 1,000 million,
simple annual rate 0.05, 60 actual days and denominator 360. It gives 25/3
million; three balanced journal entries produce zero final principal/payable
and incremental cash/equity −25/3. Interest and fees are not silently omitted
to manufacture compatibility. The final negative incremental cash denotes a
reduction relative to a funded baseline, not an independently feasible company.

Both displays are used locally and need no equation number or external reference.
The surface auditor reports three environments because it also counts the nested
`aligned` environment. There are two new display objects. Its flags are
diagnostics; no labels, references or source facts were removed to clear them.

The strongest alternative explanation for an apparent numerical explanation is
that the residual merely combines undisclosed movements and differing measurement
bases. The text preserves that uncertainty. Instrument-level roll-forwards and
matched transactions could overturn the need to leave a particular residual
unallocated; the current aggregates cannot. The weakest evidence is the absence
of those facts and of independent/human assessment, not the exact arithmetic.
