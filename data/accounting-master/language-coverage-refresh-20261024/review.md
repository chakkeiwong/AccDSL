# What now constrains the company language

24 October 2026. The language has advanced beyond the last inventory's
description: unweighted families, finite typed policy expressions, family
recovery, portable identities and return/company composition now have checked
references. The old descriptions of those features as absent are stale.
General financial-statement DSL design is still incomplete.

The next task is the boundary between actual disclosures and executable
company inputs. The retained real-source work already preserves Apple's
statement rows, note dispositions, unresolved family question and conditional
subsystem calculations. It must be reused. Those records do not supply an
exact legal trial balance, inventory quantities and costs, asset cohorts,
contract schedules or the complete tax state required by the current company
references. Calling reported aggregate amounts an executable opening would
conceal missing economics and the consolidated/legal distinction.

The next result should therefore be a traceable requirements report: what a
named source establishes, what its measurement and context mean, what remains
unknown or outside the selected profile, which operations depend on it and
what additional evidence or design would permit execution. It must preserve
useful displayed amounts while refusing fabricated state. It must not rename
Apple's fiscal periods or import Northstar/P's method premises. The next plan
is docs/plans/accounting-real-company-input-readiness-20261024.md.
