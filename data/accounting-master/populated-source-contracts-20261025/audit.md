# Pre-execution audit: populated receivables

25 October 2026. MC02/04/05/08/11/13/16/24–28/34/35; S2–S4.

The inherited plan calls its example source-backed but supplies no real invoice
or invoice-level disclosure. Constructing one and calling it a filing would
invent evidence. The next reference therefore uses explicitly constructed
JSON source documents and supplied timing metadata. It tests source binding,
not the truth or completeness of a real company's claims. Actual disclosure
admission stays on the queue. The accepted lifecycle requires an empty opening;
we populate it by replaying sales and preserve that populated state through
carry, without bypassing that condition or modifying the frozen lifecycle.

Retained comparators: DeclaredReceivableLifecycle/1, its matched command text,
CompanyBookTransition/2 and P06 select_evidence. The wrapper will bind every
sale to an exact UTF-8 source span, select original/latest-available assertion
lineages at explicit UTC and ingestion cutoffs, and retain both original and
revised assertions. Revisions reconstruct an alternative full history from
the opening: they are not retroactive mutations or newly implemented contract
amendments. Historical execution and forecasting admission stay false.

Primary criterion: exact source/claim/context binding and full equality to a
separately authored lifecycle program, with independent financial targets.
Two sales (30 and 40, each cost 10) from cash 100/stock 20 create claims 70.
Receipt 10 leaves cash 110/claims 60/stock 0, profit 50, assets/equity 170 and
operating cash 10. Carry preserves A=20/B=40. Following receipts 20 and 40
leave cash 170/claims 0, next-period profit 0 and operating cash 60.

Claim A's corrected due date changes overdue assessments, not consideration,
cash or profit. The two assertions are alternative available readings of the
same sale, never two sales. Exact source spans, rejected altered bytes,
duplicate identities, incompatible supersession, competing revisions, future
availability, unknown dates/amounts, and source-free monetary commands are
discriminating checks. Unknown values remain tagged; method approval remains
an explicit separate assumption. A claim count or balance identity alone is
explanatory. Local code/test failures trigger repair; missing real invoices
blocks actual-entity admission, not this constructed-contract diagnostic.

Engineering limits reuse the finite lifecycle and P06 chronology; a maximum
128 assertions and 32 documents is a prototype bound, not measured capacity.
Use exact rational amounts, explicitly supplied UTC instants and the existing
strict-after-due-date policy. No implied business calendar, zero imputation,
probability or standard-compliance default is introduced. Preserve source
text, extracted assertions, mapping premise, scenario commands and results as
separate fields.

Audit passes for this qualified scope. Record focused tests, exact typed/text
comparisons, source locks, manifest and read-only replay here before updating
the active checkpoint. No new manuscript build is required for an unaccepted
prototype; consolidate additions after the contract and revision semantics are
settled. This bounded work does not close the original real-source requirement.

Recovery audit: the first run failed because a transported regular-expression

balances, statement totals and direct typed-program equality on both charts.
The source-backed fields replace template placeholders; recognition approval,
receipt amounts and assessment policy remain separate supplied assumptions.
Known source availability must not place a correction before its predecessor.
Unknown timing conservatively blocks its bound lineage even in an original
view; this is a prototype veto, not proof of source inadmissibility. P06 still
preserves unknown historical byte vintage and does not establish real temporal
admission. No new method, ranking or accounting standard is being promoted.
