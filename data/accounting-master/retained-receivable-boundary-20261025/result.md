# Retained receivable disclosures do not identify executable claims

25 October 2026. Seven focused tests and exact replay pass for annual, interim
and combined views. The combined view preserves 12 observations, two directly
inspected receivable notes and 18 explicit input requirements. All 185 locked
files and the existing readiness result are unchanged. This is a source-bound
partial representation; it produces neither claims nor an executable program.

The annual statement displays accounts receivable, net of 33,410 at
28 September 2024, with a 29,508 comparison at 30 September 2023. The interim
statement displays 29,639 at 28 December 2024, with 33,410 as its comparison.
All amounts retain their original consolidated context and USD-million display
precision. They are not exact gross claim amounts. The cash-flow readings
of (3,788) annually and 3,597 in the interim are operating-asset adjustments,
not gross customer collections. Original reading-file entries, contexts,
comparison dates, PDF/HTML hashes and note passages are retained separately.

There is also a concrete method mismatch. The annual PDF, page 41 (printed 38),
and the retained interim HTML's Accounts Receivable section state that vendor
component sales are excluded from products net sales, with gains reducing
products cost of sales when the related final products are sold. The declared
credit-sale lifecycle instead recognizes sale consideration as revenue when
the event occurs. Applying it to these vendor transactions would be wrong
relative to that disclosed treatment. The policy note does not by itself
supply all facts needed to implement the vendor method.

Concentration percentages retain useful group information, but they identify
neither the individual claims nor a complete set of named counterparties.
Issuance dates, due dates, delivered units, gross consideration, legal-book
allocation, receipts, recognition facts and inventory costs remain unresolved.
Source documents are preserved; their original byte availability, ingestion
and review clocks remain unknown. The annual and interim source records carry
no supersession relation. A repeated September balance is not a new revision.

The strongest alternative explanation is that the missing schedule exists
elsewhere. This comparison covers the retained statement readings and the two
inspected receivable notes; it does not prove absence from every possible
contract, system or disclosure. A supported schedule and reviewed method could
change execution eligibility. No actual claim has been fabricated to make the
current prototype run. Source identity and field coverage are checked; semantic
completeness and independent accounting acceptance remain open.

The plan's proposed action on missing schedules is now triggered. Reuse the
existing source-constrained partial state, distinguish an unknown population
from a declared empty one, and specify which queries remain meaningful. Avoid
constructing another company-state model. Version 4.20 remains 515 pages and
not_ready. S1–S5 remain partial, S6 held, S7 deferred; no whole requirement closes.
