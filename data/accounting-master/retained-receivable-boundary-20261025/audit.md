# Pre-execution source-boundary audit

25 October 2026. The retained annual balance-sheet reading explicitly labels
trade accounts receivable as net. The cash-flow entry sits under changes in
operating assets and liabilities. Neither has the meaning of a gross sale or
customer collection. Annual PDF page 41 (printed 38), Accounts Receivable,
distinguishes trade from vendor non-trade receivables: component sales to
manufacturing vendors are excluded from products net sales, with gains reducing
products cost of sales when the final products are sold. The retained interim
HTML repeats that policy and adds concentration percentages, not customer IDs
or invoice schedules.

The newly checked lifecycle recognizes consideration as revenue and inventory
cost as cost of sales at the declared sale. Applying it to vendor non-trade
receivables would therefore compute a different accounting treatment from the
one disclosed. This is a method mismatch, independently of the missing claim
population. Record the source reading and precise mismatch; do not create a
new vendor method from the short policy note alone.

Question and exact comparator are in the linked phase plan. The primary check
is preservation of all 12 relevant annual/interim balance, comparison and
cash-flow observations, agreement with independent original reading files, and
explicit coverage of every source reading field plus necessary lifecycle
premises. Recompute the existing readiness result and retain its original
obligations and whole-package identity. The original 139-item inventory is
preservation evidence, not proof of full semantic completeness.

No round-off, currency, calendar, recognition or invoice-allocation default is
introduced. The only default is the already retained named-source mode;
historical byte clocks are unknown. Source context is known at consolidated
level, while a legal book and claim allocation are unresolved. Available
metadata is preserved even though the selected execution profile is unsupported.
The input mapping can fail while the partial representation remains useful.

Source-byte mismatches, altered original readings, dropped obligations and
promoted execution status veto the artifact. Missing schedules reject the
execution mapping and trigger partial-population design, not a continuation
veto. No stochastic inference or forecast study is run. Audit passes for this
bounded retained-source comparison. No new external accounting authority is
claimed; conclusions describe the inspected issuer disclosures and local code.
