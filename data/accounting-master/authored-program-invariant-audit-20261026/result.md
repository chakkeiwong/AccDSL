# Cross-record lifecycle duties are not implied by typed books

26 October 2026. Eighteen derived cases across two company charts confirm eight
missing outer-control relations (sixteen counterexamples) and one already
covered structural rule. The original lifecycle refuses claim/receivable total
disagreement, an absent sale event, invalid issue/due dates, negative assessment
amounts, inconsistent unassessed placeholders, future assessment and an incorrect
overdue snapshot. Typed-book validation and the authored program's sale/report
path accept those eight changed methods. Empty customer strings already refuse
under the typed memory schema and are not a gap.

Every counterexample retains the genuine period opening, authors the changed
claim field in the sale method, and checks the exact resulting state before
comparing the original lifecycle control. The initial attempt used a post-sale
report opening and correctly hit opening_at_period_start; it was not evidence
about invariants. See recovery-audit.md. All 37 protected files remain unchanged.
