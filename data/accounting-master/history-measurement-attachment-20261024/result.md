24 October 2026. HistoryMeasurementAttachment/1 preserves both complete parent
definitions and hashes, gives their variables disjoint namespaces, and conjoins
all original constraints with explicit snapshot or period-movement mappings.
It does not accept parent result/witness objects. Snapshot mappings use the
identical declared instant; flow mappings sum only supplied event incidence in
the exact half-open period and exclude the opening balance. Value context,
dimension, scale, quantity and proposed mapping interpretation remain explicit.

Discriminating cases pass: two parent families can each be feasible while their
mapping is infeasible; choosing one valid measurement assignment can wrongly
exclude a different valid joint history. Two joint witnesses retain closing
cash 15 while openings are 10 versus 8, movements 5 versus 7 and cumulative sales
5 versus 7. Both original parents and every supplied mapping are independently
checked by exact Fraction replay. Same textual variable names in different
parents cannot collide.

obligations. Missing premises prevent solving. Other unresolved obligations
permit only a relaxed candidate, with target existence unknown and formulation
completeness false. Source/accounting admission, native execution and executable
opening remain false/absent. A retained real-source temporal definition cannot
silently attach to the synthetic book: its value context fails exact mapping
checks. This does not supply the missing real-company mapping.

285 checks, 58 targeted unit methods, ten cases and 244 protected inputs pass.

and accounting modules. Semantic replay permits alternative valid witnesses
