# Retained Apple inputs: ready to read, not ready to execute

24 October 2026. The reference passes 2,113 deterministic checks against the
retained package, independent reading files and both selected target schemas.
It preserves 139 inventory entries, 102 annual and 100 interim readings, and
27 opening/comparison balances in each original statement collection. The
manifest protects 79 input files. No accounting or mission requirement closes.

The selected targets remain blocked for distinct reasons. CompanyOperationsDraft/1
requires a calendar year, fixed accounts, homogeneous inventory, complete claims
and asset cohorts, simple annual financing and an immediate-deduction tax model.
RetainedCompanyProgramDraft/1 binds the complete synthetic P case. Neither can
accept the retained Apple disclosures as an executable opening. Missing facts
do not justify substituting P's components, tax findings or event schedule.

Useful evidence remains available. Annual cash and equivalents are displayed
as 29,943, inventory as 7,286 and net PPE as 45,680, all in USD million at
28 September 2024. These figures preserve consolidated context and reported
precision; they do not identify unrestricted legal cash, inventory units or
cohort costs and lives. The displayed current and noncurrent term-debt captions
sum to 96,662 annually and 94,804 at the interim date. These are explicitly
labelled arithmetic derivations, not total debt or exact latent principal.
The annual debt-issuance dash remains a dash.

Fifteen dependencies cover every top-level field of each selected target and
connect observations and original obligations to affected operations, reports
and carry. Thirteen identify unavailable execution state. Target-specific scope
limitations are retained separately. Exact historical byte clocks remain unknown;
that limits historical as-of admission but does not block named-source reading.
Documentary assumptions remain proposed assumptions and resolve no state by
their mere presence. The actual-history family and conditional subsystem
results are preserved unchanged.

Validation rejects cross-issuer/package/view substitutions, later-only readings,
invented zeros, fiscal-date relabeling, altered contexts, dropped dependencies,
forged ready status and modified result amounts. It compares every selected
original reading to the separate retained reading file, rather than using only
the projection as its own oracle. Result acceptance recomputes the full finite
projection. The contract does not yet generalize to another packet or target.

The first checker failed on the original opening-row prefix. The initial code
and failure are preserved in before-comparison-key-repair/. Repair normalizes
only lookup keys; original row IDs and records remain unchanged. This was a
checker defect, not evidence against the source or simulation direction.
