# Commitment source reading and interpretation

8 October 2026. The declared population comprises full annual Notes 8/12,
interim Note 9, the annual lease-policy paragraph, three annual MD&A passages
and the interim change/manufacturing passages. Sixteen note units partition
the three complete note spans; six further passages record dependencies.
Fifty monetary table cells cover ROU assets, recognised lease liabilities,
maturities and purchase commitments. These are bounded author interpretations,
not independently certified semantic coverage of the whole filing.

Original annual PDF pages 28, 37, 45, 48 and 49 were visually inspected at
readable resolution, including table headings, units, comparative years and
continuations. Their printed pages are 25, 34, 42, 45 and 46. The selected
interim source paragraphs were inspected as retained HTML/text and as the
single-page derived print view. That image is not an original browser view;
inline-XBRL namespace unwrapping preserved text and the original HTML remains
retained. No network, installation or GPU work was needed.

The first source-partition check failed twice because the marker `Contingencies`
matched the note title before its subsection. The failed checker, evidence
and outputs are preserved in `first-evidence-attempt/`. An exact subsection
boundary repairs the partition; all 110 current source/arithmetic checks pass.
The no-lease-note check also now uses the actual ten interim headings, rather
than a nonexistent title field. Journal checks explicitly balance debit and
credit postings. None of those software repairs changes original source bytes.

The annual lease liability is 12,430 million, including current 1,632 and
noncurrent 10,798. Maturities are 14,790 before 2,360 imputed interest. ROU assets
are 11,303; asset/liability equality is not asserted. The 849 of additional
uncommenced payments is separate; 14,790+849=15,639 is compatible with the
rounded 15.6 billion MD&A disclosure under the conditional rounding hypothesis.
That check cannot prove contract population identity, book an additional
liability or allocate commencement cash dates. The 1,991 fiscal-2025 maturity
bucket is also distinct from the current carrying amount.

Reported typical original terms, renewal options, weighted remaining terms and
aggregate discount rates have different meanings. The 3.1% is not a complete
contract discount curve. Fixed operating cost 2.0 billion differs from fixed
operating cash 1.9 and noncash ROU additions 1.0. Variable lease cost 13.8
relates primarily to output purchases and covers the source's wider lease
scope; it is neither a fixed future payment nor safely additive to manufacturing
obligations without identifying overlap. Note 1's component combination applies
to the named facility classes, not every lease.

A18 is retained as historical selected interpretation. Its note-table boundary
is correct, but it cannot support a whole-file claim that shorter-term amounts
are all unquantified. Annual MD&A supplies manufacturing 53.0/52.9 and other
purchase 12.0/4.1 billion (total/within twelve months). Note 12 reports 11,226
million for contracts with remaining terms over one year, of which 3,206 is
payable in fiscal 2025. Contract term and payment date are different filters.
No inspected mapping proves that 12.0 minus 11.226 is a short-contract total.

The December manufacturing balance is 39.8 billion, all within twelve months.
The 13.2 displayed-centre decline is not cash or actual purchases. The authored
model treats a comparable population as undelivered fixed-price commitments
removed on delivery, with no cancellation/price/FX changes. Adding new orders
0 or 5 yields delivery 13.2 or 18.2; cash versus payable settlement then changes
the subsystem statements. All goods remain unsold, taxes/prepayments/opening
payables are omitted. The source does not establish that model for every
reported obligation, and the four witnesses do not fit all Apple statements.

The interim no-material-change statement remains qualitative; it does not
create exact December lease balances or unchanged underlying contracts.
Management's dated litigation assessment retains its reasonable-possibility,
materiality and excess-over-accrual qualifications. Neither that wording nor
its repetition implies zero losses. The annual note's concentration, custom
component, capacity, supplier-choice and manufacturing-location passages have
query consequences without invented probabilities or a manufactured shock size.
Individual litigation matters and wider risk-factor dependencies remain outside
this unit and visible in the whole-package queue.
