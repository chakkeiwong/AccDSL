# Note 20 and Note 33 source reading

The retained complete 2025 Form 10-K Note 20 was read across PDF 283–284 /
printed 281–282 at normal resolution. The first page has a legible table and
footnotes (a)–(h); the second continues with rates, the parent guarantee
paragraph and the unsecured-debt statement. The table expressly reports
carrying values by remaining contractual-maturity band, in US$ millions,
with separate Parent company and Subsidiaries blocks. Its separate beneficial-
interest table is not part of the 435,206 long-term-debt total. Column
alignment and signs were checked against the rendered page.

The Parent company block totals 253,039 in 2025 and 238,023 in 2024. Note 33's
parent-only long-term-debt balance is 254,633 and 239,626. The respective
displayed residuals, 1,594 and 1,603, equal Note 20's Subsidiaries junior
subordinated-debt subtotal in each year. This is an exact two-year arithmetic
relation. Note 20 places that row under Subsidiaries; neither page explains
why the same-sized amount appears in a parent-only balance. It would be wrong
to assert legal issuer, guarantee or consolidation mechanism from the match.

The 2025 Note 20 parent under-one-year carrying amount is 14,298; Note 33
footnote (c) reports 14.3 billion of parent debt contractually maturing in
2026. The parent 1–5-year carrying amount plus the junior row's 1–5-year
amount is 102,677; the four Note 33 2027–2030 displayed maturity figures sum
to 102.7 billion. Differences of 2 and 23 million sit within the displayed
rounding granularity but do not establish identical principal and carrying
amounts. Note 20 explicitly includes premiums/discounts, costs, valuation
and fair-value adjustments; its zero-coupon footnote gives 18.1 billion
carrying amount versus 57.6 billion contractual maturity principal for a
separately described consolidated set.

Note 20's consolidated long-term debt is 435,206. Footnote (h)'s five annual
numbers are consolidated carrying values, not the parent-only contractual
schedule. The page-284 parent guarantee paragraph reports 47.6 billion of
guaranteed subsidiary debt and structured notes at 2025 year-end, and 41.2
billion at 2024 year-end. It does not turn those amounts into parent-issued
debt or supply guarantee fair value, recognition, future payment or default
probability. Source and measurement boundaries remain attached to the figures.

The 50 checks bind 14 selected rows, eight qualifications, Note 33 comparator
hash and displayed arithmetic. The source reading is bounded, not a full
contract/issuer structure audit or independent accounting verdict.
