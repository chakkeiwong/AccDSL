8.10
Unweighted families require relational memory
For an unweighted family, replace probabilities with a relation of feasible successors, observations
and outputs. Exact aggregation must preserve joint possibilities and persistent cross-time
constraints. One-step marginal sets alone are insufficient.
A fixed-or-floating loan with two-year interest paths (5, 5) and (7, 7) has one-step marginal set
{5, 7} at each date. Recombining those sets creates (5, 7) and (7, 5), which require unmodeled
contract changes. Retaining the latent type or equivalent memory prevents these paths.
An enlarged family can sometimes provide an outer bound for a worst-case query. It is not an
exact family of possible companies. A harmful witness from the enlarged set must be validated
against original constraints before being described as economically possible. Conservatism does
not excuse mislabeling the target.
8.11
Approximation needs a query-specific error
A cash error of 0.1 may be harmless for a chart and decisive at a covenant. For a smooth output,
state error can imply an output bound under regularity. For a default indicator, an arbitrarily
small change near a threshold can reverse the classification.
If computed cash lies in [19.95, 20.05] and payment required is 20, a midpoint cannot establish
feasibility for the whole region. The correct classification is unresolved unless a stronger method
determines the sign. Refinement should focus on this boundary rather than indiscriminately
increasing precision.
If f is Lipschitz with known constant L in a stated norm and ∥x−ˆx∥≤ϵ, then |f(x)−f(ˆx)| ≤Lϵ.
