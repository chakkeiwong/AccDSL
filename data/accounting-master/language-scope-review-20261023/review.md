# What remains in the financial-statement language

The current company language can describe repeated operations across several
years and carry claims, inventory, assets and debt into later statements. Its
text and typed inputs lead to the same bounded reference execution. Revisions,
partial years, unavailable information and an explicit settlement policy now
have checked consequences. The financial-statement DSL is still incomplete.

Three gaps determine the next work. First, an author can write individual
scenario paths but cannot declare one unweighted family and obtain all its
joint financial results through the same language. Second, policy expressions
and their types, references and information dependencies are not part of the
company frontend. Third, the richer corporate, financing, bank and insurance
methods have separate contracts and examples whose connection to this
frontend remains unspecified. Durable recovery is another integration task;
pure reference replay does not demonstrate persistence.

The historical Core 0 grammar includes expressions, but its checked validator
is deliberately restricted to the debt example. It cannot certify the newer
company profile. Conversely, the original description of corporate accounting
as only an inventory of duties is stale: annual and multi-year company paths,
CompanyComposition and SectorCompany supply substantive connected calculations
within their different scopes. Joining them requires preserving entities,
accounting memory, eligibility and source identities. Renaming another case
as Northstar would not provide that connection.

The reviewed inventory preserves all 53 original obligations, six interactions
and 46 operations, including their prior evidence. Each obligation is assessed
separately for syntax, meaning, connected execution and acceptance evidence.
Every operation is mapped to the actual 14-kind company enum or explicitly
identified as a separate method, admission inquiry, exchange or research
design. The mapping is a reviewed design assessment; its mechanical checks
verify coverage and bindings, not the correctness of every prior method.

## Next design sequence

1. Add explicit unweighted scenario-family authoring around the existing
   company paths. Match text and typed inputs, bind their common historical
   opening, retain every member's joint statements and preserve partial years.
   Scenario membership must not imply probabilities, historical admissibility
   or exhaustive coverage of possible futures.
2. Specify typed expressions and conditional policy decisions using the same
   paths. Define units, references, unknown propagation, information timing and
   admissible operations before extending evaluation.
3. Specify company/family checkpoint and recovery exchanges, including absent
   and completed commits, exact retries, changed inputs and failed migration.
   Keep reference exchanges separate from physical durability evidence.
4. Connect selected richer methods through explicit module contracts and
   preserve the residual source, real-case and acceptance obligations.
5. Integrate a coherent group of settled changes into a protected manuscript
   successor and review the resulting explanation and contracts.

This order is a dependency judgment: family identity and retained outcomes
make later policy branching and recovery concrete. It does not declare source
research complete or require every obligation to receive new code. S1
continues on consequential source leads. S6 implementation remains held and
S7 predictive evaluation remains deferred. The first next unit needs no new
data, accounting rule, behavioral estimate or outside approval.

## Scope of review

The review inspected the retained obligation/operation records, current
coverage deltas, company frontend, operation-path schema and execution, Core 0
contract, current language chapter, and the two company/sector composition
contracts. It did not reread all 413 manuscript pages or rerun every historical
method. Prior evidence is preserved by exact records and file locks; where
new implementation evidence is absent, the inventory says so. No full
requirement, source-validity judgment or acceptance state is promoted.

| Decision | Primary criterion | Veto status | Main uncertainty | Next action | Not concluded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proceed to scenario-family authoring | Every retained identity reviewed once with concrete residual and next work | Verify identity sets, original records, source locks and actual operation kinds | Independent implementability and broader methods not revalidated | Execute the audited family plan | Full DSL completion, accounting compliance, production readiness or forecast accuracy |

The strongest alternative explanation is that a prior separate profile already
solves a particular join. The preserved operation evidence must be consulted
before each repair so such work is reused. A supported, matched family
frontend already connected to the current path would overturn the immediate
priority; none exists in the inspected frontend or path contract.
