The general book boundary precedes expanding the mixed timeline to every native
clock. A record can identify an account without specifying its economic role;
an account mapping must be declared and checked. Currency labels cannot perform
FX conversion. Monetary and memory staging need a shared before-state identity,
one proposed after-state and explicit verification before provisional application.

This matrix distinguishes inspected source contracts from retained older evidence.
It is not a claim that every code path,sector method or manuscript page was audited.
