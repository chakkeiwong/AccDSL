# Joint observation refinement: pre-run evidence contract

27 October 2026. Implements the audited plan at
docs/plans/accounting-joint-observation-refinement-20261027.md. MC03/05/11/12/13/
18/24/25/26/28/33/34; W01/03/04/11/12/13/16. Design evidence only.

Question: which coarse total decision rules survive a supplied refinement of
either stage's observations, and does any extra assignment reach a different
known financial row? Baselines are freshly checked coarse and refined results
on the identical protected joint frontier. Renaming supplies an equality control.
Deliberately collapsed labels are illustrative hypotheses, not observed facts.

The primary criteria are surjective erasure of full decision keys, equality of
erased refusal terms, exact equality of lifted coarse candidates and refined
candidates constant on each erasure fiber, and per-rule equality of reached-row
identities. Preserve first-message and first-action memory in the second key.
Set-level reached-signature comparison alone would miss incorrect pairings, so
the producer additionally checks the reached rows of each individual lift.

Pre-run audit: the inherited single-date result allowed an empty-family policy
population under inconsistent clocks. This target instead leaves candidates
unassessed. Neither that state nor domain overflow produces policy lifts or
behavioral comparisons. Missing selected source content remains unresolved;
the empty reached-row signature is not an actual company behavior. Pending
source regions are never discarded to make a policy appear feasible.

Material choices: two stages, binary actions, inherited financial rows and the
12-key limit delimit this comparison. Coarse token collapse is a hypothesis;
test first-stage, second-stage and simultaneous collapse against the original
mapping before interpreting differences. Root, first known outcome, midpoint
and endpoint are diagnostic checkpoints, not a general-horizon proof. Counts
and runtime are explanatory; no ranking, utility or prediction claim follows.

Refusal controls test reverse erasure, changed raw keys, sources, actor, dates,
actions and budgets, missing pending support and stale external identities.
Corrupted protected sources invalidate the affected comparison; local defects
trigger repair within at most three 120-second CPU attempts. Preserve complete
inputs, source locks, failures, manifests and result/inference tables. GPU is
intentionally hidden. No agents, installations, fees, outreach or E3 work.
