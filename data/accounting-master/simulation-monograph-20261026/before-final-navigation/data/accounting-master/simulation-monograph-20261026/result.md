## Substantive result

Journals alone do not identify a company's economic state. The proposed
representation retains a joint family of evidence-compatible histories,
contracts, ownership and accounting memory, with explicit actor information
and optional justified probability measures. Trees can show selected branches;
the authoritative family can remain symbolic, share immutable records, project
only with preserved constraints, and split at economically relevant guards.
Aggregation must preserve the requested future observations, feasible policies
and outcomes. Approximation has a query-specific error statement and leaves
unexplored possibilities visible.

The financing chapters separate promised face, issue proceeds, fees, carrying
amount, market value, recovery and settlement dates. They derive a consistent
repair to the supplied note's inherited debt convention and show why positive
recovery exposes the mismatch. Equity chapters treat share ownership, fair and
discounted issues, preferences, conversion and financing constraints. Whole-company,
refinancing, information, tax-memory and two-state debt cases make the methods
concrete. Dynamic robustness, filtering, scenario reduction, learned-equation
objectives and their failure mechanisms receive separate derivations and source
boundaries.

The failure catalogue records **67 mechanisms**, discriminating cases and
proposed repairs, including errors at the joins between modules. It is a
systematic, extensible survey, not a proof that every possible simulator failure
has been enumerated.
