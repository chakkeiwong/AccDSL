# Source and inference boundary

Apple's stated exposures, beliefs and qualifications. They are not operative
licence contracts, developer agreements, privacy laws, tax returns, hedge
confirmations, investment ledgers, employment records, store leases or market
microdata. No third-party infringement, application withdrawal, content loss,
data incident, acquisition outcome, FX move, portfolio loss, tax liability or
stock-price cause is identified.

The annual-15 page break contains retained PDF furniture; raw bytes and offsets
remain intact. The only comparison normalization is `Safari®` to `Safari`
because the PDF text layer drops the registered mark. It is not a source edit.
Full-body retention counts 28 annual bodies and two interim bodies; it does not
establish semantic coverage, accounting compliance, forecast validity or
independent acceptance. Existing source units and prior dependencies are
context, not permission to merge populations or infer terms.
