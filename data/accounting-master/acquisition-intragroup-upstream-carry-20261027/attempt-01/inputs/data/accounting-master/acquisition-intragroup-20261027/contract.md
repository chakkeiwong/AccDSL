## Skeptical audit and evidence contract

The comparator is the preserved complete acquisition-consideration /2 result,
augmented by independently authored legal accounts, elimination entries and
report changes. A balance tie alone cannot pass. Primary criterion: exact full
legal/acquisition/closing worksheet, origin and financial-report comparisons for
credit and cash-settled downstream transfers, three consideration mixes and
three ordinary NCI elections. Pre-control dates, wrong partner/currency/amount,
partial elimination, unexplained movements and missing tax treatment veto the
candidate. Corrupted source/comparator invalidates the run. Counts and runtime
are explanatory; none establishes actual compliance or complete consolidation.

Pre-mortem: a correct inventory elimination could hide an omitted external tax
liability, misallocate a parent's profit to NCI, cancel only net cash, overwrite
legal books, or leak pre-control transactions. Full signed accounts, paired
cash origins, owner/NCI totals and refusal controls discriminate these failures.
No scientific stochastic ranking is involved. Budget: 120 seconds per attempt,
at most three attempts before revising the plan; CPU only, GPU hidden.

## Explicit premises and interface

One parent P and subsidiary S, acquired on 1 July 2025; same annual closing,
currency, unit and constant ordinary ownership as the protected base request.
The fixed chart starts with additional parent inventory 20 and capital 20.
On 1 October 2025 P transfers all inventory to S for 30 on credit. All remains
unsold at closing. Optional full settlement is 1 November 2025. No impairment,
external resale, upstream transfer, FX, loss of control or further ownership
change is implemented. Dates must lie in the controlled reporting interval.

The request binds the exact predecessor request, result hash, full three-stage
legal books, paired sale/settlement events, and elimination fraction 1. A
caller cannot supply 75% to scale eliminations. The fixed chart extension is
validated against every legal account and date. It never edits original inputs.

The group worksheet eliminates the 30 reciprocal receivable/payable (if unpaid),
30 parent sale against 30 cost of sales, and 10 unrealised margin against
inventory. The parent's legal cost of sales is 20; after both profit entries
it is zero, and group inventory is 20. Parent profit is adjusted in full, while
the subsidiary's separately derived acquisition-basis profit still determines
NCI. Credit settlement creates paired +30/-30 operating cash origins that
are eliminated gross, with zero group cash effect.

Tax is an explicit conditional dependency. The supported numerical branch
supplies separate seller-current-tax and buyer-inventory-tax-base premises,
enacted-rate premises, recoverability disposition and owner attribution. The
synthetic tests stipulate seller taxable margin 10, buyer tax base 30, group
inventory basis 20, rates 1/4, and current tax payable 5/2. Recognised DTA is
5/2 only under the supplied recoverability premise; an explicitly unrecognised
DTA is zero with that disposition retained. These are hypotheses for arithmetic,
not facts about an entity or tax jurisdiction. Both effects are stipulated to
belong to parent owners for this downstream test; general attribution remains
an open method obligation. A later upstream branch needs a separate contract.

An explicitly unresolved tax request retains the complete pre-transfer report,
legal books without newly assumed tax entries, full pretax elimination worksheet
and the tax dependency. It returns tax_method_required and NO final report.
It must not silently turn unknown tax into zero. Missing tax disposition is
invalid. No branch claims actual source, tax, posting or report authorisation.
