## Reader and method boundary

The target reader is an accounting modeller after the macroeconomic source
unit. They should explain why a disruption can affect supply, manufacturing,
delivery, services, demand, recovery spending and insurance through different
paths. The local sequence is: source event classes; affected actors and
locations; operational consequences; recovery and insurance qualifiers; facts
needed for a conditional journal or forecast. No displayed equation is needed
unless the source ledger exposes a new relation that cannot be stated clearly
in prose.

Cold-reader questions fixed before drafting: (1) which event classes are named;
(2) which populations can be affected; (3) what is reported versus assumed;
(4) why does a supply interruption differ from a service outage or demand
effect; (5) why does insurance wording not establish recovery; and (6) what
evidence is needed before a journal, loss estimate or forecast?
