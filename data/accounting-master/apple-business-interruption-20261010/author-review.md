trade, physical, cyber and health mechanisms; the affected populations include
customers, suppliers, manufacturers, carriers and other partners; the two

demand without an interruption. The example preserves that alternative and
also permits both conditions together. The weak point remains the absence of
actual product/route/order histories, so the section teaches representation
and cannot validate a company forecast. Wider MC01–MC35/G1–G18, human
