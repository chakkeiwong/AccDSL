## Exact finite scope and assumptions

Input binds debtor,creditor,subject,actor,event date,information cutoff,source
text,source availability,rationale and twelve yes/no/unknown assertions.
The source binds the entire assertion map,not merely an unrelated description.
All statuses are supplied hypotheses. Evidence after cutoff or unknown
availability retains unresolved status and no conditional routes. A supplied
debtor/creditor perspective must agree with subject. The prior instrument must
be debt: attempting to relabel it equity is invalid.

Debtor scope uses three-valued conjunction for the two compound exclusions.
Common control alone or shareholder status alone cannot establish an exclusion.
A known exclusion is retained even when other scope facts are unknown. If none
is known and an exclusion or renegotiation remains unknown,the route is
unresolved. Explicit renegotiation and all exclusions false permit only a
conditional Interpretation19 method-dependency list. Extinguishment and
partial settlement still require separate analysis. Unknown is never zero.

Creditor scope never applies Interpretation19. It retains asset derecognition,
prior classification/OCI,new-share measurement,tax and relationship dependencies.
Control and acquired-business assertions determine whether acquisition-scope
review is a candidate or remains unresolved; neither shares nor a percentage
establish control. A debt claim is never inserted into prior-equity goodwill.
Pre-existing relationship settlement is a separate question; creditor reports
remain unavailable under every route.
