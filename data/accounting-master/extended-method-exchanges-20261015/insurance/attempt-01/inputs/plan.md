## Skeptical audit and assumptions

The audit found a material adaptation risk: the preceding group profile changes
one group owner and must not be copied as proof of insurance atomicity. P4 uses
separate state owners and verifies the complete read set and exact write set.
Joint issued/held/accounts changes are committed together; a partially updated
world must fail even when its individual records are schema-valid. Replay is
looked up before current freshness; a new stale request remains rejected.

The audit also rejects whole-group loss recovery for partial cover, creation of
cash for a deemed premium, double posting a loss-recovery component already in
held FCF, using inception rather than claim-incurrence rates for PAA LIC,
recognising investment repayments as revenue, unadmitted VFA mitigation and
using equal marginal claim laws as a complete joint law. Finite source examples
cannot establish real valuation or transitive cross-profile compatibility.
