# InsuranceComponents/1 — finite documentary contract

This separate profile specifies conditional historical EU2023 IFRS17 examples.
It grants no actual-law, actuarial, runtime or old-profile compatibility claim.
All tax is uncomputed; FX is supplied absent. Reduced rational quantities have
illustrative million-USD units. Schema closure never establishes accounting
correctness. Sources and facts are separate immutable records with exact bytes.

The four state owners are existing issued group, replacement issued group,
held reinsurance group and legal accounts. Groups have unrestricted identifiers;
roles and membership cannot be inferred from the identifier's spelling. Opening
heads, context, information view, rule source and event are mandatory inputs.
Changed owners advance exactly one generation together, carrying the result
reference; unchanged owners retain their exact head. The journal and all result,
state, evidence and receipt dependencies become visible with that same decision.
Unresolved evaluations publish an explicit result and no changed head or journal.
Accounts, issued liability and held asset remain separate. Loss and loss-recovery
components sit inside those balances. Initial portfolio-level presentation and
combined-company statements require P5; this component report asserts neither.

GMM future-service changes consume CSM before loss and reverse loss before
rebuilding CSM. The loss-free service operation derives CSM release from current
remaining units. Loss-component service allocation outside the exact joint
settlement remains a named residual, rather than an inferred general algorithm.
Acquisition allocation conserves paid cost; recoverability reversals are capped
at original cost. Recognition transfers the carrying acquisition asset exactly
once. The finite quota treaty has an admitted systematic covered-contract
allocation whose premium/claims/RA totals reconcile to the initial onerous group.
Loss recovery is covered loss times expected recovery share, subject to entry
timing. A late treaty is a valid different history with zero initial-loss recovery.
The covered-estimate change reduces issued loss, held FCF and the recovery cap
without a second loss-recovery posting. Collectability affects held profit, not
held CSM. Nonlinear/capped treaty expectations need the separate process-law
assessment; the quota formula is not applied to them.

Qualifying replacement uses independently supplied equivalent premium less
actual additional premium as the deemed transfer. Only the actual additional
premium changes cash. A third-party transfer instead pays its actual premium.
Absent equivalent-premium valuation is unresolved. These finite cases have no
allocated OCI; origin-specific derecognition OCI remains explicit wider work.

PAA cases admit no-more-than-one-year coverage and distinct permitted cost
elections. Service releases unearned premium and deferred acquisition cost;
onerous addition is excess FCF over existing LRC. The later claim uses its
claim-incurrence rate for profit finance and current rates for LIC measurement.
RA is separately released. Its allocation rate/date/payment must trace to the
claim admission, not to contract inception or a later convenient estimate.
The stored PAA FCF field contains the tracked RA alone; total LIC is the LIC
field, so reporting must not add FCF a second time. General PAA long-duration
eligibility and financing variants retain their prior methods and P5 joins.

VFA admission, pool share and mitigation findings are supplied premises. The
underlying return changes both the policyholder obligation and entity fee; the
finite all-profit finance case offsets asset gain and liability finance while
adjusting fee CSM. Service excludes the investment repayment. A prior documented
mitigation strategy and qualifying offset/credit findings are required to route
the stated guarantee change through finance. Without them CSM/loss ordering
applies. The variable-payment allocation checks a prospective constant rate
against the revised payment and remaining carrying amount, separately from
the current measurement rate. B134 held-underlying matching, B135-136 policy
changes and OCI-origin derecognition remain explicit finite-profile residuals.

Request identity is client plus request ID within this profile. An identical
retry returns the exact original output/receipt/publication list without a
second commit, even after the current view advances. Changed bytes under the
same identity are a conflict. A new stale request fails its whole read set or
current information view. Lost acknowledgement reveals no commit status: both
uncommitted and committed worlds must recover by the same original request.
No authored world asserts crash durability of a running store.

The independent auditor reconstructs component states, statements of the four
owners and signed debit journals from opening/event facts. It must not import
the builder, fixture expectations or its calculations as an oracle. It also
checks versioned lineage for treaty shares, claim-incurrence rates and VFA pool
shares, source/availability bindings and deliberate financial/atomicity failures.
P4b retains transition and process-law promises; P5 retains explicit migrations,
old-profile joins, ownership-change residuals and manuscript integration.
