# BankComponents/2 — finite transfer and classification contract

This is a closed documentary successor, not a running service. It retains the
reviewed canonical UTF-8 JSON, reduced rationals, domain-separated SHA256,
immutable references and atomic owner publication. Each synthetic AU USD book
binds the exact retained PDF/text, method, inspection note, annual period and
view. AASB9 and AASB15 annual starts are [2023-01-01,2026-07-01); the separately
dated AASB107 support starts2024-01-01 with no inferred end. There is no EU or
current-law admission, tax calculation or complete after-tax company report.

Transfer eligibility and risk/control conclusions are supplied findings.
Eligible-part carrying allocation uses sold and retained relative fair values,
never nominal percentage. No servicing, fees, prior OCI or separate opening
allowance is supported by that isolated example. Retaining substantially all
risks preserves the whole asset and recognises borrowing; subsequent asset
income and liability expense are separate. One-anniversary accrual uses each
specified rate once; repayment requires actual cash and discharge evidence,
plus supplied contractual principal/interest allocation. Borrowing principal is
financing and the bank's cash interest is operating and separately disclosed.

Guarantee continuing involvement recognises the capped asset and associated
liability including the initial guarantee value. Its supplied even service
pattern releases only the performed fraction. Supplied loss allowance reduces
the retained asset; no-claim expiry reverses that allowance, removes the
remaining asset and liability and releases the remaining guarantee value.
Claims, changing fair values and alternative performance patterns need methods
outside this finite profile.

For fair-value options, let S be the supplied underlying fair value, Kc/Kp the
exercise prices and Tc/Tp the supplied time values. A call has A=S,
L=min(S,Kc)-Tc. A written put has A=min(S,Kp), L=Kp+Tp. A collar has A=S,
L=min(S,Kc)+max(Kp-S,0)+Tp-Tc. The last formula uses the full put value.
Recognised assets and liabilities remain gross, with no second derivative.
Initial proceeds must agree with the explicitly isolated zero-day-one-gain
case. Subsequent changes go to profit. Time values become zero at supported
expiry; a supported physical exercise pays the applicable strike and removes
the associated liability. Unexercised expiry and cash settlement are outside
this profile. Prices are inputs, not a pricing algorithm.

The amortised-cost option keeps distinct asset and liability EIR schedules.
Both must reconcile to their specified terminal amounts at the single supplied
anniversary. Actual exercise cash is distinct from the liability carrying
amount; their difference is profit or loss. Explicit zero-allowance isolation
is required and is not an ECL estimate.

Asset classification records a supplied business-model and contractual-cash-
flow assessment. Collect/pass leads to AC; collect-and-sell/pass to debt FVOCI;
other debt goes to FVTPL. A supported initial accounting-mismatch designation
also selects FVTPL. Eligible nontrading equity can elect FVOCI irrevocably at
initial recognition; otherwise it uses FVTPL. The profile cannot revise these
findings after classification. Missing local documents leave a named evidence
obligation; they do not establish failed nonrecourse/tranche conditions.

Nontrading investment purchase/sale cash is investing. AC allowance affects
net carrying amount. Debt FVOCI impairment affects profit and OCI, leaving the
asset at fair value; disposal recycles the reserve. FVTPL and equity FVOCI have
no separate ECL. Equity FVOCI disposal never recycles to profit; optional
reserve clearance records equal and opposite within-equity movements, with
zero current profit and OCI. No coupons, dividends, costs or tax are computed.
FV disposal requires an already current fair value equal to sale proceeds.

The time-value probe compares principal plus prorated simple interest over
one supplied period and scenario. Its explicit status is explanatory_only. It does not classify an
asset or determine significance; reporting-period/lifetime scenario analysis,
benchmark construction and significance reasoning remain required findings.

Complex guarantee/option transfer cash is explicitly unclassified, including
physical exercise; gross receipts and payments remain exact. The option
exercise price has no identified cash-interest component. Accrued EIR income
is never substituted for contractual cash interest. Reports disclose
unclassified-cash status separately from disclosed contractual cash interest.
Missing borrowing-repayment interest allocation leaves an evidence obligation.

Every method needs a positive path, meaningful refusal and balanced wrong
journal. The builder imports authored expected effects; the independent
verifier imports neither those expectations nor the builder. Reports derive
from complete ordered published receipts. Exact replay precedes freshness and
financial rederivation, altered-byte identity conflicts refuse, and lost
acknowledgement preserves both worlds. Refusals publish no effects. Other
profiles, including BankComponents/1, require explicit migration and are refused
before foreign references resolve. Engineering integrity, conditional numerical
correctness and accounting/human acceptance remain distinct.
