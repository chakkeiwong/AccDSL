2. R01b: exact time/review/history/scenario/report requests/results, complete
   record and byte closure, original/corrected views, changed rate, missing
   collection input, overlap rejection and information leakage rejection.
3. R01c: named corporate commit and complete recovery cases, replay/conflict,
   stale head, partial/resume, correction/invalidation and failed migration.
   Reuse the common insurance transaction vocabulary; do not change I1 fields.

   source/typed/IR relationships and integrate into a protected successor.

No unit closes R01 alone. A JSON Schema pass establishes shape, not financial
meaning or complete dependency closure. Financial checks must compute from
independent opening/driver/event facts, not read the authored expected values.
