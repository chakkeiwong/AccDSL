invoice, followed by exact replay or a specific rejection. The independently
computed changes equal the stated documentary outcomes in all ten cases. This
is a finite specification check, not an executed accounting endpoint or a
crash/recovery experiment. The protected acquisition journal still requires

performance, invoice I1 applies all 30 of advance A1. Gross billing G and
applied credit K both become 30. T=G+A−K stays 30; every monetary delta is
zero. The company still owes performance of 30, while the invoice and credit
allocation must be retained. Omitting this event from persistence or export
would permit credit reuse. The receipt, head and identity index change once.

Exact replay returns the original receipt before current-head freshness is
compared. A changed invoice or request_id under the same business identity
conflicts. An absent identity with an old head is stale. Reused credit, false
authorization and unsupported profile fail before lookup; nonzero account
delta and lost allocation fail independent reconciliation without publication.
All rejection cases preserve the domain state.
