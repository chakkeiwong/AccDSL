# Reserving prediction: target and method admission

14 October 2026. Supplements the reserving-bootstrap method note and Version
2.86 section12.9. MC11/12/19/20/23/26/29/30/32/35. Documentary specification;
no production adapter or empirical model is promoted.

The claimed target must name the random quantity and conditioning information:
future reserve payment; lifetime prediction error; or one-year development
result with conditional closing re-estimation. Latest diagonal, complete
observed triangle and posterior conditional information are distinct inputs.
The source analysis of a Mack bootstrap does not establish consistency of
the incremental ODP procedure.

Each proposed method record must carry: exact source/version; cumulative or
incremental data; structural cell mask; exposure/calendar assumptions;
payment horizon; information cutoff and conditioning set; fitted factor and
dispersion estimators; process-law family and support; past-resampling law;
shared-parameter dependence; reserve-centering definition; unresolved-input
and failed-draw treatment; and validation target/reference.
The prior uncertainty and missing-mass rules continue to apply.

For a backward reconstruction, derive the reverse law from an explicit joint
model or state and validate its approximation. Reciprocal forward means and
variance matching are insufficient. Preserve observed diagonal values
exactly, reconstruct complete earlier states, refit, simulate futures under
the declared algorithm, and subtract a fitted reserve from a reserve.
The resulting prediction-error sample can be translated into a proposed
interval only after its conditioning target has been checked. A distribution
of fitted means alone remains a different calculation.

## Validation sequence and comparator discipline

1. Run exact finite joint-law cases for conditioning, Bayesian inversion,
   support and centering. The 53 checks in the dossier cover explanatory
   arithmetic, including a zero-development case. They do not establish a
   large-sample theorem or empirical validity.
2. For a later target-specific study, freeze portfolio/triangle provenance,
   units, development/calendar windows, censoring/masks, outlier policy,
   cutoff and heldout evaluation before fitting. Obtain actual data authority
   and a run budget separately; this note launches no campaign.
3. Compare the same quantity under a naive point/moment baseline, a tuned
   classical method appropriate to that target, the plain proposed method
   and the repaired method. Include ODP, Bornhuetter-Ferguson, calendar effects
   or robust methods only after their relevant mechanisms are technically
   inspected and the same target can be evaluated. A weak convenient
   comparator cannot establish promotion.
4. For synthetic conditional coverage, begin with an enumerated joint law
   whose backward probabilities are exact. A forward simulator plus an
   arbitrarily chosen reverse simulator is not an independent conditional
   reference. For continuous models, document numerical integration error
   or independently verified conditional sampling before interpreting coverage.
5. Declare primary coverage/tail or downstream-loss criteria, allowable
   deviation and uncertainty procedure from the intended financial decision.
   Do not inherit 500 triangles, 10,000 bootstrap draws or a 95% interval
   merely because the source used them. Use a budget/power calculation for
   that chosen decision precision; absent one, the study is under-specified.
   Separate fitting, tuning and heldout scoring. Preserve paired outer
   replications when comparing methods; inner bootstrap draws are not
   independent outer trials.
6. Crash, invalid support, missing required retuning, unidentified dispersion,
   corrupted provenance or an incorrect reference veto affected comparisons.
   A failed candidate triggers the planned repair; it does not halt independent
   method work. Numerical disagreement beyond the declared integration/Monte
   Carlo error prevents ranking. Report uncertainty for differences and tail
   estimates; descriptive interval lengths alone cannot establish superiority.
7. Check downstream cash timing, dependence, group allocation, discounting
   and measurement separately. A predictive payment distribution does not
   choose an IFRS17 risk adjustment, accounting policy or prudential measure.

## Disposition

The published 2024 consistency analysis justifies a stricter question and
validation specification. It does not supply a universally valid backward
generator or an adopted default. Literal equation5.3 is excluded; the adjacent
root and equation6.1 agree with the locally derived net-reserve center.
Full target-specific data selection, protocol instantiation, theorem-transfer
obligations and empirical execution remain open. Continue interface and
cross-module interaction work without treating this source limitation as a
continuation veto.
