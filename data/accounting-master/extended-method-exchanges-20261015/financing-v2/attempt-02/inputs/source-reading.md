IFRS2.43(c), rechecked at 26694–26704, requires additional expense for either
elected higher-value alternative. Consequently equity/share settlement needs
the cash alternative's value; a liability transfer for counterparty choice
under 39 is a separate rule. IFRS2.28(b)/43(a), IAS7.12 (2770–2773),
14(d) (2795–2797) and 17(b) (2879) imply the stated split of cancellation and
equity-choice payments into equity repurchase (financing) and employee-service
expense excess (operating). IAS7 does not explicitly give that IFRS2 split;
it is a local deduction for these authored cases. IAS7.12/31–33 (3041–3056)
also support the selected operating-interest policy and the zero-coupon
compound redemption split: debt cash basis90, implicit interest10, equity10
retained. Conversion pays no interest cash. The early-repurchase case has no
unpaid accrued coupon; its debt extinguishment loss is not labelled interest.
