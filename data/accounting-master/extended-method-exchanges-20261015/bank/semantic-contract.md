# BankComponents/1 — finite documentary contract

All records are closed and immutable. Canonical UTF-8 JSON, exact reduced
rationals and domain-separated SHA256 references inherit the inspected portable
transport. The schema and these semantics are locked by exact bytes. This is
an authored specification, not a running accounting or banking service.

One USD Australian historical conditional book owns each path. Every method
binds exact source text/PDF, method text, context, annual period, view and
synthetic admission. Other closed profiles require explicit migration and are
refused before foreign references resolve. No tax is computed. Interest uses
the supplied original-rate annual or half-year grid; no POCI, negative rate,
mixed spacing or implicit current-law admission is supported.

Loan origination either pays actual cash or credits a deposit liability; a
later external transfer is distinct. Original risk identity survives ECL,
modification and cure. Disjoint exhaustive weighted scenarios determine
discounted shortfalls. Twelve-month ECL selects defaults in the next year and
includes their later receipts; lifetime ECL includes every supplied scenario.
An observed default requires an impaired finding. Impaired interest uses net
carrying amount from the subsequent grid interval. Contractual gross stops at
maturity; expected delayed recovery still unwinds at the original rate.
Modification without derecognition discounts revised rights at that same rate;
nonzero modification fees/costs require another method. Cure needs an objective
subsequent event, independently of modified terms. Write-off requires evidence
of no reasonable recovery and preserves legal rights; recovery neither restores
gross nor erases history. Legal waiver requires another method.

The full commitment example is a dated two-year bullet with disjoint early and
later default probabilities. Supplied draw probability applies while undrawn;
observed full draw makes it one. Reserve and draw are confined to the stated
measurement date. Revolving losses are separately supplied conditional funded
and undrawn estimates; capacity arithmetic cannot derive them. Original history
and supported contractual/behavioural horizon survive drawings. Inseparable
loss is capped at gross, with excess provision. Lapse needs expired rights.

Probable-specific-loan fee cash is deferred, then reduces carrying amount on
origination. The one-year rate and terminal payment must agree. Fee-only
examples require explicit zero-ECL isolation. Expiry and equally performed
stand-ready service release their distinct deferred fees. No receipt alone
establishes revenue. Service dates and the admitted performance pattern bind.

Capacity and request create no cash or funding liability. Confirmed receipts,
partial receipts and independently verified zero are different observations;
timeout remains unresolved. Unexpected actual money is recognised with a
control breach. Actual withdrawals are limited by cash; unpaid amounts remain
within deposits. Duplicate settlement/payment evidence is refused.

Every commit atomically publishes one journal, delta, closing state, receipt,
event index and owner head while preserving other owners. Exact-byte replay is
resolved before freshness and financial rederivation; changed bytes with the
same business identity conflict. Lost acknowledgement preserves both possible
worlds and requires the original request. Refusals/unknowns publish no effects.
Reports require the entire ordered published receipt chain, gross cash and
separate interest information. Unknown principal/interest allocation remains
unresolved; EIR income is never substituted for contractual cash interest.

The packet builder cannot import the independent derivation. The verifier
cannot import authored expected cases or the builder. Each finite method needs
a positive exchange, meaningful refusal and balanced financially wrong journal.
Invalid target, source, date, component, chain or partial publication vetoes
that exchange. Counts alone establish neither bank completeness nor readiness.
