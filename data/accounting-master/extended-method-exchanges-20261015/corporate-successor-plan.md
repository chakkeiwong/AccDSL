assurance, production allocation and delayed-payable methods while retaining
the first profile's component, financial and recovery obligations? Comparator:
the P1 operation matrix and existing P17/R03-E cases, not a new generic sale.

conditional cases. It cannot accept the historical Australian warranty and
revenue methods by changing the event tag. The successor needs explicit
jurisdiction/issuer-class, reporting-period start, dated effective interval,
and **multiple** source references for AASB15 classification plus AASB137

naive reuse plan; implement these fields before authoring AU exchanges.

Define CorporateComponents/2 under a new schema namespace. Preserve all v1
cases and their meaning, with an explicit source-compatible migration subset
left for P5. Add closed returns, service warranty, distinct/integrated/mixed
modification, principal/agent, assurance/reimbursement, joint/byproduct and
supplier-financing state/event variants. Preserve exact costs, gross assets/
liabilities, service state and original allocation; no free-form payload.
Represent annual periods 2024/2025 and actual synthetic event dates separately
from relative order. AASB scope follows beginning-period boundaries; no current
authority, general US GAAP or ASBE admission follows.

Primary criterion: every new named variant has independent opening/event
arithmetic and complete authorize/propose/commit/report paths, plus the specific
source/period/role refusal. Wrong source/date, netted reimbursement, duplicate
performance, erased original revenue, wrong production cost or financing cost
capitalization veto that exchange. Existing v1 replay and recovery must survive.
Tests/build counts remain integrity evidence only. Unknown actual valuation,
reimbursement certainty, progress, rights and tax remain unresolved inputs.

Defaults: exact rational arithmetic and inherited canonical transport are
reviewed documentary conventions. Relative-sales allocation, zero recovery
costs, uniform service and operating interest cash are explicit case premises,
not universal defaults. Tax is uncomputed. No stochastic experiment or runtime
service is run; independent arithmetic in the existing Python environment is
the smallest sufficient check. No installation or external review is needed.

Output: corporate-v2/ schemas, source-binding contract, immutable records/exact
