# P3c retained technical source reading

15 October 2026. This is an implementation-reference record. `source_policy.py`
pins the retained PDF/official response, extracted text and method notes.
The original technical reading and exact examples are preserved in
group-restatement-exchangeability-20261015 and impairment-tax-repairs-20261015.
No predecessor command is rerun and no new download substitutes for a source.

IFRS10 B86(c) requires full intragroup elimination, including profit in assets;
B88 makes income/expense inclusion depend on control dates. B92-B95 govern
aligned reporting dates and policies. Thus reporting-date reciprocal balances
and transaction-period performance require separate control tests. Matching
amounts do not establish that two legal entries describe the same transaction.
Our allocation IDs and invoice/lot/event bindings are derived engineering rules.
Unmatched amounts remain unresolved. A monetary foreign-exchange difference is
preserved rather than suppressed by a plug (IAS21 45).

IAS29's nonmonetary restatement and net monetary gain/loss requirements are
applied to dated index intervals, then IAS21 42-43 to translation. The retained
nonhyperinflationary presentation comparative stays at its prior amount.
Purchasing-power cash reconciliation under IAS29 33-34 explains a change in
units; it is not a cash payment. IFRIC7 3-5 requires opening deferred tax to be
remeasured after restating the nonmonetary item, before that opening tax is
indexed to closing purchasing power. Fixed tax base and synthetic rates are
conditional example facts, not actual tax findings.

IAS36 100-103 tests individual units before the smallest group that can bear a
corporate asset. Paragraphs104-105 constrain allocation at asset floors.
AppendixC4-C8 uses notionally grossed-up goodwill in a proportionate-NCI test;
the unrecognized NCI goodwill impairment is not recorded. The supplied
proportional valuation assumption is required; ownership alone is not a
valuation. The exact floor example uses capped carrying-value proportions.

IAS12 15(a),66-68 distinguish acquisition temporary differences from the
initial-goodwill exception. Paragraph39's outside-basis liability exception
needs both control over reversal and probable non-reversal in the foreseeable
future. Paragraph44's deductible investment test needs both probable reversal
and available taxable profit. The cases supply those findings; unknowns stay
unknown unless a known false already determines the conjunction. Actual law,
tax bases and recognition evidence remain separate admissions.

EU2024/2862 Article2 and IAS21 60L-60M supply the effective-date and transition
rules. IAS21 8-8B,A2-A10 assess ability at a date, in a direction and for a
purpose, allowing normal administrative delay and requiring enforceable rights.
Management intent is irrelevant. Significance is a supplied judgment, not a
threshold inferred from the illustrated 5/1000 ratio. IAS21 19A,A11-A20 requires
an orderly measurement-date spot-rate objective when exchangeability is absent;
another purpose's rate or a later rate is not automatically admitted. No rate
estimator is implemented here. Initial application adjusts opening retained
earnings for the payable, or accumulated translation differences for the
foreign operation; it does not create period profit/OCI or restate comparatives.

The frozen GroupForeignOperation/1 acquisition/translation and alternative
disposals remain the comparator for P17-04.12,05.06,05.07. This successor
does not replace their evidence or claim cross-profile composition. P5 must
specify explicit subset migrations and combined-company statement joins.
