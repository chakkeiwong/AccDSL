Source conclusion: a zero-weight CGU reversal remains a missing generic
allocation rule. Supplying positive capacities merely defines possible amounts;
it does not select a prescribed IAS36 allocation. An individual-asset reversal
is checked separately and cannot serve as a hidden CGU fallback.

The strongest shared-assumption risk is that traceable synthetic findings are
mistaken for independent recognition/valuation evidence. Every application
retains those conditions, and the report denies complete-company after-tax
coverage. Unknown findings and unsupported routes preserve the books.
