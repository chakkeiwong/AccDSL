# PrerequisiteMethodFamily/1

The target is the intersection of a complete PiecewiseMethodFamily/1 and the
satisfaction set of selected MethodRequirements/3 predicates. A numerical
candidate is a hypothetical complete assignment, not a known actual fact. Source,
accounting, native eligibility and execution remain false in every result.

The closed record has profile, family, requirements, selected_requirements_digest,
bindings, available_capabilities and obligations. family and requirements are full
definitions; the requirements digest uses existing canonical JSON. Every declared
requirement input has exactly one binding. A method_input binding has kind, input,
premise, requirement_source_ref and method_source_ref. The two references equal
their respective original declarations, and the complete types match exactly.
An external binding has kind, binding, premise and source_ref; binding is exactly
the existing known-value or explicit-unknown record, and source_ref equals the
requirement declaration. These checks preserve supplied correspondence; they do
not certify an external fact. Proposed premises retain their original identities
and may not conflict with any parent premise. Null means missing support.

Full parent formulas, method-only inputs, every original prerequisite, external
value/reason, capability, source reference and residual condition survive. All
parent target obligations and additional obligations remain explicit. A family
witness with any target obligation leaves target existence unknown. Parent or
mapping premise absence prevents solving. Unknown required external values and
guards remain unresolved; a domain for a mapped input does not replace them.

Fixed guards are evaluated through the original requirement semantics. A guard
depending on a nonsingleton mapped quantity is unsupported in this profile.
Inactive predicates impose no condition, but required_known remains independent
as in MethodRequirements/3. External-only predicates retain the original truth
and invalid/unsupported/unresolved consequences. Mixed conjunctions retain each
fixed finding and numeric condition; fixed failure priority is invalid, then
unsupported, then unresolved. An active numeric eq/le/lt with a mapped input
constrains that shared variable, including when its parent domain is singleton.
Thus a fixed 11-unit hypothetical return can make the intersection empty rather
than being mislabeled as malformed input. This differs deliberately from a false
external-only supplied fact, whose original consequence is reported directly.

Numeric terms support exact ref/const/add/sub and multiplication with at most
one nonsingleton operand. Fixed mapped domains remain present even when a constant
is used for arithmetic lowering. Symbolic guards, general symbolic disjunctions,
symbolic Boolean choices, unknown products and unhandled syntax are unsupported.
Fixed Boolean/text/date predicates use original exact scalar semantics. Capability
availability is explicit; missing capabilities remain unsupported.

Add the complete selected numeric constraints to every original monetary arm.
Do not select a parent witness, remove a contradictory arm, impose balancing
plugs or infer native funding/identity/memory rules. Global cell/time limits and
the parent's branch cap apply. Every feasible witness replays the complete
original monetary evaluator and the original MethodRequirements/3 assessment on
the exact candidate bindings. Accepted assessments are requirements_satisfied or
no_active_requirements; both retain the no-admission boundary. A replay failure
vetoes the implementation result.

Membership constraints are retained as exact original expressions and generated
linear formulas. Result manifests retain their parent arm hashes. Conflicts use
only named original/added constraints and are rechecked; alternate valid witnesses
are permitted. Timing and unsat core choice are diagnostic. A rejected replay or
resource-unknown is never converted to family emptiness. Limits are inherited
plus at most 256 prerequisite inputs/rules and bounded emitted expressions.

The evidence contract and default audit in the active plan apply. The selected
counterexample returns 11 units against 10 sold with consistent cash amounts;
the arithmetic family is feasible but the prerequisite intersection is empty.
The positive case narrows an unknown quantity only through explicit source-linked
cash and quantity conditions. These are synthetic conditional tests.
