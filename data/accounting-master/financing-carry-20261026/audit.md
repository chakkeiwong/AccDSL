# Financing carry audit before the probe

26 October 2026. W05; MC05/13/15/16/17/18/19/20/24/25/28/33/34/35.

CompanyBookCarry/1 correctly enforces the final day, closes income/expense
accounts to a selected equity account, archives the closing book/report and
starts an empty new-period flow sequence. Its per-leaf reset capability is
deliberately generic. A typed reset of missed_amount, missed or new_shares can
erase history. A selected financing carry must require preserve for every leaf
in this example, before calling the unchanged carry engine.

Reuse the selected financing snapshot conditions and the Version 2 conditional
remedy observation binding. The extended fiscal opening must be explicit, with
unknown clause/acceleration findings and provenance, and still satisfy the
existing report opening rules. Replay E01's exact method definitions with only
the explicit memory-schema extension. Compare every retained economic state,
journal and flow with the existing independent E01 table. The contract-status
record is additional supplied state; it is not evidence of actual applicability.

Two paths on each account chart discriminate the carry risk. Both retain the
40 historical miss and 40 subscribed shares. One settles and rescinds under
explicit hypothetical conditions; the other retains cash 40, unpaid claim 40,
arrears 40 and the previously recorded acceleration. At year-end both close
the 20 interest expense into retained earnings. Unpaid claims are allowed to
remain; carry does not assert that contracts were performed or the company is
solvent. Accounting/fiscal/source admission flags remain false.

The wrapper pins the financial contract, schemas, report map, account role and
all-preserve policy. It binds a concrete report/carry request and independently
selected contract. It checks the closing and next-opening financing snapshot,
preserves every memory leaf, verifies flow/seen history and performs an opening
report on the carried state. A rejection returns diagnostic proposals only,
without an accepted top-level next_opening.

At carry, last clause status/date/digest remain historical observations.
The new period and cleared flows change the fixed economic projection, and
the date differs. Old new-acceleration evidence must therefore remain unresolved.
Retiming the old clause observation or recomputing its digest in carry would
invent a fresh observation and is forbidden by the selected preservation rule.
A new explicit observation can support the next conditional event. For the
unpaid case, subsequent settlement must still be executable from the actual
carried state; compare its full result and new-period report independently.

Primary criteria are full-state/journal/flow/report correspondence and actual
refusal of destructive policies. Generic reset success is a discriminator of
scope, not a generic-engine bug. Missing selection, stale report, invalid
boundary, malformed types or lost history veto acceptance. Preserve all original
inputs and successful writers. An invalid test fixture triggers repair; no
scientific ranking or direction rejection follows from this deterministic unit.
