# Annual alternatives and joint period reports

AnnualScenarioFamily/1 groups explicit, unweighted annual programs over one
digest-bound root. Every member retains its identity, stated assumptions and
ordered operations. All members declare the same consecutive periods through
their planned carry operations. The root's actual period must match the first
declared period. Structural compilation is distinct from financial execution.

The family budget is an explicit count of member evaluations in declared order.
The common root is validated once even at zero budget, and each evaluated member
executes independently through the protected AnnualProgram/1 evaluator. The
root validation is outside the member budget. This is a deterministic reference
composition, not a wall-time, memory or durable continuation guarantee. The
64-member/64-period limits and inherited 2MB input/100000-node/depth64 bounds are
convenience ceilings. Each member also inherits the stricter authoring limits.

Structural errors invalidate the family. An economic refusal retains the whole
member result, including successful operations and the remaining sequence;
it does not prevent independent members from executing. Unvisited members retain
identity, assumptions and request digest with null result, not a fabricated zero.
The complete supplied definition remains in the caller's request and is bound
by its digest. Increasing a budget requires a new request and recomputation;
this version does not claim durable resume.

An empty authored selection says nothing about feasible economic histories.
Equal results under distinct names remain separate. No field accepts a measure
or weights, and no aggregate expected value or coordinatewise envelope is formed.
Complete declared search refers only to this supplied list, not actual-history
membership, exhaustive reconstruction, probability or future performance.

AnnualScenarioReports/1 verifies the full family by recomputation before reading
a saved result. It returns each member's entire report, bound to that member's
full execution result and period. A successfully carried period uses its exact
archived report. The current period uses its current completed-prefix report,
even when all listed operations completed. Both remain provisional. The query
includes the actual through-date and current/deferred assessment status. A later
period not reached is unavailable. An unvisited member is unavailable for a
different explicit reason. All report arithmetic remains in the annual engine.

The reference changes neither tax treatment nor financial arithmetic. Its
comparator is the complete independently evaluated member request. The prior
company-family design supplies semantics, not a claim of schema compatibility.
Whole-company representation, source reconstruction, broader method support,
accounting admission, general authoring and production persistence remain open.
