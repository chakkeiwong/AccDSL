## Skeptical audit and plan repair

The saved plan names MethodCollections/1 and MethodStateAssessment/1 as
baselines. Inspection shows that these assess supplied finite lists and funding
conditions; they do not execute receivable collection clocks. They are relevant
to unknown/false semantics, but are not an exact monetary or date comparator.
Use independent receivable arithmetic and the retained operating collect method
for monetary equivalence. Use explicit calendar inequalities for assessment
windows. Annual-period and deferred-tax code supplies boundary lessons only;
copying its tax recognition into receivable assessment would be wrong.

The declared transition engine only replaces existing scalar memory leaves.
The first implementation therefore uses a finite, explicitly declared population
of receivable claims and assessment windows. Their remaining amounts and dated
assessment memory evolve during execution. It must not claim arbitrary dynamic
insertion, complete invoice population, impairment measurement or source admission.
No new generic engine is justified until this existing composition fails a
concrete requirement. Two account charts and matched text use the same meanings.

A bare method registry permits foreign events and carry resets. A collection
program must consequently validate every executed prefix against its selected
receivable population, protected memory and declared method meanings. Hashes
alone do not validate a rehashed result. Unknown events must not silently settle
claims; zero-net gross postings and equal-value writes still require inspection.

## Research intent and evidence contract

Question: can declared due dates and assessment windows preserve exact collection,
delinquency observations, report cutoffs and period carry on the existing book?
Candidate: a bounded data-only method builder plus explicit program validation.
Expected failures: lost receivable identity, stale observations presented as
current, same-day post-assessment mutation, skipped/overlapping windows, and
carry resetting unpaid claims or silently moving an assessment cursor.

Primary criterion: exact independent balances, state-only assessment journals,
date boundaries, full report/carry results, successful prefixes and matched text
replay under two charts. Negative cases must refuse at the first affected
command and preserve earlier successful work. Each case is named; counts are
explanatory and are calculated from executed records.
Promotion vetoes: hidden scoped writes/postings, guessed unknowns, wrong date
boundaries, changed frozen dependencies, false source/accounting admission or
inexact replay. Repair the candidate for these failures. Continuation vetoes:
unrecoverable protected-source corruption or missing execution dependencies.
Neither a rejected candidate nor missing human acceptance ends internal work.
No superiority, predictive accuracy, legal compliance, impairment, tax, complete
ageing disclosure, whole-company completion or production readiness follows.
