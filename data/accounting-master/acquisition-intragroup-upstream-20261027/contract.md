## Research intent and evidence contract

Question: does full upstream elimination precede allocation of subsidiary
profit to NCI, while preserving external supplier cash and acquisition values?
The candidate builds complete dated legal journals, replays the supplied books,
and applies consolidation entries. Independently written expectations use
closed-form balances and attribution. Exact equality of every complete legal
journal, snapshot, worksheet, cash origin and report is the primary criterion.
The initial unsold example discriminates against naive legal-profit attribution:
recognised tax leaves S's consolidated profit at 63/2 and NCI at 63/8.

Wrong NCI, scaled elimination, missing vendor cash, changed acquisition values,
unexplained legal movements, false admission or mismatched bindings veto the
candidate. They trigger local repair, not research-direction rejection.
Corrupted source/comparator inputs invalidate the affected comparison and stop
its execution. Runtime and assertion counts are explanatory only. No stochastic
ranking, actual source admission, general tax rule or production readiness follows.
Preserve cases, controls, input copies, checks and run manifest under attempt-01;
never overwrite an attempt. CPU-only 120-second budget, at most three attempts
before plan revision. No global continuation veto is currently present.

attribute the inventory elimination and its deferred effect to S's earnings.
The 27 October web verification query returned no usable result; no current
