# Credit measurement representation: pre-diagnostic audit

25 October 2026. Read the complete retained reference.py and bounds.py, the
semantic contract, source-reading record and native verifier. Inspected all
nine saved journal inventories. The current book validator requires exact
rational balances and exact zero journal sums. The manuscript permits symbolic
families and approximate measurements, but those declarations do not implement
an algebraic amount type in CompanyBook/1 or its v2 transition extension.

The native calculator computes interval enclosures of shared calculations.
Its wire journals contain separate lower/upper/width/exact records, not an
expression graph proving their dependence. The July accrual uses dG=G(F-1),
dL=L(F-1), interest=(G-L)(F-1), hence the signed journal sums to zero as an
algebraic identity. Independent interval endpoints need not have that property.
Zero contained in an interval sum certifies enclosure only, not an exact
journal. The earlier native contract already states this limit; it is not a
newly discovered defect in its report-only purpose.

Smallest diagnostic: replay all nine frozen requests exactly; count nonpoint
entries; retain the full first July accrual and select its all-upper endpoints
to exhibit a nonzero exact sum. Verify the shared factor's integer-power
inequalities and demonstrate that its midpoint does not satisfy the defining
power equation. No midpoint is posted. Inspect exact degeneracy across every
journal and monetary state field before calling a case rationally exact.

Candidate route to investigate after that witness: a single positive day
factor z per fixed-rate contract, z^365=1+r, so a d-day factor is z^d. Rational
Laurent expressions retain the same factor across journals, balances and PV.
Reducing powers with z^365=1+r can prove selected identities exactly, while
the existing interval bounds remain numerical certificates. This is a proposed
local algebraic representation, not an admitted rational book value or a new
financial default. Its dimensional, context, serialization, resource and
zero/negative-rate behavior need a separate bounded contract before execution.
