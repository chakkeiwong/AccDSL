# Consequences for the proposed bank method

Historical ASU 2016-13 permits a sharper design question, but does not itself settle operative 2025 accounting. The bank model needs separate states for a contractual commitment, the issuer's cancellation right, actual drawing, a recognised funded asset, off-balance expected-loss liability, and any distinct guarantee stand-ready liability. Each state needs its own date, entity, source and measurement basis.

For a covered commitment that cannot be unconditionally cancelled, paragraph 326-20-30-11 directs an estimate over the period of present contractual exposure. The estimate considers funding likelihood and the expected credit loss of the resulting funded position. It is not the disclosed contractual maximum; nor does the paragraph license a universal one-factor formula. The simple allowance formula in the manuscript remains an illustration with stated inputs. To turn it into a company-specific measurement requires the actual commitment terms, current applicable accounting text, portfolio segmentation, funding and loss estimates and journal presentation.

For an unconditionally cancellable commitment, the historical example excludes an unfunded allowance even though some drawings might occur before the bank detects default. The model must therefore keep liquidity possibilities distinct from the accounting-liability test. Cancellation rights are contract facts, not attributes to infer from a product label. When a commitment funds, expiry, recognition of an asset and settlement need distinct transitions so that the off-balance liability is not double-counted with the recognised-asset allowance.

For a covered financial guarantee, the source separates the contingent expected-credit-loss liability from the noncontingent fair-value stand-ready liability. This explains why the issuer's USD 175m standby allowance and USD 441m guarantee liability can coexist, but it does not independently verify those amounts or determine whether every guarantee has the same scope. Derivatives, insurance, other guarantee categories and later Codification changes require separate decisions. A freestanding protection contract also cannot be silently used to net an asset allowance.

The implementation consequence is a typed measurement record with fields for instrument scope, present-obligation period, unconditional cancellation right, funding outcome, expected loss if funded, off-balance credit-loss liability, recognised-asset allowance and guarantee stand-ready liability, each with source and as-of basis. This is a **proposed** interface decomposition, not an immutable product requirement or proof that the historical ASU governs current cases.
