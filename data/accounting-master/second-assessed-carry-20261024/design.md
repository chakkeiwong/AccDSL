# Second assessed provisional boundary

Request the full fresh following-period deferred assessment,its digest,new unique
carry id,explicit provisional model,contiguous next period and complete declared
findings. No operations field is accepted. Recompute the predecessor and require
current/deferred assessment through the old financial endpoint,loss cursor equal
to that endpoint,and closing date=end-1. Unknown/false findings are retained;
true assertions never establish annual/source acceptance.

Transfer actual recorded profit to retained earnings using debit-positive signs:
new retained=old retained-profit. Zero every income account and return the exact
balanced carry journal. Preserve other balances,component/lot/lease state and
lifetime ids. Advance financial period/date,tax close marker,opening deferred
premises and prior-year record. Reset current flows,tax adjustments and opening
interest-paid accumulator. Loss state/cursor remains unchanged at the new start.

Keep the complete prior tax_vintages unchanged. Create one period_vintages row
per stable vintage id with prior_vintage_sha256,period,opening_payable,
settlements=[] and closing_payable=opening_payable. Sum openings equals actual
aggregate tax payable. Archive the prior flows,tax adjustments,closing/report
hashes,full fresh recognition calculation/findings/capacity/measurement and
predecessor result. Do not infer capacity or a fresh tax assessment from it.
Both new-period current and deferred tax become unassessed.

The output contains opening=after,original findings with explicit statuses,
prior findings,loss state,historical vintages,new-period ledger,carry journal,
recorded profit,complete archive and four zero-movement opening statement ties.
Apply compares the complete result. Subsequent allocated payments and ordinary
events require a compatible period-aware continuation profile. No general annual
engine,compliance or full fiscal/event/source coverage is inferred.
