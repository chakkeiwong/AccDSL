# Conditional acquisition revision views /4

One original and one revised /2 worksheet request are content-bound. This
single-actor profile keeps manager source/control identity and rebinds all
embedded query cutoffs to the view cutoff before evaluating either alternative.
Both unmodified submitted requests and rebound requests/results survive.

The revision has separately hashed source text, exact acquisition effective
instant, discovery instant, selected measurement-period end, actor availability,
provisional-item status and conditional disposition. It may concern only the
equipment acquisition recognised amount, related acquired tax liability,
subsequent expense adjustment and tax release. The complete dependent adjustment
population must have explicit old/new values and changed/reviewed-unchanged
dispositions. Every other field, legal account, ownership/control request and
consideration is unchanged. Changing an unrelated amount is invalid.

Eligibility is conditional, never an adjudication of facts. Acquired-item
information with a supplied provisional status and discovery within the supplied
period can select the revised alternative once available to this actor. An
unknown availability or disposition retains the complete two alternatives with
unresolved status. A known future availability selects only the original.
Later events, nonprovisional items, discovery at/after the closed period, or
a wrong acquisition effective date require other treatment; they do not
retrospectively change this worksheet. The upper bound is explicitly the
one-year anniversary for this fixed July2025 synthetic calendar case; the
reference does not implement a universal measurement-period calendar.

Negative/unsupported proposed revisions refuse through the underlying worksheet.
All eligible outputs retain no-admission flags. A selected revision is a
conditional actor-view result, not source truth, actual accounting recognition
or authorization to rewrite the original. Prior evidence remains byte-identical.

Discovery lower bound: before discovery, retain only original regardless of an unknown availability. At discovery and later, unknown availability may retain both. The /1 failure and its counterexample remain preserved.

Known retrospective disqualifiers remove the revised financial alternative even when availability is unknown. The output retains unknown availability and makes the other-method duty conditional on access, without pretending the actor has learned it.

Evaluate a revised worksheet only if potentially available and conditionally eligible. An unsupported candidate retains the complete original and proposed request/refusal, with no selected revised result. A future or disqualified revision never erases the earlier original.
