# Conditional repair inquiry: pre-execution audit

13 October 2026. Resume from cefcf5f / Version 2.62; the baseline and source
renders were prepared before the session handoff. Their hashes are checked
before arithmetic. MC08–11/13/16/18/28/32–35; G1/G5/G15/G16; P17/P18, E01.

The question is whether an explicitly assumed difference transformation can
preserve the individual annual statement rows, and what prevents admitting it
as an Apple history. The comparator is the rejected fixed-remainder H1/H2
construction. We do not set the subtraction residual equal to a new event.

Research intent: test two alternative expense-liability hypotheses, each
conditional on an economic obligation of amount q in H1 and q−1000 in H2,
with q≥1000. The hypothesized obligation is independently required; the source
does not establish its existence. This is an alternative amount across worlds,
not a release journal occurring within one world. It shares the loss's expense
row and the capital payable's reported liability row only by hypothesis.

Primary criterion: derive zero changes in every affected annual statement row
under explicitly fixed mappings and a feasible base assumed to exist. This
establishes a conditional transformation only. It does not construct that base,
prove absolute feasibility, or establish a source-admitted company history.
The expected ordinary-accrual failure is a promotion veto for that mapping and
a repair trigger for the noncash mapping, not a continuation veto.

The earliest diagnostic is the two indirect cash-flow adjustments. The extra
retirement loss adds 1000 to Other; an ordinary operating accrual 1000 smaller
reduces the operating-liability adjustment by 1000. CFO stays fixed while the
two published rows change. Parameter alpha allocates the expense-liability
movement to a separately identified noncash addback, with that same movement
excluded from the working-liability adjustment. Only alpha=1 cancels the loss
addback. Alpha is a reporting-allocation hypothesis, never a probability or an
accounting election justified by this example. Test alpha=0, 1/2 and 1 exactly.
