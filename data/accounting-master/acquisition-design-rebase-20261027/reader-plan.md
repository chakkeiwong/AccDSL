# Bounded acquisition argument

Reader: an accounting-literate implementer who has just read the group-scope
section. They can distinguish conditional control from permission to report,
but still need to know which amounts and period segments acquisition requires.
Purpose: explain the conditional method design, not certify a transaction.
Baseline5.27 and its hashes are protected; new prose is limited to one section
after simulation/control-group-scope.tex, expected four to six rendered pages.

Argument: a newly controlled company cannot be added by multiplying its annual
statement by a share fraction. Establish acquisition basis first; distinguish
consideration from prior debt; derive excess and the NCI choice; then separate
post-control income, group-basis adjustments, distributions and cash perimeter.
The dated-source paragraph explains why choosing a calendar date alone cannot
select the applicable standard. The implementation paragraph states the actual
limited evidence and next full-worksheet requirement.

Preservation map: authority-matrix.md supplies dated branches/anchors;
acquisition-period-contract.md supplies inputs, cash/revision/measurement rules;
symbolic contract.md supplies affine equations/limits; expected.py and attempt-01
supply complete checked coefficients and adverse cases. No original equation,
citation, source boundary or caveat is deleted. Operational histories stay in
their existing notes. Both editions receive the same explanatory text.

Cold-reader questions: Why does75% ownership not justify75% of annual profit?
Why does a prior debt carrying amount not supply P or T? What differs under
proportional and fair-value NCI? What does a negative excess require? How does
acquired cash enter without a conversion cash payment? Which facts still prevent
posting? Which dates and entity choices must be resolved before applying rules?

Every displayed amount uses the same currency unit; all acquisition quantities
share the exact control date, and later amounts share its post-control interval.
E is a conditional excess, never recognised goodwill by itself. The NCI equation
is derived with constant ordinary-present rights and explicit signed adjustments.
