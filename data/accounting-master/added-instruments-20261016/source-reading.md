Inspected local35100–35130,35554–35588,35715–35925,36078–36146 and
42045–42218. IFRS9.5.1.1 requires initial fair-value recognition;5.1.1A
requires separate treatment when transaction price differs, which this case
excludes with an explicit finding. IFRS9.6.2.1/3/4/5 admits eligible external
derivatives and combined designation;6.2.6 restricts written-option combinations.
This profile uses eligible non-option derivatives in their entirety with all
valuation effects included, not separated option/forward components.6.4.1
requires documented designation and effectiveness conditions.

B6.5.13/19 preserves the old instrument measurement and starts added volume's
change at rebalancing. B6.5.19 explicitly allows instruments with differing
terms and designation after their initial recognition; this narrower example
acquires and designates on the same date, so no earlier local book history
must be transferred. B6.5.21 requires updated sources-of-ineffectiveness
documentation. The unchanged-objective condition comes fromB6.5.15; arbitrary
ratio changes to reduce measured ineffectiveness are barred byB6.5.12.

For leg j acquired and designated at value b_j, its hedge gain at time t is
V_j(t)-b_j. The original zero-value derivative has b_0=0. With no settlement,
D=sum_j(V_j-b_j), B=sum_j b_j, and total carrying value=sum_jV_j=B+D.
At acquisition debit derivative b_j and credit cash b_j; D, reserve and profit
do not change. Subsequent movements enter the cumulative effectiveness test
under6.5.11: with opposite signed D and item change H,
R=sign(D)*min(abs(D),abs(H)), I=D-R. Difference journals preserve prior earnings
and reserve, including negative incremental ineffectiveness. Same-sign nonzero
cumulative changes are outside this simplified offsetting profile.

Gross positive and negative instrument fair values are reported separately.
Their signed sum is used only in the conservation identity; the method grants
no financial-statement offsetting permission. Cash after acquisition is
opening cash-B, so cash+net derivatives=opening cash+D=capital+R+I.
All supplied valuation and eligibility findings remain conditions of the
calculation. Exact identities are not evidence that those findings hold for an
actual instrument, designation or company.
