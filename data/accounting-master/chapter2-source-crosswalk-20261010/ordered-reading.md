## Cold-reader answers and repair decision

1. Within Northstar's declared exclusions, the bridges identify annual
collections, purchases and payments. They do not identify invoices. The common
fixed-rate share jointly changes interest, tax, cash, profit and equity.
2. Revenue is recognition, commitments describe specified undelivered or
payment populations, and liquidity includes restricted and differently valued
assets. Collection, delivery and usable funds require additional dated facts.
3. Regional totals are customer-facing classifications. Their marginals do not
identify a product-region matrix, ownership graph or factory location. The
constructed matrices fit selected numbers and are not actual company histories.
4. A remedy affecting search or App Store terms can change receipts without
being a fine. A calculation needs the matter, remedy, effective dates, affected
contracts and response; recognition and settlement require further facts.
5. Adverse answer: the chapter only names third-party support. It does not yet
explain the separate duration of licences/content rights, developer incentives,
or data/payment access. These are a distinct missing mechanism bridge.
6. Adverse answer: three backward references imply prior explanations that
occur later. Use precise forward references and distinguish the investment
sensitivity already discussed from the later currency analysis.

The diagnosis warrants three reference repairs and one compact mechanism
bridge. It does not warrant sixteen additional filing-order summaries. No
displayed mathematics needs changing. Author reading and these answers do not
establish independent comprehension or final human/domain acceptance.
