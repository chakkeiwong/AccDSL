# Surface diagnostic disposition

The changed quality/inventory section has two distinct navigation labels,
no displayed equations, and no long-sentence, typo, duplicate-label or missing
reference candidates at the configured thresholds. Both unreferenced labels
remain useful stable section targets. The author read the rendered pages;
these counts do not establish readability. The separate candidate checker
checks the complete source tree, bibliography and final build logs because
this bounded surface diagnostic does not recurse through the manuscript.
