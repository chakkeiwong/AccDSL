## Research intent and skeptical audit

Question: can an ordinary gain-of-control branch retain its missing measurement
and timing dependencies while deriving acquisition excess and selected subsequent
profit/OCI allocation without inventing amounts or authorizing a report?
Baseline: ConditionalControlGroupScope/1 (23 cases), repaired endpoint/2 (30),
whole-interval history (22), and the dated acquisition authority specification
(13 PDFs; verification-attempt-03). The existing GroupPeriodReport/1 is retained
as the stable/full-ownership comparator, not extended or silently reused.

Audit passes for this bounded conditional algebra task. A balanced expression is
not a full accounting worksheet or a correctness certificate. The comparator
will be independently written coefficient maps and exact substitutions, not
numbers copied from the implementation. Only one known no-to-yes transition,
ordinary business-acquisition premise, aligned currency and constant qualifying
ownership rights are supported. Unknown history, loss/regain, separate reporting,
other routes and unsupported rights remain explicit dependencies. Exact source,
request, scope and measurement identities must bind. No annual pro-rating or
date-only truncation is allowed. A debt claim cannot satisfy a prior-equity slot.

## Evidence contract and diagnostic roles

Primary pass criterion: exact symbolic coefficients, complete original history,
required dependency lists, refusal reasons, and immutable inputs agree with an
independent expected fixture for every supported case. Selected evaluations are
formal algebra tests, explicitly synthetic; they supply no company measurement.
Promotion vetoes: missing unknowns, debt-as-equity, wrong date/currency/actor,
unsupported transition/route, clipped NCI deficit, automatic positive/negative
excess recognition, source tampering, or fabricated authorization. These require
repair of this candidate. Continuation vetoes: corrupted protected evidence,
unreliable baseline or scope binding. Stop the affected run and repair its plan;
continue independent work. Counts and runtime are explanatory only.
No ranking, compliance, goodwill recognition, financial posting, full period
reconciliation or whole requirement closure follows from a pass.
