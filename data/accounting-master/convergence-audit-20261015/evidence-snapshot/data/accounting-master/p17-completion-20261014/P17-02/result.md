## Later-information comparison

The retained Apple FY2025 annual HTML says that ASU 2023-07 was adopted for
the 2025 annual reporting period using retrospective transition. Its Note 13
then reports the 2024 and 2023 comparative segment tables with cost of sales,
research and development, selling and marketing, general and administrative,
and operating income. The selected FY2024 report had the same-period segment
sales and operating-income rows but did not expose those regional cost and
marketing rows in its Note 13 table.

The exact comparison script records 187 passing source, cell, arithmetic and
preservation checks across 126 selected cells, 20 same-period amount matches,
three retained HTML fragments. Seven source images were inspected, including
the original FY2024 PDF pages and derived later-source views. The 2024 and 2023
sales and operating-income comparatives match between the later and earlier
reports. The later table's regional rows reconcile to its reported segment
income and totals. Dashes remain reported dashes; they are never converted to
zero. The 2024 original page was rendered and inspected beside the derived
later table.

This is a later-information view. It does not rewrite the FY2024 original
view, establish that every comparative was available at the earlier cutoff,
or prove that equal displayed amounts have identical measurement scope. The
FY2025 labels “General and administrative” and the FY2024 “Other corporate
expenses, net” have matching displayed amounts for 2024 and 2023, but the
amount equality alone does not establish a general concept mapping. FY2025
also names New Zealand in Rest of Asia Pacific where the earlier wording did
not; no perimeter change is inferred.

## Chronology and query consequences

The retained Apple event ledger identifies eight annual SEC filing events for
2018–2025, while the source package separately records report, filing,
retrieval, publication/availability and ingestion roles. Filing dates are
evidenced for the event ledger, but selected issuer-hosted or converted PDF
bytes are not thereby proven identical to the filed bytes or available at the
historical cutoff. The wider accepted corpus has 56 filing-date records and
136 unverified publication dates; later supplemental declarations cover 35 of
those 136, leaving 101 without such accepted supplemental evidence in those
inquiries. None supplies an intraday availability cutoff.

Reading the content of a named retained source is possible now: the old source
has no separate Americas cost/marketing rows; the later one reports
89,587/9,802 for fiscal 2024. That content comparison does not require an
invented publication timestamp. An investor-information query at any historical
cutoff additionally needs evidence that the exact chosen version was publicly
available by then. A system-version query needs the retained ingestion and
review versions. A harmonised view needs an admitted transformation and keeps
the original assertions and lineage. Report periods, adoption plans and filing
events cannot backdate later detail. Missing clock evidence blocks the
affected as-of query, not this named-source comparison.
