# SectorCompany/1 — finite shared-sector report

Closed documentary exchange; operation=report, posts=false, mutations=[].
The reference.py shape checks are the machine-readable field contract and
authored-cases.json supplies the exact admitted examples. This is not a
controller, production adapter or general conformance implementation.

## Inputs and ownership

P/USD, B/FCU and I/FCU have the same protected historical EU profile. Two
explicit intervals close on2026-01-01 and2027-01-01, from2025-01-01 opening.
Component reports retain their original contexts. Explicit unit-scale1 mappings
admit only financial deltas; notional component capital must not be posted.
The bank schedule adapts only the dated collection path of the protected B1
request, leaving original-rate/fee rules unchanged. Insurance reuses the exact
protected loss-service request. Every locked input is checked before evaluation.

Legal openings, all rates and contractual cash allocation are supplied, finite
canonical rational strings. Bank cash300/deposits200, insurer cash200/liability100,
tax20%, deposit20, and guarantee initial6/releases2,2/ECL7,3 are fixed for this
profile. Parent cash, positive exchange rates, supported majority ownership and
cash consideration are bounded inputs. Initial investments must equal opening
ownership times net assets times opening FX. Zero/negative rates here refer to
FX, not the separate B1 interest-rate domain, and are refused.

Each required factual/support finding is a strict boolean: unknown gives
unresolved; false gives unsupported. Control, equal rights, no opening goodwill,
outside-basis exception, no tax on translation, tax-loss recoverability,
guarantee scope/ECL/revenue/initial measurement, contractual cash allocation and
operating cash policy are separate conditions. No automatic recovery from a
forecast book profit is permitted. No professional judgment is inferred.

## Calculation and report

Map loan and insurer profit/cash/liability movements into legal books. Compute
each taxpayer's loss, current tax, DTA and deferred expense separately. The
guarantee is max(unamortized initial liability,ECL); service revenue and overlay
movement remain distinguishable. Current taxes are paid at the period event.

For a foreign entity with opening equity E, after-tax profit p and opening,
event and closing rates r0/re/rc, translation OCI=E(rc-r0)+p(rc-re).
Allocate period profit/OCI using ownership during that interval. Only then
remove purchased NCI at translated carrying net assets, debit consideration to
cash and record the difference in parent equity. The separate parent investment
increases by cost; subsidiary legal books never receive the worksheet entry.

Demand-deposit matching requires identical ID, creditor, debtor, currency,
amount and cutoff. Eliminate its translated cash/deposit liability and its cash
currency effect. The external guarantee remains. Report legal books, tax
origins, guarantee components, group profit/OCI/owners/NCI, elimination records
and gross cash origins. Cash FX is derived from opening cash and dated flows,
not inserted as a plug. Each output equation is checked before return.

Input/provenance/domain refusals return report=None and components=None,
posts=false, mutations=[]. No partial report survives a failed later check.
Repeated requests are deterministic and do not mutate input or protected files.
