# Annual-07/08 source reading and integration decision

10 October 2026. Original annual PDF page 12 (printed 9) was read at normal
resolution. Both headings and all three paragraphs match their bounded source
partitions. The next intellectual-property risk on the same page is outside
this unit. Five selected dependency quotes were read in their exact retained
contexts: FIFO, historical PP&E depreciation expense, Note 12 purchase
obligations, MD&A manufacturing obligations and annual-05 outsourcing.

Annual-07 describes hardware/software, purchased components and service quality
with distinct assertions. Specialized health applications and new technologies
including AI can increase safety risks; no assurance of detecting/fixing every
defect is retained. Historical unmet service performance differs from possible
future liability, recall, modification, asset write-off, warranty/litigation/
fine costs, reputation, demand and launch effects. Third-party exploits do not
identify an actual security incident. Outsourcing reimbursement provisions
may exist while Apple generally remains responsible to the consumer; no right
or recoverable amount is established.

Annual-08 reports obsolete/excess/above-NRV inventory write-downs, necessary
order-cancellation reserves, and impairment reviews of named long-lived assets,
supplier-site capital assets and inventory prepayments. It describes excess
carrying value over fair value after impairment is determined. That is the
issuer's description, not independently checked operative methodology across
all asset classes. Current recoverability is a management belief with no
assurance against future charges. The second paragraph describes ordering and
inventory ahead of announcements/shipments, typical requirements up to 150
days, and forecast error in either direction or unutilised firm commitments.
None establishes actual defective units, surplus inventory, reserve amounts,
fair values, liability/recognition dates or a universal contractual horizon.

The source-specific bridge missing after Version 2.22 is the distinction between
available output, usable output and the obligations/measurement consequences
of excess or defective output. Add one cohesive section, not separate source
summaries, in Version 2.23. Keep existing text and mathematics. Use the
outsourcing consumer-responsibility qualification to prevent an implicit
assumption that warranty liability is always recoverable from a supplier.
Explain physical quality, demand forecasts and accounting assessment in that
order. The original skeptical audit passes; no new numeric model/default is
introduced and missing facts trigger unresolved queries.
