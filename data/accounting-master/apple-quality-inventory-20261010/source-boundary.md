# Source and inference boundary

Annual-07/08 are dated issuer disclosures. Their reported policies and current
recoverability belief are evidence, not independently checked operative
accounting authority. The source's conditional excess-carrying-value/fair-value
description is preserved without extending it to every asset or skipping an
applicable assessment. FIFO, historical depreciation expense, long-term
obligations, MD&A manufacturing and outsourcing are selected context only.

The comparison of equal reported inventory amounts is explicitly illustrative,
not a complete witness fitting all Apple statements or an identified history.
No defect incidence, actual claim/recall, supplier reimbursement, cancellation
fee, asset fair value, write-down, liability or branch weight is calculated.
The 150-day qualifier does not justify a numerical model default. It does not
establish that the Note 12 and MD&A populations are equal or disjoint. Physical
output, forecasts, accounting assessments and journals remain separate.
