Chapter 33
The accounting language
33.1
What the current language can express
A company model needs to keep disclosures, possible histories and chosen methods distinct as
its accounts evolve. A reported balance constrains a history; it does not reveal every contract
or transaction that generated it. A collection policy supplies a behavioural assumption; it does
not establish what a customer will pay. An accounting method turns an eligible event into state
changes and postings; a balanced result alone does not establish that the method applies. The
language makes these distinctions explicit so that reports retain the meaning of the facts and
assumptions behind them.
The current design has several checked, bounded language profiles. Together they cover source-
constrained histories, conditional company paths, selected shared-book operations and complete
method authoring. Their interfaces are explicit, but they are not yet one complete whole-company
grammar. The earlier source compositions in Sections A.54.17 and A.54.18 make complete
methods, registries, state invariants and full event intent inspectable. Section A.54.19 extends
that authoring to the selected opening checks, ordered eligibility and parameterized construction
and assessment recipes. One company source contains those algorithms together with explicit
events, reports and carry commands; its imports hold the opening facts, schemas and statement
mapping. The selected path reuses typed evaluators without calling the native lifecycle or
operating-method builder. This finite correspondence does not complete the wider financial
language or establish accounting applicability. Section A.54.20 extends the opening contract
to live carried claims under an explicit supplied-population declaration and the independently
selected full invariant. The source states that premise without inferring completeness from a
successful continuation. Nested term histories require a further distinction: an earlier overdue
assessment uses the due date applicable then, not the current due date.
Section A.54.21
supplies owner-qualified queries, typed per-claim and per-assessment rules, and their combined
semantic checks. Section A.54.22 binds them to an explicit selected state contract and direct
source for prospective amendments, assessments, collections and carry. This bounded synthetic
continuation preserves the separate requirements for actual-source and accounting admission.
To represent the historical record, company method selection first binds to a source-constrained
partial state (Section A.10). Statement observations have distinct relation roles rather than one
undifferentiated set of totals (Section A.11). Selected balances are tested jointly under explicit
rounding and identity hypotheses (Section A.12), with opening, movement and closing dates
kept separate across statements (Section A.13). These declarations can expose inconsistency or
missing information without inventing the underlying books.
