# Forecast coverage and chronology now have exact worked decisions

23 October 2026. This additive proposal makes the existing E3 eligibility,
timing, first-release and failure obligations concrete. It preserves the user's
fixed-company future-origin direction and leaves the pending forecast-period
and company-weight choices open. The original 18 October specification already
required these records; the increment is an orthogonal status/transition
contract and independently prescribed finite outcomes.

`contract.md` separates an eligible cell from its forecast attempt, outcome,
scale, chronology and exposure. Unknown and contrary timing evidence both
prevent timing admission, while making different factual claims. An exact
score on a late forecast remains a possible retrospective calculation.
Neither that score nor a timestamp check establishes prospective acceptance.
Missing outcomes and forecast failures retain separate reasons in the same row.
First-release and restatement branches cannot silently replace each other.

The authored reference examples pass:12 timing predicates,4 invalid time-bound
refusals,6 eligible cells,3 coverage views,10 corrupted-record refusals,
2 aggregation boundaries and3 retry cases. All checks use standard-library
date operations and exact rational point-law CRPS. No acquired code, actual
company forecast or forecasting package ran. No failed example or changed
expected result occurred.

The last row is a fictional evidence-screen result, not empirical admission.
The equal conditional means conceal very different coverage. Even keeping the
denominator at six would not make an observed contribution the full effect.
If a failed algorithm supplies no law, its CRPS is undefined rather than just
a hidden real number. A declared combined service or missingness argument needs
its own mathematical target; no fallback or penalty is chosen here.

outcome policy must not change after inspecting scores; they are not statistical
rankings. The replay example admits one identical event once and rejects a

Post-run red team: the strongest misleading interpretation is that a local
validator can certify the truth of its evidence labels. It cannot. Time bounds,
first-release identity, exposure and accounting definitions require their own
support. The supplied point laws make the arithmetic exact but say nothing
about a learned model. Actual provenance contradicting a supplied status would
overturn that cell's admission. The weakest practical evidence remains the
unadmitted source vintages and unspecified future-origin law.
