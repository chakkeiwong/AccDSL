# Author review of the forecast-accountability proposal

The proposal addresses a concrete refinement rather than claiming that the old
E3 specification omitted failure denominators. That specification is retained
as its baseline. Target identity, the six-model ladder, same economics and
information, first-release scoring, as-of positive assets, complete laws and
separate joint scores remain unchanged. Neither pending preference is selected.

The closed-interval time predicates distinguish proof, contradiction and
uncertainty. Strict lock-before-release equality is correctly contradicted;
touching uncertain intervals need not be contradicted and remain unverified.
Cutoff, creation, lock and deadline have separate meanings. The rule that
cutoff precedes creation is a proposed chronology requirement for this as-of
record, not a claim that real-world earlier predictions never exist. An earlier
prediction needs its own earlier cutoff/identity. Actual evidence quality,
target elapsedness and exposure remain external to the reference calculation.

Point-law CRPS equals absolute error under the already checked E3 identity.
The independent expected six-cell table matches the reference calculation.
Both simultaneous failures on C are preserved. E and F have raw scores but
cannot pass the supplied prospective screen. D retains its raw score despite
missing scale. Weights are explicitly fictional and remain unchanged between
views. A predeclared zero-weight cell has no contribution to that weighted
target, while all its failure and veto records remain; it is not post-result
reweighting. No successful-cell mean becomes a full-service expected effect.

The expectation partition assumes d exists and is integrable; the proposal
explicitly refuses to treat an undefined algorithm score as an unobserved
numeric variable. It supplies neither a missingness model nor a probability
law. The full-service target is still a research-design prerequisite.

Reference code is deliberately limited to finite point-law examples and supplied
time/status records. It does not implement the full proposed format or prove
source authenticity, durable append-only storage, concurrency, complete-law
validation, fallback validity or production behaviour. The result states these
limits. Counterexample controls cover the claimed invariants and do not purport
to cover every malformed input. No additional test ladder is justified for
this documentary unit after the prescribed checks pass.

Author disposition: the bounded proposal and exact examples pass, subject to
separate protection/preflight/completion checks. No independent accounting,
scientific, implementability or human acceptance is granted. The next E3
substantive task depends on the requested forecast-period and weighting
objectives; independent source/design preparation remains authorised.
