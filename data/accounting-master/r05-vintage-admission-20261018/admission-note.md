## What the retained evidence establishes

The active panel contains 192 selected reports from nine issuer families.
The QA registry contains 224 records; its 32 superseded Allianz/Unilever
records are excluded. Every selected original is hashed against active
coverage, and every joined claim retains an artifact hash and JSON pointer.
Source claims inherit the author reading and review status of their original
investigations. This is a source-identity reconciliation, not a fresh reading
of all financial reports or independent annotation adjudication.

The frozen date inventory retains its 56 filing-date metadata records and
136 unverified-date records, including the two longer URL-date qualifications.
Later investigations add twelve official SEC filing events for JPMorgan
2018–2020 and 35 publication declarations: sixteen Munich Re, sixteen Henkel,
and three HSBC annual reports. These three sets are disjoint in this panel:
103 reports have at least one of these selected-source date roles, while 89
have none in the inspected evidence. The ten reports with alternate-source
date candidates are a separate, potentially overlapping population; those
dates are not transferred to a selected version.

All 192 selected_byte_available_at values remain null and every as_of_admission
remains false. A filing event identifies its regulator accession and primary
document, not an issuer PDF's unchanged historical bytes. An attributed
publication statement gives a declared day without inventing an hour or
timezone. A later mirror, filename date, acquisition time or same-period
complete filing cannot fill this missing evidence.

The original SEC filingDate and acceptanceDateTime stay separate. For the
2018 JPMorgan annual event they remain 2019-02-26 and
2019-02-27T02:57:25.000Z; no clock conversion or correction is inferred.
HSBC's 2023 declaration remains 21 February 2024 despite filename prefix
240226. Henkel's 2025 interim PR code 08 24 0 does not override its declared
7 August 2025 publication date. The Munich Re regulation-publication and
HSBC stress-test-results passages remain excluded from report-publication
evidence. Munich Re print/editorial dates retain their different roles.

## Exposure and permissible comparisons

Every active family has source acquisition/QA exposure. Thirty-one provisional
pilot annotations concern four exact selected versions: Apple 2025 Q1, HSBC
2024 annual, Munich Re 2024 annual and Midea 2024 annual. These are author
development answers. Their cross-review did not produce blind, independently
adjudicated gold labels.

The forty JPMorgan semantic assertions concern four separately retained complete
issuer 10-Ks for 2022–2025. Those files differ from the selected annual PDFs.
The join preserves both identities and records issuer-family exposure; it
does not call the assertions exact-selected-byte annotations. Publication
readings and twelve event/cover checks add known reading exposure. These are
a nonexhaustive lower bound: an unlisted report, passage, task or family is not
established to be unseen.

The development/validation-candidate/test-candidate role map remains
not_promoted. Proposed test families include substantively inspected Munich
Re and JPMorgan. Keep all years, mirrors, amendments and related versions in
their issuer family. This prevents one split leak but establishes neither
independence across firms/years nor a clean test set. Nine purposively chosen
issuers are not a random company sample; 192 report rows are not 192 independent
forecast replications. Overlapping outcomes and common shocks need an explicit
target-specific treatment.
