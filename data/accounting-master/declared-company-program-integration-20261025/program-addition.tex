\paragraph{Run the declared company through successive periods.}
A company model needs to use these methods in one sequence, with each operation
reading the book produced by its predecessors. The finite
\texttt{DeclaredCompanyProgram/1} combines an opening book, named method registry,
statement map and an authored list of events, reports and provisional carries.
An event names its method and supplies dated, typed inputs. A report names its
cutoff, direct equity explanations and unresolved note requirements. A carry also
names the retained-equity account, next period endpoint and every memory policy.
The program constructs each method request from the current book at that turn;
future balance hashes and expected journals are not authored inputs.

An interim report can follow the last supplied transaction by several months.
Its explicit cutoff advances only the observation date of a provisional copy;
it creates no journal, cash flow or memory change. The trace records that advance,
and a failed report leaves the preceding book unchanged. Reaching December's
last day does not prove that all December events were supplied. Reports continue
to use the actual opening of their current fiscal period. After a successful
carry, its returned next opening becomes that basis; earlier income and cash
flows do not enter the new period's report. Supplied equity explanations and note
requirements belong to each report rather than being copied silently across years.

Consider the preceding declared book before its illustrative owner contribution:
cash is USD~100, inventory USD~20, another asset USD~10, liabilities USD~30 and
equity USD~100. A supplied sale for USD~40 releases USD~15 of inventory and earns
USD~25. After an interim report, paying USD~3.50 of principal leaves year-end cash
USD~136.50, inventory USD~5, liabilities USD~26.50 and equity USD~125. Carry closes
the temporary accounts. In the next year, selling the remaining inventory for
USD~8 earns USD~3; paying the remaining USD~26.50 leaves cash USD~118, no inventory
or liability, the other asset USD~10 and equity USD~128. A second carry retains the
lifetime payment total of USD~30 and all event and close identities. These exact
amounts, every constructed request and every engine result agree with separately
written calls under two different account charts. The sale's aggregate carrying
cost and applicability finding are supplied assumptions; this example does not
infer a lot valuation or tax treatment.

The bounded program checks all command shapes and method definitions before
execution, but evaluates economic prerequisites against the book at their actual
turn. Same-day operations follow their explicit sequence positions. An unknown
finding, false prerequisite, invalid date, repeated identity or failed carry
returns the exact refused command alongside the entire successful prefix. Report
and carry changes become visible together only after that command succeeds.
Canonical replay recomputes the complete result, including report and archive
references; changing an input or substituting a result prevents verification.
The initial limits of 64 commands and an 8~MiB trace bound execution for this
reference. Exceeding the trace limit retains the prefix and identifies the
oversized result by digest; it makes no scalability or durable-recovery claim.
