# Skeptical release-plan review

27 October 2026. Internal author review; not independent acceptance.

The audit initially risked following a stale 5.49 baseline. The exact 5.50 delta
is now captured and read: recovery scope plus a null-only intake. The original
snapshot is preserved. Any successor after the cutoff needs its own delta review.

Three potential overstatements were rejected. The project does have real-company
connections, exact/resumable policy calculations and a substantial typed reference
implementation. These cannot be described as absent. Scope language immediately
preceding a new method can be pedagogical progression; the inspected page 143
supports that reading, so this is recorded as a navigation/scope reconciliation
issue rather than a categorical contradiction. Missing package-relative schema
paths do not mean the schema was lost: it exists in docs/design/spec and the
archive. The packaging issue remains reproducibility from the declared bundle.

The plan explicitly avoids two opposite errors: requiring a complete production
forecasting system before releasing a design, and treating an unsupported label
as permission to abandon required design work. The original acceptance wording
controls each disposition. All 18 packages and all 114 current requirement/
interaction/operation/literature rows are mapped to specific release work.
The 201 failure rows are retained without upgrading documented designs into tests.

Wrong baselines, proxy promotion, hidden assumptions, stale actions, circular
oracles, environment mismatch, missing stopping conditions and unjustified method
ranking have explicit checks. Builds and deterministic finite examples cannot
establish human comprehension, general numerical guarantees, compliance or
forecast validity. There is no stochastic ranking in this audit.

Verdict: the C0–C7 closure sequence is suitable as a locally reviewed plan, with
unit-level evidence contracts required before new nontrivial experiments. The
current user request authorizes this audit and plan, not publication or paid
review. Independent scientific, accounting and reader acceptance is still pending.
