Write the selected company rules and commands in one source
A generated method can calculate the right journal while leaving the modeller unable to state
the rule that generated it. In the claim example, the customer check, the source of a claim
identifier and the order of assessment updates are substantive choices. Naming a template
by its digest records which choice was made, but does not let an author write or change that
choice. The selected company source therefore includes the opening conditions, state invariant,
eligibility stages and construction recipes themselves. The earlier source frontends remain
useful predecessor comparisons; this successor targets the declared opening and execution path
together. The opening template in this profile still requires an empty claim map. Section A.54.20
extends that boundary for explicitly complete supplied carried claims.
Consider a collection whose claim identifier is absent. Reading that claim’s customer immediately
would fail for a missing row, obscuring the intended refusal that the claim does not exist. In
OrderedEligibilityText/1, the author writes a membership stage before the customer stage.
A binding such as (map_contains "/claims" "claim_id") supplies the membership predicate.
A later map_field binding names the same map and event input, the customer field and
the preceding presence stage. Compilation accepts that lookup only when the earlier stage
unconditionally requires the matching membership fact. All stages compile, including ones that
an earlier refusal would prevent from running. An empty map cannot hide a malformed field
lookup.
The full event-input types, stage identifiers, failure reasons and guarded predicates are also
declarations. The retained order first resolves the outer event structure and unknown inputs,
then approval and the operation’s specific conditions. Moving claim existence ahead of the
sealed-day condition changes which reason is returned when both fail. Negating the approval
predicate changes which events are eligible. These edits demonstrate that the source controls
the selected rule; they are different rules, not equivalent ways to spell the retained contract.
