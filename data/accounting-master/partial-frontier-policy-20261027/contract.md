# Fixed-domain policy restrictions from a partial temporal frontier

27 October2026. MC01/03/05/11/12/13/18/24/25/26/28/33/34;
W01/03/04/11/12/13/16. Research intent: determine whether a known-cell policy
restriction is sound before search completes, with original pending domains intact.

Inspected baseline: the accepted policy solver returns first rules and only second
rules reachable under those first rules. Its policy domains therefore vary with
selected rows. Raw set inclusion across partial source outcomes is wrong for that
representation. Use a caller-declared fixed total policy domain, with every first
and second message key from the selected complete declared comparison. Each total
policy assigns pay/draw to all those keys, including currently unreachable keys.
This is a retrospective exact design comparison, not an online discovery procedure:
the complete saved family supplies a declared comparison universe and never
becomes a claim that pending cells have been checked. Actual actor information is
still limited by the unchanged observation declarations; the declared type tells
which messages can occur, not which hidden mechanism occurred.

Use only the five complete observation fixtures whose inputs exactly match saved
unit-step frontiers: baseline,different_actor,changed_message_labels,
coarse_refined_family,equivalent_offset_dates. Derive the exact checkpoint population from saved records before interpreting it.
No source preparation or evidence writer reruns. Verify complete observation
bindings and each frontier against their external identities first.

For known selected outcomes, retain all source-support paths and select exactly
their full action rows from the declared comparison. Every refused1 row forbids
one first action; every refused2 row forbids a first/second combination. Intersect
these constraints over the fixed full policy universe. Independent comparator:
solve the known rows with the prior forbidden-combination oracle, then enumerate
all total extensions of each reachable rule over the declared keys. Empty known
rows yield the whole declared universe as unconstrained candidates, never proven
feasibility. Complete results must equal the total extensions of the original
full reachable policies. Compare full bit-vector sets, not counts.

A partial checkpoint returns candidates plus exact known supports and pending
paths/domains. No candidates can prove conditional infeasibility even while cells
remain, because adding constraints cannot rescue a policy in the same fixed class.
Nonempty candidates before completion mean unresolved feasibility; only a complete,
consistent family with nonempty candidates establishes conditional feasibility.
Complete unresolved source outcomes remain unresolved, not zero. This selected
five-case profile requires declared maps for every possible complete outcome and
refuses any unknown/unmapped outcome instead of dropping it.

Promotion criterion: all saved pauses have identical full candidate sets under
two formulations; nested known sets produce nested candidates; complete projections
match original reachable policies; no known constraint is lost and no pending
identity changes. Candidate removal carries a refused-row witness. Promotion veto:
wrong subset,support,domain,map,source,actor,time,method,status or premature completion.
Corrupt comparator is a continuation veto for affected runs. Runtime/counts are
explanatory; no ranking,scale,probability,actual knowledge,durability or production
claim. Defaults: fixed domain is an explicit comparison hypothesis; maximum12
binary keys/4096 total rules is a tested scope bound,not a general default.
