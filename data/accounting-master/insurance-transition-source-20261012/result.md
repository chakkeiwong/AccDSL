Full retrospection reconstructs groups as if the standard had always applied.
Modified retrospection permits specified adjustments only to the extent that
reasonable/supportable retrospective information is missing. Necessary
information for that modified method cannot simply be invented; C6(a) requires
fair-value transition if it cannot be obtained. Fair-value transition needs
both the insurance group's fair value and its FCF at transition. The fair value
of the underlying investment pool is a different quantity. No fair value,
impracticability decision or actual group measurement was computed here.

Two exceptions materially affect an explanation. C5A allows a qualifying
direct-participation group to elect fair value even when retrospection is
possible, subject to prospective B115 use and prior qualifying mitigation.
C25 moves the transition date back when earlier adjusted comparatives are
chosen. Specific nil prescriptions and OCI exceptions cannot be generalized
into a rule that missing information equals zero. The permitted use of
transition-date information for an accounting restatement also does not make
the later report available to an earlier historical forecast.

The comparator is the existing Ping An original/restated-balance discussion
and transition placeholder. This AASB compilation is a scoped design source;
it does not govern Ping An's ASBE accounting by inference. Its stated period
starts run from 1 January 2023 to before 1 July 2026. Four official-source
requests acquired two indexes, followed by two 404 HTML bodies at constructed
historical AASB 108/13 PDF URLs. The detailed impracticability and valuation
dependencies remain unreviewed. The paginated official portal supplies a
