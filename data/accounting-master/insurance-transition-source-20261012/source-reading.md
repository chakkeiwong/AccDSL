# Three transition methods preserve different information

12 October 2026. Author source review, not independent accounting acceptance.
Primary source: retained AASB 17 compilation no. 4, 31 December 2022,
F2023C00382; PDF 1, 5, 57–64 read. The cover limits this compilation to
annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023 and before 1 July 2026.
Pages 57, 58, 60, 61, 62 and 63 were also inspected as readable renders.
This is a selected transition review, not a full standard or amendment audit.

The question is how to reconstruct opening insurance balances when the past
cannot simply be replayed. The manuscript's Ping An balances are disclosures
under ASBE. They motivate this question but do not establish that the following
AASB provisions governed that issuer or particular groups.

| Provision | Source condition and consequence | Reconstruction input or limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1–C2A, C25 | Initial application begins the first annual period applying the standard. Transition normally begins the immediately preceding annual period; choosing earlier adjusted comparatives moves it to the earliest adjusted comparative period. C1 has separate public-sector timing. | Store adoption, selected comparative periods and transition separately. Neither is the publication date of the restatement. |
| C3–C4 | Retrospective application is required unless impracticable or C5A applies. Identify/measure groups as if the standard always applied, handle acquisition assets separately, remove incompatible balances and recognise the net difference in equity. B115 cannot be used before transition; its prospective designation has timing conditions. | Historical contracts, grouping, assumptions, cash flows, prior services and opening balances are inputs. A single restated total cannot identify them. |
| C5, C5B | Group-specific impracticability permits modified retrospection subject to C6(a), or fair value. Acquisition-cash-flow assets have a separate impracticability decision. | Record the group or asset and the basis of the decision; do not infer impracticability from a missing downloaded document. Detailed AASB 108 decision criteria are not inspected here. |
| C5A | A direct-participation group may elect fair value despite feasible retrospection only with prospective B115 use from transition and qualifying pretransition mitigation. | Evidence of terms, designation and prior mitigation; the B115 instrument scopes remain different for entity-share and other-FCF risks. |
| C6–C8 | Modified retrospection seeks the closest retrospective result using reasonable/supportable information without undue cost or effort; maximise retrospective information. Each specified modification requires missing retrospective information to that extent. If necessary modified-method information cannot be obtained, fair value is required. | Keep an information-availability decision per modification. This is not a free choice of convenient assumptions. |
| C9–C11 | Conditional use of transition-date information for grouping, participation and discretionary-cash-flow assessments; acquired-claim classification and annual-cohort relief are also specified. | A C9 transition assessment is an explicit exception to ordinary inception assessment, not evidence that subsequent markets routinely reopen classification. |
| C12–C14A | Adjust transition/earlier estimable future cash flows for known intervening cash flows, including expired contracts. Historical yield-curve approximations or spreads use at least three years before transition. Risk release refers to similar contracts. Interim-policy relief has its own condition. | Retain actual intervening cash flows, curve histories, comparable risk-release evidence and interim policy; no invented historical calibration. |
| C14B–C16 | Systematic/rational acquisition allocation, prescribed nil amounts when its necessary information is absent, interest/service allocation of CSM and systematic past loss allocation. | The explicit C14D nil prescription is not a general missing-value-to-zero rule. Coverage units and past-service evidence remain necessary. |
| C16A–C16C | Reinsurance loss recovery requires the stated prior/contemporaneous entry condition and coverage allocation; no component is identified if necessary information is unavailable. | Recovery percentage, treaty timing and covered onerous contracts; no generic quota-share shortcut. |
| C17–C17A | Direct-participation modified measurement starts with underlying-item fair value less FCF, adjusts specified past charges/payments/risk release/acquisition flows, then removes past-service CSM. If a loss component results, set that component to nil and increase LRC excluding it by the same amount. | Underlying-item fair value is not the fair value of the insurance group in C20. C17(e) does not erase the liability. |
| C18–C19A | Discount and cumulative OCI alternatives depend on grouping, estimation and the applicable finance method; direct-participation underlying-item OCI is an express exception to nil. | Preserve group/cohort decision, curve convention, finance policy, underlying OCI and interim policy. |
| C20 | Fair-value transition determines CSM or the loss component from insurance-group fair value less FCF, both at transition. AASB 13 paragraph 47's demand-feature rule is excluded. | Two separately supported measurements are needed. Neither book value nor the underlying investment pool can silently replace group fair value. Full AASB 13 valuation is unresolved. |
| C20A–C20B | Reinsurance loss recovery uses the specified expected recovery percentage and rational coverage allocation without the B119C condition. | Do not carry the modified route's timing condition into this route without authority. |
| C21–C24 | Listed assessments may use reasonable/supportable inception/recognition information or transition-date information; cohort/rate relief and OCI choices have express conditions. | Record which information date was used. C24 nil does not override the underlying-OCI condition for direct participation. |
| C24A–C24B | Acquisition assets use transition-date costs of the specified rights; their amount is excluded from insurance-group measurement. | Distinct rights and group measurement prevent double counting. |
| C26–C28 | Limited comparative disclosure relief and claims-development history relief; unadjusted earlier comparatives must be clearly identified with their different basis. | Preserve both reported bases and disclosure omissions; relief does not establish equivalence. |
| C28A–C28E | Classification overlay for financial assets with specified concurrent AASB 9 adoption circumstances; transition-date information, impairment choices, opening equity and disclosure requirements. | Inventory only: AASB 9/139 dependencies and actual asset facts remain unreviewed here. |
| C29–C33A | Eligible asset redesignation, initial-application-date facts, optional restatement only without hindsight, disclosures, and an adapted overlay for certain derecognised assets. | Inventory only: this explicit C31 hindsight restriction concerns these financial assets; it is not a substitute for checking AASB 108's general retrospective requirements. |

The source directly supports the method conditions above. The local design
inference is to retain, for each group, its transition route, each permitted
modification, the information date and evidence used, CSM/loss/FCF/OCI and
acquisition components, and the resulting opening-equity reconciliation.
A restatement made using allowed transition-date information can be valid in
its reporting context while remaining unavailable to a forecast made before
its publication. That distinction follows from the project's separate economic
and availability clocks; it is not an additional AASB accounting prohibition.

Dependency disposition: the previously closed VFA review covers selected
grouping, measurement, participation and B115–B118 conditions. It is retained
as bounded prior support. The transition formulas also refer to discounting,
coverage units, reinsurance, OCI and interim-policy provisions; this review
maps those references but does not certify all dependencies or a complete
implementation. AASB 108 and AASB 13 primary copies were not found locally.
Two official indexes were acquired; two constructed historical PDF URLs
returned 404 HTML. No usable dependency text was acquired. The portal is a
new index lead for a separately planned follow-up, not a source-absence finding.

No numerical diagnostic is needed to distinguish the methods. Selecting
arbitrary opening fair values or estimates would only demonstrate subtraction
and would not answer the missing-information question. No issuer balances,
probabilities, impracticability judgment or accounting election are invented.
