The fourteen dependency spans retain their full quoted text. Both Note 1
passages identify the disclosed consolidation perimeter and calendars; their
remaining policies keep the prior bounded revenue/assets/tax/debt/compensation
results as dependencies rather than reimplementing them. Both Note 2 passages
retain revenue and deferral qualifications plus the product-mix sentence.
That sentence limits admissible interpretations qualitatively; no numerical
tolerance has been inferred from “generally consistent” or “moderately higher.”

Both operations statements supply comparator sales, R&D, SG&A and income cells.
Annual Note 5 supplies net PP&E. Both MD&A segment passages preserve all five
regions' dated product/currency explanations and repeated sales columns. They
are issuer explanations; their direction cannot supply individual contributions
or causal response parameters. The annual fiscal-period and interim seasonality
passages prevent mechanical annualisation. Annual Note 12's manufacturing
passage supplies a separate supplier geography, without resolving costs,
ownership or capacity. Both reporting-change passages retain the issuer's
description of additional segment expenses/CODM disclosure and planned
retrospective FY2025 adoption. Operative authority and subsequent implementation
are not checked here.

The seven declared queries correspond to issuer regions, regional sales,
reported/allocated regional profit, country sales, country assets, product-region
stress and dated restatement views. Each has a supported answer and explicit
unknown in the supplement. Actual fact recovery, quantitative interpretation
of qualitative product mix, tax/FX/control mappings, forecast calibration and
whole-package coverage remain open. No probabilities are assigned to either
allocation construction.
