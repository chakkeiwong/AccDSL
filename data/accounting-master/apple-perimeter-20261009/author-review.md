## Cold-reader questions answered from the rendered slice

1. **What does wholly owned establish?** Apple reports consolidated accounts of
   Apple Inc. and wholly owned subsidiaries. This is positive scope evidence,
   while exact immediate parents, ownership classes and intervals remain absent.
2. **Why not an exhaustive eighteen-company group?** The complete footnote
   says other names are omitted under its aggregate significance exception.
   Neither that wording nor the row count establishes scenario irrelevance.
3. **What is learned from Singapore?** The risk text supports subsidiaries
   organized there, but the exhibit has no Singapore row. Their names are not
   invented and the narrative does not allege a filing contradiction.
4. **What follows for Apple India Private Limited?** Its reported incorporation
   is India. The general segment rule associates India-customer sales with Europe;
   the legal seller and functional currency are not thereby assigned.
5. **Why does partner-held equipment not establish consolidation of the partner?**
   Equipment custody and a supply contract describe relationships different from
   corporate control. Disruption can affect supply/assets/prepayments without
   proving group membership or specifying scenario amounts.
6. **What is needed for dated tax, FX and intercompany queries?** Entity identities,
   counterparties, ownership/control intervals, compatible books, currencies,
   tax bases, transactions and applicable methods. The historic unnamed branches
   cannot be matched by country/name similarity alone.

## Skeptical result review

Strongest alternative: later filings, registries or private entity books could
resolve particular parents, currencies and branch identities. That possibility
does not make them known from this retained package. A verified dated mapping
would replace the corresponding unknown with a supported assertion, preserving
the earlier source view. The weakest boundary is legal/operative interpretation
of significance and consolidation; the manuscript preserves these as issuer
disclosure and leaves operative authority unverified. Current source statements
support the bounded conclusions; they do not identify all admissible group
histories or validate a full consolidation engine.
