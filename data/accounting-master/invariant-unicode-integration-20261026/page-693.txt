A.54.20
Begin a reporting period with live claims
A new reporting period need not begin with an empty receivables register. Customers may
still owe amounts from sales in the preceding year. The closing procedure carries those claims
forward with their identifiers, issue dates, remaining amounts and assessment history. An
opening rule that requires an empty register would reject this ordinary continuation even when
the carried book and opening statements are internally consistent.
The retained examples make that conflict concrete. Two company charts carry one live claim
with a remaining amount of 20 into 1 January 2028. Two further programs carry three claims
whose remaining amounts are 13 each, giving a receivables balance of 39. The claims were issued
during 2027 and assessed on 31 December 2027. The new reporting period ends exclusively on 1
January 2029; its period flows are empty, while the claim records and previously seen event
identities remain. The original continuation contains three commands in each one-claim case
and two in each three-claim case. Those ten commands supply an exact comparison for the
opening extension.
The selected empty-opening template rejects all four books at empty_claims. Removing that
stage alone exposes a second empty-specific condition: claim_total requires the receivables
balance to be zero. Removing both conditions makes the remaining opening checks pass, but it
also admits five deliberately corrupted books. The corruptions are a wrong receivables total,
a negative remaining claim, a missing sale identity, an issue date after the opening and an
