# Source reading and accounting consequences

The input is the retained official AASB 17 compilation no. 4, compilation date
31 December 2022, prepared 6 April 2023 and registered as F2023C00382 on
26 April 2023. PDF 1 and 6 limit it to annual periods beginning on or after
1 January 2023 and before 1 July 2026. The source URL and exact PDF hash are
preserved in standards-support.json and this unit's baseline. No new current
edition, complete later amendment chain or actual insurer applicability is
claimed. PDF 5 describes Tier 1 for-profit IFRS correspondence and different
Tier 2/nonprofit boundaries; that statement is not full local compliance.

The nineteen selected pages are 1, 5, 6, 10–14, 18, 21, 29–30, 38, 43–44 and
49–52. This covers all of paragraph 45 and B101–B118, including B117A, and
the material dependencies used below. It is not a full 96-page standard audit.
Broader recognition, component separation, significant-insurance-risk tests,
full discount/RA methods, transition and numerical valuation are not newly
established by this reading. Original general/PAA work remains separate.

The manuscript's current 20-times-10% equals 2 illustration is correct for the
explicitly conditional share component. Treating that number as the complete
guaranteed-contract residual or CSM change would be wrong relative to those
stronger targets. The finite derivation preserves that distinction: it adds
the separate floor-payment term and does not withdraw the valid multiplication.

The expected failure mode is a convenient linear fee becoming a full exposure
or accounting model without its guarantee, future-service, grouping or loss
conditions. The repair is an explanatory example and explicit state needs,
not a different default numerical model. Actual terms, inception expectations,
probabilities, market inputs, guarantee valuation, RA, service units, loss
component, mitigation designation and applicable edition remain required facts.
