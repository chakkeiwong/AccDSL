For G13, the active 192-report panel still has **56 selected-source filing dates
and 136 unverified publication dates**. This count was checked against
[coverage](../../../data/financial-report-benchmark/coverage.json) and
[selected-source evidence](../../../data/financial-report-benchmark/source-evidence.json).
Dates in [alternate discovery routes](../../../data/financial-report-benchmark/resolved-sources.json)
are leads: matching the document, version and source is required before using
them to repair selected-source availability. Even an evidenced filing date does
not by itself establish the historical availability of the exact retained bytes.
That source-preparation backlog belongs in the current plan before historical
forecast evaluation can rely on the affected reports.

Missing company facts may remain unidentifiable from public disclosures. The
internal deliverable is then a source-supported account of what is unknown,
the admissible alternatives and their consequences. A missing fact cannot be
replaced by an invented value, nor can its absence justify skipping feasible
source work. The [internal-before-expert allocation](../../../docs/design/review/internal-before-expert.md)
keeps ordinary reader feedback, software review, accounting judgment and
actuarial validation distinct. Missing human acceptance does not stop internal
preparation or provisional manuscript repairs.
