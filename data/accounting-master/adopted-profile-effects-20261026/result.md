# Adopted profiles retain complete common effects and dependencies

26 October 2026. MC20/24/25/33/34; S2/S4, G2/G18 and I04. The common
reader now accepts the exact InsuranceComponentsIdentity/1 and
BankAssessmentExactTime/1 evidence through explicit checked providers.
PublicationEffects/2 carries the financial profile, identity policy when present,
per-kind schema identities, source packet hash and unambiguous source location.
The original composition grammar remains; its evaluator adds an explicit
execution-policy mismatch finding before comparing full worlds.

The final CPU-only run passes 4,817 deterministic checks. All 77 saved legacy
text/typed compositions remain exactly equal, including their complete source
maps and assessments. All 672 retained insurer descriptions equal the old
descriptions after only the declared pair-index, world-policy and provenance
changes. Fourteen new insurer exchanges and 168 unknown transport observations
also retain complete effects or explicitly unknown effects. The 63 exact-bank
packages supply 105 descriptions; all preserve accounting heads and journals,
full world changes and native metadata. All 959 saved descriptions recompute
through fresh readers. All 588 protected files remain unchanged.

Ninety-one new complete text/typed compositions pass: 41 native insurer
commit-to-report chains, 42 exact-bank publication/replay pairs, and eight
policy, duplicate-decision, unknown, refusal, partial, receipt, cross-profile
and changed-world cases. Unknown acknowledgement propagates through three
dependent stages. The same financial decision cannot be published twice by
calling its policy change a new namespace. An old-to-adopted insurer dependency
is explicitly unsupported without an execution-policy adapter; the separate
InsuranceAssessment state adapter remains absent.

Eight malformed-provider controls refuse dropped policy binding, hidden head
changes, duplicate replay postings, unbound exchange copies, unknown imports,
invented uniform bank schemas and a financial journal on a bank query. Bank
submissions deliberately retain the original schema while request/result/profile
records use their successor schema. Ten exact schema expressions are replaced
by explicit per-kind lookups; no financial descriptor logic changes. The
composition source delta is one policy-comparison branch. Both deltas are
reconstructed and checked against the frozen source.

The insurer packet contains repeated display labels for recovery examples.
Imports use source hash plus ordinal-qualified identifiers and retain each label
as explanatory metadata. No map silently overwrites a retained example. The
bank provider loads one current native auditor to bound memory. Separate loading
of the historic auditors prevents their differently defined financial_checks
and source_policy modules from being confused.

policy reached the native exchange checker, which correctly refused its claimed
replay but returned wrong_rejection. The provider now validates policy binding
before interpreting response claims. The second failure was a test harness
exception-class mismatch: the new schema reader's refusal was not caught by the
old reader class. The harness now catches that exact class. Neither issue
changed a financial method or retained artifact.

applies. All 53 obligations, six interactions and 46 operations remain; S1–S5
