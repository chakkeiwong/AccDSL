implementation evidence. It does not update the original fixture's rule basis.

The source record therefore supports retaining historical portions and keeping
legal assessment separate. It does not yet support enabling a post-close
accounting method. In particular, a rule for recognised allowance reversal
cannot be applied solely because both transactions are informally called a
recovery. This is a source-applicability gap, not evidence that a later receipt
is impossible.
