# Proposed recovery evidence intake, before accounting admission

The next safe extension is a read-only intake linking a proposed later receipt
to a closed native credit history. It preserves evidence and reports what is
missing; it does not reopen the native financial state or create a journal.
An actual recovery would first require facts absent from all nine fixtures.

| Field group | Required information | Unknown handling and consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Historical binding | Native case, request/result identities, complete nominal record, closure date, entity/book/currency | Changed source or context invalidates the link |
| Receipt evidence | Distinct receipt ID, transaction/value date, information date, gross amount and currency, document/span reference, source availability, evidence status | Missing receipt remains unresolved; an old retained collection is not a new receipt |
| Attribution | Historical event/portion and due date; separately identified uncollected or written-off components; allocation amount and rationale | Multiple plausible allocations remain separate or unresolved; preserve the original component rather than deleting it |
| Legal assessment | Assessor/source/jurisdiction, date, scope and whether a legal conclusion has actually been reached | Unknown remains not_assessed; no Boolean survival or forgiveness default |
| Accounting proposal | Historical basis/version, treatment for post-derecognition receipt, recognition date, gross/allowance treatment, income presentation and authority | An unsupported proposal stays unadmitted; no restoration or impairment-reversal default |
| Cash-flow proposal | Entity activity, components of receipt, classification basis and gross presentation | The previous synthetic operating/investing labels are not authority |
| Preservation | Original write-off record, any later linked receipt, cumulative allocations and component-level availability | A later receipt must not rewrite the original no-recovery finding or erase original nominal amounts |

There are at least two distinct modeling choices to investigate before posting:
(a) a cash receipt with separately supported recovery recognition, leaving the
old loan measurement closed; (b) recognition of a newly assessed financial
asset or revised interest in circumstances supported by additional facts.
Neither is selected by the retained data. A blanket reversal of the old
write-off would conflate the original measurement with the new event.

For internal preparation, the proposed answer is to implement only historical
binding and unresolved receipt intake first. Verify duplicate-source rejection,
unknown preservation and absence of financial effects using existing histories.
Do not fill missing receipt dates or amounts with illustrative positives. The
later accounting proposal requires a primary source or a derivation from the
applicable definitions plus the specific facts. All feasible source work and
checked consequences should be assembled before a professional question; no
outreach or fees are authorised.
