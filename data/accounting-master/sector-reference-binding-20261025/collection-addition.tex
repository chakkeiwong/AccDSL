\paragraph{Resolve a sector assessment without turning it into a common ledger.}
The common company world names a legal book and a component handle, but that
name alone does not tell us what the handle contains. A bank assessment can
carry account balances and an insurer assessment can carry only a head pointing
to a separately recorded balance digest. Treating both as one generic state
would lose the distinction that later accounting and reporting methods need.

The boundary therefore resolves one selected native case through its own
content-addressed registry. The request names the component and the exact case;
the resolver checks the native packet, schema, record identity, dependency
closure, source blobs, owner, book, currency, state, source and information
view. A changed record body, missing dependency, wrong schema, altered blob or
different book refuses before any method is called. The resulting world retains
the unrelated books and every unresolved obligation. Reference resolution is a
copy-and-identity operation, not a calculation.

The retained bank case illustrates why the context travels with the state. It
uses a synthetic US legal book, USD millions, a supplied US GAAP/CECL basis and
a solo perimeter. Its selected accounting state contains balances, while its
assessment profile declares \code{accounting\_effect=none}. The retained insurer
case uses a synthetic legal book, USD illustrative millions and a historical
EU IFRS~17 authority; its accounting head records a balances hash rather than
materialized balances, and its assessment profile also has no accounting
effect. These cases are not numerically compared. Their matching currency
labels do not establish matching units, reporting bases, dates, legal facts or
consolidation eligibility.

This distinction changes the implementation decision. A resolved assessment
can be cited by a later method through its native profile and view, but it
cannot post to the company book or authorize consolidation. The world remains
conditional when source, law, entity admission or method obligations are
unresolved. A later adapter must declare how a native state maps to the common
book, which accounts and memory it changes, and which evidence proves that
mapping. Until then, the exact native records are preserved beside the common
world rather than flattened into a convenient balance sheet.

The checked resolver covers one named bank case and one named insurer case.
It verifies 34 bank records and 11 insurer records in their selected closures,
including 21 and 6 source blobs respectively, and retains their byte hashes.
Those counts establish identity for the selected examples; they do not measure
sector coverage, accounting compliance, valuation validity, or a complete bank
or insurance engine. The native exchange validations remain the source of their
own conditional method results. The common boundary contributes a safe route
to those references and records the unresolved work required before posting.
