# Conditional acquisition worksheet /1

27 October2026. The reference is a separate, explicitly synthetic engineering
comparison. No values belong to the debt-conversion example. Its legal opening,
immediate pre-acquisition, immediate post-acquisition and closing trial balances
remain unchanged. Debit-positive signs are used: assets/expenses positive,
liabilities/equity/income negative. Every stage balances exactly.

The complete request binds a checked symbolic acquisition request, a synthetic
case declaration, legal book histories, one cash investment transaction,
an acquired-item population and supplied group-basis adjustments. Each supplied
amount is rational in the bound currency/unit, date or controlled interval.
Fixed accounts form a declared small test chart. Extra/missing accounts refuse;
this chart is a test scope, not the general DSL schema.

The subsidiary's pre-control income is represented by its own retained-pre
balance at acquisition and closing. Post-control revenue and expenses remain
separate. A full-year income number cannot replace that partition. The parent's
own cash falls by the cash consideration and the investment rises by the same
amount. The investment amount binds to T in the symbolic descriptor; Q is supplied
under fair-value NCI or derived from the complete item population under the
proportional election. There is no prior equity/financial claim in this branch.

An acquisition worksheet starts with the parent's post-purchase legal book and
the subsidiary's acquisition book. Individually originated adjustments change
the acquired equipment basis, recognise a separately stipulated tax liability,
remove the subsidiary's acquired equity, eliminate the parent's investment,
and introduce conditional goodwill and NCI. Goodwill is permitted only when
the computed excess is positive under an explicit synthetic recognition premise;
negative/zero excess selects a separate review dependency, not a profit plug.

The closing worksheet begins from closing legal balances and repeats the
acquisition adjustments with their original identities. Additional expense
reduces the equipment uplift; an independently supplied tax-liability release
credits tax expense. Neither amount nor tax treatment is inferred from a tax
rate. The sum of the original and subsequent adjustments is reported account by
account. NCI acquisition and current profit are presented separately; no NCI
entry modifies subsidiary legal revenue or clips negative NCI.

The selected group report includes all post-control subsidiary revenue/expense,
zero parent operating profit in this small chart, exact owner/NCI allocation,
acquisition and closing assets/liabilities/equity, and opening-to-closing cash.
The group starts with parent cash only; acquisition cash is acquired cash less
cash consideration, and subsequent operating cash is the subsidiary's external
receipt (or payment in the loss case). The cash report retains both gross
acquisition components. No pre-control subsidiary cash is called an operating
group receipt. Legal snapshots and journal origins are returned without mutation.

Full expected accounts, individual adjustment entries, amounts and reports are
hand-authored before execution. Accounting balances alone cannot pass the test.
Source/control/measurement/report/posting admissions stay false. Missing item,
wrong pre-control partition, unexplained parent movement, negative-excess
shortcut, unmatched timing/currency/source, wrong investment or altered binding
refuses. Noncash consideration, actual debt conversion, prior interests, later
ownership changes, intragroup trade, FX, revisions and broad tax recognition are
outside this selected comparison and require their own methods/evidence.
