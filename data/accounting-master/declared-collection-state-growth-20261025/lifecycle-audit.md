# Identified sale-to-collection lifecycle audit

25 October 2026; S2–S4, MC02/05/11/13/15/17/24–28/34/35.

The typed successor passes ten transition tests. The next work unit is an
additive, bounded receivable lifecycle over that successor, frozen operating
monetary methods, existing requirement expressions, reports and fiscal carry.
It is intentionally distinct from the fixed-population collection wrapper.

Opening state contains an explicitly empty claim map, a declared collection
cursor and the original stock/complement memory. Existing opening receivables
are excluded in this increment; the prior fixed-population example covers that
separate case. Each sale declares claim ID, customer, due date, units and price
consideration. It creates a closed claim record with source event identity and
date, alongside the unchanged operating sale journal. No tax or impairment is
inferred. Due date must be on or after sale date; customer is an explicit label,
not admitted counterparty evidence. Receipt must name the same customer and
currency context. The sum of current claim balances must equal receivables
before and after every successful command.

Assessment is state-only and strictly after the due date. It records each
claim's snapshot and assessment date, including its own assessed flag. A new
claim after an earlier assessment remains visibly unassessed; no global flag
will falsely give it historical coverage. Windows are explicit, contiguous,
half-open, and assessed on their last included day. That day is then sealed
against subsequent sale or receipt. A snapshot does not establish continuous
coverage or valuation. Collections preserve prior assessment values.

Reporting and carry reuse frozen balance calculations. Carry preserves all
existing claim and clock leaves with exact policies derived from actual state,
not preallocated zero slots. Unsupported/unknown commands retain their exact
successful prefix and reports. Replayed results are compared structurally.
Existing command text syntax may import a new, explicitly versioned lifecycle
configuration; this is bounded command authoring, not full method authoring.

Independent case on both charts: start with cash 100, stock 20 and equity 120;
sell two units (cost 10) for 30; receive 10; assess on due day (overdue zero),
then after due day (overdue 20); carry 20 profit and the 20 receivable; receive
the remaining 20 next year. Annual cash/AR/stock are 110/20/10, assets/equity
140, profit 20 and OCF 10. Next-period cash/AR are 130/0 and OCF 20.
Test a second claim created after assessment and refusal cases independently.

Promotion requires exact journals and statements, preserved unknowns, refusal
atomicity, original-profile regressions, two charts and matching text/replay.
Counts, compilation and balanced journals are not broader accounting evidence.
The explicit local policies are synthetic engineering premises. Whole-company
source histories, sector integration, external domain acceptance and forecasting
remain open. No new approval or scientific default is involved.
