# Conditional acquisition worksheet /2: cash and newly issued parent equity

The /1 fixed-chart/item/time/rights premises remain. This explicit extension
replaces scalar consideration with typed nonnegative cash and newly issued
parent-equity components, each measured by stipulation at the acquisition date.
The combined amount binds to the parent's investment and acquisition excess.
The cash component reduces parent cash; the equity component increases parent
capital. Acquisition and closing group worksheets preserve both effects.

An equity issue is a noncash acquisition payment; it is not a cash inflow
followed by a cash payment. The cash report therefore contains cash paid,
cash acquired, their net acquisition amount, operating cash, closing cash and
separate noncash consideration disclosure. Parent equity reconciles its opening,
newly issued equity, owners' profit and closing independently of NCI.

The entire original symbolic result and input descriptors survive. The synthetic
value does not become an actual observation or a default valuation. Prior debt,
pre-existing relationships and actual debt conversion are outside the profile;
newly issued parent equity is not a synonym for a debt claim converted in the
acquiree. All admission/posting/report flags remain false.

The independent comparator fixes total consideration120 and compares cash-only,
equity-only and mixed50/70 cases under each NCI election. The original four /1
outputs must replay exactly after removing only the newly disclosed parent-
equity/noncash fields; original books and individual adjustment origins remain
unchanged. New full expected reports and parent balances are authored separately.
Negative components, wrong totals/timing/units or hidden cash effects refuse.
