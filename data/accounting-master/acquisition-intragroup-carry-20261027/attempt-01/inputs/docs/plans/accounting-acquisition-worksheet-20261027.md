## Question, baseline and skeptical audit

Can a single conditional cash acquisition be reconciled across original legal
accounts, acquired-item adjustments, group equity/NCI, post-control income and
cash, while retaining the complete origin of every change? Baselines are the
checked acquisition authority requirements, ConditionalAcquisitionExpressions/1,
and the unchanged GroupPeriodReport/1 stable/full-ownership fixture. The latter
remains a compatibility check; it is not a changing-perimeter algorithm.

Audit: the acquisition method needs a complete worksheet comparison, not only
an equation that balances. Use a separate explicitly synthetic test company,
not invented measurements for the financing case. All amounts and adjustments
in that test are stipulated premises, not empirical evidence. Independently
write the full expected accounts, adjustment journals, cash and equity reports
before implementing the reference. No solver-generated balancing plug, automatic
negative-excess gain or annual ownership prorating is permitted. These choices
make the plan suitable for a bounded engineering/design test only.

## Scope and assumptions

One ordinary business-acquisition premise; one exact mid-period gain; one
currency; cash consideration; no prior equity/debt relationship; no later
ownership change; ordinary present rights; positive excess; no FX or intragroup
trade. Those are synthetic test limits, not admissible defaults. Acquired items
include an asset-basis adjustment and a separately supplied deferred-tax amount,
with separately supplied later changes. The reference must not infer tax
recognition or a rate from an uninspected standard. Each adjustment identifies
its conditional rule premise. Partial fair-value/proportional NCI and full
ownership are separate cases. Missing measurement/adjustment facts refuse.

The test must include pre-control subsidiary revenue and a different
post-control result to expose accidental annual inclusion. Parent and subsidiary
legal histories remain independently balanced and unchanged. Acquisition and
closing consolidation worksheets use debit-positive account signs, separate
elimination origins and exact rational arithmetic. Parent investment elimination,
acquiree pre-control equity elimination, acquired basis changes, goodwill and NCI
are individually visible. The report preserves cash consideration and acquired
cash before forming the presentation amount.

## Evidence contract and research intent

Primary pass criterion: exact equality to independently authored full
account/adjustment/origin/cash/profit/equity expectations in every selected case.
Acquisition and closing identities are additional checks, not substitutes.
Promotion vetoes: pre-control profit leakage, omitted acquisition-basis expense,
ignored tax adjustment, clipped NCI deficit, unidentified item, stale input,
unpaired elimination, invented cash or any true admission flag. A candidate
failure requires repair. Corrupt protected evidence, wrong comparator or a
source interpretation outside the inspected boundary invalidates the affected
run; stop it, repair the plan and continue independent work. Counts/runtime are
explanatory. No performance ranking, standards compliance, actual company
valuation, general acquisition support or complete financial statements follows.
