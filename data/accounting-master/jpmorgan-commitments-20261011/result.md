# Commitments, allowances and future funding

Started 11 October and completed 12 October 2026. E01/E07;
MC01/02/03/08/10/15/16/21/28/30/33–35. Notes 13 and 28 of the retained
JPMorgan 2025 Form 10-K distinguish several quantities that a bank forecast
must preserve. The source ledger has 20 selected table rows, 16 exact spans

These are different reported quantities, not conflicting observations of one
quantity. The contractual-versus-allowance-population difference is 1,213,360
million, but the exclusion footnotes are rounded in billions/trillions. That
subtraction does not reconstruct an exact instrument-level exclusion ledger.
The 1,207 million carrying-value difference is likewise unallocated here;
it must not be labelled another allowance, draw or payment.

The tables support exact displayed-number bridges. The commitment allowance
rolls from 2,101 through provision of 2,969 and an other movement of 1 to
5,071. For standby letters of credit and other financial guarantees, a 175
allowance and a distinct 441 guarantee liability sum to the 616 carrying
value. The corresponding contractual amount is 29,919. No allowance rate,
draw probability or expected funding amount follows from dividing these figures.

Note 28 says maximum exposure depends on drawing/performance and subsequent
counterparty failure; management says contractual totals do not represent
expected future credit exposure or funding requirements. Cancellation remains
subject to contract or law. Note 13 excludes future draws on unconditionally
cancellable commitments from the reported allowance policy. That measurement
exclusion does not prove that customers cannot draw or that liquidity demand
is zero. Future draw, cancellation, settlement and funding decisions still
need explicit conditions and facts.

The Apple Card example makes the chronology substantive. The issuer reports
a forward purchase commitment on 30 December 2025, an announcement on
7 January 2026 and expected closing in approximately 24 months (printed
p. 47). Note 28 footnote (h) includes approximately $104 billion of expected
credit exposure at closing, including about $23 billion of estimated drawn
loans. Note 13 footnote (g) includes the latter estimate in its credit-card
commitment population, and both notes identify the $2.2 billion transaction
allowance. An estimate of acquired loans at a future closing must not be
recorded as loans already owned on 31 December 2025. These dated statements
are retained issuer evidence; actual closing and future balances remain unknown.

Scope also changes with presentation. Certain wholesale amounts are net of
risk participations in Note 28 but gross in Federal Reserve filings. The
parent's guarantees of subsidiary debt are explicitly excluded from that
table. The same paragraph describes JPMorgan Chase Financial Company LLC
as 100%-owned. This supplies a dated issuer ownership assertion, without
independent legal-register verification or a complete group guarantee inventory.

The reported credit-loss method uses five company scenarios, an eight-quarter
forecast period, one-year reversion and qualitative adjustments. Those are
source-specific modelling choices, not AccDSL defaults. Published central
macro assumptions do not reproduce the weights, conditional losses or
qualitative adjustments. The issuer's policy explanations do not replace
inspection of operative ASC requirements.
