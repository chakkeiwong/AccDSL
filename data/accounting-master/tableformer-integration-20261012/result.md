Cold-reader answers from this bounded slice, by the author:

1. TableFormer predicts table structure and cell boxes; separate PDF text
   supplies words/numbers. Geometry alone therefore cannot verify text.
2. Matching can remove duplicate columns, attach orphan text or create a cell;
   the pre-correction structure is needed to review the result.
3. The cited values are Table 4's cropped-PDF PubTabNet structure-with-content
   scores. They do not check whole financial filings or entity/period/unit and
   accounting assignments. Table 2 is explicitly kept distinct.
4. The later consumer uses an OTSL representation, and a shared score does not
   identify later model files with experimental checkpoints.
5. The proposed adapter retains source page/crop, text cells/locations, predicted
   cells, matching assignments and structural changes, with OCR identity when
   applicable. Those records support accounting review rather than accepting
   a well-formed table automatically.
