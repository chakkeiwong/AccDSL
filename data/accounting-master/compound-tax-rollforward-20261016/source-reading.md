IAS12.23 (local lines4775–4783) expressly requires the initial combined-proceeds
tax-base DTL to be charged to equity and subsequent changes to go through
profit or loss under paragraph58. Paragraphs58–61A (5438–5490) distinguish that
income effect from transactions recognized directly in equity. IAS12.8–11
(4463–4510) require the fiscal facts and the correct entity tax return; rate
alone does not supply a liability's tax base.

IAS32.28–32 (13838–13895) and AG31–32 (14795–14824) support separate components,
comparable-debt present value and original-terms maturity conversion without
gain/loss; original equity remains equity. Early conversion remains separately
unresolved. IAS33.32–38 (15302–15376) includes effective-interest discounts,
related taxes and other consequential earnings, and specifies issue/actual/
potential share timing. These technical paragraphs were inspected before
freezing expectations. They are historical accounting sources, not current-law
admission or a source for the illustrative jurisdiction's deductions.

For general proceeds/par P, opening debt L, coupon C and constant tax rate t
within this finite profile: E=P−L, I=P+C−L=E+C, initial DTL=tE; at maturity
before payment both carrying and fiscal amounts equalP+C. Current tax expense
changes by−tC and deferred expense by−tE. Hence the after-tax earnings cost is
I−tC−tE=(1−t)I. This identity is locally derived from the explicitly stated
fiscal schedule; it is not a rule for arbitrary jurisdictions or instruments.
