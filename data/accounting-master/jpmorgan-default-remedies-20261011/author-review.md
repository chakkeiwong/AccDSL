# Bounded source author review

11 October 2026. I read the 2025 default/waiver and modification text, compared
the four year-specific retained bodies, and inspected 2022 PDF indices 327–329,
2023 index 356 and 2025 indices 351–353 at 95 dpi. The 2024 body's beginning/end
page witnesses and exact normalized equality with the 2025 body are checked;
I do not claim a separate continuous rendered reading of the 2024 pages.

The rendered pages confirm the general-terms scope, the “may apply” and
summary-only qualifications, the continuation of the event list across page
breaks, the bold recovery warning, the separate trustee/holder-remedy
conditions, and all eight protected modification categories. The 2022 page 329
passes directly from the annual statement to modification; the 2023 page 356
contains the later conditional trustee-conflict paragraph between them.

The central interpretation is that an issuer insolvency event, guarantor
insolvency, a payment default, a declaration of acceleration and a contract
modification are different observations or actions. A model needs their
separate actors, timing, notices, voting populations and exceptions. The
summary cannot establish that any such action occurred or that its conditions
are exhaustive. Human, independent-reader and legal acceptance are pending.
