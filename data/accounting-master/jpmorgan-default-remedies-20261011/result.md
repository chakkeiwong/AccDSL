# JPMorgan default and remedy summaries

11 October 2026. The four retained 2022–2025 issuer-hosted Form 10-Ks separate
default triggers, acceleration, remedies and modification consent in their
“General Terms of the Series A Notes” summaries. The bounded source ledger
passes **175/0** identity, span, page-witness and clause-retention checks and
preserves 51 source spans. These checks bind documentary evidence; they do not
validate an operative legal implementation.

The section says its general terms **may apply** to the described securities,
including the Callable Notes, and that the original 2016 Indenture contains
additional information. The original Indenture and applicable supplements
remain uninspected after the recorded SEC Archives HTTP 403. Every finding below
is therefore a reported summary, not a conclusion about complete operative
terms, actual default, enforceability or the exact outstanding population.

These voting denominators must not become a generic “25% approval” or “majority
vote” rule. The exact source wording and incorporation references remain in the
ledger for later examination of the original contract.

The bounded default/modification bodies in 2023, 2024 and 2025 are identical
after whitespace normalization that preserves case and punctuation. The 2022
body differs only by lacking the later trustee-conflict paragraph. The later
paragraph says an actual or potential default may create a conflicting interest,
the trustee may have to resign, and the issuer would then appoint a successor.
Its “potential” default definition disregards notice or elapsed-time requirements.
This is a disclosure-text change; it is not evidence of an amendment to the
Indenture or of an actual conflict, resignation or default.
