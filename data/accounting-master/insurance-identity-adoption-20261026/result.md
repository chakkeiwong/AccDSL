Audit-driven repairs: the first complete run passed 1,765 checks. Review then
found that checking journal order against decision-map iteration order silently
treated a JSON object's order as chronology. The final converter compares exact
journal membership and multiplicity to retained decisions, while preserving the
original journal sequence unchanged. Reversed map/history inputs now migrate
without changing chronology; duplicated journal lines refuse. Full ambiguous and
conflicting histories and a corrupt financial proposal strengthened the final
run. No native financial method or frozen record changed.

The native protocol adaptation changes exactly two identity expressions; its
delta is saved and checked. The executor authors publications separately, while
financial verification uses the retained native financial auditor. Thus this
provides independent protocol construction and comparison to saved results, not
a newly independent implementation of accounting. Results are deterministic
conformance evidence; no stochastic ranking or default-policy claim is made.

Post-run red team: the strongest residual objection is incomplete retained
history. A converter cannot discover a decision whose index entry and all other
evidence were erased. Success remains conditional on supplied completeness; it
never grants fresh posting permission by an absent lookup alone. A changed
financial result, duplicate journal on retry, unsupported identity-policy
binding or missing preserved record would overturn the bounded result. S1–S5
remain partial, S6 held, E3 deferred. No external work or professional review ran.
