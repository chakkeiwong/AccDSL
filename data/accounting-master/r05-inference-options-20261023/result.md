# Prospective inference options result

This design unit compares five routes for the selected expected future CRPS
contrast. It finds no defensible default yet. The named companies are not an
independent sample, annual origins share refits and shocks, and the current
source panel cannot establish the amount of prospective temporal information.
The expected target must be defined under a future joint law Q, not obtained by
resampling the 192 reports or treating nine issuer rows as independent.

The finite-horizon report is valid only as a descriptive realised result. A
cohort-level HAC route is a conditional candidate if a later protocol supplies
stationarity/dependence assumptions and enough cohorts. A joint longitudinal
model can represent common shocks but makes validity model-conditional. A
martingale route is not adopted because no suitable conditional null and loss
control have been derived; clipping would change the estimand. A block bootstrap
is a sensitivity method only and cannot cure inadequate time length.

## Red team and stop rule

The strongest alternative is that a small, model-based longitudinal design could
be valid for a narrowly declared finite grid even when HAC is not. The conclusion
would be overturned by a checked target-specific dependence model with calibrated
coverage. The weakest evidence is the unknown future reporting regime and the
small number of strict pre-start annual origins. Continue design and source
preparation; stop before empirical ranking if the selected Q or a valid
uncertainty route cannot be stated without changing the target.
