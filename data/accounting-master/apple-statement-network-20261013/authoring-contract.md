# Reader and source contract

The reader is an accounting-literate software designer who has just followed
the synthetic Northstar case and Apple's source/debt example. The new section
must let that reader reconstruct why profit, equity and cash move differently,
and why equal reported totals do not identify all journals.

Sequence: profit and comprehensive income -> the complete equity movement ->
the net-asset movement -> the full indirect cash reconciliation -> cash
populations -> the unresolved transaction links. A compact before/after table
shows the balance-sheet connection; explicit arithmetic teaches the cash
bridge. Exhaustive source rows belong in the reproducibility record, with
source citations and the material premises in the manuscript itself.

Protected baseline: Version 2.59 and every file/hash in `baseline.json`.
Preserve all previous displays, citations, sources, qualifications and note
examples. New section belongs between the first Apple example and the detailed
revenue/note inquiries. The voice is provisional; human comprehension and
independent accounting acceptance remain pending.

Source pages PDF32–36/printed29–33 were inspected individually as full-page
renders. Column dates, parentheses, units, the debt-issuance dash and the
blank commitments row were read. Values are reported USD millions except
the explicitly typed per-share/share quantities. Source labels and locations
are retained, including the balance-sheet share annotation. Existing detailed
note evidence is reused by hash; it has not been independently rereviewed.

This is deterministic source and arithmetic work, not a stochastic comparison.
Source inconsistency or an invented cause would veto the affected statement
relationship. A displayed identity is an engineering check; no compliance,
unrounded-value, forecast or whole-package conclusion follows from it.
