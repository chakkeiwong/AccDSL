The p08-v4 official HTML is usable and supersedes the earlier p08-v3 access
failures for this particular source. Original response bytes and extracted
text remain untouched. The fresh built-in web search/open returned no usable
results; sandbox curl had DNS failure, trusted curl left an empty PDF. The
empty new file is a failed access artifact, never source evidence. No blanket
claim of current-law completeness follows from this retrieval attempt.

The amendment removes the former automatic first-subsequent-rate sentence
from IAS21.26. Its A15–16 allows that rate only if it meets19A's objective,
with time and inflation relevant. A17 permits another estimation technique;
it does not mandate one universal economic model. Indirect ability, direction,
purpose and insignificant amounts must be assessed separately. No numerical
significance threshold is invented here. Transition retains the separate
retained-earnings and accumulated-translation-difference destinations.

The manuscript's event identity/allocation/refusal rules are engineering
derivations supporting IFRS10's accounting target, not words prescribed by the
standard. Monetary-loss interval algebra is a local derivation: express the
loss within each interval at its endpoint, then convert it to closing
purchasing-power units. Exact cases compare it with independently restated
net assets. Synthetic dates, rates, tax bases and assessments are explicit.
Actual law, rates, indices, control, NCI and contract facts remain inputs.
This is a standards-method unit, not a new academic survey; all broader
literature and competitor obligations remain on the existing R04 ledger.
