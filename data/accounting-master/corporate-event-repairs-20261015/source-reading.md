# R03-F source and assumptions

Retained EU2023/1803 original consolidated adoption text, issued26September2023,
is reused by exact hash; no current-edition or cross-GAAP claim. Inspected local
text passages: IAS16.12–14/43–55; IFRS16.38–46; IAS19.32–41/99–112/120–126.
The source specifies conditional methods; exact illustrative numbers and
proportional homogeneous lease rights are local assumptions.

Audit: ordinary maintenance is not major inspection replacement; homogeneous
area reduction is not a general lease-modification allocation theorem; pre-event
actuarial change, past-service change, discharge and ceiling must not collapse
into one net profit. Current-service/net-interest reset is part of the method.
Actuarial measurement inputs and recoverable economic benefit are supplied
conditional facts. No pension valuation model or IFRIC14 completeness is claimed.

Illustrative methods use historical EU authority, explicit cost model, pretax
non-production expense, zero residual values, known cash and no hidden funding.
Failure modes are wrong allocation, duplicate components, wrong gain/OCI origin,
omitted payments and inappropriate benefit-plan classification. Earliest checks
independently derive component and gross-plan movements and statement balances.
These are exact illustrative checks, never performance or scientific evidence.

Reader objective: explain why replacing an inspection, surrendering lease rights
and discharging a pension obligation create different journal components. The
narrative stages the event, rule, inputs, calculation and reporting consequence;
human comprehension remains pending. The already-read narrative skill applies.
