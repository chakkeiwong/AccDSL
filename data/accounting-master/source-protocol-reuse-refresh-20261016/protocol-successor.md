For stochastic E3/E4/E5 work, hard validity screens precede any ranking.
Candidate comparisons need matched information, common target and tuning
budgets, and uncertainty appropriate to issuer/cohort/time dependence. Point
allowance or RA differences are descriptive unless a predeclared target says
otherwise. No outcome-dependent target/threshold selection is permitted.

Information prerequisites now include each contract/valuation revision's
publication and ingestion times, policy-election dates, tax-law effective and
availability dates, ownership transaction cutoff, original expected versus
actual insurance histories, and separate loan expected/contractual cash.
The192-report purposive panel and31 exposed annotations remain development
material;136 uncertain publication dates still cannot supply a blind as-of test.
New hypothetical schedules do not repair that historical-information gap.

Future budget ladder: B0 independent exact identities/oracles; B1 exposed-data
debugging and measured numerical cost; B2 separately reviewed target-specific
capacity/tuning/uncertainty plan; B3 sequestered confirmation where feasible.
The B1 measurements, eligible cohort population, dependence design and decision
cost determine later sample counts, numerical tolerances and promotion margins.
Those values stay unset until justified. An invalid target/oracle or missing
required diagnostic is a continuation veto; a valid poorly performing model
is a promotion veto and repair trigger. Failed methods do not halt unrelated
protocols. The output directory remains
data/accounting-validation/<protocol-id>/<frozen-run-id>/.

Decision: documentary target mapping is refreshed through3.36. Exact accounting
evidence supports the stated arithmetic, not prediction, causal identification,
actuarial calibration, solver superiority or human usability. No inherited
architecture, optimizer, seed count or scoring margin was promoted.
