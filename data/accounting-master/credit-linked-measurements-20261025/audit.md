# Pre-implementation audit after recovery

25 October 2026. The retained plan survives skeptical review for a read-only
finite reference. MC02/07/08/10-13/16-18/24-28/30/32-35; G2/G7/G15.
The baseline is the nine locked dated schedules, not independent interval
endpoints. No accounting-law claim or forecasting experiment is introduced.

Research intent: test whether one context-bound positive day factor preserves
exact dependence through accrual, revisions, write-off and cash collection.
The expected failure is lost dependence or a sign/origin error. Promotion needs
every required algebraic identity, independent authored target and numerical
containment check. A failed candidate triggers a recorded repair. Changed
protected sources or unrecoverable input identities veto dependent execution.
Test counts, elapsed time and page counts are explanatory only.
