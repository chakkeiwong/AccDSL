Amendment 94, dated 21 April 1997, adds EUR/978 to the code list in advance
of euro adoption on 1 January 1999. Code issuance and currency adoption are
different events. The notice also announces further amendments. Amendment
158, dated 28 March 2014, removes USS/998, the same-day US dollar funds

establish USD's start or end of validity.

These sources establish specific identities and historical events. They do
not supply a checked uninterrupted validity interval covering every required
fact period. All 873 dependent results therefore retain
partial_currency_history_unverified. That status means the required evidence
has not been established here; it is not a finding that USD or EUR was invalid.

issuer compliance remain separate questions.
