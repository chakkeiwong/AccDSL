not a forecasting candidate or the research direction. No candidate has been

explanation for any apparent cross-company consistency. Matching labels and
passing arithmetic do not resolve classification, rounding or reporting
transitions. A conflicting original statement, first-release disclosure or

prediction or claim that the protocol is ready.
