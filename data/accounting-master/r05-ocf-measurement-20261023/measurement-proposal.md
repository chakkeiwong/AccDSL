# What the operating-cash-flow forecast would predict

The proposed outcome is the signed net cash flow from operating activities
reported for the consolidated group over its next complete fiscal year. It is
the final operating subtotal in the group's cash-flow statement, with the
period, reporting basis, perimeter, currency, unit and disclosed precision
retained. This is a reported-statement prediction task. Differences between
accounting regimes remain part of the target; division by total assets does
not turn them into a common economic definition of operating cash flow.

The following FY2025 amounts identify candidate mappings in the retained
annual reports. They are source examples, not admitted historical forecasting
observations or first-release outcomes. The 39 exact LF-line passages and
original/text hashes are in `measurement-map.json`.

Apple's FY2025 interval is 29 September 2024 through 27 September 2025
under its stated 52/53-week rule. The other eight examples cover calendar
2025. The preceding fiscal-year protocol preserves the calendar anchors.
Future targets require dated calendar evidence and the rule selecting the
first complete interval strictly after the named cutoff. A fiscal-year label
alone cannot identify an interval, particularly across these nine companies.

Both forecast arms must predict the same reported target and use the same
as-of asset scale. A parent-only forecast cannot be scored against a group
outcome; an economic cash-flow forecast cannot be scored as reported OCF
without an explicit accounting bridge. Convert the outcome, forecasts and
assets to compatible monetary units before scoring. For example, treating
Midea's thousands as millions creates a factor-of-one-thousand error even if
the copied digits and score formula are correct. The present map does not
select or admit any total-assets amount.

“Group as reported” allows acquisitions, disposals and consolidation changes
during the target year to affect the realised amount. It does not define a
constant-composition series. The forecast must account for that possibility
under its declared information and economic assumptions. A material reporting
change triggers a dated target review; it is not permission to delete an
unfavourable observation or rename the target after seeing it. Revised and
constant-basis alternatives may be retained as separately specified analyses.

The issuer's first public release of this exact full-year quantity would be
the primary outcome version. Results announcements can precede the annual
report. Neither retained annual-report bytes nor the printed filing date
establish that first release. Rounded announcements, later corrections and
restated comparative columns need their own version identities and precision
rules. All nine mappings remain candidates and all 192 historical selected-byte
admissions remain false. The map supplies no independent gold labels.

The basis passages attribute US GAAP reporting to Apple and Caterpillar,
US GAAP with applicable regulatory guidelines to JPMorgan, specified
IFRS adoption regimes to Henkel, HSBC and Munich Re, and PRC enterprise
accounting standards/CSRC disclosure requirements to SAIC, Ping An and Midea.
These are inspected issuer statements, not independent compliance opinions.
Differences in operating classifications can affect interpretation across
companies even when each paired comparison is valid within its own target.

The three IFRS reporters also discuss IFRS 18 and related IAS 7 changes from
2027. Henkel discusses interest/dividend classifications and attributes its
own expected impact; HSBC says presentation effects are being evaluated;
Munich Re describes starting the indirect reconciliation from operating
result rather than net result. A changed starting line alone does not prove
a change in net OCF. The applicable authority, effective dates, elections,
classification changes and possible effect on training comparability need
inspection before future target admission. Keeping a 2025 mapping unchanged
through 2027 would leave this substantive question unanswered.
