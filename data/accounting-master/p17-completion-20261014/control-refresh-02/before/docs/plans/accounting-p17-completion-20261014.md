[remaining-phase plan](accounting-remaining-phases-20261014.md).
Addresses MC01–MC35 and G1–G18 through the units mapped below. Current state:

The output is an implementable proposal specification, not domain-engine code.

## Starting evidence and question

Use Version 2.72 and its
[53-row assessment](../../data/accounting-master/post-certainty-readiness-20261013/dispositions.md),
the [initial profile](../design/proposal/architecture/initial-profile-v1.md),

The question is whether each required operation can be reconstructed from its
facts, source authority, assumptions, state and explicit procedure, including

original acceptance requirement, not the number of populated method records.

## Shared deliverable and evidence contract

For each actual residual operation, supply its input/output contract, state
owner, temporal/jurisdictional applicability, exact source anchors, algorithm
and ordering, journal and non-journal changes, report consequences, numerical
capability, unknown/error behavior, and positive/adverse worked case. Separate
issuer facts, reported assertions, author assumptions and permitted treatments.
Record a proposed answer, strongest competing interpretation and the facts or
authority that would distinguish them. Reuse exact unchanged source judgments
with their original scope; inspect changed dependencies.

Keep one concise dossier per unit under
`data/accounting-master/p17-completion-20261014/<unit>/`, except P17-01, which
finishes the existing SQL inquiry directory. Each dossier needs a substantive
result with sources, checked calculations/expected exchanges where relevant,
remaining claims, and a P18 destination or reason for no edit. A small manifest
records the Git revision, actual commands/environment, input/output paths and
hashes, timing, and plan/result. Mark seeds or GPU status N/A when no numerical
framework or stochastic run is involved. Do not generate administration in
place of completing a method.

Pass means a reviewer can reconstruct the specified behavior and its limits.
Wrong source/version/applicability, an unproved transfer, a contradictory
state transition or an inconsistent calculation vetoes the affected claim and
triggers repair. It does not veto the entire direction. Source counts,
balanced journals and hash checks are explanatory/integrity evidence only.
No runtime compatibility, accounting compliance, calibrated forecast or method
superiority follows from these documentary cases.

## Ordering, defaults and stop rules

P17-01 is the immediate ready action. P17-02/03 and the existing-source parts
of P17-08–10 can follow independently. P17-04–07 use the common authority and
method boundary, but one inaccessible standard does not block other domains.
P18 integrates completed substantive repairs as they arrive; P17-11 assembles
the complete method set before final readiness review.

| Material choice | Provenance/status | Risk and early diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| Version 2.72 and current assessment | Checked baseline for this plan | Later/older sources could be mixed; verify exact paths and current claims |
| CorporateJointHistory/1, typed IR and bounded text | Reviewed proposal choices, not empirical winners | Domain extension may need missing state; trace bank/insurance/group exchanges before finalizing types |
| Apple, JPMorgan and Munich Re cases | Existing source-backed, scoped examples | Local coverage mistaken for whole-company knowledge; inspect material residuals and conditional assumptions |
| Dated standards and author paper versions | Claim-specific support | Wrong jurisdiction, date or theorem transfer; inspect applicability and hypotheses before calculations |
| Exact rational arithmetic | Baseline for finite examples | Discounting/actuarial methods may require approximations; declare numerical target and error behavior first |
| Installed document/source tools | Convenience, no GPU or training default | Environment mismatch; confirm tool/import/build only when needed |

Skeptical audit passes for document/source work with these limits. A complete
operation case is the primary criterion; counts, titles, matching totals and
attractive prose are not. A missing source or failed treatment is a local
promotion veto/repair trigger. Corrupted inputs, a conflicting writer or a
material permission/cost boundary is a continuation veto for dependent work.
The 6 October campaign deadline is historical; it is neither reset nor a stop
condition for this separately authorized direct work. Keep output bounded,
save full logs on disk, and avoid a new implementation/research campaign.
