## What the delivered case shows

the material listed in Item 8. Item 8 instead specifies pages 163–309, and
the inspected auditor-report heading starts at printed 163. The same two-page
physical offset applies to both. A faithful extraction can therefore preserve
an incorrect source direction. The supported correction is a new navigation
reading; it does not rewrite the source or decide accounting measurement.

The manuscript and fixture specify eight decisions: propose; accept literal
extraction; accept attribution; retain the competing source assertion; reject
the refuted target location; propose/accept the supported navigation; reject
an attempted unsupported alternative; and supersede a counterfactual earlier
mistaken acceptance. The current general review section in
`chapters/09-implementation.tex` separately defines defensible accepted
alternatives under a choice group. This case correctly rejects its refuted
alternative rather than inventing two admissible locations.
