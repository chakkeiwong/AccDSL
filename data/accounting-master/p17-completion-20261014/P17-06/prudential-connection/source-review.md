All72 extracted §3.32 text items and four tables were inspected, including
sovereign, foreign-bank, PSE, mortgage, HVCRE, past-due and other-asset
alternatives. Table content is retained separately because the first plain
paragraph extraction omitted its cells. The source XML always retained them.
Only the directly relevant central-bank/corporate clauses support this case;
inspection of other categories does not implement them. Section3.22(g) supplies
the deducted-asset exclusion that§3.31 alone did not state.

The official index linked December2024 files; no2025 revision was established
by that route. The publication dates and internal revision footers are retained
as such. These instructions are used only for the compatible ordering shown
by the historical legal clauses. A distinct incompatible amendment would
require re-admission before actual reporting. Source breadth, HTTP200 and
matching arithmetic do not settle that independent legal applicability.
