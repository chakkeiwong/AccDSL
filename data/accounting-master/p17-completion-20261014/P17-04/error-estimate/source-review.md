| 108.5 | Prior error requires reliable information available at authorization and reasonably expected to have been obtained and considered. A later different estimate alone cannot prove this. Materiality and practicability are separate findings. |
| 108.32–34A | New information or developments can change an estimate; a changed input or technique is an estimate change unless it corrects an error. |
| 108.35 | Measurement-basis change is a policy change. Difficulty distinguishing policy from estimate is not permission to label an uncertain error an estimate. |
