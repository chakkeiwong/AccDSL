| Inspected clauses | Substantive rule and present disposition |
|---|---|
| 1–8,4A | Scope includes specified public-market entities and voluntary EPS disclosure; keep consolidated and separate reporting populations distinct. One synthetic consolidated parent/class; no inferred issuer admission. |
| 9–18; A1 | Attribute consolidated profit after NCI, then equity preference effects. Current cumulative entitlement is deducted even undeclared; prior-period arrears are excluded from that deduction. Liability-classified dividends are already expenses. Preference repurchase/inducement and increasing-rate terms remain separate methods. |
| 19–24,26–29 | Outstanding shares weighted by days; contractual substance determines start. Cash consideration receivable, not arbitrary settlement lag, is the usual cash-issue trigger. Basic includes actual debt conversion when interest ceases. Split quantities adjust retrospectively; resource-changing repurchase weights prospectively. Mandatory conversion, contingent return and business combinations require their own dates/terms. |
| 30–40 | Add after-tax dividends/interest and all consequential earnings changes for dilutive instruments; use complete effective-interest expense, including relevant costs/discounts. Weight potential shares from period start or later issue to lapse/conversion. After conversion, actual shares enter both basic and diluted. YTD is calculated independently, not averaged from interim diluted EPS. |
| 41–44; A3 | Continuing-operations earnings after attribution/preferences control inclusion. Assess separate series in ascending earnings per incremental share. An individually dilutive instrument may be excluded after a more dilutive one enters. The admitted population also serves total/discontinued EPS even if it reduces the absolute total loss. Equality is not dilution under41, not a strict increase under43. |
| 45–48; A4–A9 | Ordinary cash options use average-market proceeds and incremental no-consideration shares; prices must be comparable after the split. In-the-money is necessary but does not override loss antidilution. IFRS2 future goods/services value enters assumed issue price under47A; time vesting and performance conditions differ under48. Complex tender/redemption arrangements inA6–A9 are outside the simple option case. |
| 49–51 | Convertible numerator and share effects follow33/36; preference redemption/inducement portions need separate treatment. Separate debt/preference series in the example, with full specified after-tax consequences. |
| 52–57 | Contingent basic versus diluted inclusion uses different conditions and timing. Read, retained as unsupported profile here; do not route a performance award through the simple option case. |
| 58–63; A10 | Issuer settlement option presumes shares if dilutive; holder option requires the more dilutive settlement. Purchased own-share options excluded; written puts have a distinct assumed funding calculation. Read, retained as unsupported profiles; unknown settlement terms are unresolved. |
| 64–65 | Split/bonus/reverse-split changes before authorization retrospectively adjust periods presented. Later ordinary cash issuance or actual conversion is not that adjustment. Prior diluted EPS is not restated for new price assumptions or actual conversion. |
| 66–73A | Equal prominence for basic/diluted continuing/total EPS, negative amounts included; discontinued EPS and numerator/denominator reconciliation; excluded instruments and significant subsequent transactions disclosed. Extra measures require stated basis and reconciliation. Proposed assessment carries these quantities, not a compliance certificate. |
| 74–76 | Effective-date/amendment and supersession text inspected; does not establish the later amendment chain. |
| A2,A11–A16 | Rights bonus factors, subsidiary/associate instruments, participating/two-class rights and partly paid shares read. These are explicitly outside the one-class profile; no blanket split rule for resource-changing rights issues. |
