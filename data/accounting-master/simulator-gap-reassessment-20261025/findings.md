The old recommendation to build a general book boundary has been acted on.
Repeating it would be stale. Adding more text around the same invoice would
also leave the reporting contract unchanged. A useful ready next repair is
an explicit, trace-bound indirect cash-flow explanation for the declared book.
It should compose existing operating and financing events on both charts,
preserve gross movement evidence, expose unsupported classifications and keep
accounting-policy admission separate from exact algebraic reconciliation.

This is not permission to treat a balancing adjustment as an explanation.
The current four-account bridge correctly labels its residual explanatory;
that residual must not become an automatic adjustment. A new contract needs
explicit movement classifications, exact per-event checks and independent
arithmetic cases. It should expose a missing classification even when omitted
gross postings net to zero. Native fixed-account evidence is a comparator,
not a default for all charts or sectors.

Real-source reconstruction is mission-critical but cannot be manufactured from
aggregate disclosures. It stays on the parallel internal queue. Wider group and
sector methods need their own inspected accounting/source contracts. The chosen
reporting increment is ready with existing typed event evidence and does not
depend on a new law, valuation assumption, forecast or external engagement.
