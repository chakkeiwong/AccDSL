# Scoped author review of temporal feasibility

The plan's principal risk was counting annual reports as usable pre-start
labels or independent forecast replications. The derivation uses half-open
intervals and the strict cutoff rule. For a target y, the same-company adjacent
year y-1 is unfinished; potential training ends at y-2. Inner-origin fits
must each have r earlier labels, and their outcomes must be complete by the
outer cutoff. The resulting thresholds are 2018+r+1 without inner tuning and
2018+r+v+2 for v>=1. Exact examples check both, including the no-opportunity
boundary. This remains conditional on adjacent ordinary annual calendars.

The six inventory scenarios are bounds with deliberately optimistic source
availability. They make no admissions and select no r or v. The original
72 annual source identities and all 192 false as-of rows remain protected.
Pooled/external training, delayed outcome releases and reporting changes have
explicit dispositions rather than being hidden assumptions.

The proposed start-year cohort convention is provisional and preserves Apple's
offset. Two further exact examples show its endpoint cost; no common cohort
exists when only label 2025 is available for every company. The timestamp
helper requires aware instants and conservative bounds. Its examples do not
authenticate a real source or forecast. Sixteen positive case names and six
refusal names are computed from execution, not independently asserted totals.

The design continues the user-selected zero-boundary predictive-accuracy
question. It supplies no probability law, confidence level, sample-size
requirement or valid uncertainty procedure. The inspected 2003 source remains
a dependency-limited source rather than a transplanted inference default.
The next dated IFRS reporting-transition inquiry is a substantive source
repair; lack of a ready empirical campaign is not a global stop condition.

Completion must verify inherited bytes and exact live control markers before
mutation. New before/after snapshots and a separate R08 supplement preserve
53 obligations, six interactions and 46 operations, with zero whole-row
closures. This is author review only. No manuscript, runtime or accepted
