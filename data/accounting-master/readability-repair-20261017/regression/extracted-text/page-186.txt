Employee awards require another input even when their other terms fit the option calculation.
Under paragraph 47A, assumed proceeds include the IFRS 2 value of future goods or services. If
that supplied value were 400 across these 400 options, the assumed price would be 2+400/400 = 3,
and the increment at market price 5 would be 160 shares. This conditional arithmetic neither
values the services nor calculates award expense. Performance-based awards follow the contingent-
share provisions instead; share-or-cash alternatives, written puts, rights issues and participating
or partly paid shares also need their own methods (European Commission, 2023a, IAS 33,
paragraphs 47A–63, A2 and A13–A16).
An EPS assessment therefore references a dated book, reporting population, share class,
attributed earnings, share history, prices and complete instrument terms. It produces report
amounts and their reconciliation, without changing the book. Missing average prices, future-
service values, conversion consequences or settlement terms leave diluted EPS unresolved even
when basic EPS is available. Missing attribution or ordinary-share timing can prevent both.
Known complex terms select the relevant further method; they are not grounds for silently
dropping the instrument. These distinctions let the following issuer disclosures supply facts
without being mistaken for a complete method or a complete history.
10.2.7
Carry the conversion through the whole company
The EPS calculation must use earnings and shares from the same company history. Return to
the parent and subsidiary in Section 2.7. Their unchanged annual operations produce group
profit 102, of which 7.35 belongs to non-controlling interests and 94.65 to the parent’s ordinary
shareholders. Group cash is 644 and equity 724. The opening matured convertible was already
redeemed in January; the new instrument below is a separate July issue. Supply a register
of ten parent ordinary shares outstanding from the start of the year, with no treasury shares,
splits, preference claims or discontinued operations. Capital 600 is a monetary balance and
does not establish that share count.
On 1 July the parent receives 100 for a new instrument that matures on 1 October. Its original
terms require a coupon of 4, followed by either principal repayment of 92 or conversion of that
principal into six ordinary shares. The fixed conversion feature qualifies as equity in the parent’s
functional currency; no fees, participation or other consequential earnings effects are assumed.
At the supplied comparable 92-day debt rate of 1/23,

Issue debits cash 100 and credits liability 92 and conversion equity 8. Effective interest of
4 raises the liability to 96. Paying the coupon returns it to 92. At maturity, original-terms
conversion transfers that 92 to ordinary equity and issues six shares without cash; redemption
pays 92 instead. The original equity 8 remains equity in either world (European Commission,
2023a, IAS 32, paragraphs 28–32 and AG32). This October-maturity contract differs from the
preceding January-maturity early-conversion illustration.
