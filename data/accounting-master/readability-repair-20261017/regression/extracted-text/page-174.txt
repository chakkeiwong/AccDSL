Reading an actual lease transition
Ping An’s 2019 interim report shows what happens when the dated lease rules meet an actual
disclosure. The group reports adoption of revised ASBE 21 on 1 January 2019 and leaves its
2018 comparative financial statements unadjusted. Its stated listings in Shanghai and Hong
Kong place it within the enterprise class covered by the 2018 notice’s 2019 application date (Ping
An, 2019, pp. 112–113); (Ministry of Finance of the People’s Republic of China, 2018, notice,
para. I). This supplies an actual adoption fact for the representation. The later deferred-tax
treatment described above still requires its own applicable date and tax facts.
The immediate reconstruction problem is to keep three observations apart: the original December
presentation, the January adoption balances and the June closing balances. A right-of-use
asset records the group’s recognised right to use leased property; the lease liability measures
the associated recognised payment obligation. Table 9.1 retains the amounts and their dates.
All amounts in this example are displayed millions of renminbi; arithmetic on them does not
establish exact underlying amounts or the issuer’s rounding rule.

That difference alone
does not identify an increase in retained earnings. Article 61(2) permits specified alternative
measurements of the right-of-use asset for former operating leases, including a liability-based
amount adjusted for prepaid rent; its cumulative approach adjusts retained earnings and other
relevant financial-statement items (Ministry of Finance of the People’s Republic of China, 2018,
attachment, art. 61(2)). To see why the other items matter, suppose solely for illustration that
pre-existing prepaid rent of 833 is reclassified. Debiting the new right-of-use asset by 18,500 and
crediting the new liability by 17,667 and prepaid rent by 833 balances the entry with no equity
movement. Assigning the same residual entirely to retained earnings would instead require a
different assumption about all other transition adjustments. The report does not identify the
illustrative prepayment or supply a complete opening journal. Reconstruction therefore retains
the unexplained components rather than creating an equity plug.
The policy note nevertheless narrows the permitted interpretation. It says the opening liability
discounts remaining payments at the lessee’s incremental borrowing rate on 1 January 2019. It
names five simplifications: treating operating leases with a remaining term shorter than twelve
months as short-term leases; excluding initial direct costs at transition; using one discount rate
for leases with similar characteristics; using actual option exercise and other recent information
to determine the lease term; and assessing pre-existing onerous contracts instead of a separate
right-of-use impairment test. These correspond to the specified provisions of Article 63, with
the rate and direct-cost choices appearing in the same numbered provision (Ping An, 2019, p.
113); (Ministry of Finance of the People’s Republic of China, 2018, attachment, art. 63). The
