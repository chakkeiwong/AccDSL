Constant FX and unchanged ownership remove one mechanism at a time: they test whether the
calculation changes for the right reason. For estimated loan or insurance cash, E4 instead tests
the stated conditional prediction against a credible tuned baseline. For numerical valuation,
E5 checks the declared measure and error in the downstream quantity. A correct group bridge
cannot establish the quality of a credit forecast or insurance risk adjustment used as its input.
The same separation matters elsewhere. A DTA may be arithmetically correct for supplied
recoverable losses while its recovery forecast is unsupported. A POCI schedule may conserve
cash while using a forecast made after the reporting date. A retrospective insurance method
may follow its formula while using hindsight that the route does not permit. Each comparison
must therefore bind the applicable law or policy, contractual and expected cash, historical
information vintage, valuation assumptions and ownership interval before interpreting its result.
Some checks are exact within the stated domain: a hypothetical hedge option must not generate
actual cash; a component’s notional funding must not become new legal capital; and an NCI
purchase after a close must not alter attribution before that close. Other quantities, including
predictive losses, tail estimates and Monte Carlo accuracy, require their own uncertainty design.
An error in the target or reference invalidates the dependent comparison. A valid but poorly
performing forecast motivates the specified model repair. These outcomes should lead to
different next steps.
The original E1–E6 protocols consequently remain distinct after composition. The newer
examples add contract-loss order, dated credit receipts and write-offs, dependent EPS, actual
versus aligned option values, insurance transition information and shared-sector attribution to
their test populations. They supply tractable checks before a more expensive run. Eligible data,
paired comparison budgets, dependence assumptions and decision-relevant margins must still
be fixed for each actual empirical target.
