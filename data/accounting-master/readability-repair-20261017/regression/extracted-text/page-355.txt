For an admitted probability law with finite first moment, use the negatively oriented continuous
ranked probability score (smaller is better). If X and X’ are independent draws from the
predictive law and y is the realized amount,
L(F, y) = E|X −y| −1
2E|X −X′|.
Gneiting and Raftery (2007, sec. 4.2, eqs. 20–21) supply this representation and its conditions;
the paper uses the opposite orientation before explicitly introducing this loss orientation. A
point forecast reduces to absolute error. For probabilities one half on 5 and 7 and outcome
5, the first expectation is 1 and the second is 1, giving loss 0.5. A point forecast 6 has loss 1
on this observation. This one outcome is a calculation check, not evidence of superiority. For
outcome 6 their losses reverse to 0.5 and 0.
For scale comparisons, divide all amounts for an issuer-origin by the same strictly positive, as-of
known total-assets amount, fixed before the outcome. Missing or nonpositive scale prevents that
scaled comparison; it is never replaced by a test-outcome-dependent scale. Report unscaled
amounts as well. The joint secondary loss replaces absolute differences by Euclidean norms
after fixed component scaling: the energy score with exponent 1 (section 4.3, equation 22). It
requires a joint law and finite first moments; the vector of independently optimized marginal
extrema is not a joint forecast. The source’s kernel argument in section 5.1 supports propriety
under its stated hypotheses; it supplies no assurance that this finite corporate panel can detect
every dependence error. Prespecified cash shortfall and linked-statement event checks therefore
accompany it.
For unweighted admissible sets, report inclusion of the realized quantity and width only under
a declared observation/scenario interpretation. An outcome outside an assumed scenario does
not refute conditional arithmetic; an always unbounded set is not useful just because it contains
observations. Compare equal-scope sets and their decision implications without assigning 95%
labels or manufacturing equal branch probabilities. Robustness across a prior class remains a
range of scores or decisions, not one averaged forecast.
Fix the comparison population and weighting before scoring. A candidate missing eligible
predictions cannot win on an easier surviving subset. Report paired scores on the prespecified
common coverage and the full eligibility/failure denominator; a claim about a complete service
requires a declared fallback with its own results. Never renormalize away unresolved probability
mass. A probability forecast with unresolved mass is incomplete for the above proper-score
target.
The primary effect is the paired mean loss difference, proposed minus the preselected strongest
admissible comparator, with issuer and time dependence accounted for. Promotion requires a
predeclared uncertainty interval to lie below minus a decision-justified improvement margin,
while all validity vetoes pass. A zero margin can test statistical improvement but cannot
establish material business value. The interval level, dependence model, time blocks, multiple-
comparison rule and margin are locked using development evidence before test predictions. Nine
purposively selected issuers with eight years and overlapping horizons do not automatically
support a bootstrap approximation. Without a defensible uncertainty design, report differences
descriptively and retain all screened viable candidates without ranking.
