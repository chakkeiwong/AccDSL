The group record therefore preserves each gross exposure’s identity, reporting entity, currency,
risk, amount, recognition period and admitted valuation, plus the strategy, designation, in-
strument, effectiveness calculation and reserve history. A layer also needs an identified and
tracked overall pool and the conditions in 6.6.3. A group without offsetting risks apportions
the instrument’s net gain or loss systematically to the affected lines without grossing it up; a
fair-value hedge adjusts each relevant asset or liability separately (European Commission, 2023a,
IFRS 9.6.6.3–5, B6.6.11–16). A group of items is not permission to ignore consolidated-group
external-party requirements: retain 6.2.3 and 6.3.5–6’s distinct tests. These methods need
explicit successor records and complete exchanges; the existing closed financing profiles are
preserved until those additions are specified.
10.13
Conditions, settlement choices and the earnings that each
share receives
The following conditional methods use IFRS 2 and IAS 33 in the retained September 2023 EU
compilation. They are separate from the Apple contractual examples and do not determine their
US GAAP treatment. Employee counts, fair values, service patterns and participation rights
below are explicit synthetic inputs. Valuing the awards and admitting an entity’s applicable
rules remain distinct requirements.
10.13.1
A failed condition does not always reverse an expense
An employee’s failure to complete required service differs from a share-price target that is
not reached.
For employee equity awards, IFRS 2.10–21 (European Commission, 2023a,
IFRS 2.10–23) measures the services using grant-date fair value. Service and non-market
performance conditions affect the estimated number of instruments expected to vest; market
conditions enter fair value. Revise the former estimate as information changes and use the
actual eligible number at vesting. If a market condition fails but the other vesting conditions
are met, the service expense remains. Freezing the quantity at grant and subsequently marking
every equity award to the current share price would get both sides of this distinction wrong.
