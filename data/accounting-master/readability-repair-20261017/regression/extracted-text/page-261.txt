11.7
Full loan and allowance roll-forward
Assume a fixed-rate non-POCI amortised-cost loan held to collect its contractual cash flows,
originated for 100, with one terminal payment 121 two years later, original EIR 10%, no
fees, collateral, modifications, FX or taxes. Since no payment falls due before maturity, an
earlier default in this example is a separately established qualitative credit event, such as
financial distress under the admitted default definition; it is not a missed instalment. Article 52
of ASBE 22 requires a definition consistent with credit-risk management and consideration
of qualitative indicators (Ministry of Finance of the People’s Republic of China, 2017). At
origination the assumed lifetime scenarios are: default in the first twelve months with probability
0.1, default during the following year with probability 0.1, and no default with probability 0.8.
Either default gives terminal collection 60.5. The first twelve-month ECL includes lifetime loss
from the first scenario only: 0.1×(121−60.5)/1.12=5; initial lifetime ECL would be 10. Debit
loan 100/ credit cash 100; debit impairment 5/credit allowance 5. Gross 100, allowance 5, net
95.
At year-one accrual the loan has survived the first window and is not yet credit-impaired:
debit gross loan 10/credit interest 10. Survival alone would give remaining default probability
0.1/0.9=1/9. A subsequent, separately admitted credit forecast revises that conditional proba-
bility to 0.2; it is an authored forecast update, not a Bayes calculation from survival alone. Its
lifetime weights are 0.8/0.2, with a separately established SICR finding; ECL=0.2×60.5/1.1=11.
Debit impairment 6/ credit allowance 6. Gross 110, allowance 11, net 99. The ratio change alone
is not the defined SICR test. Only twelve months remain, so twelve-month and lifetime ECL
both equal 11 under this revised forecast. This amount cannot distinguish the two classifications.
Immediately afterward, a distinct, ordered credit-impairment event changes expected maturity
collections to 60.5. This is a forecast mean, not a guaranteed or maximum recovery. Required
allowance is 110−60.5/1.1=55; debit impairment 44/credit allowance 44. Net 55. ASBE 22
Article 59(5) measures this non-POCI credit-impaired asset by gross balance less discounted
expected receipts. Articles 38 and 39(2) then make amortised cost, after the allowance, the
basis for subsequent-period interest (Ministry of Finance of the People’s Republic of China,
2017).
