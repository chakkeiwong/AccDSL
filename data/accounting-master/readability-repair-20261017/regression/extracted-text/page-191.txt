Both histories start at 15,550,061,000
shares, repurchase 499,372,000, issue a net 66,097,000 and end at 15,116,786,000. Their first
91 days average 15,509,763,000 shares: the two equal early purchases reduce the average by
40,298,000(46 + 45)/91 = 40,298,000. For the full year, the signed time-weighted changes sum
to −75,085,192,000 share-days in each history. Dividing by 364 reduces the opening count by
206,278,000, giving the reported 15,343,783,000 average. An independent sum of daily balances
gives the same result.
After 100 elapsed days, however, the first history has 15,469,465,000 shares and the second
15,535,562,000. The earlier issuance makes the difference. The fourth-quarter purchase dates
lie inside their reported intervals, but none of these illustrative dates is an estimate of Apple’s
actual transactions. This establishes non-uniqueness for the stated quantity constraints. Prices,
award-level settlement and monetary journals impose further conditions; the two examples
are not a complete reconstruction of the filing or an exhaustive family, and no probability
attaches to either. A query for shares on an intermediate date must therefore retain the timing
alternatives or obtain additional evidence.
10.2.11
Awards, repurchases and cash measure different movements
The next quarter shows how another disclosure can narrow an unknown. Note 7 describes
about 100 million repurchased shares, while Item 2’s interval table gives 41,627, 32,784 and
25,379 thousand, totalling 99,790 thousand. The balance sheet reports 15,040,731 thousand
closing shares against 15,116,786 thousand at the preceding year end. At the displayed centres,
the quantity bridge requires a net other increase of 15,040,731 −15,116,786 + 99,790 = 23,735
thousand shares (Apple Inc., 2025c, printed pp. 3, 10 and 20). This narrows the total without
identifying the events composing it. Repurchase authority is different again: both the note
and programme footnote explicitly say that the programme does not oblige Apple to acquire a
minimum amount of shares.
Restricted stock units have their own movement record. In thousands of RSUs, the fiscal 2024
bridge is 180,247 + 80,456 −87,633 −9,744 = 163,326: opening awards plus grants, less vesting
and cancellations. The next quarter gives 163,326 + 61,391 −35,683 −2,278 = 186,756 (Apple
Inc., 2024a, Note 11, printed pp. 44–45) (Apple Inc., 2025c, Note 8, printed p. 10). These
gross award movements are different from net common shares issued or incremental shares in
the diluted EPS calculation. Apple reports that most vested RSUs were net share settled and
that approximately 31 million shares were withheld for employee taxes in 2024. Subtracting
that rounded amount from 87.633 million vested RSUs does not explain the 66.097 million net
issuance line in full.
