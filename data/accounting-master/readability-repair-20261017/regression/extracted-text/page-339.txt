A successful extraction can still lose the fact
Consider an Inline XBRL amount whose literal is 1,234, whose admitted numeric transform
treats the comma as grouping, whose scale is −3, and whose context and unit identify a particular
dollar observation. The resulting amount is 1234 × 10−3 = 1.234. Scaling that normalized value
again gives 0.001234 and is wrong. An adapter must retain the literal, transform, scale, context,
unit, nil status and original occurrence so that this error can be found.
The same principle applies to dates and dimensions. A lexical instant of 31 December remains
distinct from the exclusive 1 January boundary used for an interval. Publication and ingestion
are separate timestamps. Two equal numbers with different contexts remain separate occurrences.
A nil fact is unknown, not zero. Dropping a typed dimension or context reference yields a
preservation failure for that fact even if the resulting Markdown looks clear.
A PDF can preserve characters that the reader never sees. The retained HSBC 2023 annual
report has a cover reading “Annual Report and Accounts 2023”, but both PyMuPDF and
Poppler also return 2024 in the extracted cover text (HSBC Holdings plc, 2023, cover, physical
PDF p. 1). Inspection of the drawing order explains the discrepancy: the earlier 2024 text is
covered by an opaque dark rectangle, after which the 2023 text is painted. This is an observed
difference between extracted characters and the visible title. It does not establish an accounting
contradiction or explain why the file was constructed that way. Agreement between two text
extractors would not resolve it.
The proposed exchange keeps the original PDF, the unchanged raw extraction, the page locator
and a render from the same bytes. The annotation’s literal retains both years; separate
readings record the raw extraction and the proposed visible title. A linked inspection record
identifies the extractor versions and options, rendered image, relevant drawing order and
reviewer. Thus a raw-text search still finds 2024, while the inspected cover supports a visible
report-year reading of 2023. Rejecting 2024 as the visible report year must not delete its
extracted occurrence. The current example has author inspection; independent adjudication
remains pending.
