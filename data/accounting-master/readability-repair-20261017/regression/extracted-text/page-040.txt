A be those two customers, B the carrier group, and s(S) the share of total trade receivables
owed by customers in a set S. Each customer belongs wholly to a set or does not. Treat the
displayed percentages as exact centres for this illustration. Inclusion-exclusion gives
s(A ∪B) = s(A) + s(B) −s(A ∩B) = 29% + 33% −s(A ∩B).
If both large customers are carriers, their 29% is already inside the 33% carrier share, so the
union is 33%. If neither is a carrier, the union is 62%. For a concrete construction, allocate
receivables across eleven hypothetical customers with shares 18, 11, 9, 9, 8, 7, 4, 9, 9, 8, 8 percent.
These sum to 100 and only two reach 10%. Carriers can be the 18, 11 and 4 customers, totalling
33, or the first 9, 9, 8 and 7 customers, also totalling 33. The same marginal disclosures therefore
permit both endpoint unions. If only one large customer is a carrier, the union is 44% or 51%;
exact-centre membership does not permit an arbitrary continuously divisible overlap. These
are conditional percentage examples, not recovered customer identities or probabilities of loss.
The note’s trade denominator is not automatically the balance sheet’s net receivable line, so
multiplying it by that line would add an unsupported measurement assumption. Required
credit support or collateral applies to certain customers; it does not establish universal recovery.
Vendor non-trade receivables have a separate economic origin and denominator. Apple buys
components from suppliers and sells them to manufacturing vendors that make subassemblies
or final products for Apple. Those component sales are excluded from product net sales; any
gain reduces product cost of sales when Apple sells the related final products. The two largest
reported vendor shares are 44% and 23% at September 2024, and 43% and 24% at December,
each pair totalling 67% on its own dated vendor population (Apple Inc., 2024a, 2025c, annual and
interim Note 4). Equal combined shares do not establish unchanged counterparties, component
cohorts or collection risk. A funding forecast consequently needs settlements and restrictions on
assets alongside payment dates on obligations. Keeping reported assertions distinct from those
missing facts is the starting point for representing the company’s historical record.
2.14
Sales geography is not a map of costs or assets
The liquidity question leaves a related operating question. If demand weakens in one place,
which part of Apple’s sales and profit is exposed? Apple reports five geographic segments, but
a segment is a management reporting division, not a list of incorporated companies, factories or
product lines. Reading its two segment notes as though those were the same partitions would
make a regional scenario appear more precise than the filing permits.
2.14.1
What the regional labels measure
