The representation must therefore retain both entities’ functional currencies, the monetary
balance, the settlement assessment and the origin of each OCI component. If the settlement
facts are unknown, these conditional presentations remain separate even when they agree on
total equity. The group adjustment does not overwrite the parent’s separate journal, and the
OCI history remains available for the applicable later disposal treatment. An equal consolidated
total cannot establish that the histories answer the same profit, cash-flow or disposal question.

An elimination belongs to an identified transaction
Equal balances need not describe the same debt. Suppose the parent records a receivable of
100 from subsidiary S, while another subsidiary T reports a payable of 100. Cancelling those
numbers would balance the worksheet while misstating both relationships. The group must first
establish the seller, buyer and economic event that connect the two entries. IFRS 10.B86 requires
the elimination of intragroup transactions; it does not make equality of amounts evidence that
two entries are counterparts (European Commission, 2023a, IFRS 10.B86–88, B92–93).
For each proposed elimination, retain the reporting group and perimeter version, both legal
entities and book revisions, the common economic-event reference, invoice or settlement al-
locations, transaction currency, reporting cutoff and history branch. The two books’ local
event identifiers can differ; an evidenced mapping connects them to the common transaction.
For balance elimination, use the perimeter at the reporting date; for income and cash-flow
elimination, use the intervals during which control exists. A receivable created before control
began can be intragroup at the reporting date without making the original sale an intragroup
revenue transaction for that period. Acquisition measurement and subsequent movements
