Reported succession terms give another reason to keep the obligor separate from the debt
claim. Under the general Series A summary, a successor to the issuer in a covered merger,
consolidation or transfer of all or substantially all assets must be a U.S. corporation or limited
liability company that expressly assumes due payment and the issuer’s covenants. A successor
to the guarantor must instead be a U.S. corporation that assumes the full guarantee and the
guarantor’s covenants. The continuing issuer or guarantor is the other stated route. Each
party’s route also requires that no default or event that would become a default with notice or
time has occurred or would be continuing immediately after the transaction. The descriptions
deem certain resolution-related transfers of the guarantor’s material assets to meet the all-or-
substantially-all category; they do not define a numeric percentage (JPMorgan Chase & Co.,
2023, 2024, 2025, 2026).
The same summaries say a recapitalization, change of control or highly leveraged transaction
alone receives no additional holder protection from these merger provisions; a particular
supplement may provide a put or higher interest. The model therefore needs a dated successor,
assumed payment and covenant duties, separate issuer/guarantor conditions and any evidenced
series-specific protection. Succession itself does not prove repayment, accounting derecognition
or unrestricted access to the group’s cash. These are reported terms that may apply, pending
inspection of the original Indenture and applicable supplements.
11.1.6
Loan creation, commitments and expected credit losses
Loan origination can debit loans and credit a customer deposit in the bank’s book. Subsequent
transfer to another bank produces settlement flows. Treating all loan growth as an immediate
cash outflow would therefore encode a specific funding assumption, not a universal banking
identity. A system-wide model must also link counterparties and settlement assets; single-
company simulation records external counterparties without claiming to close the whole economy.
