APPENDIX A. PORTABLE OPERATION REFERENCE
357
The five supported operations are source preparation, historical querying, conditional scenario
projection, report construction and review proposal. Each uses the I1 request/result envelope
with a profile-bound signature and tagged arguments. Record dependencies and separate byte
references follow L1. The schema lock includes both schema bytes and the semantic specification.
The 31 exchanges include the combined partial answer, named-source statement readings,
original and later segment queries, unknown historical clocks, three conditional difference arms,
six settlement paths and company-specific review proposals.
The family record represents an unresolved question about actual issuer histories. It admits
neither the illustrative branches nor a probability law. Reported observations, reported dashes,
unknown values and conditional derived amounts have distinct tags. A conditional amount
references the assumptions under which it was calculated. The complete reported-token subquery
does not upgrade the connected result’s partial status.
Coverage partitions the requested claims. For a missing-date settlement request, eventual
conditional amounts are completed and period timing is unresolved. These regions are neither
probabilities nor proportions of all company histories. Future periods are the relative indices 1
and 2; an unknown period is null. An out-of-range period or allocation parameter is invalid.
An unsupported depreciation or probability request retains its unresolved obligations instead of
returning zero.
Decoding precedes operation-tag, signature, profile, issuer, view and parameter binding. Trun-
cated bytes, a mismatched tag, invalid parameters and a cross-company binding return rejected
input with no validated request, payload or coverage. Valid named-source results may only use
observations eligible in their own view. Linking a later view in a connected result does not
authorize an original-view query to read it.
Every exchange has null expected head, no commit receipt and unchanged before and after
worlds for accounting, review, work and import owners. These empty worlds are explicit
documentary examples of candidate/read-only effects; they do not establish persistence in a
deployed service. Independent checks derive journal balances, all annual difference rows and
all settlement-period effects, verify source spans and dependency closure, and preserve the
actual source-conflict limits. They establish the stated finite specification and arithmetic, not
actual-company history feasibility or accounting admission.
