14.6
Fixing the target before evaluating a method
The following protocols specify the comparisons needed before selecting a forecasting method.
The first question is what a successful prediction would mean. A correctly parsed filing, a
retained feasible history and a useful forecast are different results. Each receives its own target
and comparison.
Information available at the forecast date
The nine-issuer, 2018–2025 panel is a purposive development resource.
Its 192 accepted
reports do not form 192 independent observations. All existing issuer roles remain provisional:
subsequent source work on JPMorgan and insurers has also exposed proposed test families. The
31 pilot annotations are development answers. The 136 unverified publication dates remain
unresolved. Even a verified filing date is insufficient when it does not establish availability of
the exact retained version at the intended cutoff. No date is imputed from a filename.
An eligible information record identifies original bytes and full hash, issuer and consolidated
perimeter, report period, original/revised status, actual public availability evidence, clock
precision, timezone and ingestion time. All language versions, mirrors and amended reports
belong to the same issuer/version family. An as-of view excludes any document whose latest
possible availability exceeds the cutoff. If only a day is known, the origin is after that day in
the declared local clock; an intraday claim is unavailable. Later comparatives cannot repair
training data retrospectively without defining a different retrospective task.
Forecast origin and target period must be distinguished. A December year-end report first
available in March cannot support an entirely future prediction of the following January–
December total: part of that total has already accrued. Such a task is a combined nowcast and
forecast. The all-future annual task uses the first complete fiscal year whose start is strictly
after the origin. A shorter future interval is admissible only if its realized quantity can be
measured independently. Neither subtracting incompatible cumulative vintages nor silently
calling an annual period a twelve-month forecast repairs this issue.

The unit is a complete declared disclosure package, with a source inventory covering statements,
note spans, policies and incorporated references. Labels preserve raw tokens, entity/perimeter,
concept, dimensions, unit, scale, sign, precision, period, availability and admissible readings.
