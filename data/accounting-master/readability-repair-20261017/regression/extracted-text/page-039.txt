For the quarter, the cash measure is 15,967 + 3,492 −6,124 = 13,335 million (Apple Inc., 2025c,
cash-flow statement and Note 4). The relation defines a residual; it does not explain it. Cash
sale proceeds differ from the carrying amount removed on disposal, and changes in measurement
or classification may also enter the stock. Apple’s specific-identification policy for securities sold
requires the disposed lots to recover their cost (Apple Inc., 2024a, Note 1, printed p. 34). Neither
the positive 8,263 nor the negative 2,303 identifies a fair-value, currency or reclassification
journal.
The valuation columns help separate another tempting explanation. Across the annual dates,
total adjusted cost falls by 13,152 million and net unrealised gains and losses rise by 7,703,
producing a total fair-value decline of 5,449. Across the quarter, the corresponding changes are
−13,512 and −1,770, producing −15,282. These totals include cash and equivalents, whereas
M0 and M1 include only marketable securities. They also differ from the comprehensive-income
statement’s net-of-tax marketable-debt amounts: 6,054 million for the year and −1,427 for
the quarter, including reclassification adjustments (Apple Inc., 2024a, comprehensive-income
statement, printed p. 30) (Apple Inc., 2025c, comprehensive-income statement, printed p. 2).
Endpoint unrealised balances and period OCI differ in population, tax and timing; their
numerical differences cannot be assigned to a tax charge or realised gain without the missing
movements.
Even reported cash needs matching populations. Annual cash-flow cash, cash equivalents and
restricted cash opens at 30,737 million, exceeding opening balance-sheet cash and equivalents of
29,965 by 772. Both close at 29,943. Thus the cash-flow change is −794, while the balance-sheet
line changes by −22; the cause and location of the earlier 772 remain unallocated. The quarter’s
endpoint populations agree, giving the same 356 million increase (Apple Inc., 2024a, 2025c,
balance and cash-flow statements).
Valuation risk is likewise distinct from a payment. The notes describe Level 2 prices or models
with significant observable inputs and counterparties that generally have high credit ratings.
Annual Item 7A reports that a hypothetical 100-basis-point rate rise at all tenors would reduce
investment fair value, including associated derivatives, by 2,755 million at September 2024
and 3,089 million at September 2023 (Apple Inc., 2024a, printed p. 27). Those are dated
issuer scenarios, not cash outflows or loss probabilities; the issuer’s valuation model has not
been replicated. Interim Item 3’s no-material-change referral does not issue a new December
sensitivity.
2.13.3
Future receipts need customers and collection dates
Receivables may turn into cash later, but their movement does not identify collections. Annual
net trade receivables rise by 3,902 million, while the indirect cash-flow adjustment is −3,788.
Their sum leaves an unallocated 114 million difference. In the quarter, the balance change of
−3,771 and adjustment of +3,597 leave −174. Vendor non-trade changes match the opposite
indirect adjustments exactly: +1,356/ −1,356 annually and −3,166/ + 3,166 quarterly. Even
that match supplies neither gross sales nor total collections (Apple Inc., 2024a, 2025c, balance
and cash-flow statements). Sales, allowances, currency and noncash movements still need their
own evidence before receipts can be dated.
