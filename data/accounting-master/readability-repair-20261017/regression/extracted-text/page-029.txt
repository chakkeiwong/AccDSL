Those missing facts have different consequences for the financial statements. Principal and

The latter also depends on carrying amount, effective-interest accrual and the treatment of
discounts and hedges; Note 9 expressly includes applicable amortisation and hedging adjustments
in effective rates. Exchange rates, hedge designations and tax treatment can affect profit, other
comprehensive income, retained earnings and closing debt differently. The cash flow statement

answer thus carries the conditional euro schedule together with these unresolved dependencies. A

To demonstrate a source-to-forecast calculation, freeze the interim balance sheet and apply a
marginal financing stress for a following 13-week period: a 200-basis-point rate increase, no
principal change, no offsetting hedge beyond the explicitly selected exposure, immediate cash
settlement and no tax effect. An assumed effective floating exposure E = 14,575 million produces
0.02 × (13/52)E = 72.875 million additional interest. This exposure uses the annual notional
as a conditional input; it is not asserted to be the interim net sensitivity. Under that condition,
debit interest expense and credit cash by 72.875. The resulting marginal statements have cash
30,226.125, assets 344,012.125, liabilities 277,327, equity 66,685.125, profit and comprehensive
income of −72.875, and operating cash flow of −72.875 relative to the frozen baseline. The
balance-sheet difference equals the equity difference exactly.
If exposure is instead only restricted to 0 ≤E ≤14,575, the conditional added interest lies in
[0, 72.875] before monetary rounding. That restriction is a stress assumption and is not claimed
to bound every real Apple exposure. This real case verifies evidence, context, mappings and a
coherent marginal propagation. Northstar supplies the complete-company forecast; a complete
Apple forecast remains dependent on its unmodelled operations and undisclosed terms.
