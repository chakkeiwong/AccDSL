# R03-M source reading

15 October 2026. Source: retained official EU2023/1803 original PDF and extracted
text in docs/design/research/p08-v3-preparation/. Both are hash-bound in the case
record. This is historical paragraph support, not a claim about current issuer
applicability or subsequently adopted amendments.

Inspected IAS32.15–27 and AG32–35. Classification follows the contractual
substance; a fixed number of qualifying own equity instruments differs from
variable shares worth a fixed amount, cash alternatives and contingent cash
provisions. AG35 measures the inducement difference at the date terms are
amended. It does not make an announcement legally effective, and share delivery
does not by itself determine legal release. IFRS9.3.3.1–4 and B3.3.1–6 distinguish
discharge, cancellation, expiry, creditor/legal release and substantial change.
The specified cases supply binding acceptance, discharge and classified
consideration. Accounting for a genuinely unresolved legal condition is not
settled by assigning a probability or using the later share quotation.

Inspected IFRS2.B42–B44C. B43(a) retains original grant-date cost and adds the
modification-date increase, with the respective service periods. B43(b) adds
new instruments at their modification-date value. B43(c) changes beneficial
vesting conditions; B44 preserves the original accounting against adverse
changes, except cancellations. B44A–C separately governs cash-to-equity
modifications and identified replacement awards; it removes the remeasured
liability, recognizes equity for services received at the new measurement date
and puts the difference in profit or loss. The two-clock case keeps original
vesting unchanged and gives only additional instruments a shorter service
period; it does not assume that a shortened original vesting condition leaves
original recognition unchanged.

Inspected IFRS9.6.2.3–6.4.1, 6.5.8–12, 6.6.1–6 and B6.6.1–16. Net risk
management must be factual; an abstract net amount is insufficient. Offsetting
cash-flow groups require foreign-currency risk and nature, volume and the
periods in which each forecast transaction affects earnings. Effectiveness
includes offsetting gross components, without early recognition of forecast
transactions. B6.6.9 and B6.6.15 support the earlier-sale/later-expense reserve
case, separate presentation and subsequent release. No-offset groups instead
allocate the instrument's net gain/loss systematically without grossing it up.
Fair-value groups adjust individual carrying amounts under 6.6.5. Nil net
positions require all four 6.6.6 conditions; layers retain 6.6.3/B6.6.11–12's
identified pool and risk requirements. Group here means a group of items; it
does not waive consolidated-group external-party rules in 6.2.3/6.3.5–6.

The net example uses external cash sales and immediately consumed services,
not acquired inventory. This avoids importing nonfinancial-asset basis
adjustment into the separate-line timing calculation. Both cash movements are
operating; the designated forward follows those hedged flows under IAS7.16
(also inspected in R03-L). Discount/interest, credit changes and forward points
are zero only in this illustration. Actual valuations, forecast probability,
designation and legal facts require admission before application.

Amounts and journals are project derivations from these conditional facts.
Exact conservation checks support their arithmetic only. No new academic
method, option valuation, empirical superiority or broad literature closure is
claimed; R04's remaining obligations are unchanged.
