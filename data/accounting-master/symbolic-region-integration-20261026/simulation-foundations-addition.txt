17.13.2
A checked path is not a checked continuous region
The common executor accepts concrete rational amounts. The selected comparison therefore
uses exact points at the coupon cap, at the first trigger, and at and immediately to either
side of the later joint cash boundary. Those executions exercise important failure mechanisms.
They do not by themselves establish that every rate in a continuous region produces the correct
accounting result.
Within a region where the guards are fixed, the amount relations above are affine. If the opening
accounts map correctly, each event has the stated affine effect, and the same guard, timing
and memory conditions govern both descriptions, induction over the accepted events gives the
same mapped financial quantities. The conditions carry the argument: a wrong guard or lost
drawing right can change which event occurs even when its monetary formula is correct. A
general checker must verify those conditions and every supported method, rather than replace
that obligation with a larger sample of rates.
The concrete evidence at this stage has two parts. The exact continuous reference establishes
its financial formulas and feasible domains. The concrete common-engine comparison checks
complete accounting execution at declared points.

17.14.4
What can now leave the unfinished-work list
For the declared two-date model, all 3,872 rate-region and instruction obligations have a checked
partition. The partition contains 918 nonempty accepted leaves and 2,960 nonempty refusal
leaves; 4,772 additional children are discarded with checked contradiction certificates. These
are computational counts, not probabilities. They depend on the selected atom refinement and
cannot be interpreted as the likelihood of repayment or default.
The saved proof archive is re-read before its jobs enter a new certified queue. Each entry binds
the source revision, clause, method and sequence certificates, domain, query and horizon. A
pending job leaves the queue only with the corresponding verified proof. Zero-budget and
interrupted JSON continuations retain the same final partition as uninterrupted processing.
Checking the saved proof registry costs work in addition to the budget for newly completed
jobs.
The earlier concrete checkpoint correctly kept these regions unresolved under its own evidence.
The new result supplies separate whole-region certificates; it does not reinterpret the earlier
witness as a proof. The distinction is useful beyond this example: completing a computation
should mean satisfying the evidence obligation attached to that job, not merely reaching its
last instruction.
This result concerns known exact values, the supplied coupon and once-only facility terms, two
bounded rates and the selected event sequence. It leaves a further decision problem. An actor
can choose differently only when the actor’s information distinguishes the relevant histories.
Consequently, a list of financially feasible region-specific actions is not yet an admissible policy.
Binding the certified leaves to the observation map and checking decisions that must agree
across indistinguishable histories remains the next step. Higher-dimensional and nonlinear
relations, unknown-value semantics, actual accounting authority, numerical quantisation and
crash-durable recovery require additional evidence.4

Reference computations that
distinguish a useful approximation
from a wrong answer
A method can be tested most clearly when a small version of its target is known exactly. This
chapter constructs such targets for cash bounds, breach probabilities, policy comparison and
residual minimization. The calculations are analytic reference cases. They are not a benchmark
ranking and do not supply empirical evidence about a real company. Their role is to expose
errors that a large simulation can conceal behind apparently stable averages.
18.1
An exact distribution from a declared prior
Return to cash C = 90−2α, and now deliberately assume α is uniform on [0, 1]. This assumption
is not inferred from the financial statements. It creates a transparent probabilistic reference.
Cash is uniform on [88, 90], with mean 89 and variance 4/12 = 1/3.
For a covenant requiring cash at least 89.4, breach occurs when α > 0.3. Its probability is 0.7.
The expected cash conditional on breach is the mean of the interval [88, 89.4), namely 88.7.
These quantities answer different questions: unconditional mean, breach frequency and severity
conditional on breach.
A simulation with the right mean but the wrong breach probability is not accurate for covenant
analysis. For example, a two-point approximation placing equal mass at 88 and 90 has mean 89
but breach probability one half. Matching the first moment does not preserve threshold events.
The exact distribution also gives quantiles. Under the usual left-continuous generalized-inverse
convention, the p-quantile is 88 + 2p for 0 < p < 1, with endpoint values defined by continuous
extension. The convention should be explicit when discrete mass points are introduced, because
quantile definitions can differ at jumps.
18.2
A discrete grid with known bias
Approximate α by equally weighted midpoints (i −1/2)/n, for i = 1, . . . , n. The mean of the
grid remains one half, so mean cash is exactly 89 for every n. Breach frequency is the fraction
of midpoints above 0.3. For n = 4, midpoints are 0.125, 0.375, 0.625 and 0.875, giving breach
probability 0.75.
