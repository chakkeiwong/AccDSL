The separate margin tables also clarify which contracts are being followed. At 1 January 2022
the reported issued-contract CSM is −22,688 million and the held-reinsurance net cost/gain is
368 million. Adding those reported totals gives −22,320 million, consistent with the rounded
−22.3 billion margin component above. The issued-contract transition table follows groups in
force at transition, and its CSM amounts concern the general measurement model and VFA;
the associated revenue also includes PAA contracts. Its later balances therefore cannot be
substituted for the whole subsequent portfolio or used as if CSM and revenue had identical
populations (Munich Re, 2024, pp. 210–211 and 254–255).
Finally, disclosure of a method does not supply all its inputs. Munich Re reports a 6% cost-
of-capital rate for its non-financial risk adjustment, applied to projected capital requirements
with diversification and discounting. Its equivalent one-year confidence level is about 90% at
transition, compared with 88% for 2023 and 87% for the comparative period. These are dated
properties of the insurer’s calculation, not universal IFRS parameters. The rate also cannot be
imported into the separate fair-value calculation merely because that discussion mentions cost
of capital. Reconstructing an individual group still requires its cash flows, capital projections,
curves, allocations and supported transition-method choice (Munich Re, 2024, pp. 165, 183 and
185).
