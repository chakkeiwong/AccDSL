# Finite shared-book reference composition

NativeReturnBookReference/1 holds P's exact legal account balances, one return
component, separate recovered lots, tax premises and event adjustments, gross
cash flows and lifetime event identities. Its account registry partitions
every account into assets, liabilities, equity or income. Original component
balances allocate the shared book; they create no separate cash or capital.

The source fixture is a projection of retained CompanyComposition attempt-03.
Its byte binding and complete projection are checked before initialization.
Initialization itself accepts a caller-supplied conditional opening; its API
does not establish source trust. Component facts needed for an opening must
be known and consistent. Unknown future tax findings may survive until the
tax-dependent step, preserving earlier valid accounting. Proposals also do
not establish external source or standards admission.

Native return settlement and whole-lot sale derive their own journals and lot
memory from typed declarations. Native P tax derives cumulative taxable income
from current-period pretax income and dated return adjustments; it subtracts
current tax already recognized. The old adjustment remains in history at the
next annual opening but falls outside that year's calculation window. The
selected method requires the retained synthetic rate and tax findings. No
unknown is zero and a tax refund route remains unsupported.

Other corporate events use a caller-selected adapter to the retained checked
method. The adapter verifies its before-state against the native P book and
recomputes the journal; it never reads expected journal or after-state records.
Return/tax allocations cannot be changed through that surrounding-event path.
S's legal book and group ownership remain in the retained adapter. Therefore
the complete company comparison is a mixed-method reference; it is not a new
native implementation of every corporate or consolidation method.

Each proposal binds full before-state, event and selected method-bundle
identity. Applying it independently recomputes the expected transition and
compares the complete proposal, including journals, cash origins and after
memory. This is trusted in-process semantic replay, not durable commit or
concurrency control. Source evidence and code trust remain caller obligations.
Input dictionaries are copied; rejected events preserve the preceding state.
run_prefix returns that state and a classified failure without manufacturing
an annual opening. The typed and text interfaces produce identical proposals
for the complete retained trace.

Annual reporting requires the full tax cutoff. It includes every supported
account, separate refund/recovery and deferred balances, gross legal cash,
profit and equity. The indirect operating bridge subtracts the 10 of opening
interest paid even though current finance expense is zero. Retained equity
movements beyond the finite profile require a different report method. Legal
investment payments remain investing; group reclassification is not copied.

Annual carry transfers income to retained earnings, resets period cash and
income, and retains lots (including disposed identities), component memory,
source references, tax-event history and lifetime event ids. The new state is
conditional derived memory. It is not a newly observed company balance sheet.
The next-year no-activity case checks that the -24 settlement adjustment and
deferred reversal are not recognized a second time; it is a deterministic
carry discriminator, not a forecast.

The 13-step source comparator checks every P balance, journal account, gross
cash origin, return balance and recovered-lot allocation. The retained method's
complete group state is also replayed unchanged. Negative cases cover changed
proposals, stale events, missing/false/ill-typed findings, funding failure,
partial tax prefixes, duplicate lots/events and incomplete annual carry.
Schema counts and timing are explanatory; exact comparisons and invariant
preservation decide finite acceptance. General return estimates, partial lot
sales, other tax methods, real-company source admission, portable normalized
IR, durability and independent/domain/human acceptance remain open.
