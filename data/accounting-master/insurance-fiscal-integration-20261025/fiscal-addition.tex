\paragraph{Carry the same insurer journals across explicit calendar years.}
The cumulative example can also support an explicitly supplied calendar-year
interpretation. The first three native events still occur on 1 January 2026,
and the next three on 1 January 2027. The host now observes and closes recorded
balances on 31 December 2025, 2026 and 2027, reopening the following year each
time. The first close has zero profit because the selected sequence contains
no event in 2025. These are hypothetical observations of the supplied sequence;
the event population remains unknown, so the empty first year is not evidence
that an actual insurer had no business.

Inserting closing commands alone is insufficient. After the first three
events, the cumulative finance-expense account is 10. Closing 2026 resets it
to zero and carries the period loss of 5 into the selected retained-equity
account. The unchanged fourth method would still require expense of 10 before
posting its negative expense of 5. The checked naive-carry attempt stops at
that condition, after preserving the valid earlier events and closing records.

The repair changes the balance conditions, leaving the journals intact. Write
$b^{\mathrm{cum}}$ for a vector of temporary-account balances expected by a
second-date method and $c$ for those accounts' balances closed at the end of
2026. Both vectors contain finance expense, service revenue, loss-reversal
income and claims expense in that order, measured in the same illustrative
units. The corresponding fiscal-period condition uses
\[
 b^{\mathrm{fiscal}}=b^{\mathrm{cum}}-c,
 \qquad c=(10,-44,-11,50).
\]
This subtraction applies to the opening and resulting balance conditions of
each of the three 2027 methods. For example, finance expense must begin at
$10-10=0$ and end at $5-10=-5$. The method still posts exactly $-5$ to expense
and $+5$ to liability. Reversing the 24 constant substitutions recovers all
three original method definitions; their monetary and memory operations do
not change. When the opening loss component is zero, the same procedure uses
that case's own closed vector, including revenue of $-55$ and reversal of zero.

Every memory leaf has an explicit preservation policy. The loss component,
next-step marker and case identity therefore survive closing, as does unrelated
company memory. The retained account must be a supplied equity account outside
the six insurer roles. Closing has no cash effect: it zeros the income and
expense accounts, transfers their signed sum to retained equity and archives
the report, closing balances and next opening together. A reset of required
memory or an omitted carry cannot be treated as valid continuation.

For the case without unrelated equipment, the resulting year-end observations
are as follows. Claim payments are shown as positive outflows; the closing
liability is the positive statement amount.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
\toprule
Year & Profit & Claim payments & Cash & Liability & Equity \\
\midrule
2025 & 0 & 0 & 200 & 100 & 100 \\
2026 & $-5$ & 50 & 150 & 55 & 95 \\
2027 & 10 & 45 & 105 & 0 & 105 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The two nonzero annual profits sum to the original cumulative profit of 5;
payments sum to 95. Reopening 2028 preserves cash of 105, liability of zero
and equity of 105, while period profit and period cash flows start at zero.
An additional equipment balance of 17 with matching opening equity survives
every step and raises each equity observation by 17 without changing profit
or cash. The two account charts and both loss-component cases give the same
correspondence under their explicit mappings.

\code{InsuranceFiscalBook/1} recomputes the complete cumulative proposal and
checks its expected hash before compiling the fiscal sequence. It binds the
calendar policy, selected retained account and complete memory policies, and
retains the original native dates, gross postings and loss origins. Complete
traces and closing archives are recorded in
\path{data/accounting-master/insurance-fiscal-book-20261025/}.
The result demonstrates recorded-balance carry under this supplied policy;
actual fiscal completeness, source applicability and durable posting remain
unestablished.
