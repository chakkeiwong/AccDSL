# R03-L source reading and interpretation

15 October 2026. Retained EU2023/1803 clauses inspected:

- IAS2 9–27,34: eligible cost, normal capacity, abnormal waste, cost formulas
  and recognition. Equivalent-unit algorithms are local derivations.
- IAS19 64,120–127 and IFRIC14 7–24: availability, unconditional refunds,
  costs, future contribution reductions, prepayments and past-service funding.
- IAS7 6,10–16,31–36,43–44 and IFRIC1 1–8: activity classification, cash
  versus noncash events, interest and restoration measurement.

The official July2012 staff paper's cover and AppendixA A35–43 were read.
It compares activity-based and capitalization-based rehabilitation
classifications and supports operating treatment for its facts. Its status
notice explicitly prevents treating this as an authoritative Interpretation.
The manuscript's proposed operating branch uses matched payment facts and
preserves the competing investing consequence.

The official December2025 IASB staff paper's cover, objective, paragraphs
47–54 and surrounding classification discussion were inspected. It concerns
possible future work, does not determine acceptable IFRS application and
does not establish a new restoration rule. It is not cited as operative
authority. No claim about subsequent standard-setting decisions is made.

Both PDFs and their extracted text are archived in sources/ and hash-bound in
cases.json. They are standards staff materials, not academic-method papers;
broader R04 literature obligations are unchanged.

The pension reduction formula uses the net amount across the two periods,
then floors paragraph20(b), and adds the eligible prepayment separately.
This avoids an incorrect per-period floor. Refund and contribution routes
are assumed incompatible in that comparison. The past-service case assumes
the contribution is entirely unavailable after payment, an actual obligation
on a distinct funding basis and no intervening interest.

The costing examples assume homogeneous units and explicit stage completion.
Normal yield is supplied before actual output; abnormal waste is not relabeled
normal to make cost conservation work. Exact rational values precede the
illustrative cent allocations. Conservation is engineering evidence for
these cases, not proof of real cost/actuarial/legal facts.
