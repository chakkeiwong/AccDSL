# Exact additivity boundary: checked within the supplied model

22 October 2026. The threshold-bonus numerator has fixed additive conversion
increments exactly when at least one of four conditions holds: the bonus or
after-tax effect vanishes; baseline profit is at or above the threshold; even
full conversion stays at or below the threshold; or at most one series has
positive interest savings. `derivation.md` proves both directions for fixed
nonnegative savings, fixed positive share increments, positive ordinary shares
and rates in [0,1]. The proof explicitly includes both threshold equalities,
zero savings and an empty inventory.

The claimed target is modularity of the complete conditional earnings
function, N(S)=N(empty)+sum a_i. The quantity computed independently is pretax
profit minus the supplied bonus minus proportional tax in every finite subset.

stated cases. Outside them, the full-set reconstruction strictly overstates
earnings by q(1-t)[sum min(I_i,H-P)-(H-P)]. This is a mathematical result about
the supplied model. Actual rights, recognition, attribution, tax applicability,
independent conversion eligibility and current authority remain separate.
