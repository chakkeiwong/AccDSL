Source identity also matters within a packet. Several insurer recovery examples have the same
display label. Selecting them through a map keyed only by that label would silently discard
earlier examples. The provider therefore uses the exact packet hash and a stable position-
qualified identifier, while retaining the original label for explanation. This distinguishes every
retained example without changing its request, receipt or financial state.
The composition language keeps its existing declarations and adds one explicit dependency check.
Equal financial profiles with different execution policies require a policy adapter before their
complete worlds can form a sequence. Financial identity still belongs to the financial profile and
request pair: changing the policy does not create a second namespace in which the same payment
may be posted again. Different financial profiles retain their separate state-adapter requirement.
An unknown acknowledgement continues to make every dependent stage unresolved, including
stages whose adjacent worlds happen to match.
The checked successor retains all 77 earlier complete text/typed composition results. Its 959

compositions include the native insurer commit-to-report chains, bank publication/replay pairs

assessments recompute through fresh readers. A wrong policy, hidden head change, duplicate
posting on replay or invented uniform bank schema is refused.

39.7.6
Checked examples and remaining scope
The effect comparison covers the retained 1,892 exchanges from the four profiles in Table 39.2,
including 412 possible crash worlds and their retries. Semantic counterexamples change native
flags, receipts, protected heads, journals, prior decisions and acknowledgement knowledge;
changed records are rehashed so that integrity checking alone cannot hide a missing binding
check. These are deterministic documentary comparisons, not observations of a deployed
transaction service.
The composition examples include 66 retained native commit-to-report sequences and matched
text/typed incompatible, partial, unknown and refused cases. No receiving request in the selected
packets consumes a receipt, so the receipt relation has a specified rule and negative checks
but no checked positive handoff. General method-input authoring, cross-profile adaptation,
whole-company source admission and independent implementation acceptance remain unfinished.
