# DeclaredWorkingCapital/1

Inputs: complete DeclaredCompanyProgram/1 body, its exact recomputed result,
a successful report command ID, explicit eight operating-account roles and unit
cost memory pointer. Output is a provisional supporting movement schedule,not a
standards-complete disclosure note. Flags remain false. Never consume an arbitrary
balance snapshot or unverified trace as evidence of supported operations.

Select a successful report trace row,including a carry's preclose report. Its
materialized report request supplies actual opening and closing. Start immediately
after the last successful preceding carry,or the program opening. Earlier interim
reports create no movement. Do not include the selected carry's closing journal
in operating movements. A stopped program may provide an earlier successful report;
a failed report cannot be selected. Check opening/closing hashes and period lineage.

Rebuild the six supported data definitions under the selected opening context,
role map and unit-cost pointer. Match exact method content digests,not registry
keys or prefixes. For each supported event,derive classified movements from its
verified full journal and typed inputs. Match each selected before/after balance
continuously. Unknown methods touching receivables,inventory,payables,payroll-payable
or the unit-cost premise are unsupported,even if their net period change cancels.
Other methods with zero effect on these accounts and unchanged price memory can
be retained as explicitly out-of-scope observations,not misclassified movements.

Four schedules use positive asset/liability presentation. Each contains account,
opening,closing,event movements,and exact reconciliation. Receivables separate
credit_sales and collections; inventory separates purchases and cost_released;
supplier payables separate credit_purchases and payments; payroll payable separates
accruals and payments. An event may contribute to two schedules. Every movement
retains event ID,date,method hash,typed inputs with provenance,request/result hashes,
signed debit journal amount and signed presentation change. Quantity and unit
price are shown only where supplied by the checked definition; no ageing or lot
valuation is inferred. Gross increases/decreases are sums of signed movements,
not absolute net changes. Zero supplied movements remain traceable.

Working-capital cash reconciliation: -delta(receivables)-delta(inventory)+
delta(payables)+delta(payroll payable). It is a contribution,not total indirect
operating cash. The selected report's profit and direct OCF may be compared only
as an explanatory diagnostic; unrelated noncash/working-capital items can leave a
residual. Do not insert a plug or claim this four-account bridge is universal.

Require complete exact role map/classification and unchanged identity/context,
verify every scoped account's journal movement and closing sum. The schedule
retains explicit missing disclosures: receivable ageing/impairment,inventory
valuation/quantity coverage,payable maturities/terms,related parties,tax and source
population. Fiscal completeness,notes completeness and admission remain false.
All supplied evidence is input-bound by digest; verify reconstructs the schedule
and compares canonical bytes. Large traces inherit the runner's declared limits.

Gross-movement check: inspect every nonzero monetary posting for unclassified
methods,not only their net account journal. Same-event offsetting postings cannot
be treated as absent. Preserve exact program bounds; a diagnostic over that bound
must be reduced rather than interpreted as evidence about schedule correctness.
