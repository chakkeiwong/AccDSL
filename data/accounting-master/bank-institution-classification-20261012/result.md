The advanced-approach rule has several routes: a G-SIB holding-company parent,
Category II bank status, a specified parent using advanced approaches, or the
bank's own election. The Category II/III bank definitions link to particular
parent categories or independent-bank tests. Group branding and a single
balance-sheet size do not establish these facts.

Entry and continued category status have different clocks. Defined averages
usually use four reported quarters, with specified shorter-history provisions;
several exit alternatives require each of the four recent quarters to be below
the relevant thresholds. For U.S. BHCs and intermediate holding companies,
the quarterly total-asset measure itself uses daily or Wednesday averages as
reported on FR Y-9C. A period-end accounting balance cannot simply replace it.
Foreign and covered savings-and-loan holding-company populations have separate
scope and reporting definitions. Classification does not establish every
provision's compliance transition date.
