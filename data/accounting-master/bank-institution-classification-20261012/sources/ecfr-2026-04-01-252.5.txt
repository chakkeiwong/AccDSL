§ 252.5 Categorization of banking organizations.
[1] (a) General. (1) A U.S. bank holding company with average total consolidated assets of $100 billion or more must determine its category among the four categories described in paragraphs (b) through (e) of this section at least quarterly.
[2] (2) A U.S. intermediate holding company with average total consolidated assets of $100 billion or more must determine its category among the three categories described in paragraphs (c) through (e) of this section at least quarterly.
[3] (3) A foreign banking organization with average total consolidated assets of $100 billion or more and average combined U.S. assets of $100 billion or more must determine its category among the three categories described in paragraphs (c) through (e) of this section at least quarterly.
[4] (b) Global systemically important BHC. A banking organization is a global systemically important BHC if it is identified as a global systemically important BHC pursuant to 12 CFR 217.402.
[5] (c) Category II. (1) A banking organization is a Category II banking organization if the banking organization:
[6] (i) Has:
[7] (A)(1) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, $700 billion or more in average total consolidated assets;
[8] (2) For a foreign banking organization, $700 billion or more in average combined U.S. assets; or
[9] (B)(1) $75 billion or more in average cross-jurisdictional activity; and
[10] (2)(i) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, $100 billion or more in average total consolidated assets; or
[11] (ii) For a foreign banking organization, $100 billion or more in average combined U.S. assets; and
[12] (ii) Is not a global systemically important BHC.
[13] (2) After meeting the criteria in paragraph (c)(1) of this section, a banking organization continues to be a Category II banking organization until the banking organization:
[14] (i) Has:
[15] (A)(1) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, less than $700 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; or
[16] (2) For a foreign banking organization, less than $700 billion in combined U.S. assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; and
[17] (B) Less than $75 billion in cross-jurisdictional activity for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[18] (ii) Has:
[19] (A) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, less than $100 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[20] (B) For a foreign banking organization, less than $100 billion in combined U.S. assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; or
[21] (iii) Meets the criteria in paragraph (b) to be a global systemically important BHC.
[22] (d) Category III. (1) A banking organization is a Category III banking organization if the banking organization:
[23] (i) Has:
[24] (A)(1) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, $250 billion or more in average total consolidated assets; or
[25] (2) For a foreign banking organization, $250 billion or more in average combined U.S. assets; or
[26] (B)(1)(i) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, $100 billion or more in average total consolidated assets; or
[27] (ii) For a foreign banking organization, $100 billion or more in average combined U.S. assets; and
[28] (2) At least:
[29] (i) $75 billion in average total nonbank assets;
[30] (ii) $75 billion in average weighted short-term wholesale funding; or
[31] (iii) $75 billion in average off-balance sheet exposure;
[32] (ii) Is not a global systemically important BHC; and
[33] (iii) Is not a Category II banking organization.
[34] (2) After meeting the criteria in paragraph (d)(1) of this section, a banking organization continues to be a Category III banking organization until the banking organization:
[35] (i) Has:
[36] (A)(1) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, less than $250 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; or
[37] (2) For a foreign banking organization, less than $250 billion in combined U.S. assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[38] (B) Less than $75 billion in total nonbank assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[39] (C) Less than $75 billion in weighted short-term wholesale funding for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; and
[40] (D) Less than $75 billion in off-balance sheet exposure for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; or
[41] (ii) Has:
[42] (A) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, less than $100 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; or
[43] (B) For a foreign banking organization, less than $100 billion in combined U.S. assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[44] (iii) Meets the criteria in paragraph (b) of this section to be a global systemically important BHC; or
[45] (iv) Meets the criteria in paragraph (c)(1) of this section to be a Category II banking organization.
[46] (e) Category IV. (1) A banking organization is a Category IV banking organization if the banking organization:
[47] (i) Is not global systemically important BHC;
[48] (ii) Is not a Category II banking organization;
[49] (iii) Is not a Category III banking organization; and
[50] (iv) Has:
[51] (A) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, average total consolidated assets of $100 billion or more; or
[52] (B) For a foreign banking organization, average combined U.S. assets of $100 billion or more.
[53] (2) After meeting the criteria in paragraph (e)(1), a banking organization continues to be a Category IV banking organization until the banking organization:
[54] (i) Has:
[55] (A) For a U.S. bank holding company or a U.S. intermediate holding company, less than $100 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[56] (B) For a foreign banking organization, less than $100 billion in combined U.S. assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[57] (ii) Meets the criteria in paragraph (b) of this section to be a global systemically important BHC;
[58] (iii) Meets the criteria in paragraph (c)(1) of this section to be a Category II banking organization; or
[59] (iv) Meets the criteria in paragraph (d)(1) of this section to be a Category III banking organization.
