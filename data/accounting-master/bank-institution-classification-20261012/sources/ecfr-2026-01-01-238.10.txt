§ 238.10 Categorization of banking organizations.
[1] (a) General. A banking organization with average total consolidated assets of $100 billion or more must determine its category among the three categories described in paragraphs (b) through (d) of this section at least quarterly.
[2] (b) Category II. (1) A banking organization is a Category II banking organization if the banking organization has:
[3] (i) $700 billion or more in average total consolidated assets; or
[4] (ii)(A) $75 billion or more in average cross-jurisdictional activity; and
[5] (B) $100 billion or more in average total consolidated assets.
[6] (2) After meeting the criteria in paragraph (b)(1) of this section, a banking organization continues to be a Category II banking organization until the banking organization has:
[7] (i)(A) Less than $700 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; and
[8] (B) Less than $75 billion in cross-jurisdictional activity for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; or
[9] (ii) Less than $100 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters.
[10] (c) Category III. (1) A banking organization is a Category III banking organization if the banking organization:
[11] (i) Has:
[12] (A) $250 billion or more in average total consolidated assets; or
[13] (B) $100 billion or more in average total consolidated assets and at least:
[14] (1) $75 billion in average total nonbank assets;
[15] (2) $75 billion in average weighted short-term wholesale funding; or
[16] (3) $75 billion in average off-balance sheet exposure; and
[17] (ii) Is not a Category II banking organization.
[18] (2) After meeting the criteria in paragraph (c)(1) of this section, a banking organization continues to be a Category III banking organization until the banking organization:
[19] (i) Has:
[20] (A) Less than $250 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[21] (B) Less than $75 billion in total nonbank assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[22] (C) Less than $75 billion in weighted short-term wholesale funding for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; and
[23] (D) Less than $75 billion in off-balance sheet exposure for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; or
[24] (ii) Has less than $100 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters; or
[25] (iii) Meets the criteria in paragraph (b)(1) of this section to be a Category II banking organization.
[26] (d) Category IV. (1) A banking organization with average total consolidated assets of $100 billion or more is a Category IV banking organization if the banking organization:
[27] (i) Is not a Category II banking organization; and
[28] (ii) Is not a Category III banking organization.
[29] (2) After meeting the criteria in paragraph (d)(1) of this section, a banking organization continues to be a Category IV banking organization until the banking organization:
[30] (i) Has less than $100 billion in total consolidated assets for each of the four most recent calendar quarters;
[31] (ii) Meets the criteria in paragraph (b)(1) of this section to be a Category II banking organization; or
[32] (iii) Meets the criteria in paragraph (c)(1) of this section to be a Category III banking organization.
