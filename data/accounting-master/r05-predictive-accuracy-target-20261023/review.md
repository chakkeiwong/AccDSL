translated into a lower-CRPS directional comparison, while the frozen
material-improvement rule remains separate. Exact point-law fixtures catch
sign reversal, asset-scale mismatch, survivor reweighting, late comparator
selection, invalid weights and nonpositive assets. Diagnostics are not
promoted to statistical evidence.

about cash shortfall or financing decisions rather than score accuracy. That
would require a new decision loss and margin; it would not change this first
study's stated estimand. The weakest evidence is the future-origin law and
dependence-compatible interval, both still unselected. A negative descriptive
mean would remain nomination evidence until those gaps are repaired.

No forecast, inference, economic-value claim, source admission, runtime or
human acceptance follows.
