# Predictive-accuracy target recorded

The first E3 study now has a declared research purpose: compare predictive
accuracy. The primary directional target is the expected candidate-minus-locked-
comparator difference in operating-cash-flow CRPS after the same positive
as-of-asset scaling. Negative is better; zero is the equality boundary. The
user did not select an economic decision or numeric material-improvement
margin, so those remain outside this target.

The equal-company estimand is one ninth per named issuer, with symbolic
within-issuer origin weights. The full denominator survives unequal origin
counts and missing cells. Exact fixtures verify three point-law CRPS cases,
two scaling cases, four weighted-effect cases, four comparator-lock cases and
seven invalid-input refusals. They use no empirical observations and admit no
inference procedure.

Promotion remains conditional on a dependence-compatible uncertainty bound
below zero and all validity vetoes. A material-improvement claim additionally
needs a separately justified negative margin. The future-origin law, temporal
weights, asynchronous grouping, refit/tuning cadence, financial scope,
service policy, inference theory, confidence, multiplicity, replication and
resource budget remain open. A scalar CRPS result cannot promote the secondary
joint cash/debt task or economic value.
