The plan passes for the scoped reading. All 79 source/arithmetic checks pass,
including hashes, exact selected reading sequences and the joint rounding
witness. Use the preceding acquisition bridge as the reader's entry point.
Introduce the actual table scope and signs before the compact two-population
comparison. Work the apparent numerical discrepancy, then distinguish the

No new authority, actual valuation, accounting compliance, human comprehension
or software implementation follows. The author voice stays provisional.
The strongest alternative explanation is that combined segments conceal
different groups, dates and cash-flow treatments; that is why the public table
does not identify the preceding example's A. A binding-language difference,
issuer correction or incompatible unrounded data would reopen the reading.
