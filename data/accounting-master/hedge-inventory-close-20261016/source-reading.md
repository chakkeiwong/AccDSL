# Hedge reserve into inventory: source and boundaries

Inspected IFRS9.6.5.11(a)–(d), local36084–36139: cumulative effective hedge
amount is OCI; when the forecast purchase produces a non-financial asset,
remove the accumulated reserve directly into initial cost. This is not an OCI
reclassification adjustment. IFRS9.6.5.12,36144–36160, governs expected versus
cancelled future cash flows separately; no cancellation is assumed here.
IAS2.10–11,2380–2393, supplies purchase cost; IAS2.34,2577–2586, recognizes the
carrying cost on sale. IAS7.16,2848–2867, classifies identifiable hedge cash
flows with the hedged position. Thus supplier, inventory-sale and this purchase-
hedge settlement are operating cash flows, with separate gross origins.
IFRS9.3.2.3(a),34445–34453, and3.3.1,34771–34776, support removal of an expired
asset right/discharged liability. The example explicitly supplies complete
settlement and discharge at carrying value, without fees or further valuation.

Earlier close at the selected observation is an explicit supplied contract
finding, not inferred from the hedge's original final maturity date. All130
units are then purchased and recognized at the contemporaneous supplier price,
with no extra acquisition costs and NRV at least the adjusted cost. All units
are later sold; sale recognition is supplied rather than an IFRS15 method.
The report covers the cumulative pre-tax subledger from original designation
through the sale date, not a claim that observations from different fiscal
years belong to one annual EPS or tax period.

For supplier cost C, signed reserve R, derivative D and cumulative hedge
profit I=D−R, inventory is C−R. Debit reserve R, credit inventory R; a negative
R reverses both directions. Settle derivative by debiting cash D and crediting
derivative D. Subsequent sale S gives cost of sales C−R and cumulative pre-tax
profit S−C+R+I=S−C+D. Cash ends at opening cash+S−C+D. The initial cumulative
OCI R and direct basis transfer −R reconcile reserve to zero; the latter is
not an OCI reclassification or earnings item. Final equality alone cannot
verify the route: posting R directly to profit would misstate inventory and
pre-sale earnings, even if final cumulative profit happened to agree.
