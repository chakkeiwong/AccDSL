26 October 2026. MC03/05/07/17/20/24/25/33/34; P17-06.01.
CreditCashClassification/1 checks a separate read-only classification layer over
all 36 retained credit projections: nine schedules, two charts and presence or
absence of an unrelated equipment balance. Three explicitly synthetic policy
premises yield 108 classification cases and 684 cumulative observations.
At 372 rational observations, the unchanged CompanyBookReport/1 reports and
reconciles cash, balance, income and equity. The remaining 312 retain their exact
single-root financial amounts without rounding them into host-book admission.

Each cash origin binds to its immutable index, content digest and native journal
prefix. Price, integral cost, integral fee, collection and zero-valued origins
remain distinct. Exact declared components may split an origin but must preserve
its sign and total; offsetting components cannot hide gross flows. Classification
does not change the financial journals or native reports. The original archived
unclassified acquisition report remains refused.

In the independently checked price/cost example, price pays 98, integral cost
pays 2 and collections receive 44 and 55. Under the synthetic mixed premise,
investing has receipts 99, payments 98 and net 1; operating has payments 2 and
net -2. Total cash change is -1. These labels are supplied test premises, not
an IAS 7 or issuer-policy conclusion. A separate all-financing branch checks
the third supported label without proposing it as an accounting treatment.

Missing classification of the zero integral-fee origin still yields unresolved
classification, despite an unclassified net of zero. An unknown later collection
does not contaminate the complete earlier prefix, but makes later statements
partial. The unknown reason survives. Twelve negative cases refuse stale
case/origin/policy/context bindings, duplicate origins, bad signs/sums, unsupported
activities, noncanonical amounts, invented admission and hidden netting.
