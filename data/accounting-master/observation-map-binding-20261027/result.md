decision dates, certified observation keys, source heads, action meanings and
explicit tag evidence. Finite first-stage maps cannot index hidden rights, full
world IDs or future observations. Second-stage messages retain prior messages
and the actor's own action. Internal mechanism IDs remain support labels; only
declared tokens are placed in the actor's history.
