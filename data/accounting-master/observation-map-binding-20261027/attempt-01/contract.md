# Actor observation-map binding: inspected execution contract

27 October2026. MC01/03/04/05/11/12/13/14/18/24/25/26/28/33/34;
W01/03/04/11/12/13/16. Conditional design reference; product implementation held.

Question: can finite observation declarations and their source/actor/time premises
be transported and reused without granting hidden mechanism identity or excluding
an unfavorable source outcome? Baseline: protected BoundTemporalPolicyFamily/1
and both accepted common-history solvers. Preserve successful predecessor writers.

## Interface inspection and skeptical audit

The existing solver's reveal switch exposes each distinct mechanism ID. A visible
tag can instead have the same value in two mechanisms. Always call both solvers
with reveal=never and encode only declared tags in actual messages. Keep source
and proof IDs in support records. Compare unique, colliding and absent tags.

A mapping indexed by full world rows could leak hidden state. Maps therefore use
only certified available keys: coarse first signal, refined first signal/trigger,
and second trigger/cash-coverage pair. Require exactly their complete observed
key population. Hidden rights, financial mode, future messages and row IDs cannot
be first-stage mapping keys. Unknown general expressions refuse. This closed
scope is a conditional information profile, not an empirical actor finding.

First decision time equals the family cutoff; second is strictly later. Exact
rational times preserve offset equivalence and submicrosecond boundaries. A tag
binds actor, selected source head, source bytes, visible token and an explicit
synthetic time observation. Known access cannot precede source authorship in any
retained timing support; unknown/late/insufficient evidence returns unresolved.
A source hash never grants a tag. Author-time checks can be conservative: this
profile does not refine the temporal family with new tag evidence. Unknowns
retain the original family rather than becoming false observations.

Numerical reuse depends on the entire certified financial/action population,
actual encoded messages, action meanings, methods and evidence identities.
Duplicate source supports with identical numerical meaning may share computation,
but all source outcomes and proof supports remain attached. Message labels belong
to the action interface: changing labels invalidates reuse even when feasible
policy counts coincide. Actor/source/cutoff changes may preserve the numerical
kernel but must change their binding. No incomplete or unresolved family borrows
an old answer. External expected identities are supplied premises, not proof of
source truth, authentication or actual actor knowledge.

## Evidence contract

Primary criterion: full cold/reused policy sets, source/world populations,
independently reconstructed mappings and expected change masks agree. Compare both
policy formulations and original four/zero/16 cases. Include changed labels,
swapped meanings, first/second/colliding tags, actor change, late corrections,
unknown access, tag chronology, missing/duplicate keys, forged prior payloads
and distinct zero-policy problems. Counts and runtime are explanatory only.

Promotion veto: hidden-identity leak, dropped unresolved outcome, missing support,
wrong policy set, wrong actor/time/source binding, altered protected inputs or an
admission flag. Repair the candidate. Correct infeasibility/unresolved outcomes
are expected evidence, not a continuation veto. Corrupt baselines or invalid
comparisons stop affected runs until repaired. No probability, actual knowledge,
accounting permission, general optimality or performance claim follows.
